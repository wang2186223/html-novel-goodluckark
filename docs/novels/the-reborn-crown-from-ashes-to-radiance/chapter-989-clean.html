Chapter 989

"Back off. She's my wife. I got this," Julian snapped, locking eyes with Milo. Zero apologies in his stare.

Milo backed down, probably because Elara was watching. He turned to her, his face softening. "Seriously, call me if you need anything. Any time." Then he finally walked out.

The second the door closed, Julian gave Rhys a quick nod, grabbed Elara's hand, and steered her out.

Elara's brain was still spinning over Rowan, but her body was done. Ever since that damn auction, it had been one shitshow after another. No breaks. Just pure, exhausting chaos. She was running on fumes.

Once they hit the house, Julian didn't nag. He saw the dark circles under her eyes. "Go crash," he said. "I've got some stuff to handle."

She headed straight for the bedroom. He turned toward his study.

She was too dead to even think. She threw herself under a hot shower, crawled into bed, and blacked out the second her head hit the pillow.

Hours blurred together. When she finally blinked, bright morning sun was slicing through the drapes. A whole new day.

She rolled over. Cold sheets. Julian's side was untouched. He hadn't even come to bed.

A knot tightened in her chest. What the hell was going on? Was it her? That other girl? Did he want her so bad he couldn't even stand to sleep next to his own wife?

She let out a dry, bitter laugh. She’d actually thought about fixing things. What a joke. Maybe there was nothing left to save. Time to stop hiding and face the music.

She stared at the ceiling for another minute, then dragged herself out of bed.

Downstairs, Julian was parked on the couch. He was glued to his tablet, whispering voice notes into his phone. Totally zoned out. He didn't even hear her footsteps.

A beat passed before he finally looked up. He forced a smile. "Hey. You should've stayed in bed. You look wiped."

"I'm fine," she said, locking eyes with him. "Did you—"

"Got a meeting at the office in a bit," he cut in, his eyes already darting back to a fresh notification. "But look, we need to talk later. What's your schedule today?"

She hesitated. "I'm heading to the hospital. Need to check on Rowan and talk to her doctor. Got some loose ends to tie up. I'll text you when I'm free."

"Sounds good." He stood up, grabbing his keys. "Food's on the table. Eat something before you leave. I gotta run, but seriously, call me if you need anything."

He paused, stepped closer, and pulled her into a quick, tight hug. He planted a kiss on her forehead, then bolted out the door.

Elara stood frozen, watching the door click shut. Whatever he wanted to "talk" about, she knew. It was about *her*.

Her stomach turned. She slumped at the kitchen table as the housekeeper set down a plate. She wasn't hungry, but she forced down a few bites anyway.

Once she finished, she drove over to the Sutton place, grabbed Blair, and they headed straight back to the hospital.

Rhys hadn't moved. He was still hunched over Rowan's bed, whispering to her. But Rowan just lay there, completely still, like a ghost.

"Oh. Hey," Rhys rasped, looking up. He blinked, surprised to see Elara and Blair walk through the door.
