Coraline was clueless. Elara had already mapped out her entire game. When Elara nodded yes, Coraline practically buzzed with triumph. *Gotcha,* she thought. In her head, Elara was just another sucker walking straight into a meat grinder.

"My turn first!" Coraline snatched her glass. She tipped her head back and chugged, throat working fast. No breaks, no breathing. She had to lock this in before Elara could chicken out.

Elara just stood there, a tiny, wicked smirk tugging at her lips. On her walk over, she’d already spiked Coraline’s cup. One look at Coraline's stash, and Elara knew the exact chemical. So, she slipped in a matching dose of her own special blend.

Talk about a joke. Coraline had zero clue that this exact love-potion—the hot, pricey stuff people used for wild bedroom games—was Elara’s own recipe. She’d literally cooked it up for her own late-night fun. And here was this idiot, trying to poison the chef with her own dish.

Too bad the stuff didn't touch Elara. She was totally immune. Plus, she always kept a blocker in her pocket, just to be safe. Coraline thought she was some master villain, but she was playing checkers against a grandmaster.

Elara didn't even blink. She raised her glass, smooth and cool, looking like absolute royalty. The whole room stared, jaws dropping at her insane tolerance.

"No way. How is she still upright?" a guy muttered, eyes popping.

"Swear to God, her liver is made of steel. She’s not even buzzed."

"Wild. I’ve never seen anything like this," someone else said, shaking their head.

"She literally drank the heaviest hitters under the table tonight, and now she’s taking on Coraline? Insane."

"She’s a freak of nature, seriously. If I wasn't seeing this, I'd say it's fake."

"Pro tip: don't ever challenge Elara unless you want to get absolutely wrecked."

"But wait, she’s had a million drinks. She’s gotta hit a wall soon, right?"

"Duh. Coraline waiting until she’s wasted to jump her is dirty as hell."

"Yeah, totally rigged if you ask me."

"Doesn't matter. Elara looks fine. Like, she doesn't give a crap."

While the room buzzed, Blair Sutton and Kaelen Steel glared daggers at Coraline. They were pissed. Cornering Elara after she’d been chugging all night? That was a garbage move.

"Seriously, Coraline? Do you have any self-respect?" Blair spat, her voice dripping with pure disgust. "You’re completely sober, and Callie’s been drinking for hours. Now you want a face-off? That is so cheap."

Coraline looked over, a nasty little smirk on her face. "Hey, I'm just giving her a chance to show off. How else is everyone gonna see how tough she is?"

"Oh, my bad, should we throw you a parade?" Blair snapped, shaking with rage.

Coraline shrugged. "Just giving her the spotlight, babe. Besides, she’s not a quitter. Look at her—she’s totally down."

"She’s wasted! Of course she’s not thinking straight," Blair fired back. "But what's your excuse? Are you just a total snake?"

"Yeah. Zero shame," Kaelen chimed in, giving her a look like she was trash on his shoe.

Right then, Victor cut through the crowd. The temperature in the room seemed to drop ten degrees as he walked up. He stepped right up next to Blair and Kaelen, his eyes locked onto Coraline like a predator.
