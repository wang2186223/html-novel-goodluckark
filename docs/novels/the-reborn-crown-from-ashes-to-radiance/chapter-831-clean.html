"That's total bullshit!"

A voice cut through the noise. Cody Vance marched in, already clutching a live mic.

Heads snapped. Every eye in the room locked onto him. The guy looked straight out of a magazine—killer jawline, fresh streetwear, exactly what his stans drooled over online. Except he wasn't here to pose. He was here to shield Chloe Sterling.

Hold up. What?

Side-eyes darted toward Cassian. Confusion buzzed through the crowd. Wasn't he a Jewell? He was supposed to rip Elara to shreds like the rest of his clan. But nope. Cassian wasn't playing backup for his family today. He was gearing up to fight for Elara.

Then the real weirdness clicked. This was a Jewell family presser, but half the seats were empty. Edgar, the oldest brother? Ghosted. Cassian, the third son? Just showed up.

"Uly, what the hell?" Leo Maxwell threw his brother a hard, shut-the-fuck-up look. "Didn't you have some big meeting or whatever?" Leo prayed Cassian would just stay quiet. If he started trashing them now, the Jewells were done for.

Julian glared daggers, too. He saw the trainwreck coming. Cassian had hated this plan from day one. God knows how Elara brainwashed him, but the guy was totally in her corner now. He used to loathe her more than anyone. Now? He was playing her white knight.

"Who said I was busy?" Cassian smirked, cold as ice. "Lysander, I warned you. I said don't do this stupid press conference. We should've sat down with Callie first. Sorted this out instead of throwing this public tantrum."

"And all these lies? Where’s the proof, huh?"

"You've got zero. You're just throwing wild accusations around because you're drooling over Grandpa's cash. You don't even know the actual number! You're just pissed that Callie got it and you didn't."

"Seriously, did Mom or Dad ever give a crap about the real Callie?"

"Did they cry even once?"

"Our own sister. Mom almost died in labor, and she blamed her for it her whole life."

"She treated Elara like some kind of curse, a bad omen."

"When Callie died, Mom didn't shed a single tear."

"And Dad? Just as cold."

"What about you two? Did you ever spare a thought for our dead sister?"

"Hell no. No grief, no sadness. Just dollar signs."

"Actually, scratch that. This isn't even about Grandpa's estate, is it?"

"It's about the cash Elara's holding right now. You want to rip it out of her hands."

"You're just straight-up greedy."

Cassian walked right up to the stage. The Jewell family's faces turned pure purple with rage.

"Cassian, shut your mouth!" Lysander bolted upright, shaking. "I don't know what kind of mind game Elara is playing on you, but we're just taking back what's ours!"

Fine, if Cassian wanted to be useless, whatever. But stabbing them in the back on live TV? Unacceptable.

Walter glared, his voice a low, gravelly growl. "Uly, drop it. The truth is going to come out anyway. The reporters, the crowd—they'll see right through this."
