Chapter 939

Elara hauled herself up into the chopper. Down on the tarmac, Blair was waving like a maniac, arms flailing.

A cold knot tightened in Blair’s gut. Rowan was in deep shit. But she shook it off. Elara was a straight-up badass. No freaking way Callie would let Rowan slip away.

Once they got back, she and Elara were going to absolutely wreck Rhys. That asshole had it coming.

The rotors roared. The bird shot up, ripping through the clouds until it shrank into a tiny black dot.

Blair stared until her eyes watered, waiting for the dot to vanish. She finally turned to head back to the venue.

One step, and she bumped into a wall. Victor. He was staring straight up at the empty sky, face like stone. He didn't blink for a solid ten seconds. Then, those dark eyes snapped down to her.

"That was Callie, right?" he demanded. "Where the hell is she going? What’s so damn important she bails on her own party?"

The party was still rocking inside, but she’d just bolted like the building was on fire.

Blair looked him dead in the eye. "Yeah," she muttered. She didn't hate the guy. Sure, Victor was bad news, but he’d never try to hurt Callie. Hell, Callie had literally pulled him back from the grave once.

"Talk to me. What’s the play?" Victor’s voice was pure ice.

"Rowan. She’s dying," Blair said, her voice cracking. "She’s our girl. It’s a code-red situation, so Callie went to pull her out."

"Rowan?" Victor’s brow furrowed. He paused, digging through his head, then his eyes widened. "The quiet chick? The one who follows Rhys around like a shadow?"

Blair nodded. "Yeah. Her."

"Wait, isn't she out with Rhys? In Stormgard?" Victor looked pissed. "That place is a literal meat grinder right now. If they’re on the ground, Urian’s supposed to have her back. She’s his freaking student. You’re telling me Urian can’t even keep his own kid alive?"

He let out a harsh laugh. "Is Rhys really that pathetic? He has to beg Elara to bail him out?"

"Bingo! Total waste of space!" Blair snapped, her blood boiling. "If he hadn't dragged her into his mess, Rowan would be fine. This is one hundred percent on him."

"He screws up, and Callie has to sweep up the glass," she spat.

Victor’s eyes turned into slits. "So Elara’s flying solo into Stormgard? To cut Rowan open and save her? Did Rhys send that chopper?"

"Rhys? Are you freaking kidding me?" Blair barked. "Callie pulled the strings herself. Rhys just made the call. If we waited for his ride, Rowan would be in a body bag by now."

Victor went dead silent. His face darkened. "So she did this all on her own. She’s flying into a warzone, solo, for one life?"

"Does she have any damn clue what Stormgard is? Is she insane?" He shook his head, muttering under his breath. "She’s walking right into a meat grinder."

Blair couldn't argue. Everyone knew Stormgard was hell on earth. But Rowan was too unstable to move. Elara had no choice. She had to go in.

Blair swallowed the lump in her throat, her eyes stinging. She glared back at him. "So what? We just sit here and watch her die? Screw Rhys, I don't give a shit about him. But I am not letting Rowan go."

If she could actually do anything useful, she’d be on that chopper too. Bullet or no bullet, she’d rather bleed out next to her friend than sit here uselessly.

Victor’s jaw ticked. He whipped out his phone and swiped the screen. "Yeah. Now. Get the bird here. Move it," he barked, then slammed the phone shut. He turned to Barnaby, his voice like steel. "Pack the gear. We're out."
