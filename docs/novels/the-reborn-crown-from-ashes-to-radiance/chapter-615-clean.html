Chapter 615

Beatrix marched up and down the sterile corridor, her stilettos hammering a frantic beat against the tile. She blocked the young nurse’s path, totally ticked off by the girl's chill attitude.

"You have zero clue what we're dealing with here!" Beatrix barked, her volume spiking. "Elara is no saint, okay? For all I know, she’s cooking up some sick plan to screw us over!"

"The girl has no soul. None! She’d sell her own brother down the river in a heartbeat. And seriously, she’s a walking curse. A total disaster magnet. The second she crawled into our lives, our luck tanked. Hell, I nearly kicked the bucket just delivering her! From her very first breath, she’s done nothing but ruin my life!"

The nurse just stood there, brain short-circuiting. *Hold up,* she thought, completely thrown. *Elara is her kid?*

Who blames a newborn for a rough labor? Crazy. Any normal mom would take a bullet for her kid. But this woman? She literally hated her own flesh and blood just for existing.

It made zero sense to the nurse. Then again, she’d handled plenty of nutcases on the ward before. She knew exactly how to play this.

"I hear you, ma'am," the nurse said, keeping her voice buttery smooth. "Look, why don't you hang out here? Let me go poke my head in and see how the doc's doing, yeah?"

She gave a quick nod. "I'll tell Elara to speed things up in there."

"Whatever. But if things go south, you tell me immediately!" Beatrix snapped, crowding the operating room door like she was ready to kick it down.

"Ma'am, this is a restricted area. Stay put, please. I'll come grab you the second I hear anything," the nurse said, her tone hardening.

"Fine, jeez!" Beatrix threw her arms across her chest, scowling.

The double doors swung shut behind the nurse. Beatrix started pacing again, her blood pressure ticking up with every step.

Minutes crawled by. Still no sign of the nurse. Beatrix finally snapped.

"She’s totally playing us. Not a single update!"

"Babe, relax," Walter muttered, trying to calm her down. "They said it's a brutal procedure. It takes time."

"Duh, I get that," Beatrix hissed. "But what if she's dragging her feet on purpose? Just to make us sweat out here!" She glared at Walter, her eyes icy. "Elara has always hated us. You know that."

If they weren't totally backed into a corner, she’d never have let that girl touch Edgar. Just picturing her golden boy under Elara’s scalpel made her physically sick.

Walter shifted, his own doubts starting to creep in.

They stood there, tense, waiting for the other shoe to drop. Then, the doors popped open. Elara walked out, looking totally wiped but completely composed.

Beatrix lunged. "Elara! What in the actual hell took so long? Why did that take forever?"

"Did you mess up? Where is he? Tell me you didn't kill him!"

She threw a wild slap at Elara's face. Elara sidestepped it like a pro, snatching her mother's wrist mid-swing.

Another doc rushed out right behind her. "Hey! The surgery was a total home run. He’ll be out in a sec. Ma'am, seriously, chill out!"

Beatrix ripped her arm free, giving Elara a massive side-eye.

"For real? He’s actually gonna walk?" she pressed, her eyes squinting hard.

Elara kept her voice deadpan. "He’s got rehab ahead, but yeah. We fixed him."

Beatrix just glared, not buying it for a second.
