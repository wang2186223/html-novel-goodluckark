Chapter 649

"So, I'm hanging out here for a bit. Julian too," Elara said, her eyes scanning her brothers across the couch. "No issues with him crashing, right?" She tossed them a hopeful look. "Seriously, he's dying to bond with you guys. I figured, hey, why not? Good vibes all around."

Rhys, Keaton, and Finnian traded quick, knowing looks. Honestly? They had questions. A lot of them. And maybe a healthy dose of suspicion. Back when Callie was with Ethan, they’d totally checked out. They figured, *whatever, Callie’s got this*, especially since she always defended the guy. Nobody gave Ethan the third degree. Big mistake. Huge. That trainwreck of a wedding still made their blood boil. Not this time. Julian was getting the full background check. They needed to know if this guy was the real deal or just another jerk. If he flopped, Elara was going to hear about it. Fast.

"Duh, of course," Rhys shot back, cracking a warm smile. "Bring him in. One big happy family under one roof—best way to break the ice."

Keaton nodded along. "Yeah, totally. Let's see what he's got."

Finnian leaned in, a smirk playing on his lips but his eyes dead serious. "Hey, Callie... you cool if we, like, put him through a little trial by fire?" He wanted to prep her. No need for her to blow a fuse if they started grilling her new guy.

"Go for it," Elara shrugged, waving a hand. "Julian literally told me to tell you guys to bring it on." She wasn't sweating it. They wouldn't actually murder him.

"So, what's the move for dinner?" Rhys asked. "Is he eating with us tonight?"

"Actually, no. We're doing dinner at the old Baker place tonight," Elara said. "But tomorrow? Christmas Eve? We're all here. Huge family dinner."

Keaton shrugged, totally fine with it. "Cool. Just the boys tonight then." No complaints there. They knew she was making time for them, and with her staying over, they'd get plenty of quality time anyway. Plus, the Bakers were family now. She had to play nice with the in-laws.

After some more trash talk, they headed out for a massive grocery run. Bad idea. Walking around with three ridiculously hot guys—especially Finnian, who was literally a household name—was a circus. Even with a cap pulled low and a face mask on, people clocked him instantly. Necks were snapping left and right. Phones slipped out. Flashbulbs went off. To the average shopper, they looked like a walking Hollywood cast.

Creeped out by the staring, they speed-ran the aisles. They grabbed steaks, chips, and a mountain of party food, then bolted straight for the exit.

Right on cue, Julian rolled up to grab Elara for the big pre-Christmas dinner at the Bakers'. He walked in, shook hands with Rhys and the guys, kept it super polite, and then whisked Elara away. The guy had charm, respect, and zero of Ethan's baggage. He was already winning. For now, the brothers gave him a pass.

Soon, Elara and Julian pulled up to the massive colonial Baker estate. Alaric was already on the porch, waiting. The rest of the loud, chaotic Baker clan was rolling in—minus Ethan's side of the family, thank god. Agnes spotted Elara instantly and rushed over, throwing her arms around her.

"Callie! Oh my god, finally! I was literally dying of boredom," she squealed. "Wait, okay, that face cream you gave me? Holy crap, it's a miracle in a jar. Did you actually whip that up yourself? Where do I buy a lifetime supply?"
