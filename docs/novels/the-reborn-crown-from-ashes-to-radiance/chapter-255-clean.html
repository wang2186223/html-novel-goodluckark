Chapter 255

Elara stared at her phone screen. No panic, no racing heart. Just a flat, dry look. She knew the face staring back at her in the photo.

High society in Capitalton was a small, gossipy world, and this girl was a regular fixture. Word on the street was she was Julian’s side-piece—or at least, he paid her bills.

But who sent the text? And why? A street address popped up right under the image. Obviously, someone wanted her to play detective and catch them red-handed.

God, what a cliché. Elara wasn't about to waste her time on some cheap drama. Besides, Julian wouldn't touch her. Sure, the girl had brains and hustle, but she wasn't his type. If Julian wanted her, he would’ve locked her down years ago.

She swiped the notification away. What kind of loser had time for these petty mind games? Now, if it were the girl Julian actually gave a damn about, maybe she’d pay attention. But this one? Total snooze fest. Elara locked her phone and looked back up at her team.

"Look, we stick to the plan on this project," Elara said, tapping the desk. "Do exactly what we laid out. I’ll handle the casting calls myself when the time comes."

"Got it. I'll loop the director in," her assistant replied, packing up. "That's all I need, Ms. Jewell. I'm gonna head out."

"Sure. See ya," Elara said with a quick nod.

Meeting over, Elara grabbed her keys and drove to the hospital. She’d stopped to grab a bag of Asher’s favorite junk food and a couple of action figures. By the time she walked into the room, Upton and the guys had already signed the discharge papers.

"Mommy! We're going!" Asher ran over, his little face falling a bit. He wrapped his arms around her waist, looking up with big, hopeful eyes. "When are you coming to Sea City? You gotta visit. We can show you all the cool spots!"

"Probably around Christmas, kiddo," Elara said, messing up his hair. "If my schedule clears up, I'm there. Oh, almost forgot—this is for you." She handed over the bag.

"Whoa! Awesome! Thanks, Mommy!" Asher snatched the toys, his face lighting up.

"Save the unwrapping for the car, okay? And hey, if you feel sick, you tell someone right away. Take your meds on time, deal?"

"Yes, ma'am!" Asher snapped a goofy salute.

Elara laughed, then looked up at Upton. "Keep me posted. Any issues, call me. I'll fly out if I need to."

"Will do," Upton said, shoving his hands in his pockets. "Julian's a no-show?"

"Yeah. Tied up with some work stuff," Elara said, keeping her voice flat.

Upton shrugged. "Alright, we gotta hit the road. Watch your back. Seriously, if anyone gives you crap here, just say the word. We've got people in Sea City who'll drive down to Capitalton in a heartbeat to handle it."

"I know. Thanks." Elara walked them to the lobby and watched their car pull out of the driveway. Once the taillights disappeared, she spun around.

"They're gone," Elara called out, her eyes locking onto the dark corner of the hallway. "So you can stop hiding. You're not getting any answers out of them anyway."

Beatrix and Lysander stepped out of the shadows, scowling.

"Cut the crap, Elara," Beatrix spat, her eyes narrowing. "You know where the Miracle Doctor is. Don't play dumb." Beatrix was losing her mind. She knew the kid was fully recovered, but her spies hadn't seen a single doctor go in or out. She'd even grilled the hospital staff, and they all acted like they didn't know jack.

"Oh, I know exactly where he is," Elara said, crossing her arms over her chest. "Actually, I already told you guys. You just didn't believe me."

"She told us?" Beatrix whipped her head toward Lysander. "When?"

Lysander scowled, racking his brain. "She didn't," he muttered, certain she was bluffing. "She never said a damn thing."
