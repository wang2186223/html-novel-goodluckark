Chapter 226

Asher spun toward Upton. "Look, Dad, we’re clean out of options here. My clock is ticking anyway. Why not roll the dice? Go big or go home, right?

But hey, let's get one thing straight. If I don't make it, no crying, and zero blaming Mom. This is on me. She’s gonna bust her butt, but if I kick the bucket, that’s just the hand I was dealt. This whole procedure is a massive gamble for her, too. The pressure is insane, and she’s got serious guts for even trying."

Upton gave a slow nod. "Got it, bud."

"Sweet! Mom, Dad’s on board. Go do your magic," Asher beamed, flashing a massive grin.

Elara nodded. "Okay, I'll prep the gear over the next couple of days. Shout if you guys think of anything else."

"You're the absolute best in the game," Upton said, staring at her with total faith. "I mean, you were Dr. Maxwell’s sole protégé."

"Hop on the bed, Asher. Let's get some needles in you to steady your vitals while I'm here."

"On it!" Asher scrambled onto the mattress, flopping down instantly.

Elara prepped the needles. Upton stood close, tracking her every move.

Asher stared up at her, eyes shining. "Man, I really dig you."

Elara let out a soft laugh. "I dig you too, kiddo."

"Hey, Callie, why don't you just marry Dad? Then you'd be my actual mom! Seriously, if I were older, I'd put a ring on you myself!"

He puffed his cheeks, dead serious. If he could just hit a growth spurt, he'd shield Callie from the whole world.

"Cute, but sorry, bud—I'm already hitched."

Elara smirked. Asher bolted upright in pure shock, but she caught him, pressing him gently back onto the pillows.

"Whoa, hold up! Married?! Since when? How did I miss this? Where's my cake?!"

"My bad. Guess it slipped my mind."

She shot Upton a quick look. Back when she practically rose from the dead, her brain was fried working with Sitas. Telling them about her marriage had been the last thing on her radar. Plus, Upton was overseas chasing deals back then. Even if she’d sent an invite, he couldn't have made the trip anyway. Upton missed the ceremony, but the grapevine had already clued him in. He didn't even blink.

"Is Julian treating you right?" Upton asked. "If he ever steps out of line, remember—Asher and I will handle him."

Elara smiled. "Nah, he's great. No issues."

"Fine. But if things go south, you call us."

She nodded, a warm feeling hitting her chest.

Thirty minutes later, she pulled the final needle. Asher bounced up like a coiled spring, practically vibrating.

"Whoa! I feel like I could tackle a bull!" he yelled.

"Whoa, killer, slow down," Elara warned, packing her kit. "You still need to chill. And stick to the strict diet, got it?"

She rattled off a few more rules, then headed for the exit. Upton and Asher trailed right behind her.

Stepping into the hallway, she saw Edgar and his crew still loitering. Their eyes locked onto Upton instantly.

"Don't bother walking me out, guys. I've got this. Asher, see you tomorrow for a checkup."

"Catch ya later, Mom!" Asher waved like crazy, then paused, nudging Upton.

"Yo, Dad, didn't anyone teach you to walk a lady to her car?"
