Chapter 793

"Are you for real right now?" Keaton's eyes locked onto her leg, his brow furrowing. "Rowan, you're literally dripping blood. You think you're just gonna stroll out of here?"

She opened her mouth to argue, but he didn't give her the chance. He just swept her off her feet, cradling her like a bride. No hesitation, totally casual.

The guy had serious muscle. He hauled her weight like it was nothing, clutching a bucket in his free hand as he marched toward the riverbank.

A few yards away, Rhys and Julian were shooting the breeze. Julian's head snapped up first, his eyebrows hitting his hairline.

"Damn, look at them go," Julian muttered, elbowing Rhys in the ribs. "They're already getting super cozy, huh?"

"Man, you're losing this game. He's literally stealing your girl right in front of your face."

Rhys spun around. His eyes locked onto Rowan, who was tucked tight against Keaton's chest, looking way too relaxed.

Instantly, blood rushed to Rhys's face, turning it bright red.

He stomped over, steam practically coming out of his ears. "Yo! What the hell is this?"

"You guys just met and you're already all over each other? Rowan, seriously? Am I a ghost or something? Like, if you're hooking up, a heads-up would've been nice!"

He shot Keaton a death glare, grabbed Rowan's arm, and yanked her toward him. He used way too much force.

Rowan lost her footing. She crashed straight onto the dirt, a sharp gasp escaping her lips.

She tried to push herself up, but a white-hot flash of pain shot up from her bad foot. She hissed, gritting her teeth.

"Rowan!" Keaton lunged forward, panic in his eyes. He gently propped her up.

Rhys froze. Something was off. He blinked, looking from Rowan to Keaton, his anger faltering.

"Dude, seriously?" Keaton snapped, totally fed up. "Her foot is busted. She's bleeding out. I was trying to get her to the car to look at it."

The color drained from Rhys's face. His rage evaporated, replaced by instant shame and a sudden spike of panic.

"Wait, she's bleeding? What happened?" He reached out, desperate to help, but Rowan shook her head. She dragged herself back, limping away from his touch.

"I've got it," she muttered, her voice ice-cold.

Rhys looked totally crushed. "Rowan, look, I'm sorry. I didn't know. I just... I thought you guys were..." He mumbled, rubbing the back of his neck.

She didn't even look at him. Instead, she reached up and let Keaton pull her to her feet.

Right then, Elara and the rest of the crew jogged over, drawn by the drama.

"Hold up, let me look," Elara said, pushing through. She guided Rowan onto a rock to check the damage.

"Okay, it's not a total disaster," Elara muttered after a quick squint at the cut. "Probably just a jagged rock or a shell. But we need to wash this. My first aid kit is back at the cabin."

"Yeah, let's move," the others chimed in.

Elara glanced at the heavy tension vibrating between Keaton and Rhys. "Rhys, stop standing there. Help get her into the car, will you?"

Rhys didn't waste a second. He stepped in, carefully hoisting Rowan into the back seat.

The group scrambled to grab their gear. Elara, Blair, and Rowan piled into the SUV and sped off.

Luckily, the cabin was just down the road. The second they parked, Elara sprang into action. She grabbed her kit, scrubbed the cut, slapped on some antiseptic ointment, and jabbed her with a quick shot just to be safe.

"All done," Elara said, giving her a quick pat on the shoulder. "You're good to go."
