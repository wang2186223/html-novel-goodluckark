Chapter 986

Rowan was a total wreck, which meant Rhys was running on empty too.

We hit the house, scrubbed the grime off in the shower, threw on some fresh threads, and hauled ass straight back to the clinic.

Rowan hadn't blinked an eye. Blair and Milo had already set up camp by her bed. Milo was supposed to be on a later flight, but the second he heard about Rowan, he booked the fastest bird home.

When Elara and Julian slipped into the room, Blair and Milo were whispering to the quiet girl in the bed.

Rhys sat slumped in a chair right next to her. The guy looked like absolute hell. Unshaved jaw, ratty hair, wearing the same clothes for God knows how long. He wasn't the slick, put-together suit Julian usually dealt with. Julian took one look at him and winced.

"Callie! Thank God!" Blair jumped up, her voice cracking with pure relief. "Are you okay, babe?"

"I'm fine," Elara said, forcing a quick, reassuring smile. "Seriously, don't sweat it. Any word on Rowan?"

Blair let out a heavy, exhausted sigh and shook her head. "Zip. We've been talking to her for hours, begging for a twitch, a sigh, anything. Nothing." Her eyes welled up, voice shaking. "Callie, what the hell do we do?" The thought of Rowan staying under forever was killing her.

"She's gonna wake up," Elara said, her eyes locked on Rowan, hard as flint. "Rowan is the toughest chick I know. This isn't the end of her. She's fighting back."

"Yeah. Yeah, you're right," Blair nodded fast, desperate to believe it. "If anyone can beat the odds, it's her."

"She's just stuck in her own head right now. She'll snap out of it soon," Elara said, keeping her voice steady and warm.

"Rowan, we're all right here waiting. You gotta wake up, okay? Please," Blair whispered to the pale face on the pillow.

"Yeah, Rowan. Don't leave us hanging," Milo muttered.

Julian jerked his chin toward the door, signaling Rhys. They stepped out into the hallway and headed for the smoking deck. Julian slid a cigarette over. Rhys snatched it, sparked it up, and took a massive drag, trying to blow his panic out with the smoke.

"How did things get this messy?" Julian asked, watching him closely. "Is she physically okay? Just... won't open her eyes?"

"Callie says the medics did their job. The body's fine," Rhys said, his voice dead. "But if she's still out, maybe she's just... done. Maybe she doesn't want to come back to this."

"So what's the play?" Julian pressed. "What if she never opens her eyes?"

The question hung there, heavy and cold. Rhys had checked out of reality, doing nothing but staring at Rowan while his whole life went down the drain. His guys had been blowing up Julian's phone, begging him to step in and run the show because Rhys was totally MIA, letting the business rot.

Julian got it, though. He'd been in that exact dark place. When the girl you love is slipping away, the rest of the world can burn. You throw everything in the trash just to hold her hand. That kind of desperation eats you alive. You can't fight it.

"I don't know," Rhys finally muttered after a long, suffocating silence. "I just want to be near her. I screwed up so bad. I should've treated her right when I had the chance."

He stared at the floor, his voice dropping to a raw whisper. "I should've said yes the very first time she told me she liked me. Now she won't wake up, and it's because of the hell I put her through. She probably hates the sight of me."

He let out a dry, pathetic laugh, his face twisting. "It's all on me. Every single bit of it."
