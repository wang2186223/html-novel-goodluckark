Chapter 860

Cake was gone, small talk done. They downed their coffees and hit the mall for a quick walk. Elara sure as hell didn't expect to run straight into Coraline and Beatrix.

The duo was on a shopping spree. Beatrix was hovering over Coraline, basically playing personal stylist with the jewelry.

Blair Sutton spotted them first, and her face instantly dropped. "Ugh, what are those two doing here? Strutting around like they own the place—some sucker must've bailed them out, because they're suddenly loaded again."

Elara didn't even get a chance to answer Blair before Beatrix and Coraline locked eyes with them. Coraline flashed Elara a nasty, smug little grin.

"Well, look who it is. Elara. What a freaking coincidence. Didn't expect to see us, did you?"

"Did you seriously think that after you ditched the Jewell family and caused all that drama, we'd just crash and burn?"

"Yeah, sorry to burst your bubble, but ever since you—our resident jinx—finally packed your bags, the Jewell family has been absolutely killing it."

"Honestly, our lives got way better the second you walked out. Right, Mom?" Coraline was practically purring, her voice dripping with pure attitude.

Blair's mouth twitched in annoyance. "Who's funding you guys anyway?"

Coraline rolled her eyes and scoffed. "Like I'd tell you. What, so you can go ruin it? Please. You two aren't even on our radar."

Beatrix threw Elara a look of pure disgust, then turned back to Coraline, her voice instantly softening into total mom-mode.

"Sweetie, look at this bracelet. It is so you. Let me get it for you, baby."

"Don't waste your energy on toxic trash like her. She's nothing but bad vibes. You're my lucky charm, and I'm going to spoil you rotten. We're going to be just fine, okay?"

Beatrix completely iced Elara out, pouring all her attention into Coraline's shopping. Coraline leaned right into it, hamming it up to show off. "Oh my god, Mom, I love this one. And this one! Can I try them all on?"

"Duh, of course, sweetie. Whatever you want, it's yours!" Beatrix beamed, waving over the sales clerk to get the jewelry out.

Coraline spun around, throwing Elara a nasty look. "Jealous much, Elara? Sucks to not have a mom who actually gives a crap about you, huh? That's what you get for treating the Jewells like garbage—no wonder Mom can't stand you."

"Actually, I heard you don't even know who your real mom is. She probably dumped you, right? God, that's pathetic."

"Imagine your own mother throwing you away. You can't even track her down." She grabbed Beatrix's arm, clinging tight. "But me? I have a mom. And she actually loves me."

Elara just stared at her, totally unfazed. "If you want to call a piece of trash 'Mom,' go right ahead. Garbage belongs with garbage. I'll pass."

Blair let out a sharp laugh. "Seriously, Coraline? Aren't you a little too old to be clinging to Mommy's skirt?"

"A whole grown woman running around chanting 'my mommy loves me, my mommy loves me'—can you even function on your own?"

"And Beatrix—she might be totally blind, but you, Coraline, are just cold-hearted. Honestly, you two deserve each other."

"Oh, and by the way, is your mom only buying you this cheap mall jewelry? There's a yacht auction in a couple of days. Think you can convince her to buy you something there?"

"These cheap little bracelets? Please. I could buy Callie ten of those right now if she wanted."

"To be honest, Callie could buy out this entire store if she felt like it."

Blair turned to Beatrix, flashing a fake, sweet smile. "You must be so proud to have a daughter like Coraline, huh? A grown-ass woman still leeching off you—what a catch. If you'd raised a better kid, you'd be retired and living the high life by now. But hey, some people just love being a doormat!"
