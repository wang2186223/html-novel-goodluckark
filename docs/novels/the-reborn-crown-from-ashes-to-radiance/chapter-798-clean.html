Chapter 798

Elara perched on the mattress, her eyes locked on Rowan. "Come on, he went ballistic when Keaton flirted with you. Like, total green-eyed monster. And he’s still playing dumb about his feelings?"

Rowan stared down, her voice barely a whisper. "Sure, he lost his temper. But he’s shut me down so many times already. The guy just isn't into me."

"Bull," Blair scoffed, throwing her hands up. "He’s practically drooling over you. It’s so freaking obvious. That wasn't even his first jealous fit!"

Rowan let out a dry laugh. "Please. I'm just his intern to him. Nothing more."

She forced a grin, but it looked totally fake. "Whatever. I'm done stressing. No more expectations. He’s my boss, my mentor... I need to get over this crush. Besides, rich guys like him always end up in some corporate arranged marriage anyway."

She took a deep, shaky breath. "I told him I'm dropping it. No more feelings, no more drama. If he doesn't give a crap, I'm out."

She was going to fake it till she made it, even if her chest ached.

Blair and Elara traded a look, then lunged forward, sandwiching her in a tight hug.

"Hey, screw him. You've got us, Rowan."

"Exactly," Blair added. "If Rhys wants to be blind to what's right in front of him, fine. Plenty of hot, decent guys will actually appreciate you."

Rowan managed a weak, thankful smile.

But deep down, she was just exhausted. Exhausted from chasing a fantasy. That was it. She was done with Rhys. No more daydreaming, no more wishing on stars.

Rhys stayed scarce over the next few days, which actually let the rest of them relax and have some real fun.

Day by day, the weight on Rowan's shoulders started to lift.

Then vacation ended. It was back to Capitalton, back to the grind, everyone slipping right back into their daily routines.

Elara hadn't even unpacked her bags before Rex's text flashed on her screen: Declan was slipping away. Fast.

She’d pumped him full of every experimental drug she could find, desperate to buy him some time, praying for a miracle. But his body was giving out. There was nothing left to try.

When she called, Rex’s voice was dead quiet. He sounded like a total stranger.

But she knew the act. He was breaking inside and needed backup. They’d dragged out their stay in Capitalton for months just because Declan wanted to take his last breaths here and be put in the ground here.

The news knocked the wind out of her. She’d literally just walked into the lobby of Jewell Group, ready to sign off her final paperwork and quit for good. Her hand was hovering over the elevator button when her phone buzzed with Rex's call.

Screw the board meeting.

She spun on her heel to leave, but Coraline and a pack of board suits marched straight through the entrance.

Coraline cut her off, planting herself right in Elara's path. "Going somewhere, Elara?" she sneered, her voice like ice. "Running away?"

"Move," Elara snapped. "I have an actual emergency."

"Oh, really? You call a high-stakes board meeting and then just bail? What’s the matter? Scared because we finally figured out your little secret, and now you're trying to skip town?"

Coraline squinted, her eyes dripping pure venom.

"Elara! You and I need to talk right now!" Beatrix’s voice boomed down the corridor as she marched in, flanked by another wave of board members.
