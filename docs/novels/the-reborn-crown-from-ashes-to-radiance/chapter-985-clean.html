Julian swore to Elara he’d keep his hands off Phantom. "Look, I got guys for this," he told her, shutting down her panic. "They’ll handle his mess. Just chill. I’ll ping you the second I hear anything."

She bought it. That was just Julian—the guy never asked for permission to do anything, so why bother lying to her now?

They packed their gear and hit the road, burning rubber straight toward Capitalton.

Victor tagged along in the backseat, trading jabs with Julian the whole drive. It was all trash talk, though. Julian had pulled his ass out of the fire, so they kept the claws pulled back.

The second they hit the city limits, Victor bailed. "Got errands to run. Catch you later," he said, leaning close to Elara. "Hey, my offer’s still on the table. You ever realize you deserve an upgrade, you know where to find me."

Julian rolled his eyes so hard it hurt. "Uh, hello? Standing right here, man. You seriously trying to hit on my wife in front of my face?"

"Hey, divorces happen," Victor shrugged, totally unfazed. "Callie, babe, don't forget me. I'm coming back for you!"

"She’s not waiting, and she’s sure as hell not interested. Jesus, does everything have to be a dick-measuring contest with you?" Julian snapped.

Victor’s smirk vanished. He actually looked dead serious for once. "You don't get it. This isn't some game to piss you off. I actually give a shit about her. If she ever dumps your ass, I'm waiting. So you better treat her like gold, Julian. No lies. No treating her like she's disposable. There’s a long line of guys who’d kill to have her. Real catches. Like me."

"Right, so this was just a massive ego trip for you," Julian muttered, his brows knitting together. "Relax. I'm not lying to her, and you're never getting your hands on her." He yanked Elara against his side, locking his arm tight around her shoulders. His stare was pure steel. "She stays with me. Period. I’m keeping her safe, keeping her happy, and nobody is taking her away. Ever."

"Is that a promise?" Victor stared back, his eyes drilling into Julian. There was a weird, creepy vibe in his look—something Julian couldn't quite put his finger on, but it gave him the chills.

"Damn right," Julian said, not flinching.

"Then don't screw up. Don't make her cry, and don't give her a reason to hate you," Victor said, his voice ice-cold. He jerked his chin at Barnaby, and they turned and vanished down the street.

Julian guided Elara to the passenger side. Once they were buckled in, he dialed down his voice. "Hey. Did something happen while I was away? Did Victor pull some crap with you?"

"No, nothing." Elara shook her head, forcing a quick smile that didn't even touch her eyes.

"Is this about Rowan Grey?" Julian asked softly. "Look, I'm sorry. I had no clue things got this bad, or that you even went back there."

"I... yeah, stuff happened. I couldn't reach you." She waved off his apology with another shake of her head. "Forget it, it's fine. We can deal with our stuff later. Right now, I just need to get home, take a hot shower, change, and get over to the hospital to check on Rowan."

"Okay, let's do that," Julian said. He didn't quite catch the weird vibe underneath her words, but going to the hospital made sense anyway. He needed to check in on Darian Blackwood, too.
