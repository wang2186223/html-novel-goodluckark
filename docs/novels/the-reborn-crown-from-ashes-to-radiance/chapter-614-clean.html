Chapter 614

Callie was a saint. Seriously. Yet her own flesh and blood treated her like some amateur, constantly doubting her skills. How did a bunch of losers like them even produce a genius like her? It made zero sense.

"Don't like our OR? Fine. Pack up your half-dead kid and hit the bricks!" The chief of surgery barked, his neck turning a bright, angry crimson.

"Callie, babe, getting stuck with a family like this? That's just a straight-up curse."

He threw Walter and the rest of the crew a look that could kill. He was totally done with their crap.

Lysander jumped in, hands up, trying to play peacemaker. "Whoa, Chief, hold on. My mom didn't mean anything by it. She's just stressed about Edgar, you know? Please, don't take it the wrong way. We're super grateful you let us use the room. Edgar's life is in your hands, and, seriously, we appreciate it."

The chief just rolled his eyes. Hard.

"Yeah, whatever. Just don't come crying to me and blaming my staff when things go sideways! Honestly, if it weren't for Callie, I would've tossed you guys out on your butts hours ago." He snorted, spun on his heel, and stormed off. The hallway went dead silent. Awkward as hell.

Walter whipped around to Beatrix, his voice dripping with irritation. "That was the head of the freaking hospital! Did you really have to trash-talk him?"

Beatrix bristled, her jaw tightening. "Oh, so now I can't even worry about my own flesh and blood? What if she screws up in there?"

She glared at the double doors of the OR. "Who even knows if Elara has a clue what she's doing? Watch her try her best and still totally botch the whole thing."

Walter shifted, suddenly looking pretty nervous himself. "Come on. It can't be that bad, right?"

"Says who?" Beatrix snapped, her fingers twisting together like crazy.

"Guys, stop. Don't talk like that. Edgar's gonna make it," Lysander said, trying to keep the peace. "Let's just sit tight and wait."

Walter tried to find a comfortable spot on the cheap plastic waiting room chair. "Yeah. We wait. God knows how long this is gonna take..."

Coraline, slouching in the corner, chimed in. "Hey, Callie's the miracle worker, remember? I'm sure she's got this totally under control."

Nobody answered. They just sat there, stiff as boards, waiting for the doors to swing open. The silence was deafening, and the tension in the room just kept ramping up.

Hours dragged by. Still, Edgar was under the knife.

Beatrix finally snapped. She couldn't sit still for another second. She lunged at a young nurse walking past, grabbing her by the arm.

"Hey! What's the deal in there? Why is this taking forever? My son's been in surgery for hours!"

The nurse gave her a tired, patient smile. "Ma'am, this is a really intense procedure. We don't have an exact clock on it. But look, trust me. Elara is top-tier. If anyone can pull this off, it's her."

"If she's so damn good, why is it taking a lifetime?" Beatrix hissed. "What if she messed up?"

She jabbed a finger toward the OR doors. "Go get Elara out here. Tell her I need to talk to her. Right now!"

The nurse stared at her, totally dumbfounded. "Ma'am, Elara is literally mid-surgery. She can't just walk out for a chat. She's been on her feet for hours, working her butt off. Please, just hang in there. Once they're done, they'll wheel him out and you can see him."

"And when the hell is that?" Beatrix huffed. "Are you sure she hasn't already ruined everything? It's been way too long!"

"If anything goes wrong, you'll be the first to know," the nurse said, her voice hardening. "But right now, you need to trust your doctor."
