NovelVibe

Chapter 273

The party was going wild. Phones were up everywhere—half the crowd was shooting video, the other half was already live-streaming. Once word got out about the alligator pit, the viewer count started exploding.

Ethan’s phone wouldn't stop buzzing. His friends were spamming his tags. That’s how he found out Elara was actually at Tempest’s psycho dinner party, trying to bail out Julian while Tempest dragged her through the mud.

[Yo Ethan, your ex is a straight-up savage!]

[Bro, you never said she was this wild. No offense, but she blows Seraphina Vale out of the water.]

[Dude, get on the stream right now! It’s insane. She’s literally about to jump in with the gators!]

[Damn, Elara has zero fear. How did you let a girl like that slip away?]

[This is nuts! She’s a total badass.]

Ethan swiped through the endless notifications clogging his feed. Everyone was losing their minds over Elara. His brain couldn't even process it.

He tapped on the clips. There was Elara, slamming back shots like a seasoned bartender. Then she was shredding on a banjo, keeping the whole room dead silent and hooked. Next clip: she’s cleaning house at the poker table, shoving stacks of cash into a cheap plastic bag and tossing it to Rhys like it was trash.

Ethan left his friends on read. He just stared at the screen, his jaw dropping lower with every video. They had dated for years. Years. And he had absolutely no clue she could do any of this.

The Jewells always talked about her like she was some useless nobody. So, yeah, he’d bought into it. He figured she was just basic.

But this? This was next-level. The girl was a freaking onion, just peeling back layer after layer of hidden talents.

His head was spinning. Watching her on screen was like watching a star explode into life.

He connected the dots. She’d walked straight into that death trap of a party just to pull Julian out of Tempest’s clutches. The comments section was already calling it true love. *She’s literally jumping into a pit of gators for him,* they wrote.

Reading those words made Ethan’s stomach twist into a hard, tight knot. He couldn't believe what he was seeing.

Was she seriously not terrified of getting ripped apart? Or did she actually care about Julian enough to die for him? Did she love him that much? What about all those times she’d whispered in his ear that *he*, Ethan, was her one and only?

Scowling, Ethan tapped the live link. There she was. She was wearing some guy's oversized tux jacket, standing right on the ledge of the alligator pit.

His face went pale, then dark. She was actually gearing up to jump. Was she insane? Did she not realize those things would tear her to shreds?

Ethan wasn't the only one watching. The three Jewell brothers and Coraline had also been spammed with the links.

They were all hanging out at home when Lysander dragged Cassian into Edgar’s room. The three of them huddled around a screen, watching Elara chug liquor and shred the banjo. When the clip ended, they just stared at each other, totally speechless.

Their DMs were blowing up with people mocking them. *Did you seriously not know your own sister was this badass?* *Why did you guys always call her trash?* *If she’s useless, what does that make you losers?*

Elara’s new fan club was coming for Edgar and his brothers, hard.

"Wait, since when does she drink like a sailor?" Cassian muttered, scratching his head.

"And the banjo?" Lysander’s brow furrowed, his mind completely blown by how good she was. "Where the hell did she learn that?"

It wasn't just some amateur strumming, either. The clip was going viral. Some legendary, world-famous musician had already reposted it, calling her a genius.
