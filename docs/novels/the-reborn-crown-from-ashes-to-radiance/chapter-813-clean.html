Chapter 813

"Look, Mr. Myers, seriously, don't get the wrong idea here. Some rookie probably just screwed up. No way in hell your wife did any of this!"

Chief Miller jogged to keep pace with Julian's long strides down the corridor. He let out a tight, shaky laugh, sweating through his collar. The guy was drowning and he knew it.

Elara alone was a headache. Add Julian? That was a straight-up death sentence. What kind of rotten luck did his precinct have to piss off the absolute worst duo in the city? And honestly, Elara ripping off the Jewells? Give me a break. If she wanted to pull a scam, she’d target some tech billionaire or a Wall Street big shot, not those losers. The Jewells' entire net worth wouldn't even cover the tax on Elara's pocket change.

Miller’s temples throbbed, but he had to play nice. He plastered on a fake, desperate grin, basically begging for mercy.

"My bad, man. Seriously, it's just a massive mix-up. I'm on her side, totally! The Jewells are just stirring up drama. Your wife is clean, I know it!" He kept the grin glued to his face as they pushed through the double doors into the main bullpen.

Miller snapped his fingers at a passing intern. "Hey, get Mr. Myers some coffee. Now." He turned back to Julian, hands raised. "Take a load off, man. Sit down, grab a cup. I'll sort this mess out. Just chill right here, okay?"

"Where's my wife?" Julian's voice cut through the room like a blade.

"She's, uh, back there..." Miller snagged a passing deputy by the arm, whispering fiercely, "Where is she?"

The deputy swallowed hard. "Room two. Blake's in there with her now."

"Blake? The idiot actually locked her in there?" Miller winced, turning another weak, pathetic smile toward Julian.

"Show me," Julian said, his tone deadpan and lethal. "Or I'll go door to door."

"Right, yeah, this way! Follow me!" Miller's throat went dry. Panic hit him like a truck. He scrambled ahead, practically sprinting down the hallway to lead the way.

They were still ten feet away when Blake’s loud, cocky voice drifted through the drywall.

"Oh, so we're playing the silent type? Think you're tough? I'll get you to talk, sweetheart. Honestly, I don't care if you're guilty or not. If I say you did it, you did it. You think I can't just manufacture some proof?"

*BOOM.*

Julian’s boot slammed the door off its latch, sending it crashing against the wall. Blake and Elara both flinched, heads snapping toward the entrance.

Elara expected the chief, but seeing Julian standing there made her brows shoot up. She’d only buzzed Miller. How the hell did Julian get here so fast?

"Well, look who showed up," she muttered, surprised.

"Heard those Jewell bastards were trying to frame you. No way I was staying home," Julian said. His voice went soft, almost sweet, just for her. But the second his eyes locked onto Blake, the warmth vanished. He looked ready to kill.

"What did you just say? You're gonna fake some evidence? If you say she did it, she did it? Who the hell do you think you are, man? You think you own this town?"

Blake took a step back, his face pale, stammering nonsense as his eyes went wide.

Julian scoffed, then whipped his head toward Miller. "This how you run your precinct? Setting up my wife? Just making up whatever shit you want?"
