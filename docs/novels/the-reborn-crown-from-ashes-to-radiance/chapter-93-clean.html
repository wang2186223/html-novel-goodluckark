Chapter 93

"Marry me."

Ethan dropped the bomb out of nowhere, his eyes locking onto Seraphina Vale.

"Wait, what?" Seraphina blinked, her voice cracking. "Ethan, what did you just say?"

"I said, marry me, Seraphina." His stare burned right through her.

He needed this. He needed Elara to choke on her own choices. If Elara could shove her new, perfect life in his face, he could do the exact same. She had to feel it. She had to regret walking away.

Besides, Elara used to lose her mind over Seraphina. Every single time he threw Seraphina a bone, Elara watched. She burned with jealousy.

"I—" Seraphina’s chest throbbed. Her heart hammered against her ribs. Mrs. Ethan? Finally? She’d dreamed of this forever.

But wait. Why now? Why this sudden rush?

She scanned his face, trying to read the play. Should she play hard to get? Or just scream yes? She couldn't let this slip away.

"I... I’ve always loved you, Ethan," she stammered. "But... what about Callie? You love her."

Ethan snapped. "Don't bring her up! When did I ever say I liked her? Huh? I don't. I'm asking *you*."

Seraphina bit her lip, her voice dropping to a whisper. "I'll do whatever you want, Ethan. Anything."

Ethan stared at her, but his brain glitched. He wasn't seeing Seraphina. He was picturing Elara’s lips—red, soft, tasting like heaven. Suddenly, he lunged. He slammed his mouth onto Seraphina’s, a desperate, messy kiss that wiped out the room.

He lost track of who he was holding. In his head, it was all Elara. Her bright eyes. Her stupid, perfect smile.

Who the hell did she think she was, dumping him for Julian? Throwing herself at that guy just to spite him?

He grabbed Seraphina and hauled her into the bedroom. They crashed onto the thick mattress, sinking into the sheets.

The lights clicked off. In the pitch black, Seraphina wrapped her arms around him, her head spinning with joy.

Was this it? Did he finally love her back? Was her dream actually coming true?

She tried to read his face in the dark, but his eyes were blank, staring right through her.

"Ethan, I love you," she breathed. "Do you have any idea how much?"

"Good," he muttered. "You're mine now."

"I love you so much. I'd give up everything for you. My whole life. You're my only light, Ethan."

"I knew it," Ethan growled, his voice thick. "I knew you'd crawl back. Callie, you're mine. You called me a kid? Said Julian was better?"

He pressed down harder, his hands gripping her tight. "Am I just some three-second joke to you? Am I good enough now? Elara, tell me! Am I strong enough for you?" He mashed his lips against hers, trying to bruise her, trying to wipe her out.

Seraphina’s high crashed instantly. Cold reality hit her. Her face twisted in the dark. Elara. Again. It was always Elara. God, she was sick of it.

"Ethan... stop, you're hurting me!" She shoved at his chest, but he only pressed harder, locking her down.

"Hurting? We're just getting started," he snarled, his grip tightening on her waist. "I'll show you exactly what I'm made of tonight."

He was completely gone. Out of his mind. He was using Seraphina like a punching bag for his rage at Elara.

"Ethan, let me go! Seriously, stop, it hurts!" Tears leaked from her eyes. He was crushing her, suffocating her under his weight.

He wanted to leave a mark. He wanted her to feel a pain she’d never forget.

Then, he froze.

Her screaming finally pierced through the fog in his brain. He snapped back to reality. He reached over and slapped the light switch.

The bright light flooded the room. Ethan gasped, his stomach dropping.

It wasn't Elara tangled in the sheets with him. It was Seraphina.

He sat back, completely paralyzed, like he'd just been struck by lightning.
