Chapter 244

A hand is everything. Boom—Upton snapped Coraline’s wrist. Brutal. Sick. But the Jewells loved their own skin way too much to play hero. This was Upton. One word from him, and their family was history. So they swallowed their pride and aimed all that burning hate straight at Elara.

"Touch Asher again," Upton said, his voice dropping to a cold whisper as he stared Coraline down, "and I’ll make sure you lose a hell of a lot more than a hand."

Coraline gnawed her lip, clutching her mangled wrist. Her face went totally white. The pain was pure torture. Hate burned in her eyes, but she didn't dare look Upton in the face.

"If Callie didn't have a history with you guys, I wouldn't have been this nice," Upton said, his voice freezing the room. "You should be thanking her. Now get the hell out of my sight."

Nobody hesitated. The brothers scooped Coraline up and scrambled down the hall toward the OR.

Outside the surgery doors, Edgar, Cassian, and Lysander stood in a tense, angry huddle. They were absolutely fuming.

"Elara is dead to me," Cassian hissed, his jaw clenched so tight it looked ready to snap. "I swear to God, I'll make her pay for what she did to Coraline."

"She just stood there and watched," Edgar muttered, shaking his head in disbelief. "That’s not the Callie we grew up with. No way."

"She doesn't give a crap about us," Lysander scoffed, dripping with bitterness. "We're just her blood brothers. Why would she care about Coraline? She’s too busy riding Julian's coattails, acting like she's hot stuff."

"She really thinks Julian’s going to protect her forever?" Cassian sneered. "And Upton? He’s heading back to Sea City sooner or later. This is far from over."

Just then, Beatrix stormed down the hallway, her heels clicking like gunfire on the tile. "I just got the call! What the hell happened to Coraline?" she demanded, her eyes wide and panicked as she grabbed her sons.

"Who else? That bitch Elara," Lysander snapped.

"What did she do this time?" Beatrix’s eyes narrowed, sharp as glass.

"She’s the reason Coraline’s wrist is shattered!" Cassian spat, grinding his teeth. "The witch didn't lift a finger. She knew where the Miracle Doctor was. She knew how to save Edgar. But she chose to do absolutely nothing. She could’ve stopped Coraline from getting hurt, but she just stood there and watched. She wanted us to suffer." He leaned in, his voice dropping to a venomous whisper. "Elara is a curse. A disease on this family."

Beatrix’s face twisted with pure rage. "Where is that little brat? I’ll handle her myself."

Lysander grabbed her wrist before she could storm off. "Not now, Mom. She’s in the room with Upton’s kid. Julian is in there, too." He swallowed his anger. "We wait. We’ll get her when they’re not around."

Beatrix let out a sharp breath, shaking all over. "Fine." She turned to the OR doors, her eyes filling with tears. "God, I hope they can fix Coraline’s hand. She’s so talented. Her hands are her entire life."

The three brothers nodded, their faces tight with the same dread.

Meanwhile, Elara had no clue what was going on outside. She was too busy laughing with Asher, the two of them goofing off like carefree kids.

Julian and Upton sat a few feet away, keeping their voices low as they talked business. Every now and then, their eyes drifted over to Elara and the boy.

Two alpha males, totally dominant and in control—yet whenever they looked at the girl and the kid, their hard stares melted into something warm and protective.
