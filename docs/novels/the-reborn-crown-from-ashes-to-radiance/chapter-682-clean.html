Chapter 682

"Shoot me your routing number," Elara said. "I'll front you the first month's cash."

Caleb didn't blink. "You got it."

She left him there and jogged over to Rhys and the rest of the crew.

"Hey, guys! Miss me?" she called out, putting on a bright face.

Four heads snapped toward her, all of them wearing matching frowns.

"Jesus, Callie, you good?" Rhys asked, squinting. "You were locked in the restroom for like, an eternity. Stomach bug or what?"

"No, totally fine!" Elara waved a hand, shaking her head. "Just had to take a quick call. Milo's in some kind of mess, and I gotta go bail him out. Seriously, don't wait up. Head back to the house, and I'll meet you there."

She paused, flashing them her best puppy-dog eyes. "Oh, and do me a favor? Start on dinner. Wash the greens, prep the ingredients. If I make it back fast enough, I'll handle the stove."

Lying came easy to her, and they bought it hook, line, and sinker. But they still hovered.

"Is he alright, though? Need backup?"

"Yeah, if it's heavy lifting, we're in."

"Seriously, Callie. We can tag along. More muscle, you know?" They all stared, waiting for her green light.

Elara shook her head fast. "Nah, it's small-time stuff. Too many cooks will just mess it up. I can handle it, promise. Besides, look at the time. Go home and get chopping."

Julian shrugged, and the guys finally gave in, turning to leave without another word.

Elara spun on her heel and marched back to Caleb. "Okay. Let's roll."

Caleb nodded. "Right behind you," he said, stepping out front.

They climbed into the car. As Caleb pulled out, Elara turned to him. "So, where's Malachi hiding?"

"He's at his usual spot in Capitalton," Caleb said, eyes on the road.

"A house? Since when?" Elara's brow furrowed. Something felt weird. "I thought he was always overseas. Last time I checked, he was crashing at some hotel."

"He remodeled one of his old properties," Caleb explained. "Word is, he's digging his heels in here for a bit."

"So he's nesting. All to wear Julian down, right?" Elara knew exactly what the old man was playing at. There was only one reason he'd stay.

Caleb gave a dry nod. "Bingo."

"Unbelievable. So if Julian doesn't play heir, Malachi just stays forever? What is his deal?" Elara scoffed, her voice sharp with irritation. "He literally threw Julian away back then. Basically ran him out of the city on a rail. Now he's obsessed?"

"Because Julian's a beast now," Caleb said, his tone deadpan. "The boss worships power. He didn't think Julian would amount to much back then. Now? He sees what Julian's built, and he's not letting go. The old man is aging out. His empire needs a boss, and his other kid is a total trainwreck. Julian is the only one with actual teeth. Of course he wants him back."

Elara got the logic, but Julian wasn't going back. No way in hell. Sure, maybe a little boy Julian had craved a father. But now? The guy was practically old enough to have his own kids.

Malachi was living in a fantasy land.
