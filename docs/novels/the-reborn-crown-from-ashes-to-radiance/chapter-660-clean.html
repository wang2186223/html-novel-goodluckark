Chapter 660

"Look, you're Callie's family," Julian shrugged, keeping his voice deadpan. "Whatever floats your boat, I'm down."

Finnian shot him a look, one brow twitching up. "You seriously think Callie's just gonna roll with this?"

"Nope," Julian said, shaking his head. "You guys had the balls to pitch this, so I figure you've already got a game plan to sell it to her. That's on you, anyway. Besides, if I tried to hide behind her right now, you guys would call bullshit in a second."

Damn, the guy was quick. No arguing with that.

The brothers traded looks, giving slow, matching nods. Okay, round one to Julian.

"Hey, quick question," Finnian said. "Mind if I do a reading on you?"

Julian didn't even blink. "Sure. You're her brother. Go nuts, ask whatever."

A smirk tugged at Finnian's mouth. "Sweet. I won't pull any punches then."

"Go for it," Julian said, cool as ice.

Finnian fished a beat-up, frayed deck of tarot cards out of his jacket. "Give me your birthdate."

Julian rattled off the numbers. Finnian started shuffling, his easygoing smirk fading into a hard stare as he flipped the first few cards.

Rhys and Keaton leaned in, eyes locked on the tightening knot between Finnian's eyebrows. A cold knot formed in their guts. Finnian's weird voodoo stuff was always spot-on. The guy never missed. Seeing him look this spooked? Yeah, that was bad news.

Honestly, they wanted him to clear the hurdle. Sure, they had to grill him, but Julian was the only guy who actually deserved Callie. She was crazy about him, and let's be real—nobody else even came close to his level.

"Well?" Rhys blurted out the second Finnian laid down the last card. "What's the deal?"

Keaton and Julian stared, waiting for him to spit it out.

Finnian's face had gone totally white. He locked eyes with Julian, dead silent for a beat, before whispering, "Dude... I don't know how to put this. But the cards... they say you're not... like, alive."

Rhys blinked, totally thrown. "Wait, what? What do you mean, 'not alive'?"

"I don't freaking know," Finnian muttered, rubbing his temples. "This has never happened. It's just... fog. There's no future here. Not for you, not for Callie. Just a massive, empty blank."

It freaked him out. Seriously freaked him out. He'd never pulled a blank reading, especially not when it came to his sister. He wanted her happy, not devastated by some tragedy. But a total blackout? That was unheard of. And Julian was sitting right there, breathing, yet the cards treated him like a ghost?

It gave him the literal chills.

Keaton's brow furrowed. "Hold on. How can a guy sitting right here not be alive?"

Rhys looked just as clueless. "Yeah, Finn, what the hell are you talking about?"

Finnian just shook his head, lost. "I'm telling you, I have zero clue. The connection is dead. I've never run into this before."

He kept his eyes glued to Julian, begging for some kind of explanation.

Julian let out a dry, self-mocking laugh. "Maybe my fate's just glitched? Doesn't mean I'm doomed, right?"

Finnian didn't buy it. "Good or bad, it feels wrong. A reading like this... it usually means your clock is ticking. And if you check out early, Callie's the one who's gonna end up broken."
