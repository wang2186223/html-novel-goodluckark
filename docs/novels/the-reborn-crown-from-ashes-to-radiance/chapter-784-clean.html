Chapter 784

No backup. Coraline saw the writing on the wall and swallowed her pride. "I'm sorry," she choked out. Why make it worse? Words were cheap. A beating, though? That would hurt like hell.

"Let you off?" Sharon laughed, her lips curling. "In your dreams, babe. I’m no saint. You poked the bear, and honey, I don't do free passes. Time for a little attitude adjustment." She leaned in. "And honestly? This isn't even about me. You touched Callie. That's a wrap for you."

Sharon was done talking. She glanced over at Tanner, who was chilling nearby. "Hey, babe. Give me some room, will ya? Let me handle this bitch."

Tanner looked at her like she hung the moon. "You got it, gorgeous. Don't scuff your knuckles, though. If your arm gets tired, just let one of the boys take over."

"Relax, love. I've got this."

"Oh, I know. You're a total badass—literally the toughest chick on the planet." He beamed, practically glowing with pride.

Sharon smirked. "Nah, second toughest. Callie's the real beast."

"True," Tanner agreed, nodding. "Callie's number one, hands down."

Coraline's brain short-circuited. Were these two freaking psycho? What kind of weird-ass conversation was this? What were they about to do to her? Panic clawed at her throat.

She scrambled back, but her heels hit a dead end.

*Crack!*

A riding crop sliced the air and slammed right across Coraline's back. White-hot agony exploded. She shrieked, "Are you out of your mind?!"

She’d thought it was a bluff, a sick scare tactic. But the burning welt on her skin and the tears spilling over her cheeks proved otherwise. Holy shit, it hurt. It hurt so bad.

"Crazy?" Sharon shrugged, totally unfazed. "Maybe a little."

"You hit me! I'll call the cops, I swear to God!" Coraline's voice cracked, shaking violently. "I said I was sorry! What else do you want?!" She cowered, staring up in sheer terror.

"You really don't get it, do you? You don't know who I am, or whose turf you just stepped on," Sharon sneered. "You're nothing to me." She raised the crop again. "Time for some fun. Don't worry, I won't kill you. No broken bones either. But oh, it's gonna sting. You messed with Callie, so don't expect any mercy."

*Whack! Whack! Whack!*

The crop rained down, fast and brutal. Coraline tried to squirm away, her face twisted in a mask of terror, but Sharon's aim was perfect. No escape. Within seconds, Coraline was curled in a fetal position on the floor, sobbing, her entire body throbbing with pain as she glared up with pure hatred.

Around them, the crowd whispered. Everyone knew Sharon was bad news, a straight-up psycho, but nobody dared step in. The girl had too much pull. Word on the street was, if Sharon wanted you gone, you'd disappear, and the cops wouldn't find a single bone.

Coraline knew the rules of the game. Power like that was untouchable. She bit her lip, swallowing the copper taste of pain, and chalked this up as a massive loss. At least with all these eyes on them, Sharon wouldn't actually murder her. She'd pin this whole nightmare on Elara. Oh, she'd make that bitch pay.

A shadow fell over her. Coraline looked up, expecting some savior from the Jewell family. Instead, she stared straight into Elara's face.

"Elara? What the hell? You here to take a swing at me too?"
