Chapter 361

"Look, let's just drop it. Let's eat, okay?"

Walter wanted to throttle Kodiak, but he forced his temper down. He swiped his tongue over his lips, tasting leftover gravy, then snatched a napkin to scrub his face. He grabbed his fork.

Sure, Coraline had a VIP pass to this joint, but the prices were insane. They only came here to show off for Elara. Well, they were here now. Might as well get their money's worth. The four of them gripped their forks, ready to dive in.

Walter aimed his fork straight at a juicy slice of roast beef. He lunged.

*Whoosh.* The plate vanished.

Walter blinked, staring at the empty space. What the hell?

He looked up. Kodiak’s hairy paw was resting on the lazy Susan. The beast gave it a hard spin, bringing the roast right to his own face. One quick swipe, and the meat was on Kodiak's plate.

*Chomp, chomp.* Gone.

Walter sat there, mouth open. Edgar and the others froze, realization hitting them like a slap.

They were starving. They’d skipped lunch because of that massive fight earlier, and there was no way they were letting some damn pet eat their expensive dinner.

Beatrix went in for the kill, reaching for another plate.

But every single time a fork went out, the lazy Susan spun. *Zip.* The food ended up right back under Kodiak’s nose.

The furball inhaled another dish. The speed was freaking terrifying.

Walter snapped. He targeted a rack of ribs, stabbing downward with his fork.

*Smack.*

Kodiak’s paw slapped Walter's hand away. The ribs clattered back onto the platter.

Walter stared, totally dumbfounded.

Kodiak gave the lazy Susan another casual flick, and the ribs slid right back to him.

It became a war. Kodiak kept spinning and grabbing, while Walter and the rest scrambled like maniacs to snag a bite. They got absolutely nothing. They were panting, exhausted, and still starving.

Meanwhile, Kodiak cleared the entire table in seconds.

"Elara, did you bring this monster just to starve us?" Beatrix shrieked, jumping up. "You psycho! You're doing this on purpose! This is why I can't stand you! You're nothing compared to Coraline! I don't care if you're supposedly better—we still hate you!"

In a blind rage, Beatrix grabbed a bowl and hurled it straight at Elara's face.

Elara tilted her head. The bowl buzzed past her ear.

Walter grabbed Beatrix by the arm. "Lysander, get your mother to the bathroom. Now. She's exhausted. She's acting crazy."

Lysander stared at Elara, looking totally lost. But he didn't argue. He grabbed Beatrix and dragged her out.

Elara looked at Walter, completely chill. "I'm good. Thanks for the food."

She stood up, ready to walk.

"Callie, wait! We're not done here. Sit down, let's talk. Come on," Walter pleaded, swallowing his rage. He had to play nice. The business deal depended on it.

Kodiak stood up, stretched his neck, and let out a massive, wet burp right in Walter's face.

Walter stared at the beast, completely broken.

An entire family, ruined by a damn dog.
