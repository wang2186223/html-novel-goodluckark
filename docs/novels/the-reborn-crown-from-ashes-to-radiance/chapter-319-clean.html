Chapter 319

Elara slipped through the heavy doors of the VIP joint. She had zero clue a shadow was glued to her back.

Ethan hung back, blending into the crowd but keeping his eyes locked on her. This place was the real deal—a high-end hotspot sitting right on the prime lakeside real estate downtown. Everyone who was anyone knew about it.

Normally, you needed major pull to even sniff the lobby. If you weren't Capitalton royalty, yeah, good luck getting past the velvet rope.

No way Elara belonged here. Ethan figured she was riding Julian’s coattails—that guy had the kind of juice to make things happen. But nope, she was solo. Julian’s ride had already peeled out.

What was her game? Did she swipe Julian’s black card or something?

Had to be. On her own, she was a nobody. Ethan himself had to bleed cash and beg for favors just to get his own name on the list last month.

He snorted, shaking his head. Man, he was overthinking. Elara walking in here on her own merit? Pure fantasy.

Especially today. They had some ultra-exclusive auction going down inside—rare relics, big money. Security was dialed up to eleven. Without Julian pulling strings, she didn't stand a snowball's chance in hell.

Ethan trailed her, keeping his distance as she neared the heavy oak doors. Then, his jaw hit the floor. The bouncers didn't stop her. They just bowed their heads, totally respectful, and let her glide right past. No ID. No card. Nothing.

Hold up. This was wrong. This place was notorious for its ironclad security. Even the big-shot regulars had to jump through hoops. But Elara? She didn't even miss a beat.

He lunged forward, trying to slip in right behind her, but a heavy arm blocked his chest.

"Whoa there, sir. Can I see your VIP pass?" The suit offered a plastic, professional smile.

"Are you kidding me? You guys know who I am," Ethan snapped, his voice rising.

She just walked in, and he was getting the third degree?

"We know you, Mr. Baker. But rules are rules. Card, please."

"If you know I'm a member, why the hassle?" Ethan hissed. "And what about her? You didn't check her. What, she gets a free pass and I don't?"

The guy let out a soft chuckle. "Mr. Baker, you've got it wrong. That lady isn't a VIP."

"Then how the hell did she just walk in?" Ethan's brain was short-circuiting.

"Well, technically, she owns the place," the guy smirked. "Can't really card the boss, can we?"

"The boss?" Ethan froze. "You're telling me she owns this joint? Come on, man. I know the owner is some shadow figure, but her? A girl? No way."

"She's the boss, Mr. Baker. For real."

"Yeah? What's her name then?" Ethan scoffed, crossing his arms.

"Sorry, sir. That's classified."

Ethan stood there, completely dumbfounded.

Elara running this empire? Bullshit. If she was the big boss here, then he was the freaking King of England.
