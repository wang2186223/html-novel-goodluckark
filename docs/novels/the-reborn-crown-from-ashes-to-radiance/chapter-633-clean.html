Chapter 633

Elara tapped send. She raised her eyes, and boom—there was Cody Vance, walking right her way. Cassian caught her eye the exact same second. His face twisted into a heavy frown. He marched over, hauling a ridiculous mountain of bags behind him, looking totally pissed off.

"Since when are you here? You're picking me up and didn't even text?" Cassian snapped, his voice dripping with irritation. "Ugh, if I knew, I wouldn't have booked an Uber. It's literally on its way right now!" He kicked a massive suitcase toward her feet. "Grab some of these, Elara. Move it." He wasn't asking. It was a straight-up order, the kind that made your blood boil instantly.

Elara glared right back. "Who said I'm here for you? Because I definitely didn't." She had zero clue he was even landing today. Talk about terrible luck.

Cassian snorted, a harsh, mocking sound. "Oh, please. Not here for me? Right. You just love chilling at arrivals for the vibes?" He gave her a look like she'd lost her mind. In his head, it was obvious. Why was she acting too cool to admit she came for her own brother? He was a big deal now, a celebrity. She should be bragging about him, not playing dumb and acting like they were strangers.

"I'm waiting on someone," Elara snapped. "And newsflash: it's not you. Get over yourself." She crossed her arms. "Their flight is just delayed."

Cassian rolled his eyes hard. "Yeah, sure. Who is it then? Or are you just throwing a tantrum because I asked for a hand with my luggage? Seriously, Elara? I'm your brother. God forbid you do me a favor." He sighed, shaking his head. "Coraline would never do this. She'd have grabbed these bags the second she saw me. But you? You're just a pain in the ass."

He stared at her, totally let down. Coraline was sweet, always eager to help with a smile. Elara just had endless attitude. Ever since she took over the family business, she acted like she was too high and mighty for basic chores. Cassian shoved the heavy bags right against her shins. "I don't care who you're waiting for. I'm family. Grab the bags and take them to the curb. Now."

Elara looked down at the luggage, completely unfazed. She didn't budge an inch. "Pass. Do it yourself. I'm occupied."

Cassian lost it. "Are you kidding me, Elara? You want to start a fight right here? Let everyone see what a terrible sister you are?"

"And what kind of brother does that make you?" a freezing voice cut in from behind.

Cassian spun around. Finnian Shaw stood there, glaring down at him with pure, icy contempt.

"She's your sister?" Finnian drawled. "Huh. Weird. I always figured she was mine." He turned to Elara, offering a hot paper cup. "Here's your coffee, Callie."

"Thanks," Elara beamed, her face instantly softening as she took the cup. The look in her eyes said it all—she was incredibly glad he was there.
