Chapter 1003

Elara’s flight with the crew was tomorrow. Then the text hit: Rowan Grey was finally awake. Elara dropped everything and bolted for the hospital.

She slammed through the clinic doors right as Blair Sutton pulled up. Their eyes locked. No "hey," no "how are you." They just booked it toward the elevators.

The doors slid shut. "Rhys call you?" Blair panted.

Elara nodded, chewing her lip. "Yeah. But he sounded... off. Super sketchy. Like he was hiding something bad about Rowan."

"Right?" Blair groaned, pacing the small space. "He wouldn't tell me crap. Just kept saying, 'Get here now.' I kept pushing, like, 'Is she okay?' and he just went, 'You'll see when you get here.' Ugh."

The silence in the elevator was suffocating. Elara forced a breath, trying to stop her own hands from shaking. "Hey, she’s gonna be fine," she said, squeezing Blair's arm. "Her body was totally fine before. Waking up is a win, right?" She flashed a quick, fake-it-till-you-make-it smile. "Don't freak out yet."

Blair exhaled. "Yeah. Okay. As long as she's breathing and intact, we can handle the rest."

They sprinted down the ICU hallway. There was Rhys, leaning against the wall outside Rowan's room. Still in his expensive suit, still looking like a billionaire boss, but his face was a total trainwreck. He looked devastated, not happy. Elara and Blair exchanged a quick, holy-crap look.

"Hey, Mr. Stewart," Elara said, marching up to him. "What gives? Rowan's awake, right? Why are you out here in the hall?"

Blair chimed in, "Yeah, seriously. Why aren't you in there with her?"

Wasn't this the guy who'd been praying for this exact moment? Now he's playing hallway hall-monitor?

Rhys stiffened, his jaw tight. He looked like he wanted the floor to swallow him whole. "She kicked me out," he muttered, staring at his shoes. "Look, just... go in. See for yourselves." He paused, rubbing his temples. "Actually, scratch that. Let's all go. Together."

Elara and Blair shared a WTF look. This made zero sense. Rowan worshipped the ground Rhys walked on. Loving him was basically her full-time job. Why the hell would she kick him out now?

But they followed him anyway. Rhys cracked the door open, stepping inside like he was walking on eggshells. "Rowan?" he murmured, his voice cracking a bit. "Look who's here."

Elara and Blair pushed past him. Rowan was sitting propped up against the pillows. She looked pale, yeah, but her eyes were bright, and some color had finally returned to her face. She was definitely back.

The second she saw them, her face crumpled. "Callie! Blair!" she sobbed, tears spilling over. "Oh my god, I missed you guys so much!"

But then, in a split second, the warmth vanished. Rowan's eyes darted past them, locking onto Rhys. Her expression turned ice-cold, pure disgust.

"Wait, Callie, Blair... who is this creep?" she snapped, her voice dripping with irritation. "Why does he keep lurking in here? He's seriously getting on my nerves. Can you guys make him get out?"

Elara froze. Blair's mouth literally fell open. They both whipped their heads around to stare at Rhys. What the actual hell? Rowan didn't know him? And she wanted him thrown out?

It took a solid five seconds for Elara to find her voice. She stepped closer to the bed. "Rowan... sweetie... you seriously don't know who he is?"

Rowan rolled her eyes, looking totally annoyed. "Should I? He's just some annoying guy who won't leave me alone."
