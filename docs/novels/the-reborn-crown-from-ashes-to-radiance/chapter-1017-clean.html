Chapter 1017

Rhys couldn't take another second in Rowan’s room. He bailed, marching straight down the hall to hunt down her lead doctor. There was no way in hell he was buying this. Out of everyone in her life, how was he the only one wiped from her brain?

The doc shifted, looking totally awkward under Rhys’s stare. "Look, man, I can't tell you if she's faking," he said, shrugging. "But honestly, why would she? The girl barely survived that crash. Her head took a massive hit. Memory loss? Totally on the table." He tapped his clipboard. "Will she get it back? Who knows. Some people wake up tomorrow and remember everything. Others? Blank slate forever. Honestly, if she can function, just let it go. Don't sweat the details. She knows everyone else, right?"

"Just talk to her," the doc added. "Rebuild the timeline. Her brain isn't broken, she just lost some files. She can still process things."

Rhys clenched his jaw, his chest tight. "So you're telling me it's totally normal to target *just* me? Like, a surgical strike on my existence?"

Sure, he could try to jog her memory, but she was acting so cold, keeping him at arm's length. The thought of begging her to remember made him feel like he was choking.

"Actually, yeah, it is normal," the doc said, softening his tone. "Amnesia is weird like that. The brain locks away the stuff tied to the trauma. You're the reason she got hurt, right? Her subconscious is probably just protecting her from the pain."

"Seriously, Mr. Stewart, don't lose sleep over this. She's alive. That's the win here. It's just a few missing files." The doc leaned back. "And let's be real—did you guys actually have some epic, unforgettable love story? Show her some old photos, maybe that sparks something. But medically? I'm out of moves. This stuff is subjective. It's on her friends and family to fill in the blanks. I don't know your history."

Rhys shut his mouth. If Rowan actually wanted to remember him, he wouldn't be standing here interrogating a guy in a white coat. And photos? God. He racked his brain. They had nothing. No cute couple selfies, no vacation snaps. Just work. Missions. Him barking orders, acting like a hardass. Did they even have a single good memory?

It wasn't the amnesia that made him want to punch a wall. It was the realization that she didn't love him anymore. That was the knife in his gut. His ego was trashed. He used to be her entire universe, and now? Poof. Deleted. How could she just turn off her feelings like a light switch?

Rhys stormed out, his head spinning. He didn't even know what he was fighting for anymore.

The doc watched him leave, shaking his head with a half-smirk. He scratched his neck, totally baffled by why this guy was throwing such a massive tantrum.

Back in the room, Rowan took a bite of the breakfast Blair Sutton had grabbed for her. She chewed, trying to sound totally casual. "So, uh, his family is setting him up, right?"

Blair blinked, holding a coffee cup. "Wait, who?"

A beat passed. Then Blair's eyes went wide. "Oh! You mean Rhys!"

"Yeah."

"Oh, totally. Word is the Stewarts are hooking him up with some old-money heiress. Apparently, she's, like, super sweet too."
