Chapter 469

"Yeah, whatever. Like I even count," Elara muttered. She threw her hands up, took a slow, heavy step back, and sank into the sofa cushions. "I’m basically a stranger to the Jewells now. Why would I get a room?" She shook her head, letting out a sharp, bitter laugh. "I don't belong here. And honestly? Even if I did, nobody gives a crap about what I say."

Her eyes flicked up, locking onto the massive framed portrait above the mantle. "See? Case in point." She pointed a finger at the glass, a mocking smile on her lips. "Look how perfect you all look. So damn happy. And where am I? Oh, right. Nowhere. Just the third wheel looking in."

Walter shifted, trading tense looks with the boys. That picture had been gathering dust for years, totally ignored. But now? The empty space where Elara should've been practically screamed at them. Sure, they’d talked about booking a new shoot, but they always blew it off. She was completely erased. Even the small frames scattered across the side tables only featured Coraline. Not a single shot of Elara existed in this house.

A heavy wave of guilt hit the men in the room. When Coraline was ruling the roost, she sucked up all the oxygen. Elara had just been background noise to them. But with Coraline out of the picture, the fog finally cleared.

"Whoa, Callie, hey—don't talk like that. We're listening, okay?" Walter jumped in, waving his hands. "Look, your brother needs that master bedroom. It makes total sense. Let's move Edgar into Coraline's room, and she can take the smaller one down the hall." He nodded eagerly. "And we are absolutely getting a new family picture done. Like, ASAP."

The boys chimed in. "Hell yeah. A fresh one. Everyone included this time."

Elara shrugged, playing it cool. "I mean, if you guys are down, I won't stop you. But hey, if we're doing this, why wait? Let's swap them right now. Coraline's room has way better light, and it's huge. Edgar needs that space to heal up after his surgery. Plus, that massive balcony is perfect for him to get some fresh air and vitamin D, right?"

She paused, tilting her head. "I mean, Coraline wouldn't throw a tantrum over this, right? Edgar's literally done everything for her. She's not that petty to make a fuss over a stupid room, is she?"

She was throwing Coraline's own favorite guilt-tripping lines right back at them, but the guys were too clueless to catch the irony. To them, Coraline was just the sweet, chill sister who'd gladly step aside. Besides, the swap made perfect sense for Edgar's recovery. It looked like Elara was just being a super caring, protective sister.

Beatrix walked in right then, catching the tail end of the pitch. She paused at the doorway, biting her lip, but kept her mouth shut. It felt fair, honestly. Edgar had already surrendered his own space for Elara anyway. Sure, she loved Coraline, but her firstborn boy was her weak spot. Asking Coraline to take one for the team just felt like the right move.

With Elara pulling the strings behind a sweet smile, the guys got to work. They hauled Coraline’s stuff out, dragged Edgar’s gear in, and got him totally set up in the prime real estate. By the time they finished sweating, dinner was finally served, and they collapsed around the table.

"So, about that eyesore on the wall," Elara said, gesturing with her fork toward the couch. "We're still replacing it, right?"

Walter beamed, piling food onto his plate. "Oh, absolutely. The second Coraline gets back, we're booking a photographer. Eat up, Callie. We're getting the whole crew in the frame this time. Nobody gets left out."

Elara tilted her head, a nasty little smirk playing on her lips. "Wait? Why the hell should we wait for her?"
