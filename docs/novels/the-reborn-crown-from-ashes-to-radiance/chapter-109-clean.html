Chapter 109

"Keep dreaming, Victor," Julian spat, his voice pure ice. He locked his fingers through mine, dragging me toward the exit.

"Hey, Ms. Jewell! I got you covered on that favor!" Victor yelled after us, craning his neck like a total show-off. "We gotta grab dinner sometime. My treat, to say thanks!"

I kept my mouth shut. No way I was poking the bear. Julian was practically radiating heat next to me—the guy was absolutely livid.

I knew their history. It wasn't a simple hate-match; it was messy. They used to fight side-by-side back in the day. Hardly anyone knew that, but I’d accidentally dug up the truth. If Victor actually kicked the bucket, Julian would be devastated, even if he’d never admit it.

Blair shot Victor a quick, awkward grin, threw up a wave, and scrambled to catch up with us.

The hallway was a war zone. Victor’s muscle lined the walls, locking eyes with Julian’s guys in a silent, heavy-breathing standoff. Kaelen and Jasper stood right in the thick of it, giving me a quick, polite nod. Blair trailed behind us, her eyes wide, totally freaked out by the vibe.

We hit the elevator lobby. Julian and I took the lead, with Blair sandwiched between Jasper, Kaelen, and a small army of suits. It looked like a mob scene. A couple of random people waiting for the lift took one look at us and immediately noped out.

The silence was suffocating. I peeked up at Julian’s rigid jaw.

"Are you pissed?" I mumbled. "Look, I can explain. I had to ask Victor. I couldn't just let a guy die, okay? It had nothing to do with him specifically. I would’ve done it for some random dude on the street."

"Seriously, please don't be mad at me."

Julian’s forehead creased, his face twisting. "You got hurt, Elara!" he snapped, his voice cracking with raw worry.

"Oh, this?" I forced a shrug and a tiny laugh, waving my hand. "It's just a tiny singe. It’ll be gone in a couple of days. Seriously, no biggie."

"I don't care," Julian growled, his voice dead serious. "Nobody touches you. Period."

The doors slid open with a ding. Julian marched right in, and I scrambled to keep pace, slipping in right behind him.

Blair paused at the threshold, realizing Jasper and Kaelen hadn't budged. "Uh, you guys coming?"

"We'll take the next one," Kaelen muttered, clearly terrified of stepping into Julian's blast radius.

"Yeah, good call. I'm staying here," Blair said, backing away. She threw me a 'good luck, sister' look. "Have fun talking it out!"

The doors slid shut, sealing us in. The temperature in the metal box plummeted to sub-zero.

I looked up at him, trying to break the ice. I grabbed his hand, giving it a little tug, trying to be cute. "Babe, come on, don't be like this. Even if Victor is still breathing, you know you're ten times the man he'll ever be, right?"

"I don't give a shit if he breathes or bleeds out," Julian said, his voice flat and freezing.
