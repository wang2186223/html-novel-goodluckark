Chapter 963

Elara locked eyes with Kaelen, her face dead serious. "How the hell did you cross paths with Phantom?"

"Hey, we didn't go looking for a fight!" Kaelen shot back, hands up. "We were heading your way and literally bumped into his guys. They were hunting for your crew, saw us, and figured we were with you. And Phantom? Man, he clocked Victor instantly. His eyes just lit up."

"Seriously, we had zero skin in this game. We could've just walked away." Kaelen paused, looking down, guilt eating at him. "But I gave Victor my word. Plus, he drove me out here. I owed the guy."

He looked up, trying to read her. "Elara, tell me straight. Did I just screw things up for you?"

It was pathetic, really. They’d come to play savior, and now they were the ones begging for backup.

"Phantom..." Elara pinched her nose, hard. "That absolute psycho. If Victor’s in his hands, we’re in deep shit."

Kaelen’s face drained of color. "Wait. You don't mean... Phantom's gonna, like, force himself on him? Is he gonna...?"

"Ugh, no, not like that," Elara snapped, rolling her eyes. "He’s sick, but he’s not a rapist. He’s just... totally off his rocker. And why the hell did Victor think showing up here was a good idea anyway? Like I need some knight in shining armor." She shook her head, rubbing her temples. "God, my head is pounding."

She took a breath. "Look, Victor’s not dead. Not yet, anyway. Phantom’s a lunatic, but he doesn't just pop people for fun. He likes his little games. He’ll take his sweet time."

She let out a heavy sigh. "Nobody else can touch this. Phantom’s got too many connections, and he’s way too dangerous."

"So, what, even Rhys can't handle him?" Kaelen asked, his voice tight with panic.

Elara shook her head. "Nobody in this town can. If I want Victor back, I have to go grab him myself." She shot a frustrated look toward the back room. "But I can't leave right now. Rowan Grey still needs me."

Her eyes softened when she looked back at Kaelen. She wanted to run out the door this second, but her hands were tied.

Kaelen let out a long, shaky breath. "So now what? Is Victor gonna survive? If I don't get him out of there fast, I won't be able to live with myself. I'm totally solo here—can Mr. Stewart loan us some muscle?"

"Won't matter," Elara said. "And Rhys is just as short-handed as you are."

She pointed down the hall.

"Go get cleaned up. Once I get Rowan Grey stabilized, I'm coming with you. We'll take down Black S together." She muttered under her breath, "That freaking psycho... why is he starting shit again?"

"Starting shit?" Kaelen blinked, totally lost.

Elara gave him a flat look. "The guy's got major issues. Now go wash the dirt off."

Right then, Rhys stepped out of a side room. His eyes locked onto Kaelen. "Kaelen? What the hell happened to you?" He frowned, scanning him up and down. "Where are your clothes? You look like a hobo who just crawled in off the street."

Kaelen just stood there, mouth open.

"He had a little run-in with some muggers," Elara said, her tone dry.

Rhys shot him another sharp look. "And what took you so long anyway? I figured you flaked. Where's Mr. Myers?"

"Can't get ahold of him. Something must've gone sideways on his end," Kaelen said, trying to keep his voice steady. "I came to watch Elara's back, but turns out she's doing just fine. I'm the one who got wrecked."

Rhys just sighed and shook his head. "Go hit the shower. I'll have someone grab you some clean threads."

Kaelen nodded, relieved. Then he stopped and looked at Elara. "Oh, wait—how's Ms. Shepherd?"

"She's out of surgery," Elara said quietly. "It's rough, but she's still breathing."
