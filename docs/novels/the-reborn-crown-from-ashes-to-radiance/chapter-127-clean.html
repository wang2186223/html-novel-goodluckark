NovelVibe

Chapter 127

The tires hummed on the asphalt as the car snaked down the empty country road. Elara leaned against the passenger door, her brain working overtime. She’d spent the last hour throwing out wild guesses, but Julian’s face remained a total blank.

"Okay, so no helicopter," she said, ticking fingers off. "No private jet. What about fireworks? Or, wait, a massive drone light show?" She sighed, sinking back into the leather seat. She was officially out of ideas. Every single thing she’d ever mentioned liking had already been crossed off her mental list.

Julian let out a low laugh, cutting his eyes over to her with a look that was pure, soft affection. "Just hold your horses, babe. We’re almost there."

"Ugh, you're killing me here. Seriously, my head is spinning." She rolled her eyes, though she was secretly loving it. Honestly, she didn't usually get hyped about gifts. Why would she? She had enough cash to buy whatever she wanted on a whim. Plus, her three overprotective, ridiculously rich brothers spent half their lives trying to outdo each other, buying her crazy, one-of-a-kind stuff just to put the world on a silver platter for her.

But this? This was driving her nuts. What the hell could Julian have gotten her? Most gifts were so damn predictable. She’d run through everything—a vintage grand piano, some rare acoustic guitar, even, like, custom-made acupuncture needles. Yeah, she’d gone that far down the rabbit hole. Nothing clicked. Especially not after Jasper Reed let slip that Julian had spent days sweating over the details to set this whole thing up.

"Two minutes," Julian said, his voice dropping to that soft, private tone he only used with her. Out in the real world, the guy was a ruthless shark. Right now, he was just a guy trying to make his girl smile.

The car rolled to a stop in front of Myers Manor. Elara swung her door open, her boots hitting the gravel. Julian grabbed her hand, his fingers locking with hers as he pulled her toward the backyard. Jasper trailed a few paces behind them, keeping his eyes peeled.

They hadn't even cleared the corner of the house when Kaelen Steel’s loud voice drifted over, mixed with a bunch of other guys talking.

"Yo, what the hell is that?"

"Boss’s gift to the lady. Hands off."

"Wait, is that thing breathing? It’s moving!"

"Who knows? Word is she likes 'em. Some kind of pet, maybe? But damn, that's a massive pet."

"Seriously, look at the size of that cage. Whatever's in there is bigger than me."

Elara stopped dead, her eyes darting to Julian. "Wait. Is it a horse?" She loved riding—nothing beat the rush of tearing across an open field on horseback.

Julian just smirked, shaking his head. "Nope. Come on, see for yourself."

Her heart did a little flutter. Part nervous, part hyped, she hurried ahead of him. There, right in the middle of the lawn, sat a massive iron cage. A heavy black tarp covered the whole thing, but she could hear rustling inside. Something was definitely breathing in there.

She didn't hesitate. She grabbed a fistful of the dark fabric and yanked. The heavy tarp slid off, pooling onto the grass.

Elara gasped. Inside the iron bars stood a beast. It was pure, blinding white from nose to tail, looking like some crazy mix of a wolf and a massive dog. It had this huge, fluffy tail, and the sheer size of the thing was insane—it was easily bigger than a full-grown tiger.

"Is that... a wolf?" Elara stammered, her voice catching in her throat. No dog on earth looked like this, but it was way too massive to be a normal wolf. An arctic wolf, maybe? A giant one?

*Awoooo!*

The beast locked its eyes right on Elara and let loose a wild, happy howl that shook her chest. Elara froze, her mind going completely blank.

Julian stepped up behind her, his voice a soft murmur near her ear. "You don't remember him? It's Kodiak."

"Kodiak..." Elara whispered, the name tasting familiar. Then her eyes went wide. "Kodiak?!"

She whipped her head to Julian, her jaw dropping, then stared back at the gorgeous white beast. "Oh my god. Kodiak? Is that really you?"

And just like that, a flood of old, buried memories came crashing back.
