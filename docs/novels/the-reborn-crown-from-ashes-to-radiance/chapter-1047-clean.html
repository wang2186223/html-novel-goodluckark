"Seriously? What is it with you guys and the kneeling?" Elara threw her hands up, totally over it. "Look, I can't just waltz into a random OR and start cutting. I don't work here. This isn't my patient." She shook her head, pacing. "There's red tape, okay? Rules. You think this place doesn't have a legal department?"

"For you? They'll rip up the rulebook, Ms. Jewell!" a young resident blurted out, practically bouncing. "Seriously, the Miracle Doctor! Chief's obsessed with your work. He talks about you 24/7."

"I'm grabbing him right now!"

The guy bolted. Honestly, these docs wanted a front-row seat to the legend just as much as they wanted the guy on the table to live. Seconds later, the big boss himself marched in.

The chief didn't even say hello. "Prep OR Three. Dr. Elara is taking the lead," he barked.

Elara blinked, her jaw dropping. "Wait, seriously?" She’d showed up just to watch, and now they were handing her the scalpel.

"I'd trust you with my own life," the chief said, dead serious. "Howell is flatlining. You're his only shot."

Elara had zero intention of getting her hands dirty today. But looking at the panicked faces around her, and with Victor's clock ticking down to zero, she knew she was the only one in the building who could pull this off. She let out a heavy sigh and started scrubbing in.

Kaelen Steel tore through the hospital corridors, checking every floor. Finally, he spotted Victor's goons hovering like vultures outside the surgical wing.

"Where the hell is my wife?" Kaelen snapped, storming up to them.

"Inside. Operating," one of the suits said.

Kaelen froze. "She's what?"

"The local surgeons choked. Said the case was too messy," another goon chimed in. "But Ms. Jewell? She's the real deal. A total badass. She stepped up, and the chief practically kissed her feet to let her do it."

"Man, what a woman," a guy mumbled. "She belongs with the boss."

The crew started whispering among themselves. To them, Julian was a total loser who didn't deserve her. Victor, though? Victor was a king. He and the Miracle Doctor were a match made in heaven.

Kaelen's ears burned. He glared at the group, his eyes digging into them. "Are you guys out of your minds? She's married. To me. She has zero connection to Howell."

"She keeps saving his life, man," one of the goons argued back. "That's not nothing. She clearly cares."

Kaelen rolled his eyes so hard it hurt. "It's called basic human decency. You see a guy bleeding out, you help. Stop fan-fictioning her life. It's weird."

He made a mental note to tell Elara to steer clear of Victor. These idiots were getting delusional. Still, he had to admit, it was crazy how she kept playing guardian angel to the exact same guy.

Under the bright OR lights, Elara was pure ice. The chief had packed the room with his top-tier surgical team, but they just stood there, completely starstruck. She looked like a kid, but her hands moved like lightning—steady, ruthless, perfect. She was pulling off advanced maneuvers they'd spent decades failing to master, making it look as easy as breathing.

"Okay, now I get the hype," a nurse whispered under her breath. "She's, what, twenty-something? And her hands aren't even shaking. It's insane."

"How is she this damn good?" another muttered.

During a quick suture swap, one of the senior surgeons leaned in. "Hey, Doc... I thought Eastern medicine didn't really do the whole cutting-open thing?"

"I'm dual-certified," Elara said, her voice deadpan as she went right back to work. "Unless we're talking sci-fi level stuff, there isn't a surgery on this planet I can't pull off."

"Jesus, Ms. Jewell," someone breathed, completely blown away. "You're unreal."
