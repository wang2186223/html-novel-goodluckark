Chapter 659

Julian locked eyes with them, dead serious.

"Look, call me a liar if you want, but this is the god's honest truth. I fell for Callie way before we ever became a thing," he muttered. "We're talking way back. I was what, ten? Just a dumb kid, and I was already crazy about her. The older we got, the harder I fell. But she didn't even see me. It was always Ethan, Ethan, Ethan. Hell, I think I actually creeped her out back then."

His gaze dropped. You could tell those old memories still cut deep. He’d spent years standing in the background, praying she’d look his way just once. She never did. To her, Ethan was the only guy on the planet. She loved him, period. It was brutal.

He hated himself for not stepping up sooner, for not fighting. But back then? He was just some scrawny, broke kid. He figured he’d wait. Build himself up, get some real money, make something of his life, and *then* make his move. Except by the time he actually made it, she was already gone. Someone else had her heart.

Even now, his chest squeezed tight just thinking about it. Especially the nightmare of how she died in that other life—destroyed by their toxic circle, suffering the worst kind of death. He’d been too late. That failure still ate him alive. But not this time. This time, he’d clawed through hell to bring her back.

"Believe me or don't," Julian growled, his voice turning raw. "But if she dies, I’m marching straight down to hell to drag her back myself. I don't care about the price. I'll burn my own life to the ground. I’d spend a thousand lifetimes in agony just to keep her safe."

He flashed a faint, dry smirk, but his eyes stayed cold and dead-set.

Rhys Kincaid traded a look with the other two. Nobody said a word.

The guy was clearly whipped. You can't fake that kind of obsession. But they weren't just going to nod and trust him. Talk is cheap, man. Any idiot can claim they’d die for a girl. It’s when the shit hits the fan that you see who a guy really is.

Sure, Julian seemed okay. But you never trust a guy with your sister until you watch him under pressure. Look at the Jewells. Callie swore they were great to her, and they played the perfect family for the cameras. Total bullshit, as it turned out. And then there was Ethan. Everyone thought he was the perfect boyfriend—until the mask slipped.

Girls get blinded when they fall in love. That’s why her brothers had to play guard dog. Now that the three of them had Julian cornered, it was the perfect time to squeeze him.

"Yo, Julian," Rhys blurted out. "Why don't you crash in our room tonight?"

"Wait, what?" Julian blinked. He’d braced himself for an interrogation, not a freaking sleepover.

A knot tied up in his stomach. This was new. And Rhys didn't mean just the two of them. He said *us*—meaning all four guys packed into one guest room like a bunch of drunk college freshmen after a pizza run.

"Got an issue with that?" Keaton asked, cocking an eyebrow. "Or are you hiding some shady shit you don't want us seeing once the lights go out?"

Julian let out a dry laugh and shook his head. "No secrets, man. Just didn't see this coming, is all." He shrugged, keeping his cool. "But yeah, whatever. I'm down. I'll bunk with you guys."

"Seriously?" Ethan chimed in, looking surprised. "You're actually cool with this? No whining?"

Julian smirked. "Totally cool. Just don't snore, okay?"
