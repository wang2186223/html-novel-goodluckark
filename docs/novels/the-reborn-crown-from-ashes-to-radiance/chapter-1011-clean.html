Chapter 1011

"Get over there and talk to Ms. Jewell," Finch hissed, elbowing his ribs. Her voice buzzed with hype. "Dude, you won't believe this. She’s C. Like, *the* screenwriter C."

"Wait, the genius behind those massive box-office hits? *Her*?" Finch stared across the room, totally starstruck. "No freaking way. Her scripts are all over the place, some of them are so deep and dark. Everyone in the industry thought C was some old, grizzled dude with a lifetime of trauma, not some gorgeous young girl."

Cassian shut his mouth and cut his eyes at Finch. She didn't know a damn thing about his family's messy baggage. She’d been on medical leave forever and just got back to managing him, so Elara’s name meant nothing to her. Finch had zero clue Elara used to be his sister. Or that they hated each other's guts now. Honestly, if Elara kicked his ass off this movie set right now, it’d just be a regular Tuesday.

"Hello? Earth to Cassian," Finch snapped, her forehead creasing. "Move your feet! Go pitch yourself. Word on the street is she’s not just writing this thing, she’s funding it too. She’s loaded, like stupid rich, and her network is insane."

"Yeah. She’s the money and the brain," Cassian muttered, his eyes tracking Elara across the floor. She wouldn't even look at him, but a weird, stubborn spark of pride flared up in his chest anyway.

"So what the hell are you waiting for? Look how tight she is with Finnian Shaw. You need to get in that circle, like, yesterday. Finnian is literally God right now—chart-topping albums, sold-out movies, the whole package. Teenagers love him, moms love him, everyone is obsessed." Finch was practically bouncing. "And oh my god, have you heard about his tarot readings? People say he’s psychic or something. I’d kill for a reading, but he only does it for VIPs. Man, if you could pull even half his numbers..."

Cassian let out a dry, pathetic laugh. Yeah, right. Keep dreaming.

"Up we go!" Finch grabbed his sleeve to yank him forward, but he planted his boots and yanked back.

He locked eyes with her, his throat tight. "Hold on," he mumbled. "I need to tell you something first."

Finch froze, her face dropping. "Oh, god. What?" she whispered, panic creeping into her eyes. "Did you screw up? Did some fan catch you doing something stupid?"

"No, nothing like that," Cassian said, shaking his head. "But it’s basically just as bad." He knew the industry gossip mill. If people linked his family to Elara, and then realized Elara was the mastermind C, his fanbase would cancel him in a heartbeat. She already had a massive cult following without even trying. The second the public connected those dots, she’d be untouchable, and he’d be trash.

"Spit it out, Cassian," Finch demanded. Her stomach did a sick flip. Was her golden ticket to becoming an A-list agent about to burn down before she even got started?

He looked at her terrified face and just spilled the whole ugly story. He told her how his parents took Elara in as a kid, how they treated her like garbage while spoiling their adopted daughter, and how they finally found out Elara was actually a real-deal Jewell. A total, multi-decade family disaster.

"Are you freaking kidding me?" Finch stared at him, her jaw hitting the floor. "You have *that* kind of history with her? Is your family literally insane? Of all the people on this planet to piss off, you chose her? So let me get this straight—she’s a medical prodigy, a legendary screenwriter, and she’s probably hiding even more crazy shit?"
