Chapter 782

Right on cue, Valentina Rossi stormed toward Coraline. Elara saw it coming a mile away.

Meanwhile, Coraline was still rubbing up against Tanner. Seriously, the girl had a death wish. Elara had to hand it to her, though—the chick had serious balls.

Tanner’s fiancée, Sharon, was a straight-up hurricane. Yeah, they were only engaged, not hitched yet, but nobody in their right mind messed with her. Plus, she was totally obsessed with him.

Sharon belonged to the Lynn family—shady mob types with blood on their hands. She grew up throwing punches in dirty alleys. A total mafia princess, and she had the reputation to prove it.

Coraline had picked the absolute worst target. For a split second, Elara actually felt a pang of pity.

Sharon cornered Coraline. She wore a killer black gown and a blinding diamond tiara. One look from her could literally drop you dead.

But Coraline was clueless, still trying to wiggle her way into Tanner’s personal space.

Tanner recoiled like he’d just stepped on a hot coal.

"Whoa, babe, chill! I swear, I want nothing to do with this hot mess!"

"She’s freaking psycho! I told her no like a million times, but she keeps clawing at me. It’s honestly sad!"

"Babe, you’re my ride-or-die. You know you're the only girl for me!"

"Seriously, honey, don't hate me. You'll literally kill me if you walk away!"

Sharon flipped her hair, giving Tanner a smug grin. "Relax, babe, I know. Love you too. Some trashy girls just don't know their lane. Always trying to swipe someone else's property."

She whipped around, locking her eyes on Coraline. "What gutter did you crawl out of, you cheap gold-digger? You seriously thought you could snatch my man?"

Coraline froze, her jaw dropping. "Wait... fiancé?"

Walter literally just told her Tanner was single. How the hell did he have a fiancée?

Sharon’s screaming match drew a massive crowd. Every eye in the room locked onto them.

"Who’s the chick dressed like a cheap Christmas tree? Why is she throwing herself at him?"

"Doesn't she know Tanner is locked down by Sharon? Nobody touches him. Her family is straight-up mafia."

"And this nobody thinks she can hit on him? At Sharon’s own freaking party?"

"She’s got a screw loose!"

"Oh, she’s dead meat."

"Didn't the last bitch who tried this end up in the ICU?"

The room buzzed with nasty whispers. Everyone knew Coraline was toast.

Coraline still didn't get the memo. She scowled back at Sharon. "Hey, I didn't know he was taken!"

"Oh, so you just grab whatever guy you see? Just steal them and ask questions later?"

"Are you freaking kidding me? You couldn't even do a quick background check?"

"You think you can take what's mine? Who the hell are you anyway? What's your family name? Let’s see who thinks they can mess with me!"

Coraline shrank back, her voice shaking. "I... I'm a Jewell..."

"The hell is a Jewell? Never heard of them," Sharon spat.

"Callie's family," Tanner cut in, nudging Sharon. "She claims she's Callie's sister. Thinks she's way better than her, too."

"Wait, what? This is the bitch who’s been giving Callie hell?"

"Oh, I remember Callie talking about her. Always acting like a princess, scheming, trying to ruin her life!"

Sharon's eyes flared with pure, unadulterated rage.

Tanner nodded, looking dead serious. "Exactly. I told her she doesn't hold a candle to Callie, and she totally lost her shit!"
