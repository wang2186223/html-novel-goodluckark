Chapter 712

"Not half bad, honestly," Finnian Shaw muttered.

"Yeah, but we gotta watch him," Keaton whispered back, eyes narrowing.

Rhys Kincaid hauled himself up, wearing a lopsided smirk. "Alright, man. You and me. Let's go a round."

"Wait, what? A real fight?" Julian scrambled up, staring.

"I gotta make sure you can actually back up Callie," Rhys Kincaid said. His voice went dead serious, way too sober for a guy who'd been chugging drinks.

"Whatever. Let's do it," Julian said, cracking his neck.

They bolted out to the grass before Elara could even open her mouth. In seconds, they were throwing hands like a couple of wasted frat boys after a tailgate.

Elara and Lily ran out after them, Finnian and Keaton hot on their heels, already screaming for Rhys Kincaid like they were in the bleachers at a championship game.

Then the buzzed crowd realized nobody was backing Julian. Instantly, Finnian and Keaton started bickering over who they actually wanted to win.

"Your brothers are a total trip," Rhys said, leaning in next to Elara.

"They're just wasted," Elara grumbled, face pinched.

"Still, Kincaid's got moves. Even plastered, the dude's solid. And Julian's holding his ground. Never seen anyone go toe-to-toe with him like this," Rhys noted.

Elara just chewed her lip, eyes glued to the mess.

The brawl turned ugly fast. Knuckles cracked against bone. Glares turned icy. They weren't playing anymore; someone was going to end up in the ER.

Nobody else was moving. Elara knew she had to end it. She bolted.

"Whoa, hold up!" Rhys lunged to grab her jacket. "They're out of their minds right now! You'll get wrecked!"

She ignored him and threw herself straight into the meat grinder.

A blur of fists later, Julian and Kincaid, blind with adrenaline, swung toward the idiot who dared step between them.

Big mistake. Elara moved like lightning. A couple of quick, brutal takedowns, and she had both giant idiots pinned to the dirt, wheezing.

"Done. Over. It's a draw," she snapped, glaring daggers. "I'm Callie. Touch me and you're dead!"

That killed the vibe instantly. The two meatheads looked like kicked puppies. Elara waved Lily and the others over. "Help me drag these losers inside."

By then, Julian and Kincaid were completely gone, slurring and tripping over their own feet. Elara shoved all four guys into separate bedrooms before they could start round two.

Finally, quiet.

Elara let out a massive breath.

Rhys stared at her, totally blown away. "Damn. You're a badass."

"Yeah, yeah, save it," Elara said, scanning the room. Rowan Grey was nowhere to be seen. She turned to him. "Hey, Rhys. Got a second?"

"Depends," Rhys said, instantly on guard. "What's up?"

"Rowan Grey," Elara said, locking eyes with him. "Don't play dumb. I'm not here to grill you or force your hand. Just talk to me, okay?"

Rhys hesitated, then shrugged. "Fine."

They slipped out the back door, leaving the noise behind.

"Do you really feel absolutely nothing for Rowan?" Elara asked softly. "Because, man, leading someone on just to yank the rug out? That's messed up."

"I know," Rhys muttered, looking down. "That's why I didn't want to give her false hope in the first place. Honestly, that's why I bailed on the Christmas dinner at the last second."
