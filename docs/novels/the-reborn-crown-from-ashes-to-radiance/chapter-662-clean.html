Chapter 662

Julian kept his mouth shut. He just slid right into the dead center of the mattress.

The bed was decent-sized, sure, but cramming four fully grown dudes onto it? Total squeeze. But they’d pushed for it, so whatever. He just wanted to keep the peace, even if he had zero clue what their actual angle was.

The second Julian settled in, Rhys Kincaid slapped the light switch. Pitch black.

Julian started out flat on his back. After a few minutes, though, his muscles started aching. He rolled onto his side, only to freeze. A phone screen glowed inches from his nose, lighting up Keaton’s pale face like some cheap horror movie jump scare.

Julian’s heart did a little skip. He didn't make a sound, though. He just gave Keaton a tight, polite smile, then rolled the other way.

Boom. Face-to-face with Rhys Kincaid. The guy was just staring at him, totally lost in his own head.

Julian let out a silent groan, flipped back onto his spine, and stared straight at the ceiling. He went stiff as a board.

God knows when he actually drifted off.

All he knew was he was sandwiched tight in the middle, barely breathing, terrified he’d bump into the guys on either side and wake them up. Meanwhile, the other three didn't give a shit. They thrashed and rolled around however they wanted, leaving Julian pinned like a specimen.

Honestly, it took pure mental grit not to lose his mind. He’d never even shared a mattress with one guy before, let alone three.

Next thing he knew, it was morning.

He didn't wake up on his own, either. He’d somehow crashed hard, only to be jolted awake by Finnian Shaw screaming bloody murder.

Finnian bolted upright with a yell, glanced down, saw a dude right next to him, and let out an even louder shriek. It took him a solid second scream to realize, *oh, wait, it’s just Julian.*

Rhys and Keaton dragged themselves up, rubbing their eyes, totally out of it.

The racket brought Elara running into the room.

She stopped in the doorway, staring at the four of them tangled up on the single bed. Her face was a mix of... well, a lot of things. But, damn, she had to hand it to them. Her three brothers and Julian were all seriously easy on the eyes. Seeing them all messy-haired in one bed? Kind of a moment.

She whipped out her phone and snapped a photo. *Click.*

The guys just blinked, still half-dead.

"Whoa! Callie, stop! Delete that!" Keaton snapped, finally waking up.

Rhys didn't say anything. He just shot Julian a look, opened his mouth, then shut it.

"So," Elara smirked, scrolling through her gallery, looking pretty damn proud of her work. "Did you boys actually get any useful dirt out of him last night?"

"Not really," Rhys muttered, scowling.

"Whatever, let's move," Keaton said, throwing his arms up in a massive stretch. "Let's grab some food, then hit the mall this afternoon."

"Works for me," Julian said.

Finnian, finally calm, turned to Julian. "Wait, Mr. Myers, did you actually sleep at all?"

"Me?" Julian shook his head. "Yeah, actually. Slept like a baby. No clue when I passed out, honestly."

Seriously, he’d expected to stare at the ceiling all night. But his body had just shut down. He couldn't even explain it. He was usually way too paranoid to sleep around other people.

Keaton narrowed his eyes, leaning in. "So, that stuff you told us last night... you weren't just pulling our legs, right?"
