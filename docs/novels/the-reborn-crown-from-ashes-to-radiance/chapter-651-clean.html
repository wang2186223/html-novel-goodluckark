Chapter 651

"You sure you actually have a clue, Margot?" Agnes shot her a look, one brow spiking up. Pure shade.

Margot’s face went hot. She opened her mouth, but nothing came out. Total brain freeze.

"Drop it, Agnes," Ethan snapped from his wheels. "Seriously. I know who got her pregnant. I’m not some idiot raising another guy's mistake."

His eyes strayed. Over to Elara and Julian. God, they looked perfect. Cozy. The kind of couple that makes you want to throw up. A sharp, ugly knot tightened in his gut.

What if he’d never touched Seraphina? What if he’d just done what he was supposed to do and put a ring on Elara? Where would he be? Probably living the dream instead of this nightmare.

The sheer anger of it almost choked him. He used to be the golden child. The chosen heir to the Baker empire. Now? Bankrupt, broken, and bypassed. All because of one girl.

Elara was gone. And the girl he was shackled to—the one he’d once lusted after like crazy—now made him sick to his stomach. Seraphina had wrecked him. Period. Without her, his life wouldn't be this massive dumpster fire.

Clawing his way back into the company? Forget about it. They’d already given his desk away while he was in rehab. He’d begged his uncle, but the man just shrugged. "Get your legs working first, kid."

Weeks bled into months. Panic set in. He wasn't just benched; he was getting erased. Even his crappy backup gig at the branch office felt like it was slipping away.

And if Baker Group dumped him? He was blacklisted. Nobody in the city would hire him and risk pissing off Julian. He was radioactive.

"Oh, sweetie, don't be so naive," Agnes purred, her voice pure poison. "You're stuck in that chair. Meanwhile, certain people have no problem hitting on their own uncle-in-law. Who knows who else she's riding while you're not looking?"

She tossed her hair, playing the saint. "Hey, don't hate the messenger! I'm just trying to save you from looking like a fool."

Agnes was a certified bitch, and she owned it. Stirring the pot was her favorite hobby, especially if it meant making Margot’s life hell. Their misery was her dopamine hit.

Ethan didn't even have the energy to clap back. Instead, he burned a hole through Seraphina with his eyes. This was on her. If she hadn't crawled into his uncle's bed, Agnes wouldn't have this loaded gun to shoot at them.

Margot was boiling over. Yeah, she wanted her grandbaby safe, but she hated Seraphina for dragging their name through the dirt. The whole clan knew the dirty laundry. With Christmas around the corner, she was dreading the family dinners. The aunts and cousins would tear her apart. She’d skipped her bridge club for months, her husband was giving her the silent treatment, and the country club gossip was brutal.

*If only Elara was my daughter-in-law,* Margot thought, tasting copper. None of this crap would have happened.

"Seriously, Agnes, what is your problem with me?" Seraphina snapped, her voice cracking. What had she ever done to this woman? Sure, they weren't besties, but she’d always played nice.

Agnes sighed, loud and theatrical. "What did you do? Uh, reality check. I'm just stating facts. Stop playing the victim. You wrecked Callie, which means you wrecked me. I ride for Callie, and I'm not letting you turn this family into a joke."

She rolled her eyes so hard they almost got stuck. "Pro tip: if you don't want people talking trash, don't act trashy. Simple math, babe."

"Agnes, shut it!" Margot finally blew up. "It's almost Christmas. Are you seriously trying to start a war under this roof?"

Agnes shrugged, zipped her lips, and leaned back.

Meanwhile, Elara just sat there. Cool. Untouchable. Like the drama was happening in a completely different universe.
