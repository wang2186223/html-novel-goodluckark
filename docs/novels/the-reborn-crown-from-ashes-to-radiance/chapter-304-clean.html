Chapter 304

"Come on, Callie. It’s a tiny ask," Edgar begged, his eyes practically pleading. "Just do us this one solid, please?"

Elara stared right through him. Total blank face. She didn't say a word. In her head, Edgar was just another clone of the rest of these jerks.

"Elara, seriously, I’m losing my patience here!" Lysander barked, his voice cracking with irritation. "When we play nice, you say yes. Got it?"

"Or what?" Elara shot back, popping an eyebrow. "Is that a threat?"

Lysander ground his teeth, burning a hole through her with his eyes. But he had nothing. No leverage, no dirt. Zero.

The old, people-pleasing Elara was dead. She was done sucking up to them. And she definitely wasn't crashing on couches anymore. Hell, her new mansion made their place look like a trashy roadside motel.

"Go on. Try me." Elara’s voice didn't even shake. Her face was pure ice. "I’d love to see you actually scare me."

Cassian scowled. This chick was supposed to be easy. But with Julian Myers in her corner, she was basically bulletproof, doing whatever the hell she wanted.

"Don't get cocky, Elara. Actions have consequences, you know?" Cassian sneered. "What happens when Julian dumps your ass? You think you won't crawl back to us? We’re throwing you a lifeline here."

"She won't have to," a voice cut in from the door.

Julian walked in. The guy owned the room the second he stepped inside, making everyone else look like total nobodies.

He strolled straight over to Elara, radiating power. "Hey, babe. I'm home," he murmured, his eyes melting as he looked down at her.

Elara gave him a quick nod.

"I'm not going anywhere unless she kicks me out," Julian said, deadpan. "And even if she dumps me, she won't crawl to you guys. I'd hand her every single dime I own and walk away broke. She’d still be richer than all of you combined."

That shut Lysander and the crew up real fast. Their faces turned completely sour.

"Mr. Myers, seriously? Aren't you spoiling her rotten?" Lysander scowled. "Does she even deserve all that?"

"Hell yeah, she does." Julian let out a soft laugh, looking at Elara like she was his whole world. Then he whipped his head back to them, his eyes turning to daggers. "If she doesn't deserve it, nobody on this earth does."

"You're gonna regret this, Mr. Myers," Lysander pushed. "She’s got so much baggage!"

Julian smirked. "So what? I love her baggage."

Lysander choked on his own spit.

"Come on, Mr. Myers, the girl has zero empathy," Cassian chimed in with a greasy grin. "If she treats her own family like trash, she’ll do the same to you. What if you go broke or get hurt? She won't give a crap. She’ll freeze you out when you need her most!"

"She’s a snake! Sleep with a viper, and you're gonna get bitten!"

"Oh, really?" Julian sat down right next to Elara, kicked his legs crossed, and stared at Cassian like he was a clown. "Even if she’s a cold-hearted snake, I still want her."

Cassian just stared. The guy was officially out of his mind.

"Callie, look, just help us, alright?" Walter cut in, completely bypassing Julian to look at Elara. "Fine, don't help. But at least tell me—what's your deal with Caspian, huh?"
