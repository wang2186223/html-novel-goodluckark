Chapter 438

They had zero clue about her business empire. She never bragged, never dropped hints. Now, sitting across from her, they realized they didn't know this girl at all. She was a total stranger wearing their sister's face.

The sweet, quiet kid who used to tag along? Gone. Elara didn't give a damn about their validation anymore. She was running her own show now. Their brotherly love meant nothing to her—she’d dumped it in the trash and traded them in for a new set of brothers. Her warmth for them had iced over.

Lysander stared, his chest tight. He knew he’d screwed up big time, but his pride wouldn't let him say it. He wanted the old days back. But every time he opened his mouth to fix things, he ended up taking Coraline’s side and stabbing Elara in the back all over again.

Sure, he was in the wrong, but why couldn't she just get it? They were blood. She was supposed to love them, period. What was her issue anyway? Coraline was the one with nobody in her corner.

The three men trailed behind Elara back to the executive suite, their faces a messy mix of guilt and confusion.

Elara claimed a spot on the reception couch, kicking one leg over the other. Pure confidence. She locked her sharp eyes onto Walter. "So, where’s the paperwork?"

Walter scrambled to his feet. "Right! Yeah, I’ll get the legal team on the contract. But, uh, Callie, this stuff takes a minute, you know?"

"Fine," Elara said, cool as ice. "Take your time. I’ll do my part."

"Okay." Walter nodded, sucking in a breath. He sneaked a look at her. "Look, Callie... I’ve been thinking. I messed up. You’re my kid, and you should've had these shares ages ago. I know you’re mad, but hey, give your old man a break. Come over for dinner. Your room is waiting."

Elara let out a dry chuckle, sizing him up. "You sure about that? Won't I just ruin the vibe? Pretty sure I'm an eyesore to you guys."

"What? No way! You're my daughter, Callie!" Walter blurted out, hands waving. "Look, Thanksgiving is right around the corner. Bring Julian. We want you there."

She gave a lazy shrug. "Maybe."

Walter didn't push. He didn't want to piss her off and ruin the deal, so he hurried out to get the lawyers moving.

Edgar and Lysander just stood there, staring at her in the quiet room. Finally, Edgar cleared his throat. "Callie. I'm sorry."

Elara blinked, raising an eyebrow. "Come again?"

"I mean it. I'm sorry. I ignored you. Especially after you literally got me back on my feet with that surgery. I was wrong about you." He couldn't stop thinking about how she’d always had his back, while he’d tossed her aside the second Coraline whined.

Elara smirked, a bit bitter. "What, worried the stitches might pop? Relax. I took the Hippocratic Oath. It was just my job."
