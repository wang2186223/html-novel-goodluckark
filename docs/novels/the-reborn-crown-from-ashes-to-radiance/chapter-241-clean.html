Chapter 241

"Look, Thorne, Elara was the one babysitting," Lysander snapped, trying to talk some sense into the guy. "You can't just let her off the hook because you two are tight. And Coraline? No way. She doesn't do kidnappings. That's not her style."

Upton spun around, eyes cold. "Yeah? We'll see about that the second I get my boy back."

They hit the exit of the theme park just as Elara’s phone vibrated. She swiped answer. "Got it. Coming now." She hung up and grabbed Upton’s arm. "They got him. He’s at the hospital. Sounds like he’s in bad shape, totally freaked out. Move, now."

They shoved Coraline into the backseat and slammed the doors. Tires screeched. Cassian and his guys tailed them, weaving through traffic like maniacs, heading straight for the ER.

Inside the pediatric ward, Asher was already hooked up to monitors. A bunch of high-priced doctors stood around, whispering and looking totally lost. Julian stood right by the mattress, glaring down at the kid with a heavy scowl.

Jasper Reed caught Elara’s eye from the doorway and jogged over to spill the details. Honestly, Julian was Elara’s first call the second the kid vanished. The guy practically ran this town; if anyone had the juice to track a missing kid, it was him. Once Julian realized the boy was Elara’s godson, he didn't just send his goons—he went hunting himself. It didn't take him long to crack the case.

"They pumped him full of some sedative," Jasper muttered. "Locked him in some rotting, abandoned shack with a couple of hired thugs watching the door. Kid was terrified out of his mind. That's why he's so sick right now."

"I'm going in," Elara said. She pushed past the threshold. The medical team took one look at her face and backed off, clearing a path.

"Mommy!" Asher whimpered, his voice trembling. "My stomach... it hurts so bad."

"Hey, baby. I've got you. You're safe now," she whispered, forcing a soft smile. She grabbed his tiny wrist, checking his pulse. In one smooth motion, she whipped out a sleek silver case, slid out a few thin acupuncture needles, and tapped them into his belly with practiced ease.

Asher blinked, staring at her.

"Better?" Elara murmured.

The kid actually managed a tiny grin. "Yeah... it feels warm now. Thanks, Mommy."

He looked over at Julian. "You too, Daddy Julian! You're like, totally badass. Just like my real dad!"

Julian didn't even crack a smile. "Way cooler than him."

Asher just stared. Great. Another guy with a god complex.

Once the kid's breathing evened out, Elara looked up at Julian. "You get the bastards?"

"Yeah," Julian said, his eyes shifting over to Upton. Jasper had already filled him in on the drama.

Upton walked over to the bed, staring down at his son. Seeing the kid breathing easy, he let out a massive sigh, his shoulders dropping. "Asher," he said, his voice tight. "Who took you?"

Asher’s little finger pointed straight at the door, where two heavy-duty guards were pinning Coraline's arms. "Her! That mean lady put something over my mouth and made me sleep!" Asher yelled, glaring. "She's bad!"

Coraline went pale. She hadn't expected them to find the kid in an hour, let alone that the brat would actually point her out.

"No way! That's total bullshit!" Lysander exploded. "The kid's confused! Toddlers mix up faces all the time! Or maybe this is your doing, Elara? Did you brainwash him to frame Coraline? He worships the ground you walk on. He'll say whatever the hell you tell him to say!"
