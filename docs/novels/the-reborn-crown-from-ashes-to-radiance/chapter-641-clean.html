Lysander knew he had Elara wrapped around his finger. That car was proof. She was totally powerless against him. One word from him, and Marco's career would be history. He was 100% sure of it.

"Oh, my bad. You're my boss now? Funny, I don't recall seeing your name on half my paychecks."

Marco let out a dry laugh, shaking his head. "Dude, I've been in Callie's life for years. We aren't just work buddies; we're tight. And you? Sure, you share some DNA, but you don't know jack about her. Stop playing the sibling card. She could dump your ass tomorrow if she felt like it."

"And honestly, Lysander, I'm doing you a favor here. Don't touch the ride. It's not yours. You wreck it, you're screwed. You have any idea what this thing costs? One tiny ding and you're filing for bankruptcy, bro."

Marco's jaw tightened, his expression turning to ice. He'd tried to let the guy off easy, but since Lysander wanted to act like a clown, the kid gloves were coming off.

Big deal if he was Elara's brother. Besides, Marco knew the truth—they weren't even related by blood anyway.

"Pay? Why the hell would I pay?" Lysander snapped. "This is my car now. If I want to drive it off a bridge, that's my call, not yours!"

"Don't get cocky just because you're Callie's little helper and you get to hang out with her. You don't run my life, Marco!"

Lysander tuned him out completely, his ego flaring as he stood his ground like a stubborn idiot.

Marco just shook his head. Talking to this guy was like talking to a brick wall. He just wouldn't get it.

A crowd started to form, people muttering and stretching their necks to catch the drama. Everyone was dying to know: if Elara walked in right now, who would she back?

Cassian, another one of her "brothers," almost stepped in to talk some sense into Lysander. The guy's ego was out of control. Elara wasn't the pushover she used to be. She held all the cards now.

Plus, she had plenty of other brothers. He and Lysander weren't the VIPs they thought they were.

It felt like their little sister had been replaced by a stranger. She didn't belong to them anymore.

Then, a sharp, icy voice cut through the air. "Who said Marco was bossing you around?"

Heads snapped around. Elara was standing right there.

Lysander whipped around, his mouth open to argue, but he froze when he saw the three guys standing right behind her.

Rhys Kincaid, Keaton, and Finnian Shaw stood side-by-side, looking insanely hot in bespoke designer suits. They looked like straight-up movie stars. The whole room literally forgot to breathe.

Whispers erupted instantly.

"Holy crap, who are they?"

"Are you seeing this? They are gorgeous!"

"Wait, what's their deal with Elara?"

"Are they models? They look like they walked off a GQ cover!"

"Hold up. Is that Finnian Shaw? The guy from the news?"

"Oh my god, it is! And he's hanging out with Elara? Our boss is seriously connected. She's a total rockstar."
