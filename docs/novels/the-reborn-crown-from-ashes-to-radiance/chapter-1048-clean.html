Chapter 1048

"Damn, Doc, I should probably bow down right now," one old surgeon snorted, shaking his head.

"Seriously, you're unreal."

"Right? Half our age and she's already running circles around us. Guess the kids really are taking over."

The room buzzed with praise, but Elara blocked it out. She kept her eyes glued to the incision, working the final stretch. Minutes crawled. Then, she finally let out a breath she'd been holding. "Okay, vitals are solid. Close him up."

She backed off, lungs burning for fresh air. Another doc slid into her spot. He took his sweet time, but his needlework was clean.

They tied off the last thread and rolled the gurney out. The surgical crew spilled into the hallway, whispering like gossiping teenagers. "Man, if that was me, I would've botched it ten times over," one muttered. "How the hell did she do that?"

A second guy shook his head, totally dazed. "She didn't even sweat. It's insane. I've never seen anything like it."

"Well, Howell's breathing. That's what matters. Cleanest run I've ever witnessed."

"No kidding. That 'Miracle Doctor' hype is 100% real. I got more out of watching her for three hours than a decade of boring-ass seminars."

"And hey, she didn't even try to hide her tricks. No gatekeeping at all, even with skills like that."

"Yo, Dr. Jewell," one of them called out, grinning like a hopeful puppy. "If you ever want to teach or do a residency, hit us up first. Seriously, we'd sell our souls for your secrets."

Looking at these gray-haired legends—guys who literally wrote the textbooks—Elara felt a weird twitch of ego mixed with pure awkwardness. She forced a tight smile. "No students right now, guys. But thanks. Now, if you'll excuse me, I gotta run."

She hit the double doors and walked straight into a madhouse. Over a hundred of Victor's thugs packed the lobby shoulder-to-shoulder. Kaelen Steel was shoved into a corner, looking like he was about to start cracking skulls.

"Yo, Ms. Jewell! How's the boss?"

"Did he make it?"

"Is he gonna live?"

The crowd swarmed her, blocking the exit, throwing a wall of panicked faces in her way.

Elara raised her voice to cut through the noise. "He's fine. The surgery was a total success. Just keep him quiet and let him rest."

The room erupted.

"Hell yeah!"

"Thank God!"

"The boss is back!"

"Man, I can finally breathe!"

Right on cue, orderlies wheeled Victor out of recovery. The mob instantly pivoted, swarming his gurney with massive grins. In ten seconds flat, the lobby was empty.

Kaelen pushed through the leftover dust. "You good, boss? Everything go smooth?"

"Perfect," Elara said, her shoulders dropping. "Let me dump these scrubs, and let's get the hell out of here."

"You got it," Kaelen said.

Ten minutes later, they were cruising toward Myers Manor. But as they neared the gates, Kaelen slammed on the brakes. A human wall blocked the driveway: Walter, Beatrix, Edgar, and Lysander.

"What the hell do these parasites want?" Kaelen growled, gripping the wheel. "Do they have zero self-respect?"

Elara stared at them, her eyes ice-cold. "They want cash. Obviously." She popped the door and stepped out before he could stop her.

Her boots barely hit the asphalt before Beatrix lunged forward, sobbing hysterically. "Callie, please! We raised you! You were our girl for years! The family is going under, and everyone's blacklisted us. I'm begging you, just this once, save us!"

"Seriously, Callie," Lysander chimed in, his eyes wide and desperate. "You ate our food, slept under our roof. You can't just watch us drown, can you?"

Edgar just stood there, jaw clenched, silent as a tombstone.

Walter let out a ragged sigh, looking defeated. "Callie, we screwed up. We shouldn't have treated you like trash. Can you just... forgive us? We're sorry. For all of it."
