Chapter 223

Coraline’s eyes snapped wide. She locked onto Seraphina.

"Wait, this is perfect. If the kid is doomed anyway, why not get some mileage out of it?"

"Are you insane?" Seraphina growled, her face darkening. "How is this perfect? If I lose this baby, I lose my leverage. I’m screwed. How the hell do I turn that into a win?"

"Stop being stupid and taking the hit," Coraline said. "You’re getting hitched, right? So, find a fall guy. You didn't miscarry because of some medical issue. Someone did this to you. Picture it—your big day, and boom, you lose the baby because of some psycho. How do you think everyone reacts? What about Ethan and Margot? They’ll be wrecked. They'll be pissed. They’ll wrap you in bubble wrap and destroy whoever you point your finger at."

"Holy shit, you're right." Seraphina bolted up and grabbed Coraline’s hands. "Coraline, seriously, you’re a lifesaver. I’d be totally lost without you."

"Hey, I’ve always got your back," Coraline laughed. "But look, I gotta run. Got things to handle. Keep the pregnancy on the down-low for now. We need the perfect timing to pull this off."

Seraphina nodded, letting out a massive breath. "Got it."

The weight lifted off her chest. Her mood did a total 180. If this kid couldn't survive, it was going to work for its keep.

*Sorry, baby,* she thought. *You were doomed anyway. Might as well do Mommy one last favor.*

Seraphina strutted out of the hospital, her spirits high. Right at the exit, she almost slammed into Elara, who was walking in holding a medical kit.

Seraphina’s stomach dropped. She shoved her ultrasound paperwork behind her back, fast.

She glared at Elara. "What are you doing here?"

Elara stopped, giving her a cold once-over. "None of your business."

Seraphina rolled her eyes, scoffed, and pushed past her.

Elara watched her walk away, her mind working. Then she headed for the elevator, aiming for the top floor where Upton and Asher were staying.

The elevator doors slid open, and she ran straight into a wall of people: Lysander, Edgar, and Coraline. Elara’s eyes dropped to Edgar’s wheelchair.

Zero surprise.

But the three of them looked like they’d seen a ghost.

"What the hell are you doing here?" Lysander barked, instantly on edge.

Coraline gasped, playing dumb. "Callie, please tell me you didn't come here to gloat. Fine, you were right about Edgar's legs, but he's still your brother. Don't kick him while he's down."

Edgar narrowed his eyes, staring at Elara like he was trying to read her mind.

Lysander sneered. "You’re loving this, aren't you? Seeing your brother crippled probably made your whole damn week."

Elara didn't blink. Her face was a blank slate.

"You don't belong here," Lysander snapped. "Whatever game you're playing, drop it."

"Relax. I don't have time to trade insults with you." Elara looked at Edgar in the chair. No emotion. She used to lose sleep over him, burning the candle at both ends to find a cure, running herself ragged.

Now? She felt absolutely nothing. Just pure, cold peace.

"I'm not here for you anyway."

She walked right past them.
