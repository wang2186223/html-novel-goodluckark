Chapter 10

"Get the hell out."

Elara scanned the room. Walter first. Then her three brothers. Finally, her eyes locked onto Beatrix and Coraline, who were hovering on the stairs.

She looked over at the massive family portrait on the wall. What a joke. Every single face was up there—except hers.

They shot that photo a year after Coraline moved in. Coraline had locked Elara in a room that morning, so the rest of them just went to the studio and posed without her.

When she finally got out, they screamed at her for "running off." She tried to tell them what happened. Nobody gave a shit.

Coraline insisted on hanging the damn thing in the living room. So there it hung, a constant reminder that Elara didn't exist.

"I get it. I'm just dead weight to you guys," Elara said. "Well, I'm done. I'm never coming back. Oh, and by the way? Julian and I got hitched. He's my husband now."

"Jesus, Elara, stop acting so dramatic!" Edgar bolted upright. "Do you always have to ruin everything? Yeah, we treat Coraline great. Because she actually deserves it!"

Elara whipped around, her lips curling into a cold, nasty smirk. "Right. She's the golden child, and I'm the trash."

"Coraline fixed my leg! I'd be in a wheelchair without her!" Edgar snapped. "She literally saved Lysander's life. He'd be dead! And Cassian's big break in Hollywood? All Coraline! You should be thanking her! We appreciate her, but it's not like we starved you. Why do you always have to turn everything into a competition with her?"

Elara let out a dry laugh. "Don't sweat it. I'm officially dropping out of the race."

*She* was the one who actually saved them. She did all of it. But of course, nobody believed a word. In their minds, it had to be sweet, perfect Coraline. Not the evil, blood-related sister. Whatever. She was the odd one out anyway. Time to go.

Elara grabbed her bags, wheeled them toward the door, and walked out. She didn't look back once.

Beatrix grabbed her chest, gasping for air. "What a selfish little bitch! Good riddance. She's a curse anyway! Remember what the priest said when she was a baby? He said she'd ruin us! Honestly, it's a blessing she's gone. Coraline is our real lucky charm."

Edgar let out a heavy sigh. "She's completely lost her mind."

Lysander agreed. "Yeah, let her see how tough the real world is. Once she gets a reality check, she'll be begging to come home."

"Mom, please don't be mad at her," Coraline whimpered, putting on a perfect show. "Maybe I'm the problem. I shouldn't have crashed your lives. I took her place, so of course she hates me."

"Stop it, sweetie. This is on her, not you!" Beatrix said.

Edgar chimed in, "Yeah, Coraline, don't do that to yourself. You did nothing wrong."

...

Ethan finally rolled back into town that night. He'd been away on business and completely forgot their wedding was yesterday—until Coraline called to remind him.

He wasn't sweating it, though. Elara was obsessed with him. Even when he screwed up, she'd be the one apologizing and begging for his attention within twenty-four hours.

It was just a ceremony. She was so desperate to lock him down that she probably just made up some excuse to the guests and ran the whole wedding by herself anyway.

Ethan checked his watch. Five o'clock. Knowing Elara, she was probably dying to see him and already on her way over.
