"Yeah, Chloe's the move," Alaric said. He nodded, backing Julian up. If Julian wanted her, Alaric wasn't about to fight him on it. "Ethan's a mess, honestly. The kid just doesn't fit here."

"So, what's the play?" Julian asked. "We put him in some fake, easy job?" Look, Julian knew the deal. Ethan was Alaric's blood. If the old man wanted to baby his grandson, Julian would play ball—but Alaric had to say the word first.

Alaric shut that down with a head shake. "Nah, don't bother. If he sucks at the job, cut him loose. Why pay him to do nothing? He's dead weight."

Julian nodded.

"If he actually has some fire in him, let him hustle in the real world," Alaric said. "But if he just wants a free ride, then yeah, he's got no business being here."

You could see the letdown in Alaric's eyes. When Julian first brought Ethan in, Alaric hoped the kid would step up. Sure, he wouldn't be Julian, but he could've at least kept up. Instead, Ethan went totally off the rails in just a few months. Alaric was done covering for him. Julian didn't make calls based on vibes. If Julian picked Chloe, Chloe was the one. Ethan was out. Besides, Alaric had other grandkids. If the older one choked, the younger one could step up to the plate.

Whatever Ethan did next was on him. If he ruined his own life, tough luck.

"Don't sweat me," Alaric said. "Run it how you want. I gave you the keys to this place, and they're yours. Hell, if you want to hand it off to someone else down the road, go for it." Alaric was done micromanaging. Julian was the boss now. This was his call.

Julian nodded. "Got it. I'll handle it. But hey, if you ever have a problem with how I do things, tell me."

"Will do," Alaric said. "But I won't. I trust your gut, and I trust your word."

Julian and Elara hung out with the old man for the rest of the morning. They grabbed lunch, then headed out to the car.

Julian started the engine, pulled away from the estate, and glanced over at her. "Hey, back there, you said something about Ethan's kid. Like, is there really something wrong with the baby?"

Elara nodded. "Yeah. From what I saw, I'd say there's an eighty or ninety percent chance Seraphina's baby is going to have some serious issues."

Julian frowned, gripping the wheel. "Like what?"

"Could be mental, could be physical," Elara said. "Hard to say exactly, but the odds are terrible. If they don't get some real testing done, they're in for a nightmare."

Julian's face fell. "But they won't listen, right? They're just gonna push through anyway." He shook his head, pissed off. Bringing a kid into the world like that was just going to drag the whole family down, and the poor kid wouldn't even have a shot at a decent life.
