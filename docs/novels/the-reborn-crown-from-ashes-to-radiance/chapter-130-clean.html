Chapter 130

Ethan popped into Elara’s head. God, what a joke. She’d brought up Kodiak a million times, and he’d just shrugged it off. Didn't lift a finger to help her search. Not once.

Talk about a reality check. Side-by-side, the difference was night and day. It finally clicked.

She leaned in close to Julian, her voice dropping to a whisper. "Hey. Thanks."

...

Right then, Julian’s phone buzzed. Work emergency. He had to haul ass back to the office.

With him gone, Elara figured she’d show Kodiak around the massive estate.

Kaelen Steel and the security detail couldn't keep their eyes off the wolf. They trailed behind, peppering her with questions.

"He’s a lab project," Elara explained, keeping it simple. "Born a wolf, but his brain is basically supercomputer level."

"Seriously, you know those memes about dogs taking the SATs? Yeah, this guy could actually ace them."

"No kidding?" Kaelen stared at the beast, totally blown away.

"For real," Elara said, nodding. "Just treat him like one of the guys."

Kaelen swallowed hard, got serious, and stuck his hand out. "Hey, Kodiak. Kaelen Steel. I run this place for Mr. Myers. Need anything at all, I'm your guy."

*Awooo!* Kodiak let out a soft bark, bobbed his head, and offered a massive paw. A proper, formal handshake.

The paw was huge, but man, it was so soft. Kaelen’s inner animal lover totally melted.

Kaelen glanced back at the wolf. "So, uh, the boss keeps some pretty wild pets back there. Is Kodiak gonna freak if he sees a tiger or something?"

Elara let out a soft laugh.

Kodiak’s ears perked straight up. He let out a couple of sharp barks.

"He says he wants to meet them," Elara translated. "We cool to go look?"

"Sure, why not." Kaelen led the way toward the massive enclosures in the backyard.

Julian owned a pitch-black stallion and a gorgeous, massive white tiger named Frost. Both were absolute beasts.

Normally, those two were total nightmares to deal with. But as the group walked up, Kaelen noticed they were weirdly quiet. No growling, no stomping.

"Aw, Frost is gorgeous," Elara said, eyeing the tiger. "Can we let her out of the cage?"

Kaelen paused, rubbing the back of his neck. "Look, she won't attack unless the boss says so, but she doesn't know you. I don't want her giving you a heart attack."

"Relax. She won't touch me," Elara promised.

Kaelen sighed, popped the latch on Frost's gate, and let the black stallion loose too.

Kaelen’s jaw dropped. Frost trotted right up to Elara like they were old college buddies, rubbing her massive head against Elara's hand. She was basically a giant, purring house cat.

But the real shocker? Frost and the stallion both bowed their heads to Kodiak. Total submission. They knew who the boss was.

*Woof, woof!* Kodiak reared up on his back legs and literally patted the tiger and the horse on their heads. Like, *good boy, good girl.*

Kaelen stood there, mouth wide open.

The guards behind him went stiff, hands hovering over their holsters, waiting for a bloodbath.

Except nothing happened. The tiger and the stallion didn't even twitch.

These apex predators, usually proud as hell, were suddenly acting like trained puppies.

"Guess you don't need to lock them up anymore," Elara teased. "Looks like they just elected a new pack leader."

Kaelen couldn't even find his voice.

It wasn't just the wolf, either. Kaelen watched these wild, untouchable beasts practically melting under Elara's touch. They were totally under her spell.
