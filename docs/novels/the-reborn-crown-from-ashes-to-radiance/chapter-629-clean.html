Chapter 629

Malachi stared down at the bodies littered across the floor. "Some chick tosses a handful of dust, and my guys just fold? You’re telling me this crap isn't poison?"

He’d never seen his top-tier security detail drop like a bunch of cheap folding chairs. It looked like a straight-up assassination. I mean, what else makes a whole squad collapse in two seconds?

"Uh, doesn't look like it," the doc muttered, sounding totally unconvinced himself. He dropped to his knees, checking pulses, then shook his head. "Nah. They’re just snoring. Dead to the world, but, like, totally fine."

Malachi cocked an eyebrow. "No poison? Damn. That’s some high-grade stuff. Beats the hell out of hospital anesthesia."

The doc nodded. "Yeah, whoever made this skipped the lethal stuff. It’s basically a super-powered roofie."

"So they're not gonna die?" Malachi felt a weird spark of interest. This Elara girl was something else. She’d just put his elite guards to bed like they’d chugged a keg at a frat house.

"Yeah, they'll live," the doc said. "But, man, it’s freezing in here. They lie on this tile all night, they’re gonna wake up with pneumonia. We should probably get some blankets on them."

Malachi shot him a look. "So get to it."

The doc blinked, startled, but scrambled to the closet and started hauling out spare quilts.

By then, Elara was already back at her place. She pulled the tiny glass vial out of her pocket. It was her own custom blend—basically a heavy-duty tranquilizer on steroids. One puff of this stuff, and you were down harder than a guy after a massive Thanksgiving dinner.

She’d only packed it today in case Edgar’s surgery went sideways. She never figured she’d have to use it to put Malachi’s goons to sleep.

The second she walked through the door, she called for Kaelen Steel. Kaelen had left a couple of lookouts at the hospital to watch the Jewell family, but he’d already headed back to the Myers mansion.

Elara sat down right across from him. Her face was dead serious.

"Kaelen, I need some straight answers."

Her voice was so cold it gave Kaelen a mini panic attack.

"Sure, ma'am. Whatever you need, just say the word."

He’d never seen her look this intense before. It was kind of scary.

"Tell me about Julian’s dad. What’s his deal?"

She knew Kaelen had been Julian’s right-hand man forever, so he had to know the real dirt.

"His dad?" Kaelen blinked, totally thrown off. "Where is this coming from?"

"Alaric told me the guy is living large overseas. Super rich, but apparently his youngest kid is really sick." Elara leaned forward, eyes locked on him. "What’s the vibe between Julian and his old man?"

Kaelen hesitated, rubbing his neck. "Honestly? Julian never talks about him. Sure, the guy’s his biological father, but he literally tried to erase Julian just to protect his own wallet and status."

Kaelen’s jaw clenched hard at the thought.

"So there's really nothing there? Just pure hate?" Elara pushed.

"None. Zero," Kaelen said. "He acts like the guy doesn't exist. But I’ve been around Julian long enough to see through it." He looked her dead in the eye. "Trust me. He absolutely despises the man."
