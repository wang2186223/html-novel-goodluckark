Chapter 279

Three phones, three pairs of eyes, zero blinking. The Jewell brothers tracked the live feed like hawks.

Is she freaking nuts? That was the first thought. Any sane person would hug the exit, ready to sprint the second those gators snapped. But nope. Elara just sauntered straight into the danger zone, practically begging for a death wish. She looked like a trainwreck waiting to happen, wearing stuff that practically screamed *eat me*. They braced for the bloodbath.

Instead? Not a scratch. Not even a single splash of swamp water on her clothes.

Sure, the beasts lunged. But they got absolutely wrecked. Elara literally punted them like damn soccer balls, sending multi-hundred-pound reptiles airborne.

Cassian aggressively rubbed his eyelids. *No way. My eyes are playing tricks on me.* Where the hell did she get that kind of freakish power? Was she secretly some Marvel character?

"Tell me you guys saw that," Cassian stammered, his jaw hitting the floor. "Edgar? Lysander? Since when is she a tank?"

Those weren't baby lizards; they were massive, scaly monsters. Put a pro MMA fighter in there, and he’d get ripped to shreds. But Elara just cleared the room solo. It made no sense. Who was the crazy one here—her, or them?

Lysander stared at his screen, his brain short-circuiting. "Yeah. I saw it," he finally muttered. This was not the Elara he knew.

Edgar’s forehead creased. "Wait, Callie never said a word about her being some powerhouse!"

"Oh, just like she forgot to mention she’s tight with Upton?" Lysander spat, rolling his eyes. "Turns out our dear little sister has a ton of dirty little secrets."

Cassian squinted, his voice turning cold. "Please. She never saw us as family anyway. And big deal, she can throw a punch. What’s she gonna do, join the Olympics? Muscle doesn't pay the bills."

"Exactly," Lysander sneered, his shock fading back into pure contempt. "So she can play the banjo, play chess, and kick a gator. Big whoop. Can she run a business meeting? Nope. She'd tank the company in five minutes."

Edgar nodded along. "Yeah, totally useless real-world skills."

They all shook their heads. Elara wasn't even in the same league as Coraline. Sure, Coraline couldn't wrestle reptiles, but she knew how to run an empire and work a room. She was Capitalton's darling, everyone's favorite socialite. Elara? People hated her. Hell, even Ethan—her boyfriend of years—dumped her ass for Seraphina Vale. That told you everything you needed to know. Elara was a loser.

"Uh, guys? The internet is dragging us," Cassian hissed, his thumb flying across his screen. "They're calling us out for hating on her. They think she's some kind of god." He threw his hands up. "She’s not even a Jewell anymore! Why is she still ruining our lives?"

"Then she needs to drop our last name," Lysander growled. "Whatever. We'll set the record straight. Her little circus tricks don't mean jack. No college degree, no real career, zero business sense. We're speaking facts here."

"Totally. Zero marketable skills."

Cassian let out a dry laugh. "Seriously, unless she's planning to wrestle bears for a living, who cares? She’s never done a damn thing for this family. Coraline is the one who got me my big acting gig. Elara doesn't have the brains, the network, or the class. She's got nothing on Coraline."
