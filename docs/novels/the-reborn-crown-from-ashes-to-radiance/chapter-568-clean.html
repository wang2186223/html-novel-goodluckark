Chapter 568

"Seriously, people would literally kill to be in your shoes right now," the chief said, nudging her shoulder. "Get ready to watch a straight-up miracle go down." He cracked a quick joke, then steered Elara down the hallway.

They didn't make it far before Seraphina blocked their path. She was clutching a plastic lunchbox, her lips curling into a wicked, knowing smirk the second she locked eyes with Elara.

"Well, look who it is! Hey, Aunt Callie," Seraphina chirped, her eyes gleaming with some secret joke. "What's a girl like you doing in a place like this? Visiting Ethan?" She paused, tapping her chin like she was plotting something. "He's actually right in there. Total drama queen, crying about wanting to apologize. Trust me, you're exactly the medicine he needs."

Elara opened her mouth to shut it down, but Seraphina didn't give her the chance. She grabbed Elara's arm and hauled her toward the door.

The chief let out a heavy sigh. "Alright, I'm out. Catch you later, Elara. Just find your own way back."

"Will do, thanks," Elara said, tossing a quick nod over her shoulder.

He trudged off, looking totally bummed. He'd been dying to rope her into a quick game of chess, but that plan was officially dead.

Inside the room, Ethan was sprawled across the mattress. Both of his legs were wrapped in massive plaster casts, but honestly, the guy looked way too energetic for someone who'd just been wrecked.

The second the door clicked open, he started barking. "Seraphina! Where the hell have you been? Trying to starve me to death? This is all on you, damn it. If you hadn't screwed up, I wouldn't be stuck in this stupid bed!"

He cut himself off mid-shout. His head snapped around, and his jaw dropped when he saw Elara standing there, watching him with a freezing stare.

"Callie?" The sudden spark of hope in his eyes vanished, replaced by pure, unadulterated panic. He looked like a kid caught red-handed with a fistful of stolen candy.

"Callie... what are you doing here?" his voice cracked, dropping to a shaky whisper.

"Just dropping by to see if you'd finally kicked the bucket," Elara said, her eyes scanning his casts. "Bummer. Looks like I've got to wait. What a letdown."

Ethan's face flushed a deep, ugly red, but he kept his mouth shut.

Seraphina snickered. "Oh, don't worry, Aunt Callie. His legs might be trash, but the rest of him is fully functional—especially his massive ego." She shot Ethan a dirty look. "Besides, he's totally helpless now. I heard he treated you like garbage. Here's your chance to get some payback!"

"Are you freaking kidding me, Seraphina?" Ethan snapped, his voice cracking. "Whose side are you even on? Shut the hell up!" He glared daggers at her, but Seraphina just rolled her shoulders and shoved the lunchbox into Elara's hands.

"Gotta run, bathroom emergency!" Seraphina squealed, clutching her stomach. "Be a doll and feed him for me?" Before Elara could dump it back on her, she bolted down the hall.

Elara looked down at the box. Inside were a couple of sad-looking turkey sandwiches and a bag of chips. She wrinkled her nose in disgust and tossed the whole thing onto the bedside table.

Ethan scrambled to speak, his voice dripping with desperation. "Callie, look, I messed up. Seriously. I'm so sorry for everything. I should've never treated you like that. But honestly? It was all Seraphina's idea! She's the one who talked me into it!" He kept babbling, desperately trying to pass the buck.

Right outside the door, Seraphina had crept back. She held up her phone, quietly snapping a bunch of photos through the glass. She got a handful of solid shots before slipping away, a massive grin plastered on her face.

But Elara's ears were too sharp. She caught the faint, distinct click of the shutter, even if anyone else would've missed it. She knew exactly what kind of game Seraphina was playing.
