Chapter 810

"Screw that," someone muttered. "We don't give a crap about your family feud. Is this place going under or what?"

All eyes locked onto Elara. They looked desperate. Like she was their last lifeline.

Elara let out a soft, easy smile. "Look, guys, I appreciate the love. Seriously. But Coraline’s got a point. I’m officially out of the Jewell family tree. I don't belong in this room anymore."

She shrugged. "Besides, the cat’s out of the bag. I already dumped my shares. I think a few of you already did the math."

"But you guys trusted me," she continued, leaning against the table. "So I wanted to look you in the eye and tell you myself: I’m walking away. Before I pack up, though, I’m giving you a full rundown of where we stand."

She cut a look at Walter and his little clique. "I’ll be out of your hair in a minute. Just let me finish this first. Like you all said—this is a business meeting. We can do the family soap opera later."

Walter looked like he’d just sucked on a raw lemon. The room was dead silent, and the message was loud and clear: nobody was backing the Jewells. They wanted Elara.

It burned. Walter had sweated with these people for decades. They’d gone through hell together. Now? They were tossing him aside for a girl who’d barely been here five minutes.

His chest tightened with pure, hot rage. Was he really that useless? Did all those years mean absolutely nothing to them?

Elara clicked onto the next slide. She started rattling off the hard data—the cash flow, her growth strategies, the exact blueprint to keep the ship afloat.

Walter and his cronies didn't hear a damn word. They just sat there, stewing over the old man’s will. All they cared about was clawing back their inheritance.

"Callie, come on, don't do this!" a manager blurted out. "We’re dead in the water without you!"

"Yeah, the roadmap is gold, but you're the only one who can actually run it!"

"If you walk, we’re totally screwed!"

The room erupted into a chorus of begging. Elara just shook her head, her expression tight.

"I’m sorry," she said, her voice quiet but firm. "It's a done deal."

"Seriously? No wiggle room?"

"Man, you’re the only real boss we recognize!"

"Who cares about the shares? We’ll pool our cash and buy you back in!"

"Honestly, those clowns shouldn't even be allowed in the building..."

A senior VP glared daggers at Walter. "Walter, do you even care if this company survives? You and your family literally just chased Callie out the door!"

"We were finally making money, and you went and blew it!"

"You complete moron! You can't even tell which of your daughters actually has a brain!"

"Totally blind!"

Walter’s face flushed a nasty, violent red.

"This is my damn company!" he barked, slamming his hand down. "Yeah, maybe I don't own the majority anymore, but the sign on the building still says Jewell! I’ll hand it to whoever I want! Elara’s a parasite. She’s here to bleed us dry!"

He sneered at her. "You’re real good at playing the crowd, aren't you, Elara? Is that how you played Alistair to get his cash?"

The heavy double doors of the conference room swung open. A squad of uniformed cops marched in, boots clicking on the hardwood.

"Which one of you is Elara?" the lead officer asked.

"Her!" Coraline shrieked, jumping out of her chair and pointing a shaking finger. "That's the bitch! She’s a total fake! She’s been playing dress-up as a Jewell! I bet she murdered the real Elara and stole her life! She ripped our family off for millions! She even poisoned Alistair!"
