Chapter 922

"Holy shit!"

"No way a girl that young can down liquor like that."

"Mad respect, seriously."

The room buzzed, everyone crowding in, hyping Elara up. She was the absolute center of attention.

Elara didn't even blink. She just took another casual sip, leaving Coraline in the dust. Coraline was basically choking now, her face screwed up in pure misery with every single gulp.

"Tap out," Elara said, her voice deadpan. "Seriously, stop. Keep this up and you're looking at alcohol poisoning. Or worse, if you're unlucky."

"Are you wishing death on me or something?" Coraline wobbled, trying to take a step, throwing a murderous look at Elara. Her eyes burned with pure rage, her jaw clenched so tight it looked like it might snap.

Elara just shrugged. "Think whatever you want. Keep drinking if you want to, but I'm barely warmed up. How much more you can take? Honestly, no clue."

Jett stepped up, looking worried. Eleanor Vance rushed over to grab Coraline.

"Coraline, drop it. You lost, just admit it," Jett said, keeping his voice low and gentle. "Don't try to be a hero. Some people just have a stomach made of steel. There's zero shame in not keeping pace with Ms. Jewell."

"He's right, sweetie. Don't push it," her mom chimed in, panic creeping into her voice. "You're going to end up in the ER."

The crowd realized the show was over. The hype died down, and people started shaking their heads. But then some guy yelled over to Elara, "Hey, Ms. Jewell, you got more in you? Show us what else you can do—blow our minds!"

"Yeah, for real, you drank a ton. You sure you're not faking it?"

"Wait, Ms. Jewell, you're not even buzzed?"

"Can we test you?"

Some guy started throwing brain teasers and logic puzzles at her. Elara knocked them out of the park, boom, boom, boom, without a single pause. No sweat. She wasn't even trying. For her, this was just a regular Tuesday night.

"Damn!"

"What a beast!"

"Honestly, most guys here would be under the table by now."

"No kidding. Going up against Elara is like trying to drink the ocean dry. Not worth dying over."

A few nervous chuckles went around, and the crowd backed off. Nobody had ever seen a liver like hers. She was playing a whole different game.

Coraline finally collapsed. It was over. She was completely wasted, eyes glassy, slumping back with a totally blank, spaced-out look.

Just then, Ms. White walked into the room. She expected to find Elara passed out, but instead, Coraline was the one who could barely stand. Ms. White’s eyes narrowed. Damn it. Coraline had botched the job.

Looks like she’d have to handle this herself.

She strutted over to Elara, her face a mask of cold confidence. "Elara," she said, her voice dripping with ice. "Are we sure everything's legit here? Coraline is totally trashed, and you're standing there looking like you just had water. You didn't, like, spike your own drinks with something to stay sober, did you?"

"How about you and me go head-to-head? I'll drink from this side, you drink from that side. What do you say?" Ms. White's eyes flashed, throwing down the gauntlet.

Elara grinned, completely unfazed. "Sure. Why not?"

As she spoke, Elara's eyes darted down to Ms. White's hand. In a split second, she caught the sneaky little flick of her fingers as Ms. White slipped something into the cups on her side of the table.

*Amateur hour,* Elara thought, letting out a quiet, amused chuckle. If this woman thought she was being clever, she had no idea who she was messing with. Just from the scent alone, Elara knew exactly what kind of cheap, dirty trick she was trying to pull.
