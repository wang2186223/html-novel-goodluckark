Chapter 853

Julian laced his fingers through Elara’s, pulling her back inside.

He set up the chessboard with Alaric and patted the seat right next to him, keeping Elara close. Between moves, he peeled grapes, popping them past her lips. It was pure, shameless PDA—a loud, clear message to the room that she was his whole world.

Seraphina glared from the sidelines, her expression souring by the second.

Ethan’s eyes kept drifting to Elara too, regret eating him alive. He knew he was totally out of the running, but his brain wouldn't stop spinning. Was there any universe where she’d take him back?

One look at them together, though, and that fantasy died. It was just a pathetic pipe dream.

He was stuck. He couldn't even pull the trigger on divorcing Seraphina. His only play now was waiting for her kid to pop out. If the Bakers actually cared about the baby, maybe Julian would throw him a bone and let him back into the Baker Group. Just for the kid's sake.

His current gig working for his buddy was a total drag—exhausting, thankless, and he was sick of being treated like a doormat.

God, he missed the Baker Group. Back then, people actually showed him some damn respect. He’d been the golden boy, the guy everyone wanted to please.

Now? He was basically dirt. And the people he’d pissed off in his glory days? They were lining up to kick him while he was face-down in the mud.

So Ethan and Seraphina just sat there, drowning in their own bitter thoughts, their eyes constantly darting back to Elara.

Elara, though, was glowing. She kept up a light banter with Alaric, then leaned in to whisper jokes with Julian, the two of them locked in their own little world.

Eventually, Alaric looked up from his knight, flashing Elara a grin. "So, Callie, when are you and Julian gonna make me a great-grandkid, huh?"

"Wait, what?" Elara blinked, totally caught off guard. Her eyes shot straight to Julian.

Julian didn't miss a beat. "We're just letting nature take its course, Grandpa."

Alaric gave a loud snort but let it slide.

Two games later, Alaric threw his hands up and stretched. "Okay, that's it for me. I'm wiped out."

"Go hit the sack then," Julian said, zero coddling.

It was late as hell anyway. Everyone packed up and headed out of the old family house.

During the quiet drive back, Elara kept stealing glances at Julian. He hadn't said a word about the baby talk, and she was totally overthinking it.

Did he actually want kids with her? Or was he just dodging?

Her mind drifted to that locked spare room she’d never stepped foot in. And the fact that Julian still hadn't told her who really held his heart.

Like, yeah, sometimes he acted like he was head over heels.

But other times? She felt totally lost.

She wanted to just ask him, but she figured if he wanted to open up, he would’ve done it by now. And he hadn't. Not once.

"Hey, you're quiet. What's up?" Julian asked, catching her mood. He glanced over at her.

"Oh, nothing," Elara said, shaking her head.

"You sure?" Julian raised an eyebrow. "If something's bugging you, just spit it out. Did Ethan say something?"

"No, seriously, it's not him," Elara lied, squeezing out a smile.

Julian stared at her for a second, then reached over, gently tucking a loose strand of hair behind her ear. "Look, you can talk to me about anything, okay? Don't go bottling things up. Callie, I've got your back. I want you happy, and I don't want secrets between us. Period."

Elara stared at him, bit her lip, and finally let it out. "So... what Grandpa said. What did you think about that?"

"Huh?" Julian looked blank for a beat. Then the lightbulb went off. "Oh. You mean... the baby stuff?"

Julian locked eyes with her, his gaze turning serious. "Is that what you want, Callie? You want us to have a kid?"
