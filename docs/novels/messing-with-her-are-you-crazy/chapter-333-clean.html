Chapter 333

Black Mirage clung pathetically to the tree trunk, his grim gaze shifting to Nicholas.

When he saw the man's face, his pupils contracted sharply, and the savage expression on his face froze.

"You... It's you..."

His eyes widened as he looked from Nicholas to Serena and back again.

A flash of almost desperate madness crossed his eyes.

He suddenly burst out laughing. "Hahaha, you two... you actually got together! Do... do you know who she is?"

He pointed at Serena, looking at Nicholas.

The man's cool, almost ethereal face was impassive under the cold moonlight.

His long, captivating eyes held a hint of a chill, but his thin lips curved into a smile. "Oh? And who might she be?"

Black Mirage's laugh grew more savage. "You really don't know..."

"Her? Isn't she just my delicate fiancée?" Nicholas's low, lazy voice held a hint of affectionate amusement.

His voice wasn't loud, but it cut off Black Mirage's laughter abruptly. He stared at Nicholas as if he'd seen a ghost.

Nicholas smiled again and held out a hand to Serena. "My lady, time to end this."

Serena was speechless.

She glanced at Nicholas.

Under the moonlight, his sparkling, captivating eyes seemed to shimmer with scattered light, giving him the otherworldly air of a spirit.

He was utterly bewitching.

Finally, Serena's gaze landed on the hand he held out to her.

She paid no mind to this man who was preening like a peacock at a time like this.

Nor did she ask about his relationship with Black Mirage, or what Black Mirage's words had meant.

She had always known Nicholas had another identity, and she wasn't surprised that Black Mirage knew him.

Who didn't have secrets?

He didn't ask about hers. She didn't ask about his. That's how they coexisted peacefully.

Serena slapped away the hand Nicholas offered her, and with a swift movement, charged straight for Black Mirage.

She couldn't be bothered to waste any more time on him.

Close the net, finish the job, and get some sleep!

Nicholas chuckled as he looked down at his rejected hand, then with a move of his own, he followed right after her.
