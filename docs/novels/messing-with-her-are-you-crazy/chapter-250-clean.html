"Besides... Kurobara Shroud is vicious and has no morals. If I keep hiding and they can't find me, they might shift their target to the people around me."

Images of her family members from the Lancaster family flashed through Serena's mind.

Her eyes darkened as her slender fingers tapped on the tabletop. "Deploy more people to my family's home. Protect everyone in the Lancaster family in secret."

Although the Lancaster family's security system was top-notch and their bodyguards were the elite of the elite, there were no guarantees when facing unhinged psychos like the members of Siphon and Kurobara Shroud, who had no morals and used twisted methods.

Her family was her bottom line. She would not tolerate any harm coming to them.

Jaxon knew that while the boss appeared cool and distant, earning her the title of "the demon" in the Gray Zone, she was actually the most loyal and sentimental person he knew.

The herb she had come to the Gray Zone to pick all those years ago was for Old Mrs. Wynn.

This was one of the reasons they were willing to follow her to the death.

"For the time being, I'll find a place to stay outside," Serena said after a moment's thought. "Have the 'Black Vipers' move into the Lancaster estate. They'll stand guard in shifts, 24/7. Report any activity immediately. If necessary, use any means to prioritize my family's safety."
