Now, seeing them visit in person with apologetic smiles, Marianne felt a surge of vindication and was secretly thrilled.

Mrs. Cole maintained her graceful, high-society smile, acting as if there had never been any unpleasantness between the two families. “Marianne, our families have a long-standing marriage agreement...
