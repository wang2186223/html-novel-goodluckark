But right now.

There was no way in hell Isabella was going to admit that.

She had no choice but to bite the bullet and maintain her flawless genius persona, forcing a stiff smile. "The algorithm is... certainly quite complex."

She offered a highly ambiguous answer. "It's entirely possible that Serena just happened to have researched this specific niche before. As for that parameter... it was incredibly obscure. I didn't want to boldly point it out the way she did, for fear of embarrassing Professor Warren."

"Honestly, Professor Warren was likely just giving the principal some face by offering her an easy out, since that problem has always been heavily debated anyway."

The other students exchanged complicated looks, deeply unsure if they should buy Isabella's explanation.

But Bianca immediately latched onto it, gritting her teeth in agreement. "It was pure, blind luck. I bet the principal forced Professor Warren to put on this little theatrical performance just to make her look good."

"She's a country bumpkin who didn't even graduate high school. How could she possibly understand any of this? If she was actually that brilliant, why didn't she take the entrance exams? Why does she always act so shady and avoid the question whenever someone brings up her grades?"

"Hmph. The principal is really bending over backwards just to make us accept her."

While her explanation was technically plausible.

Compared to accepting the reality that a lazy slacker was actually a once-in-a- generation genius, they were far more eager to believe it was a rigged stunt.

After all, real life wasn't some dramatic movie.

People didn't just transform overnight.

And more importantly...

For a room full of apex prodigies.

Admitting that someone else was vastly superior to them-especially someone they had openly mocked and despised-was a pill they simply refused to swallow.

It was a direct insult to their own intelligence.

But, even so...

As much as they desperately wanted to believe Bianca's theory.

The way they looked at Serena had undeniably shifted.

Meanwhile, the news of Serena utterly dominating the notoriously hot-tempered Professor Warren spread through Marington College like wildfire.

The other professors were burning with curiosity.

What kind of student could possess the sheer talent required to make Old Man Warren lose his absolute mind with excitement?

And so, during the afternoon's cross-disciplinary lecture on Chinese Medicine and Biopharmaceuticals.

Professor Lewis walked into the classroom holding a sealed, transparent petri dish. The second she walked in, her eyes immediately locked onto Serena.

"I brought something incredibly special for you all today," Professor Lewis said setting the dish down carefully on the podium. "Come on up. Take a close look."

The students instantly swarmed the front of the room.

The only exception was Serena, who remained slumped in her chair, staring blankly out the window, completely unbothered.

Professor Lewis glanced at her, then

smiled warmly at the class. "This is a mutated strain discovered deep within a tropical rainforest. It is currently entirely unrecorded on the commercial market, and there are only three known specimens in the entire world."

"Can anyone tell me what this is? What its medicinal properties are? And most importantly, how do we extract its active compounds?"

The students craned their necks, studying the plant with intense scrutiny.

But no one had an answer.

It was far too rare, completely undocumented in standard texts.

They simply couldn't identify it.

Professor Lewis smiled and looked

at Bianca. "Bianca, Doyle Pharmaceuticals is one of the top pharmaceutical empires in Elyndor. I imagine you must have at least some insight into this specimen, correct?"

Bianca was the eldest daughter of the Doyle family.

Her family's entire legacy was built on pharmaceuticals.

She had practically been raised swimming in medical texts. This was undeniably her domain.
