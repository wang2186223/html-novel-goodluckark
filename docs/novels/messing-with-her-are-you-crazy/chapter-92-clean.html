Looking at Mr. Langford's fawning behavior, Isabella felt a bitter taste rise in her throat. The last shred of hope she'd been clinging to crumbled in that instant.

Her carefully orchestrated plan to "expose Serena's true colors" had instead become the stepping stone for Serena to shine even brighter and win more of the family's affection!

It made all of her own actions look like those of a complete fool!
