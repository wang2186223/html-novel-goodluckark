Calliope had no idea what was going on with Archie. As soon as Mavis showed up, Calliope started chatting with her.

Apparently, Mavis had originally made plans because of Uriah Stewart, so it was actually Uriah who'd bailed.

Calliope wanted to ask what happened, but Mavis didn't seem eager to talk about it, so she let it go. She knew there was some complicated history between Mavis and Uriah, but Uriah was just so uptight that things always ended up messier than they should be.

She could guess, though: Uriah had probably wanted to invite Mavis, his protégé, to his family's dinner or something like that. But then, worrying about appearances or whatever would raise eyebrows back home, he'd chickened out.
