Gideon didn't respond to his friends. He just kept watching, growing more and more astonished with each clip. Despite having been with Calliope for years, he had never known she knew all this.

But now it was clear she was anything but ordinary. She seemed to possess an endless array of talents.

Gideon's emotions were a whirlwind. Every move Calliope made was like watching a star shine brightly.

After piecing the story together, he realized that she was attending this perilous party to rescue Silas, who Yara had detained. Everyone said Calliope must really love Silas, considering she would jump into an alligator pit for him.

When Gideon read these comments, a knot tightened in his chest, and he felt a wave of disbelief crash over him.
