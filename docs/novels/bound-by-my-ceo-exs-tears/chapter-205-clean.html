NovelVibe

Chapter 208

I’m running on empty. Seriously, I adore Asher, but I just want this damn party over so I can crash. Planning this whole gig kept me busy for a minute, but it didn't stop my brain from spinning. I’m still a total mess. One look at Declan and my mind goes into overdrive. Do I take him back? Yeah, I love the guy. But can I actually move past the hell he put me through? I don't know.

I shake it off and whip out my phone. I text Eleanor: *Yeah, bring Aurora.* Obviously, Corbin’s coming. So are Declan’s folks and Eleanor. They’ve never missed one of Asher’s birthdays. I can't freeze them out just because of my own drama. That’s petty, and honestly, Asher wants them here. My phone buzzes instantly. *See you in thirty,* Eleanor texts back. Call me childish, but I want to see how Declan reacts. I need to know if he’s still carrying a torch for Aurora.

"Mom!" Asher yells, nearly making me jump out of my skin.

"What's up, bud?" I spin around. He’s wearing this massive, goofy grin. Just like that, my anxiety evaporates. Sure, baby Aurora is still with Declan and Orion right now, but those two kids are my entire world. Even if Declan and I are totally done, having my babies is enough.

"Is it party time?" He’s literally bouncing off the walls.

I can't help but laugh. "Not quite," I say. "Give it a few minutes. People will start showing up soon, and I'll need my big helper at the door."

He nods so hard I think his head might fall off. The kid is practically glowing. "Deal. Shout when they're here!"

I give him a nod, and he bolts up the stairs, mumbling something about making sure he looks "super handsome." I chuckle, a genuine spark of warmth hitting my chest.

"Lyra!"

I turn. Mom and Dad are walking through the front door. I started calling them that again after I grabbed baby Aurora the other day. It just clicked. They loved it, too. Edmund actually told me he’d seriously missed hearing me say "Dad."

"Hey, Mom. Hey, Dad," I say, throwing my arms around them.

"We're early! Didn't want to miss a second," Mom squeals, practically vibrating. "It's our first real birthday with our grandson. We had to be here."

"Yeah, we brought a few things. Hope it's not overkill," Dad says. "We've got a lot of missed years to make up for."

My jaw hits the floor. Two of their drivers haul in a literal mountain of wrapped boxes. I thought Declan and I spoiled him, but this is insane. I'm just standing there, mouth wide open, while the drivers look around, totally lost on where to dump the haul.

"Take them out back," Declan's voice rumbles right next to my ear. "We set up a gift table. You can't miss it."

I whip around, heart racing. I didn't even hear him walk up. To be fair, the house is a zoo right now. His footsteps were totally drowned out.

"Beatrice. Edmund," Declan says, keeping it polite.

"Hey, Declan," Mom smiles. Dad just gives him a stiff, guy-to-guy nod.

Baby Aurora is dead to the world on Declan's shoulder, drool dripping onto his shirt. Honestly, I'm shocked. The kid usually needs total silence to pass out, but she's snoring right through this chaos.

"Well, she's out cold," Mom says. "How about us girls take the baby upstairs for a nap, and you guys can... catch up?"

She doesn't wait for an invite. She slides Aurora out of Declan's arms, grabs my wrist, and hauls me away, leaving the two guys standing there looking incredibly awkward.

We don't say a word on the way up to the nursery. Mom tucks Aurora into her crib, real quiet. Once the baby's settled, she pulls me over to the window seat and squeezes my hand.

"So, spill. What's the deal with you and Declan?"

I look at her and sigh, shaking my head. "I don't know, Mom. I'm still totally stuck."

"Look, I've been thinking. You need to get back into therapy," she says. "Piper told me you quit, but honestly, you need to go back. You've been through hell, Lyra. Moving past that isn't something you just do overnight. You need professional help to sort through this mess."

I open my mouth to protest, but she holds up a hand.

"I know, sweetie. But listen to me. Declan or no Declan, you need this. You can't just wish the trauma away. If you don't face it, it's going to keep dragging you under, no matter who you're with. Just think about it, okay?"

I let her words sink in. She's right. I'm clinging to the old hurts like a safety blanket. If I don't face the music, I'm never going to move forward.

"Fine," I sigh. "I'll go."

Mom squeezes my hand. "Good. And if you guys try to make it work, you need couples counseling. Declan probably needs his own therapist, too. Let's be real—you guys started off in the worst way possible. You were obsessed with a guy who was taken. I'm not excusing his behavior, but honestly? Any normal person would have snapped. He was devastated, furious, and he lost his girl because of you. Sure, you were both wasted that night, but you still stalked him. You cornered him at a bar when he was vulnerable and drinking. That was a crappy move, Lyra. But you were young. We all do stupid, toxic things when we're young."

Her words hit me like a slap in the face. It was true. Declan and Aurora were crazy about each other back then. I should've killed my crush instead of letting it turn into a full-blown obsession. Yeah, I was drunk when we finally hooked up, but I was dead sober when I followed him across town. I was sober when I sat next to him at that bar, knowing damn well he was wasted. And I definitely wasn't drunk when I started hitting on him.

I take a long, slow breath. I've spent years blaming Declan for our trainwreck of a marriage, but how much of that wreckage was on me?

I look at Mom. "I don't know if we have a future. But if we try? Yeah. Couples therapy is a must."

"Good girl," Mom smiles, patting my hand. "Now let's get back down there before your father accidentally kills Declan."

I grab the baby monitor and laugh. "We both know there'd be nothing accidental about it."

We head back downstairs, chatting. Just as we hit the bottom step, the front door swings open. Eleanor walks in, looking flawless as usual. But the person trailing behind her makes my jaw drop.

It's Aurora. And honestly? She looks like absolute shit. I don't even mean that to be mean—she literally looks like she just crawled out of a dumpster fire.
