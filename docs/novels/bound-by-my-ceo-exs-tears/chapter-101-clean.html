Chapter 102

My confession hits, and Mom drops into the chair like her legs gave out. The sheer devastation in her eyes guts me. That look of pure disappointment? It almost breaks me right then and there. Corbin, who’d been holding onto me, lets go like I’m a hot stove. He backs off, putting a solid few feet of distance between us.

I can feel the shockwaves hitting everyone else in the room, but honestly, I don’t give a damn about them. Not when my own flesh and blood are staring at me like I’m some random stranger off the street.

"Tell me this is some sick joke," Mom whispers, her voice cracking. "Please. Tell me you didn't have a baby and hide him from us all these years."

I want to lie. God, I want to lie so bad just to wipe that crushed, disgusted look off their faces. But I can't. The gig is up. There’s nowhere left to run, no more hiding from the truth.

"I'm sorry, okay? I'm so, so sorry," I sob, taking a shaky step toward her. "I wanted to tell you, but I was just... I was so damn ashamed."

I reach out to grab her hand, but she flinches. She pulls away like my touch is toxic.

And there it is. My absolute worst nightmare. Lyra was right all along. I was the golden child. The perfect daughter who never made a single mistake.

Except for that one time. Now my pristine image is completely trashed. Now they see I’m just as messed up as the rest of them. Just human. The one trump card I had over Lyra? Gone.

I’m stuck in the exact same boat as her now, drowning in my own past mistakes. And sleeping with Silas? Still the absolute worst thing I’ve ever done.

"How? Like, when did this even happen?" Corbin paces back and forth, practically vibrating with frustration. "Did Dad know about this?"

"No, Dad didn't know. Nobody did. Just Silas and Clara." I stare at the floor, unable to look my mother in the eye.

"Start from the beginning. Every single detail," Mom growls, her eyes flashing with sudden fury. "I want to know why the hell you kept my grandson a secret from his own family."

I hate digging up the past, but I don't have a choice. If I don't spill everything right now, they’re going to hate me even more.

"When Declan married Lyra, I completely shattered. I tried to fake it, tried to make you, Dad, and Corbin think I was fine because you guys were stressing so much. But I was drowning, Mom. My entire world blew up, and I had nothing left. When I went back to school, I fell into a really dark depression. I didn't tell a soul."

I sink onto the grass, feeling just as small and pathetic as I did the day I found out Declan had slept with Lyra.

"I was basically a ghost. I stopped eating, stopped sleeping, skipped all my classes. I barely even left my room. Clara saw what was happening. She kept pushing me, and when I wouldn't snap out of it, she threatened to call you guys. I couldn't let you see me like that. And I definitely didn't want Lyra knowing how badly she’d broken me."

The memories flood back, thick with all the bitter rage I had for Lyra back then. I used to think that if she ever found out how low I’d sunk, she’d be throwing a party. It took me a year and a half to realize she was living in her own personal hell, too.

"But things got better after that. Clara became my anchor. My grades went back up, and I wasn't crying over Declan every damn second." I take a shaky breath. "Then, I ran into Silas. Total coincidence. Turns out we went to the same college, and he was Clara's lab partner."

"We didn't really talk. I mean, we weren't friends. I only tolerated him because of Clara. Once I realized he wasn't acting like the obsessed creep he was in high school, I let my guard down. We just coexisted. He stayed in his lane, and I stayed in mine."

Jesus, this is brutal. I want to stop talking so bad, but looking at them, I know they won't let me off the hook.

"Like I said, things were fine for a while. Not perfect, but I was getting by. Until the night Mom called to tell me Lyra had a baby boy, and that Declan fell in love with him at first sight. Everything crashed. All that pain I’d buried just exploded." I try to breathe through the memory, but it hurts like hell.

"I was hurting, and I was so incredibly pissed off. Mad at myself for turning down Declan's proposal. Mad at Declan for getting wasted and sleeping with Lyra. Mad at Lyra for getting pregnant and marrying my guy. And yeah, I was even mad at the baby for being born."

Someone gasps. I don't even have to look to know it's Declan. I still can't stand being around Asher, because in my head, he should've been my kid.

"I wanted to hurt Declan. I wanted him to feel the exact pain he gave me. I knew he always hated Silas because of how Silas used to chase me. So that night, I went to Silas. I seduced him. I figured word would get back to Declan, and it would destroy him. That's the only reason I slept with him—I wanted his friend to spill the beans. I wanted my revenge so the hurting would stop."

I leave out the part where that was my first time. I’d been saving myself for Declan, and instead, I gave it to the guy who’d been begging for my love.

"I hated myself the next morning. It was so stupid, sleeping with a guy I didn't even like just to get back at the one I loved. I told Silas it was a massive mistake and to forget it ever happened. I snuck out of his room, thinking that was it. Just a terrible one-night stand."

God, I was so wrong. I didn't see what was coming. I didn't see what fate had in store.

"We went back to ignoring each other. But then my period was late. A cheap drugstore test, and then a doctor's visit, confirmed it. I was pregnant."

It was the worst day of my life. Deep down, I always thought Declan and I would find our way back to each other. I didn't want some unwanted baby ruining my chances. I didn't want a constant reminder of my biggest screw-up.

"Why didn't you just take Plan B the next morning?" Mom asks, her face tightening.

"On top of being a total basket case, I didn't know anything. Honestly? I was clueless. It was my very first time, and I didn't think you could get pregnant from just once. If I’d told Clara, she probably would've made me take it. But I was too ashamed to tell anyone."

"Wait, you're telling me you and Declan never...?" Corbin looks at me, totally baffled. "You were still a virgin?"

I knew Declan wasn't. Before we started dating at seventeen, he’d sleep with anything with a pulse. But when I told him I wanted to wait, he respected that. We agreed to wait until I was ready. My biggest regret is making him wait.

Declan groans, shifting uncomfortably. "Can we seriously not do this right now? That was years ago."

"To answer your question, yeah, I was a virgin. Anyway," I pause, swallowing hard. "I told Silas about the baby. I didn't want it. I wanted to get an abortion, but he wouldn't let me."

"An abortion?" Mom whispers, looking completely horrified and disgusted.

I can only nod. "Silas threatened to tell you and Dad if I went through with it. I couldn't let you find out about my mess, so I agreed to have the kid if he kept his mouth shut. It was absolute hell. I was forced to carry a baby I hated."

"So that's why you completely ghosted us," Corbin mutters. "You wouldn't even let us come visit."

I’d made up so many excuses back then. Once I started showing, Silas took me to a house his late grandfather left him. I didn't want Declan finding out. That's where I hid until the baby came.

"I convinced my school to let me do online classes. Since my grades were fine, they didn't need to contact you. I stayed off-campus with Silas until I gave birth."

Mom's eyes well up with tears. I know it kills her that she missed her grandson's birth, but I didn't care. I didn't want that kid.

"The second he was born, I told the nurses to hand him to Silas. I wanted nothing to do with him. I did my time, and I wanted my freedom back from Silas and his blackmail."

"You didn't even look at your own baby?" Mom asks, her voice trembling.

"No. Why would I? He was just a reminder of my biggest mistake. I didn't want to see him, hold him, or have anything to do with him."

I know that makes me sound like a heartless bitch, but honestly, I don't care. It was my life, my choice.

I checked out of the hospital the next morning. Silas worked two jobs just to pay off the medical bills. I didn't give a damn how the baby was doing, what he was eating, or if he was okay. I just wanted to forget I ever had another man's kid.

"And you just went on living your life like he didn't exist?" Mom stands up, staring down at me. "Like he was absolutely nothing?"

"Mom..."

"Just shut up!" she screams, then drops her voice to a harsh whisper. "Just shut the hell up. You just destroyed whatever was left of my heart today. Losing your father and Lyra wasn't enough? Now I find out my own daughter is a monster... I can't even look at you right now. Get away from me."

She walks off, leaving me sobbing on the grass. I look up at the others, but one by one, they turn their backs and walk away. Corbin is the last one standing there.

He just shakes his head, looking at me with pure disgust. "Of all the people, Aurora. I never expected this from you. Not you." Then he turns and leaves, too.

I lie there on the ground, crying my eyes out. Everything was perfect until she showed up and ruined my life. This is all Lyra's fault. Her and her big, fat mouth. I will never forgive her for this. Never.
