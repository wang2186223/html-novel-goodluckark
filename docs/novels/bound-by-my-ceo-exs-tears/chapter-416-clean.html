Chapter 424

"Come on, Si. Talk to me," Clara coos, her voice all soft and coaxing.

My throat goes bone-dry. I gulp. No way in hell am I spilling this. It’s my mess to deal with. My own private disaster. I’m taking this secret straight to the grave. Nobody can ever find out how spectacularly I screwed up.

"It's fine, seriously," I lie through my teeth, brushing a tear off my cheek. "Just... ugh, one of those crappy days, you know?"

Dead air stretches between us. It drags on so long I actually check my screen to see if she hung up. Then her voice comes back, low and super careful.

"So... I just got off the line with Eleanor."

My chest does a violent jerk.

"She spilled," I mutter, my voice dead.

"Yeah. She said she begged you to track down Asher last night because he went MIA. And then you pinged her later saying you located him. That he was totally fine."

Crap. My brain scrambles. "Uh—yeah. I tracked him down. But, like... things got messy on my end."

"What did he do? Seriously, if he opened his mouth and said one nasty thing to you, I will literally chop his balls off."

She’s not joking either. Clara Sterling has thrown down with her own cousin for my sake plenty of times. Like back when we were thirteen. Asher made a total fool of me in front of the whole class, and Clara iced him out for weeks.

I had to beg her to drop the grudge, and even then, it took her a solid month to actually get over it.

God, I want to dump the whole story on her. But the sheer embarrassment chokes me. It blocks the words, stopping my usual verbal vomit.

We don't do secrets. Never have. But this? This dirty little secret stays locked in my vault forever.

"Just the usual Asher drama," I say, keeping my voice totally flat. "He wasn't exactly thrilled to see my face."

I mean, it’s not technically a lie. Just a highly filtered version of reality.

"Oh, Si..." she sighs.

Just two letters, but I feel the weight of them. She gets it. She knows how much it hurts, even without the gory details. Nobody—and I mean absolutely nobody—takes the brunt of Asher's venom like I do. Clara's my ride-or-die. She knows exactly how deep his claws can dig into me.

"I'm fine," I say, but my voice hitches. "I'm numb to it by now, remember? It... yeah, it bites, but whatever."

Total bullshit. It doesn't just bite. I am completely, utterly broken.

We go quiet. It’s a comfortable hush, though. Familiar. We’ve played this exact scene out a million times. Over the years, we analyzed every single theory on why Asher hates my guts, always hitting a brick wall. Eventually, we just stopped trying to decode his psycho brain.

"I should probably head home," I mumble.

The second the words slip out, I want to bite my tongue.

"Wait, where are you right now?" she asks, her radar instantly going up.

"I'm just... out. Took a drive to clear the cobwebs. Needed some breathing room."

She lets out a heavy sigh. "I'm going to rip him a new one, Si. Seriously, screw whatever he said. Don't let him get under your skin. You're loved, okay? Always have been. Just ignore my dumbass cousin. I love the guy, but he's not worth a single tear you've wasted on him."

I appreciate the loyalty, but let's be real—it won't change a damn thing. Plus, who knows if Asher will blab about what we actually did? I can't take that gamble. Besides, my plan is to go right back to ghosting him, just like I did for the last six years. Give it a few months, and this whole mess will be ancient history.

I inhale slowly, trying to let her comfort sink in, even though I still feel like a walking disaster.

"Thanks, Clara. Seriously, you're the best. But don't confront him. It's pointless and we both know it. Just drop it."

"Fine. And don't thank me, you'd do the same for me," she sighs, giving in. "Just text me when you walk through the door."

"Will do."

I end the call. I sit in the quiet cabin for a second, then twist the key and hit the gas.

The drive back is a total blur. I navigate the streets on pure autopilot, not even registering the turns. All I feel is this heavy, throbbing ache in my ribs.

I pull into the driveway, kill the engine, and bolt straight inside.

Shadow is right there waiting at the door. Her tiny, high-pitched meow actually coaxes a tiny smile out of me. I scoop her up, bury my face in her soft fur for a second, then set her back down.

I toss my keys onto the entryway table and head toward the kitchen. Shadow trots right at my heels, her soft purrs and meows taking the edge off my anxiety. Just being home in my own zone with my cat feels like an instant reset button.

I slump onto a bar stool and pull out my phone to text Clara. That’s when I spot the notification. A text from Eleanor.

[Thank you so much, sweetie. You really didn't have to, but you saved my life. Asher just grabbed the twins, and honestly, he looks better than he has since Seraphina passed. Thank you, love you!]

I stare at the glowing screen. My thumbs hover over the keyboard. I want to type something back. But I freeze.

I don't even know why. Maybe because she's completely clueless about what actually went down. Maybe because I did way more than just "find" Asher last night, and now this massive wave of guilt is hitting me.

I toss the phone face down.

I force myself to lock it all away. Last night. Asher. The whole damn thing. But some memories... they don't just vanish because you want them to.
