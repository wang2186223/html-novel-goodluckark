Chapter 105

Sunlight stabs through the glass, forcing my eyes open. I don't move. I just lie there, palm flat against my stomach, tracking the tiny, rhythmic kicks from inside. Six months. I glance at the nightstand calendar. Half a year down. God, having a baby is terrifying. It's a total crapload of "what-ifs." Every single milestone feels like a miracle, and I whisper a quick thank-you to the big guy upstairs. Not every pregnancy makes it this far, and I know it.

I slide out of bed, skip the shower—that can wait—and head straight downstairs. My stomach is literally growling. Yesterday was such a shitshow I forgot to feed myself. And speaking of yesterday... Declan. My brain immediately drags me back to that kiss. I let him. Worse, I liked it. Like, really liked it.

It makes me sick how much I wanted more. I wanted him to push me against the wall and take it further. Sure, I could blame the pregnancy hormones, but that’s a total cop-out. Declan wrecked me. Craving him makes me want to punch myself in the face. I swore I was done with him, yet there I was, practically eating his face in my own living room.

Maybe he’s changed. But honestly? Who cares? Even if he did some magical 180, it’s too late. The damage is done. You don't just sweep that kind of wreckage under the rug. I grab the eggs and bacon, trying to focus on breakfast, but my brain won't shut up about the kiss. Why the hell is it messing with me this much? Seriously, what is my problem?

I let out a loud groan, wishing I could scrub his lips off mine.

"Whoa. What's got you in such a mood?"

I jump, losing my grip on the eggs. They smash onto the counter.

"Jesus, Cal! You scared the crap out of me!" I yell, slapping a hand over my racing heart.

"My bad," he says, flashing a crooked grin. "Thought you heard us. Jasper and Asher aren't exactly stealthy."

I look past him. The boys are already face-first in the cookie jar, demolishing yesterday's batch.

"It's fine. Just... lost in my own head, I guess."

Cal's grin fades. "Still stressing about Aurora?"

I nod. Total lie, but he doesn't need to know I'm actually obsessing over a kiss.

"Hey, Mom, what's cooking?" Asher calls out.

"A massive fry-up," I say.

"Yes!" Jasper pumps his fist. "Fry-ups are the best. It's like a cheat code for breakfast."

I can't help but smile. Seeing him happy warms my heart. I just hope Aurora gets her head out of her ass and fixes things with him before she loses him forever.

"Is it ready yet?" Asher asks, stomach probably bottomless. "I'm starving."

"Give me five minutes. Go wait in the living room before you drool on the floor."

"On it!" they yell in unison, scrambling out of the kitchen.

Cal grabs a rag and starts wiping up the raw egg I dropped. "Sorry for crashing. I should've given you some space today, but the boys begged for your cooking."

I chuckle. Cal’s not a terrible cook, but he’s got zero game compared to me. Their words, obviously.

"Don't worry about it. I was going to pack some up and bring it over anyway," I say, cracking fresh eggs into the skillet.

"So, how are you holding up with everything?" I ask him.

"Honestly? Relieved." He pauses, tossing the dirty rag aside. "It's good to have Jasper out in the open. He's a great kid. He shouldn't have to live like some dirty little secret. After my granddad passed, I was totally alone until Jasper came along. I was fine with just the two of us, but it wasn't fair to him. His mom has a whole family. He deserves to know his uncle and grandma."

Personally, I think Corbin and Eleanor are trash human beings. But they treat Asher right, so maybe they won't poison Jasper. Ugh. I need to drop the grudge. They're out of my life now. What they did to me doesn't matter anymore.

"You're right," I tell Cal. "Aurora had no right to keep him hidden."

Before he can reply, the doorbell rings. Footsteps thud as Asher sprints for the door. Please don't let it be Declan. I cannot deal with a macho showdown between him and Cal this early.

"I'll get it," I say, stepping out of the kitchen.

"Who is it, sweetie?" I ask Asher.

He shrugs, holding up a piece of paper. "Nobody's there. Just this."

He hands me a folded note with my name scribbled on the front. A cold shiver crawls straight down my spine.

"Okay, go back inside. I'll be right there."

He nods and runs back to the kitchen. Standing alone in the entryway, feeling like someone is watching me through a sniper scope, I unfold the paper.

*YOU WILL PAY FOR EVERYTHING.*

Holy shit. My heart hammers against my ribs. I grip the paper so tight my knuckles turn white. I peer out the window, scanning the empty street. Nothing. No cars, no suspicious figures.

I shove the note deep into my pajama pocket, lock the door, and walk back to the kitchen, trying to keep my face blank.

"Everything cool?" Cal asks, brow furrowed. "You look like you saw a ghost."

"Yeah, totally fine. Just a ding-dong ditch." I shrug, forcing a casual laugh while my chest feels like a techno club. "Probably some neighborhood kids."

"Yeah, probably," he agrees.

I finish up the food, and we dig in. The boys swap stupid school stories, and Cal rants about work. I laugh in all the right places, but my mind is miles away, looping back to that note.

My gut is screaming at me. This isn't a prank.

Someone is coming for me. Again.
