Chapter 19

I yank my hood down. Gotta look normal, not like a walking corpse.

"Hey, why the beanie, Mom?" Asher squints at the screen.

We’re finally on Skype. I’d blown him off a dozen times because I couldn't keep my eyes open for more than two seconds. But today? I actually feel human.

I prop myself up against the headboard. The knit cap covers the thick gauze on my skull. Asher doesn't know about the accident. He never will. I'll make sure of it.

"Just chilly in here, sweetie," I lie.

God, the guilt eats at me. But it's better this way. No need to freak him out.

"Uh, we have a heater, Mom. Turn it on."

"It’s busted. Forgot to call the repair guy."

Damn it. I hate lying to my kid. Ever since Dad died, it feels like I do nothing but feed him BS. Makes me feel like a total trash mom. But the other part of me? It knows I have to.

"If you say so," he mumbles, totally not buying it.

"So, what did you do today?" I pivot. Fast.

I live for his updates, even if I'm stuck here missing out. His smiles are my life support. I’d kill to keep him happy.

His suspicious look vanishes. Boom. Huge grin.

"I saw dolphins! Like, actually swam with them! It was crazy cool!" he yells, bouncing.

"Ugh, I wish I was there."

"Don't worry, Grandma got it on video. She’s sending it."

I nod. I kept the phone Declan bought me. Turns out, the guy didn't just buy a device—he swapped out my SIM card, too.

I dodge Declan's calls like the plague. He rings sometimes to check my pulse. I keep it cold, fast, and strictly business. I just want a quiet life. Declan in my business means zero peace. Especially with Aurora in the picture.

"Mom, why was Aurora at Dad's place?"

The question hits me like a cold splash of water. "Wait, what?"

"I Skyped Dad yesterday. She was right there. Sitting super close, holding his hand... I hated it." He scowls. It’s the exact same look his father gives.

I try to play it cool, but that hurts. Like a physical punch. Declan is already playing happy family with her. The old pain I buried deep? Yeah, it's back.

Why do we lie to ourselves? We think we're over it, then one little trigger blows the whole illusion to hell. The pain just doubles.

"I don't know, baby. You'll have to ask him," I say, keeping my voice steady. Barely.

I’m not explaining this mess to Asher. Declan wants to parade his side-chick in front of our kid? Fine. He can explain it.

"I want you guys back together. I want our family," he says, sounding so small. My broken heart shatters a little more.

"Asher, listen. Your dad and I... we're just too different. We can't be together."

We put on a show for him. Tried to play the happy couple. Total joke. Declan couldn't stand the sight of me, but Asher never noticed.

Honestly, I should've said no when Declan proposed after I got pregnant. I was so dumb back then. Thought I could make him love me. Thought time would fix it, that he'd fall for me the way I fell for him. Never happened.

His heart was locked tight, and Aurora had the key. Even when we hooked up, it was nothing to him. Just a physical itch to scratch. I put my soul into it; he put zero feelings. We never made love. It was just raw, empty sex. And he always made damn sure to use protection. No way he was making the same mistake twice.

"Don't you love him?" Asher asks. The exact same question he threw at his dad weeks ago.

I want to lie. But I'm done with the BS.

"I do. But sometimes, love just isn't enough. You're too young to get it now, but you will one day." That's all I've got.

God, I hope he never goes through this hell. I want him to have the real deal. And honestly? I hope he gets the kind of love Declan and Aurora have. The ride-or-die kind that survives everything. I hope I find that too, someday.

A knock rattles the doorframe.

"Someone's here for you, Lyra," Felicity says.

I finally got her to drop the "ma'am" crap. Thank God Piper talked me into letting her stay. She's a lifesaver. Cleans, helps out—I'd be drowning without her.

"Who's that, Mom?"

I motion Felicity to let them in, then look back at my boy.

"That's Felicity. She helps me around the house," I say, my mind already spinning on who's at the door.

Probably Piper or Adrian. They've been dropping by to check on me.

"Why do you need help? You're Super Mom. You never need help," he says, eyes narrowing.

He's not wrong. I used to do everything myself. Even at Declan's mansion. I thought it would make him respect me. Show him I wasn't some useless princess like Aurora, who couldn't even boil water. I wanted to win a point.

What a joke. He didn't give a shit. I was so stupid, thinking home-cooked meals would make him hate me less. Thinking I could run a house, raise a kid, and work a job, and he'd finally care.

"Mom?"

I need an out, and I get one when Corbin walks in. He's the last person I want to see, but hey, perfect scapegoat.

"Asher, I gotta go. Your uncle just walked in. I'll call you back."

He sighs. "Okay, Mom."

We say bye and hang up. The second the screen goes black, my smile dies.

"I thought I told you to stay the hell away from me," I say, my face turning to stone.

He shifts, looking uncomfortable. "You're my sister. I wanted to see how you were."

I let out a dry, bitter laugh. "Sister? Really, Corbin? Because I don't remember being your sister for the last nine years. Actually, let's be real—never. You only ever had one sister, and you made damn sure I knew it."

It still stings. Getting dumped by your husband, your in-laws, and your own blood? Rejection is a bitch, and I've had a lifetime supply.

"Lyra..."

"Save it," I snap. I don't want to hear his excuses. "I stayed away from your precious sister and her man, just like you wanted. Now do me a favor and stay away from me. Simple."

"We're family," he says.

I hold up a hand. "Stop right there. You, Mom, and Aurora are family. I'm the outsider. Always have been, and you guys never let me forget it."

It hurts to say it, but it's the truth. They shut me out of everything. Made me feel like trash.

"You gave Piper a shot! Why not us?" he demands, his temper flaring.

"Because Piper actually treated me like a human being! She didn't treat me like garbage for a mistake I made at eighteen when I was young, dumb, and blindly in love with a guy who didn't care about me."

Willow told me the truth. Corbin spilled everything to her when they got serious, after she noticed I was banned from family dinners.

"Get out, Corbin. And don't come back. Just pretend I'm dead. Forget you ever had a sister named Lyra."

I roll over, turning my back to him. Silence hangs in the room, then I hear his footsteps fade away.

My pillow gets wet. He wants too much. I gave them everything. I loved them even when they hated me. I kept hoping, praying, pouring my heart out, thinking they'd finally love me back. Instead, they just stepped on it. Broke me, over and over.

Now he wants back in? Too late. They broke me into pieces, and I've got nothing left to give them. I'm completely empty.
