Chapter 133

I blink open my eyes. I’m draped right over Declan. His arm is a heavy band around my waist, pinning half my body to his.

Slowly, I prop myself up off his chest. This is new. We look like a real couple. Like we’re actually in love. But that’s a lie. Sure, there’s love in this marriage, but it’s a one-way street. Mine.

I slide off him, quiet as a ghost. No way do I want to wake him. I need space. I need to process this whole mess. Ever since I woke up from that coma, my life has been a total trainwreck.

It’s only been forty-eight hours, but it feels like a damn rollercoaster. My head is spinning. I don’t even know what to trust anymore—my eyes or my stupid heart.

My eyes land on the baby bottle on his nightstand.

Right. Aurora woke up three times last night. I handled the first two feeds. The last time, Declan told me to crash. Said he’d handle it. I was too wiped out to argue, so I just let him.

I throw on my robe and creep out of the room. I pop in to check on Aurora and Asher. It’s early Saturday morning, so Asher gets to sleep in. No school.

Down in the kitchen, Martha is already wiping down the counters.

"Morning, ma'am," she says, looking up with a smile.

I force a warm smile back. "Morning, Martha. And seriously, call me Lyra. 'Ma'am' makes me feel ancient."

She stops wiping and looks at me. "Need anything?"

"Actually, yeah. If it's cool with you, I want to whip up breakfast for everyone."

Her face lights up. She nods fast.

"Oh, absolutely! Asher's gonna lose his mind. He’s been whining for days that my pancakes aren't like yours." She gives me a quick wink.

I let out a soft laugh. Classic Asher. But then my smile fades. God, how hard was it for him while I was out cold in that hospital bed?

Martha heads out, and I get to work. But honestly? It feels weird.

Declan never touched my food before. He’d skip breakfast, skip dinner. The only time he’d swallow a bite of my cooking was when Asher begged us to eat as a family. Even then, he’d take two bites and claim he was stuffed.

It used to gut me. Knowing he hated everything about me. Like my food was poison. Eventually, I got over it. It hurt like hell, but I sucked it up for Asher.

I did everything to hide Declan's coldness from our kid. Asher thought we were the perfect couple, and I played the part. I’d do anything to keep him happy. Even fake a happy marriage.

I shake the thoughts out of my head and focus on the batter.

Who knows? Maybe almost losing me changed him. I still love the guy, despite all the crap he’s pulled. Maybe this is our shot at a do-over.

Five minutes later, I slam the flour bag down. Tears prick my eyes. Why is this so damn hard?

"Hey. What's going on?"

Declan’s voice. Right behind me.

Before I can move, his arms slide around my waist, pulling me back against his chest. He brushes my hair off my neck and rests his chin on my shoulder.

Normally, this would freak me out. But right now, I’m too pissed off to care.

"I used to make Asher’s favorite pancakes in my sleep," I choke out, gripping the edge of the counter so I don’t collapse. "Now? I can't remember a single thing. I don't know what the secret ingredient is."

"Hey, it's okay. Don't sweat it," he murmurs, trying to soothe me. It doesn't work.

"No, you don't get it!" I wrench myself out of his grip and spin around. "It's not just the pancakes. I can't read the damn measuring cup. I don't remember how you take your coffee! Hell, I can barely read some of these words!"

The tears spill over. I feel completely useless. Everything that used to be second nature is gone. Numbers are just a blur. I’ve been staring at a measuring cup for thirty minutes, and my brain is just... mush.

It’s like my mind can’t process what my eyes are seeing.

"Shh. Lyra, stop. It's okay," he says, stepping closer. "Don't do this to yourself."

He turns me to face him. He’s shirtless. Just low-slung sweatpants.

My throat goes dry. A different kind of heat flares up inside me. I force my eyes away from his chest and look straight at him.

He leans down. I freeze, my breath catching in my throat. Then, his lips hit mine.

I gasp, and he takes the opening, sliding his tongue past my lips. The kiss is intense, hot, and I’m completely gone. Our tongues tangle, and everything else fades.

One of his hands grips my waist, the other wrapping around the back of my neck. I grab his hips just to keep my knees from buckling.

I’m lost in him. His scent, his touch, just... Declan. It’s perfect. Nothing can ruin this.

Until I hear a sharp gasp behind us.

I tear my mouth away from Declan’s and spin around.

My eyes lock onto a crying woman. A woman I thought I’d never have to see again.

Aurora.

What the hell is she doing in my house?
