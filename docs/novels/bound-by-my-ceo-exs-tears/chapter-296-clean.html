Chapter 302

"Yeah, do that," he says. "Grab the weekly IT reports for me? Yesterday was a total shitshow, so I couldn't get around to them."

"On it," I say. "Let me dump my bag in my office first."

He nods, and I bolt. I toss my stuff onto my desk and head straight for the other departments.

The second I walk into the first department, the vibe goes dead silent. Every single head snaps toward me. Seriously, guys? Get a life. I ignore the burning stares, grab the files, and walk out.

Making friends here was never an option anyway. Not after Bianca told everyone I was some cheap hoe sleeping with Orion. One rumor, and boom—I was the office pariah.

I finally hit the last department and let out a breath. Suddenly, people are throwing me these fake, sugary smiles. I don't buy it for a second. Now that the truth is out, they're all trying to play nice. Classic gold-digging behavior—they just want to ride my coattails.

"Hey, Willow." Rebecca, one of Bianca's little pets, steps right in front of the elevator, cutting me off.

I roll my eyes. "What do you want, Rebecca?"

"Oh, I just thought... maybe we could be friends?" The redhead pulls this wide-eyed, innocent act.

Is this chick for real? A snort escapes my mouth before I can choke it back. "Are you kidding me?"

"Why would I be?" she asks. "I mean, I literally tried to stop Bianca from dragging you yesterday. She just wouldn't listen. That's gotta count for something, right?"

I pause. Okay, yeah, she did try to mumble something to Bianca about some official company statement.

"If that was your sad attempt at sucking up, you failed," I say. "Cut the crap. We both know you weren't doing me any favors."

Her sweet-girl mask cracks instantly. Boom. There’s the toxic bitch I know and hate.

"You're being seriously mean," she spits, her jaw tight. "I'm literally just trying to be nice here."

I can feel the entire floor watching us, but I don't give a shit. This girl is pushing my last button.

"Mean?" I snap. "Mean is you and your little clique dragging my name through the mud. Mean is calling me a slut when we all know who the actual trash is. You've treated me like garbage since day one." I stop, taking a hard breath. "I don't want your friendship, and I sure as hell don't need it. I'm not an idiot, Rebecca. You're only sniffing around because I'm married to Orion. And honestly? Even if I weren't, I wouldn't touch your crowd with a ten-foot pole. Now move. I have actual work to do."

She glares, eyes burning. "You're a total bitch."

"Takes one to know one," I shoot back as the elevator doors slide open.

"Got a problem here?"

A deep voice cuts through the air. I look past her. Declan is standing right there by the elevator.

Rebecca spins so fast she nearly trips over her own feet. "Oh! No, no problem, Mr. Wood."

"Didn't sound like it," Declan says, his voice dropping to a freezing chill. He looks at me and jerks his chin, telling me to get over there.

I walk over and stand right beside him. Every drop of color drains from Rebecca's face.

"I don't tolerate people throwing insults at my sister-in-law," Declan says, his brow furrowed in a dark scowl. "Especially not in my own damn building. It pisses me off. You better remember right now: Willow is a Wood. If she wants you gone, you stay the fuck away. If she tells you to do something, you do it, or you'll be packing your desk. We clear?"
