Chapter 445

Eleanor gives a knowing nod. "That's just the mom effect," she says. "Look, I was freaking out when I got pregnant with Asher. Totally terrified. But then it hits you—this tiny human relies on you for literally everything. So you suck it up, put your big-girl pants on, and handle business."

Her words hit me right in the chest. Warm. Solid. I'd burn the world down for this kid. And yeah, that includes telling Asher to go straight to hell.

"Thanks, guys," I choke out, blinking back tears.

A collective "Aww" goes around the room, and suddenly I'm buried in a massive group hug.

The chatter shifts. We move from my doctor's visit to a flood of baby advice. I just sit back and look at my aunts. Honestly, they're total badasses. Running empires, managing massive charities, and they still make time to babysit, talk trash, and fight over who makes the best cheesecake in the city.

Right in the middle of it, Aunt Audrey is lecturing me on "being there" for the kid. Suddenly, Clara Sterling freezes. Her eyes pop like she just cracked the secrets of the universe. She jerks forward, nearly knocking over her juice.

"Oh my god, I knew it!" Clara blurts out. "It's your company, right? The one behind those... you know, those spicy adult toys? It has to be! Think about it. You guys are fierce, high-standard women who dominate every room you walk into. Of course you'd crush that market too."

She looks incredibly proud of herself, like she just solved a murder mystery.

The room goes dead silent. Everyone stares at her.

Victoria cocks an eyebrow. "And how exactly do you know which brands are 'the best,' sweetie?"

Clara's face goes totally white. "I... uh... read the reviews online?" she stammers.

I bite my lip so hard it hurts. I know exactly how she knows, and it sure as hell isn't from reading reviews. The mental image is too much. My shoulders start shaking as I try to hold it in.

Aunt Piper smirks. "Oh, please. We all know how she knows."

Clara glares at Piper, muttering under her breath, "Well, I didn't know the stuff in my nightstand was made by my own mother and aunts."

That does it. I lose it. A loud snort escapes me, and I almost dump my juice everywhere.

"Hey, don't laugh!" Clara snaps, her eyes narrowing. "You use the exact same ones!"

Now she's cracking up too. The sheer irony of it hangs in the air. I laugh even harder, clutching my stomach. Like, seriously, what are the odds?

Victoria's jaw drops. The other aunts look like they're about to slide off their chairs from laughing so hard.

"Okay, before this conversation ruins my childhood forever," I giggle, "let's open some presents."

I start unwrapping. Aunt Audrey got me a super soft baby blanket. Eleanor got this ridiculously cute onesie that reads "Future CEO." Aunt Piper put together a massive basket of organic baby stuff, and Beatrice got the tiniest, cutest little shoes and socks. Looking at the pile, I realize I won't need to buy a single thing except a crib.

"Thank you, seriously," I say, my eyes watering again. My heart feels so full. This is exactly what I needed. A reminder that I've got a village. I'm not doing this alone.

They start grabbing their coats and purses, already planning the next hangout. "We're coming over right after your next checkup," Beatrice says. "No excuses. And print out every single ultrasound photo."

"Deal," I smile, feeling that warm glow inside.

As they head for the door, Eleanor pulls me aside. "Piper, seriously. Are you sure we don't know who the dad is?"

Her eyes lock onto mine, searching. I can see the gears turning in her head, trying to connect dots I need kept separate.

My heart starts hammering against my ribs. Keep it cool. Don't flinch.

I look her straight in the eye. "Yes. I'm sure."

She sighs, but I spot that tiny flicker of doubt. She doesn't buy it. And once Eleanor gets a sniff of a secret, she won't stop digging until she unearths the whole damn thing.
