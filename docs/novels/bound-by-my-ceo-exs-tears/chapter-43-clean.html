Chapter 43

Saturday morning. The post-gala hangover was real, but the event had been a total win. I rolled out of bed and dragged myself to the bathroom. Adrian had dropped me off last night, but he didn't stay. Early shift. He didn't want his pre-dawn exit waking me up. Sweet guy.

I squeezed toothpaste onto my brush, my mind looping back to yesterday's drama. Aurora had cornered me, and I was totally ready to throw down. Then Declan walked in. I felt him there before she even noticed. Weird how I always knew when he was in the room. I wanted to rip her lies apart right in front of him. He needed to see who he was actually drooling over. Every word out of my mouth was pure truth. The guy needed a massive reality check.

To the rest of the world, Aurora was a saint. Flawless. Incapable of doing wrong. Sure, maybe back in the day. But now? No way. Maybe losing Declan years ago broke something inside her. Or maybe climbing the legal ladder turned her into a shark. Who knows? Bottom line: she’s different now, and not in a good way.

I spat, rinsed, and hopped into the shower. Quick scrub, then back to the bedroom. I grabbed my usual uniform—comfy jeans, a basic tee, and flats. Simple.

Downstairs, I whipped up some breakfast. I had a nine o'clock debrief with Chloe at Hope House—our standard post-event routine. I was mid-bite when my phone buzzed on the counter.

*Adrian: Morning, beautiful.*

A smile tugged at my lips. God, it felt amazing to actually be wanted. I used to crave this exact feeling from Declan, but he always kept his distance. We could've been great. Whatever. His loss. Now I had a guy who actually appreciated me.

*Me: Morning! 😊*

*Adrian: What's the plan for today?*

*Me: Not much. Heading to Hope House now, then meeting Piper tonight.*

*Adrian: Nice. Miss you already.*

*Me: Oh yeah? Miss me, or just miss my body? 😉*

I bit my lip, staring at the screen. Holy crap. I'd never been this forward before. It was terrifying, but honestly? Kind of hot.

*Adrian: Both. Fuck, I'm literally hard just thinking about how tight you feel.*

*Me: Seriously?*

My stomach did a little flip. He was turned on just by thinking about me. I used to think I had that effect on Declan, but now? Who knows. Maybe he was just picturing Aurora every time we slept together.

*Adrian: YES. Kind of awkward since I'm in the squad car and pretty sure my partner is staring at my lap right now.*

I snorted, laughing out loud. Adrian knew exactly how to boost my ego. He treated me like royalty. Declan? He just made me feel invisible.

*Me: Poor baby. Maybe I can help you out with that later.*

*Adrian: You sure? I was pretty rough last night. You're not sore?*

*Me: Totally fine. Don't worry about me. 😉*

A few minutes passed before his next text popped up.

*Adrian: Damn. Guess I gotta try harder next time. The goal was to make you feel every single slide with every step you took today.*

I finished my food and tossed the plate in the sink. I'd wash it later.

*Me: You can make it up to me tonight.*

No reply. He was probably busy. I grabbed my keys, locked the front door, and hopped into my car. I was practically buzzing as I drove. Finally, life was going my way. It was a quick drive to Hope House.

The place was stunning—all sleek lines, massive glass windows, and warm, inviting colors that blended perfectly with the green lawns. I drove up the long, tree-lined driveway toward the massive double-height glass entrance, flanked by towering columns.

Inside, the foyer was huge, with a killer chandelier dripping light everywhere. The layout was perfect—gorgeous but totally functional. The living room was bright and airy, packed with deep, comfy couches. The kitchen had top-tier appliances, marble counters, and a massive island with barstools.

Every kid got their own customized bedroom, styled exactly how they wanted. Plus, there was a game room, a theater, a library, a gym, an indoor pool, and a massive playground outside. Every Hope House was built exactly like this on acres of land. We didn't cut corners for these kids. When I hired the architect, I told him I wanted a place they'd actually be proud to live in. Next to Asher, this foundation was my entire world. Saving these kids made everything worth it.

The younger kids swarmed me the second I walked into the living room. It was a lazy Saturday, so everyone was just chilling. I hugged a bunch of them, then headed deeper inside.

"Hey, Lyra," the teens called out from the couches.

"Hey, guys," I smiled. "Anyone seen Chloe?"

"In the office," Kira said. She was seventeen, kicked out by her parents at fifteen when she got pregnant. She'd been with us for two years now. She chose to keep the baby, and we backed her up. Her little girl, Monica, was the sweetest, most energetic baby ever. Everyone loved her.

"Thanks, Kira." I headed down the hall, waving to more kids and staff along the way.

Chloe was at her desk, flipping through a magazine. She looked up and grinned.

"Well, look who's famous," she said, tossing the magazine over.

My face was right on the cover, mid-speech from last night. The headline read: *AVA SHARP REVEALED AS MYSTERY FOUNDER OF HOPE FOUNDATION, NATION'S LARGEST CHILDREN'S CHARITY.*

I snorted and tossed it back down.

"There's a bunch more," Chloe said, fanning out a stack of tabloids on the desk. "The rumors are wild. You should read this stuff."

"Pass," I said, shaking my head. "I don't give a damn. Let's just get to work."

"Fair enough." She swept the magazines aside. "How are the numbers looking?"

"Amazing. The gala was a home run. Donations literally doubled overnight." She handed me a folder. I flipped through the pages, seriously impressed. Sure, half these rich snobs only donated to look good, but I didn't care. Money is money, and it was going to the kids.

"Look at this," Chloe said, tapping a line on the spreadsheet. "Your ex-husband dropped ten million dollars last night."

I stared at the number, completely stunned. Declan was always our top donor, but he'd never written a check that big in one go.

"Crazy, right?" Chloe smiled.

I nodded, staring at the page. Since day one, Declan had sent money every single month. Total count? Almost seventy million.

My phone buzzed in my hand. A text from Adrian:

*Adrian: Sorry babe, got slammed with work. Couldn't reply.*

I started typing back, but Chloe's voice cut me off. She sounded terrified.

"Lyra..."

I looked up. Her face was completely pale, eyes wide with sheer panic.

"Chloe? What's wrong?" My chest tightened.

"Your... your..." she stammered.

"Just say it!" I snapped, my heart hammering against my ribs.

She swallowed hard, her voice trembling. "Your house... it's on fire. Someone torched it."
