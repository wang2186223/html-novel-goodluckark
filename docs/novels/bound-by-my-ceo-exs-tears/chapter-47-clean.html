NovelVibe

Chapter 47

My face throbbed under the bag of ice. Orion stared. "Jesus, man. What did you do?"

"Adrian," I muttered. I pressed the cold plastic harder against my cheek. My brother was the last person I wanted to deal with right now. God, I was an idiot. I actually let that prick get under my skin enough to swing at him.

Orion raised an eyebrow. "The cop? Lyra's new guy?"

That did it. I ripped the ice pack off my face and hurled it. It hit the drywall with a wet smack. "He's not her guy," I barked, shoving myself out of the chair.

My chest burned. How could Lyra be so blind to his bullshit? The background check my guy ran came back clean. Just a regular, boring cop. But my instincts were screaming. The guy was hiding something behind that fake smile. And my gut? It never lied.

"Well, rumor says otherwise," Orion said. "So, what went down?"

I sucked in a breath, trying to stop the shaking in my hands. "We were hauling boxes into her new place. The bastard cornered me. Told me to stay away. Said she belonged to him now, and he wasn't letting me screw it up."

Orion just blinked at me. He looked at me like I'd lost my mind, trying to scan my face for sanity. "Are you kidding me? You threw hands over that?"

"Hell yeah I did!"

He dropped onto the couch with a heavy sigh. "Look, man. She's your ex. They're together. He's got every right to tell you to step back. Didn't you lay down the exact same rules for Lyra when you hooked up with Aurora?"

My knuckles turned white. Why was I the only one seeing through this guy? "It's not the same," I growled. "There's something seriously off about him. Why are you guys so damn blind?"

I started pacing the rug, back and forth, my blood boiling. I was right on the edge. First, Aurora threatens my kid and Corbin just shrugs it off. Now, I have to watch Lyra and this cop play house, staring at each other like teenagers. She was totally hooked. Brainwashed. Lyra usually doesn't trust anyone, yet she just lets this random dude move right into her life? It made no sense.

Orion sat up straighter. "What exactly are you getting at?"

I kept moving. If I stopped, I'd punch a hole in the wall. "I can't put my finger on it, okay? But he's bad news. I feel it in my bones." It sounded crazy, but I couldn't shake it. That's why my private investigator was digging into his life.

Orion watched me in silence. When he finally opened his mouth, I wanted to deck him. "You sure you're not just jealous?"

"Like hell I am!" I roared. "Why would I be jealous? I don't give a shit about Lyra like that. We've been over this." It was ridiculous. Me, jealous? No way. She was just my kid's mom. I was protecting my family. That was it.

"Dude, you guys were married for nine years," Orion countered. "You don't live with someone that long without catching feelings. And she's not a bad person."

I stared at him, dumbfounded, then shook my head. "No way. Not a chance. I stayed for Asher. Period. My heart was always with Aurora, and you know it. That's why I never felt anything for Lyra." The guy was nuts. Plenty of people stay in loveless marriages. Nine years didn't erase how much I resented her. Especially after she tricked me into her bed all those years ago.

"Then why do you care so much about her and Adrian?" Orion pressed.

"I told you! Any other guy, fine. But this cop? He's dirty." We were talking in circles, and my temper was redlining. I thought my own brother would have my back. Instead, he's playing therapist, spinning this dumb theory about me being secretly in love and jealous.

"Look, what she did back then was messed up," Orion said. "We dragged her through the mud for it. But what if she wasn't lying about being wasted? What if she told the truth?"

"No way," I snapped.

"Are you sure? We all pushed for you and Aurora. Except Lyra. When it blew up, we needed a scapegoat. It was easy to pin it on the girl who'd been crushing on you forever, rather than admit you both just screwed up."

I froze, staring at him. What the hell was wrong with him tonight? He pulled out his phone and started tapping the screen.

"What are you doing?" I asked, my eyes narrowing.

"Something we should've done years ago. I'm getting the truth about that night."

The line rang. I watched, numb, as Orion ordered the guy on the other end to pull the security tapes from the bar and the hotel. "I want them in an hour," he barked, then clicked off.

"Why are you dragging up this old shit?" I muttered. It was over. Done. We knew what happened. What was the point of digging up the grave?

"To get the truth, Declan. And to clear your head," Orion said, locking his grey eyes—so like my own—onto mine. "You've had feelings for Lyra for years. You're just too bitter about her 'betrayal' to admit it."

I spun around and dropped into my chair, keeping my face blank. No way was I letting him see how much this was shaking me. He was still using this cop drama to push his stupid theory about my buried feelings.

Silence stretched between us. I glared at him, but he didn't care, just kept tapping away on his screen. We sat there in the quiet of my office for what felt like hours. Then, his phone buzzed.

Orion stared at the screen. His face went pale, then completely slack with horror. My chest tightened. What the hell was on that screen? "Orion?" I muttered.

He looked up, his eyes totally hollow, and slid the phone across the desk. My hand shook as I picked it up. I already knew this was going to ruin me.

I looked down. The video started playing. I watched the screen, and my entire world shattered. Every single thing I'd believed for nine years crumbled to dust right in front of me. The real story of that night played out in grainy color.

"Fuck," I breathed, slamming my hands on the desk as I stood up. The weight of it was crushing.

She hadn't lied. Not once. We were both completely wasted. For nine fucking years, I'd hated her, punished her, blamed her for a setup that never happened.

"Goddammit!" I roared, the guilt ripping through my chest like a bullet.
