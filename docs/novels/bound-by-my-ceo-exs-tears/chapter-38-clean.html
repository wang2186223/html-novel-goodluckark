NovelVibe

Chapter 38

I was wrestling with my suit. This charity gala was the last place I wanted to be, but duty called. The big boss of the Hope Foundation was throwing a massive thank-you bash for the money guys. Since I write them huge checks, my name was on the VIP list.

My phone buzzed. Garrett. I swiped green. "Make it quick, Garrett. I'm in the middle of something."

"We got a hit," he said, skipping the small talk. "The blood from Lyra's place matched a profile."

My chest tightened at her name. God, the nasty shit I screamed at her still burned in my head. I shouldn't have lost my temper like that, but seeing her lay a hand on Aurora had pushed me over the edge.

"And?" I pressed. "Give me something good." I needed a win. I just wanted this whole nightmare over so we could all move on.

"It's not good, boss," he sighed.

I groaned. "Spit it out."

"The guy's street name is Black Serpent. Professional hitman. We've been tracking him forever. An anonymous tip gave us his location, but we got there too late. Someone put a bullet right through his forehead." Garrett sounded pissed. I felt the exact same rage. Our only lead was cold. We were right back to nothing.

"Did he leave anything behind? A motive? Why Lyra?" I was grasping at straws here.

"Zero," he said.

"What about the tipster?"

Garrett let out a heavy breath. "Probably the killer. My bet? They realized we were closing in on the guy's DNA. They silenced him before we could cuff him and make him squeal on whoever paid him."

The puzzle pieces clicked. I hated where this was going. "Wait. If they knew about the DNA, that means the threat is inside her circle. How else would they know we even had a sample?"

The press had kept it vague. The gossip rags only knew she got jumped at home. The cops never leaked a word about the blood evidence.

"Exactly. Or they have a mole feeding them info from the inside," Garrett said.

Just when I thought we were making progress, the rug gets pulled. How the hell do we catch a ghost?

"Anything else?" I asked.

"That's it for now."

"Keep your eyes open." I ended the call and yanked my tie into a knot. Going to this stupid party sounded like absolute torture now.

I grabbed my jacket and headed down to the waiting limo. Aurora was my plus-one, so we had to swing by her place. The driver pulled out and made the quick trip to her building.

I buzzed her door, and she was already waiting. She looked stunning, sure, but the old spark just wasn't there anymore. I felt nothing.

"Cat got your tongue?" she asked.

She sat opposite me, looking like a million bucks. I stared into her blue eyes, searching for... something. I didn't even know what.

"Just work stuff," I lied.

"Want to talk about it?"

Nope. Not in a million years. How could I admit that her sister was rent-free in my head?

Her lips pressed into a tight line. She hated being shut out, but she kept her mouth shut and glared out the window. I let out a breath. The silence in the car was suffocating. I hate tension, so I started tapping my fingers on my leg, begging the universe to make the ride go faster.

Thank god, we finally arrived. The limo stopped, I hopped out, and offered Aurora a hand. The second our feet hit the red carpet, the paparazzi went wild. Flashes blinded us.

"Mr. Woods! Are you dating your ex-wife's sister now?" a reporter yelled.

"Is Aurora the one who got away, and Lyra was just a mistake?" another shouted.

"Where's your ex-wife tonight, Declan?"

"Aurora, how's it feel sleeping with your sister's leftovers? You guys share a kid!"

Aurora's nails dug into my arm at that last one. I shielded her, pushing through the barrage of shouting reporters. We finally slipped past the heavy doors into the lobby.

The venue was decked out. I usually hate these fancy setups, but they nailed it. An usher showed us to our table. Orion, Corbin, Piper, and Ingrid were already there, sipping drinks.

Aurora did the polite greeting thing, but I skipped it. I pulled out her chair, sat down, and zoned out. My brain was stuck on Lyra. Was she safe? I couldn't even hear the chatter around me—until her name shattered my daydream.

"What the hell is Lyra doing here?" Aurora hissed, glaring at something behind my shoulder.

I spun around. Lyra was walking in with Adrian. Holy shit. She looked like a literal goddess. Her brown hair fell in perfect curls, and she wore this shimmering gold dress that hugged every curve. I'd never seen her look so stunning. I couldn't look away if my life depended on it.

She glided forward, Adrian right beside her. His hand rested on her bare lower back, sending a jolt of heat through me. They walked right past us. She gave Piper a quick wave and a smile, but she completely iced the rest of us.
