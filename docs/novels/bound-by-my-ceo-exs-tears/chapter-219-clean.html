Chapter 223

"You're kidding me, right?" His face says he doesn't buy a single word.

Before I can even open my mouth, a sharp knock rattles the door. Garrett mutters an apology, slides out of his chair, and pulls it open. Of course, Declan walks in. Why wouldn't he? I don't even blink.

"Perfect timing, man," Garrett says, waving him in. "Lyra was just about to drop the real shooter's name. She's convinced the cops got it wrong and it wasn't Aurora."

Declan shuts up. He just locks his eyes on me. I stare right back, keeping my glare steady, though the fire in my chest is finally starting to cool down.

"Look," I say, leaning forward. "This isn't some wild guess. It's facts. I saw the person who pulled the trigger. It wasn't Aurora. Someone's using her as a fall guy."

Declan watches me, his eyes scanning my face. "You remembered," he says. No question mark. Just a flat-out fact.

I nod. My heart does a stupid little flip. Seriously, how does he do that? I didn't say a damn thing, and he already knew.

"Who was it?" Declan's voice cracks a little, almost like he's begging.

"Ingrid," I breathe out. The name still tastes like poison. "Right before everything went black, she pulled down her mask. Just enough for me to see that nasty, smug grin. I'll never forget it."

The room goes dead silent. Declan and Garrett freeze, their faces totally blank, but I can feel the shock waves hitting them.

"Are you positive?" Garrett finally asks.

I get it. It's a tough pill to swallow. Every single piece of evidence pointed straight at Aurora. It was a closed case.

"Yes, I'm sure. Think about it. Who hates my guts more than Ingrid? Nobody. She's been throwing herself at Declan for years, trying to be his little side-piece, but he never gave her the time of day."

Declan shoots me a warning look. Too bad. He can hate the truth all he wants, but it's right there. Ingrid wanted him, and she didn't care if she was just a dirty secret as long as she got him.

"She was always pissed about that," I continue. "She couldn't stand that he chose me over making her his mistress. We clashed, like, a dozen times. She threatened to ruin me more than once. Especially after Declan kicked her to the curb for messing with me."

Just thinking about Ingrid gives me the literal chills. The woman is a total psycho. Honestly, how did everyone miss the red flags?

Declan starts to open his mouth, but I cut him off. I need to wrap this up, go home, and sleep for a week.

"Like I told Declan, Aurora is a lot of things, but she's no killer. I knew it the second you cuffed her. It kept nagging at me, this gut feeling that she was innocent. I couldn't explain it until my brain finally unlocked the memory this morning."

"Okay, I believe you," Garrett says, scratching his head. "But explain this: how did Aurora's prints end up on the gun that almost put you in a casket?"

I already have the answer ready. No use wasting time.

"Ingrid played her. The second Aurora got back, Ingrid acted like her new best friend. They bonded over hating me, sure, but Ingrid had a plan. She set her up. But if you want to prove it, you need to get Aurora in here."

Garrett hesitates, looking torn. Then he sighs, shoulders slumping, and grabs his phone. He barks an order to bring Aurora down and hangs up.

We sit in heavy silence until the door clicks open. A deputy leads Aurora in. God, she looks like hell. I've never seen her this wrecked. She looks completely hollowed out, like she's just waiting to die.

My chest tightens. Seeing her like this hurts. The girl standing there is just a ghost of who she used to be. It does something to me.

"Hey, you okay?" Before I can stop myself, I reach out, grab her hand, and give it a gentle squeeze.

She blinks at me, totally thrown off, and her eyes instantly well up. She doesn't say a word, but she grips my hand back like a lifeline.

"Aurora, Lyra thinks you didn't do it," Garrett says, breaking the silence. "But I need some details before I can let you walk."

Her eyes go wide.

"I need you to focus, okay? This is huge," I tell her. She nods, looking like a scared kid.

"Ingrid shot me. She framed you because she knew everyone would blame you first."

"W-what?" she stammers.

"Did Ingrid ever have access to your place or your clothes?" I ask, giving her hand a little shake to keep her focused.

"Yeah," she whispers. "She had a spare key. We hung out there sometimes."

Garrett steps in. "Okay. Did you ever touch a gun she showed you?"

Aurora starts to shake her head, then freezes. Her face goes pale.

"Oh my god," she gasps. "That absolute bitch."

"You got something?" Declan prompts, leaning in.

She nods fast. "A few weeks ago, she took me to her family's ranch. Said I needed to clear my head. We were walking through the house, and she showed me this glass gun case. Said her family loved hunting. She asked if I wanted to hold one. I'd liked shooting when we practiced, so I said sure. She put on these white gloves and handed me the gun. I was so depressed, I didn't even think about why she was wearing gloves and I wasn't."

Jesus. Ingrid is a cold, calculating psycho. She planned every single step of that little ranch trip.

"Can you describe the gun?" Garrett asks, leaning forward, eyes sharp.

Without a second thought, Aurora rattles off the details. When she finishes, we all look at Garrett.

Garrett lets out a long breath, staring at the ceiling before looking back at us. "Well, damn. You're right, Lyra. That's the exact gun. Only me and two other guys on the force knew the model. If Aurora knows what it looks like, she's clean. We need to grab Ingrid before she runs."
