Chapter 361

Lyra stepped up. She gave Jasper the real-deal mom love I starved him of. The exact stuff he craved from me. I see it now, clear as day. The second she took him in—way before anyone knew the truth—Jasper checked out on me. He just stopped trying. He threw in the towel on us.

"I get it, Aurora. I really do." Celeste slides a tissue across the desk. "But seriously, where was this fire back then? Why did you freeze Jasper out?"

I’ve beaten myself up over that exact question a million times.

Eight years. For eight damn years, I pretended he didn't exist. Treated him like garbage. Kept him locked out of my life.

"God, it sounds so pathetic now," I choke out. "But back then? I couldn't stand anything reminding me of the time Declan and I were apart. Jasper was just... an accident. A mistake. I didn't want some other guy's kid messing up my clean slate with Declan. I wanted to look perfect for him."

"No offense, but that's incredibly dumb, isn't it?" Celeste doesn't sugarcoat it. "Declan had Asher with Lyra. Same deal, totally unplanned. But even if you two ended up together, Declan would never have dumped his kid."

Boom. Right in the gut. Her words absolutely wreck me.

She’s right. Declan would’ve never abandoned Asher for me. Hell, he dumped me the second he heard the toxic crap I said about his boy. Meanwhile, I threw my own son away. Who am I kidding? I bailed on Jasper the second he popped out of me.

My chest tightens, a sharp pain ripping through me. I feel totally stripped bare, like my worst sins are on display for the whole world. How do I even fix this with Jasper now? Celeste just held up a mirror and showed me what a monster I was to a helpless little kid. My own kid.

"Hey, I’m not here to beat you up or judge you, Aurora," Celeste says, softening her voice. "My job is to help you heal, but to do that, I have to show you who you really are. You need to face your screw-ups so you can actually forgive yourself. I have to drop some hard truths so you can finally move the hell on."

She stops, locking eyes with me while I try not to sob. "What you did to Jasper? To your own flesh and blood? It’s messed up. You probably scarred him for life, just like your family scarred Lyra. Think about it. How did he feel every time you ignored him? Every time you treated him like trash? Every time you pushed him away? He’s a little boy, Aurora. That kind of rejection does serious damage."

I gasp, my lungs locking up. I can't breathe. My throat is tight, totally choked with tears, and my soul feels bruised black and blue. I don't want to hear this. I want to run away from her words. They’re like knives stabbing right into my bleeding chest. The agony is totally overwhelming.

I thought I knew what pain was when I found out Declan slept with Lyra. Nope. That was a walk in the park compared to this. This is gut-wrenching. It’s soul-crushing. I don't even know how to crawl back from this.

Celeste gives me a look of pure pity. "Yeah... I think we're done for today."

Her voice barely registers. My ears are buzzing, drowning in the sound of my own heart shattering. It kills me. I did the exact same toxic shit to Jasper that my parents did to Lyra.

How do I even look him in the eye now? Maybe I don't deserve him. Maybe I don't get a do-over.
