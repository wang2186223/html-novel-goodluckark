Chapter 470

The whole room freezes. I lock up. Look, I’m a workaholic. My inner circle knows the rule: do not show up at my office unless someone is literally dying.

"Si?" Martha’s voice snaps me out of the daze.

"My bad," I blurt, scrambling. "Right behind you."

My chest is hammering. Worst-case scenarios start looping in my brain. Like, is there a cop downstairs waiting to tell me someone crashed their car?

I scan Martha’s face, but she’s a brick wall. Zero emotion. Not a single clue about what’s waiting for me down there.

I look back at my crew. They throw me some pity smiles. God, it’s middle school all over again—getting dragged to the principal because Clara Sterling talked me into some dumb, wild stunt.

"Go on," Benjie nudges. "I got this covered."

I give a quick nod and tail Martha out. Right at the threshold, Blair sneers, loud enough for me to hear.

"Bet it's her geriatric sugar daddy. Probably buying her silence so the wife doesn't find out."

I roll my eyes. Seriously, where does she get this fanfiction? The mental gymnastics she does to make me the bad guy is honestly impressive. I tune her out and keep walking.

"Hey, is everything okay? Who's down there?" I press.

Martha looks over her shoulder, sporting a tiny smirk. "You'll see."

Super helpful. But hey, she’s smiling. That’s a good sign, right? Bad news usually comes with a frown. I cling to that thought, even though my hands are clammy and my pulse is going haywire.

We hit the lobby. Empty. I look around, confused, and Martha just chuckles, sliding behind her desk. "Check outside."

I take a breath, steel my spine, and push through the glass doors.

The moment I hit the pavement, there he is. I’d recognize that posture anywhere, even from behind.

Ugh, damn it. What is his deal? Why is he here?

My brain goes into overdrive. Okay, pivot. I’ll just slip back inside before he turns around. Avoidance is totally a valid strategy. I do not have the bandwidth for his drama today.

I spin on my heel, ready to run, but his heavy baritone stops me cold.

"Really, Piper? You're just gonna walk away?"

I let out a massive sigh and turn back. "Yeah, actually."

Boom—he’s right in my face, glaring daggers. God, I hate being short. I wish I could look him in the eye without having to crane my neck.

"Pretty sure we're done talking," I snap, keeping my voice steady. "So why are you stalking my office? I blocked your number for a reason, dude."

"You think you can just walk away? After what you did? After the crap you pulled?"

Classic. Another round of the blame game for stuff I didn't even do. Honestly, I bet he makes this shit up in his head, or maybe Seraphina and Delilah just feed him sob stories to keep him hating me. Yeah, definitely both.

"Okay, fine," I sigh, crossing my arms. "Do tell. What's my crime today?"

"Don't play fucking dumb!" he rumbles, the anger vibrating in his chest.

"Well, I must have amnesia, so spell it out for me."

"You slapped Delilah!" he explodes, practically vibrating with rage.

Oh. Right. Totally forgot about that.

"Okay, yeah, I did. But she—"

"So you admit it!" he cuts in, triumphant. "You actually hit her."

"I literally just said yes!" I snap, my patience wearing paper-thin. Dealing with Asher these days is like babysitting a toddler having a massive meltdown. It's freaking exhausting.

"And then you had the nerve to tell her to pack her bags? Claiming you're gonna replace Seraphina in my life and my house?"

"Whoa, hold the hell up, I never—"

He invades my space, crowding me so close I can barely breathe.

"You jump her at the mall, and then you trash-talk her like that? She was hysterical! Utterly humiliated! And now you're threatening to kick her out and take Seraphina's place? Seriously, Piper, that is low, even for you."
