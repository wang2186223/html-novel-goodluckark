Chapter 80

"Rise and shine!"

I groan, keeping my eyelids glued shut. The voice is a distant echo. Must be dreaming. I mean, why else would it sound so damn familiar?

"Lyra, wake the fuck up!"

Okay, eyes open. That was way too loud for a dream. And seriously, why would I dream about her of all people?

My vision’s a blurry mess. I blink, trying to focus. Then, boom. The memory hits me like a truck.

Son of a bitch. Kidnapped. Again.

My head feels like it's stuffed with cotton. Chloroform, definitely. I take a slow breath, praying the chemicals didn't mess with my baby.

I’m stuck in a chair, wrists bound tight behind my back. I give them a tug. Nothing. The ropes bite hard into my skin. Whoever grabbed me wasn't taking any chances.

"You done yet?" she asks.

Great, so it wasn't my imagination. I whip my head left. Aurora. Also tied to a chair. She looks way more alert, meaning she got dragged in here before me.

"What the hell are you doing here?" I blurt out, sounding like an idiot.

I scan the room. It’s just a massive, empty concrete box. No furniture, no nothing, except our two chairs. Zero clues about where we are or who owns this dump.

"Oh, I don't know. Maybe we both got snatched?" she shoots back, dripping sarcasm.

I roll my eyes. "No shit, Sherlock. I mean why would anyone grab *both* of us?"

I don't know who's behind this, but I have a pretty good guess. The only real threat left is someone who thinks Aurora matters more to the Sharps and Declan than I do.

"No clue."

"How long have you been sitting there?" I try to slow my breathing. Panicking won't help.

But the more I try to chill, the more my brain drags me back to the night Adrian screwed me over. It’s the exact same movie playing again. Only difference? Back then, it was Piper tied up next to me, not Aurora.

Cold dread creeps down my spine. What if it happens again? What if I’m about to find out another close friend stabbed me in the back?

"Honestly? Not sure. I woke up right when they threw you in here," Aurora’s voice snaps me out of the spiral.

"And how long was I out?"

"Like, fifteen, twenty minutes max."

I shut up and try to do the math. It’s useless. I have no clue how far they drove us from the ice cream parlor.

Then the real panic hits. Asher. Oh my god. Who’s picking him up? School lets out at three-thirty. It was barely noon when I got to the shop. What if they went after him too?

*Get a grip,* I tell myself. *Breathe. The school will call Declan if you don't show. He’s safe.*

I repeat it like a mantra until my chest stops heaving. Asher is fine. Right now, I need to worry about my own skin.

"So, the rumors are true. You’re pregnant," Aurora says, her eyes dropping to my stomach.

Even tied to a chair as a hostage, the girl looks gorgeous. Like, how is that even fair? Was she a literal goddess in a past life or what?

I don't say a word. I just stare straight ahead. My baby is none of her business.

I start wriggling, trying to loosen the knots. The hemp burns my wrists, but I don't care. I am not sitting here playing nice with her.

"Can you just shut the fuck up and let me think?" I snap.

She scowls. "Why do you have to be such a bitch? That’s not helping."

"And your mouth is? Zip it."

She just won't stop. Her voice is like nails on a chalkboard. She always got on my nerves, but right now, it’s driving me insane.

The door swings open and a guy walks in. I glare at him. "Hey, asshole. Why don't you untie me? Once I'm done with her, you're next."

The guy just chuckles. He thinks it's a joke. To him, I'm about as threatening as a toddler.

"Oh, you think that's funny?" I sneer. "Is this your day job? Snatching defenseless women? Does drugging girls make you feel like a big, tough man?"

"Shut your mouth!" he barks, his face twisting into an ugly scowl.

He stalks toward me. Heavy, dangerous steps. Like a predator closing in.

"Or what?"

"Are you insane? Stop," Aurora whispers, her voice shaking. "Don't push him."

I ignore her and smirk right at the giant. "They should honestly just castrate you. Any guy who needs drugs to handle two women is a total pussy. You're pathetic."

Honestly, I don't even know why I'm poking the bear. I just can't help it.

I shut up as Damien—the guy pacing in front of us—starts talking. My heart is hammering against my ribs.

"See, your ex-husband pissed off the wrong crowd," Damien says, stepping back and forth. "And sucks for you two, but you're caught right in the middle."

Aurora looks petrified, though she tries to hide it. Me? I'm scanning the room, trying to find a way out of this mess.

"What did he do?" I ask, keeping my voice dead even.

Damien stops pacing. A nasty smirk spreads across his face. "He crossed someone very powerful. Someone who doesn't let things slide."

"And that involves us how, exactly?" I snap, faking a confidence I don't feel.

"You're leverage," Damien says flatly. "If we can't touch him directly, we use you."

My stomach drops. Shit. This is bad. Really bad.

"We have nothing to do with his shit," Aurora says, her voice cracking a bit. "Why are we paying for his mistakes?"

Damien laughs. "Because, sweetheart, that's life. Sometimes the innocent get screwed over for someone else's sins."

My fists clench. Anger flares hot in my chest. "And you think that's okay?"

"Doesn't matter if it's okay," Damien says, leaning in close. "It works."

The door behind him flies open. Another massive goon steps in, looking stressed.

"Boss, we got a situation," the guy says, looking at Damien.

Damien frowns. "What is it?"

"It's Declan. He's here."

Damien's eyes go wide, then a grin splits his face. "Perfect. Bring him in."

A wave of relief hits me, mixed with pure terror. Declan is here. But what the hell is going to happen now?

The door bangs open again and Declan walks in. He looks absolutely murderous, his eyes scanning the room.

"Lyra, Aurora," he says, his voice low and dangerous. "You guys okay?"

"We're fine," I say quickly, though my heart is in my throat.

Declan glares at Damien. "Let them go."

Damien chuckles. "Or what? You're gonna kill me?"

"If I have to," Declan says, dead serious.

Damien's smile vanishes. "You don't get to make demands here, Declan."

"Try me," Declan says, stepping forward.

Before I can blink, the goon next to Damien lunges at Declan. They grapple, but Declan slams him to the floor in seconds, knocking him cold.

Damien looks pissed, but he doesn't move. "This isn't over, Declan. You're gonna pay for this."

Declan doesn't even bother answering. He walks over and starts tearing at my ropes, then Aurora's. "We're leaving," he says, his voice dropping to a soft murmur as he looks at me.

I nod, a massive wave of relief washing over me as my hands go free. Aurora and I scramble up, and Declan leads us out.

As we head for the exit, a tiny spark of hope flares up. Maybe, just maybe, we're gonna make it out of this in one piece.
