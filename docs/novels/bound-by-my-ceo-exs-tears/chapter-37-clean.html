Chapter 37

Sunlight hits my eyelids. I blink, totally disoriented. Wait, how did I get back to my own bed? Then I feel it—a heavy, warm hand draped over my waist. Oh. Right. It all comes rushing back.

My chest tightens. Panic mode: activated. I can't wake Adrian up. Seriously, not right now. My brain is a total dumpster fire, and the last thing I need is him witnessing my mental breakdown. I slide out from under his arm, moving like a ninja.

He rolls over, grunting some half-asleep gibberish, but thankfully stays under. I let out a breath I didn't know I was holding. I grab some clothes, snatch my phone off the dresser, and slip away.

I tiptoe toward the door. I twist the knob, and—*creak*. God, I freeze. My heart literally leaps into my throat. I look back, terrified. But Adrian doesn't move. Thank god.

The blanket is tangled around his hips, showing off those ridiculous, chiseled abs. One arm is thrown over his eyes. I swallow hard, my throat totally dry, and slip out the door.

I creep down the stairs. Honestly? I feel like I'm doing the walk of shame, which is hilarious because this is *my* house. But my thighs are sore. A very real, very physical reminder that Adrian took his job of distracting me from my pain *very* seriously last night.

The second my feet hit the kitchen tile, the dam breaks. All that anxiety I was trying to choke down upstairs? It hits me like a freight train.

"Get a grip," I mutter to myself. "People hook up. It's fine." But my heart is hammering against my ribs like a trapped bird. It's not helping.

I start pacing. Back and forth. I still can't process it. I slept with another guy. I spent years convinced Declan would be the only man to ever touch me, the only one to see me naked. Yet here we are. I didn't just let Adrian kiss me; I let him straight into my bed.

I slump onto a kitchen stool, exhausted from pacing. My foot taps a frantic rhythm on the floor. What's the play here? How do I act when he wakes up? Is there a rulebook for this?

Do I make him coffee? Pancakes? Does he even want that? Was this a one-time distraction, or are we... something else now?

I press a hand to my chest. It feels like it's about to explode. I've never done the casual thing. Even without the Declan mess, I always thought I wasn't built for this.

*You don't need to be in love to have sex, Lyra,* a voice whispers in my head. *You just have to want him.*

I want to argue with my own brain, but my phone buzzes, cutting me off. I swipe to unlock it.

HAPPY BIRTHDAY.

I stare at the screen. Who even...? Then I see the sender. Declan. My jaw drops. Then, the heat rises.

The audacity. Seriously, the nerve of this guy. He never once wished me a happy birthday when we were actually married. And now he sends this? After all the toxic garbage he spewed at me?

I jump up and start pacing again. Why now? Why after the divorce? Why today of all days, literally hours after I just climbed out of another man's bed?

Look, I know you're not supposed to compare, but Adrian absolutely wrecked me last night. In the best way. It was all heat and teeth and raw passion—everything Declan never gave me. Adrian had me bent over, twisted in ways I didn't know my body could go. I loved it. And I hated it, because it just proved how dead my marriage had been.

The only time Declan ever touched me with that kind of intensity was our first time. And that was only because he thought I was Aurora. The freaking love of his life.

I spent years begging for that spark. I knew something was missing. It wasn't awful, but I always wanted *more*.

Now, after one night with Adrian, I get it. The missing piece was passion. And we didn't have it because Declan didn't want *me*.

I force the ache in my chest back down. No. I am not going down the rabbit hole of wondering if my ex-husband held back in bed because he was fantasizing about someone else.

I look back at my phone, desperate for a distraction.

My screen is full of birthday texts. Corbin, Piper, my mom, even Orion. I ignore them all, making a mental note to text Piper back later.

I just don't get it. Why are they all reaching out now? What do they want?

*To be forgiven?* that annoying voice whispers.

Forgiveness. Right. Such an easy word, such a massive pain in the ass.

How am I supposed to forgive them when they never gave me a pass? They broke me. They wouldn't let me breathe after everything went down.

Adrian was right. Declan and I were both wasted that night. But who got dragged through the mud? Me. Just me. I was the villain, the home-wrecker, the girl everyone looked down on.

I took all the screaming, all the insults. I swallowed the blame because I loved Declan.

The more I replay it, the hotter my blood gets. Tears prick my eyes, hot and angry, and this time, I don't try to stop them.

I am so done. So freaking tired of taking the high road. I lost everything that night, too.

But nobody cared about my guilt. Nobody saw me drowning. It was always about Declan. Always about Aurora.

It was always about him. Why did I have to shatter just to keep us afloat? Why did I have to break so they could feel clean? They ruined me, and nobody even cares. Nobody sees my pain.

Something snaps inside me. The dam doesn't just leak; it explodes. All the agony I've been shoving down for months comes roaring to the surface.

A scream tears out of my throat. It sounds wild, animalistic, echoing off the kitchen walls. I lash out. My fist slams into the nearest breakable thing on the counter.

Glass shatters. Wood cracks. The noise fills the room, a perfect echo of the absolute wreck inside my chest. My heart is splintering all over again. The pain is eating me alive.

Every single ounce of buried misery claws its way up, tearing through my throat.

I hate them. I hate Declan for what he did to me.

"Lyra."

I spin around.

Adrian is standing in the doorway, shirtless, staring at me in absolute shock. The kitchen is a disaster zone. The barstools are knocked over, glass everywhere.

Seeing him there, my knees just give out. I collapse onto the floor, not even caring about the glass shards biting into my skin. Physical pain is nothing compared to this.

"I hate him," I sob, my voice cracking. "I gave him everything. He just took and took, and I let him. He left me with nothing, Adrian. I'm empty. It's so dark and cold. How do I live like this? How do I let go? I'm so tired of fighting. This weight is too heavy."

Why couldn't I see it before? I was suffocating, trying to save a sinking ship. I was living in the wasteland they banished me to.

Then, his arms are around me. "Let it go, Lyra. Just let the pain out. It's the only way," he murmurs.

So I do.

My nails dig into his shoulders as I sob, pouring all my agony into him. He doesn't pull away. He doesn't make a sound. He just takes it.

He holds me tight while the wreckage of my past, my unhealed wounds, lie scattered around us on the floor. A physical mess for my mental disaster.

My demons tear me apart as I completely unravel, letting all the years of hurt finally explode.

Eventually, I go limp against him, totally empty. He gently scoops me up, carrying me bridal-style up the stairs. My eyelids grow heavy, and for the first time, a weird sense of peace washes over me.

Maybe I was guilty. Maybe I messed up. But I've paid my dues. It's time to leave the trash in the past. Time to heal.

I'm done hiding in the dark. It's time to actually live.
