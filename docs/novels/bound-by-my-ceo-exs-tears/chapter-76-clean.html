I locked the guilt in a box just to let my body get its fix.

I didn't love Lyra, but cheating was off the table. I watched my parents' trainwreck of a marriage and swore I'd never break my vows. The temptation was always there, throwing itself at me, but I never bit. I couldn't do that to her.

I fist my hair, tugging hard enough to hurt. God, the raw hunger that just ripped through me nearly buckled my knees. Her body is burned into my retinas. All I can picture is sliding her lace underwear out of the way and burying myself deep inside her.

Malls aren't my thing. My wardrobe is strictly custom-tailored. But Mom had begged me for a favor.

Running into Lyra wasn't on the agenda. I was literally starting my engine when I spotted her in the lot. She was acting twitchy, looking over her shoulder. Curiosity flared. What the hell was making her so jumpy? So, I trailed her.

Huge mistake. Now she's going to build a ten-foot wall between us. I slam my car door, crank the ignition, and tear out of there, heading straight for my folks' place.

My chest is so tight I can barely draw breath. Every muscle is a coiled spring. And Lyra—damn it, she's still stuck on loop in my head.

I fly down the highway and park. I grab the toy from the passenger seat and march inside. Quick drop-off, then I'm heading straight to my club to get absolutely wasted.

"About time. Did you find it?" Mom looks up from the couch. I give a stiff nod, grinding my teeth to keep from snapping.

But moms have radar for this shit. She frowns, reading my face instantly. "Okay, what's wrong?"

I don't do feelings, but for a split second, I actually want to spill. Then sanity kicks in. How do I even phrase that? *Hey Mom, remember that girl we spent ten years despising? Yeah, she's got me completely whipped. I can't think about anything else.*

How do I tell her that if Lyra hadn't shoved me, I would've taken her right there on the fitting room floor? And that wouldn't even have satisfied me—I would've dragged her back to my place to do it all over again.

"Is this about the press?" Mom's voice cuts through my head.

"What press?" I blink.

She sits up, grabs her phone off the coffee table, taps the screen, and shoves it at me. I sink into a chair, staring at a headline from an hour ago: *Declan Thorne spotted at baby boutique with ex-wife Lyra. Baby number two on the way? We're digging for details on the pregnancy rumor...*

"Son of a bitch," I growl, my hands curling into tight fists. Only four people knew about the baby. Lyra is going to absolutely lose her mind when she sees this.

I toss Mom's phone aside and whip out mine. I speed-dial my PR fixer in the media industry. She answers before the first ring finishes. "Sir?"

"Kill the story about me and Lyra. Now," I bark, barely holding back my rage.

"On it. Give me ten minutes," she says.

"And put the word out—any outlet that touches Lyra's personal life gets sued into bankruptcy."

"Understood, sir."

I kill the call, chest heaving.

Lyra hates the spotlight. I'll shield her from this media circus, no matter the cost. Period.

"What's the deal with you two, Declan?" Mom asks quietly. Her eyes drill into mine, searching for the truth. Thank God she doesn't ask if the rumor is true. That's Lyra's secret, and I'd take it to the grave anyway.

"I don't damn well know," I snap, frustrated.

"Eleanor says you're freezing Aurora out. Aurora claims you dumped her and went radio silent. Is Lyra the reason?"

I consider lying, but why bother? "Partly," I mutter.

Mom gasps. I keep my eyes glued to the floor, not wanting to see her disappointment.

The silence stretches. To break it, I ask, "Did you know Lyra wasn't actually Eleanor and Frederick's kid?"

"Yes," she says simply.

"Is that why you treated her like trash?"

"No," she replies. That catches me off guard.

"I hated her because she snuffed out your spark," Mom says. "You were crazy about Aurora, and Lyra's obsession ruined that. Watching you turn into a walking corpse nearly broke me.
