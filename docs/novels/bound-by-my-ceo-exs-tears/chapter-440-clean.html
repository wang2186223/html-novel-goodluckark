Chapter 449

Honestly? If it weren't for my sisters and the twins, I would’ve ended it all a long time ago. When the dark thoughts hit, they were my anchor. I couldn't bear the thought of breaking them.

"How’re you holding up, big bro?" Aurora asks. She’s trying to hide the worry in those blue eyes of hers, but I see right through it.

"Busy," I shrug. "Between the kids and the business, I barely have time to breathe."

"Look, parenting alone is a beast," she says, keeping her voice low. "But we’ve got your back, okay? You don't have to play the tough guy twenty-four-seven."

God, I love my sisters. Seriously, they’re the absolute best, and I’m so damn proud of the women they’ve become.

My throat tightens up. I’m about to choke out a reply when Mom yells from the kitchen. "Asher! Quit hoarding your sisters and get your butt in here!"

We exchange a quick smirk and head toward the noise.

The second I cross the kitchen threshold, I dive straight into Mom's hug.

"Alright, spill. What’s wrong?" she whispers, holding onto me tight.

"Nothing," I shoot back, way too fast. "Just sleeping like crap lately."

She pulls back and gives me that classic, laser-focused motherly squint.

"Don't BS me, Asher. A mother always knows. Plus, you’ve dropped weight this week."

I look away, dodging her gaze. If she keeps digging, she’s going to unearth the exact secrets I’m killing myself to hide. "Where's Dad?" I ask, changing the subject.

"In his office," she says, her eyes still drilling into mine. "He'll be out in a sec."

In the living room, the twins are already giggling with their aunts. The whole house feels alive, filled with that loud, infectious kid-energy that warms up the cold walls.

For a while, I actually thought about packing up and moving back here. Ever since Seraphina left, my place hasn't felt like home. The warmth walked out the door with her, leaving nothing but freezing rooms and empty echoes.

"Grab the silverware and help me set the table," Mom commands.

I’m halfway through throwing forks onto the table when Dad walks in. His face lights up the second he spots the twins, and they sprint straight for him.

He scoops them up like they weigh nothing, planting big kisses on their cheeks.

"We missed you, Grandpa!" they yell in unison.

"Missed you too, you little troublemakers," he chuckles.

"Hey, what about me?" I pipe up, teasing. "Do I not exist?"

Aurora snorts, while Hazel and Eliza start giggling.

"Nope," Dad grins, not missing a beat. "I only care about my girls and my grandkids."

"Mom, look at him!" I whine, playing along to make the twins laugh.

"Don't listen to him, sweetie," Mom says with a wink. "You'll always be my baby boy."

I stick my tongue out at them, and the kids mimic me instantly.

Once dinner is served, we crowd around the table—all eight of us. It’s pure chaos. Everyone’s talking over each other, throwing jokes back and forth.

I lean back in my chair, letting the noise wash over me. In this room, it’s easy to tune out the rest of the world. Easy to forget the agony. Easy to forget that I’m walking around with a shattered heart.

Everything is perfect. Then Mom clinks her fork down. "Okay, guys, I have some news."

"What, are you pregnant again?" Aurora teases, leaning in.

Mom rolls her eyes. "Hardly. But Piper is."

The words hit me like a physical punch to the gut. My hand jerks, and my fork slips. The walls feel like they're closing in. The room goes dead silent except for the frantic thudding of my own heart. For a second, I literally can't breathe. My chest just freezes.

The table absolutely explodes. The girls start squealing like crazy. Like I said, everyone loves Piper—my sisters included. I’ll never understand what the hell they see in that leech.

Then the interrogation begins.

"How far along is she?" Hazel asks.

"Is it a boy or a girl?" Eliza chimes in.

"Is there a wedding coming up?" Aurora’s eyes are practically sparkling.

Then Dad chimes in. "So... who's the guy? Is he decent? Someone reliable?"

I instantly choke on my food. Every single eye at the table snaps to me. I fight like hell to keep my face straight, trying to act like his question didn't just wreck me. I have to play it cool.

"You okay there, Asher?" Mom asks, her eyes narrowing again.

I swallow hard, forcing a nod. "Yeah. Yeah, just went down the wrong pipe. I'm good."

She stares at me, her gaze sharp, like she's slowly putting the pieces of a puzzle together. She lets out a soft hum and turns back to the others.

The chatter shifts right back to Piper. Everyone’s gushing, already planning how to spoil the kid. Aurora even claims the title of official babysitter.

I clench my jaw, keeping my face a total blank. But inside, my mind is spinning, panic clawing at my throat.

I have to get ahold of Piper tonight. I need to know if she opened her mouth to my mother. Because if she did—even a single word—I will make her life a living hell.
