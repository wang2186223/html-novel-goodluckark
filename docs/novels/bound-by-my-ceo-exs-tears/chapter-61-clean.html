NovelVibe

Chapter 62

I’m scrubbing the kitchen counters like my life depends on it. Sweat drips, but I don't care. Anything to stop my brain from spiraling. I’m pregnant. Still can’t wrap my head around it.

Declan shut down the whole "let's have another kid" talk ages ago, so I buried the dream of giving Asher a sibling. And now? Boom. Bun in the oven. I'm a total mess of emotions.

My phone buzzes on the table. Usually, I’d ignore it. Go ghost. But not today. Shutting out the world has only made things worse. I slide onto a chair, bone-tired, and swipe answer.

"Hey, Piper," I mumble. God, I've been so exhausted lately. Duh. It all makes sense now.

"Oh my god! You actually picked up!" Piper shrieks, her voice cracking with a sniffle. "I thought you were ghosting me again. It’s been weeks, Lyra."

"I'm sorry," I sigh, rubbing my temples. "I panicked. Everything got too heavy, so I shut down." That's my move. Stress hits, and I lock myself in a vault. Bury the feelings, pretend they don't exist, keep moving. I thought it was survival. Now I know it's just toxic.

"Are you okay now?" she asks.

"Not 100%, but I'm getting there." I mean it. I'll fight my way back. I still shudder thinking about how dark things got—how I actually considered ending it all, for both me and this baby. But this kid is mine. My blood. Just like Asher. I'm going to be the best damn mother to them. I shake my head, forcing the ugly memories back into the dark. No looking back.

Silence stretches over the line. I take a breath. "I'm pregnant," I whisper.

"Wait, what?!" Piper screams. "Since when?!"

"Found out last week."

Total accident, just like Asher. But I already love this tiny speck. Sure, I can't stand the dad right now, but the baby is innocent.

"Oh, sweetie... congratulations!" Piper’s shock melts into pure, warm joy.

"You sound happy," I say, relieved. "Honestly, I thought you'd freak, considering who the father is."

"Are you kidding? A baby is a miracle. This is exactly what you need to heal from all that trauma. It's your lifeline, Lyra. Perfect timing." She gasps, fighting back tears. "You were drowning, babe. We all saw it. This little one did what none of us could—it pulled you out of the deep end."

She's right. Asher and this new baby are my anchors. They're the reason I'm finally getting help.

"Thanks for not giving up on me, Piper. Even when I was a total nightmare." My eyes sting. Everything makes me cry these days. Hormones.

"Duh. You're my bestie. Basically my sister. I'd do it a million times over because you'd do the same for me."

We talk for another hour, keeping it light, laughing about stupid stuff. By the time we hang up, the heavy weight on my chest feels a little lighter. I actually feel like myself again. Not fully cured, but on the right track.

I finish up the chores and collapse onto the couch, completely wiped out. Then, the front door clicks open.

I stiffen. Who the hell just walks into my house?

Then, a tiny, familiar voice explodes through the hallway.

"Mommy! Mommy, I'm home!"

My heart leaps. I scramble off the couch, grinning like a maniac, and sprint to the foyer just as he bursts through.

"Asher!"

He slams into me. I squeeze him so tight, tears spilling over my cheeks. He fits perfectly in my arms. God, I missed him. I cover his face in sloppy kisses.

"Mom, stop!" he giggles, but he doesn't pull away.

"I missed you so much! What are you doing here?" I ask, pulling back just enough to look at his face, keeping my hands on his shoulders. We're sitting right on the floor, and I don't give a damn.

"Dad picked me up. He said you needed me. We wanted to surprise you, so I kept my mouth shut when we called yesterday!"

My eyes snap up. Declan is standing right there in the doorway. Our gazes lock. There's something intense in his eyes, but I can't read it.

"Hey," I say softly.

He’s been showing up every single day to check on me. Supportive. Gentle. It's bizarre. This isn't the Declan I know, and I don't know how to handle it. He actually hooked me up with a therapist three days ago, just like he promised. Every nice gesture catches me off guard.

"Hey, Lyra... how's today going?" he asks, offering a soft smile.

"Good," I say, shrugging. I'm taking baby steps, and that's enough.

Asher yanks on my sleeve, wide-eyed. "Is it true? Are you having a baby?"

I shoot a sharp glare at Declan. He rubs the back of his neck, looking totally guilty.

"Yeah, buddy," I say, my stomach doing a nervous flip. He's never mentioned wanting a sibling. I hold my breath.

But Asher just pumps his fist in the air, a massive grin splitting his face. "Yes! That is so awesome!" he yells. "Can you make it a girl? I really want a baby sister."

The tight knot in my chest loosens. If Asher is on board, I can handle this.

"It doesn't quite work like that, sweetie," I say, smoothing his hair. "It's a fifty-fifty shot. Might be a boy."

"Nope! It's a girl," he insists, crossing his arms with pure determination. "I'm getting a sister because I want one, and I always get my way."

God, he looks exactly like Declan right now. It's scary.

"Asher..." I start, wanting to manage his expectations.

"No, really—"

"Hey, champ," Declan interrupts. "How about you give your mom and me a minute to talk?"

Asher looks between us, nods, and runs upstairs. I'd given him a FaceTime tour of the place, so he already knows exactly where his room is.

I stand up, dusting off my knees. "How did you know I needed him?"

Declan shrugs. "Call it a gut feeling. Just knew."

"I'm thrilled he's here, but is it safe? The Kaelens are still out there, even if they've been quiet."

He sighs, shaking his head. "Don't sweat it. They won't touch Asher. I can't guarantee anything for the rest of them, but Asher is off-limits. That's all I care about."

Honestly? I don't care about the others either. Aside from Piper, the rest of them can go up in flames. Asher is my only priority.

"Thanks for bringing him back. And for... everything else lately," I say, genuinely grateful. He's really stepped up.

"Anything for you, Lyra."

I blink, a frown creeping onto my face. Hearing those words from him feels totally surreal. He's never cared about me before. I seriously want to feel his forehead to see if he's running a fever.

Before I can overthink his sudden personality transplant, heavy footsteps come thundering down the stairs.

"Mom! My room is sick! It's way cooler than it looked on video!" Asher yells, skidding into the room.

I laugh. Whatever is going on with Declan doesn't matter. It's not my problem. I have bigger fish to fry. Like how the hell I'm going to face Adrian tomorrow.
