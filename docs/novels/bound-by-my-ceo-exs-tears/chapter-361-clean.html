Chapter 368

I whirl around, scanning the massive space, then lock eyes with Orion. He’s just standing there, waiting for my reaction.

"Whoa. Orion, this place is unreal." I glimpse a hallway leading to more rooms—I'll check those out later. "Seriously, how many bedrooms are we talking about?"

He closes the gap between us in two strides. "Eight bedrooms. Plus two for guests."

My jaw drops. I just stare. My childhood home was big, sure, but it topped out at five bedrooms. And that felt like a mansion.

"Ten bedrooms? Are you insane?" A nervous laugh slips out. Like, what are we supposed to do with all that empty space?

He steps right into my personal bubble, slides his arm around my waist, and yanks me flush against him. I plant my palms on his chest. His heart thumps steady under my fingers.

"I wasn't joking about wanting a big family, Willow." His gaze locks onto mine, intense. "I'm just planning ahead."

"Oh my god! Am I getting a baby brother or sister?!" Clara’s screech shatters the moment.

We snap our heads toward her. Orion keeps his grip tight on my hip. Clara is practically vibrating, her eyes wide with hope.

"Not quite yet, kiddo, but fingers crossed," Orion says, flashing a cocky grin. Panic spikes in my chest. Oh god, we haven't been using protection lately.

Clara lets out a deafening squeal. She flies at us, throwing her whole weight into a hug.

"I'm gonna be a big sister!" She bolts, tearing through the empty rooms, her yells bouncing off the bare walls.

I shoot Orion a look. He’s just grinning. "Great. Now you've got her hopes up."

"Yep." He winks, smirk fully intact. "Because I know I can deliver on that promise."

I sigh, wiggle out of his hold, and scan the massive room again. "It's still way too big, Orion."

"Hear me out, Willow. Think about us, my brother, our whole crew. We're gonna have a small army of kids running around. Where do they crash during sleepovers? Or when someone needs us to babysit so they can get some, uh, private time?"

Okay, he has a point. I've gotten super close with Lyra, Celeste, and Piper. Lyra’s got two toddlers and a baby bump. Celeste is pregnant right now, and Kaelen is basically Orion and Declan's clone—he’ll definitely want a whole pack. Piper isn't pregnant yet, but she's dying to start a family.

It’s like he said—we're talking a massive squad of kids. We need space for them to crash when those backyard BBQs I keep picturing run way past bedtime. Or if the adults decide to crash here too.

"Wait, can Piper get her own room for sleepovers?" Clara skids to a stop right in front of us, interrupting her lap.

She clasps her hands under her chin, hitting us with the ultimate weapon: the puppy-dog eyes. She rarely deploys this move, but when she does? Game over. She gets whatever she wants.

Orion glances at me, checking if I'm cool with it. My chest tightens in a good way. He’s actually asking for my input instead of just making the call himself like he used to.

"Sure thing, kiddo. If that's what you want—and if your mom agrees to move in here," Orion tells her.

Her face lights up. Pure, infectious joy practically vibrates off her.

Both of them turn their gazes on me.

"So, what's the verdict, babe? Can you see us making this place our home?" Orion asks, his eyes searching mine.

I stare back. God, I love his eyes. Those grey depths are swirling with so much raw emotion right now. Hope. Anticipation. A whole future. It literally knocks the wind out of me.

"Please, mommy?" Clara pleads. The word slips out before she can catch it.

I look away, scanning the empty space. Suddenly, I can see it. Clear as day. Clara and a bunch of siblings tearing around the living room, laughing, tackling Orion to the rug while I watch and laugh from the kitchen counter.

A smile tugs at my lips. "Yeah. I can see it." I take a breath. "Our home."
