NovelVibe

Chapter 474

"What the actual fuck."

I stare at the screen. I blink, hard, hoping the words will magically scramble into something else. No dice. The text stays right there, burning my eyes.

**BILLIONAIRE HEIR NOAH WOODS SPOTTED IN PARKING LOT BRAWL WITH SIERRA MEYERS OUTSIDE BIO-RENEW**

My jaw goes tight. I swipe down. The internet is having a field day, and each title is a bigger trainwreck than the last.

**IS NOAH WOODS HEADING TO JAIL? ASSAULT CHARGES LOOMING FOR BILLIONAIRE HEIR**

**WOODS CORP HEIR CAUGHT IN VIOLENT SCANDAL**

**PR NIGHTMARE: IS THE WOODS EMPIRE CRUMBLING?**

Then come the photos. It’s me, grabbing Piper outside her office. The camera caught me at the absolute worst angle, freezing a split-second mistake and plastering it everywhere for the internet to tear apart.

**BILLIONAIRE HEIR ATTACKS SIERRA MEYERS DAYS AFTER HEIRESS STEPS IN TO SHIELD HER BESTIE**

"Dammit," I growl, squeezing Clara’s phone so hard I can hear the screen groan.

She snatches it back before I can smash it to pieces.

Her eyes could literally kill. "Are you out of your mind, Asher? Seriously. I know you can't stand Piper. I know you don't want the kid. But putting your hands on her? At her own office? What is wrong with you?"

"I didn't attack her," I mumble. But damn, even I don't believe me.

"Well, look at the photos, genius. That’s exactly what it looks like. You don't manhandle a girl half your size and expect people to see it any other way."

Shame hits me like a physical punch. I’ve got zero defense. Sure, Piper and I have had our wars, but I’ve never laid a hand on her. I’ve thrown nasty words, yeah. I’ve treated her like dirt, absolutely. But physical violence? That’s not me. I don't even know who the guy in those pictures is.

"Who even are you anymore, Asher?" Clara’s voice is dripping with pure disgust. "Seriously, this is a new low. Even for you."

Irony is a bitch. Those are the exact words I threw at Piper not long ago.

"I didn't mean to, okay?" I snap, slamming my fist onto the boardroom table. The bang echoes off the empty walls. "It just happened."

My knuckles throb, but the physical pain doesn't touch the chaos ripping through my chest. I'm drowning, and the current is pulling me under fast.

"That is a pathetic fucking excuse," she spits, her tone freezing over. "You’re the guy who’s always in control. Always. Don't sit there and play the 'I lost my temper' card like some hormonal teenager. That’s not you."

Her words hit a nerve because she’s right. My whole life has been about control. Think first. Measure twice. Keep your cool. And yesterday, I threw all of that out the window.

"Delilah told me—"

"Oh, shocker," Clara rolls her eyes, throwing her hands up. "One word from that snake and you lose your damn mind."

"Watch your mouth," I growl.

"Or what? You gonna grab me too?" She steps right up to me, staring me down. "I’ve been trying to warn you about them for years, but you’re too blind to see it. Delilah is poison, and Seraphina was a straight-up liar. Delilah whispers in your ear, and you go play tough guy with Piper. Now the whole world is watching you fall apart because you can't tell when you're being played."

I remember back in high school, right after Seraphina and Delilah transferred in. Seraphina, Clara, and Piper were inseparable. They did everything together.

Then, out of nowhere, Piper and Clara iced Seraphina out. Seraphina told me Piper got jealous when we started hanging out, and she turned Clara against her.

By the time we got married, the friendship was basically dead. Afterward, they cut Seraphina off completely. Clara wouldn't even look at her at family dinners. She loved the twins, but she absolutely hated their mother and sister.

"Delilah wouldn't lie to me."

"You are a special kind of stupid, Asher. Seriously. I don't even care what she told you—it's a lie. She wants you on a leash, and you're just handing her the collar."

I clench my jaw. "She said Piper wants to take Seraphina's place. That she's trying to kick her out."

Clara lets out a harsh, dry laugh. "First off, I’d kick Delilah out myself. She’s a total parasite. Second, Piper doesn't want you, Asher. Not your cash, not your family, and definitely not your love. You’ve broken her too many times. Get that 'replacement' garbage out of your head."

Damn. Why does that hurt to hear? It shouldn't bother me, but it does. I can't even explain why.

"And honestly," Clara adds, her voice cold as ice, "do you really think Piper would ever say something like that?"

I squeeze my fists so hard my nails bite into my skin. "It’s not like she’s been an angel to Delilah. She’s always hated both of them."

Clara just looks at me, her eyes full of pity and disappointment. She shakes her head. "You’re a lost cause, Asher. Seriously. Mark my words: one day, you’re going to regret everything you did to her."

Her words sink deep, like a drop of poison in my veins. A cold shiver runs down my back.

"Fix this mess," she orders. "Do you have any idea what you just did? Our dads built Wood Corp’s name from nothing, and you dragged it through the mud in five seconds. Investors don't do pity. They smell blood, and they will eat us alive."

My stomach does a flip. She’s right. One stupid move, and I’ve put the whole empire on the line.

Clara gives me one last dirty look, turns on her heels, and storms out. The door slams shut behind her.

I collapse into my chair, burying my face in my hands. Ever since that night with Piper, my life has been a non-stop trainwreck. Not a single second of peace.

Getting the media to take down the articles is easy. Making people forget? That's the real nightmare.

I reach for my phone, and right on cue, it starts buzzing.

My heart drops when I see the caller ID.

Dad.

I slide to answer, taking a shaky breath.

"Get your ass over here. Now." His voice is pure, shaking rage.

The line goes dead.

And just like that, I know things are about to get a whole lot worse.
