Chapter 69

Silence. Total, awkward silence. We chewed our food without saying a word. My brain was still short-circuiting from his apology. What did he want from me? Forgiveness? Yeah, right. Not happening.

The ride back was just as dead. Both of us staring ahead, lost in our own heads. I had no clue how to handle this new version of him. It was weird. Seriously weird.

"Thanks," I said when he pulled up to my place. "For the doctor's appointment. And the food."

"No sweat." He tried to smile, but it didn't reach his eyes.

I nodded and reached for the door handle. He grabbed my wrist.

"Let me know when your next appointment is," he said, locking his eyes onto mine.

I stared at him, trying to read his mind. "Why the hell would I do that?" I yanked my hand back. His touch felt like literal fire.

"Because I want to be there," he said simply.

"Again, why? You weren't there when I was pregnant with Asher. Now you want to play daddy to a kid that isn't even yours? Do you know how insane that sounds?"

He was messing with my head. I hated being confused. I hated when people acted out of character. Plus, I didn't want any drama with Aurora. If she found out Declan was tag-teaming my doctor visits, she'd lose her mind.

"I just want to be there. Is that so hard to believe?"

"Uh, yeah. Let's be real, Declan. You've never cared about me." I sighed. "Actually, that's putting it nicely. You hated my guts. So yeah, this sudden nice-guy act is sketchy as hell."

He didn't say a word. Just kept staring at me with that intense, heavy gaze. I started fidgeting under his stare. It was super uncomfortable.

"Just tell me," he finally said. "Because if you don't, I'll find out anyway and show up. Just like today."

"Have you even thought about Aurora? How is she going to feel about this?" I was totally using her as a shield, but she was my best bet to make him back off.

"Aurora doesn't fucking matter right now," he growled, his eyes turning ice-cold.

My jaw literally dropped. I just stared. Never in a million years did I think I'd hear him say Aurora didn't matter.

"What is wrong with you?" I asked, completely baffled. "Do you hear yourself? You've been obsessed with her your entire life."

The hits just kept coming. I couldn't even process them. Every word out of his mouth was rewriting my reality.

He clenched his jaw, his nose flaring. Mentioning his decade-long love for Aurora clearly pissed him off.

"Drop it," he snarled.

I guess Aurora was still a sore subject. Whatever. I knew they'd end up back together eventually. Aurora always got what she wanted, and Declan could never stay mad at her for long.

I'd said my piece. I pushed the car door open and got out, walking toward my house.

"Remember what I said, Lyra," he called out, making me turn around. "I'll be at those appointments. Whether you tell me or not."

He revved the engine and sped off. I watched him go, totally annoyed. He was being so pushy. Why couldn't he just go back to being a jerk?

I was almost at my front door when I noticed a guy watering his lawn next door. He turned around, like he felt me staring. Our eyes locked. I held my breath. Must be the new neighbor.

He was hot, sure, but that wasn't it. He looked incredibly familiar. I just couldn't place where I'd seen him before.

I shook it off, flashed a quick smile, and headed inside.

"Mommy! You're home!" Asher yelled the second I walked in.

Since my appointment was in the afternoon, I'd had his nanny pick him up. She was part-time, only coming when I was busy.

"Hey, buddy. How was school?" I asked, dropping my bag.

"Awesome! I got a perfect score on my math test!"

"Look at you, my little math genius," I teased, making him blush.

"Mom," he groaned. "Stop."

"What? It's true. I'm super proud of you."

He gave me this sneaky grin, and I knew I was being played.

"So, since I'm a math genius, can I play video games?"

Yep. Hook, line, and sinker.

I sighed. "Fine. But only for an hour."

He bolted upstairs, screaming "thank you!" over and over. I couldn't help but laugh.

"Hey, Maria. You're good to go," I told the nanny, walking into the kitchen.

"You sure?"

"Yep. Go ahead."

She smiled, grabbed her stuff, and left. Fifteen minutes later, the house was dead quiet, and I kind of wished I'd asked her to stay.

With Asher upstairs, I was totally alone with my thoughts.

I was thinking about starting dinner early when the front door clicked open.

"Yoohoo! Lyra, where you at?"

Piper's voice instantly cheered me up.

"In the kitchen!" I yelled back.

A minute later, she walked in—with Audrey right behind her.

"Look who I ran into outside," Piper said, hopping onto a barstool.

I'd told Piper how Audrey defended me against Anita, and Piper immediately loved her for it. Anyone who had my back was cool in her book.

"What are you doing here?" I asked Audrey.

"Well, you didn't kick me out at the restaurant, so I figured the door was open," Audrey smiled. "I've always wanted to be friends. So, here I am."

Part of me wanted to put my guard up. But I shut that feeling down. It was time to stop pushing people away.

"Well, welcome to my place," I smiled back, genuinely glad she came.

"I actually came for a different reason," Piper chimed in. "Have you seen the gossip blogs? It dropped like an hour ago."

"No. What happened?"

She slid her phone across the counter. I read the headline, and my stomach dropped.

*Billionaire Declan Thorne spotted on a lunch date with ex-wife Willow, just months after their divorce—and weeks after rumors of him dating her older sister, Audrey.*

Right below it was a photo of Declan guiding me into the restaurant, his hand resting on my lower back.

"Shit," I muttered. "This is going to cause so much drama."

Aurora was going to lose her mind. I wasn't scared of her, but I just didn't want the headache. The photo made us look way too cozy. Anyone looking at it would think...

"Is there something going on here?" Piper asked, while Audrey just watched us.

"Nothing," I said. "He crashed my doctor's appointment and then begged me to grab lunch so we could talk." I filled them in on everything. Even the apology.

By the end of it, I was more confused than ever. None of this made any sense.

"What if he's actually sorry?" Piper asked.

I'd been asking myself the same thing. I just didn't have the energy to deal with it.

"I don't know," I sighed. "You have to earn forgiveness. He hasn't earned crap. He's just giving me a massive headache."

Seriously, how was I supposed to just forget everything? The guy spent nine years telling me he hated my guts, and now he's suddenly sorry?

A complete 180 like that doesn't just happen. People don't change overnight.

"What do you think, Audrey?" Piper asked.

"I think he's in love with you and doesn't even realize it," Audrey said.

I burst out laughing. "Yeah, right. Declan doesn't love me."

I mean, come on. He literally screamed it in my face when he came over to defend his precious Aurora.

"Think about it, though," Audrey insisted.

"She might be onto something," Piper agreed. "Why else would he suddenly care? It only makes sense if he always cared but was too dumb to realize it. And why now, after the divorce? Audrey's right. He was probably in love with you the whole time, and losing you finally woke him up."

I didn't want to hear their crazy theories. I changed the subject.

We ended up having a great night. Audrey was officially part of the squad now.

But even though I was happy, their words kept echoing in my head.

Declan couldn't be in love with me. They were wrong. Declan had only ever loved one woman.

And her name was Audrey.
