Chapter 155

Piper’s voice is stuck on a loop in my brain. I can't shake it. That day, I came home early expecting some quiet, physical time with Lyra. Instead, I got hit with a wrecking ball: Piper telling Lyra she was falling for Adrian.

My chest literally ripped open. Sure, I hated her being with Adrian, but I’d convinced myself it was just a cheap, physical thing. Just sex.

Finding out she actually caught feelings? That cut way deeper than the thought of them in bed. Knowing she saw a future with that guy damn near destroyed me.

So I got pissed. It was easier than admitting that the thought of her loving him, even a little, absolutely pulverized my soul. I couldn't even say the words out loud. It hurt too bad.

Now the "what-ifs" are eating me alive. What if Adrian hadn't been a total scumbag? Would she be in his arms right now? Would she have traded her love for me for him? Would I be completely out of the picture?

It’s driving me crazy. The only reason I even have a shot with her is because Adrian blew his own life up.

"Declan!"

The shout snaps me back to reality.

I blink, staring at my brother. When the hell did he walk in? I was so deep in my own head I didn't even hear the door.

"What?" I quickly shuffle some random paperwork and shove it to the side.

He strolls over and drops into the chair opposite my desk. "I've been calling your name for like five minutes, man."

I stay quiet. What am I supposed to say? Lyra has owned my brain for days. Every single second, she's all I think about.

Damn it. We were married for ten years and she never had this kind of hold on me. Now? I'm hooked. She's my drug.

She's so deep under my skin I couldn't rip her out if I tried. And honestly? I don't want to. She's mine. I'm not letting go.

"You look like hell," he says, giving me that heavy, judging look I absolutely loathe. "Want to talk about it?"

God, this sucks. How do I admit I'm totally insecure about her feelings for Adrian? I don't do insecurity. But with Lyra, I'm a mess. I can at least admit that to myself.

I can't sit still. I push out of my chair and walk over to the massive glass window. I stare out at the city. It's the whole reason I took this office on the fifteenth floor—the view is insane. I can see the entire skyline and the sun dipping below the lake.

"Declan."

I let out a heavy breath and turn around. "I don't even know where to start, man."

"Let me guess. Lyra?"

I drag a hand through my hair, totally messing it up. "Yeah."

"You know you can tell me anything, right? Spill."

I hesitate for a split second, then just dump it all. I can't keep this bottled up anymore. It's making me lose my mind.

He does his usual thing—just sits there, quiet, listening to the whole mess. No judgment, no cutting me off.

When I'm done, I sink back into my chair, staring at him, desperate for him to say something. Anything. I'm at the end of my rope here.

"Sounds like you're still jealous of Adrian," he finally says.

I shoot him the nastiest look I've got. "No shit, Sherlock."

"Then what's the issue? Adrian's locked up. You've got Lyra. Shouldn't you be, like, relieved? Happy?" He looks genuinely baffled.

I squeeze my hands into fists, taking a slow breath so I don't scream.

"Sure, but I know Lyra. She's going to want to see him. For Iris to know her father, and because she'll want answers from the guy who got her pregnant."

His eyes lock onto mine. He finally gets it.

"You're terrified she's going to fall for him again."

"Yes!" I jump up and start pacing the floor. "The slate's clean now. No lies, no secrets. What's stopping her? Sure, he messed up, but I treated her way worse than he ever did."

The thought of losing her makes my stomach drop. I love her too much. It scares the shit out of me.

"What's stopping her is that he's behind bars," my brother says. "Twenty-one years is a lifetime. She's not going to wait around."

"This is Lyra we're talking about. Her loyalty is insane. She loved me when I was a total monster to her. You think a prison cell is going to stop her if she catches feelings again?"

He sighs, shaking his head. "Yeah. Good point."

I let out a defeated sigh.

"But look," he adds, "don't spiral over this. Just take it day by day. Show up for her. Love her so hard that she can't even imagine a life without you."

He's right. There's nothing else I can do. It's her call. All I can do is give her everything I've got.

I force myself to take a breath and let the tension drain out. The weight on my chest lightens up a bit.

Then my phone buzzes.

I grab it and swipe answer without even checking the screen. "Yeah?"

"Declan, we have a situation."

Edmund's voice. My stomach instantly knots up.

"What happened?"

"Get to the hospital," he says, his voice tight. "Lyra's in the ER."

Fuck. I'm going to kill someone.
