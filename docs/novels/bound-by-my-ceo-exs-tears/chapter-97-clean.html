Chapter 98

A voice cuts through the noise. I spin around, and holy crap—there’s Declan, sitting right next to me. Talk about manifesting. But the real shocker? No suit. For the first time ever, the guy is dressed down in a tight black V-neck and blue jeans.

"Uh, what the hell are you doing here?" I stutter, staring at him like he's a ghost.

"Asher said you'd be around. So, yeah. Here I am." He shrugs, totally casual, like he didn't just sound like a total stalker.

I can't take this. I bolt upright and march away without a word. "Lyra!" he calls out, but I keep my eyes forward and keep moving.

I duck into the restroom, chest heaving. Seriously, why now? Where was this guy years ago? He expects me to just buy this sudden personality transplant right when I'm finally moving on? No way. Life doesn't work that way.

I splash cold water on my face and lock my emotions down. I don't care why he suddenly wants in. We're over. Honestly, we never even started.

Once my breathing slows, I push the door open and immediately collide with a solid chest. That expensive cologne hits my nose, and I instantly know who it is.

I jump back like I just touched a hot stove. My body is doing weird things, and the last thing I need is him thinking I want this.

"Lyra," he rumbles, his voice low and rough.

"What's the play here, Declan? Seriously, what do you want?"

I don't get him, and honestly, I don't want to. The guy broke me once. I'm not signing up for round two.

"No games, I swear," he says, his voice actually wavering. "I just... we need to talk. I have to tell you something."

Okay, now I'm thrown. Declan doesn't do "unsure." I've known him forever, and he's always been a steamroller. He says his piece, hurts your feelings, and doesn't give a damn. But right now? He's hesitating.

I scan his face. Those gray eyes are locked onto mine with an intensity I've never seen before. He's different. I can feel it, even if I can't put my finger on it.

He actually looks... scared. Like he's terrified of the words in his mouth, and even more terrified of how I'll react.

He shifts his weight, pacing on the spot. Nervous. Declan Woods does not get nervous. This is getting weirder by the second.

"Spit it out," I say, crossing my arms over my stomach and tapping my toe against the tile.

But before he can open his mouth, Silas swoops in.

"Hey, Lyra. This guy bothering you?" Silas steps right between us, squaring his shoulders and throwing a death glare at Declan.

Declan scoffs, curling his lip. "Oh, look, the stray dog. Seriously, dude, do you only drool over my leftovers? First you stalked Aurora back in high school like a pathetic puppy, and now you're sniffing around Lyra? Back the fuck off. She's mine."

Anger flares hot in my chest. He does not get to talk to Silas like that. And I am definitely not his property. Never was, never will be.

"You're a real piece of work, you know that?" Silas growls, stepping up. "Stop talking about her like she's some prize you won."

Silas's fists are clenched so tight his knuckles are white. His jaw is locked. He looks ready to tear Declan apart.

I look from one to the other, completely disgusted. They're acting like toddlers. Silas is radiating pure hatred, while Declan is practically green with jealousy.

But Silas's vibe is off. Yeah, he had a thing for Aurora back in the day, but so did half the school. Just because she chose Declan shouldn't make him this bitter years later. Why is there so much bad blood here?

"Butt out," Declan snarls, his eyes darkening like a thundercloud. "This is between me and Lyra."

Silas lunges forward, shoving Declan's chest. Declan barely moves, but his face goes completely feral.

"Hey! Stop it, both of you!" I yell.

Neither of them even blinks. They're completely tuned out, locked in caveman mode.

I glance around, panic rising. People are starting to gather. Since Declan's face is all over the news, a couple of teenagers already have their phones out, recording.

"Put your hands on me again, I dare you," Declan spits, getting right in Silas's face.
