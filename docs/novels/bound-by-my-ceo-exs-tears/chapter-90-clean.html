Chapter 91

I grunt, letting go as the tension snaps. God, I needed that. Best stress relief in the world.

"So," she whispers, eyes bright. "Tomorrow?"

She looks relaxed now. Guess we both needed the release. I grab a towel, wipe her bare back, and keep my mouth shut. She knows how this works. I call. She shows up. Never the other way around.

She starts pulling her clothes on. It’s past ten. I need sleep before tomorrow's grind.

No sleepovers. No strings. Just two people hooking up.

"Orion?"

Only she uses my full name. Hate it. Sounds like some holy angel, and I’m the exact opposite.

"Drop it," I growl. The question already suffocates me. "I call the shots here. We do this when I say so."

Pain flashes across her face. I look away. She knew the rules when she signed up for this.

"So I just wait?" she whispers. "Wait in line while you screw around with other girls?"

I never lied to her. She knew she wasn't the only one. I like variety. Today I wanted her. Tomorrow? Maybe her again, maybe someone else.

"Yeah," I say, cold.

The spark in her eyes dies. She tries to hide it, but I see her swallowing the hurt.

Dammit. I hate the feelings talk. Two years of a perfect setup, and now she’s catching feelings. Time to kill this before it gets worse.

She’s busy, I don’t do dating. It was the perfect deal. Plus, she’s my secretary. Even if I wanted a girlfriend, she doesn't fit my world.

"I thought you could handle this," I say. "My bad. We're done. No more hooking up. You keep your job, we act like this never happened. Got it, Eden?"

She stares right through me.

"I can't do this anymore," she whispers, tears welling up. "I love you, Orion. But you're locked up tight. I can't keep sleeping with a guy who'll never love me back."

She’s a killer secretary, but whatever.

"Guess you better write that resignation letter then," I smirk.

She doesn't say a word. Just shakes her head, turns, and walks out. I sigh, stay put, and tap my phone to unlock the front gate. Then I faceplant onto the mattress.

She’s right. My heart is locked. No love for me. Sure, my folks have a fairytale marriage, but they're the exception, not the rule.

Love ruins people. It hollows you out.

Look at Declan. When Aurora left, he turned into a walking corpse for months until Asher came along. Just drank himself blind. Why would I hand a woman the weapon to destroy me?

He got wrecked over Aurora, and now I'm terrified it's happening again.

He doesn't even realize he loves Lyra yet. When it hits him, it's going to break him. Especially after how he treated her.

I blow out a breath, head to the bathroom, and turn on the shower. Ten minutes under the hot water and my chest is still tight. Something's wrong. I have a bad feeling it's Declan.

I grab a towel, throw on some sweatpants, and reach for my phone. Before I can dial, the security system chimes. Only one other guy has the code. Declan.

I jog downstairs. He’s stumbling through the front door.

"Declan? What the hell, man?" I grab his arm, taking his weight.

Wasted. Completely hammered. He hasn't touched the bottle like this since his dark days. My stomach drops.

I haul him over to the couch and sit next to him.

"Talk to me, Ro. What happened?"

"You were right," he slurs, head hanging. "Fucking right. I ruined everything. How do I fix this? How do I fix what I broke?"

The raw pain in his voice cuts deep. He's my brother. When he hurts, I hurt. I'd take the hit for him if I could, but I can't.

"Start from the beginning. What did you break?"

He looks completely shattered. Like his whole world just caved in.

"I love her, Orion. I fucking love her," he yells.

"Who?"

"Lyra!" he screams. "I love her. It hit me today."

And it hit him like a freight train. Just like I feared. The truth finally caught up, and it's tearing him apart.

"Why didn't I see it?" he groans, a tear slipping down his face. His grey eyes—just like mine—are full of agony. "Nine years. I spent nine years making her pay for that night. I broke her. She'll never believe me now."

My chest aches watching him unravel.

"What do I do? How do I glue her back together when I'm the one who smashed her heart?"

I pull him into a rough hug, gripping his shoulder. Declan never shows weakness. Not even when Aurora walked out. Back then, he didn't cry or scream about fixing things. He just drank in silence.

Now, his shoulders are shaking. He's fighting the tears. It kills me. He's my twin, and I have no clue how to help him.

Nine years of damage doesn't just vanish. Lyra isn't the same girl. She rebuilt herself from the ashes. She won't let him close easily.

"I don't know, Ro," I mutter. "But you're Declan fucking Woods. You get what you want. You'll fix this. You don't quit, so don't start now."

He stares straight ahead, eyes blank, lost in the dark.

"What if she won't have me?" he whispers.

This isn't my brother. The confident, arrogant Declan is gone. Lyra has him completely on his knees. Honestly? She has more power over him than Aurora ever did.

Aurora was just young, easy love. Puppy love. That's why it fell apart at the first bump. What he has with Lyra is different. It's heavy. It's real.

"Then you fight like hell," I whisper.

We sit there in the quiet until his eyes heavy up and he drifts off. I haul him up and drag him to his guest room.

I dump him on the bed. Even asleep, his face is tight with pain. I want to tell him he'll win her back, but I'd be lying. Nine years of torment. If I were Lyra, I'd never look back.

I just have to hope she still cares. Because if she doesn't, this will kill him.
