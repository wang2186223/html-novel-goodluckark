NovelVibe

Chapter 29

My brain is fried, my body is wrecked, and I just want this nightmare to end. Three hits. Three escapes. But seriously, how long before my luck dries up? Whoever wants me dead is doing everything to keep me from Asher.

A cold shiver hits my spine. He was right there. Inside my house. Ready to force himself on me and then end my life. I swallow hard, fighting back the hot tears stinging my eyes.

No more crying. I'm done with it. But why me? I didn't do anything to anyone. Okay, maybe Aurora, but she's with Declan now, so we should be even, right? I don't deserve this crap.

My absolute worst fear? They actually pull it off. And I won't be there to watch my little boy grow up.

Missing every single milestone. The thought alone tears my chest wide open.

"Hey, sweetie, you okay?" a voice cuts in.

I look up. An old lady is staring, her eyes full of worry. She's checking out my face. The swelling is down, thank god, but I've still got a split lip, a shiner, and a bruised cheek.

"Yeah, I'm good. Thanks," I mumble, trying to force a smile that just won't come.

Ever since the attack, I've been a zombie. Just going through the motions. Everything feels fake, like a bad dream I can't shake off. Part of me thinks if I just play along, it'll disappear.

"You don't look good, honey. Want me to call someone?" she asks.

Man, if only everyone was like her. She doesn't know me from Adam, but she stopped in the middle of a grocery aisle because she saw me unraveling.

"No, really, it's fine. Just... a lot going on," I say.

She looks doubtful. I plaster on a fake grin. I appreciate the concern, but seriously, I just want to crawl into a hole.

She steps closer, giving my hand a quick squeeze. "Hang in there. Keep the faith, okay?"

She gives me one last look and walks off. I let out a massive breath and turn back to the soup cans.

"Wow. You look like absolute trash." That annoying, smug voice makes me whip around.

Oh, great. Just what I needed.

I try to ignore her, but she's clearly looking for a fight.

"Did some wife finally catch you trying to steal her man?" Ingrid sneers. Declan's secretary.

My hands ball into fists. She's baiting me, and I know it. The whole city knows what happened. Someone leaked the story, and the tabloids went nuts, digging up the other two attempts on my life. Declan was still trying to find the leak after burying the first two.

Ingrid has always hated my guts. Any chance to rip me apart, she takes it. And Declan? Never believed me. Always took her side, telling me I probably started it.

"What's your game, Ingrid? You know exactly what happened to me," I say, keeping my voice dead level.

"No game, just facts. Honestly, I bet some pissed-off wife wanted you gone. That's your thing, right? Stealing men. You're just a cheap home-wrecker."

I scan her up and down. She looks flawless, put-together. I look like I just went ten rounds in an MMA cage.

"Years of this, and you're still using the same lines? Get some new material. It's getting boring," I shoot back.

Her jaw drops. She didn't expect me to snap back. I never did before, always too scared of upsetting Declan. But today? I don't give a damn anymore.

She recovers fast, a nasty smirk spreading across her face. "So, how's it feel losing Declan? Must burn knowing he's with your sister right now."

Yeah, it stings. Sometimes. But I'm letting him go, piece by piece. Killing that love. Plus, Adrian's been a pretty great distraction.

"Ingrid, you threw yourself at Declan for years hoping he'd notice you. Even when we were married, you tried to slide into his bed, and he didn't want you. Sure, he didn't love me, but I was the wife. You were just the help. How's it feel knowing he'd rather touch me—even hating me—than ever lay a finger on you? And now that Aurora's back? Honey, you're completely out of the running." I smirk, feeling a rush of pure satisfaction.

"You ugly bitch!" she screeches, lunging at me.

I dodge. She trips over her ridiculously high, overpriced heels.

She scrambles up, charging again. Without thinking, I grab a carton of milk from my cart and hurl it right at her.

It hits, drenching her expensive dress. We both freeze in shock. Then, with a literal battle cry, she flies at me, screaming like a banshee. I grab whatever's close and start throwing.

A crowd gathers. People pull out their phones. It's a total circus. She swings to slap me, but someone grabs her around the waist. Another guy grabs me, dragging me backward.

"Let me go!" I yell.

They haul us right out the doors. The guy holding Ingrid dumps her outside, still kicking and screaming.

"Both of you are banned. Come back, and I'm calling the cops," the manager barks before slamming the door.

"This is your fault!" Ingrid yells.

"My fault? You threw the first punch. I was just defending myself from a psycho."

"You'll pay for this, Lyra. Mark my words!" She spins on her heel and stomps off.

I sigh, walking to my beat-up car. I drive home, my mind a mess. Half of me is thrilled I finally stood up to her, the other half is pissed I let her get under my skin.

It's no secret she wanted Declan. He ignored her, so she took her frustration out on me. I shake it off and focus on the road.

Pulling into the driveway, my mood instantly lifts. Adrian is sitting on my front steps. I park and get out.

"Where were you?" he asks, standing up. "I called. You didn't pick up. I was worried."

"Sorry! Left my phone inside when I ran out for groceries," I say, unlocking the door.

I wave him inside and head straight to the kitchen.

"Okay, so where are the groceries?"

I give him a sheepish look. "Well... I got into a fight. Got kicked out. Actually, I'm banned for life."

"What?!" he growls.

"I know, stupid. But Ingrid's been a nightmare forever. I just snapped," I mumble.

I brace myself for the lecture. Declan would've lost it. Told me to grow up, stop acting like a kid, stop starting drama with his staff.

Instead, Adrian pulls me close. His hand slides to my hip. He gently tilts my chin up, forcing me to look into those crazy blue eyes.

"I'm just glad you're okay, and she didn't hurt you," he murmurs. "Never apologize for standing up for yourself, Lyra. Ever. I'm proud of you for putting her in her place."

I'm completely frozen. His eyes, his jaw, his lips—it's like I'm under a spell. My heart hammers against my ribs as his face gets closer. Then his lips press against mine, warm and demanding, sending a jolt straight through me.
