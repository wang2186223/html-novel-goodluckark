Chapter 96

My sneakers slam against the asphalt. Morning runs are my usual jam, but not tonight. It’s seven PM, and my brain is screaming for this.

I push the pace. Let the thighs burn. Run fast enough, maybe I can drop the guilt. Drop the heartbreak. Drop the absolute, total stupidity of my own choices.

What I did to Lyra is rotting me from the inside. Just straight-up eating me alive. Ever since the truth of how I feel hit me, I can’t even look her in the eye.

The guy in the mirror? A total piece of trash. I look at him and want to puke. The way I treated her makes me sick to my stomach.

I seriously had myself convinced I was one of the good guys. Loyal. Devoted. I wore my lingering obsession with Aurora like some badge of honor, thinking it proved my heart was pure. Meanwhile, I was busy shredding the woman I actually loved to pieces.

"Fuck!" I yell at the empty street. How did I manage to screw up this badly?

The local gas station blurs past. I kick into overdrive. No slowing down. The second I ease up, the regrets catch up, whispering every single screw-up in my ear.

Shutting my eyes only brings back her face from months ago, right before the divorce papers landed. The exact words I threw at her are a blur, but the damage wasn't. I can still see her eyes going dead right before she spat out that she hated me. Back then, I just rolled my eyes. Now? I’d literally beg for a shred of the love she used to waste on me.

I blew it. Everything is completely trashed, and I’m totally clueless on how to patch it up.

A buzz in my pocket snaps me out of the spiral.

I swipe the screen blind, gasping for air. "Yeah?"

"Hey, Dad! It's me!" Asher's voice practically vibrates through the speaker.

We do the phone thing, sure, but I've been keeping my distance. Seeing my kid means seeing her, and I can't handle that yet.

"Hey, bud. What's up?"

"I'm so good! Seriously, I'm so hyped!" he yells.

I know I might hate the answer, but I can't help asking. "Oh yeah? What's got you bouncing off the walls?" A small laugh slips out.

Hearing his voice is like getting a hit of oxygen. I've been suffocating, and he's the only thing keeping me afloat.

"Okay, so you know Jasper, right? My best friend?"

"Sure do."

"So, he's had a rough time lately, and I wanted to make him feel better. I begged Mom, and she said yes! We're hitting the theme park tomorrow. Me, Mom, Jasper, and his dad!" He screams the last bit.

A hot spike of jealousy hits me straight in the gut. Some other guy hanging around her? Hell no. I know I told myself she needs a better man, but the reality of losing her makes me want to tear things apart.

"Is that so?" My voice goes flat and cold.

"Uh-huh," Asher says, his tone dropping as he notices my lack of hype. "You okay, Dad? You sound kinda weird."

I pivot on my heel and start stomping back toward the house. My chest is on fire. What if she hooks up with this guy? What if they're already a thing? What if she falls for him? Every single scenario makes me want to punch a wall for being such a dumbass.

"Which park?" I snap.

"The one a town over," he says. "Seriously, Dad, you good?"

"Yeah. Totally fine," I lie through my teeth.

"If you say so..." He sounds totally unconvinced. "Anyway, just wanted to give you a heads-up so you didn't drop by tomorrow. Catch ya later, Dad. Night."

"Night, bud," I mutter, killing the call.

I keep walking. This run was supposed to clear my head. Total fail. Now I’m just stressed and furious.

Half an hour later, I'm back at my place. The chilly night air didn't do shit to calm me down. I want to drive over to Lyra's right now, grab her, and tell her she's mine. Tell her she's not going anywhere with this Silas dude. Tell her I love her.

"Sir, you have a visitor," my butler says the second I cross the threshold.

"Who?"

Before he can name her, her voice cuts in. I mutter a curse under my breath and turn around. God dammit. I do not have the bandwidth for this right now.

"What do you want, Aurora?" I ask, hearing Bennett slip away.

I look at her. Sure, she's pretty, but the spark is totally gone. I try to find even a flicker of the old obsession, but there's nothing. Just a cold, empty void where those feelings used to live.

"How've you been, Ro?" she asks, dodging my question.

I don't want to be a dick, but my patience is at zero.

"Just tell me why you're here, Aurora. I've got a lot of shit to deal with."

She lets out a sigh, her face going pale. "It's about us. We need to talk about where we stand."

My expression goes completely blank. I let the ice drip from my voice. She winces, but I honestly couldn't care less.

"What relationship? There is no 'us,' Aurora. Get that through your head," I say, my fists clenching at my sides.

Seriously, she needs to get out. Doesn't she get that her presence just pisses me off now? Everything is different. I can barely stand to look at her.

"That's a lie. We love each other. We always have. You can't just say it's over. Is this because of Lyra?" Tears start welling up in her eyes.

The love is gone, but I don't hate her. I don't want to crush her, but if being brutal is the only way to make her get it, so be it.

"Yeah, it is. But honestly? I don't love you anymore. Maybe I did once, but that's dead. You're wasting your breath here. Go find someone who can actually love you back, because I can't."

The tears spill over. Her face crumples. Part of me wants to comfort her, but I can't. I've spent too long hurting Lyra. I'm not doing it anymore. Not for anyone.

"You're in love with her, aren't you?" her voice cracks.

Seeing her hurt pinches something deep inside me—a ghost of my old feelings. I crush it immediately. No way I'm letting that take over.

"Yes. I know it sucks to hear, but yeah. I love her," I say, trying to soften the blow just a little.

"And now that we're clear on that, I'm going after her. Don't get in my way."

"She won't take you back. She'll never forgive you for what you did," she spits, bitter and hurt.

I drag a hand through my hair, frustrated as hell. "Maybe. But that's not gonna stop me from trying to get her back."

"Well, I'm not stopping either. I lost you once, Declan, and I'm not letting you go again. I don't give up that easy," she says, her jaw tight.

Before I can tell her she's delusional, she storms past me and slams the door.

I head up the stairs, tossing the whole drama with Aurora out of my head. None of her words matter. It's all about Lyra now.

Time to get my ex-wife back.
