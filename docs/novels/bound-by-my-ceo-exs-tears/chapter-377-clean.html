Chapter 385

Jasper slips out from some tiny side door I didn't even know was there. He locks eyes with me and freezes solid.

God, a kid shouldn't make me shake like this. I lose my footing a little under that stare—those eyes look just like mine, and right now, they're drilling holes through me.

"What the hell are you doing here?" he spits out. His forehead creases up, and his hands ball into tight fists. A cold shiver runs down my spine. The sheer, raw hate in his look is suffocating.

I try to speak, but my throat is completely dry. My chest squeezes tight, my pulse hammering like crazy. I can't catch a single breath.

"J-Jasper..." I choke out. That's it. Just his name. The rest of my vocabulary just vanishes.

His jaw goes rigid, teeth grinding. Seeing him glare at me with that much venom... man, it hits me. I did this. I wrecked him.

Kids aren't supposed to carry this kind of baggage. It's on me. I took his childhood and dragged it through the dirt. It makes me sick to see that look on his face.

I press a hand to my chest, trying to stop the ache, but it's like a physical weight crushing my ribs. I'm paralyzed. All I can do is stand there and look at the wreckage of my own ego.

"Koda, come," he mutters, looking down at the dog.

The dog—usually a total pain to deal with—scrambles right up and trots over to him. Jasper turns on his heel, heading back toward that little door.

Panic snaps me out of it. He's walking away. I have to say something right now.

"Jasper, wait, I'm sorry!" I blurt out, my brain scrambling to find the right words.

He halts. He doesn't look back, though. His shoulders are stiff as a board.

He stays put, so I just keep pushing.

"I'm so sorry for what I did to you. For breaking your heart, over and over, and putting you through hell. I was selfish, Jasper. I only cared about myself. But you're my boy. My son. Look, I know a stupid 'sorry' doesn't fix this, but please, let it be a start."

Nothing. Total silence. I gulp down some air, trying to swallow the lump in my throat and stop the tears from spilling over.

"I'm sorry for how I acted. For withholding the love you should've had. I made you feel worthless, like you weren't enough, and that's on me. I wasn't there. I wasn't the mom you needed, the mom you deserved. I didn't love you the way I should have. I'm just... I'm so incredibly sorry. I'm begging you to forgive me. Give me a shot to make this right, even if I don't deserve one."

The dam breaks. Tears stream down my face, and a sob cuts off my breath. "I want to be your mom," I choke out, my voice cracking. "Just give me a chance. I want to be everything you ever wanted, to give you everything I withheld. I want to be there. But... look, this isn't about me anymore. It's about you, Jasper. For once in my life, I'm putting you first. If you want me gone, I'll go. I'll respect that. But I'm never going to stop fighting for you." My knees go weak, and I slide down against the door behind me, using it to stay upright. My head is spinning, and my words are probably a jumbled mess, but God, I hope he gets what I'm trying to say.

I run out of breath, and he just walks away without a word. My legs give out completely. I collapse onto the floor, burying my face in my hands, sobbing. All I can see behind my eyelids is his furious, hurt face, ripping whatever is left of my heart to shreds.

I don't give a damn if the neighbors are watching. Who cares? My kid hates my guts. I did this. I just pray to God he heals from this, even if he never lets me back in.
