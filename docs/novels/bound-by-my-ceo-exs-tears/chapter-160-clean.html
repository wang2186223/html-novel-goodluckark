Chapter 162

I’ve been out of the hospital for a week now. Everything’s running smooth. Too smooth. Honestly, I’m just waiting for the other shoe to drop.

Aurora wasn’t lying back then. Declan was always hers. Sure, he’s a guy, not some trophy, but she owned his heart. I never figured out how she did it.

I used to obsess over her. Like, what made her so damn special? How did she get a powerhouse like Declan wrapped around her finger? It drove me crazy. It ate at me. She got his soft, sweet side. I got the monster.

But hey, the tables turned. And yeah, I’m still waiting for the catch. I don’t buy this sudden 180.

I see how he looks at me now. Warm. Loving. Exactly what I wanted for years. But my gut is screaming that something’s off.

When did he flip the switch? Before I took a bullet, or after? Did almost losing me actually wake him up? Did he finally see what was right in front of him, or is there some other game playing out?

He did say he lost his mind when I started seeing Adrian. Maybe that’s the key. Guys are possessive like that. He probably couldn’t stomach another dude taking his place, especially since I’d worshipped him since I was a kid.

"Hey, Lyra? Should I get Aurora’s bath ready?" Martha’s voice cuts through my thoughts from the living room doorway.

"Yeah, please. Thanks."

The sun is beaming outside. Perfect day to wash my little girl.

A few minutes later, Martha calls out that it's good to go. I scoop Aurora up, head to the tub, test the water with my wrist, and strip her down.

Asher’s at school. Declan’s at the office. Don’t get me wrong, I love my baby girl, but I am bored out of my mind.

I don’t do well sitting around. Aurora basically just sleeps. Unless she’s wet or hungry, she rarely makes a peep.

So yeah, my days consist of taking care of a super easy baby. That’s it. I want to get back to work, but that’s a hard no for now.

I laugh as she splashes. She’s staring at the bubbles like they’re magic.

Once she’s clean, I towel her off and lay her on the changing mat. I blow raspberries on her tummy, and she lets out this tiny, perfect giggle. My heart melts. I get her dressed and decide we need some fresh air.

Time to go grab some vitamin D.

We’re barely on the porch when Martha jogs over.

"Ma'am, you have a visitor."

I let out a heavy sigh. "Martha, seriously. Call me Lyra. How many times do I gotta say it?"

"Sorry," she says, giving me a sheepish grin.

I can't even stay mad. She’s too sweet. A million times better than the last house manager. That woman straight-up hated my guts. Honestly, Declan probably kept her around just to torture me. He knew she treated me like trash, even though I was his wife.

"Who is it?" I ask, shaking off the old memories.

"Mr. Sharp."

There’s only one Mr. Sharp left. And honestly, I don’t know if I have the stomach for him today. Corbin spent years making my life hell. His cold shoulder broke me. I never understood how my own flesh and blood could hate me so much. Now I get it. We aren't blood. That’s why he didn’t give a shit about hurting me.

"Fine. Let him in," I say, ignoring my gut.

I look up at the sky, praying I don’t regret this. Martha nods and heads back inside.

"Hey, Lyra."

I turn around to face the guy I spent my whole life calling my brother. Except he never saw me as family. Not once. Aurora was his golden child. I was just the bitch who ruined his sister’s life.

"Hey," I say, keeping it flat. "What do you want?"

"How are you doing?"

"Cut the crap, Corbin. We both know you don’t give a shit about how I am. Why are you here?"

Wow. I’m actually shocked by my own spine. It feels weird, but damn, it feels good. I guess the old, pathetic version of me finally died. I'm done holding back.

He flinches like I slapped him. I don’t care. He never cared when he was ripping my heart out.

He hesitates, shifting his weight, then finally spills it. I sigh as the puzzle pieces click.

"Please, Lyra. You gotta talk to Declan. You’re the only one he won’t shut out right now," he begs.

My eyebrows go up. "Talk to him about what, exactly?"

"Aurora got locked up after she attacked you. Look, I know she lost her mind, but it’s been two weeks. She’s rotting in there."

I freeze. Honestly, I hadn't even thought about her since that day. It never even crossed my mind that she was behind bars.

"And what’s that got to do with Declan?"

"He’s the one who put her there," he says, taking a shaky breath to keep from breaking down. "I know you guys hate each other, but please, Lyra. She’s suffering. The other inmates are tearing her apart."

He’s practically crying now. I can tell he’s tried everything else. He probably begged Declan on his knees, and Declan laughed in his face.

"What about Mom? Doesn't she have connections?" I ask.

"She won't touch it. She wants Aurora to learn a lesson. Says she needs a reality check."

Wow. Okay. Didn't see that coming. I figured Mom would be tearing the city apart to get her favorite child out.

"Aurora’s a lawyer. Can’t she bail herself out? Or get her firm on it?"

He shakes his head, looking completely defeated. "I wish. Declan’s word is law. Fighting him is like fighting God. It’s a losing game. We’ve tried everything. Nothing works."

I bounce Aurora a bit as she starts to whimper.

"Please, Lyra. I know we were monsters to you. We don’t deserve your help, but I’m begging you. Just talk to Declan."

I don't say a word. I don't even know what to say. Do I actually help them out, or do I just sit back and let karma do its thing?
