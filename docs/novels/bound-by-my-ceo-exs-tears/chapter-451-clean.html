Chapter 460

Delilah left, but my nerves were still shot. Standing by the glass, I watched her march to her shiny black Audi, slam the door, and gun the engine. She tore down the street like a bat out of hell. Honestly, I kept listening, waiting for the inevitable screech and metal-on-metal crunch at the next intersection.

Call me a bitch, but a total wreck would’ve been doing the world a favor. The girl is pure poison. She basically wrote the manual on being a psycho mean girl.

She was absolutely livid. Fuming. It was the exact same temper tantrum Asher threw yesterday when I finally stopped playing by his rules.

Was I seriously that much of a doormat before? Like, why did they both assume I’d just roll over? Why was my surrender their default setting?

It’s a bitter pill to swallow, but they loved me when I was quiet, weak, and easy to control. Now that I’m actually standing my ground? It completely blows their minds and pisses them off.

Once the dust settled, I took a deep, shaky breath. I dumped some food in my sweet girl's bowl, whipped up a quick snack for myself, and crashed hard. Seriously, pregnancy needs a giant warning sticker: *Warning: Will drain your battery to zero.* I’m constantly exhausted. All I want to do is crawl under a rock and sleep until next season.

Five hours later, I finally blinked my eyes open, feeling semi-human again. I grabbed another bite to eat and just vegetated on the couch. Might as well milk this temporary leave for all it's worth before my real-life job drags me back next week.

My phone buzzed, snapping me out of my trashy mafia romance. I tossed the paperback onto the cushion, swiped the screen, and pressed it to my ear.

"Hey, Clara," I muttered. She’d been running around like crazy, so we hadn't hung out since her sudden drop-by, even if we kept up over text.

"Hey, babe!" her voice exploded through the speaker, fast and bubbly. "So, what's the game plan for tonight?"

I pinched the bridge of my nose. "Wait, meeting?"

"Uh, yeah." Her tone immediately shifted into *you-screwed-up* territory.

"For what exactly?"

"Are you freaking kidding me, Si?" I could literally hear her grinding her teeth. "Dinner. Our Saturday night spot. Don't tell me you forgot."

Oh, crap. Saturdays are sacred. If we're both in town, it's always dinner and then cocktails. But with my life currently burning to the ground, the routine completely slipped my mind.

"But, like, we skipped last week..." I mumbled, trying to defend myself.

"Yeah, because I had to catch a red-eye to Beijing on Friday!" she shot back. "Our local team totally tanked a deal we spent a month prepping."

Right. That, too, had totally slipped my mind.

"Honestly, I'm just not feeling it," I whined, sounding like a total toddler. "Plus, I really don't want to drive."

"Who said anything about driving? I'm chauffeuring. Door-to-door service, babe."

"But—"

"Save it. You're getting some fresh air," she ordered, shutting me down with her usual boss-lady energy. "See you at six."

Click. She hung up before I could even open my mouth to complain.

I stared at the blank screen, groaning. Clara was a force of nature. If I wasn't dressed, she'd literally drag me out of the house in my baggy hoodie and leggings. I checked the time. Ninety minutes. Fine. Just enough time to wash off the laziness and look like a functioning human.

I took a fast shower, threw open my closet doors, and let out a massive sigh. Packed to the brim, yet absolutely nothing to wear. Seriously, how does that work? You can have a literal department store in your room and still feel totally naked. It’s a universal female curse. Every single girl on earth does this exact same dance in front of a full closet.

I sighed again—probably my hundredth time today—and grabbed a basic black dress. No frills, no drama. Today was definitely a low-effort kind of day.

I kept the makeup super basic: a dab of foundation, some concealer, and a swipe of lip balm. Heels? Absolutely not. I slid into my black flats. Easy, comfy, done.

I was just snapping my bag shut when a loud honk echoed from the street. I parted the blinds and saw Clara's ride idling by the curb. I quickly filled Shadow's bowls with kibble and water, locked the front door, and headed out.

The second I opened the passenger door, she gasped. "Uh, what is that?"

"Uh, clothes? Ever heard of them?"

"Please tell me you're going to change," she begged, her eyes wide with genuine horror.

I looked down at my dress, then back at her. "What? What's wrong with it?"

Her face was pure, unadulterated drama. "Are you for real right now?"

"Dead serious. Hey, at least I'm not wearing sweatpants." I hopped in and shut the door.

"But what if we run into some hot guys?"

I just gave her a look, raising an eyebrow.

"Clara, I'm literally pregnant," I said, rolling my eyes hard.

"And? Your point is?" She slammed on the gas, merging into traffic with one hand on the wheel, driving way too fast.

Clara was a solid driver, sure, but she was also a total speed demon. The girl drove like she was fleeing a crime scene.

I stared at her side profile. Like, seriously?

"It's literally everything," I deadpanned. "I am not on the market."

"Si, having a baby doesn't mean you put your life on pause. You deserve to be happy. If Asher wants to be a deadbeat, fine. We'll just find a guy who actually wants the job."

My jaw dropped. "Okay, you've officially lost your mind."

For a split second, Asher's face popped into my head. That sharp jawline, those intense, dark eyes, that stupidly perfect face. It was pathetic how my brain still went straight to him. But the fantasy instantly soured, replaced by the cold reality of how cruel he'd been.

I blinked the thought away and snapped my attention back to Clara.

She smirked. "Nope. It's actually a genius plan."

"Genius? It's batshit crazy," I countered. "I don't need some random guy playing daddy. I can raise my kid by myself, thanks."

I love her to death, but sometimes Clara's ideas are straight-up delusional.

"Hear me out, babe," she pushed. "My future nephew needs a dad. My cousin is a total coward, but I swear, we can find a real man to step up."

I stared at her, completely speechless. She threw me a quick glance, and oh my god, she wasn't joking. How could she not see how ridiculous this was?

"I don't even have time to date," I argued. "Between my job and a newborn, I'll be drowning. I'm perfectly fine being single."

Her grin turned wicked. "You don't have to hunt, but you're not allowed to hide in a cave either."

"No way. Keep me out of whatever crazy scheme you're cooking up."

"You won't have to lift a finger." Clara gave me a sly wink. "Leave it all to me."

That look in her eye made my stomach do a nervous flip.

That's the thing about Clara. Once she locks onto an idea, she's like a dog with a bone. It doesn't matter if it's brilliant or totally brain-dead. She will run with it like her life depends on it.

"Clara, seriously—"

She cut me off, wearing a massive, victorious grin. "Actually, I think I already know the perfect guy."
