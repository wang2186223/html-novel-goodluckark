Chapter 280

Clara’s eyes darted between me and her dad. She was sizing us up. I could practically see the gears turning in her head, trying to figure out what was going down between me and Orion.

This was a massive mistake. Years of zero contact, and I’m still sweating over him? No way. I swore I’d killed those feelings. After the hell he put me through back then, I figured any spark was dead and buried.

Guess I was dead wrong. Fast forward to now, and our lips were literally inches apart. God, I felt sick. How did I let myself slip like that? My body just took over, and I totally folded.

"Wait, were you guys just kissing?" Clara asked, totally blank-faced. My breath hitched hard in my throat.

My brain short-circuited. What do I even say to that? Spill the truth? Lying wasn't really an option when she walked right in on us.

"Uh—I mean, we—" I choked, my tongue tying itself in knots.

Panic flared. Owen was the only guy she’d ever seen me get close to. Aside from Orion, Owen was my only real partner. If I admitted this, would she hate me? Sure, Orion was trying to step up now, but Owen had been her actual dad for over five years. I couldn't let her think I was throwing Owen under the bus, even if Orion and I technically had a ring on our fingers now.

"Yeah, we were," Orion said.

I whipped my head around and shot him a death glare. Was he out of his mind? Why the hell would he just blurt that out to her?

I wanted to sweep it under the rug and pretend it never happened. Getting caught by my kid was humiliating enough, but the real kicker? I actually wanted it. I had been dying for him to press his mouth against mine.

"Oh. Okay. Um, can I get some breakfast then?" she asked, hopping onto a barstool.

Her face was a total mask. I wanted to pry, to know what was going on in her head, but I knew better than to push. Not while she was still freezing me out over the whole secret-dad situation.

I looked at Orion. "Can you feed her while you eat?"

I had to get out of there. My skin was crawling, and I needed space to breathe.

"Yeah, got it," he said.

I clattered my mug into the sink and bolted. I ran straight to my bedroom, slammed the door, and threw the lock. I pressed my back against the wood and slid down to the floor.

I felt like a total traitor to myself. Letting him get that close was a massive betrayal. This was the guy who tore my life apart. He broke my heart, stomped on the pieces, and left me in ruins. He literally destroyed me. So how the hell could I still want his mouth on mine?

Hadn't I learned my lesson? Touching him was like playing with fire. Getting close to Orion again was a straight-up death wish. I couldn't go through that meat grinder again, no matter how much he acted like a changed man now.

I stood up, wiped my face, and walked into the bathroom. I had a couple of hours to pull myself together before we had to leave.

I stripped down and cranked the shower to scorching hot. I stepped under the spray, hoping the heat would scrub away the phantom feel of his fingers on my skin.

I stayed under until my skin was raw, then shut off the water. I wrapped a towel tight around myself and stepped out. Clara was sitting right there on my mattress. I froze. "Clara? Everything okay?" I asked, clutching the towel to my chest.

It felt like eons since we’d actually talked. She was pissed at me, and honestly, she had every right to be. But her silent treatment was absolutely killing me.

Every time I walked into a room, she walked out. She ignored my texts, shut down my conversations, and blocked me out completely. This week had been pure hell, and I missed my kid so damn much.

"Hey... can we talk?" she mumbled. The crack in her voice wrecked me.

"Yeah, just give me a sec to throw some clothes on," I said, grabbing my outfit and ducking back into the bathroom.

Two minutes later, I was dressed. I walked back out and sat down right next to her on the edge of the bed.

"I'm sorry," she whispered, looking down. "I was super mad, but I shouldn't have been such a brat. You're literally the best mom ever."

My eyes stung. I blinked hard to keep from crying, then reached out, took her hand, and pressed a kiss to her knuckles.

"I didn't mean it when I said I hated you. I was just venting. It took me a minute to realize you didn't lie to protect yourself—you did it to protect me."

"Clara..." My voice cracked.

"No, let me finish. I'm sorry for being so stubborn and rude. Please don't be mad at me."

She started crying, and that was it. I pulled her right onto my lap, even though she was way too big for it now, and wrapped my arms around her, squeezing her tight.

"I could never hate you, baby. Never, okay?" I mumbled into her hair, my voice shaking as I tried to hold it together.

"I love you, Mom."

"Love you more, sweetie," I choked out, a lump forming in my throat.

We just held each other until a knock rattled the door. She slid off my lap and went to open it. Orion was standing in the hallway.

I reminded myself: it’s just physical. Just a stupid crush. As long as I didn't catch feelings, I'd survive. Lust I could handle. Love? No way.

"You guys ready?" he asked, his eyes locking onto mine.

"Yep," Clara answered for the both of us.

"Cool. Let's move." He turned and walked off.

I grabbed my bag, stood up, and walked over to Clara by the door.

I went to step out, but she grabbed my wrist, stopping me in my tracks.

She flashed me a wicked little smirk. "Just so you know... a little brother or sister would actually be pretty cool."

She let go, spun on her heel, and strolled down the hall, leaving me completely frozen.
