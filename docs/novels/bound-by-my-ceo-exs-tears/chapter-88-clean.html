Chapter 89

Days later, Declan’s sheer audacity still has my blood boiling. The guy actually played dumb about my pregnancy hell. Unbelievable. Him, the Sharps, his whole damn family—they put me through that. I almost miscarried, and they wonder why I won't play nice?

It makes me want to scream. I was going under, and they just watched. Why? Because of *that* night. Like I slept with myself. Like I dragged Declan kicking and screaming into my bed. He wanted it just as bad, but I got the villain edit. They punished me for it.

Honestly? It was just a convenient excuse. They never wanted me in their little club anyway. That night gave them the perfect out to dump me.

I meant what I told Declan. I can’t regret him—not when he gave me Asher. But God, I wish I’d packed a bag and run the second the test turned positive. My life would be totally different.

*Everything happens for a reason, Lyra,* my brain whispers. *The trauma loop got you here.* It brought me to my parents, Piper, Audrey, Silas, and Jasper. It led me straight to Adrian. And now, this new little bump. It all lined up for something better.

I take a breath, and the rage just... leaks out. She’s right. Sure, running away sounds nice, but then who do I have? No Asher. No Adrian. Erasing Declan means erasing my whole world.

I sink into the couch cushions, forcing my shoulders to drop. I’m moving on, period. People keep picking at the scabs, but I’m done bleeding for them.

I’m just about to stretch out when—*clatter*. A massive crash echoes from the kitchen. My spine goes rigid. My heart starts hammering against my ribs.

I slide off the couch and creep toward the noise. I used to yell at the screen during horror movies. *Why are you walking toward the monster, you idiot? Just run!* Yet, here I am, doing the exact same stupid thing.

I should dial 911. Seriously. But what if it’s just Cal’s dumb dog? That mutt loves breaking in to raid my counter for cupcakes.

I peek around the corner. Empty. I slide past the island to check the back door. Shattered glass everywhere. Oh, God. Someone’s in here.

Ice floods my veins. Not this shit again. And I’m alone, pregnant, and totally defenseless. I spin around to bolt for my phone, but my legs turn to lead.

Kaelen is standing right there. He looks messy, unhinged, and completely lethal.

"Hey, Lyra," he says. The sound of his voice makes my skin crawl. If Declan’s voice is ice, this guy is a straight-up blizzard.

My hand flies to the knife block. I slide out a blade and point it right at his chest. "Back the fuck up!"

He raises his hands, palms out. "Whoa, easy. I’m not here to hurt you."

I let out a harsh laugh. "Yeah, right. You look real peaceful."

"Seriously. I just want to talk, okay?" He drops his hands and slides onto a barstool like he owns the place.

I stare at him, my eye literally twitching. The nerve of this guy. He’s acting like a houseguest, not a psycho who kidnapped me ten days ago.

"Talk about what? I’m not playing a part in your sick little vendetta against Declan. Go ruin his life, knock yourself out. Just leave me and my kid out of your mess."

Something flashes in his eyes—guilt? Regret?—but it vanishes before I can catch it. "I don't do kids," he says, his voice flat. "That’s a hard rule in my line of work."

That throws me, but I keep my face blank. Doesn't change the fact that he used me as bait. "Then why are you in my kitchen? What do you want from me?"

"Drop the knife," he orders.

"Not a chance in hell. I’m not an idiot. You want to talk? Talk. But this blade stays right here."

He smirks. "Feisty. I like that."

"What do you want, Kaelen?" I repeat, my grip tightening. God, I’m such an idiot. Why did I leave my phone on the coffee table? I could have texted Declan under the counter.

"Your boyfriend sent me a little message," he says, his lips curling into a nasty grin.

I blink, totally lost. "What boyfriend? I don’t have a boyfriend."

"Adrian. That ring a bell?"

My stomach drops. Oh, God. What did Adrian do? Does he have a death wish? The cops have been chasing this guy for years and got nothing. He’s a ghost. A powerful, terrifying ghost.

"Yeah, he hired a guy to hunt me down and put a bullet in my head," Kaelen says, chuckling. "Playing the hero for his pregnant girl. Cute. But he forgot one tiny detail: I run this city's underworld. You can’t put a hit on the guy who signs your hitman's paychecks."

I’m not even shocked he knows about the baby.

Damn it. Please tell me Adrian didn't just sign his own death warrant with Damien. I didn't survive all that drama with my ex just to have my baby's father get murdered before he even meets his kid.

"Can’t blame the guy for trying, though," Kaelen muses. "He didn't know I’d never actually hurt you."

I blink, my voice cracking. "W-what?"

He ignores me, staring off like I’m not even there. "Sure, I wanted to mess with Declan’s head. I knew he’d pick you, which meant Aurora was a goner. But even if he’d picked her, you were safe. I wouldn't have touched a hair on your head."

I just stare at him, my brain short-circuiting. None of this makes any sense. "Are you insane? Declan would’ve picked Aurora in a heartbeat. You were playing Russian roulette with my life!"

"No, he wouldn't," Kaelen says, deadpan. "Maybe the old Declan, but not now. I’ve been tracking him. The guy would’ve let Aurora bleed out if it meant keeping you breathing. His feelings changed. Or maybe they were always there and he was just too stupid to see it until you walked away."

My throat goes dry. Declan told me the exact same thing, but I brushed it off. Hearing it from his worst enemy, though? That hits different. My head is a total mess now. I shake my head hard, trying to clear the fog.

I look at this terrifying guy. He’s so chill, like he’s not a wanted fugitive who could get busted any second. It’s wild. "If you weren't going to hurt us, why the hell did you kidnap us in the first place?"

"I said I wouldn't hurt *you*," he corrects, pointing a finger at me. "Aurora? She was collateral damage." God, this guy is a walking riddle.

"Aren't you going to ask me why?" he asks, tilting his head.

I let out a long, exhausted sigh. I just want him out of my house. "Fine. Why?"

He smiles. For once, it’s not a creepy, serial-killer grin. It’s actually... normal. "Because you’re pregnant with my niece or nephew. I can't exactly go around hurting family, can I?"

My jaw drops. "Wait, what? That’s impossible. I’m an only child. The only way you’re an uncle is if..."

The realization hits me like a freight train. Oh, crap. Just what I needed. More drama. Now my kid has a literal psychopath for an uncle.

"There it is," he says, leaning forward with a spark in his eyes. "Smart girl."

"Does... does Adrian know?" I ask, my brain still spinning.

"Nope. He was too young to remember. I was twelve, he was a baby, maybe one, when my dad sent me to juvie on some gang errand. I didn't even realize it was illegal back then—I was just being groomed to take over. By the time I got released, our old man was dead and Adrian was already adopted."

He goes quiet, staring into space. The ghosts of his past are practically visible in his eyes. Unlike the guy Declan put down, Damien—or Kaelen, whoever he is—never had a choice. He was drafted into this war as a kid.

"Why didn't you go find him?" I ask, locking eyes with him. "Once you got out and were old enough to make your own moves?"

"Because I love him," he says simply. "I wasn't going to drag him into this gutter. He was happy. He had a family who actually loved him. If he’d been bouncing around foster care, or if his adoptive parents treated him like shit—like yours did to you—I would’ve grabbed him. But I saw how much they cared. So I walked away. It was the only shot he had at a normal life."

Damn it. My chest tightens. How am I supposed to hate a guy who gave up his own brother just to keep him safe? "But... he still ended up in prison," I whisper. "Even after all that."

"Yeah," he sighs. "I should’ve kept tabs on him. Maybe I could’ve steered him clear of that mess."

I let out a dry snort. "Right. By helping him plan a cleaner way to murder me?"

That creepy smirk slides back onto his face. "Hey, a brother’s gotta help. I’ve put plenty of people in the dirt, so yeah, I’ve got some solid tips on how to do it without getting caught."

I should be terrified. Honestly, I should. But I’m not. Not anymore.
