NovelVibe

Chapter 72

Sunday dinner. Again. The Woods and the Sharps have been doing this monthly ritual since I was a five-year-old kid. Our moms grew up together, practically attached at the hip. Naturally, the friendship trickled down to us. Two families, one giant, messy circle.

"Dad, seriously? Speed it up. Grandpa's steak is gonna burn," Asher grumbles from the passenger seat, his forehead scrunched up.

Honestly, if it weren't for my kid, I'd have skipped this whole thing. I used to live for these dinners. Back when Lyra was blacklisted. Back when I knew she wouldn't show. It was my safe haven, a room packed with people who despised her just as much as I did—everyone except Asher. Now? God, it makes me sick. I hate myself. I hate all of us for what we did to her.

"I'm doing the speed limit, bud," I say.

"Bull. My grandma walks faster than this car," he snorts, crossing his arms.

Great. Roasted by an eight-year-old. I shake my head, let out a dry laugh, and nudge the gas pedal down.

The car goes quiet, and boom—Lyra's back in my head. She looked so damn gorgeous when she opened her door earlier. Wearing this breezy white dress, her dark curls bouncing, her skin glowing, and that tiny, perfect baby bump. She looked like some kind of goddess. My chest tightened. All I wanted was to grab her, drag her into her bedroom, lock the door, and lose myself in her for hours.

It hit me out of nowhere. I've never felt this way about her, and it's throwing me off. She's rent-free in my head. No distractions work. It's terrifying, honestly, how bad I've got it. I catch myself wondering what she's doing at random hours. Did she eat lunch? Is she sleeping okay? Should I hire a housekeeper to take the load off her? It freaks me out. I never gave a shit about helping Lyra before. Now, this urge to protect her, to take care of her, is eating me alive.

"Hey, Dad?" Asher's voice snaps me out of it.

"Yeah?"

"We're here."

I blink. Sure enough, the car is parked in my parents' driveway. I don't even remember the drive. I was on autopilot, totally zoned out. It's stupid and dangerous, especially with my kid in the seat next to me, but I can't help it. Lyra is a constant noise in my brain.

"Alright, let's move. Grandpa's got a steak with your name on it," I say, trying to shake the weird vibe.

Asher doesn't move. "You okay, Dad?"

Damn. I hate making him worry. But what am I supposed to say? *Hey kid, I'm losing my mind over your gorgeous mom.*

"Yeah, buddy. Just work stuff. Don't worry about it."

He nods, clicks his seatbelt open, and we hop out. I lock the doors, and we head up the walkway. The front door swings open before we even reach the porch.

"Grandma!" Asher yells, sprinting ahead.

"Oh, my sweet boy!" Eleanor kneels, catching him in a tight hug as he slams into her.

My parents always adored Asher, no matter how ugly things got with Lyra. Everyone loved the kid. We just chose to hate his mother for what we thought she did to me and Aurora.

"Where's Lyra? I sent her a text," Eleanor asks, standing up and scanning the driveway behind me.

"She couldn't make it," Asher says. "She's visiting Grandma Beatrice and Grandpa Edmund today. She was super excited because they missed last week's visit for some work trip."

Asher misses it, but I don't. Eleanor's face completely drops. A raw flash of hurt crosses her eyes. It's got to sting, watching Lyra bond with her real parents while Eleanor is left in the cold. But she brought this on herself. She burned that bridge, cut Lyra too deep for any quick fix. Lyra is done with both our families. The only reason she even tolerates me is because of Asher—and because I refuse to let her shut me out. If I weren't pushing my way in, she'd have dumped me in the trash with the rest of them.

"Well... maybe next time," Eleanor whispers, her smile cracking.

She's right on the edge of crying. Asher has no clue about the family drama, and I need to keep it that way. If he sees his grandma break down, he'll start asking questions. And if he ever finds out how we treated his mother? He'll explode. Asher is fiercely loyal to Lyra. If he learns the truth, we're dead to him. He'll cut us out of his life without a second thought.

"Come on, let's get inside. Everyone's waiting," I say, stepping in and nudging them through the door.

Eleanor ducks into the hallway bathroom, and Asher and I head out to the patio. I swear, my stomach drops when I see the crowd. The whole damn family is here. Asher bolts ahead, leaving me behind. He says hi to everyone—except Aurora. He doesn't even look her way. He hates her, and he doesn't care who knows it.

"Glad you made it, son," my dad says, slamming a hand onto my shoulder. He knows I hate that, but he does it anyway.

"Yeah," I mutter. I already want to leave.

"Wow, try to contain your excitement," Orion drawls, dripping sarcasm.

I shoot him a death stare. He doesn't even blink. Twin perk, I guess—he's the only guy who isn't intimidated by me.

"Oh, come here, Declan. Give your mother a hug," my mom says, holding her arms out. "Missed you."

I pull her in for a quick, one-armed squeeze. "Missed you too, Mom."

We pull apart just as Eleanor walks out. Her eyes are bloodshot, her face blotchy. Over by the picnic table, Asher is already piling his plate, chatting with Trevor. Trevor's girlfriend, Piper, skipped today. No surprise there—she refuses to hang out with the people who trashed Lyra. Her loyalty to Lyra is putting a massive strain on their relationship. Trevor is terrified she's going to dump him, especially since he was part of the crowd that hurt Lyra. The only reason he's still got a girlfriend is because he's practically begging for forgiveness.

"Declan? Can we talk?" Aurora's voice cuts through the noise.

Shit. I didn't even see her slide up next to me.

"Nothing to talk about, Aurora," I say, turning to face her.

I've been dodging her calls for weeks. I'm still pissed about the garbage she spewed about Asher. Honestly, I just don't want her near me. Not when Lyra is the only thing on my mind.

I used to worship this girl. Even when I was married to her sister, I was obsessed with Aurora. I thought she was my end-game. I used to pray she'd come back to me. I swore that if she ever gave me another shot, I'd dump Lyra instantly and build a life with her. Well, she's been back for months now. And that epic, eternal love I swore I'd never feel for anyone else? It's completely gone. I feel like a total idiot. One second I'm shouting my love for Aurora from the rooftops, and the next I can't stand her touch. One second I'm convinced I hate Lyra, and the next I'm dying to get close to her. These two sisters are driving me absolutely insane.

"Declan..." she starts.

"What?" I snap. The air feels tight.

Her eyes well up with tears, but honestly? I don't give a shit. Not anymore. I don't even wait for her to reply. I just turn on my heel and walk away.

For the longest time, I played the "what if" game. What if I finally got the girl? What if we got married, had kids, and lived the life we were supposed to have years ago? Those questions haunted me for years, especially when Aurora first got back. I thought it was our second shot at happily ever after. I was wrong. We're different people now. We'd never survive as a couple, and honestly, even if we'd stayed together back then, we probably wouldn't have made it. She'll always have a piece of my history as my first love, but it's time to face the music. My feelings for her are dead. Hell, they've probably been dead for years.
