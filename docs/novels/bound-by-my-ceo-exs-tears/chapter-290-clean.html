Chapter 296

My foot barely hits the pavement before his hand clamps onto my wrist. He yanks. Hard. I stumble, head snapping up, and meet a pair of absolute blowtorch eyes.

"Where the fuck is your ring?" he snarls, drilling holes into me.

Oh, crap. What?

My gaze drops to my bare left hand. You ever have those brain-fart moments? Like, you hear the words, you know the answer, but your brain just spins its wheels? Yeah. That’s me. Totally blanking.

"Willow. The ring. Where is it?" He grinds the words out, stepping out of the driver's seat.

He stands up, all six-foot-something of him, completely blocking out the sun. I’m frozen. Just totally shut down under his shadow. He gives my arm a quick, sharp tug to snap me out of it.

"I don't... what is even happening right now?" I mumble. Seriously, why is he losing his mind over a piece of metal?

His expression goes from pissed to downright lethal. Like my confusion just flipped a switch in his head.

"What's happening," he says, voice dangerously low, "is you're missing my ring. Explain."

I blink. "I didn't think it mattered." Great. Real smart, Willow. But honestly? My brain was already fried from the gym session and whatever the hell just went down in the backseat.

"You didn't think it mattered?"

I just nod, dumb as a rock, watching his jaw clench so hard it might crack. Red alert. I try to take a step back, but nope. His hands lock onto my shoulders, trapping me.

"I gave you that ring for a reason. You wear it. Period. Now, where is it?"

"My purse," I squeak.

He doesn't hesitate. He snatches my bag, zips it open, and digs around. I just stand there, mouth open, as he fishes out the band. He grabs my hand, shoves the ring back onto my finger, and glares.

"Keep it on. Got it?"

I nod. No way am I poking the bear right now. He dumps the purse back into my arms, brushes past me, and heads for the elevator, leaving me standing in the cold garage. I let out a heavy sigh, exhausted, and jog to catch up.

Seriously, today was a total trainwreck. I couldn't focus on a single task. My brain was split between looping the weirdness with Orion and worrying about Clara Sterling at school. I was cranky, exhausted, and basically useless. I kept staring at the bottom right corner of my screen, praying for 5:00 PM so I could crawl into bed.

I almost asked Orion if I could bounce early, but changed my mind. I'm here to learn. You don't call in sick just because you're having a bad day.

Around noon, I dropped off some files, and he asked me to get lunch. Call me a chicken, but I couldn't do it. I lied and said I had too much work. He didn't argue, but his jaw ticked. Message received. I bolted back to my desk. Seriously, I run away from this guy way too much.

Cut to 4:30 PM. I hear a voice: "Well, look what the cat dragged in."

I look up, see Bianca, and realize my day is about to go from bad to absolute garbage.

I sigh, turning around. "What is it, Bianca?"

This girl has been riding my back all week. I’ve been playing nice, but my fuse is getting dangerously short. It was almost funny at first, her little mean-girl act, but now it's just annoying. She’s got her two little clones flanking her, looking smug.

Bianca sneers, scanning me up and down, then her eyes lock onto my left hand. Her jaw drops. I'm confused for a second, then I remember the ring.

"Oh my god," she shrieks. "You're married!"

We're in the breakroom, and suddenly every head whips toward us.

"You have a husband," she yells, fake-gasping, "and you're still sleeping with Orion to climb the ladder? Wow. That is so trashy."

I freeze. I can't believe she just said that out loud. In front of the whole office. Just to humiliate me.

"Shut your mouth," I snap, finally finding my voice. "You don't know what you're talking about."

She steps right into my face, smirking. "Oh, really? You're hooking up with the CEO while wearing a wedding band. That makes you a gold-digging whore *and* a cheater."

The room goes quiet, then the whispering starts. More people walk in, crowding the doorway. Great. Bianca feeds on the audience.

"Does your husband know you're sleeping around?" she mocks. "Or does Orion know? Do you slip the ring off when you're hooking up with him?"

I'm literally vibrating with rage. My patience? Gone. I'm about to swing on her when Carol, another one of her minions, bursts in and grabs Bianca's arm.

"Bianca, stop," Carol whispers frantically. "The company's Insta just posted—"

Bianca shoves her back, nearly knocking her over. "Get off me!" She turns to the crowd, raising her voice. "I seriously can't stand girls who use their bodies to get promotions. Spreading their legs because they have zero talent. It's pathetic that Willow has to sleep her way to the top."

People are muttering, nodding along. God, this is humiliating. My fists clench. I'm half a second away from slapping the highlights out of her hair when a voice cuts through the room like a chainsaw.

"What the fuck did you just call my wife?"

Orion strides in, his presence instantly sucking all the oxygen out of the room. He steps right up next to me, his eyes locking onto Bianca like a predator.

"Wife?" she squeaks.

The color drains from her face. Her smug little attitude completely evaporates. I let out a slow smirk as she swallows hard, pure terror in her eyes.
