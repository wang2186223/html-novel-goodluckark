Chapter 152

I scoop her up and head straight for the library. It’s my absolute favorite spot in this giant house. I slide onto the seat by the massive bay windows, yank down my camisole and bra, and let her latch. She doesn't waste a second.

I look down at her while she gulps. Those big blue eyes lock onto mine, totally trusting, totally spaced out. A dry laugh slips out of me. Seriously? Not one of my kids got my eyes. Both of them took straight after their dads.

I trace a finger down her baby-soft cheek, my mind drifting. What did Adrian actually look like? Aurora is basically my mini-me, minus the eyes. Without that piece of the puzzle, I can't even picture his face.

Once she pops off, I hoist her over my shoulder to burp her. Usually, she’s an easy baby—feed her, and she’s out. Not today. She starts squirming, kicking, and screaming her head off.

I’m about to lose my mind trying to calm her down when Declan strolls in. He’s ditched his jacket, sleeves rolled up to his elbows. Without a word, he slides his arms under Aurora. Boom. Instant silence.

"Hey, what's all the noise about, princess?" he coos, flashing that soft, melting smile.

Aurora just gapes at him, completely mesmerized. Seriously, the kid looks like she’s looking at God.

I sink into a chair and just watch them. Declan keeps babbling to her, and I stay quiet. My brain is spinning. Too many loose ends. My head is a total fucking disaster zone right now.

"Look, I'm sorry I snapped earlier," his quiet, guilty voice cuts through my thoughts. "It's just... I lose my mind when I picture you with Adrian. I get so damn jealous."

That hits me out of nowhere. I freeze, totally blanking on what to say, so I just shut my mouth and let him talk.

"It eats me alive that I almost lost you to that guy. My own goddamn stupidity pushed you right into his bed. That's why hearing his name makes my blood boil. He did what no one else could. He actually got inside your head—got into a heart that was supposed to be mine."

He’s not wrong. And honestly, that’s the part that messes with me. How did Adrian manage that? Sure, I feel absolutely zero for him now, but I can't deny the truth. Back then, there was a spark. I was falling.

"Declan... where is Adrian?" A cold spike of fear hits my chest. I know how psycho Declan can get when he's possessive. I remember how he acted with Aurora. What if he snapped and did something terrible to Adrian?

He lets out a heavy sigh. "Behind bars."

My jaw drops. "Wait, what?" No way I heard that right.

"Adrian's in prison."

Okay. Did not have that on my bingo card.

"How the hell did that happen?"

So he spills the whole story. Adrian was Beatrice and Edmund's adopted kid. The guy played me like a fiddle, got sloppy, got busted, and got locked up.

"Holy shit," I breathe, staring at him, completely dumbfounded. "I was literally hooking up with my adopted brother while he was plotting to put me in a grave. That is some straight-up Lifetime movie garbage."

Declan lets out this low, pissed-off growl. It jerks Aurora right out of her doze.

"What did I say about putting you, Adrian, and the word 'sex' in the same goddamn sentence?"

I shake my head, my brain still trying to process the madness. "My bad." Silence settles over us. We're both locked in our own thoughts. Honestly, it's a miracle I haven't ended up in a psych ward after all this.

I mean, seriously? My baby daddy is my biological parents' adopted son, and he tried to whack me just to steal the CEO chair. You can't make this shit up.

"I was thinking about letting him see Aurora. Is that... a terrible idea?" I ask, my stomach twisting. I don't exactly want a literal psycho playing dad.

Declan groans, rubbing his face. "Man, I hate saying this, but yeah. Let him be in her life. Sure, he was a snake at first, but he actually felt like shit about it at the end. Pretty sure the guy actually fell for you."

He practically choked on those words, but they did the trick. "Thanks for being straight with me. That's all I wanted." I get up, slide over next to him, and rest my head on his shoulder. I catch the flash of worry in his eyes but decide to ignore it.

Whatever secrets he's still keeping, they can wait. I'll dig them up eventually anyway. Right now, I have a bigger problem: how the hell do I visit Adrian in prison without starting World War III with Declan?
