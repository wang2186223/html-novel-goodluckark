Chapter 215

I gave a quick nod. Yeah, that phone call definitely rattled him. My gut screamed at me to bolt right then, but we had a house full of people. Asher’s birthday bash was still roaring downstairs.

"We finish the party first," I said, pushing myself up from the chair. "Then we go deal with Garrett." Everyone murmured in agreement, and we headed back into the chaos.

Sure enough, Declan had dumped the whole Aurora mess onto my folks. I walked over to my friends, who were already wearing matching looks of pure panic.

"Okay, what's the deal?" Piper asked, her eyes darting around the yard. "Where did Aurora, Trevor, and Eleanor vanish to?" She was definitely hunting for her guy.

"Aurora got busted," I said flatly. "Trevor and Eleanor went with her to the precinct."

"Wait, what?!" they yelled.

A couple of toddlers playing nearby froze and stared. I waved my hands to quiet them down, then spilled the whole story. Silas just stood there, totally stone-faced, while Audrey and Piper looked like they'd been slapped.

"Why the hell would they pin this on Aurora?" Audrey asked. It was the exact same question that had been eating a hole in my brain.

"Your guess is as good as mine," I sighed. "The deputies just said they had a warrant for her arrest. Zero details. No explanation whatsoever."

We just sat there in a heavy silence, everyone locked in their own heads. After that, the party became a total blur. My mind kept looping back to Aurora and whatever the police chief needed to tell us. I was completely checked out. Even when Piper tried to make small talk, I barely heard a word.

Piper bailed early. I didn't blame her. She didn't even like Aurora, but she needed to back up Trevor. She'd hesitated, torn between staying with me or leaving. I told her I was totally fine and practically shoved her out the door to go find Corbin.

The second the party wrapped, my skin started crawling to get to the station. We ushered the guests out, until the crowd thinned down to just the inner circle.

"Hey, Mom, can Jasper sleep over tonight?" Asher piped up, his face still glowing from all the birthday hype.

I shot Silas a questioning look. He gave me a quick nod. I turned back to Asher with a weak smile. "Yeah, sure, buddy. Sleepover's on."

The boys let out a wild shriek. Asher grabbed Jasper's wrist, and they bolted up the stairs. A second later, a bedroom door slammed shut upstairs.

"Ready to roll?" Declan asked, jingling his keys. "We can head out right now."

My parents stared at me, totally confused. Right—I hadn't told them a thing. I quickly gave both sets of parents the bare-bones version of the drama.

"Just go," Declan's mom said immediately. "We've got the kids covered here."

With the babysitting sorted, we piled into our cars. I hopped into the passenger seat next to Declan.

"So... do you think she actually did it?" I asked after a few minutes of quiet driving. I wanted his take, sure, but mostly I wanted to see his face. This was his ex, after all. The girl he used to love.

"Honestly? No clue," he said, cutting me a quick glance before locking his eyes back on the asphalt. "But I mean, she had a reason to. And it's not like you two haven't been at each other's throats since she got back into town."

"Seriously?"

"Yeah. Word got back to me that she cornered you, talked some major trash, and told you to back off from me. Like, multiple times. So, yeah, she's totally capable of it. The old Aurora? No way. But this version of her? She's a wildcard. She could do anything."

I shut my mouth. My brain was a mess. With my amnesia, I had zero clue what kind of person Aurora had turned into. Sure, she'd been a total bitch to me twice since I woke up from my coma, but did that really mean she wanted me six feet under?
