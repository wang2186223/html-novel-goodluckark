Chapter 191

The ride was quick. We traded a few words, but mostly kept our mouths shut. Honestly? It didn't feel weird for once. Usually, car rides with Declan were a total disaster. He’d freeze me out, I’d babble like an idiot to break the ice, and we’d both end up drowning in pure awkwardness. Not tonight, though.

He killed the engine, hopped out, and opened my door. I caught him grinning. "What's that face for?" I asked, stepping onto the pavement.

God, that smile of his. It’s practically lethal. The guy is already a ten, but when he flashes those teeth? Game over.

He cocked his head, eyes locked on mine. "What, a guy can't be pumped to take his girl out?"

A giggle slipped out of my throat. Gross. I don’t giggle. Ever. I used to roll my eyes at grown women who did that high-pitched, girly crap. Yet, here I was, acting like a teenager.

"Ugh, sorry," I muttered, shaking my head to clear the blush. "No idea where that came from."

He grabbed my hand, pulling it up so I could loop my arm through his. Feeling his hard bicep under my palm sent a jolt straight to my chest. This whole 'us' thing felt so damn real, yet so fake. Like, wake me up before I fall out of bed, please.

"Don't apologize," Declan murmured, squeezing my fingers. "It's cute. I like it."

Yeah, right. I didn't buy it, but I flashed him a grin anyway.

We walked up to the entrance. One look inside and my suspicions were confirmed: fancy, over-the-top, and dripping in old money. I snorted mentally. Classic Declan. The guy didn't have a single clue what I actually liked.

"Ah, Mr. Wood. Right this way, your table is set," the hostess chirped the second we crossed the threshold.

Declan gave her a curt nod. She spun around and led the way. I kept my eyes glued forward, ignoring the whispers and staring eyes. Declan’s a big deal, and Piper keeps reminding me that my charity foundation makes me a target for the cameras too. I loathe the spotlight. Suddenly, the buzz of our date started to fizzle out.

The hostess poured two glasses of red, promised our waiter would be right over, and vanished.

The silence stretched for a second before Declan leaned in. "Did I mention how gorgeous you look tonight?"

I smirked over my glass. "Nope. Not yet."

"Well, you do, Lyra. Seriously. You're stunning. I can barely look at anything else."

Okay, the girls were totally right about this dress. The look in his eyes was pure, unadulterated hunger. But god, it was frustrating. He talks a big game, but does he have any idea what he's doing to me? Sharing a bed with him every single night, waking up to his lips on mine, feeling his hands grip my hips and yank me against his chest—only for him to pull the emergency brake the second things get hot? It's torture. Absolute, slow-motion torture. And I bet he doesn't even realize he's doing it.

"Thanks," I said, taking a long gulp of wine to cool down.

I looked around the dining room. Sure, it was beautiful if you're into the whole five-star, white-tablecloth vibe. But me? Not so much. I just had no clue how to break it to him.

"Lyra?"

His voice snapped me out of my head. I blinked. "Huh? Yeah?"

"You went somewhere else there for a second."

"Oh. Sorry."

I tried to lock in, but my brain wouldn't cooperate. God, I felt like a total jerk. I’d been dying for this date, and now that I was here, all I wanted was to run out the back door.

A low chuckle made me look up. Declan was kicked back in his seat, one hand resting on the table, wearing this incredibly smug smirk. I cocked my head, annoyed.

"What?" I snapped. "What's so hilarious?"

He didn't answer. He just stood up and offered me his hand. I stared at his palm like he'd lost his mind.

"Up," he said, waving his hand. "We're leaving."

"Wait, what about our food?"

"Forget the food. You think I don't know you detest these stuffy joints? This was a test, babe. I wanted to see if you'd actually speak up or just sit there and suffer."

My jaw dropped. He knew? I couldn't decide if I wanted to kiss him or punch him. Honestly, punching him felt more appropriate. Why couldn't he just be straight with me from the start?

"Come on. Time for the actual date," he said, grabbing my hand and hauling me out of my chair. He snatched my purse off the table and tucked it under his arm.

"But the reservation?" I hissed as we headed for the exit.

"Don't sweat it. Orion and I own this joint. The hostess already knew we weren't staying."

He opened my door, shut it behind me, and jogged around to the driver's side. We buckled up, he cranked the engine, and I turned in my seat to glare at him.

"Alright, spill. What's the real plan? You've got my attention."

He laughed, shifting into drive. "Man, you have zero patience. Just chill, okay? I've got this. You're gonna love it."

I couldn't help the stupid grin spreading across my face. Sure, he’d totally played me back there, but once the shock wore off, I was actually thrilled. He actually paid attention to what I liked. I had no idea how he figured it out, considering we’d never gone out before, but whatever.

We chatted as he drove, and then my jaw dropped as he pulled into a fast-food drive-through lane.

"No way," I gasped, turning to him. "Are we really doing this?"

"Oh, absolutely," he said, flashing a smile that made my chest do a little flip. "What are you having?"

I rattled off my order, and he spoke into the intercom. A minute later, we grabbed the bag of greasy goodness and hit the road. Gosh, I was buzzing. This night was turning around fast.

"I gotta admit, I'm shocked," I said, tearing open the bag. "You don't exactly look like a burger-and-fries kind of guy. I figured you were strictly a five-star-dining snob."

He shot me a quick glance. "Well, that just proves you don't know me at all, babe. I'd take grease over fancy any day of the week. Honestly, after we split up and before Martha started cooking for me, this stuff was basically my entire diet."

I burst out laughing. The mental image of Declan living off cheap fast food was just too much.

"Seriously," he chuckled. "If I didn't hit the gym like a maniac back then, I'd have a massive beer gut by now."

That made me laugh even harder. Declan with a dad bod? Impossible. The man was pure muscle.

"Well, thank god for your workout routine," I teased, leaning closer. "Otherwise, I'd be deprived of the view every time you strip down."

His laugh went low and husky, sending a shiver down my spine. Gosh, this was everything I’d ever wanted. Just Declan, relaxed and genuinely happy to be with me. It felt like a dream come true.

"And... we're here," he said, pulling the car to a stop a few minutes later.
