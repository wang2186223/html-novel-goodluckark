Chapter 463

Stuffed. I slump back in my chair, rubbing my bloated belly with one hand and stifling a massive burp with the other. Clara Sterling dabs her lips with a napkin, a wicked little smirk already creeping onto her face.

"Right," she says, tossing the crumpled paper onto her empty plate. "Bar. Now. Let's go."

I deadpan at her. "Clara. I'm literally growing a human. Remember?"

She snorts. "Chill, mama bear. Mocktails exist, you know. You can sip juice from a fancy martini glass while I get buzzed. Win-win, babe."

I roll my eyes, but a laugh slips out anyway. "Seriously, who drags a pregnant chick to a pub?"

"Correction—only I know when said pregnant chick desperately needs to escape," she says with a wink. "Come on, Si. It'll do you good."

I pause, weighing my exhaustion against her sheer stubbornness. But she's right. I need this. I need some good vibes after the total trainwreck of dealing with Asher and Delilah.

I sigh, shaking my head. "Fine. But only because you'll literally die of boredom without me."

She rolls her eyes and snorts, but her face lights up.

We split the bill and bounce. Half an hour later, Clara pulls into the lot of a cozy little dive bar glowing warmly in the dark. Bass thumps softly from inside, people are laughing, and the whole place feels alive but not totally chaotic.

We step inside. The air smells like lime juice and sugary booze. Clara grabs a booth in the far corner, and I slide in right after her, glad to be away from the loud crowd.

The server pops over. Clara orders some fancy cocktail I can't even pronounce, while I play it safe with a club soda.

While we wait, I lean in, shredding my straw wrapper. "So... Delilah showed up today."

Clara's eyebrow shoots up. "Wait, Delilah?"

"Yep, the bitch herself," I mumble. "And it wasn't a social call. She literally threatened me."

Clara's playful vibe vanishes instantly. Her face goes stone-cold. "She did *what*?"

"Who the hell does she think she is? Where does she get the nerve? And why on earth did Asher open his big mouth about you being pregnant?"

The words fly out of her like rapid-fire bullets. I almost laugh, honestly. That's just Clara—ready to burn the city to the ground if anyone touches a hair on my head.

"Trust me, I was totally blindsided too," I say.

I should probably spill the whole story—Asher creeping around last night, the morning panic attack. But I don't want to freak her out. Plus, her relationship with Asher is already a dumpster fire because of me. No need to throw more gas on it by telling her he was making threats too.

"I told you we should've handled her ages ago," she grumbles, crossing her arms tight. "You gotta squash bugs like her before they get too big to stomp. Now she's got the balls to threaten you because you played nice and wouldn't let me destroy her and Seraphina."

Back when we first realized how toxic they were, Clara wanted to "handle" them. Knowing my psycho bestie, "handling" could mean anything from blackmail to a shovel in the woods. I wasn't trying to go to prison.

"You know Asher treats her like a sister," I sigh.

Clara snarls. "She's a straight-up leech, is what she is."

Honestly, Clara had tried warning Asher about Seraphina and Delilah's mind games a million times. But the guy was blind. He always figured Clara was just talking trash to defend me. He actually thought I was the puppet master, pulling Clara's strings, so he shut her down every single time she tried to talk sense into him.
