NovelVibe

Chapter 79

Woke up today feeling like absolute garbage. Mostly because Asher was still giving me the cold shoulder. Why? Because I kicked Declan out. I thought I played it cool, but nope. The kid saw right through my crap.

Honestly, we should’ve never played happy family in front of him. Bad call. We thought we were shielding him, giving him some picture-perfect childhood. Total BS. We just fed him a lie.

Now he’s convinced his dad and I were some epic love story. He thinks we can just patch things up.

How do I break the news without crushing his soul? Hey kiddo, everything you believe about me and Declan is a total sham. Yeah, try saying that.

My worst nightmare? He finds out we lied and ends up hating our guts. But ugh, we can't keep this charade going. I can't let him think there's a happily-ever-after waiting for us.

I groan, dragging myself out of bed. Crashed hard after Asher left for school. Every single day, this baby bump gets bigger, and my energy levels hit a new rock bottom.

I shuffle to the bathroom and turn on the shower. The hot water hits, but the exhaustion is deep in my bones. It’s not going anywhere. I grab a dress—white, spaghetti straps, covered in blue wildflowers. It hits right above the knee, putting my pregnant belly on full display. No more hiding it. Mom and Dad know, so whatever.

Skip the makeup. Just slap some concealer under my eyes to hide the zombie look.

I head downstairs. Right on cue, the doorbell rings.

Seriously? I’m in no mood for human interaction. I feel like death warmed up.

I yank the door open. Instantly regret it. Corbin is standing on my porch, looking like he just crawled out of a dumpster.

I start to slam the door, but he shoves his hand in, blocking it.

"Lyra, please," he croaks. He looks exhausted.

"What do you want?" My voice is pure ice. Even surprises me.

Funny how fast my tone flips these days. No effort required. Just instant, cold detachment. Like I’m talking to some random guy on the street.

"Can I come inside?"

"Hell no. Say your piece and get lost."

Okay, I’m lying if I say I’m not curious. Last time I saw him, Mom had just dropped the bomb about how I actually ended up with the Sharps.

He hesitates. Now I’m really intrigued. Corbin is usually a smug, arrogant prick. Seeing him sweat on my porch is actually kind of wild.

"Spit it out already. I don't have all day," I snap. He’s been quiet too long.

Why am I even listening to him? I should’ve just dialed 911 on his pathetic ass.

"Can you... talk to Beatrice and Edmund?" he finally mumbles. I have to lean in just to hear him.

"About what?"

"Sharp Corp. They’re destroying us. They’re getting all our investors to bail." He sounds completely broken. "The company is going under, Lyra. We’re losing clients, cash, everything."

I let out a breath. Honestly, I thought my parents' threats were just talk. Didn't think they'd actually go scorched-earth on the Sharps for what they did to me.

But Corbin? He loves that company more than life itself. It’s his entire identity. For him to be here, practically begging me for a lifeline? He’s hit rock bottom. He’s tried everything else, and I’m his absolute last resort.

"I don't give a shit if your company burns," I say, crossing my arms.

He looks totally wrecked. Like he's been running on fumes for weeks.

Piper hadn't warned me about this. Then again, I banned her from ever mentioning his name around me.

"Please, Lyra. I’m begging you. We’re family."

That word. It makes my blood boil. Instant, hot rage.

"Family?" I laugh, a harsh, ugly sound. "Are you kidding me, Corbin? You cut me off. You told me I was dead to you. You threw me away years ago. And then you kept proving it."

"Lyra—"

"Shut up," I snap. I don't want to hear a single word.

"Every time you backed Aurora, every time you treated me like dirt. Every single time you laughed when Declan ripped my heart out because I supposedly hurt your precious little sister. Did you think about family then? What about when you said I deserved to suffer? When Mom and Dad acted like I didn't exist? When you all iced me out? Was I family then?"

He shuts his mouth. Nothing to say. He knows he’s guilty. Back then, I wasn't family. I was just an annoying pest they wanted to sweep under the rug.

"So don't come here playing the family card now. It’s not going to work."

I stare him down. I used to look at us and wonder. Corbin and Aurora didn’t look identical, but you could tell they were siblings. Me? I looked like an alien next to them. Guess that was the first clue.

"Let’s be real. You never cared about me. You’re just trying to use me. Go home, Corbin. And stay the hell away from my house."

I shove him back and slam the door shut. Hard.

I lean against the wood, chest heaving. Silence. Then, the sound of his engine roaring to life as he speeds away.

I need to get out of this house. Now. I grab my keys. On my way out, I spot the baby clothes Declan bought. I grab the bag. Might as well drop them off at a shelter.

A few minutes later, I’m driving. My brain is a chaotic mess. First Declan, now Corbin. The sheer nerve of these guys, thinking they can just waltz back in and demand forgiveness. Like everything can just be wiped clean. Delusional.

If Declan weren’t Asher’s dad, I’d have cut him out completely. Everything I do is for my kid. I’ve wanted to pack up and run so many times, but Asher loves his dad too much. I can't do that to him.

I want to move far away, but it would break Asher's heart. Declan thinks Asher loves me more, but he has no clue the kid worships him just as much.

I spot an ice cream parlor and pull over. The front lot is packed, so I whip around to the back. I need a serious hit of dopamine. Some sugar to clear my head.

I walk into the cute, cozy little shop and order a massive bowl. Usually, I’m not picky with flavors, but when I’m stressed and need comfort? Plain vanilla, all the way.

My mind drifts back to Corbin. I’m not sure how to feel about my parents’ revenge tour. I’m not really a get-even person. I usually just let karma do the heavy lifting because she’s a real bitch when she wants to be.

I’m torn. Part of me wants to watch them crash and burn. The other part just wants to forget they ever existed. Does that make me a bad person? Wanting to see them suffer?

I finish the bowl and walk out, still totally conflicted. Maybe I should call my therapist. Get some professional perspective.

I head toward my car. I don't even make it to the door.

Suddenly, hands grab me from behind. A palm clamps over my mouth and nose before I can even gasp.

The world spins. Then, total darkness.
