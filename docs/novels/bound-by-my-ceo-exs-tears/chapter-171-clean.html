NovelVibe

Chapter 173

I’m staring at my baby daddy. The guy I actually trusted enough to let into my bed. My brain is totally frozen, just trying to process his face.

He looks different. I mean, yeah, he’s still drop-dead gorgeous, but he’s not the guy from my fuzzy memories. He grew a beard. Honestly? It makes him look even hotter.

Wait. I love Declan. Why the hell am I checking out Adrian? I’m not. Seriously. I’m just, like, making a mental note that he’s good-looking. That’s all.

"Hey," I finally choke out. Literally the only word my brain can manage.

God, this is awkward. I have zero clue what to do or say.

His eyes shift from me down to our little girl. That blank, unreadable look on his face just melts. Suddenly, it’s pure, raw love.

"Can I... can I hold her?" He doesn't look up, his eyes glued to her.

He loves Aurora. You can just tell. That alone takes some of the weight off my chest. I was terrified he’d want nothing to do with her. Like, what if he saw her as some huge mistake? Makes sense to worry, considering he was using me and literally planning my murder.

"Yeah. Sure."

I stand up and gently transfer Aurora into his waiting arms.

Adrian presses a soft kiss right on her forehead. "Hey there, baby girl."

He tries to blink back the tears, but I see them. He’s not fooling anyone.

Reading this guy is impossible. Just like Declan, he keeps his guard way up. I don’t even know what I expected when I agreed to this visit. But warmth? Gentleness? Definitely not this.

After everything people told me he did, I expected some cold, heartless prick. But this? This is throwing me off. Piper did warn me, though. She said I was happy around him. Free. That he helped me grow up.

According to her, Adrian got me out of my shell. Helped me explore my wild side and get comfortable with my body. I didn't buy it at first, but looking at him now? I get it. I don't feel any sparks, but there’s this weird pull. Something about him just draws me in.

"Thanks for bringing her," he says, his voice all thick and raspy. "I haven't seen her since the day she was born."

"Wait, why? Didn't Edmund and Beatrice bring her by?"

"She was in the NICU, remember?" he says softly. "But they brought me photos."

Right. I keep forgetting Aurora came early. I always blank on the fact that while I was out cold in a coma, she was hooked up to machines in intensive care.

"I'll bring her by as much as I can," I promise.

We go quiet. He just plays with Aurora’s tiny fingers. She clamps her hand around his thumb, tight. It makes me wonder if, deep down, she knows. Like, does she feel the connection? Sounds crazy, but I swear she knows Adrian is her dad.

"Look, I'm glad you're here, but... why?" Adrian asks, still staring at her. "I'm shocked you came. Mom said you lost your memory."

Damn. That’s one pill I’ll never be able to swallow. The fact that he’s my parents' adopted son, and yet, here we are with a kid together.

"Besides letting you see Aurora, I wanted to meet you," I say. "Figure out who you are, since we're going to be co-parenting."

"Didn't think you'd want to," he mutters, his eyes never leaving the baby.

I let the comment slide. Silence stretches between us.

"I know what everyone else says about us," I tell him. "But I want to hear your version. Tell me our story."

His head snaps up. Surprise flashes in his eyes. Guess he figured I’d just take everyone else's word for it and shut him out.

He takes a slow, deep breath and just lays it all out.

No sugarcoating. He tells me everything—from the moment he found out I was alive, to drawing up the scheme, to pulling the trigger on the whole plan.

My chest aches hearing how he played me. I can’t even fathom the kind of heartbreak I must have felt when the betrayal hit. My lungs squeeze. It literally feels like a fist is wrapping around my heart and squeezing tight.

I don't remember the pain, but the scars are still on my soul. That’s why hearing him admit it hurts so bad.

I sink back into my chair, just watching him. By the time he finishes, Aurora is fast asleep, snuggled right against his chest.

"I never got to say it, but I am so damn sorry, Lyra," he whispers, his voice cracking. "There’s no excuse. I had this twisted idea that the company belonged to me, not you. I’ll regret it for the rest of my life."

You can see the raw truth in his eyes. I don't know how, but I just know he means it. What he did was totally screwed up, but how can I hold a grudge when he’s this broken up about it?

"I... I don't even know what to say."

And I don't. I'm completely blank and totally confused.

"I'm not defending myself," he says. "But everything flipped the second I fell for you. I thought I had the whole game figured out, but you ruined my plans. I never saw you coming, Lyra. You completely wrecked my world."

Holy shit. What are you supposed to say when a guy you don't even remember tells you he’s in love with you?

Thank God I don't have to answer. A guard cuts in.

"Time's up."

I watch in silence as he kisses Aurora’s head one last time and hands her back to me.

"Look, I don't remember the past," I say, trying to sound hopeful. "But maybe we can move past it. For Aurora. You seem like a decent guy, despite what you did, and you deserve to be in her life. So... friends?"

He looks stunned for a second. Then, a slow smile spreads across his face.

"Friends."

It’s a lot to process—all of this history and whatever it is I'm feeling right now. But it feels like a step in the right direction.

"I'll bring her back as often as I can," I tell him.

"I'd really like that."

He stands up, and the guard slaps the cuffs back on his wrists. They start to walk out, but Adrian stops and turns back to me.

"Watch your back, Lyra. Things aren't what they look like. Declan isn't the golden boy he plays." He holds my gaze. "Take care, love."

Before I can even open my mouth, they’re gone. I stand there holding my daughter, heading for the exit, his warning about Declan looping over and over in my head.
