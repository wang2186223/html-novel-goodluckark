Chapter 251

Silas. I still remember day one. He was the new scholarship kid, and I was the girl running the welcome committee. Duh. I mean, I was the obvious choice. Who wouldn't want me as their personal tour guide on their first day? Not to toot my own horn, but I knew my stats. Head cheerleader, straight-A student, loaded, and hot. Plus, I played the "relatable girl" card perfectly, so basically everyone loved me.

Sure, Lyra and her little clique hated my guts. Pure jealousy, obviously. I had Declan, and they couldn't touch him. Every girl in school drooled over him, just like every guy—minus Corbin and Orion—drooled over me. We were the ultimate power couple. We weren't official yet when Silas showed up, but please. It was a done deal. Just a matter of time.

Anyway, I went to the principal’s office to grab Silas. My first thought? Hard pass. Definitely not my type. The guy was a total geek—no offense to geeks, but seriously. Zero fashion sense. He was so scrawny his pants kept sliding down, and he had to keep yanking them up. Massive, ugly round glasses, face full of acne. The only saving grace was that he actually showered, unlike the other nerds.

Right on cue, he took one look at me and fell hard. Classic. I didn't sweat it at first. I was used to guys drooling, and I figured he’d get over it. Nope. He didn't. He turned into a total pest, right up there with Lyra.

Sometimes I think, what if he and Lyra had hooked up instead? Would they still be a thing? Would Declan and I have made it? Would we be happy? But I have to shut that down. It's useless. Declan was right—if you regret the past, you regret the kids. No Asher, no Jasper. I can't go there.

Jasper. God, my chest literally hurts just saying his name. I press my hand against my ribs, trying to stop the ache. I’m a trash mom. The absolute worst. I didn't even hold him when he popped out. Didn't even look at him. I hate myself so much for that. I treated my own baby like an annoying obstacle, like he was just standing in the way of me and Declan.

I was so damn dumb and selfish, and now karma is collecting the bill. I should've been like Declan. He stepped up. Asher wasn't planned, but Declan loved him anyway. He became an amazing dad. Sure, he hated Lyra back then, but he never took it out on the kid. He didn't treat Asher like garbage the way I treated Jasper. I was a monster. I'm finally admitting it.

"Hey, you okay?" Clara’s voice snaps me back.

Her face is tight with worry. Everyone’s freaking out over me, but honestly? They shouldn't bother. I’m just getting what I deserve. I was a total bitch. Clara literally packed her bags and flew out here on a two-week leave because she couldn't stand being miles away while I was spiraling. "Yeah," I mumble.

"Cut the crap, Aurora. I know you too well. Let's try that again."

She’s right. Nobody gets me like Clara. Not even my mom. She knows exactly what makes me tick, how I blow up, how I hide my pain. She knows me better than she knows herself, and I know her the same way.

"Honestly? I have no clue," I choke out. "I don't know how I'm holding up. I don't know if I'm okay, or if I'll ever be okay again. I just... I don't know. I'm totally numb. I'm lost. I feel like I'm drowning."

The whole world is caving in on me. How do I explain that the guilt is literally eating me alive? How do I tell her that regret follows me like a shadow? How do I say that I'm slipping away a little more every single day?

I was so awful to Silas and Jasper. How do I even bounce back from that? How do I apologize for treating them like absolute garbage? I dragged them through hell just because I was a selfish brat who only cared about herself. I collapse onto the mattress, completely exhausted, and just let the tears stream down my face.

I used to think Lyra was the bad guy. The monster who wrecked me without a second thought. I swore I’d never be like her. But guess what? I’m worse. I broke Silas. I broke Jasper. Over and over, and I didn't give a shit. I saw the hurt in their eyes every time I walked away, and I just ignored it. I pretended they didn't even exist. I’m way worse than Lyra. She was drunk; I was completely sober. I knew exactly how much I was hurting them, and I kept doing it anyway.

"I'm a monster, Clara. Seriously, give me the crown for the biggest bitch on earth," I say, clawing at my chest. God, it hurts so bad.

Lyra was right about me. I hit rock bottom. I used to be the girl everyone wanted to be, and now look at me. How did I screw up this badly?

"What do I do?" I sob, choking on the words. "How do I fix this?"

That's the million-dollar question. When you ruin everything this completely, how do you even start to fix it? How do you get your soul back?
