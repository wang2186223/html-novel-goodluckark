Chapter 158

"Look, I have no idea what you're on about. She passed out in the restroom, okay? I just called her folks." Aurora tries to play it cool, but her voice is shaking like a leaf.

"Oh, really?"

"Yeah! She went down. I couldn't just leave her on the floor, so I rang them."

I've known this girl forever. People don't change that much. I can spot her bullshit from a mile away.

"Then explain the gash on the back of her skull."

She claws at my fingers, trying to pry them off her jaw. I squeeze harder. Yeah, it hurts. Do I care? Not a single damn. She touched my girl.

"I swear, I don't know! She was just lying there!" She's scrambling for an excuse, but her eyes are screaming guilty.

"What were you even doing at that restaurant?" I need the whole story. Was this a setup, or did she just snap?

"I—I was having lunch with Ingrid," she stammers. "We had no clue Lyra and her family were gonna be there." She sucks at lying. Seriously.

Ingrid. That name clicks everything into place. That bitch despises Lyra too. Now the puzzle pieces are coming together.

"Last chance. Spill the truth right now, or I swear to God, you're gonna regret breathing," I growl. My fuse is completely gone.

"I don't know anything, I swear!"

"Cut the crap and talk, Aurora!" I roar. The walls practically shake.

Corbin steps in, trying to shield her. "Hey, man, maybe she's telling the truth."

"Shut the hell up, Corbin. Nobody asked you."

I dig my fingers deeper into her face. I don't hit women, but damn, I'm right on the edge. Tears start spilling down her cheeks. I feel absolutely nothing. Whatever I used to feel for her is dead. Ashes.

"It was an accident!" she wails, breaking down. "I didn't mean to do it!"

"Aurora, what did you do?" Corbin's voice cracks with panic. He knows my rules. You don't mess with my people. Ever.

"I pushed her... okay? I don't even know what she said, but I just lost it and shoved her. She went flying back and hit her head on the wall."

I drop her face like she's toxic waste. The sudden release sends her stumbling back, barely keeping her feet.

"See? Was that so hard?" I flash a nasty smirk.

"Please, it was an accident!"

I'm done with her excuses. Before she can blink, I fist my hand in her hair and yank. She screeches, but the sound doesn't even touch the rage boiling inside me. I want blood.

Corbin lunges to pull her away. No chance. I plant a hand on his chest and launch him backward onto the sofa.

"Listen up, and listen close, because I'm only saying this once. One: I don't love you, Aurora. You disgust me. Get it through your thick skull—whatever we had is dead. The girl who actually owns my heart is the one you put in the hospital today. And instead of owning up to it, you acted like a total coward."

She opens her mouth to whine, but I cut her off. I let go of her hair and wrap my hand around her throat. She's sobbing, completely shattered by my words. Good.

"Two: you messed up big time touching Lyra. Accident or not, you're going to pay for this. Three: stay the hell away from her. If she walks into a room, you leave. If she's walking down the street, you cross it. I don't care if you have to dive under a bus to avoid her—just don't let me catch you anywhere near her. Got it?"

"Y-yes," she chokes out, nodding frantically, tears streaming down her face.

I let go. She collapses, sucking in air like a dying fish. I turn my back on her and head for the door.

"Is... is that it?" Corbin asks, his voice shaking. "Are we good?"

I let out a cold, dry laugh. "Not even close." I shoot Aurora one last look of pure disgust, then turn to Corbin. "You should've warned her. Now she's gonna find out exactly what happens when you cross me."

I walk out, slamming the door, and pull out my phone to make a call. If Aurora has half a brain, she'll stay far away from my life. Because if she doesn't, I will absolutely ruin her.
