NovelVibe

Chapter 428

My brain is a screaming match. The same damn questions keep looping while I pack up my hospital crap.

Do I keep this kid? Or do I flush it?

Sure, nobody has to know. But *I* would. And the guilt? Oh, it’d rip me to shreds, knowing I killed a tiny thing that’s literally half of me. This isn't just Asher's mess. It's my baby, too.

I beat the living hell out of a pillow. God, I’m so wound up I can barely breathe.

It’s a sickness, really. I can’t leave a room messy. Doesn't matter if the hospital pays people to scrub the place down. I physically cannot stop myself. I smooth out the wrinkles, fold the scratchy blanket, and line up the pillows. It has to be perfect.

"Whoa. What did that pillow ever do to you?"

I jump, my heart slamming straight into my throat. Benji.

I was so deep in my own head I didn't even hear the door click.

"Nothing," I croak. I slide back onto the mattress, totally drained.

Fuck. Seriously, what the hell am I supposed to do now?

Benji sits on the edge of the bed. "You look like trash."

*No shit, Sherlock.*

"Yeah, well, I feel like trash," I mutter. I'm too exhausted to play nice.

"Is it the test results?"

I just stare at him.

Look, I love Benji. I do. But why do guys always ask the most brain-dead questions when your life is imploding? Like, duh. Of course it's about the results.

I almost snap at him, but I swallow the venom. Not his fault. None of this shit is.

"You don't have to dance around it," I say, my voice barely a squeak. "Just say the word. Pregnancy."

He fidgets, looking super uncomfortable. "Right. Uh... are you freaked out about being pregnant?"

"Terrified."

He nods, totally out of his depth. I get it. What do you even say to a girl whose entire life just got hit by a tornado?

We just sit there. The silence is heavy enough to suffocate us, both of us drowning in our own thoughts.

"Hey, Benji?" I mumble.

"Yeah?"

"Can we keep this between us? Like, strictly hush-hush for now?"

It’s not that he’s a blabbermouth. I just can't have the office gossiping before I even figure out my next move. I need a minute. Just some goddamn time to think before everyone starts hounding me.

"Say no more," he says, giving my hand a quick squeeze. "I've got your back. Ready to get out of here?"

"Yeah. But... my car is still parked at the office."

He stands up, stretching. "Easy fix. I'll drop you home. You can grab an Uber tomorrow morning. Assuming you're even going to work."

Honestly? Work can go screw itself. Maybe I'll call in sick. Maybe I'll just vanish. I just need to clear my head before I make a choice.

"Okay. Let me go pay the bill, then I'll meet you out front."

He nods and heads out, leaving me alone with the quiet.

Thank God Benji grabbed my backpack before dragging me to the ER. I throw it over my shoulder, take one last look at the depressing room, and walk out.

I float toward the nurse's desk like a zombie. Sign here. Swipe card. Grab receipt. It feels like I'm watching someone else do it. Like I'm totally detached.

You ever feel like that? Like you're living in a dream? You're awake, your eyes are open, but nothing is registering. Just totally numb and empty.

A car horn blares, snapping me back to reality. Oh. I'm outside.

I hustle over, almost eating pavement, and slide into Benji's passenger seat.

He gets it. He doesn't say a word the whole ride, giving me the quiet I need. He knows the way to my place anyway, so I just stare out the window.

"We're here," he says softly.

I blink. Sure enough, we're idling right outside my building.

"Thanks, Benji." I grab the door handle. "Seriously. I owe you big time."

"Anytime."

I get out, wave as his taillights disappear, and face my front door.

The second I unlock it, Shadow starts meowing her head off. Usually, her little furry face makes me feel better. Not today. Her cries just add to the noise in my head.

I flop onto the couch, staring at the walls.

This place used to be my safe zone. My escape. Now? It feels totally alien, and the panic starts creeping up my throat.

Suddenly, the room starts morphing in my mind.

A crib tucked in the corner. A baby blanket draped over the sofa. Toys scattered on the rug. A playpen blocking the window. The vision is so vivid it makes my stomach turn.

It's too much. I can't breathe.

The tears hit me fast, hot and heavy. I wipe my face, pissed off at myself, and grab my phone. My fingers are shaking so bad I can barely punch in her number.

"Please tell me you're calling for outfit advice for tonight," Clara's voice pops through the speaker.

"No," I whisper, trying not to sob. "I have to cancel."

She groans loudly. "Ugh! Seriously? That is the fifth date you've blown off!"

Ever since that night with Asher, Clara made it her personal mission to hook me up with every hot, rich bachelor in the city. Her grand plan? Distract me until I forget him.

It's sweet, sure, but I don't want to date. Not now. Probably not ever.

"Something came up," I say.

"You always say that," she sighs.

"I'm actually telling the truth this time."

She gasps. "Wait, so you *were* lying before?!"

"Clara, that's not the point—"

"It totally is!" she cuts in. "I am literally handing you the absolute best guys in the business world, and you just dump them!"

To Clara, the perfect guy is sweet, polite, insanely successful, and filthy rich. She thinks I deserve the world, and to her, that means a guy with a massive bank account.

"Can you just come over after work?" My voice cracks, the weight of the secret finally crushing me.

Silence on the line. "Are you okay?"

"I just need you, Clara."

No more teasing. No more questions.

"I'm on my way," she says, and the line goes dead.

I slowly lower the phone, staring at the dark screen. And despite the absolute shitshow my life is right now, I actually smile.

Because that's the thing about my best friend. She shows up. No questions asked. And I'd do the exact same for her.
