Chapter 252

Orion.

One week. That’s how long it’s been since Willow crashed back into my life. Years of zero contact, and suddenly here we are. I never planned on tracking her down. Guess life loves a sick joke.

Our divorce? Best day of my life. I wanted her out, grabbed the pen, and signed the papers without blinking. Good riddance. I didn't give a crap where she went or who she slept with. She was dead to me the second she walked out my door. Total radio silence in my brain. Until the board of directors started breathing down my neck, that is.

My knuckles turn white just thinking about the hoops those old bastards made me jump through. Look, I don’t need their cash. My own side businesses pull in plenty. But Wood Corp? That’s family blood. You don't just walk away from the empire your great-grandfather built. It’s a pride thing. A legacy.

The board played dirty. They knew my weak spot. They knew I’d burn the world down before letting them strip my title, so they squeezed. And yeah, I bent. I did what I had to do.

Cut to right now. I’m leaning back, watching my driver hoist suitcases into the trunk. Willow and Clara Sterling are standing right there. My eyes lock onto Willow. Damn, she changed. No point denying it.

The frumpy, awkward kid who couldn't pick an outfit to save her life is gone. Her hair flows way past her shoulders now. And her body? She used to be skin and bones, but now she’s packed with dangerous curves. The kind that make a guy lose his mind, especially when she takes a step and those hips start swaying.

Shit. I despise her, sure, but I’ve got eyes. And right now, the view she’s giving me is pure, unfiltered trouble.

She’s gorgeous. My brain registers it instantly. Talk about an ugly duckling turning into a total knockout.

She feels me watching. Her head snaps up, and those pitch-black eyes lock onto mine, burning with pure hate. I don’t look away. I just flash a slow, cocky smirk. Yeah, I’m checking you out. Deal with it.

"All loaded up, boss," the driver says, cutting through the tension. I finally break eye contact with my ex—well, my future wife.

"Let's move," I say, checking my watch. "I'm already a week behind on work." I stand back, letting Clara Sterling and Willow climb into the backseat first. I slide in after them, Jared hits the gas, and we merge into traffic.

"Mom, seriously, who is this guy?" Clara Sterling asks, shattering the quiet. "Where are we even going? For how long? And uh, why does he look so familiar?"

I glance at the kid. My daughter. The word tastes weird in my mouth. It’s crazy. I have a kid, and Willow kept her a secret all this time.

I’m not a kid guy. Never wanted the white-picket-fence life, the wife, the screaming toddlers. Sure, Asher, Aurora, and Jasper—who basically lives at Declan’s—are fine, but other people's kids? Pass. Never wanted them, never planned for them.

But now Clara Sterling is sitting right here, and I’m clueless. I can play the cool uncle, sure. But a dad? No way in hell. Call me a monster, but I only brought the kid into this to force Willow's hand. I needed that marriage license signed, and Clara was my leverage.

"He’s... well, he is..." Willow stammers, her forehead creasing as she scrambles for a lie.

Watching her squirm brings a grin to my face. I let out a soft laugh. Making her sweat is my new favorite hobby. I’m still pissed about how she trapped me in our first marriage years ago.

"I'm an old friend of your mom's," I chime in, throwing Willow a lifeline since she’s choking on her words. "We go way back."

Why lie? I could've just blurted out the truth. 'Hey kid, I'm your dad.' But I kept my mouth shut. It wasn't to protect the feelings of whatever guy she thinks is her father, either. I just didn't want to unpack that whole mess right now.

"Right. So where are we heading?" Clara asks, raising an eyebrow. "We packed half our lives, so we're obviously staying there a while. Plus, my friends texted me. They said you told my teacher I’m out for the rest of the semester."

Smart kid. But hey, she’s got my DNA. It’s not bragging if it’s true. Of course she’s sharp.

"We'll talk about it when we land," Willow says, dodging the bullet.

Clara just glares at her. Those icy grey eyes cut right through her mother. I bite back a laugh. Damn, she’s a Wood. No doubt about it. She’s got the family stare. Asher used to freak people out by staring down grown men, and now my own kid is doing the exact same thing. It’s wild.

"We're here, sir," Jared announces, pulling the door open.

I hadn't even noticed the car stopping at the tarmac. I clear my throat and step out. Willow and Clara follow me, while Jared grabs the bags and heads toward my private jet. Time to fly home and drop the bomb that I have a secret kid. Yeah, this week is going to be absolute hell.
