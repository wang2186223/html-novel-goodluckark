Chapter 12

I watch the cop drag Lyra away. His hand is wrapped around hers. Why the hell is he touching her? Seriously, is the hand-holding necessary?

It pisses me off. Seeing them together like that makes my blood boil, and I don't even know why. I hate whatever vibe they have going on.

Something soft touches my hand. I look down. My fingers are balled into a tight fist.

"You good?" Aurora asks.

I look at her. Her face is perfect. It pulls me right back to reality.

*She's the one,* I tell myself. *The one I've always wanted.* I force Lyra out of my head.

I don't want Lyra. So why do I care if some guy wants her? I shouldn't.

"Yeah, I'm fine," I say, flashing her a smile.

She smiles back. It hits me just like it did the first time. It hooks me, dragging me back to the old days when we were good.

A few minutes later, Lyra walks back with the cop. She's laughing. Some joke he made. She looks totally relaxed around him. Easy.

My chest tightens. That anger I just got rid of? It doubles. Dammit. What is wrong with me?

I never gave a shit about her before. So why do I want to beat this Adrian guy to a pulp?

"Not even gonna say hi?" Corbin asks.

He sets his gun down and pulls off his ear protection.

Orion does the same, stepping up next to me. We're twins, but we don't look it. Side by side, you wouldn't even guess we shared DNA.

Lyra glares at her brother. The happy look she had a second ago vanishes.

"Nope. No point," she says.

"What do you mean 'no point'?" Corbin's voice hardens. He's getting annoyed. "We're family."

"Since when?" Lyra snaps. "Last I checked, you only had one sister. Declan only had one girl. And Orion? He never wanted me around. Since the girl you all actually like is standing right there, I'll pass on the small talk."

Her face is a blank slate. Cold.

We all just stand there, shifting our weight, looking like idiots. She's right. We treated her like trash, even when she tried.

"Don't be a bitch," Aurora snaps. "Your brother's just trying to check on you."

Lyra lets out a sharp, nasty laugh. I've never heard her sound like that.

"Thanks, Aurora. Honestly, you guys always thought I was a bitch anyway. Might as well play the part. It's actually pretty freeing."

Aurora starts to argue, but Lyra cuts her off.

"Now, excuse me. I'm gonna get out of here before your drama scares Adrian off."

She grabs Adrian's hand and walks away. Doesn't even look back.

So his name is Adrian. I'm running a background check on him. Something about the guy rubs me the wrong way.

They head to a shooting lane. Adrian starts showing her the ropes, probably the same drill we just did. I force myself to look away.

"What the hell was that?" Corbin asks, totally lost.

Orion smirks. "Looks like Lyra finally grew a spine."

He's loving this. The rest of us? Not so much.

"Let's just finish up and leave," Aurora says, sounding pissed.

She throws a dirty look at Lyra, grabs my hand, and spins me around.

Orion and I already have our carry permits. I only came to keep Aurora company. Orion came because he wanted to shoot some targets.

After a minute, I give up. I can't focus.

Fuck. I should be looking at Aurora. But my eyes keep drifting back to my ex-wife. I can't stop watching her and that bastard.

And I'm noticing everything. His hands on her hips, adjusting her stance. How close his chest is to her back. His lips brushing her ear as he whispers something.

"Ro?"

"What?" I snap at my brother.

This is new. Lyra has never gotten under my skin like this. I don't get it.

"You're losing it," Orion says quietly. "What's the issue?"

"Nothing. Why would anything be wrong?"

Dammit. Now he's sliding his hand down her arm. It looks like a stroke. And she's giggling. What the fuck is so funny?

"Maybe because you're burning a hole through your ex and her savior," Orion says.

"He's not her fucking savior."

"He is. He saved her life, man. That makes him a hero to her."

Lyra looks up at the cop. It's a look I've never seen on her face, and I hate it.

"Shut up, Orion," I growl.

He chuckles. He thinks this is a joke.

"Seriously, get it together," Orion says. "You're here with Aurora. Stop staring at Lyra. You wanted Aurora, remember? And she knows you're checked out."

That hits me. I look at Aurora. She's sitting down, staring at her lap, looking miserable. Shit. Orion's right. Aurora doesn't deserve this. We're trying to start over, and here I am, obsessed with my ex who has clearly moved on.

I put my gun down and sit next to her.

"Hey. Sorry, babe. My head's just all over the place today."

It's the truth. Lyra has me completely messed up today.

She takes my hand and kisses my cheek. "It's okay. You guys were married for nine years. It makes sense you'd watch out for her. We don't know this Adrian guy. He might be a cop, but he could be trouble."

I let out a breath and nod. She's right. I'm just looking out for her. She's Asher's mom. That's all this is.

Thirty minutes later, Lyra heads for the exit. For once, the cop isn't glued to her side. I stand up and follow her.

I corner her in the restroom, locking the door behind me.

"What the fuck, Declan?" she yells, spinning around.

I walk toward her, slow and steady.

"What the hell was that out there?" I growl.

"I have no idea what you're talking about," she says, her brow furrowing.

I'm not playing her games. I just want to say my piece and get back to Aurora.

"Don't play dumb. What's going on with you and the douchebag?"

She smiles, but her eyes are ice. "Oh, him? None of your business. And his name is Adrian."

"I don't care what his name is. What are you doing with him?"

Why is she being so difficult? She used to be so easy to handle.

A nasty spark lights up her eyes. I know I'm going to hate whatever comes out of her mouth next.

"Well, they say the best way to get over someone is to get under someone else. So, that's what I'm doing."

Something snaps. I see red. I lunge forward, pinning her against the wall, trapping her with my arms.

"You're sleeping with him?" I growl, furious at the thought of another guy touching her.

"It's none of your business who I'm fucking, Declan. We're divorced."

She's right, but I don't care. I want to wipe his touch off her skin. My hands move on their own, grabbing her hips. They fit perfectly.

Her breath hitches. She feels it too. I lean in close, so close I can taste her breath. I forget where we are. I forget who's outside.

Suddenly, she shoves me back. Hard.

"What are you doing, Declan?" she whispers, eyes welling up. "You don't get to do this. I won't let you. Aurora is right out there. You want to kiss someone? Go kiss her. Stop playing with me."

She slips past me and walks out, slamming the door.

I take a deep breath, running a hand through my hair.

What the hell did I just do? What was I thinking?

"Fuck!" I yell, punching the wall right where she was standing.

She's right. I have Aurora now. Why am I trying to ruin it by kissing her sister?

Aurora is the one I wanted. So why can't I get my ex-wife out of my head?
