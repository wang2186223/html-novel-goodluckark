Chapter 426

Whatever. I was done. Let her feed her own delusions if it helped her sleep at night. I literally couldn't care less.

"Yo, Jocely!" Benji snapped, his voice dripping with pure rage.

"Hey, it's f—"

The words died in my throat. My gut did a free fall. The entire room spun on a crazy axis, and then—bam. Lights out.

That harsh, chemical bleach smell dragged me back to reality. I blinked, but the overhead glare burned my retinas, forcing my eyelids shut.

I tried again, squinting through the haze. Great. A hospital room.

I bolted upright, a move I instantly regretted as a wave of pure sickness hit me like a truck. I scrambled out of bed, lunging for the bathroom. I barely got my head over the bowl before my stomach turned inside out. I dry-heaved, shaking like a leaf, puking until I was running on empty.

Finally, I slid down onto the cold floor tiles, gasping for air.

Seriously, what is going on with my body? This stomach bug had been torturing me for a solid week. Mom’s special herbal tea did jack shit. Even the heavy-duty drugstore pills didn't do a damn thing to stop the churning.

Did I eat something bad? I totally called it when Clara Sterling dragged me to that sketchy-looking diner last week. She swore up and down it was some "undiscovered treasure."

Yeah, right. Some treasure. Now my gut is paying the price for my stupidity.

"Piper?"

Benji's voice drifted in. I forced my eyes open.

"In the bathroom," I wheezed, hitting the flusher.

He poked his head through the door, his forehead creased with worry. "Uh, why are you chilling on the floor?"

"Just threw up my soul," I mumbled. "Zero energy to move."

A soft grin broke on his face. He knelt down. "Come here, let me grab you."

He scooped me up like a feather and carried me back to the hospital bed.

"How did I get here? Did you bring me?" I asked.

"What do you actually remember?" he countered.

I squeezed my eyes shut, digging through my brain. "Jocely being a total drama queen."

"Yeah, well, you passed out cold, so I hauled you over here," he said softly. "The docs did some bloodwork. We've just been hanging out, waiting for you to come to."

"Thanks, Benji," I said, letting him tuck me in. "Seriously. For the bathroom rescue, the ride, and, like, actually staying with me."

He let out a soft laugh, giving my hand a quick squeeze. "Duh. I got you, always."

Okay, time-out. Before you guys start shipping us, let's set the record straight. Benji is strictly a platonic bestie. We've been close since he started on my team last year. Plus, well... he's gay. So yeah, there's zero romantic vibe here.

I took a deep, shaky breath, trying to settle my rebellious stomach.

I was just about to sink into the pillows when the door clicked open. A middle-aged guy in a white lab coat stepped inside.

"Hey there, Piper. I'm Dr. Elias," he said with a warm smile. "Glad to see you back with us."

I forced a tiny smirk. "Bet you use that line on everyone."

That got a laugh out of him and Benji. The heavy tension in the room instantly evaporated.

"Alright, so your lab results are back," he said, his voice turning serious.

"Is it bad?" I asked, chewing on my bottom lip. "I swear, if this is because of Clara's stupid 'hidden gem' diner..."

He chuckled. "Well, unless Clara Sterling is secretly a dude, she's definitely off the hook for this one."

I froze, totally lost. I looked at the doctor, then at Benji, my mind blanking.

"Wait, what? Isn't it just a stomach bug? Didn't the tests say I got food poisoning?"

"Oh, the test was positive, alright," the doctor said, a tiny smirk playing on his lips. "Just not for bad food."

My pulse spiked. A cold shiver ran straight down my spine.

"Then... what's wrong with me? Am I sick?" I whispered, panic creeping in.

He stepped closer, his expression softening. "Hey, relax. You're perfectly fine. Healthy as a horse."

I blinked, totally confused.

"You've just got a bun baking in the oven."
