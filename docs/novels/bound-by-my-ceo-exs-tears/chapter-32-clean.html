NovelVibe

Chapter 32

Today was actually a win. The dinner date with Adrian went great, I had work tomorrow, and my birthday was right around the corner. Just like I figured on Saturday, Adrian made the dark clouds vanish. Five minutes at his place and I was already cracking up.

The guy could seriously cook. The food was unreal. Honestly, a man who can whip up a gourmet meal and make you laugh is a cheat code. We had a blast. It got even better when I got home and called Asher. He’d cooled off. We rambled about random crap until he drifted off, still on the line. Best part of my day, hands down.

I was in the middle of baking. I needed some serious comfort food, so chocolate cake and cookies it was. I was wiping my hands on a dish towel when the knock came. I opened the door. Half of me gasped; the other half totally expected it. Aurora.

"What do you want, Aurora?" I snapped, not hiding my annoyance. Her showing up was always bad news. Last time we threw down, I almost ended up in a body bag a few hours later.

Her face was pure stone. "We need to talk."

Every cell in my body screamed *hell no*. But against my better judgment, I stepped back and let her into the living room.

"Say your piece and get out," I said, dropping into a chair. I watched her like a hawk as she sat down.

She scanned the room, her nose upturned. Then she sneered. I rolled my eyes. Of course she hated it. No gold trim, no designer labels. Just a cozy, normal home.

"You gonna keep making faces at my walls, or are you actually going to speak?" I leaned back, studying her. Aurora wouldn't know a home if it hit her. Mine had soul. Hers was just a massive, sterile icebox.

She crossed her legs. "Stay away from Declan."

I let out a massive sigh. "Are you kidding me? You drove all the way here for this garbage again?" I was so sick of playing this broken record. How many times did I have to say I didn't want him? The guy put me through hell. Why would I ever want a repeat?

"It's not garbage," she hissed. "He wants a future with me, but you keep pulling him back. Just let him go. He doesn't love you, Lyra. He never did."

Ouch. That last part stung, even if I didn't want it to. Nobody wants a reminder that they wasted years loving a man who despised them, whose heart was a locked vault.

"And how exactly am I pulling him back? Huh?"

"The attack two weeks ago," she spat. "He ditched our movie night and ran straight here because he heard you got hurt."

I shook my head. This girl was supposed to be a top-tier attorney, but she was acting dumber than dirt.

"How is that on me, Aurora? He's your guy. Put a leash on him," I snapped. Why were we even doing this? She won. She got the prize. I just wanted to move on. Adrian was great, and I wanted to see where things went without this toxic drama dragging us down.

"Please. You're just playing the victim to get his attention," she mocked, wearing this smug, punchable grin.

"Get out." I stood up, pointing a hard finger at the door. I was done. No way was I letting her ruin my good mood.

She stood up too, eyes flashing. "What, mad because I called you out? It's only a matter of time before everyone realizes these 'attacks' are totally staged."

"Are you done showing off your low IQ?" I asked. "You think I'm faking? Let me tell you what I think. I think *you're* the one behind them. You're my only enemy. Who benefits if I end up dead? You. You get Declan all to yourself, and you get full custody of my kid."

She froze, jaw dropping. Maybe she was shocked I connected the dots, or maybe she just couldn't believe I had the balls to say it. But the more I thought about it, the more it clicked. Aurora wanted me gone. Either for payback, or because she was pathetic enough to feel threatened by me.

"How dare you!" she shrieked.

"No, how dare *you* bring your bullshit into my house. We aren't kids anymore, Aurora. Grow the hell up. Now get out. I'm done."

She was vibrating with rage. If looks could kill, I'd be six feet under. I didn't flinch. I wasn't letting her walk all over me anymore.

I tapped my foot. "You walking out, or do I have to throw you out?"

She clicked her tongue, turning toward the door. I breathed a sigh of relief. Too soon. She spun back around.

"One more thing. Keep your little brat out of my way," she snarled.

My blood ran cold. "What did you just say?" My voice went dangerously quiet. Surely she didn't just talk trash about Asher.

"I'm not letting him ruin my relationship just because he throws tantrums. He's a spoiled brat. Once Declan and I are married, I'm shipping him off to boarding school. He's a kid; he doesn't get a say. You better muzzle him, or I swear you'll only see him twice a year."

*Smack.*

The sound cracked through the room. Aurora stumbled back, her hand flying to her cheek, her eyes wide and welling with tears.

"Did you... did you just slap me?"

My palm stung like hell, but I didn't care. "Yeah. And if you don't get the fuck out of my house right now—"

"You'll pay for this!" she screamed. She spun on her heel, stomped out, and slammed the front door so hard the walls shook.

How could Declan want a monster like her around our son? The venom she just spewed about Asher was sickening. What happened to her? She used to be cold, sure, but never this evil.

I slid down the wall, trying to breathe. My perfect day was officially trashed. The happiness was completely gone.

The oven timer buzzed. I pulled the cookies out, but the thought of eating them made me sick. I left them on the counter and turned on the TV, hoping to drown out my thoughts.

An hour passed. I hadn't processed a single frame of the movie. I shut the TV off, deciding a hot bath might help.

I was halfway up the stairs when the doorbell started buzzing like crazy. I groaned, thinking it was some neighborhood kid selling stuff. I went back down and opened it.

Declan stood there, looking like he wanted to murder someone. Before I could even speak, he shoved past me, slammed the door, and pushed me back into the hallway near the kitchen.

"What the hell is wrong with you?" he roared. The sheer rage in his voice made me shudder.

"What?"

"You think Aurora wouldn't tell me? I can literally see your handprint on her face!"

He started pacing, furious. The pieces clicked. That's why he was here.

"Declan, you don't understand—" I started, but he cut me off.

"Understand what? That you hit her for no reason? That you're accusing her of trying to kill you with zero proof? Or should I understand the disgusting lies you told her?" He stepped closer, his eyes burning holes through me.

I had no idea what story she spun, but she definitely left out her own nasty comments.

"I won't let you touch Aurora, Lyra. You lay one finger on her again, and I swear..." his voice dropped to a deadly whisper.

I had never seen him like this. For the first time, I was actually terrified of him.

"Declan, it wasn't like that. She—"

"She what? Told you the truth? That I never loved you? She's right. You're nothing, Lyra. You'll always be nothing. You told her we slept together like it was some victory, but I only used you because you were there. You were convenient." His eyes glinted with pure malice.

"Please, stop," I whispered, tears blurring my vision. But he didn't care.

"You weren't even good at it. Every time we were together, I was picturing Aurora. I wanted her. Every time I touched you, I saw her face. You were just an easy distraction. A total joke."

*Smack.*

For the second time today, my hand connected with a face. Declan’s head snapped to the side. Tears streamed down my cheeks, his words cutting through me like glass.

"Get out, Declan. Get out and never come back. Take your security, take everything. I don't want your help, and I don't want you near me. You're dead to me." The fight drained out of me, leaving me hollow.

I turned and started up the stairs. I paused, not looking back.

"You have two minutes to get out before I call the cops."

I ran to my room and locked the door. A moment later, I heard the front door slam. I collapsed onto the floor and completely broke down.

Declan had destroyed me. No weapons, just words. He shattered my soul into dust.

I sobbed, the tears coming in waves, my chest so tight I could barely breathe. He broke me over a liar's words.

But I would get up. I would move on. And karma would find him and my pathetic sister. I would never, ever forgive him for today.
