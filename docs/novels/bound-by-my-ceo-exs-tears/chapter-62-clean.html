Chapter 63

"I love her, Adrian."

Man, when I started this whole mess, falling for her wasn't on the menu. Talk about a massive, backfiring joke.

I figured it’d be a breeze. Just end her, grab the cash, and walk away. Simple. But damn, it turned out to be the hardest thing I’ve ever had to do.

Lyra isn’t some girl you just brush off. You don't just look past her. She’s the kind of woman who gets under your skin. The kind who makes you want to stop being a piece of shit.

I felt it happening. Tried to slam on the brakes, but no way. It was like watching a head-on collision in slow motion. You just brace for impact.

By the time I woke up to my feelings, the damage was done. The clock was ticking, and the truth was bound to blow up in my face. But I didn't back off. I didn't let go. I just clung to her, greedy for whatever seconds we had left.

Hurting her? That’s gonna eat at me forever. Her pain literally ripped through me. Every time her heart cracked, mine did too. I threw away our shot at a life together because I wanted money. If she hates me forever, yeah, I get it.

"Yo, Adrian. You got a visitor," the guard barks.

I’ve been dodging my parents every time they show up. I’m too fucking ashamed to look them in the eye. They took me in, gave me their name, loved me like their own—and I repaid them by trying to put a bullet in their actual daughter.

I don’t get why they’re even here. Why they still care. If the tables were turned, I’d have written myself off.

"Adrian!" the guard snaps again. I glare back, cold.

For a second, I’m about to tell him to send them away. Tell them I’m out. But the words get stuck in my throat.

I just nod. He points at my wrists. I hold them out, the cuffs click shut, and he hauls me out of the cell.

I’m the new guy in this block, but nobody messes with me. They know better. I’m a mean bastard, and I can hold my own.

Then I step into the visiting room and freeze. Holy shit.

Sitting in the far corner is the last person on earth I expected to see.

My feet move like lead. I’m in a total daze. Lyra. Actually here.

The guard pops the cuffs off.

"Lyra?" The word barely scrapes out of my throat as I slide into the chair across from her.

I stare at her, trying to lock every detail of her face into my brain. This is it. The final look. She’s probably here for closure. That’s what girls do before they delete you from their lives.

She’s twisting her fingers, looking down. She won't even make eye contact. Like just looking at my face physically hurts. Seeing her like this makes the loss hit ten times harder. It’s over. No future. No wedding. I’ll never get to see her belly swell with my kid.

The ache goes straight to my bones. My chest hurts in a way it never has before. My own damn greed ruined the only good thing I ever had.

"I wasn't sure if I should come," she says, her voice barely a whisper, shaking. "Or if you’d even care. But... I had to."

My chest tightens like a vice. Hearing her sound so small, so unsure around me—it’s proof of how badly I broke her. I wrecked all her progress.

"What's up, Lyra?" I ask, keeping my voice as soft as I can.

God, I want to reach out and hold her. But I can't. She’s not mine anymore. I don't have the right to touch her.

She takes a shaky breath. "I... I..."

She stops. My chest tightens. What’s wrong? Why is she struggling so hard to say it?

"Lyra..."

"I'm pregnant," she whispers to the metal table.

I go totally numb. No way. I must have misheard her. There’s no fucking way.

"What?"

"I'm pregnant," she says again. "Found out last week. Got the ultrasound. I'm about three months along."

Jesus. I’m going to be a dad. Lyra and I... we made a kid. My mind flashes back to her skin, how it felt to hold her, how touching her felt like absolute heaven. I’m so damn proud she’s the one carrying my baby. She’d be the perfect mom.

Then, a cold realization hits me like a brick.

"You're here to tell me you're getting rid of it, right?" My voice goes stiff. My whole body locks up.

Her head snaps up. Her brown eyes are suddenly blazing. For a second, the old Lyra is back. The fierce girl I fell for before I ruined her.

"Why the hell would you say that?" she snaps. "Yeah, okay, when I first saw the test, I panicked. I thought maybe it was better if this baby was never born. But I got my head straight."

I let out a breath I didn't know I was holding. Thank god. I don't know what I would’ve done if she’d aborted my kid.

"I came because I needed to know where you stand," she says. "You don't care about me, so maybe you don't care about the baby either. Do you even want to be a part of this?"

I stare at the table, thinking fast. "No."

It kills me to say it. But the kid is better off without a monster like me.

She goes quiet. Then she grabs her purse and stands up. I drop my head, trying to hide the agony ripping through me.

She starts to walk away, but then she stops. She turns around and sits right back down.

"Why?" she demands. "Because I was just a game to you?"

"What can I actually give them, Lyra?" I ask, desperate for her to see. "I'm locked up. By the time I get out, the kid’ll be grown. I’ll miss every single birthday, every first step. I can't be a real dad from a cell. And who wants a father who did what I did to their mother?"

She doesn't answer right away. I think I’ve finally convinced her. But then she throws me a curveball.

"I get it," she says. "But you're still the father. Prison doesn't change biology. You can still be there, Adrian. I’ll let you in. You don't have to miss everything. This baby deserves a dad, and they’ll love you anyway, no matter what you did. You just have to choose to show up."

I stare at her, dumbfounded. "You'd actually bring our kid here? To see me?"

"Yes."

My jaw drops. She is unbelievable. How the hell did Declan spend nine years with her without falling head over heels?

"Thank you," I choke out, my throat tight.

She nods, standing up again. "I gotta go. But I’ll keep you posted. I’ll let you know how the next doctor's visit goes. Here... take this."

She slides a piece of paper across the table. An ultrasound scan. It takes me a second to process the little gray blob. I’m not a crier, but damn, my eyes start watering.

"I care about you," I blurt out before she can walk away.

She freezes, turning back to me. "W-what?"

"I can't let you walk out that door without hearing this," I say, the words rushing out. "I fucking love you, Lyra. I don't know when it happened, but it did. I love you with everything I’ve got."

She gasps, her eyes welling up. "It’s too late for that now," she whispers.

"I know. I ruined everything. But it doesn't change how hard I fell."

"I actually saw a future with you, you know?" she says, her voice cracking. "It felt so real. I was ready to give you everything. My whole heart, even the broken parts. And you just blew it up."

I stand up, the pain almost too much to bear. I knew she liked having me around, but I had no idea she actually felt that way. That she was falling for me.

It’s killing me. Knowing what I threw away because of my own stupidity. I lost a great woman, and I lost the chance to be a real, everyday dad.

"Will you ever forgive me?" I ask, my voice cracking.

"Maybe someday," she says. "But not today."

Before my brain can stop me, I reach out, pull her close, and kiss her. It’s hard, desperate, and breathless. I pour everything into it, knowing it’s the absolute last time she’ll ever let me touch her.

This is our goodbye. And deep down, I know I’ve officially run out of chances.
