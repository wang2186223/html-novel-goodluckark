NovelVibe

Chapter 149

Yep, it’s official. I’m totally pathetic. Declan’s been gone, what, two hours? And I’m already staring at my phone, itching to dial his number. I’m the idiot who practically kicked him out the door this morning, telling him to get his butt to the office. Now? God, I regret everything.

I ran out of chores fast. Martha already had the whole place spotless, so I was basically useless. Total boredom. The baby’s knocked out cold, Martha’s running around doing actual work, and I’m just... here. Alone. I tried to bake something to pass the time. Disaster. Just like the pancake fiasco, my brain totally glitched on the measurements and the recipe.

I grab the baby monitor with a heavy sigh and head out back. I make a beeline for the gazebo. It’s gorgeous, seriously. The first time I saw it, it literally stopped me in my tracks. I have zero memory of this thing existing before. Must have been built during those four blank years of my life.

This whole amnesia thing? It’s a total mind-trip. A blessing because Declan is suddenly the perfect guy, the dream husband I always wanted. We’re finally a real family. But it’s a curse, too. Everything is brand new and weird. And the worst part? The stupid side effects. I can’t even read basic words or do simple math. It makes me feel like a total idiot.

"Ma'am? You have a visitor." Martha’s voice makes me jump. I was totally zoned out.

I squint up at her, shielding my eyes from the glare. "Who is it?"

"Willow," she says.

I run the name through my head. Nothing. No spark. But hey, if she’s showing up at my house, we must be acquainted, right? "Send her back," I say. Martha nods and heads inside. A minute later, she walks back out, trailing Piper.

"Wait, I thought you were Piper?" I blurt out, totally confused.

Martha slips away, leaving Piper to claim the seat right next to me. I turn to look at her. Honestly, she feels like a complete stranger.

"Oh, Piper's just a nickname," she says, smoothing her skirt. "My grandma started calling me that when I was a kid, and it just stuck."

I give a slow nod, keeping my mouth shut. Cue the awkward silence. It’s heavy, loud, and totally uncomfortable. Neither of us seems to know how to start a conversation. The whole vibe is just bizarre. This girl probably knows my deepest secrets, and I don’t even know her real name.

"So... how've you been? How's life?" she finally asks, cracking the ice after a long, painful pause.

I hesitate, chewing on my response. Right then, Martha saves us by bringing out a pitcher of ice-cold lemonade and two glasses. She pours, then vanishes.

I take a long sip of the sweet drink before answering. "Pretty good, actually. No complaints."

"So, Declan's treating you right?" she asks, leaning in.

The question instantly rubs me the wrong way. "Why do you ask?"

Look, I know Declan and I used to be a disaster zone. She’s supposed to be my best friend, so of course she’d know that. But Declan swore we turned a corner. Was he feeding me a load of crap?

"Oh, no reason," she says quickly, waving a hand. "Just checking in, making sure you're good."

But her eyes tell a completely different story. She’s trying way too hard to keep her face neutral, fighting to mask whatever she’s actually feeling. Something is definitely off here. Just like with Declan, the math isn't mathing. She’s hiding something from me, too.

"Is there something I should be worried about?" I press. Maybe I can crack her. Get her to spill whatever secret Declan is keeping from me. Anything is better than this constant, exhausting guessing game.

"No, seriously, nothing," she says, letting out a long breath. "I'm just really glad to see you so relaxed and happy. I always knew being with Declan would change things. I can see how much brighter you are with him."

"Was I really that miserable before?" I ask. "Declan said we had this deal—an open marriage, since he was with Aurora and I was seeing Adrian."

She freezes, staring at me like she's trying to read my mind. I can practically see the gears turning in her head, some intense internal battle raging behind her eyes. I don't get it.

"Yeah," she finally says. "I wasn't around right when Aurora showed up again, but I knew the drama. It absolutely destroyed you when Declan dumped you to the side for her, even if you pretended it didn't. You just bottled up all that hurt and tried to bury it."

Well, that’s a reality check. Not that I'm shocked. It always sucked knowing that no matter how hard I loved Declan, he only had eyes for Aurora and never gave me a real shot. I still don't buy his sudden change of heart, though. He tried to explain it when we talked about Adrian, but his story had major holes. The guy detested me for years. How does seeing me with another dude suddenly make him fall head over heels? It makes zero sense.
