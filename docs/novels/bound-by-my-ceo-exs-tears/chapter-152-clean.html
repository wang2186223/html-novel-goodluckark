Chapter 154

Beatrice and Edmund pull up about thirty minutes later. Honestly, I’ve been cooped up inside since that quick ride home from the hospital. I’m dying to see the streets. Four years is a lifetime for a city this fast; everything must be different now.

The doorbell buzzes. I jump up, practically running to the door, and yank it open.

"Hey! You and the baby ready?" Beatrice asks.

She’s just as hyped as I am. They both wrap me in a massive hug. Man, it feels so good. So familiar. Like this is what they always do.

"Yeah, let me grab her."

I spin around and head back to the living room. I scoop up my little girl, throw a quick "bye!" to Martha, and head out.

I’m about to hop into their backseat when this massive bodyguard steps in front of me.

"Sorry, ma'am. No can do. You can't leave," he says. Talk about a total buzzkill.

I don't even know his name, though I've seen him pacing around the yard a few times.

"Excuse me? Why?"

"Just doing my job," he says, trying to sound nice. But honestly? It ticks me off.

"Call him," I snap. My voice sounds way sharper than I expected.

He hesitates, then pulls out his phone and dials. I would've called Declan myself, but no way was I admitting to anyone that my eyes are so messed up I can't even read the contacts on my screen.

"What's the hold-up?" Edmund asks from behind me.

I hold up a hand to quiet him. I'm not trying to be rude, I'm just losing my mind here. I hate this. I feel like a caged animal. A prisoner in my own damn house.

The guard shoves the phone toward me.

"What?" Declan's voice grunts through the speaker, rough and annoyed.

"Rowen, why is your guy blocking the driveway?" I ask, mimicking his attitude.

"Where are you going? He can drive you, or run the errand for you."

"Beatrice invited me out for lunch. With her and Edmund. I'm bored out of my mind, so I'm going."

I brace myself for a massive argument, but he just lets out a heavy sigh. "Put Edmund on."

I hand the phone to Edmund. He walks a few paces away to talk. A minute later, he walks back and hands the phone back to the guard.

"Well?" Beatrice asks her husband.

"We're good. We just have to take his security detail," Edmund says, turning to the guard. "Grab a couple of your guys. You take one car. My security will lead, and you guys tail us."

The guard nods. "Copy that, sir."

They move fast. Within minutes, we're cruising down the road.

The ride is dead silent. Before I know it, we pull up to this super high-end restaurant. The driver parks, and Edmund helps Beatrice and me slide out of the car.

"Mind if I hold her?" Beatrice asks as we walk up.

"Sure, go ahead." I pass baby Aurora over. Beatrice beams, staring down at her granddaughter.

Edmund leads the way inside. A couple of guards stay by the entrance, while the rest follow us in.

The hostess takes one look at us and immediately ushers us to the VIP lounge. These two clearly have serious cash and clout. It totally reminds me of Declan's family.

The hostess gets us settled and walks away. I waste no time. "So, can you tell me about Adrian?"

Edmund and Beatrice exchange a quick, heavy look before turning back to me.

"So... Declan told you about him?"

"Yeah. But only that he's your adopted son, Aurora's dad, and that he's locked up."

Declan clearly hated the guy, so I knew I wouldn't get the real story from him. But these two raised him. Maybe they can give me the real picture.

"What do you want to know?" Beatrice asks.

"Do you guys go see him?"

They both nod. Baby Aurora is awake now, but she's totally chill in her grandma's arms, so I let her stay there. No crying, no fuss.

"Yeah, we do," Beatrice says. "Look, I know people judge us for it. But he's our boy, no matter what he did. I just... I really hope you don't hate him. He screwed up big time, but he's not a monster."

Honestly, I can't even bring myself to hate him. How do you hate a stranger you don't even remember? Right now, my whole brain is just processing what everyone else tells me.

I listen close as they paint this picture of their Adrian. They explain the whole mess, and how I ended up with the Sharps.

I try to follow along, but by the end of it, I'm just more confused about Declan. They keep hyping him up like he's some saint. Honestly? It feels like a sales pitch. They're listing all of Adrian's best traits but hiding who he actually is.

It just makes me want to see him even more. If I want the truth, I need to look him in the eye, not get it secondhand.

"Excuse me for a second," I say, needing some air.

I leave them with the baby and ask the hostess for the restrooms. My head is absolutely pounding. It's like my brain is short-circuiting from all this info.

I get to the bathroom and splash cold water on my face. I take a deep breath, exhaling just as the door swings open.

"What are you doing here?"

I blink. Aurora is standing there. And behind her? Ingrid. I had no clue they were even friends. Does Aurora even know Ingrid has been throwing herself at Declan for years?

"Just came to give you a little warning," Ingrid says, both of them glaring at me like I'm dirt.

"So you decided to corner me in a public restroom? Classy."

"We were having lunch when you and your little entourage walked in," Ingrid shrugs.

I remember how nasty Aurora was the other day. I grab a paper towel, dry my hands, and try to push past them. But Aurora grabs my wrist, stopping me cold.

"Declan is mine, Lyra. Back off," she spits, her voice dripping with pure venom.

"Oh, really?" I challenge. "Then why isn't he with you? Why does he come home to my bed every single night? Why is he holding *me* while he sleeps, and not you?"

Honestly, I shock myself. Where did that come from? Usually, I'd just back down to avoid a scene.

She shoves me. Hard. My head cracks against the tiled wall.

"I know your little game," she snarls. "You faked this whole amnesia thing to play the victim and trap him. But guess what? It's not gonna work. I'm going to expose you, and the second I do, Declan is coming straight back to me."

Her words start fading. A blinding, white-hot pain explodes through my skull, and my knees buckle.

The last thing I see before everything goes black is Aurora's face twisting into pure panic, while Ingrid just stands there with a sick, twisted smirk.
