Chapter 230

Six months. That’s how long it’s been, and honestly? "Happy" doesn't even cut it. Ingrid got locked up in the loony bin for life, but boom—two weeks in, she drops dead. No explanation. Her uncle vanished off the face of the earth, though the rumor mill says someone whacked him and tossed the pieces to the gators. Kaelen’s fingerprints were all over both hits. I knew it, but hey, I slept like a baby anyway.

Declan spent this half-year showing me what real joy actually feels like. My bruised heart finally got some room to breathe and heal under his watch. Jumping off that cliff and giving him another shot? Best damn decision I ever made.

I could ramble on about this forever, but words are cheap anyway. Every single morning, I wake up, count my lucky stars, and realize something crazy: you actually can fall deeper in love with the guy you already worship.

He brought peace and pure, unadulterated joy into my world. I never thought I'd get to have this.

Looking in the rearview mirror, I’m just damn grateful for the ride. Sure, we aren't some flawless fairytale. But he’s patient. Plus, shrink sessions—both solo and joint—did wonders. We still hit the occasional speed bump, and yeah, he still ticks me off sometimes, but we talk through the crap now.

We needed this detour. People might judge, but all that agonizing heartbreak? It dragged us right to this spot. Fate, destiny, whatever—our timing sucked back then. We had to grow up first, let our messy feelings mature before we could build anything real.

We had to dump the baggage and the old grudges. We needed solid ground to actually bloom. Right now, the soil is perfect. Years ago? We would’ve choked each other out.

Even our rebounds helped. Me with Adrian, him with Aurora. It forced us to face the truth we’d buried deep down. Aurora made him realize his feelings for her were totally dead—it was me he wanted. And Adrian? He was just proof that my heart belonged to Declan, no matter how badly he'd screwed up in the past.

It got me out of my bubble, too. I’d never actually dated around. Testing the waters proved I wasn't clinging to Declan just because he was my first. I chose him because I actually, deeply loved him.

"Ready, babe?"

I gasp, snapping out of my daze. "Jesus, you scared the crap out of me." I wheel around to face him.

His hands slide around my waist, yanking me flush against his chest. He kisses me hard, stealing the air right out of my lungs. For a hot second, I want to blow off our reservations and drag him to bed, but he’s been hyping up tonight for weeks.

"Are we going or what?" Asher pipes up, bouncing on his heels. He’s holding baby Aurora, who’s busy babbling some cute gibberish. "We're gonna be late!"

Aurora’s first birthday is right around the corner, and we’re all hyped. The way she clings to Declan is wild. She calls him "Pa," but Adrian is "Dada." I can't help but grin, remembering the day she coined those names. Both of those big, tough guys literally burst into tears. I’ll keep that memory locked in my brain forever.

"Alright, alright, let's roll," I tell Asher. He’s already tuned me out, pulling goofy faces at his baby sister to make her giggle.

We pile into the car and hit the road. The drive is the usual chaotic mix of loud laughs and endless talking. Forty minutes later, we pull up to the fancy spot Declan reserved for our family dinner.

We hop out, Declan cradling Aurora in his arms as we head inside. Right away, my radar goes off. Declan is sweating bullets, looking incredibly twitchy, while Asher is practically vibrating with excitement. The kid looks ready to burst.

Asher yanks the front door open. My heart nearly leaps out of my chest as a massive crowd of our friends and family pops out of nowhere.

"Surprise!" they scream.

I blink, totally lost. It’s not my birthday. What the hell is happening here?

"Uh, guys? What's going on?" I let out a shaky laugh.

Asher’s grin stretches ear to ear. He grabs my shoulders and spins me around. My breath catches. Declan is down on one knee.

I watch her face cycle through shock, confusion, and pure joy. Her eyes well up, but she blinks hard, trying to keep the tears from spilling over.

I reach out, grab her trembling hand, and press a kiss to her knuckles.

"Lyra," I start, my chest tight with nerves. "You’re my light, babe. Seriously, outside of Asher and Aurora, you are the best fucking thing that ever happened to me. I was clueless about real love until you. I don't even know when it hit me, but it did. We’ve dragged each other through hell—mostly because of my own stupid mistakes—but we made it. I’m the luckiest bastard alive because you gave me a second shot." Damn, I suck at speeches. But seeing the raw love shining in her eyes wipes that thought right out of my head.

Asher snickers at the swear word. Oh, I’m definitely getting teased about the "bastard" comment later. I grin, keeping my eyes locked on Lyra.

"I promise to cherish you every single day, because stopping is physically impossible for me. Our love shouldn't have survived, but it did. We beat the odds. We made this work, and nothing is stopping us from getting our happy ending now. I’m in this forever, Lyra. Through the good, the bad, the pain, and the healing. I’m right here, every step of the way."

My hands shake as I fish out the diamond ring Asher helped me pick. A tiny, terrified voice in my head whispers that she’ll say no. That she’ll realize she can do way better than me and walk. It’s a stupid fear—she’s never given me a reason to doubt her—but it’s there.

She drops to her knees right in front of me, eyes darting between my face and the sparkling rock.

"I want fifty years with you, Lyra," I say, trying to keep my voice steady. "Will you marry me?"

"How about seventy?" she whispers.

Holy shit. My heart explodes.

"Is that a yes, then?"

Those unshed tears finally spill over. "Yes. Absolutely, yes."

The room erupts in cheers as I slide the ring onto her finger. The second it’s on, I scoop her up, spinning her around while devouring her mouth. "I love you so damn much, Lyra," I breathe against her lips.

"Love you too," she murmurs back. "Always."
