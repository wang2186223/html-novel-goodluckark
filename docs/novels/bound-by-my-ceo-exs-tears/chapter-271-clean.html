Chapter 277

"Nice to meet you, Mrs. Wood," the guy says, flashing this blinding, perfect smile.

"Likewise," I say, grabbing his hand for a quick shake. "And hey, just call me Willow."

"Alright, now that we're introduced," Orion cuts in, snapping our attention back to him. "Arthur, Willow’s working with you. Teach her the ropes. Show her everything."

"You got it, Boss."

Orion starts to turn, then pauses. "Oh, and keep the wife thing under wraps for now. If anyone asks, you know nothing." He rounds his massive desk and drops into his chair.

Arthur glances from me to Orion, totally lost. I can't exactly explain it to him right now. Orion and I agreed—no marriage announcements until his parents get the news.

"You two can go," Orion mutters, already staring at some papers. We basically don't exist anymore.

"This way, Willow," Arthur says, holding the door. "Let's do a quick lap around the departments. Get you introduced to the crew."

I step out, and Arthur falls in line right next to me.

"So," I say, keeping pace. "How long have you been Orion's P.A.?" Just curious.

Honestly, I'm still shocked Orion has a male assistant. If I had to guess, it wasn't his call.

"Like, five months," he says. "The board brought me in after—"

He cuts himself off, suddenly tense, like he's terrified of offending me. Come on, dude. I know my husband.

"After he slept with the last one?" I finish the sentence for him. No hesitation.

Arthur clears his throat, face turning red. "Uh... well..."

He's totally tongue-tied, but I already know the deal. The board wanted to keep Orion on a leash. Guess that's why Arthur and I are both here.

"Don't worry about it," I mumble, trying to kill the awkward vibe. "I've known Orion a long time. I know exactly what he's like, Arthur."

We walk in silence after that, heading toward the elevator. This one is definitely not the fancy private lift Orion and I used earlier.

"First stop, finance," Arthur says as the doors slide open.

We step out into a massive, open-plan office. Instantly, every head turns. I spot that same blonde from earlier staring right at me.

"Where do you want to start?" Arthur asks.

My stomach is doing flips. I'm trying so hard to play it cool, but I absolutely hate being stared at. It feels like the walls are closing in.

"Wherever is fine," I stammer, wishing these people would just mind their own damn business and look away.

Arthur starts to lead the way, but some guy cuts him off.

"Arthur, hey. I need you to look at something real quick," the guy says, giving me a weird look before focusing back on Arthur.

"Is it urgent?"

"Yeah."

Arthur looks torn.

"Hey, is it cool if I leave you here for a second?" he asks, looking super guilty. "I swear I'll be right back."

Inside, I'm screaming *don't you dare leave me here*. But I just force a nod and smile. "Go ahead."

They walk off, leaving me standing in the middle of the room like a total idiot.

Then, this gorgeous woman with glasses walks up. She’s got that whole sexy librarian vibe going on.

"So, you're the new bitch Orion's playing with?" she sneers, scanning me up and down. "Don't get too comfortable. You're just his flavor of the week. It won't last."

Okay, scratch that. She's not gorgeous. She's a straight-up bitch with a capital B. Who even talks like that?

I glance over at the blonde from earlier. Yep, she definitely ran her mouth.

"Stay the fuck away from Orion. He's mine, got it?" she whispers, getting right in my face.

I take a step back, my expression hardening. A cold wave of anger hits me.

"I don't know who the hell you are, and honestly, I don't give a shit," I spit out, clenching my jaw. "Watch your mouth. I'm not the one to mess with."

"Everything good here?"

Neither of us saw Arthur coming back. We both snap our heads toward him.

I force a fake-ass smile. "Yeah. She was just leaving."

She huffs, rolls her eyes, and stomps off. Arthur watches her go, looking worried.

"That's Bianca," he says quietly. "Don't let the sweet face fool you. She's a total snake. Honestly, I have no clue how she hasn't been fired for bullying people."

I just smile, watching Bianca glare at me from across the room.

The rest of the day goes by without any more drama, but I've got a bad feeling. Bianca is going to be a massive fucking problem.
