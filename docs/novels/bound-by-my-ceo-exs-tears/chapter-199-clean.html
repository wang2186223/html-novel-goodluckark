Chapter 202

"I didn't use Lyra. It was you I wanted," he says, sliding the key right into his pocket. Well, shit. Guess I'm trapped.

"Wanted me? Seriously?" I scoff. "Then why did you sprint to the shower the second you got off me? Why the hell did we never do it without a condom? You always held back, Declan. You barely even kissed my lips! And you expect me to believe you wanted me? Give me a break."

Every single insecurity I’d shoved down came roaring back. I hated feeling this exposed. It sucked. So, I flipped the switch and let pure rage take over.

"After our date, I remembered sleeping with Adrian," I spit out. "And let me tell you, it was actual sex. Hot, wild, passionate. He made me feel wanted. Desired. With you? I was just a chore. A box to check. Don't lie and say you wanted me. Adrian showed me what a real man does when he actually wants a woman."

That memory had hit me out of nowhere, just like the rest of them. But it opened my eyes to how empty my bed with Declan really was. I hated comparing them, but I had to prove a point. I wasn't some idiot he could just gaslight.

I lock eyes with him. A flash of pure hurt crosses his face at the mention of Adrian. Good. I don't give a damn anymore. I'm way past caring about his feelings.

Honestly? The memory of Adrian didn't actually spark anything in me. No butterflies. Nothing. It didn't even come close to the crazy pull I felt toward Declan.

"The only time you ever touched me like you actually craved me was our very first night. And we both know you were wasted and thought I was Aurora."

He squeezes his eyes shut, then blinks them open. "You know me, Lyra. I don't do shit I don't want to do. Seriously, would I have climbed into bed with you if I didn't want to? If some part of me wasn't dying to have you?"

I open my mouth to snap back, but the words die in my throat. Damn it. He's right. Declan is stubborn as hell. Nobody makes him do anything he doesn't want to.

"It was how things ended with Aurora," he sighs. "It messed with my head. No closure for either of us. And yeah, I could've found some side piece. But I wanted you. Even when I thought I hated your guts, I chose to sleep with my supposed enemy over some random mistress—even though I knew you wouldn't have cared."

I flop onto the mattress, just staring up at him. "Fine. If that's true, why the hell did you always hold back?"

"Because a part of me felt guilty for wanting you," he admits. "You ruined my future with the girl I thought was my soulmate. How was I supposed to just let myself crave you after that? How could I—"

"Look, I lost my temper and talked shit. You know how I get when I'm pissed—I don't think. It's no excuse, but just listen. Aurora told me you trashed her and said some really nasty stuff. I didn't know she was lying, so I came to get even. I wanted to hurt you the way I thought you hurt her. I knew those words would destroy you, and yeah, that's exactly why I said them."

My body goes a little slack, but only a bit. Hearing his confession just makes the ache in my chest ten times worse.

"So this was recent then," I spit, refusing to buy his pathetic excuse. "Must be nice to love her so much that you'd absolutely wreck me just to play her white knight."

Honestly, I didn't even care what happened between me and Aurora. If I did something to her, I definitely had a damn good reason. I don't just go around bullying people for fun.

"I don't love her!" he growls, stepping closer.

"Sure fooled me," I snap. "Why else would you say such vile shit to me? I never trapped you, Declan. We got married for Asher, period. I wouldn't have blinked if you got a side chick, as long as you kept it quiet and out of the press. But using me? That's just sick."

I thrash against him, driving my elbow straight into his gut. He groans, his grip slipping. I scramble to my feet, bolting for the exit, but he blurs across the room, beats me to the door, and throws the deadbolt shut.
