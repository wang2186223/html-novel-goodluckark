Chapter 115

I freeze. The words bounce around inside my skull.

A girl. Lyra had a baby girl. A daughter. Holy shit, Asher is gonna lose his mind. He’s been begging God for a little sister, and boom, he got his wish.

"Can I see her?" I blurt out.

"Look, I get you're dying to see your kid, Mr. Woods," the doc says, "but we need to run some checks first. Just hang tight."

My brain lags for a second. Then it hits me—he thinks I'm the dad. Honestly? I don't even care. It just caught me totally off guard.

"She's super early—only twenty-six weeks," he explains. "Plus, the trauma from the gunshot and the fall... yeah, we're putting her straight into an incubator. She's staying in the NICU until she's strong enough to go home."

Wait. He didn't say a damn word about Lyra. Is she... is she not gonna make it? My chest tightens, heart hammering against my ribs. No. She has to pull through. She has to. How the hell are we supposed to breathe without her?

"But you need to brace yourself," the doc adds. "We'll do everything we can, but there are no guarantees. Even if she pulls through, we're talking potential long-term stuff—cerebral palsy, learning delays, or issues with her eyes and ears."

"That's just a worst-case scenario, right? Not a guarantee?" My voice cracks. I already feel this massive ache for the poor kid. Born way too soon, and she's already fighting a war most adults couldn't handle.

"Exactly. Just possibilities, not certainties. We just have to lay out all the cards so you're prepared."

I just nod.

"And Lyra?" I stop him before he can bail.

"Look, I'm sorry, but I don't have answers yet. She's in a really bad way. We're just taking it minute by minute." Before I can even process that, he slips back through the double doors.

I just stand there, totally numb. His words slice right through me like broken glass, tearing up my insides. It hurts like hell.

A few minutes pass, and a nurse pops out. Lyra's in the OR. I wanted to see her first, but they wheeled her out a back exit. The nurse says the head wound is bad. Real bad. They didn't want us seeing her looking like that.

Once she's gone, I sink into a plastic chair. I start talking to a God I haven't spoken to in years. I try to make deals. I get angry. Then, finally, I just fucking beg. Please, just hear me. Save her. Save my girl.

"Hey, Ro. Want me to grab you a bite?" my mom asks softly.

I shake my head. The thought of food makes me want to puke. My nerves are shot to hell. Eating is the absolute last thing I care about.

Mom gets up, and Orion slides into her seat. Dad's on my other side. I hate feeling weak. I fucking hate it. But right now? I'm completely powerless, and I have no clue how to fix it.

The clock keeps ticking. Nothing. Then the heavy doors swing open and Asher comes flying at me. He slams into my chest. I wrap my arms around him, breathing in his kid scent. It helps, but only a little.

Silas walks in right behind him, Jasper in tow. I catch Silas's eye and give him a quick nod—*thanks, man*. He nods back. Aurora goes stiff when she spots them, but Eleanor just starts crying the second she sees her grandkid.

"Dad, where's Mom?" Asher asks, looking up at me, totally freaked out. "Cal picked me up, and when I asked why Mom wasn't there, he just said you'd tell me."

God, this sucks. I had all this time to figure out what to say, but my brain is totally blank.

"Is she having the baby?" he presses. "Is that why we're here?"

I take a slow, shaky breath. "Hey, buddy. Look at me. Your mom got hurt. Really bad. The doctors are working on her right now, and they're doing everything they can to fix her up."

His eyes instantly fill up, and my chest just shatters. He's just a kid. He shouldn't have to deal with this shit. He shouldn't be sitting in a hospital worrying if his mom is gonna die.

"What about... the baby?" his voice cracks.

I force a small smile. "You got a baby sister, bud. Just like you wanted."

His eyes go wide. For a second, he looks totally amazed, and this huge grin spreads across his face. "Can I see her?"

"Not yet, buddy. The doctors need to make sure she's totally okay first."

The grin holds for a second, then slowly melts away. The tears he was holding back start spilling over. No sobbing, no noise. Just silent tears rolling down his face.

"Is she gonna die? Like Grandpa did?" His voice hitches. "I don't want her to leave, Dad."

I pull him in tight, burying his face in my chest, trying like hell to keep my own shit together for him. He shouldn't even have to think about that. No kid should.

Whoever did this to Lyra is fucking dead. They picked the wrong family to mess with. I'm going to make them pay, ten times over, for every single tear.

"No way, buddy. Your mom is the toughest person I know. She's gonna make it," I whisper into his hair.

We hold onto each other for a bit, then he climbs up onto my lap. He rests his head against my chest, gripping my shirt like a lifeline. He's usually such a tough kid, but right now, he's completely shattered.

So we wait. I get up, pace the floor, sit back down, repeat. Asher doesn't let go of me once. Every single minute that ticks by without an update feels like a slow death. The longer it takes, the more I'm convinced everything is going to shit in that operating room.

Hours later, Lyra's parents finally burst through the doors.

"Where is she? Where's my daughter?" Beatrice demands, her eyes red and swollen.

I've never seen Beatrice and Edmund look this wrecked. Usually, they're picture-perfect, even when Adrian got locked up. Today, they look like they've been dragged through hell. Can't blame them, though. They already missed twenty-five years of her life, and now they might lose her forever.

Before we can even say anything, the OR door swings open. A doctor steps out. One look at his face and my stomach drops. It's not good.

I turn to my mom. "Hey, can you take Asher and Jasper to get some snacks? They need to eat."

She nods, calling the boys over. Asher looks at me, suspicious, but I give him a reassuring nod. *It's fine, buddy.* A total fucking lie, but I can't let him hear whatever awful shit this doctor is about to say.

"Mr. Woods..." the doctor starts, then hesitates.

My jaw clenches so hard my teeth grind. "Just fucking say it," I growl, bracing myself for the worst.

"She's alive," he says, his voice flat. "But she slipped into a coma during the surgery."

Another punch straight to the gut. None of us saw it coming.
