Chapter 28

My blood is boiling. Scratch that—I’m fucking livid. The bitch slipped through my fingers. Again. She’s supposed to be a corpse, but nope, still breathing.

I glare at Kael. "How the hell is she not dead yet? Explain."

"Dude, I was right there," he mumbles. "Like, seconds away from putting her down. Then her damn gorilla of a bodyguard showed up and ruined everything."

Does he think I’m an idiot? Like I can't see through his bullshit. Ever since we started, all I get from this clown are excuses. Three strikes, three misses. No body. The only silver lining? His pockets are still empty. Thank god I didn't pay this loser upfront.

Half a million bucks. That was his payday. You’d think five hundred grand would make a guy move his ass and hit the deadline. Apparently not.

I down a shot of whiskey, the burn doing nothing to stop the rage eating at my chest.

"Oh, really? Almost had her?" I snap, my temper spiking. "Is that why you're standing there bleeding like a stuck pig, genius?"

"Next time, man. I swear," he says, staring hard at his shoes. "She won't get away again."

"That's three times, you idiot!" I yell, seriously tempted to just put a bullet in his useless head right now.

Kael shuts up. He just keeps studying the floorboards like they hold the secrets to the universe.

He’s screwing up my entire timeline. This hit should've been wrapped up months ago. Instead, Lyra is still out there, taking up oxygen.

People told me this guy was the best. A real closer. What a joke. All I've gotten are cheap words and zero results.

"Seriously, how hard is it to clip one single chick? It's a basic hit. What's the issue?"

Honestly, it boggles my mind. How does anyone get that lucky? Three times. She should be six feet under, but she's just walking around with a few bruises.

"I don't fucking know, okay?" he spits back, his face twisting. "It's never this complicated. Usually, I pop 'em on the first try and walk away."

I pace around the room, restless. This was supposed to be a cakewalk. She's the roadblock. The only thing keeping me from my prize. The second I realized she was in my way, I drew up the target.

I wanted it clean—an accident. No way I was letting the cops sniff around after all my hard work. Then the Reapers' Angels popped up. Perfect. They were the ultimate scapegoat to take the fall.

Except now the gig is up. They figured out the Reapers had nothing to do with it. It was a setup.

It’s only a matter of time before they trace it to Kael, and then straight to me. No way. I didn't scheme this hard just to crash and burn at the finish line.

"Fuck!" I scream, hurling my glass. It shatters against the drywall, sending shards flying.

The clock is ticking. I should be sipping cocktails on a beach right now, living large. Instead, I'm stuck in neutral. Actually, scratch that—I'm miles away from the finish line.

"Just give me one more shot," Kael begs.

I let out a harsh laugh. "So you can screw up again? Yeah, no thanks."

Seriously, what is up with this girl? Is she immortal? I'm not a religious guy, but damn, it's like she's got a guardian angel on speed dial. Surviving three hits isn't luck. It's practically a miracle.

Luckiest bitch on the planet. And it's seriously pissing me off. I don't lose. Ever. I get what I want, but putting Lyra in the ground is turning into a major pain in the ass.

"Hey, you think it's so easy? Let's see you try," Kael snorts, cutting into my head.

That does it. My fuse blows. I grab the nearest heavy object and hurl it straight at his head. He doesn't even have time to flinch. The heavy glass whiskey bottle smashes right into his smug face.

A red streak starts dripping down his temple. I grin. Seeing him bleed actually takes the edge off my rage.

"You wanna run that mouth again?" I ask, stepping closer.

He clamps his jaw shut, glaring at me with pure hatred, but he shakes his head. Good. The idiot finally learned when to shut up.

"We need a fast play," I mumble, pacing again. "I'm done wasting time on this chick."

If her death wasn't the key to the whole vault, I'd just walk away. She's a magnet for trouble. Every failed attempt just leaves a trail of breadcrumbs for the cops. If I get caught, everything I built goes down the drain. I can't let that happen.

"Look, she's tough to crack," Kael says, snapping me out of my thoughts. "Now the cops are sniffing around, plus her billionaire husband has her locked down with heavy security. It's a whole different ballgame."

My fists tighten at the mention of Declan. No shit, Sherlock. I already knew all that. I didn't need this loser stating the obvious just to feel important.

I drop into a chair, gears turning. The old playbook is trash. Time for a pivot. I need something foolproof. A final blow.

Before, I was trying to be precise. Just her. Now? Screw it. I don't care about collateral damage.

If she's inside a building, I'll blow up the whole damn block.

As long as she ends up in a body bag, anyone else can go down with her.

A grin creeps onto my face. My chest loosens up. Finally, a solid plan. I feel totally chill now.

"You're smiling," Kael notes, looking at me like a dog waiting for a treat. "Got something?"

The cops have his DNA from the scene. I need to clean that up before the lab runs it. He's got a record, so a match would be instant. That's a loose end I can't afford.

"Oh, yeah," I smirk, standing up. "I definitely do."

He grins back, looking relieved.

This time, Lyra is done. Even if I have to pull the trigger myself.
