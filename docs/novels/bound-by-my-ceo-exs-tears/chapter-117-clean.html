Chapter 118

My blood starts to boil. Garrett can say whatever he wants, but Kaelen is still on my shit list. Seriously, the guy literally snatched Lyra. Nobody pulls a stunt like that without some twisted angle. And Garrett’s logic? Total garbage. If Kaelen actually cared about keeping her safe, why drag her off and force my hand? It doesn't add up.

"Why the hell are you here?" I spit out, glaring.

He looks past me, scanning the room. The guys are already on their feet, ready to throw down, but Kaelen doesn't even blink. That’s his brand of crazy—totally fearless. I’m a cold bastard, sure, but this guy? He’s a whole different beast. A straight-up psycho wrapped in a slick, lethal package.

"Came for Lyra. Duh," he says, wearing this smug, punchable smirk. "What, you think I came to check out your ugly mug?"

My jaw aches from clenching. I want to rip his head off, but we're in a hospital. Security would toss me in a second, and I need to stay right here.

"Oh, and we're just supposed to buy that?" Orion chimes in, stepping up. "You literally took her hostage a few months back."

Kaelen locks his creepy gaze onto my brother. I don't scare easy, but the Reaper's stare is pure ice. It sends a shiver straight down your back. Just empty, soulless eyes. Honestly, the guy probably doesn't even have a soul. Who cares.

"Give a shit what you think," Kaelen says, totally chill. "I'm not here for you clowns. Now, you gonna tell me how she's doing, or do I go hunt down her doctor?"

His smug voice is driving me nuts. Seriously, what is his deal? Why is he even lurking around here?

"Why should we tell you crap?" I growl, teeth gritted. "For all we know, you're the one who ordered the hit."

He blinks, then throws his head back and cracks up. The laugh echoes off the sterile walls, giving me literal chills. Then, boom—he stops, locking his eyes right back onto me.

"Trust me, pal," he says, voice dropping back to sub-zero. "If I wanted her gone, she'd already be in a casket."

"If you didn't do it, then why show up?" Beatrice asks. "Why do you even care about her?"

Kaelen looks away from me, scanning the room until his eyes land on someone behind my shoulder. I turn around. It's Audrey. He stares at her, totally hooked, and she's staring right back like she's hypnotized. It takes him a second to tear his eyes away. Huh. Well, that's interesting.

He looks back at Beatrice. "Well, she's pregnant with my niece. So yeah, I care." He drops the bomb like it's nothing, leaving the whole room speechless.

Okay, this guy is out of his mind. No way I heard that right. How the hell is he the uncle? He's not related to Lyra. Unless he's some secret brother she never knew about.

"No... that's impossible," Piper stammers out.

Looking at Beatrice and Edmund, there's no way Kaelen is their kid. So if he's not lying, the connection is on the other side. I stare at his face, and suddenly, the pieces fall into place. "You're Adrian's brother," I say, seeing the resemblance now.

"Bingo."

"But how?" Beatrice whispers, pale. "The adoption agency said Adrian had nobody left."

"I was locked up in juvie," Kaelen says. "One of my old man's guys told me Adrian was in the system. I had them scrub his files so it looked like our dad was his only living relative." He stops for a second. "Just like I told Lyra, I wanted the kid to have a shot at a normal life. So I gave him one. Didn't want him ending up like me."

"Yeah, and he still landed in a cell," I scoff.

He chuckles. This time, it actually sounds almost... human. "Lyra said the exact same thing."

Silence hangs in the air until Edmund breaks it. "Sounds like you've been spending time with my daughter."

"Not exactly hanging out... but we've been talking," Kaelen says. Then he spills. He explains the whole kidnapping plot, and how he approached her later, asking to be part of the kid's life. And Lyra, being too damn sweet for her own good, actually said yes.

"You know she'll get dragged by the cops if they find out you guys were talking, right?" Audrey asks.

"Relax. I've got it covered," he says, throwing Audrey a wicked grin but keeping the details to himself.

"Since you two were talking, did she say anything?" I ask, desperate for a lead. "Like, did she feel threatened? Anything?"

Kaelen fills us in. He talks about the first creepy note she got, then the next two. The last one arrived yesterday. Right before she got shot.

A sick wave of jealousy hits me. She went to him instead of me? I force the feeling down. Doesn't matter right now. Not while she's hooked up to machines in a coma.

"I screwed up," Kaelen mutters. "Should've dug deeper. I could've stopped this." I stare at him, shocked to see actual guilt in his eyes. He looks uncomfortable, like he's never had to feel bad about anything in his life.

Nobody speaks. We're all drowning in our own guilt, thinking we could've done something. I sink into a chair, letting out a heavy breath just as Aurora walks through the double doors.

The second Kaelen spots her, his face goes totally blank. Before anyone can move, he lunges. He grabs Aurora by the throat and slams her hard against the wall.

"What the fuck are you doing?!" Corbin yells, rushing forward, but Kaelen doesn't budge. Aurora gasps, choking, her eyes wide with pure terror.

I look up at the ceiling and sigh. "Let her go, Kaelen." He ignores me, tightening his grip.

Corbin tries to yank him off his sister, but Kaelen is like a brick wall. The rest of the room is just frozen.

"If I find out you had a hand in this," Kaelen growls in her face, "I will literally gut you. I'll make those serial killer documentaries you love look like a Disney movie. Got it?"

How the hell does he know she watches that stuff? Whatever. It's Kaelen. The guy knows everything.
