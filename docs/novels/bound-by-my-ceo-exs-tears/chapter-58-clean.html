Chapter 59

It’s been a whole month since the Adrian disaster. Am I fine? Hell no. Does it still sting? You bet your ass it does. Have I let go? Not even close.

Every single day is a struggle. I’m basically sinking in this massive wave of misery. I actually convinced myself I was doing great when I chose to take that step with Adrian. What a joke. I was just lying straight to my own face.

Adrian’s backstabbing pulled up every single piece of garbage I tried to bury deep down. All the old trauma I wanted to forget? Yep, right back at the start. Except this time, my soul is covered in fresh, ugly scars.

I’m basically a zombie lately. Just coasting on autopilot. Days blur together because I’m not actually here. I’m just breathing. Getting through twenty-four hours at a time.

It feels like the whole world kept moving while I got left behind. Just trapped in this loop of pure misery. Everything is pitch black, freezing, and I’m totally on my own.

"Hey, Miss Sharp, you good?"

Arthur, one of my kids in class, snaps me out of it.

God, I absolutely despise that name. It’s just a constant slap in the face from the people who ruined me. I’ve been wanting to ditch it, but what else do I use? No way I’m taking the Howell name. I barely know those people. Plus, I haven't even muttered a word to them since that blowout at my place.

"Yeah, I’m fine... just get back to your work," I mumble, staring hard at the textbooks on my desk.

I used to live for this job, but now? Total drag. I walk into this building every morning just praying the clock ticks faster so I can bolt. I just want to be left alone, but Piper and Declan won't stop breathing down my neck, constantly checking in.

The kids are starting to notice. My lessons aren't fun anymore. The bubbly teacher they liked is gone. I’m basically a machine. Dead inside. A bunch of them are even cutting class now. I have zero clue how to find the old me.

*Why try to resurrect the old you? Just build a new one,* a voice inside my head whispers.

Is that even a thing? Rebuilding myself from scratch. A version of me that isn't the same girl who got shattered as a kid, a teen, and now a woman.

The bell cuts off my thoughts. Thank god. I grab my stuff and fly out the door without even saying goodbye to the class.

I keep my eyes glued to the floor, power-walking to the breakroom. I just need a second to breathe before the next period starts. Luckily, the hallway is a clear shot. I don't have the energy for small talk today.

I slide into the lounge and stop dead. I thought it'd be quiet, but the place is absolutely packed. Ugh. I let out a quiet groan and head straight for the corner chair.

The second my butt hits the seat, my phone starts buzzing. Beatrice. I’ve been dodging her calls because I don't know how to deal with them—especially since they're tight with Adrian. I go to swipe decline, but my stupid thumb slips and hits answer.

"Lyra? You there?" her voice comes through.

I stay quiet. I just let out a long, shaky breath.

"Please, sweetie, don't shut us out. Don't do this to your father and me," she begs, her voice cracking.

Still, nothing comes out of my mouth. I couldn't speak even if I tried. My jaw is literally locked. Not a single damn word.

"You're my baby girl, Lyra. I want to be there. I want to be the mom I never got to be. I know you're in pain, and I want to help. I already lost one kid, please don't make me go through that again. Finding you and losing you right after? It'll destroy me." She’s sobbing now, and it completely guts me.

My eyes start watering. Great. I’ve been a total crying mess lately.

"I... I just need some time," I choke out, trying to keep my voice steady.

She lets out a huge sigh of relief. "Take all the time you need, honey. Just know I love you. I never stopped loving you, even when I thought you were gone forever. I just hope you can trust me. I'm always here."

Wow. It feels amazing to actually be wanted by someone. But trust? I'm not there yet. We'll see.

"Okay," I mumble, and end the call.

I hear her, but my brain is spinning. What if she's just trying to fill a void? That's my biggest fear. Being a placeholder. A backup plan, just like I was to Declan.

I'm not trying to be a bitch to them. I'm just trying to guard whatever tiny pieces of my heart are left.

"Well, damn, girl," Carol says, strutting over to my table.

"Shit," I mutter under my breath.

She teaches middle school and is basically the most annoying person on campus. Why? Because she can't keep her nose out of anyone's business.

"Look at all that food," she gasps, staring at my plate. "Seriously, with how much you're stuffing your face and how cranky you've been, I'd swear you were knocked up!" She bursts out laughing.

It’s a joke, obviously, but my body completely locks up. Her words loop in my head like a horror movie soundtrack. Pure terror shoots straight through my veins.

Carol’s laugh dies down, and her eyebrows knit together. "Whoa, Lyra, you good? I was totally kidding, you know?"

I scramble to my feet, panic hitting me like a freight train. I bolt out of the lounge, sprint down the hallway, and burst through the school doors. I throw myself into my driver's seat and peel out of the parking lot.

Oh god. No, no, no. This cannot be real. Please, let this be a mistake.

I try to count the days, to do the math, but my brain is a scrambled mess.

I slam on the gas, not giving a single crap if a cop pulls me over. I just need to prove Carol is dead wrong.

I screech into a parking spot at the drugstore and run inside. Of course, my luck is trash, and I run straight into Aurora.

"Lyra?" she blinks, totally caught off guard. Probably because I look like an absolute trainwreck.

I blow right past her and sprint to the family planning aisle. I grab a handful of boxes, rush the register, pay, and bolt.

I speed home, park, and run inside. I know how this works. I chug three massive glasses of water, head straight to the bathroom, and take the test.

No. No way. This is not happening. I cannot be pregnant. Not now. And absolutely not with Adrian's kid.

"Why me, God?" I sob, tears streaming down my face.

Silence. No answer. No explanation for why my life is such a joke, or why I have the worst luck on the planet.

I try to drag myself up off the bathroom floor, but my body is completely shot. I have zero energy left.

Is this my destiny? Just a cycle of oops-pregnancies? First Asher, and now this nightmare.

I stare blankly at the bathroom tiles, my mind rewinding. Adrian and I had one unprotected night. I was supposed to grab Plan B the next morning, but it completely slipped my mind. By the time I remembered, it was way too late.

I actually told him about it back then. I thought he'd blow a fuse, but he was totally chill. He calmed me down, and we both figured the chances were super low anyway.

Sure, my body was acting weird—like my period being late—but I blamed it on stress. And my crazy appetite? I always stuff my face when I'm stressed or depressed, so I didn't think twice about it.
