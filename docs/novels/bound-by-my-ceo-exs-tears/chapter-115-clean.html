NovelVibe

Chapter 116

"A coma? What the hell do you mean, a coma?" Edmund’s voice shakes, totally out of control.

My chest goes wild again. My heart slams against my ribs like it’s trying to bust right through the bone.

I can't think. My brain just locks up. The doctor’s voice drags out in slow motion, drowned out by this high-pitched ringing in my ears.

I trip backward, hitting the plastic chair I just stood up from. Orion and Dad reach out, hands landing on my shoulders, but I jerk away. Don't touch me. I don't want a hug. I just need the doc to say the surgery worked, that Lyra’s gonna open her eyes in a couple of hours.

"Four entry wounds," the doctor says. "Head, chest, gut, thigh. We dug three of them out. But the one in her skull? It's buried too deep. Touching it would kill her on the table."

Fuck. My mind goes totally blank. He’s saying she has to walk around with lead in her brain? How is that fucking fair? She was literally fine this morning. Then everything went to hell.

*But she's alive,* some voice in my head whispers.

I shut it down. Sure, she's breathing, but for how long? That's the real question.

"We patched up the bleeding, inside and out," the doctor continues. "Drained some fluid to keep the brain swelling down. But her heart stopped. Twice. After the second crash, she went under. She's in the ICU now."

Watching her take those bullets was brutal, but this? This is worse. Hearing she died twice rips me to shreds. It feels like a thousand blades twisting in my gut. I wouldn't wish this hell on my worst enemy.

"Is she gonna wake up?" My voice is a dry rasp. "Can she actually bounce back from this?"

"Hard to say," the doctor sighs. "It's not a medically induced coma. There's no timeline. Could be tomorrow, could be next week, months, or... maybe never. We just have to wait a few days and see what her brain does."

That "never" hits me like a physical blow. I almost collapse right there.

No. Shake it off. Don't go down that rabbit hole. Lyra is tough. She's gonna wake up. Give it a few days, and she'll be right back to glaring and biting my head off.

"And the bullet?" Beatrice's voice cracks, tears streaking her face. "What does living with that do to her?"

I can't even fathom what Beatrice is going through. If this were Asher... God, I can't even go there. It would break me.

"Some people recover fine," the doctor says. "Others don't. She could face memory loss, speech or hearing deficits, or trouble reading and processing numbers. Brain trauma is unpredictable. We won't know anything until she opens her eyes."

*If* she wakes up. He doesn't say it out loud, but the look in his eyes says it all. He's totally unsure.

Beatrice just nods, crying harder. Edmund wraps his arms around her, pulling her tight against him.

The power couple I usually see? Gone. Just two wrecked, terrified parents left in their place.

"Visiting hours are over anyway," the doctor adds. "Come back tomorrow. But only one of you gets in the room at a time. Now, excuse me."

We nod, and he walks off. Right then, Mom shows up with the kids.

Asher runs straight to me. Jasper heads for his dad.

"What's the word? Is Mom gonna be okay?" Asher looks up, his eyes wide and full of hope.

This is the absolute worst part of parenting. Do you tell your kid the brutal truth, or do you lie to protect them?

Do I tell him his mom is in a coma and might never wake up, or do I play it off like everything's fine?

"The doctors aren't totally sure yet, buddy," I say, choosing the truth. "We just have to wait and pray."

If I lie and she doesn't make it, he'll hate me forever for telling him she was okay.

He doesn't say a word. Just stares at me, then drops his gaze to his shoes.

After a quiet minute, I turn to the group. "Look, we can't see her tonight anyway. You guys should head home, get some sleep, and come back in the morning."

"No way," Audrey and Piper snap together. Beatrice and Edmund shake their heads too.

I tell them I'll call the second anything changes, but they aren't moving. Finally, everyone digs in to stay, except for Aurora, Cal, Eleanor, and my folks.

My parents only leave because I point out Asher can't sleep here and needs them. Cal takes off for Jasper's sake—the kid is yawning, though he looks scared out of his mind.

Once they're gone, Orion slides into the seat next to me. We don't talk. Just sit there in the quiet until Garrett walks in.

"Sorry I'm late," Garrett says. "We were wrapping up witness statements on the shooting. Man, I am so sorry about Lyra."

I nod. "You get anything useful?"

"Nothing new. Just what we already saw on the security footage."

Dammit. I was hoping for a lead. Anything to start with.

"Can we talk to her? Is she up for questions?"

My face goes cold. My voice drops an octave. I know he doesn't know, but it still pisses me off.

"She's in a coma," I spit through clenched teeth.

"Shit, sorry," he says, looking genuinely bad, but it doesn't make me feel any better.

"Is there anything you can tell me? Threats? Stalkers? Anyone creepy hanging around? Even the smallest detail helps."

I shake my head. "Nothing. I got nothing."

He nods, then looks at the others. "Maybe I'm overthinking, but when she called me earlier, she sounded totally freaked out. Desperate. Not like herself at all. I figured she was spiraling over her past, especially after the whole Jasper thing set her off."

"Who the hell is Jasper?" Garrett asks.

"Aurora's kid," Corbin says. "She kept him a secret until Lyra dug up the truth and spilled it."

Aurora stares at her shoes, keeping her face blank.

"Why hide her own kid?"

"Didn't want him, didn't want us knowing," Corbin explains. "Silas—the dad—moved next door to Lyra with the kid. That's how Lyra put it together."

Garrett turns to Corbin. "And how did everyone react?"

"Terribly. We're all furious. She's still acting crazy about it, so Mom gave her an ultimatum: fix this mess or she's dead to us."

"Bet Aurora loved that," Garrett says dryly.

"She lost her mind. Still furious, mostly at Lyra for blowing up her secret."

Garrett rubs his chin, totally zoned out. I've known him forever—I can practically hear the gears grinding in his skull.

"Right," he says after a long beat.

"See what?" Orion and I ask together.

"Based on what Corbin just said? I think I have our first real suspect."

"What?" Corbin blinks. Audrey whispers, "Who?"

Garrett lets out a tight, grim smile. "Aurora."

Everyone stares at him like he's lost his damn mind. I just quiet down, thinking. Looking at it from his perspective, yeah, she's got a motive. But is she really that pissed? Angry enough to actually hire a hitman to put Lyra in the ground?
