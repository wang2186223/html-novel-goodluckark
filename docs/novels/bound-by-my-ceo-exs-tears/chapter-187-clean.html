Chapter 189

He shoves a sleek black Amex into my palm. "Take it," he says. It's not a request.

I stare at the plastic, my chest tightening. "Declan, I can't..."

"Just take it, Lyra. You're with me now. That means my vault is your vault. No arguments, okay?"

My brows knit together. I look from the card to his face, totally lost. What does he mean, *he* has money? Like, obviously he does, but still. I want to press him, but one look at his stubborn jaw tells me it's a losing battle. He's not backing down.

"Fine," I sigh, slipping the card into my purse. "Thanks."

I have zero intention of touching his money, but hey, what he doesn't know won't hurt him. I kiss him goodbye and head out. The girls and I pile into a single SUV, with Graham, one of the heavies, taking the wheel. Destination: the mall.

My heart is doing flips. I can't shake the buzz. We pull up, Graham parks, and we hit the pavement. He trails a few paces behind us, playing the silent shadow.

We slide into this ultra-high-end boutique, and Piper nudges me. "Alright, what's the vibe today? What are we hunting for?"

I glance at a nearby rack, catch a glimpse of a price tag, and my stomach drops.

"Uh, Piper?" My voice squeaks. "I think we're in the wrong league. One look at these tags and I'm ready for the ER. Seriously, my heart just stopped."

Audrey and Piper exchange a look, then stare at me like I've grown a second head. "Wait, what?" Audrey asks, her brow furrowing. "Are you kidding me?"

"I'm on a teacher's budget, guys. I can't drop a month's rent on a top. And no, I'm not touching Declan's black card. Let's find a place that won't bankrupt me."

Piper grabs my wrist and drags me over to a plush velvet sofa. Audrey waves off the sales associate with a polite, "Give us a second, thanks."

Piper leans in. "Okay, did Declan seriously not tell you? Lyra, your parents are loaded. Like, filthy rich. You know the Howells, right?"

I nod. Duh. Everyone in the city knows that name.

"Well, surprise. Beatrice and Edmund Howell? Those are your biological parents."

My jaw hits the floor. Is she messing with me? I haven't spoken to them lately, even though my phone's been blowing up with their missed calls. I was actually planning to stop by their place next week to sort things out.

"And that's not even the best part," Piper continues, dropping another bomb. "You don't need Declan's cash, and you don't need theirs. You're sitting on your own fortune, babe. You're a literal millionaire. You could buy this entire store if you wanted to."

"Wait, what? How?" My brain short-circuits. None of this makes sense. If Declan hadn't stepped up and covered all of Asher's medical bills, I'd be drowning in debt right now. Or so I thought.

"We'll explain the details later," Piper says, waving it off. "Right now, we need to find a dress that will make Declan absolutely lose his mind when he sees you tonight."

We hit the racks. After a marathon of trying on silk, lace, and sequins across three different boutiques, we find *the* one. It's perfect. And since I'm apparently rolling in cash, I lose all self-control. I go wild. Dresses, skirts, killer heels, cute tops—I grab it all. I even grab a bunch of toys and clothes for the kids.

By the time we head to the exit, my feet are killing me, but my adrenaline is through the roof.

"I'm seriously so happy for you, Lyra," Piper says as we load the bags into the trunk and head home. "Seeing you and Declan finally click... it's the best."

Audrey grins from the backseat. "Oh, totally. She called it first. She knew he was crazy about you even when you were in total denial. She's been the president of the Declan fan club since day one."

A warm, genuine smile spreads across my face. I'm happy. Like, actually, deeply happy. I just pray to God nothing comes along to screw this up.
