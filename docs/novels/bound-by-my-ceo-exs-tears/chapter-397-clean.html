Chapter 405

Three years later.

Aurora.

"Okay, seriously, babe. When are you getting back in the game?" Lyra plops down on the bench beside me, nudging my shoulder.

My eyes drift to the lawn, a grin tugging at my mouth. The backyard is a chaotic mess of balloons and toddlers. It’s Frederick’s first birthday—Piper and Corbin’s little guy, named after our late dad.

Two years back, those two tied the knot. Corbin didn't waste a second. The moment I opened my eyes in the ICU after that near-fatal wreck, he popped the question to Piper. As for the idiot who hit me? He’s rotting in a cell, serving five years for reckless driving. Hope the prison food teaches him some damn sense.

Watching me almost kick the bucket shook Corbin up. It made him realize life’s too short to wait. Piper, of course, bawled and said yes. They had this gorgeous, flower-heavy spring wedding.

Lyra was my ticket into this crazy inner circle. Take Celeste and Kaelen—they did a super low-key, family-only wedding. Four months later, boom, baby Serenity arrived. Now Celeste is rocking a massive baby bump again. Round two, another girl.

Willow and Orion are also on the baby train. Number three is on the way. Orion actually freaked out at first—he saw the brutal labor Harp went through and swore off more kids, which is hilarious because he used to brag about wanting an entire basketball team. They had some massive screaming matches over it, but Willow wore him down. She’s absolutely glowing now.

Then there’s Lyra and Declan. Still madly in love, still unstoppable. Three kids deep, but honestly? I bet they’ll have another soon. Oh, and Asher and Aurora? They had a little surprise three years back—a sweet baby girl named Hazel.

"Earth to Aurora..." Lyra nudges me again.

"I'm good, Lyra. Seriously. Dating is the last thing on my radar."

Truth is, I love my life right now. If some guy walks into it, cool. If not, also cool. Not everyone gets the fairy tale, and I’m totally fine with that.

"Uh-uh, no way," Lyra snorts. "You used to play the injury card, but you’re out of that wheelchair now, babe. No excuses."

Yep. I beat the odds. The docs said I’d never walk right, but I proved them wrong. Sure, my legs still ache when it rains, but I’m fully mobile. The hardest pill to swallow? Saying goodbye to stilettos. I wore a pair once and literally cried from the agony. So, yeah, flats are my life now. Honestly? Not the worst thing ever.

I wiggle my eyebrows at her. "If I tell you there's a ridiculously hot new attorney at my firm, will you shut up?"

She lets out this ear-piercing shriek. Heads turn.

"Jesus, Lyra, pipe down! You sound like a dying bird."

"Are you kidding? This is huge!"

"Everything good, Mom?" Jasper’s voice startles me. I didn't even see him walk up, cradling his three-month-old brother. He’s called me 'Mom' ever since my hospital days, and honestly, it still melts me every single time. I lean over, planting wet kisses on Jasper's cheek, then on baby Milo's, before scooping the little guy into my arms.

"All good, sweetie. Your aunt is just having a drama queen moment," I say, blowing raspberries on Milo's tummy. The baby flashes me a gummy, drooly grin. God, my heart.

Okay, quick recap because it sounds crazy. I’m talking about dating, but holding a baby who calls me Mom? Milo isn't mine. He belongs to Silas and his wife, Aurora. They tied the knot last year, and Milo was their instant honeymoon souvenir.

After my crash, Silas and I actually patched things up. He let go of the past, just like Jasper did. We built this really solid, beautiful friendship out of the ashes.

And his Aurora? Total angel. She clicked with the group instantly. She’s incredibly sweet, keeps Silas smiling, and loves Jasper like she carried him herself.

"Hey, I am not dramatic!" Lyra huffs, crossing her arms. "I just want the tea on this hot lawyer your mom’s hiding."

Jasper rolls his eyes, making a gagging face. "Okay, gross. I'm out. You good with the baby, or should I take him?"

"I've got him. Go play, kiddo."

He bolts toward Asher and the guys. Silas was amazing enough to rewrite our custody papers. Now we share Jasper fifty-fifty. Silas gets him during the week, and I get him for weekend fun.

"Alright, back to the hot guy," Lyra demands, snapping her fingers in front of my face.

"Do you ever shut up?" I laugh.

"Nope. Spill it."

I sigh, tickling Milo's chin. He lets out a tiny, squeaky giggle that makes me melt. "Seriously, there's nothing to tell. He’s just the new guy at the office."

"Uh-huh. You called him cute, which means you stared. Is he into you?"

"Maybe? He did ask me out yesterday," I murmur, distracted by Milo grabbing my finger.

"Oh my god! You said yes, right?" Lyra literally has her fingers crossed, staring at me like her life depends on it.

"Nope."

"Wait, what?!"

"I told him I'd let him know."

"Are you fucking kidding me, Aurora? Who says that?!" Lyra groans. I look up to find her staring at me like I just grew a third eye.

"I do. And I did. Look, I’m not rushing this. If it’s meant to be, it’ll happen naturally. I like him, he likes me, and that’s plenty for now." We lock eyes, a silent standoff. Finally, she rolls her eyes and lets out a massive sigh. "Fine. Whatever."

"Come on, let's go. I want a front-row seat when Frederick face-plants into his birthday cake." I push myself up, carrying Milo, and she falls into step right beside me.

Life didn't go according to my perfect little plan, but hey, that's how the game works. It shoves you onto a detour. A better one, if you're brave enough to take the turn. I'm happy. And honestly? That's all that matters.
