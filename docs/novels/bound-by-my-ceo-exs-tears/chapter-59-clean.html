Chapter 60

"Why the hell are you here?" I choke out through my tears.

He drops to his knees right in front of me, locking his eyes on mine with some intense, unreadable look.

"Aurora saw you at the store," he says softly, his thumb brushing a tear off my cheek. "She said you were totally losing it, and that you bought a bunch of pregnancy tests before bolting."

God, Aurora and her massive mouth. What did she think she was going to achieve by running to Declan with that?

"She shouldn't have said a damn word. It’s none of her business, and it’s sure as hell none of yours," I spit.

He doesn't even flinch. "Did you take them?"

I just nod, fresh tears blurring my vision.

"And?"

I keep my mouth shut. I can't bring myself to say the words out loud.

When I don't answer, his eyes scan the bathroom. They land on the plastic sticks sitting by the sink. He stands up, walks over, and picks them up. I should be pissed that he’s invading my privacy like this, but honestly? I can't even bring myself to care. I have way bigger things to freak out about.

He walks back, but instead of kneeling, he plops down on the floor right next to me.

"Congrats," he says, his voice cracking just a little. "You're gonna be an awesome mom."

I let my head fall onto his shoulder. "Am I? You never wanted another kid with me, even when I begged you for one. I always figured you just thought I was a crappy mother."

Declan always made me feel so damn insecure. But I knew the real reason. He was holding out hope that Aurora would come back to him. I just lied to myself because believing I was a bad mom was an easier pill to swallow than admitting he just didn't want a baby with me.

"I never thought you were a bad mom, Lyra. You're the fucking best. Anyone can see that just by looking at you and Asher." He pauses. "Can I be real with you for a second?"

I nod against his shoulder.

"I've always been jealous of the bond you have with him. Honestly, I still am," he admits.

My head snaps up in shock. "Seriously?"

I still can't wrap my head around the fact that Declan is sitting on a bathroom floor with me right now. The guy I knew wouldn't give a shit, let alone wipe away my tears.

"Yeah," he mumbles.

We sit there in silence. Soon, a heavy exhaustion washes over me. I don't even remember falling asleep or him carrying me to bed. The last thing I register before blacking out is the soft press of his lips against my forehead.

I wake up late the next afternoon. There's a plate of breakfast on my nightstand, probably ice-cold by now.

I drag myself out of bed and call my ob-gyn to book an appointment. After a quick shower, I throw on some clothes, but I still feel completely drained.

I ignore the food—my stomach is in knots anyway. I’m guessing Declan left it for me.

I get into my car, twist the key, and drive as slowly as humanly possible. I'm doing everything I can to stall. It takes me almost an hour to get to the private clinic. Taking a deep breath, I force myself out of the car and walk inside.

The waiting room is full of pregnant women and their husbands. It hits me right in the gut, flashing me back to when I was pregnant with Asher. Declan never showed up to a single appointment. I was honestly shocked when he insisted on being in the delivery room, considering how little he cared about me or the baby back then.

I shake the memories off, give the receptionist my name, and take a seat to wait.

"Willow," a voice calls out about forty-five minutes later.

I stand up and walk into Dr. Raven’s office.

"Good to see you, Lyra. What can I do for you today?" she asks, sitting down at her desk.

"I need an ultrasound," I say, twisting my fingers together. My heart is hammering against my ribs. "I took a home test yesterday and it was positive, but I just... I need to be sure."

"We can definitely do that. Why don't you lie down on the table while I get everything ready?" she says kindly.

I climb onto the table and stare up at the ceiling tiles, trying to calm my racing pulse. It’s not working.

"Shirt up, please."

My hands shake as I pull my top up. She squirts the cold gel onto my stomach and starts moving the wand around.

"Yep, you're definitely pregnant. Looks like you're about three months along," she says cheerfully.

Right on cue, the rapid, steady thumping of a heartbeat echoes through the room.

I blink back tears, gripping the hem of my shirt. She sounds so happy for me, but I feel completely empty.

Everything after that is a blur. She wipes the gel off and starts talking about prenatal vitamins and diet. I walk out of her office, grab my next appointment card and the printed ultrasound photos from the receptionist, and bolt out of there like the devil is on my heels.

I drive on autopilot, totally numb. I had been holding onto this tiny, stupid hope that the tests were wrong. False positives happen, right? I wanted that to be the case so badly.

But no. Here I am, driving around with the cold, hard proof that I'm carrying Adrian's baby sitting in my backseat.

I drive aimlessly for God knows how long until I finally pull over. I get out of the car and walk toward the edge of the cliff like a robot.

How am I supposed to be happy about this? I don't want this kid. I don't want a baby conceived out of lies and betrayal. A baby fathered by the exact same man who tried to end my life.

How am I ever going to look at this child and not feel a wave of resentment? I wanted to erase Adrian from my mind forever, but this baby will make sure I never do. This kid will be a permanent reminder of his betrayal.

All I have to do is take one step. Just one single step forward, and the pain stops. No more hurting, no more sadness, no more drowning in this dark hole. I’d finally be free.

A car door slams in the distance, but I don't look back.

"What the hell do you think you're doing, Lyra?" Declan's voice roars from behind me.

I stay frozen. The wind whips around us, pushing against my back, almost urging me to just take that final step.

"Lyra, please. Step back from the edge. Come to me," he pleads, his footsteps slowly getting closer.

But I don't move. I'm just so damn tired. Tired of crying. Tired of hurting. Tired of the endless, suffocating pain. I have absolutely nothing left to fight with. The ache is constant, slowly eating me alive and turning me into someone I don't even recognize.

"I can't do this anymore, Declan. I just want it to stop. I want to know what peace feels like, because I haven't had it since the day I was born. I'm empty. I can't fight," I sob, completely broken.

"Ending your life isn't the answer," he says, and before I can react, his arms wrap around me and yank me backward.

I had been so lost in my own head that I hadn't realized how close he’d gotten.

"Let go of me! Let me go right now! Let me just end this!" I scream, thrashing wildly in his grip, desperate for him to release me.

"Snap out of it, Lyra!" he yells back, holding onto me like a vice. "Think about Asher! Are you really going to leave him without a mother? Force him to grow up with the trauma of losing you? And what about your unborn child? Are you really so fucking selfish and cruel that you'd kill an innocent baby? Your own blood?"

He shakes me, his face hardening with every word.

The second he mentions Asher's name, the fight drains out of me. I freeze.

"Declan..."

"No! I don't care if you hate my guts for this, but I'm booking you a therapist. You're going to work through this, and you are going to be the best damn mother to your two kids. You will love them both unconditionally, no matter who their father is. Or so help me God, Lyra, I will..." He cuts himself off, but the threat hangs heavy in the air. I know exactly what he means.

He’s panting, his grey eyes wild and stormy. His grip on me is incredibly tight.

I just collapse against him. The fight is completely gone. He scoops me up bridal-style, holding me close against his chest. I can feel his heart hammering against his ribs.

The familiar scent of his cologne washes over me, and my mind finally starts to quiet down.

"How did you find me?" I whisper as he carries me toward his car.
