Chapter 323

"You got canned because you're a toxic nightmare," I snapped. "Stop pinning your own screw-ups on me."

"This is on you!" she shrieked. "If you hadn't crawled out of whatever hole you came from, I'd still have my job!"

My reflexes lagged. Before I could even blink, she lunged. Her hand connected with my face in a sharp, stinging slap. I reeled back, losing my footing for a split second before locking my knees. That was it. I’d let this psycho slide on a lot of crap, but a physical assault? Hell no.

Pure instinct took over. I balled my fist and swung, catching her square in the face. A double-header of screams pierced the air.

"Son of a bitch, that hurts!" I howled, clutching my knuckles.

"You hit me!" she wailed.

She hadn't braced for a counterattack. She hit the floor hard, hands instantly cupping her nose as blood leaked through her fingers. My hand throbbed like crazy, but damn, watching her squirm on the floor felt incredibly good.

"Willow!" Orion roared from somewhere behind us. I didn't look back. My eyes stayed glued to Bianca, waiting for round two.

Suddenly, a wall of white cotton blocked my view. Orion’s massive chest filled my vision.

"Are you hurt? God, I'm sorry, I should've gotten here faster," he panted, words tripping over each other. "I figured she'd just pack her bags and cry at home after the termination email."

I tilted my chin up to meet his gaze. His slate-grey eyes were swimming with pure panic and care, sending a sudden, stupidly warm rush straight to my chest. I blinked hard, trying to shake off the butterflies.

"I'm fine. I can handle my own battles," I muttered, cradling my throbbing hand against my ribs. Seriously, why did throwing a punch have to hurt the puncher so damn much?

Orion shifted, and that’s when I noticed the crowd. Everyone was staring. Of course. Bianca lived for the spotlight. Leaving quietly wasn't her style; she had to stage a whole cafeteria brawl. God, what an absolute psycho.

"Did you miss the part where you got fired?" Orion growled, turning his fury on Bianca as she scrambled to her feet. "You actually thought it was a good idea to trespass and attack my wife in my own building? Are you brain-dead or just begging for trouble?" His spine was a rigid line of pure rage, his tone dropping to a sub-zero chill. A shiver ran down my arms—he was terrifying.

"She broke my nose!" Bianca shrieked, clearly lacking any self-preservation instincts.

"Because you laid hands on her first!" Orion snapped back instantly.

I slipped behind his shoulder, enjoying the front-row seat to the show. Orion was completely commanding the room, and honestly? Watching him go full protective-alpha was doing things to my heart rate.

"Get security down here. Now!" he barked at the room. Half a dozen people immediately fumbled for their phones.

Within two minutes, two breathless guards burst through the doors.

"How the hell did she bypass the lobby?" Orion demanded. The guards traded panicked, clueless glances. Great. Bianca must have slipped past them like a rat in the shadows.

"Drag her out of here. If she so much as steps foot on this property again, you're both collecting unemployment," Orion ordered. His deep, authoritative voice sent a weird, electric jolt straight down my spine. A feeling I absolutely did not want to analyze right now.

Bianca started screeching and thrashing as the guards clamped onto her arms.

"And Bianca?" Orion’s voice cut through her hysterics like a knife. "Expect a call from my legal team. We're pressing charges for assault."

Her wails went up an octave as she kicked and fought, but the guards dragged her backward out of the cafeteria. Even after the doors swung shut, her muffled shrieks kept echoing down the hallway.

"Show's over. Back to your lunch," Orion commanded the room. The crowd instantly scattered, burying their faces back in their salads.

He grabbed my good hand, his grip firm but gentle, and pulled me toward the elevators. We stepped inside in total silence. Neither of us said a word as the elevator shot back up to the executive suites.

We paused right by Arthur's desk.

"What are you craving?" Orion asked, his eyes scanning my face for any scratches.

"Literally anything," I blurted out before I could filter myself. "I'm so starving I'd eat cardboard if it had salt on it."

A small smirk tugged at the corner of his mouth, and that stupid, warm feeling flared up in my chest again.

"Double my order and give it to her," Orion told Arthur, then steered me into his private office.

The moment the heavy door clicked shut, he let go of my hand and began pacing the floor like a caged predator.

"I am so incredibly sorry, Willow. I never thought she'd pull a stunt like this and target you," he said, his voice thick with guilt. I hated seeing him beat himself up over it. He gently nudged me down onto the leather sofa, then immediately went back to pacing.
