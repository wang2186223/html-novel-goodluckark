Chapter 178

"Hey, wake up. Dinner's ready." Declan's voice drags me out of sleep.

I was in the middle of some weird, fuzzy dream. Not a nightmare, just... confusing. The kind where you wake up squinting, trying to piece together blurry shapes.

"Aurora?" I mumble.

"Relax. She woke up, knocked back a bottle, and crashed right out again," he says, his eyes locking onto mine.

I just nod, kick off the blankets, and swing my legs out of bed. I arch my back, stretching until my spine pops in all the right places. Oh, God, that felt good.

"So, why are you crashing in here instead of our room?" Declan asks, his gaze drilling into me.

Boom. Reality hits me like a freight train. All the crap I've been dodging comes rushing back, reminding me exactly why I'm hiding out in this spare bed. My mood instantly tanks. The peace evaporates, leaving nothing but a sour taste in my mouth. Seriously, why the hell is he lying to me? What's with the secrets? Is this his idea of a fresh start? Building a life on a pile of bullshit?

"Don't start, Declan. Seriously. I am not in the fucking mood," I snap, shoving past his shoulder.

I'm a grown-ass woman, but right now? I want to slap him silly. After all the hell he's dragged me through, he still has the nerve to keep me in the dark. What is wrong with his head? Hasn't he ruined my life enough already?

I don't wait for him to say anything. I march straight to the dining room. Asher and Orion are already there, sitting down. I slide into my chair and start piling food onto my plate. A minute later, Declan slinks in and sits down.

"So, Lyra. How's it going?" Orion asks, trying to cut through the thick tension.

I blink at him. How do I even answer that? For one, I'm absolutely livid. Two, since when do we do small talk? Orion and I aren't buddies. We barely speak. This is just weird.

"Fine, I guess," I say, keeping it simple. "Just hate being cooped up. I'm not really a stay-at-home person."

I shove a forkful of steak into my mouth. Oh, man. It practically melts on my tongue. Pure heaven. Martha seriously knows how to work a kitchen.

A tiny pang of jealousy hits me. I used to be good at this. But the other day? I tried to whip up a new recipe for the family. Total disaster. Thanks to my brain scrambling numbers, I dumped two and a half tablespoons of sugar into the pot instead of a half. It was completely inedible. I cried in the bathroom for thirty minutes before pulling myself together. Martha had to bail us out with a backup meal, and we never spoke of the sugar incident again.

"You going back to the classroom?" Orion asks, snapping me out of my pity party.

I swallow my food. "That's the plan. But, yeah, with everything going on, it's not happening anytime soon."

Declan stays quiet, just staring at me. I can see the gears turning in his head. He's totally conflicted. I roll my eyes internally. Good. Let him sweat over whatever dirty little secrets he's keeping.

"Hey, Mom, what about Jasper?" Asher pipes up. "You said he'd come over last weekend, but you never called Silas."

My eyes dart to Declan. His face goes totally blank, like a brick wall. Interesting. We haven't talked about Silas at all. I have no clue if they've actually buried the hatchet for the kids' sake.

"Oh, buddy, I'm sorry. It slipped my mind," I say, softening my voice. "Tell you what. Your birthday is next week. Let's have him over for the party, and he can stay the night. Sound good?"

Asher throws his hands up, cheering. The whole table cracks a smile. "Yes! That's gonna be so awesome!"

"You can invite your whole class, too," I add. "We'll do a backyard party. Plenty of room out there." Asher's grin stretches from ear to ear.

Seeing him that happy makes all my stress melt away. This is why I do it. For him and Aurora. I don't care what kind of mess Declan and I are in. My kids come first. If they're smiling, I'm good.

"My friends are gonna lose it," Asher beams, then pauses, his face scrunching up. "Well, everyone except Piper. Don't invite her. She's a total party pooper."
