Chapter 46

My blood was still boiling when I pulled up to the new place. "Home." Yeah, right. That word felt totally foreign. I killed the engine, hopped out of the truck, and froze. No freaking way. Declan was sitting right there on my porch. I marched over, throwing him my nastiest glare.

"If you're here to lecture me about Aurora, turn right back around," I snapped, pointing a finger at his shiny black Aston Martin. "Get in your car and get lost." Seriously, if he came here to start drama, I was going to launch him into orbit.

Declan stood up, looking genuinely lost. "What the hell are you talking about?"

"Oh, please. That little psycho definitely called you to whine and lie, didn't she?" I tapped my boot, waiting for his confession. The timing was too perfect. I just had a massive blowout with Aurora, and now here he was.

He ran a hand through his messy hair, sighing. "I have zero clue what kind of crap you're talking about. Whatever drama you two have, I'm not part of it."

"Then why are you lurking on my porch?" I demanded.

He took a step toward me, and I instinctively backed up. "Corbin called. Said you needed muscle to move furniture."

Hearing that name made my blood pressure spike again. God, those two. Seriously, how did we share the same DNA? "Don't you dare say his name to me," I hissed.

Corbin had known Asher his entire life. Yet he just stood there like a statue while Aurora threatened my kid? And then he had the nerve to lecture me for losing my temper? Unbelievable.

"What did Corbin do?" Declan asked.

"He watched Aurora threaten Asher and did nothing. Then he bitched at me because I almost took her head off."

I braced myself. Usually, this was the part where he'd scream at me and defend her. But he didn't. Total shocker.

"She did *what*?" Declan roared. No joke, I felt the ground vibrate under my feet.

I gulped, totally thrown off. I was ready for a shouting match, not this. "Look, I tried to walk away," I stammered. "But she blocked me. She said she'd make Asher pay because he's the reason she lost you."

I shrank back. Declan's eyes turned pitch black, practically sparking with pure rage. The guy looked ready to commit murder. I'd never seen him look at Aurora with that kind of lethal hatred before.

"If she lays a single finger on him, she's dead," he growled, his voice dropping an octave. Holy crap. My jaw dropped. He wasn't just talking tough; he meant every single word. If Aurora touched Asher, Declan would literally put her in the ground.

"Whoa, okay, chill," I said, trying to de-escalate. "I threatened her pretty good. She won't even look his way." I'd seen the sheer terror in her eyes. She knew she'd crossed a major line, one that carried heavy consequences. Any sisterly bond we had died years ago. She knew I'd tear her apart if she touched my boy, and she believed me.

Declan tried to take a breath, but his anger was a runaway train. The guy had a legendary temper, and keeping a lid on it was always a struggle. "Listen..." I started, but his gaze suddenly shifted past my shoulder. His eyes went totally cold, his rage spiking tenfold. Then, I heard a car door slam behind me.

I pinched the bridge of my nose and groaned. Adrian. Great. He loathed Declan, and Declan despised him right back.

"What the hell is he doing here?" they barked in unison.

I stepped back to look at the two of them. They were locked in a death stare, jaws clenched, fists tight, looking like they wanted to rip each other's throats out.

"Well, since you both showed up to help me move, how about we actually do that?" I didn't wait for their attitude. I turned, walked to the truck, and popped the tailgate. Look, six hands are better than four. Some of this stuff was heavy as hell, and having two big guys carry it was way better than me and Adrian struggling.

"Are you guys going to keep having a dick-measuring contest, or are you going to help?" I yelled when they didn't budge.

Declan grunted and stomped over. Adrian was right on his heels.

"Alright, what are we grabbing first?" I muttered. Still, they just stood there. Seriously, they were testing my last nerve. Neither was going to leave, but neither was actually working. God, I should've just hired movers.

Finally, Adrian grabbed one end of the couch. Declan ground his teeth, then grabbed the other. They hauled it into the living room without a word. I grabbed some lighter boxes and followed them inside. The silence was deafening. I tried to make small talk, but they both just scowled. Total buzzkills.

About half an hour later, the heavy lifting was mostly done. I was grabbing some throw pillows from the truck when a massive crash echoed from inside. I sprinted back in. Oh, great. Adrian and Declan were rolling around on the floor, throwing wild punches.

"Stay the fuck away from her!" Declan roared, planting a solid fist right into Adrian's jaw.

I just stood there, completely dumbfounded. Seriously? They were acting like toddlers fighting over a plastic shovel. Not that they were fighting over me—Declan would never do that.

"Like hell I will!" Adrian yelled, slamming a hard punch into Declan's ribs. "You had your shot and you blew it, asshole!"

"Stop it! Both of you, stop!" I screamed. They completely ignored me, knocking over my stuff and wrecking the place. I dropped the pillows, marched into the kitchen, grabbed a big bowl of cold water, and dumped it right over their heads.

They froze, sputtering, and stared up at me like I was the crazy one.

"What is wrong with you two?!" I yelled, absolutely livid. "You're wrecking my damn house!"

"He threw the first punch," Adrian muttered, sounding like a whiny five-year-old.

"I don't give a shit who started it!" I took a deep breath, then glared at Declan. "What is your deal today? Seriously, what is your problem?"

His eyes flared. "My problem is that you're hooking up with this loser!"

That threw me for a loop. Why the hell did he care who I was with? I crossed my arms. "Who I date is none of your business, Declan. Honestly, shouldn't you be thrilled? He's keeping me busy so I'm out of your hair."

"If it were anyone else, I wouldn't give a shit," Declan snapped. "But this guy? He's a whole different story."

"Stop calling him names!" I yelled. Adrian didn't deserve this trash talk from my ex. It was totally out of line.

"Why are you so blind?" Declan sneered. "He's fake as hell. Open your eyes and look at him! Are you really that desperate for a guy to love you? Are you that pathetic that you're ignoring what's right in front of your face?"

His words cut deep, like glass slicing right through me. My hands balled into tight fists. I wanted to punch him so bad. "Get the fuck out of my house, Declan. I'm not letting you disrespect me or Adrian anymore."

He glared back, eyes full of venom. "Fine. If you want to be stupid, I'm out. I'm not staying here to watch you drool over some fake prick who's just using you."

He spun around, but stopped at the threshold for one last shot. "Mark my words, Lyra. That bastard is playing you. He's going to ruin you, and when he does, don't come crying to me. You brought this on yourself."

He slammed the door so hard the walls shook.

He was wrong. I didn't know what his deal was, but he was dead wrong. Adrian would never hurt me. Unlike Declan, Adrian actually gave a shit about me.
