NovelVibe

Chapter 10

Seven days. That’s how long Asher’s been gone, and honestly? I’m a total mess. We’ve never been apart this long, and I’m not even going to pretend I’m holding it together. Without him, I’m just a ghost floating through my own life, totally lost. He’s my anchor. Every single day, I just stare at my phone, waiting for it to buzz. Hearing his voice is the only thing keeping me from spinning completely out of control.

As for Declan? Radio silence since the airport. Sure, a stupid part of me still aches for him, but let’s be real—it’s better this way. We were a dead end. You can’t build a life with a guy who looks right through you. Lately, things have been dead quiet. Not like anyone’s giving me updates anyway. No gunfights, no body bags. I guess the bad guys finally crawled back into their holes.

*Bam.* I slam right into a solid chest, snapping out of my head.

"Oh my god, I'm so sorry! Totally zoned out," I gasp, immediately dropping to grab my books. I’m walking home from work, completely exhausted, just craving my bed.

"Hey, don't sweat it. I wasn't looking either," a voice says.

That voice. I freeze, my head whipping up. Yep. Adrian.

He kneels down, helping me scoop up the papers, and we stand up together. He flashes this killer smile, and damn, I can't help but smile back.

"What are you doing around here?" I ask.

He’s in his full police uniform, and holy hell, he wears it well. Sure, I’m officially done with men, but a girl can still appreciate the view. And he is definitely a view.

"Got a call about some scumbag dealing to kids nearby," he says, pausing to look at me. He cocks an eyebrow. "Let me guess. You teach here?"

"Yeah, I do," I mumble, suddenly super self-conscious.

I get it—billionaires' wives don't usually grade papers for a living. But I love my job. My parents, of course, hated it. They wanted me to have some fancy-ass career like my sister Aurora, the hotshot lawyer, or Corbin, the big-shot entrepreneur. Just another reason for them to look down on me.

"So, what's your subject?" Adrian asks. He actually looks like he wants to know.

Declan never asked about my work. Not once. Pretty sure he didn't even know what I taught.

"Biology," I say.

"Ah. Brains and beauty. My favorite combo," he says, throwing me a wink.

My face goes hot. "Uh, thanks," I mumble, tucking a stray strand of hair behind my ear.

He’s totally throwing me off. I’m just not used to hot guys noticing me. Usually, I’m invisible. Next to Aurora—who’s basically a walking, talking blonde goddess—I’m just a plain little mouse. Brown hair, brown eyes, tiny frame. Nothing special. I learned to accept that early on because everyone made sure to remind me. Back in school, guys only talked to me to get to my sister. It used to make me furious, but eventually, I just accepted the harsh truth: I'm just not the kind of girl guys want.

"Hey, sorry I haven't called," he says after a beat. "Work's been crazy. Zero free time."

I force a smile. "No worries, I get it. Being a cop is a 24/7 gig."

But I knew the truth. It was just a polite excuse. He had zero intention of calling me when he walked out my door last week.

An awkward silence settles over us. I shift my weight, suddenly super uncomfortable. He just stares, his blue eyes drilling right into me. I look down, unable to hold his gaze.

"Adrian!" another cop yells from down the street, waving him over.

"On my way!" Adrian yells back. He turns to me, giving me a soft look. "Great bumping into you, gorgeous. See you around?"

"Yeah, see ya," I mumble.

Before I can react, he pulls me into a quick, tight hug and walks off. I just stand there, totally dazed. Like, what just happened?

I shake it off and start walking. I need groceries, and since the market is close, I just go on foot. My arm is finally out of the sling. My shoulder still throbs a bit, but it works. I try to focus on my shopping list, but my mind keeps drifting back to Adrian. The way he looks at me... it's the exact opposite of Declan. I don't even know how to process it. Nobody has ever called me beautiful. No guy has ever winked at me. For a second, Adrian made me feel like I actually had some appeal. But let's be real. If my own husband didn't want to touch me, why would any other guy?

*Get a grip,* I tell myself, letting out a dry laugh. He was just being nice. A guy that hot has his pick of actual supermodels. Why would he look at me? I shake my head, shutting down the fantasy. No point in lying to myself about what I see in the mirror.

I grab a few things at the store—just the basics, since it's only me. I pay, grab my bags, and head out. I didn't drive today because my shoulder was too stiff to handle the steering wheel. I raise my hand to hail a cab, and then my heart stops.

There they are. Declan and Aurora.

Walking down the sidewalk, fingers laced together. I don't know where they're going, and honestly, it doesn't matter. Aurora whispers something, and Declan throws his head back, laughing. A real, deep, happy laugh.

That old, familiar ache rips right through my chest. God, it hurts. He never, ever laughed like that with me. If he had just given me a single shot, I would have done anything to make him happy. But he was too busy keeping Aurora locked inside his heart. It fucking hurts. I thought I was over this, but nope. My chest feels like it's cracking open all over again, and I'm drowning in it.

Suddenly, he stops. It’s like he feels me watching him. He turns, his laugh dying instantly. The smile vanishes, replaced by a dark scowl. Figures. I guess I’m just a dark cloud to him. No wonder he never smiled when I was around. He starts to turn his body toward me, like he’s about to cross the street.

*Yeah, right.* Just my pathetic brain playing tricks on me.

I look away, yank open the door of a yellow cab, and slide inside. "Just drive," I tell the driver. I don't look back. I refuse to watch them. He’s moved on. So damn fast. But hey, when you never loved someone in the first place, letting go is easy, right? It’s time for me to do the same. I don't care how hard it is, I'm going to find my own damn happiness. Declan is history. Period.
