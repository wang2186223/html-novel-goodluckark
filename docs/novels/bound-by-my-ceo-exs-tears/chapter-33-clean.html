NovelVibe

Chapter 33

My phone buzzes. Again. That’s like, what, the hundredth time today? Piper’s name lights up the screen. I slide it face down. Same as before. She’s been blowing up my phone since yesterday, but my head is a mess. Talking to her means dealing with the real world, with *them*. I can't do it. I'm stuck.

"Hit me again," I tell the guy behind the bar the second the buzzing stops.

Happy birthday to me. This is the big celebration: sitting solo at a sticky counter, sipping some neon, sugary garbage, still reeling from the venom Declan spat at me.

I try to block it out. Really, I do. But his words are stuck. They’re like a cheap tattoo burned right into my skull. Nine years of marriage, and I never knew. He thought I was just a cheap hookup. A warm body to stand in for Aurora. My chest has been a hollow ache since that afternoon at my place.

Of course he bought Aurora's sob story. Why wouldn't he? That’s classic Declan—trusting literally anyone over the woman who shared his bed for nearly a decade. People say physical hits hurt worse. They lie. Declan completely shattered me this time.

"Here you go," the bartender says, sliding the glass over.

He gives me this look. Pity. He knows I’m here to drown some serious demons. He probably watches girls like me do this every single weekend. I grab the drink, keeping my eyes on the counter. Keep your sympathy, dude. Just give me a lobotomy. A fresh heart. A soul that Declan didn't drag through the mud.

If I had a crystal ball back then, I would’ve run. Fast. If I knew loving him meant ending up like this, I would’ve boarded a flight to another country—no, another planet. I want to go back in time and slap some sense into my younger self. Maybe then I wouldn't be in this mess.

I take a sip, staring at the neon signs. My brain is totally offline. Not wasted yet, but the warm buzz is creeping in. Good. That’s the goal. I just need a timeout from the agony. Getting trashed is my only escape, even if it’s just for a couple of hours.

I slam the rest of the drink down. Over on the floor, the crowd is moving. God, when was the last time I actually danced? I need to let go. Screw it, it’s my birthday.

I push off the stool and head into the crowd. I close my eyes, letting the bass thump through my chest. I sway. I move. Slowly, the noise in my head starts to quiet down. Right now, I can play pretend. I’m not a walking ghost. I’m whole.

I lose track of the songs. Just chasing that sweet numbness. Guys press up against my back, grinding to the beat, but I don't even open my eyes. A few try to hit on me, whispering in my ear to come with them, but I shake them off. Eventually, they get the hint and beat it.

My legs start to feel like lead. I stop, blink against the flashing lights, and weave my way back to the bar. I slide onto a stool and order another round. Right on cue, my phone starts vibrating again.

I reach to decline it, expecting Piper's name, but stop. Adrian. His caller ID is flashing.

I swipe to answer.

"Hey, Adrian," I say. My voice sounds weirdly high and squeaky. Ugh.

"Lyra? Where are you? Are you okay?" He sounds frantic. "Piper literally woke me up screaming. She’s losing her mind because you’ve been completely off the grid since yesterday."

I blink, wondering how she even got his number. Oh, right. I gave it to her on our first date. She insisted on a backup contact just in case I went missing.

"I'm fine! I just... I don't want to talk to her right now," I yell over the noise. The music isn't deafening, but it's definitely competing with my voice.

"Are you at a club or something?" he asks. Right then, some girl nearby shrieks, "Oh my god, I love this song!"

"Yeah, basically."

"Are you drunk?"

"Just a little buzzed," I lie. My actual plan is to drink until I forget my own name.

"Do you have a ride home?"

I let out a little giggle. There’s the cop. I kind of like it, though. It’s nice having someone actually worry about me. "No, but I'll just call an Uber."

"Absolutely not. Stay put. Ten minutes," he says, and the line goes dead.

I stare at the screen, totally confused. Whatever. I toss the phone aside. Tonight is about turning my brain off.

I lose track of time, but suddenly, someone slides onto the stool next to mine. I look up. Blue eyes. Adrian.

"Adrian? What... how are you here?" I stammer.

"I told you, ten minutes," he says, giving me a look. "Don't you remember our call?"

I just stare at him. He feels like a hallucination. How did he even track me down? "I mean, yeah, I remember. I just didn't think you were serious."

He watches me, and I watch him back. Look, I like the guy. I really do. But I didn't want him here tonight. Tonight was supposed to be my solo pity party.

"What's going on, Lyra? You're not the type to hit the bars late at night. Especially on a Tuesday. Don't you have school tomorrow?" He frowns, his forehead creasing with genuine worry.

No school. Not after the Declan disaster. I couldn't face it. I'd already called the board and begged for an extension on my leave, lying that I was still sick. They bought it.

"It's my birthday," I mutter instead. The DJ has finally killed the music, so I don't have to shout anymore. Most of the crowd is filtering out.

"By yourself? At a bar? At two in the morning?"

How do I explain that I have literally nobody? That my birthday is just another day to everyone else? Even when I was with Declan, he'd conveniently forget or just ignore it. How do I tell him my own flesh and blood hates me so much they stopped celebrating the day I was born nine years ago?

I shrug, looking down. "Nobody to celebrate with. Asher's away, obviously. And the rest of my family? They hate my guts. They couldn't care less that I'm a year older."

He looks stunned for a second, then his expression softens. He hops off his stool and gently pulls me down by my hand. Without a word, he guides me over to a dark, quiet booth, sliding in right next to me.

He turns to face me. "Why would you say that? I know something's up with your family. It's written all over you." He hesitates, then presses on. "Is this about Declan? What did he do? I see how much you're hurting, Lyra. Why do you think your family hates you?" The questions hit me like rapid-fire.

I look at him. If I'm going to try with Adrian, I have to be honest. This might completely ruin how he sees me, but he deserves the truth.

I take a shaky breath. "Because when I was eighteen, I slept with Aurora's boyfriend. And I got pregnant."
