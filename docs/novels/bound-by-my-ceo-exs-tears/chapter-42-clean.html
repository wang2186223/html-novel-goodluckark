NovelVibe

Chapter 42

Aurora stood up and slipped out. I watched her go. Normally, I wouldn't care. But she left right after Lyra. That set off alarms in my head.

My gut screamed at me to follow. Lyra’s words about Aurora were still spinning in my brain, driving me crazy. I needed the damn truth. Aurora had been acting weird anyway.

She’d been so hyped for this party. Now? Total buzzkill. I’d bet my whole damn company it was because Lyra was running the show. Lyra wasn't some broke loser after all.

Everyone else was having a blast. Orion was literally on the dance floor spinning some girls around. Corbin looked miserable watching Piper, but he was holding it together, especially when Piper sat down at our table.

I pushed my chair back and stood up. The guys stared. I didn't say a word. Just walked out.

Outside, there they were. Nose to nose.

They didn't even notice me. Too locked in.

"Oh, so you think you're hot shit now because of some charity?" Aurora sneered.

Lyra let out this long, exhausted sigh. "I don't have the bandwidth for your drama, Aurora. Move."

Looking at them, it hit me. A cold realization. I’d been dead wrong. My whole life lately was a giant lie.

"You're a nobody, Lyra," Aurora spat. "Money doesn't change a thing. We all hate you. Declan hates you most."

Lyra didn't even flinch. "Done? Because you've been playing the same broken record since you got back." She paused, eyes cold. "Now get out of my way. I'm over this."

She tried to brush past, but Aurora lunged and grabbed her wrist. Hard. That was going to leave a mark. I took a step forward, but Lyra yanked herself free.

"Declan is mine," Aurora hissed. "Back off. Don't think you're on his level."

Aurora's back was to me, so I couldn't see her face. But Lyra? She was reaching her limit.

She threw her hands up. "How many times do I have to say it? I don't want Declan. Get it through your thick skull. He's yours. Take him. I don't give a shit."

That stung. Something heavy settled in my chest.

"Liar!" Aurora yelled. "I saw him follow you to the balcony. You wrecked us once. I won't let you do it again. I'll ruin you first."

Lyra scoffed. "Is that why you lied to him? Playing the victim while making me the bad guy? Did you tell him you came to my house to start shit both times you claimed I attacked you?"

My hands balled into fists. What? Aurora told me Lyra came after her. I’d been a total idiot. I actually believed her.

"Who cares?" Aurora laughed, sounding proud. "He only knows what I tell him. Don't you get it, Lyra? Declan will always choose me. He loves me. You're nothing to him."

I squeezed my eyes shut. God, it hurt. Lyra was right. How many times had I blamed her for shit she didn't do? How many times had I punished her based on a lie?

The guilt was a physical weight, crushing me. I hated myself for it.

Lyra laughed, a dry, sharp sound. I opened my eyes.

"It's pathetic," Lyra said. "The lengths you go to keep him. You say you love him, but you just lie and manipulate. That's not love. His might be real, but yours? It's toxic. You wouldn't play this dirty if you actually cared."

Aurora opened her mouth, but Lyra cut her off.

"What happens when Declan finds out? When he hears what you said about Asher?"

My heart stopped. Asher. My kid. Nobody talks shit about my son. Nobody.

"He'll never find out," Aurora smirked. "He believes whatever lies I feed him. That's how much he trusts me."

"A trust you trash daily." Lyra sighed. "Look, I despise Declan. I'd push him off a cliff myself. But he doesn't deserve to be played by the woman he's loved for years. It's messed up."

She tried to walk away again. Aurora grabbed her arm. Again.

"Let go," Lyra warned, her voice dropping an octave. "Or my security will throw you out just like Ingrid and Brenda."

I stepped out of the dark. Time to end this.

"Won't be necessary," I said. "I've got this."

They both spun around. Lyra ripped her arm away and walked off without a backward glance. Aurora just stood there, pale, looking like a deer in headlights.

"How... how long have you been there?" she whispered, shaking.

"Long enough to know you're a liar," I growled, pure rage bubbling up. "Spit it out. The truth. Right now. Lie to me again, Aurora, and I swear to God you'll regret it."

She swallowed hard, nodding slowly.

"Did Lyra slap you two weeks ago?" I demanded, my jaw locking.

"Yes..."

"Why?"

Silence. She stared at her shoes. Funny how she had so much to say to Lyra, but now she was mute.

"Answer me!" I roared.

She needed to realize I wasn't some dumb kid anymore. I wasn't going to blind myself to her bullshit just because I loved her.

"B-because..." She choked up, tears welling in her eyes.

"I'm not waiting," I snapped. "Tell me, or I go ask Lyra. And you don't want that. I want it from you."

She swallowed again, trembling, shrinking back. I didn't care.

"I... I told her to keep Asher on a leash," she sobbed out. "That I wouldn't let him ruin us. I said I'd make you send the brat to boarding school to get him out of our hair."

I took a step back, trying to breathe. I couldn't believe it. I had defended her. I went to Lyra's house and screamed at her for this woman. And it was all a lie.

The only real part was the slap. And Lyra only did it to protect Asher. Just like I would have.

Where did it go wrong? Aurora used to be sweet. The kindest girl. Not a mean bone in her. Everyone loved her.

This woman? She was a stranger. Bitter, cruel, snake-like.

"We're done," I said, the words tasting like ash.

"W-what?" she gasped.

"Our history is the only reason I'm not ruining you right now. But nobody messes with my kid. Nobody."

"Declan, please!" she cried, reaching for me. "This was our second chance!"

She threw herself at me, but I peeled her hands off and pushed her away.

"Guess not," I said, turning on my heel.

She sobbed, screaming my name, but I kept walking. Her tears didn't do it for me anymore.

This was the right call. Not just because of what she did, but because my head was a total mess.

I didn't want to hurt her, but it was bound to happen. I couldn't pretend to love her when my ex-wife was living rent-free in my head. I couldn't be with her when the mere thought of Lyra with another guy drove me absolutely insane.

I thought I was over Lyra. But what if I was totally, completely wrong?
