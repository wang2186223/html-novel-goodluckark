NovelVibe

Chapter 172

My insides are on fire. Literally vibrating. My heart is slamming against my ribs, and my stomach is doing gymnastics.

I stare at the gray prison concrete. Do I go in? Do I run? I can't remember if I’ve ever been here to see Adrian. Honestly, it doesn't matter. Right now, walking in there feels like meeting a total stranger.

"You gonna stand there staring at the brick all day, or actually walk in? Some of us have actual work to do," the gate guard sneers. Total prick.

I squeeze Aurora closer to my chest and shoot him a death glare.

Okay, I get it, but seriously? No need to be a dick. Besides, his job is literally to stand there. I'm not wasting his precious time.

"Excuse me? What did you just say?" I snap.

If there’s one thing that pisses me off, it’s unprovoked attitude.

He just rolls his eyes. Oh, great. Now I want to slap the smirk right off his face.

"You heard me. Pretty sure you aren't deaf. Why are you even here? Looking for a fix? Or maybe you're selling something else? If you're hooking, name your price. I'm down for a quick ride."

Wow. A hooker? Seriously? Look, no shade to sex workers, do your thing, but having this asshole assume that about me? Pure disrespect.

God, what a crappy start to the day. I was already a ball of nerves, but I was actually excited to see my baby's dad. Now this arrogant jerk has completely ruined the vibe.

"Do you have any damn clue who I am?" I step right into his personal space, growling.

I haven't even crossed the threshold yet, and he’s already treating me like garbage. Is there some law saying I can't take a second to breathe before going inside?

He smirks, his lip curling. "Let me guess. The Queen of Sheba?"

I let out a cold, nasty laugh. "I'm Willow."

His face goes blank. Then, boom. The realization hits him like a truck. The color drains from his skin, and suddenly he looks about eighty years old.

Honestly, it was a total bluff. I didn't know if it would work. My name used to mean jack shit. But things are different now. Maybe it's Declan's influence, or my parents, or even Adrian. Whatever it is, I'll take the win.

"Ma'am, I—I'm so sorry. I didn't realize," he stammers.

I don't buy it. "Save it," I spit. "You decided to be a jerk for no reason and threw some disgusting insults my way."

"It won't happen again, I swear."

I scoff. Look at him change his tune the second he realizes I have power. Would he care if I was just some random girl? Hell no. He isn't sorry.

"You're damn right it won't," I say, stepping past him. "Because your boss is going to hear all about this." I don't even look back as I cross the threshold.

Will I actually report him? Who knows. But I'm not letting him off easy. People need to learn.

The fight actually distracted me from my panic. But now, staring at the massive doors marked 'VISITORS,' the anxiety slams back into me tenfold.

I walk inside. The front desk asks for my ID and who I'm here to see. My chest is pounding as I give them the details. Turns out, the system says I've been here once before.

They hand me a visitor's pass and lead me through.

"Take a seat at that table. I'll go grab him," another guard says.

I sit. Aurora is babbling, looking around. Usually, her little baby noises soothe me, but right now? Nothing is working. I'm going to be a wreck until I see him.

A few minutes pass, and then a guard leads a guy out in handcuffs.

Suddenly, a flash hits me. A memory. Or maybe a dream? A guy is standing outside some random house, holding a bunch of flowers. He hands them to me. I smile, leaning in to kiss his lips.

The vision vanishes as fast as it came, leaving me gasping for air and totally thrown off. But the guy in that memory? It's the exact same guy walking toward me in cuffs. Adrian. No doubt about it.

I watch him sit down across from me. He holds his wrists out to the guard, who pops the cuffs off. Then, he looks up. His eyes are this piercing blue—the exact same shade as Aurora's.

"Hello, Lyra."
