NovelVibe

Chapter 74

That weekend was pure gold. Nothing crazy happened, but just hanging out with Beatrice and Edmund felt right. They actually did the whole "parent" thing right. Warm. Present. Caring. Total opposite of my actual birth parents, who only had eyes for Aurora and Corbin. Hell, they even treated Declan and Orion like royalty while treating me like some stray, despite the whole "she's our daughter" lie. The more I hang with Beatrice and Edmund, the more they feel like my real family. I get why Adrian worshipped them now. He always talked about them like they hung the moon. They really are the best. No contest.

"Seriously, where the hell is she?" Audrey snapped, tapping her fingers.

We were sitting in our usual booth, waiting on Piper. Classic Piper. The girl literally works as a secretary but can't read a clock to save her life.

"She'll show. Probably got caught in traffic," I mumbled around a mouthful of blueberry muffin.

My pregnancy cravings were officially out of control. It was driving me insane. Right now, my diet consisted of muffins and cupcakes for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Everything else tasted like straight cardboard.

"We said two o'clock sharp," Audrey grumbled, her perfect face twisting into a scowl.

I snorted. "Look, Piper is never on time. Accept it now, and save yourself the ulcer."

Audrey just sat there, steaming. She's a total neat freak about time—the type who shows up an hour early just to be safe. I ignored her and kept stuffing my face. I was starving. Constantly.

With Asher, I couldn't keep a single thing down. No morning sickness meds worked. I was a walking ghost until month six. That's why Declan's cold shoulder cut so deep back then. I was literally starving, barely holding on, and he was too busy moping over Aurora to notice. He didn't care that I was wasting away or that the pregnancy was high-risk. I ended up in the ER multiple times because the doctor was terrified for me and the baby. Sure, his heart was broken over Aurora, but seriously? Your own unborn kid's health doesn't top your heartbreak?

I shook off the bad memories and looked at Audrey. Her foot was drumming a mile a minute.

"Okay, what's up?" I asked.

"I have this business idea for us," she said, leaning in. "It's been bouncing around my head, driving me crazy. I wanted to wait for Piper, but I can't."

"Shoot," I said.

"It's going to sound wild. Definitely not the kind of high-society business our families would approve of."

Now she had my attention. "Go on."

"I want us to start a sex toy line," she said, totally deadpan.

I choked on my muffin, coughing hard. "Wait, what?" I gasped.

"A pleasure product company," she repeated. "Think about it. The market is booming. I bet seventy percent of women own at least one vibe, single or not. Even guys are buying them to spice things up."

I just stared at her, my jaw hitting the floor.

"Whoa, why does Lyra look like she saw a ghost?" Piper asked, sliding into the booth.

I hadn't even seen her walk in. That's how stunned I was. Piper looked between the two of us, trying to read the room.

"I just suggested we launch a sex toy brand," Audrey said.

I waited for Piper to freak out with me. Instead, she squealed. "Oh my god, yes! That is brilliant!"

"Are you guys losing your minds?" I asked, but they completely ignored me.

"It's a goldmine," Audrey insisted. "Seriously, what girl doesn't have a little helper in her drawer?"

"Me," I said.

They both stopped and stared at me like I had grown a second head.

"Wait, seriously? None?" Piper gasped.

"Nope. Never owned one."

"Oh, honey, you are missing out," Audrey said.

Truth was, I'd thought about it once. But my sex life with Declan was never at a place where I could just bring that up. For him, sex was just a quick release. For me, it was a desperate attempt to feel some kind of connection we lacked everywhere else.

"We are doing this," Piper declared. "Audrey's right, it's a cash cow. We just need a killer angle to stand out."

"Perfect. I'll draft the business plan and email it over once it's ready," Audrey said, finally relaxing.

"Hold on a second! I didn't say yes to this!" I protested.

Piper totally tuned me out. "We need market research. Since Lyra is a total newbie, she should buy the top-rated toys and test them. Her fresh perspective will tell us exactly what's missing in the market."

Audrey grinned, loving the idea. I, on the other hand, felt like a glorified guinea pig.

"Do I get a vote here?" I snapped.

"Nope," Piper chirped. "We're going to make you filthy rich."

"I'm already loaded, guys."

Piper shrugged. "Whatever. Didn't you say you wanted a new challenge? Something to keep you busy without wearing you out? Well, here it is."

I stared at them, then let out a long sigh. How bad could it really be? "Fine," I muttered.

They both squealed, throwing their arms around me and squeezing the air out of my lungs. Seeing them this hyped actually got my own adrenaline pumping. It had been a minute since I felt excited about anything. Maybe this was exactly what my soul needed.

"Okay, business aside," Piper said as we pulled apart. "I have a question."

I raised an eyebrow. "Shoot."

"Who's Silas?"

I blinked. I hadn't mentioned Silas to anyone. Plus, I hadn't even seen him since that first day. Jasper came over almost daily, but his dad was a ghost.

"He's my new neighbor. How do you even know his name?"

"Corbin told me," Piper said.

I frowned, completely lost. "Wait, how does Corbin know him?"

"Well, apparently at the family dinner, Asher brought him up. Actually, he did more than that. He told Declan that Silas makes you happy, and that he wouldn't mind if you two got hitched and gave him some new siblings. Corbin said Declan absolutely lost his shit."

"No way," Audrey gasped, her eyes wide.

"Yep," Piper grinned. "Corbin said he looked so pissed he almost snapped the dining table in half."

I burst out laughing. I laughed so hard my stomach actually cramped. It was hilarious. The idea of Asher playing matchmaker to make his dad mad was wild. But Declan being jealous? That was pure comedy. If there was one guy who wouldn't care if I slept with the entire city, it was Declan.

"Okay, very funny. What's the punchline?" I asked, wiping a tear. It had to be a joke. The only woman Declan ever got possessive over was Aurora. Not me. Never me.

"I'm serious," Piper insisted. "Even Corbin is starting to wonder what Declan is actually feeling."

"Why is that so hard to believe?" Audrey asked.

"Because I know the guy," I said. "He's not jealous. He's just mad that Asher keeps trying to push us back together." I turned to Piper. "Tell Corbin to chill. There is nothing going on. When his sister came back, he literally begged me not to ruin her happy ending with Declan. And I haven't."

"I don't think that's it," Piper whispered. "I think Corbin is realizing he misjudged the whole situation. He thinks Declan actually has feelings for you."

I remembered Corbin's warning at the hospital. He was so terrified I'd ruin Aurora's perfect life.

"The only thing Declan Thorne feels for me is pure hate," I said flatly. "You guys are delusional. You don't despise someone for nine years and then suddenly fall in love. That's not how real life works."

"It is if those feelings were always there, just buried deep down," Audrey argued.

I shook my head, irritation bubbling up.

"Lyra—"

"No, Piper. Drop the fucking subject," I snapped. "I don't want to hear his name. Even if he magically caught feelings—which is total BS—it's way too late. Period."

Piper shut her mouth. Audrey stared down at her hands. The fun vibe was officially dead. Now I just wanted to go home and wash off all this Declan drama. Why couldn't they just let it go? Why push a fantasy that didn't exist? They were out of their minds. I knew Declan. He only had resentment for me.

I shook my head to clear the noise. I wasn't going to waste my energy on this. My focus was on my kids, my foundation, and this crazy new business venture. Declan was in the rearview mirror. And that's exactly where he was staying.
