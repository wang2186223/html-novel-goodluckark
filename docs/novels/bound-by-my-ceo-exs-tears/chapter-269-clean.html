Chapter 275

"Move it, Willow. You're burning my time," Orion barked.

I snapped my head up. His forehead was a knot of pure irritation. I let out a heavy breath and popped the door open. Classic Orion. Cold, cocky, and a total prick.

I smoothed my skirt, grabbed my purse, and stepped out. He took off. I trailed behind like some sacrificial lamb. My chest was pounding so hard I thought my ribs would crack.

I was walking straight into his kingdom. His turf. It sucked, honestly. Knowing he pulled every single string in this place made my skin crawl.

Orion jabbed the elevator button. The doors slid open. We stepped inside, and I tried to force my lungs to take a normal breath.

"Family only," he said, staring straight ahead. "Takes us right to the executive suites. I'll get your biometrics in the system so you don't have to deal with the lobby crowd."

Jesus. Talk about a power move. My family had cash, sure, but we didn't have a VIP elevator. Even our CEO rode with the interns. "Fine," I muttered.

"I'm setting you up with a personal ride and a driver," he went on, whipping out his phone. "I pull crazy hours, but you've got Clara Sterling to worry about. You're off the clock at five unless I say otherwise. Use the driver for whatever."

I just nodded. I could drive myself, obviously, but I wasn't about to pick a fight over a free chauffeur. Besides, I wanted those evenings. I wanted to be home before Clara went to bed, help her with homework, eat dinner. I had to give him credit—he actually thought about that.

"We ride in together," he added. "Except when I have early meetings or have to bounce early."

"Wait, why?"

This was a terrible idea. Too much face time. I just wanted to survive these two years, grab my family's legacy, and ghost.

"We're married, Willow. People expect us to be obsessed with each other. The world knows I'm not exactly a monk. No one's ever caged me—"

"You mean you're a serial manwhore," I cut in.

He threw me a killer glare. I didn't flinch. Let him look mad. I was just calling it like it was.

"Anyway," he gritted out. "Since they know my track record, they'll think you've got some magic touch. We have to sell the fairytale. The whole lovey-dovey routine. Commuting together, holding doors, lunch dates, charity galas. All that garbage."

"Are you freaking kidding me?" I stared at him.

Working in the same building was bad enough. Now I had to play house over salads and red carpets? It was too much.

"Dead serious," he smirked, dripping sarcasm. "The board wanted me hitched. We need to make them believe I finally met my match."

The elevator dinged. We stepped out into pure luxury. White walls, bold modern art, plush carpets that swallowed my heels. Orion dropped a hand onto my lower back, steering me forward.

"Morning, Mr. Wood," two voices chimed.

Two employees blocked our path, their eyes glued to his hand on my waist.

"Why are you on this floor?" Orion's voice was absolute ice.

A pretty blonde started shaking. "We... we were just bringing up the files you wanted from the MD and finance."

"You know the rules," Orion snapped. "No one comes up here. Ever. Next time, tell Arthur to grab them, or have Tristan and Kael bring them up themselves. Got it?"

The girls looked like they were about to wet themselves. Their knees were literally trembling.

"Y-yes, sir," they squeaked, then bolted like the floor was on fire.

"What's the deal with the ban?" I asked once they were gone.

He nudged me along. "Two years ago, a guy tried to swipe a proprietary file to sell to our rivals. Would've cost us billions. Declan and I locked the floor down after that. Execs and personal assistants only."

"Damn. That's brutal. Where is he now?"

"Dead," Orion said, his tone completely flat.

I gasped. "You killed him?"

"No. But by the time we finished with him, he wished we had. He hung himself in his cell a few months into his sentence."

"And that doesn't bother you?"

Orion stopped. He turned to me, his eyes freezing the blood in my veins. This was the monster behind the suit.

"Get this straight, Willow. Nobody steals from me. Nobody touches my family. So no, I don't lose sleep over some dead thief. I slept like a baby."

He started walking again, his hand guiding me as if he hadn't just casually dropped a body count.

Lesson learned: do not cross Orion Wood. He won't pull the trigger, but he'll make sure you do it yourself. Honestly, I didn't know which was worse. I just needed to keep my distance. Work and Clara. That was it. How hard could that be?
