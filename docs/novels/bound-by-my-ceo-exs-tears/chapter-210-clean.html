Chapter 213

"Hey, Asher! Cake time!" I yell the second my feet hit the grass.

His head snaps up. Next thing I know, he’s sprinting my way, grinning ear to ear. Right behind him, Declan jogs over, cradling Aurora. He’d just scooped her from my folks.

We launch into the birthday song. Asher’s absolutely loving it, soaking up every single second of the spotlight. He takes a deep breath and kills the candle. Boom. The backyard erupts. Our families are definitely making the most noise, screaming and cheering like crazy.

The racket scares Aurora. She starts to wail, but Declan and I lean in at the exact same second, planting twin kisses on her cheeks. Total accident, zero planning. The crowd goes wild anyway. "Awww," everyone coos.

"Happy birthday, buddy. Love you tons. Don't you ever forget it," I whisper, squeezing him hard.

He wraps his little arms around me. "Love you too, Mom."

Then comes the knife. He slices the cake, and I pop a bite into his mouth. Declan does the same. Asher giggles and stuffs a forkful into both our faces. Plates go flying out to the guests, and before we know it, it's present-opening time.

Watching him beam, the heavy stuff just melts away. No regrets. None. Jasper’s right beside him, wearing this massive grin, and that’s when the truth hits me like a truck. Maybe Aurora’s a wild card, but Declan, Silas, and I? We’d do it all over again. Every tear, every heartbreak—our kids are worth it. Period.

"Um, excuse me, miss?"

A squeaky little voice snaps me back to reality. I look down. Oh, man. Cutest kid ever. She’s rocking a pink chiffon dress, glasses, and blonde pigtails. Oval face, bright green eyes, tan skin, and rosy lips. I’m instantly obsessed.

"Hey there, sweetie," I say, dropping to one knee.

She squints at me. "Are you Asher's mom?"

I nod, smiling.

"You're, like, really pretty," she says, staring at me like I'm some kind of superhero.

"Well, thank you, doll. And who do I have the pleasure of speaking to?"

Her grin stretches even wider. Seriously, this kid is going to break some hearts when she grows up.

"I'm Piper. Just so you know, I'm gonna marry Asher one day."

I burst out laughing. Ah. So *this* is the girl making my son lose his mind. I instantly dig her vibe. Bold, blunt, zero filter. I wish I’d had half that confidence at her age.

"Oh, really?" I tease, raising an eyebrow.

She nods, dead serious. "Yep. He claims he hates me because I'm 'weird.' Apparently, liking bugs and plants is a crime. I'm gonna be a biologist, you know. But don't worry, I'll make him see the light."

My smile falters. God, how do you tell a kid that you can't force someone to love you? Especially a stubborn guy who's just not into it.

*But wait, didn't you do exactly that with Declan?*

The thought hits me like a slap. Sure, I turned him around. But look at the wreckage. The agony, the tears. Was it even worth it? I wouldn't wish that kind of hell on this sweet girl.

Before I can reply, Martha sprints over, looking pale. I scramble to my feet.

"What's wrong?" My stomach does a flip.

She leans in close, her voice a frantic whisper. "Cops. At the front door. They're asking for Aurora."

Oh, great. Fantastic.

"Go find Declan and the guys," I snap, adrenaline pumping. "I'll handle the front. Keep the kids out here and keep the party going so nobody panics."

My heart is hammering against my ribs. What do the police want with Aurora? Declan dropped the charges. She's supposed to be safe.

"Hey, Piper, sweetie, I gotta run. We'll chat later, okay?" I say, already backing away.

"Sure, Miss Lyra!"

I turn and bolt inside, weaving through the rooms. Two cops are standing right there in the living room.

"Can I help you, officers?" I ask, trying to keep my voice steady.

They spin around. "We're looking for Audrey," the tall, blonde cop says. "We have reason to believe she's on the premises."

"Why? What did I do?"

I whip around. Aurora is standing right behind me. Behind her, the whole squad—Trevor, Eleanor, Orion, and Declan—are filing in.

"Are you Audrey?" the second cop barks.

"Yeah," she says.

They move like lightning. Before I can even blink, they've slammed Aurora face-down onto the sofa. Handcuffs click into place.

"Audrey, you're under arrest for the attempted murder of Willow."

Well, shit. Did not have that on my bingo card.
