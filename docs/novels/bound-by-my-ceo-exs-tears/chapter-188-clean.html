Chapter 190

I check my reflection. Damn, I look good. I went with a low bun tonight, leaving a few curled pieces to frame my face. Since this is my actual first date with Declan, I needed a different vibe. Cue the smoky eyes and bold red lips.

The dress? A black, knee-length number. It has a killer plunging neckline held up by skinny straps—just enough cleavage to tease without looking trashy. Plus, the back is completely open. It’s a total man-killer.

I slide my hands down the soft fabric, staring. It hugs every single curve. Honestly, this pregnancy did wonders for my body.

"Holy hell, babe... if I weren't straight, I’d totally bend you over," Piper squeals through the screen, practically beaming. "You are smoking. Like, actual fire."

"Agreed," Audrey chimes in.

They left my place hours ago, but I dragged them onto Skype for a final vibe check. I needed brutal honesty. This night is huge. First real date with Declan, so yeah, it has to be flawless.

"Thanks, guys," I say, flashing them a grin.

I plop onto the mattress to strap on my heels. Seriously, when was the last time I actually got dressed up? Declan never took me out back then. Not even for those boring work dinners. I never had an excuse to look nice.

Maybe things changed later down the road—I mean, I remember looking pretty damn hot when Adrian came to pick me up that one time. I shake the thought away. Focus on tonight.

"I'm freaking out, though," I admit. "What if we have zero chemistry? Sure, we're married, but what if we're totally incompatible? Or what if the night ends in a total disaster?"

Ugh, my head is spinning. Half of me wants to bail right now because I'm terrified. The other half just wants to shut up, take the plunge, and enjoy it.

I've waited forever for this. I wanted Declan to chase me, to actually court me, but he couldn't care less back then. Now it's happening, and my brain is just listing worst-case scenarios.

"Shut up, Lyra. You're spiraling," Piper snaps. I spot a shadow moving behind her—definitely Corbin, since they share the place. "Just breathe. Go out, eat some good food, and let yourself have fun."

Audrey doesn't say a word, but she nods along in total agreement.

I let out a long, shaky breath. "Fine. You're right. I'm overthinking."

"Duh. Go have a blast. You deserve to be happy, babe, especially with your dream guy," Piper gushes, wearing a massive grin.

We hang up after chatting a bit more. I grab my clutch and take one last look in the mirror. The girls were right. I look hot, and I'm totally feeling myself.

I head downstairs. Before I even reach the bottom, I hear Declan and Asher. Declan got ready in the guest room while I claimed the master suite.

My stomach does a little flip. I can't wait to see his face. I've never dressed up like this for him, so he's in for a shock.

I walk into the kitchen. Declan is rocking Aurora, blowing raspberries on her tummy. She's shrieking with laughter, and the sound melts me. Asher is sitting right next to them, looking like a moody teenager.

"Ready?" I ask.

Declan looks up. His brain completely short-circuits. He does a double-take, his eyes dragging slowly down my entire body. He swallows hard, his gaze darkening. Oh, yeah. He definitely approves.

"Dad, you're literally drooling," Asher mutters, letting out a dry chuckle.

I can't help but smirk. Declan stands up and closes the distance between us. Still holding Aurora with one arm, he hooks his free hand around my waist, pulls me tight against his chest, and kisses me senseless.

Behind him, Asher groans.

I use all my strength to push him back. "Hey! You're messing up my lipstick."

"Don't care," he growls, his eyes locked on my mouth.

"Are we going? We're gonna be late," I say, stepping out of his personal space before his heat sucks me back in.

Honestly? I love how he can't take his eyes off me. This is brand new territory, and I am absolutely living for it.

"We could just stay home," he mumbles, his voice thick and raspy.

"Nice try, big guy. You wanted this date," I laugh. "Move it. Let's go."

He sighs but gives in, calling out for Martha. She walks in and takes Aurora from his arms.

"You look gorgeous, Lyra," Martha says warmly.

"Thanks, Martha."

I plant a kiss on Aurora's head, then turn to Asher. He's still glaring at me, clearly pissed. I don't care. I pull him into a hug anyway. He resists, but I hold on tight, kiss his cheek, and wish him goodnight.

He doesn't budge. He just crosses his arms, looking stubborn as hell.

"Asher..." Declan warns, his voice dropping an octave. He's clearly losing his patience with the attitude.

"It's fine," I cut in.

I'm not letting anything ruin tonight. Since Asher is playing mute, I grab Declan's hand and pull him toward the door. I'll give the kid some space.

"I'm still mad at you, Mom. But you look really pretty tonight."

His voice stops me cold. I turn around. He's still got that stubborn scowl, but right now, I don't care.

"Thank you, sweetie."

We head out, knowing Asher will get over it eventually. I'm not sweating it. He's my kid; I know his game. He's just testing my boundaries to see if I'll cave. Once he realizes I'm holding my ground, he'll drop the act.

"So, where are we heading?" I ask Declan once we're settled in his car.

"It's a surprise. Actually, a few of them," he says, shifting gears. "I know you think I don't get you, Lyra. But I do."

I smile but keep quiet. I highly doubt it. Declan never paid attention to me or what I liked, so there's no way he knows my tastes. But hey, let's see.

I can't wait to see what he has up his sleeve.
