Chapter 40

"The hell is wrong with you?" Lyra spat, her voice pure venom.

Brenda just rolled her eyes, looking bored out of her mind. "Oh, please. The brat needs to watch where she’s going. This is a limited-edition LV dress, okay? She almost soaked it in juice."

Yeah, Brenda and Lyra? Total oil and water. Always had been. Brenda used to make Lyra’s life living hell back in high school.

The little girl was shivering behind Lyra’s legs. Maybe four or five. Cute kid—pink dress, round cheeks, dark hair.

Suddenly, a weird thought hit me. *What if we had a girl? My gray eyes, Lyra’s brown hair...*

I froze. Whoa. Where the fuck did that come from? I shook my head, clearing the mental noise, and locked eyes back on Lyra. She was finally going to tear Brenda a new one.

"Are you freaking kidding me?" Lyra’s forehead creased. "You put hands on a kid over a dress? At a charity event *for* kids?"

"It’s not just a dress!" Brenda stomped her foot like a spoiled toddler. "I’m not letting some charity-case orphan ruin a piece of designer clothing that costs more than she’ll ever make in her life."

The room went dead silent. A collective gasp echoed. Brenda blinked, finally realizing everyone was staring at her.

"I—I didn't mean it like that," she muttered, trying to backpedal under the heavy glares.

"Save it. Get out," Lyra said, waving over a massive security guard by the door. "If you hate these kids so much, you don't belong here."

Brenda gasped. "Wait, what? You’re throwing me out?"

Lyra rolled her eyes. "Duh."

"You can't do that! I donated a shit ton of money to this stupid charity!"

Lyra smirked. It was a cold, wicked look. "Chloe," she called out.

Chloe jogged over, already tapping her tablet. "On it."

"How much did she write a check for?"

"One mil," Chloe said.

Lyra looked Brenda dead in the eye. "See, we don’t need your money. Especially not from someone who thinks a bank account gives them a pass to be a piece of shit." She glanced at Chloe. "Wire it back. Every cent."

The crowd watched, completely stunned. Nobody breathed.

"Done," Chloe said, flashing a grin.

"Now that you’ve got your cash back, get the hell out of my venue. You’re blacklisted from everything I touch." Lyra’s voice was pure steel. I’d never heard her sound like that.

Brenda started screaming curses, but the guard grabbed her arm and dragged her out, kicking and yelling.

Damn. Just like Ingrid, Brenda’s social life was officially dead. Lyra just destroyed her. Best revenge ever.

Lyra scanned the room. "Anyone else feel the same way? Because let me make this clear: we are here for these kids. If you touch them, you deal with me."

Her voice carried across the hall, sharp and loud, no mic needed. She looked like some badass avenging angel right then. Ready to murder anyone who even blinked wrong at a child.

Silence.

Lyra smiled, the tension vanishing. "Great. Sorry about the drama, guys. Enjoy the night."

I couldn't take my eyes off her as she turned and walked out toward the terrace.

Aurora was buzzing in my ear about something, but I tuned her out. I stood up, scraping my chair back, and walked away. Aurora called my name, but I didn't care. I just marched straight toward the balcony.

There she was. Standing by the railing, looking out at the dark sky, her hair whipping in the breeze.

Now that I was actually here, my throat went dry. I hadn't talked to her since that day at her place. Two weeks of ignored texts and calls.

I took a step. The heel of my shoe clicked, and she turned her head. When she saw me, her shoulders stiffened.

"What do you want, Declan?" she sighed.

I walked up and stood right next to her.

"Why didn't you tell me?" I asked, genuinely lost. She was my wife, and I knew nothing.

"Tell you what?"

"Any of it. The Hope Foundation. The money. Why did you let us treat you like garbage?"

She let out a dry laugh and faced me. "And when was I supposed to do that, Declan? You couldn't stand being in the same room as me. You did everything you could to avoid me."

I stared into her brown eyes. There was a new spark in them. But something else was completely gone.

She looked back out at the garden. "Besides, would you have even cared? You never gave a shit about anything I did."

I looked down at the guests mingling below. She was right. I’d been a cold, heartless bastard. Back then, I justified it. I told myself she ruined my life, so she deserved it.

I hated her, and I made sure she felt it for years. I always thought of myself as a good guy, but looking back? I was a total prick. So much of a prick that my own wife had to hide her entire life from me.

"Look, about Aurora..." I started, trying to apologize for the shit I'd said.

She cut me off. "Don't. I don't want you, Declan. I'm not trying to steal you from Aurora. Relax. Whatever feelings I had for you? Dead. You killed them." She took a deep breath. "Loving you was the biggest mistake of my life. I'm done making it."

Jesus. It felt like a physical blow to the chest. Like a sledgehammer to my ribs.

"But I'll always be grateful for Asher," she added. "He's the only good thing that came out of us. I regret you, but I'll never regret our son."

She turned toward the doors and smiled. I spun around to see what she was looking at.

Adrian.

"I'm sorry for the mess," she said, her eyes locked on him. "Sorry my mistake messed things up with you and Aurora. I know you don't believe me—nobody does—but I was wasted that night. Anyway, hope you guys are happy."

Adrian caught her looking and flashed a smug smile.

"I'm staying out of your way, and I want you to do the same," she said, still staring at Adrian. "Don't come to my place. Don't send bodyguards. Don't call me unless it's about Asher, and even then, have your lawyer talk to mine."

The way she looked at him... God, it burned. There was definitely something going on between them, and it made my stomach twist in knots.

She finally looked back at me. "That's everything. Goodbye, Declan."

She started walking away.

My chest tightened. I wanted to grab her, to pull her back, my fists clenching at the urge.

She stopped and turned around. "Oh, one more thing. Let Aurora know she has nothing to worry about. And honestly? You might want to get the real story from her. If you want your relationship to last, maybe don't build it on the lies she told to get me in trouble."

Then she walked out.

"Lyra..." I whispered, sounding pathetic.

I watched Adrian meet her halfway. He took her hand and led her out to the garden. I stared as they walked down the path.

And then he kissed her.

My heart hammered against my ribs. I looked away, sick to my stomach.

This was what I wanted, right? For her to move on. To leave me and Aurora alone. I got exactly what I asked for.

She was gone. She had a new guy.

So why did it feel like my entire world was collapsing? Why did it feel like I just lost everything?

She was supposed to be the villain. But watching her walk away, I just felt completely fucking lost.
