Chapter 68

My foot won’t stop tapping. Tap, tap, tap on the clinic floor. I’m just sitting here, waiting for them to call my name, sweating through my clothes. Calling me nervous is a joke. I’m straight-up panicking inside. It’s like some sick rerun. Baby number two, and I’m sitting in a waiting room completely solo. The only twist? Adrian actually wanted to be here but couldn't make it. With Declan, he just didn't give a single shit.

I tried so hard to play pretend, to act like there wasn't a human growing inside me. Then my jeans stopped buttoning a few days ago. The bump is real now. Soon, the whole world is going to see it. I let out a heavy sigh. I need to call my parents. God, how do I even say it? "Hey, I’m pregnant with Adrian’s kid." Yeah, super weird. He’s their adopted son, I’m their biological daughter. It’s a total mess. But whatever. The kid is coming. No turning back now.

"I can literally feel you vibrating from here, Lyra." That deep voice hits me like a physical blow. I whip my head around so fast my neck pops. *Ouch.* I wince, staring at him. Declan. Standing right there. His grey eyes are locked onto mine. He looks ridiculously good, but his face is actually... relaxed. For once.

"Declan? What the hell are you doing here?" I blink, expecting him to vanish. He just shrugs, a small smirk playing on his lips. I stare. Pretty sure that’s the first time he’s ever smiled at me. Like, ever.

"Just showing up. Didn't want you doing this alone." He says it so casually. My alarm bells go off. What's the catch? Is this some kind of scam? I step closer and press my palm to his forehead. "Are you sick?"

He frowns, swatting my hand away. "What are you doing?"

"Checking for a fever." He feels totally normal. No fever. So why is he here? What is going on in that twisted head of his? Before I can figure it out, the nurse calls my name. Declan grabs my hand, pulls me up—gently, though—and starts leading the way. I just stare at his broad back, completely stunned.

We walk into the room. Dr. Raven is already there, smiling like she just won the lottery. "Hey, Lyra! Ready to see the little peanut?" I nod, still totally dazed. She glances at Declan. "So, is he the dad?"

"No!" I blurt out before he can open his mouth. "He's just... moral support." Declan's jaw tightens. He squeezes my hand hard enough to bruise. What is his damn problem? Why does he look so pissed?

"Right," Dr. Raven says, dragging out the word. "You know the drill. Hop on the table, shirt up." Declan helps me up. I pull my top up, feeling super awkward as his eyes lock onto my bare stomach. It’s weird. He hasn't seen me naked since... what, last year?

Before I can overthink it, the cold gel hits my skin. Dr. Raven slides the wand around. "Hmm," she murmurs, staring at the screen.

"Is something wrong?" Declan barks the question before I can even breathe.

"Everything's perfect. Strong heartbeat, growing right on schedule." She turns to me. "Wanna know the gender?"

"No," I say.

"Yes," Declan says.

Dr. Raven chuckles. "Since you're not the dad, buddy, Mom's vote wins. Lyra?"

"Keep it a surprise. Just like Asher."

"My lips are sealed. Let me print these pictures for you." She shuts off the machine and grabs some tissues.

"Can you make it two sets?" I ask, thinking of Adrian.

"Sure thing."

I wipe off the goo and pull my shirt down. Declan is still staring at the blank screen like a zombie. "Declan?" I call out. He blinks, looks at me, and stands up. We walk out, handle the paperwork at the front desk, and head for the exit.

"Let's get lunch," he says out of nowhere.

I look at him, suspicious. "Why?"

"We need to talk."

I scan the street for a cab. I didn't feel like driving today, so I took a taxi. "Bad idea. We have nothing to talk about." He runs a hand through his dark hair, looking frustrated. "Declan..." I start, ready to tell him to beat it, but his face goes stone cold.

"Get in the car, Lyra. Either you walk, or I carry you." He points to his ride.

"You wouldn't dare."

"Try me, fucking try me." He takes a step toward me. I know he's not bluffing. With a loud huff, I turn on my heel and stomp over to his passenger door. He clicks the unlock button, and I climb in, glaring daggers at him as he starts the engine.

I keep my mouth shut. I'm furious and totally confused. I want the old Declan back. The cold, predictable one. This new guy is messing with my head. We pull up to some fancy restaurant I've never seen before. He gives his name, and they seat us immediately.

"What is your deal, Declan?" I demand the second the waiter leaves. "You're acting crazy. You showed up to my doctor's appointment for a baby that isn't even yours, but you couldn't be bothered to show up once when I was pregnant with Asher." He doesn't say a word. Just stares at me with that familiar, icy look. Finally. The cold, detached guy is back.

"You used to hate having me around. Now you're everywhere. Look, thanks for pulling me out of the dark back then, but can we just go back to normal? Can you please go back to ignoring me?" I plead. I don't want more drama. I don't want Aurora breathing down my neck. I just want to heal, and I can't do that with you around. You can't heal in the place that broke you.

"I'm sorry," he says. It's so quiet I almost miss it. "I was pissed. At myself. I let you take the fall for that night, and then I punished you for something that wasn't even your fault." My jaw drops. Declan apologizing? That's a literal miracle.

The more he talks, the angrier he gets. "I treated you like trash when you were just an eighteen-year-old kid, terrified and trying to survive. I let you go to those appointments alone. I'll regret that forever."

"Why now?" I whisper. "Why apologize when it's too late? It doesn't change anything. The damage is done. Let's just be civil." Seriously, where was this energy before? Now that I'm ready to leave them in the past, everyone suddenly wants to say sorry.

"I'm so fucking sorry for what I did to you, Lyra. I don't make mistakes, but how I treated you? That was the biggest goddamn mistake of my life."

The words hit me, but they don't sink in. How am I supposed to forgive him? How do I forget the hell he put me through? I stare down at the table, silent. I have nothing left to say.
