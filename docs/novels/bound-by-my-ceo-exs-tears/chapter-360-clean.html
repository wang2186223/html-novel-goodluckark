NovelVibe

Chapter 367

My jaw hits the floor. I try to squeeze out a word—any word—but my throat locks up. My eyes whip from Orion to the massive house, then back again.

"Oh my god, this place is insane!" Clara Sterling squeals. She is practically vibrating, hopping from foot to foot like she’s about to bolt and explore. "Are we staying here? Like, is this actually our new house?!"

Orion tears his eyes away from me and looks down at our daughter. She’s wearing a grin that stretches ear to ear. "Well, if your mom digs it, then yeah. It's ours."

I look back at the place, totally stunned.

The mansion sits there, dominating the rolling hills behind it. It looks like a million bucks. It’s this perfect mix of old-school cool and modern sleekness, all wrapped in blinding white marble that catches the sun. Stone arches and sharp corners give it this heavy, timeless vibe.

Up front, two massive wooden doors stand tall, covered in crazy-detailed carvings and flanked by thick columns. Right above them, a huge arched window with black iron grates lets the sun flood the entryway.

Outside, the yard is spotless. Bright flowerbeds pop against the neon-green grass. A cobblestone path cuts straight to the front door, lined with sharp hedges and bursts of blooming roses.

Giant trees and shaped bushes block out the rest of the world. It feels totally private, like a secret hideaway. It’s fancy, sure, but in a way that makes you want to kick back and just stare.

"Orion..." I choke out. The words just won't come.

I’ve wanted a massive estate forever. Seriously. I used to picture my future kids tearing across a giant backyard, chasing dogs. Growing up with just Edward made me crave a huge, loud family, so this dream house was always the goal. I wanted the whole package—weekend BBQs, friends over, kids running wild.

How the hell did Orion know this was my exact dream?

"Look, just check out the inside before you decide," he says, his voice dropping. He sounds almost desperate. "If you hate it, we’ll walk. We can find something else."

He’s sweating it. I can tell. He’s terrified I’ll shoot down this massive gesture, that I’m not on board. My silence is probably freaking him out, making him think I hate it. He has no clue I’m just speechless because this is literally my childhood fantasy brought to life.

I nod quickly, dying to see the inside. Clara takes off down the cobblestones. Watching her skip down the path, she looks exactly like Dorothy on the Yellow Brick Road.

Orion grabs my hand, fingers locking, and pulls me along. I can feel the nervous energy buzzing off him. He’s like a kid dying to show off a new toy. It makes me smile—it’s a total 180 from his usual cold, dominant, alpha-male routine.

"Whoa, slow down, big guy," I laugh, trying to keep up. "You’re gonna make me trip."

He doesn't even stop, just looks back over his shoulder with a smirk. "I mean, I could just carry you."

"Yeah, no way."

We catch up to Clara at the front step. She’s practically bouncing out of her shoes. I can't help but laugh—like father, like daughter. They’re both losing their minds.

Orion throws the heavy doors open.

We step inside, and the vibe hits me instantly. It’s quiet, expensive luxury. The foyer is huge, with soaring ceilings and crazy-detailed crown molding. A massive crystal chandelier hangs right in the middle, throwing light everywhere. Dark wood paneling pops against the bright marble floors.

I slip my hand out of Orion’s and take a few steps in.

The whole place flows. It’s open, airy, and seriously high-end. To my side, the formal living room opens up with massive windows looking out at the gardens. There’s a huge fireplace with a carved mantel, topped by a giant, fancy mirror.

Right next to it, the dining room is just as sleek. It’s got a matching chandelier hanging over a massive, plush rug. More floor-to-ceiling windows let the sunlight stream in, framing the backyard.

I turn around and—boom—the kitchen. It’s a chef’s dream. High-end appliances are built right into the custom cabinets. A massive marble island sits in the center, perfect for prepping food or grabbing a quick bite. There’s a walk-in pantry, a wine cellar, and cool modern pendant lights hanging overhead.
