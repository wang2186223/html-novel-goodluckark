Chapter 212

I lock eyes with her. Can't look away. It's like a trainwreck. She's slumped in the corner, totally solo. Mom's off playing host, and Trevor's doing the same. Aurora looks ready to fold into a cardboard box and disappear. But her eyes? Jesus. They're screaming. I get pain. God knows I've had my share. But hers? I finally get it. It's Jasper. Her boy.

"Hey, Lyra? Earth to Lyra." Piper's voice is a million miles away.

I'm zoned out, glued to Aurora. Then Silas grabs my shoulder, giving me a hard shake. I blink, forcing my head around. He's giving me this look—like, *what is wrong with you?* Honestly? I just want to go over there and hug her. Weird, right?

"Seriously, why are you staring at Aurora like a deer in headlights?" Audrey frowns, squinting at me.

I glance back. Aurora's not looking at me anymore. She's just staring holes into the tablecloth.

"She's... she's hurting," I mumble, the words sticking in my throat.

Piper snorts, glancing over. "Karma's a bitch, huh? Word is, she's eating herself alive with guilt. Can't handle it."

I cut my eyes to Silas. Nothing. Total poker face. Did he stop caring? Does he hate her guts? Or is he just shoving all that crap deep down so his chest doesn't explode? I scan the room. Silas. Aurora. Then Declan. Declan's shooting the breeze with his dad, Orion, and a few other guys. Trevor's right there, too, and man, you could cut the tension between him and Declan with a knife. Trevor's laughing, though, keeping Aurora clamped tight against his side.

I can't help but wonder: what if I'd just let go of my crazy obsession with Declan? Now that I'm older, it hits me. Did my obsession wreck four entire lives? Think about it. If I hadn't stalked Declan that night, the whole domino effect never starts. No one-night stand. No shotgun wedding. Declan and Aurora stay together. No downward spirals. Aurora never hooks up with Silas, never strings him along for a decade.

Sure, losing Declan would've sucked, but I would've gotten over it. Silas would have, too. Honestly, he was already moving on before he ended up in bed with Aurora.

I let out a heavy sigh and push my chair back. "Back in a sec," I mumble, slipping away from the table.

I head inside and crash on the living room couch. The thought that I'm the root cause of all this misery? It feels like a ton of bricks on my chest.

Look, I get it. People make their own choices. We all walk our own paths. Declan, Silas, Aurora—their screw-ups are their own, not mine. But damn, I was the spark. My obsession lit the fuse that blew up their lives.

"Whoa, Lyra, you good?" Martha's voice jumps out at me. I nearly jump out of my skin.

"Yeah, totally," I lie, rubbing my temples. "Just zoning out."

"Well, cake time," she says.

My jaw drops. Wait, what? Didn't I just walk away from the table like five minutes ago?

"Already?" I blink, totally lost. "Is dinner over?"

Martha gives me a bizarre look. I ignore it, pushing myself up and stretching out my stiff shoulders.

"Lyra, you've been in here for over an hour."

Holy crap. Seriously? I had no clue I'd been spacing out that long. It felt like a blip. I guess your brain really warps time when you're spiraling.

"Why didn't anyone come grab me?" I ask, heading toward the back door with her.

"Oh, we were going to," Martha smiles warmly. "But Mr. Declan stopped us. Said to let you be until the cake. He knew you've been running ragged planning this thing all week and just needed some breathing room."

I nod, a sudden warmth blooming in my chest. God, I'd just been sitting here questioning our entire relationship, and then he pulls a move like this. It's a tiny detail, sure, but to me? Huge. It means he actually gets me. I needed space, and he gave it to me. No questions asked.
