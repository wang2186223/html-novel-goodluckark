NovelVibe

Chapter 425

One month. That’s how long it’s been. Asher is ghosting me, or maybe I’m ghosting him—either way, good riddance. Sleeping with him was the single dumbest move of my entire life, hands down.

Sure, I was buzzed, but that’s a cop-out. I had exits. I could’ve grabbed my keys, hit the ignition, and bolted. When his mouth hit mine, I should’ve shoved him back. Instead? I froze. I stayed. My bad. My fault.

I was spineless, plain and simple. Those stupid grey eyes reeled me right in, and like an idiot, I let them. I knew better, but I still bought into his whole vibe. I couldn’t just walk, and God, I hate myself for being that weak.

I tried to shake it off. No luck. The memory just sits on my chest like a ton of bricks. I still taste him. I still see those dark, stormy eyes. I feel the heavy slide of his skin against mine, hear those low, breathless groans when he lost control. It ruins my sleep. I’ve tried scrubbing my brain clean, but it’s no use. That night is inked straight into my skull.

I let out a breath and snap back to reality. I lock my eyes onto the petri dish on the lab bench. *Focus on the cells, Piper. Focus on the data.* Literally anything but his face.

"Hey, Piper?" a soft voice breaks my trance. "You good? You've been, like, totally spaced out lately."

I open my mouth, but a nasty sneer beats me to it.

"Please. She's just plotting her next shortcut. You know, riding her fancy connections to another promotion."

Meet Blair. My personal office demon. Pretty sure everyone has one, but mine is extra special.

I shoot her a death glare. "Not today, Blair. Seriously."

"Like I give a shit?" she spits back, her lip curling.

"Dude, what is your deal?" Leo—we call him Benji—pipes up. "Why are you always riding her back?"

"I'm not riding anyone. She's just a sad, fragile little thing," Blair scoffs, rolling her eyes with a loud *tsk*.

God, her vibe is pure poison. She’s targeted me since our college days, and I have no clue why. At first, I took the high road. But her constant, toxic BS finally got under my skin, and now? Yeah, I hate her guts right back.

I figured graduation would be my escape. Nope. The universe had other plans. We both finished at the top of our class, and—shocker—the exact same firm hired us both.

"Can you shut the attitude down for five seconds?" I snap, grinding my teeth. "We have a massive deadline. How about you do some actual work instead of flapping your gums?"

"Oh, I'm working," she says, tossing her shoulders. "But trust me, when you finally crash and burn? I'm throwing a massive rager to celebrate."

"Don't hold your breath," I say, keeping my voice deadpan. "You'll be waiting a long-ass time."

She starts to bark back, but Benji shuts her down. "Piper's the lead here. Back off and show some respect."

I want to tell him to drop it, that Blair's just doing her usual mean-girl routine and it doesn't phase me. But I don't have the energy. My stomach is in knots, I'm exhausted, and a shouting match sounds exhausting.

So, I just give Benji a quick, tired nod of thanks.

"Nepo baby," Blair mumbles, loud enough for us to hear. "That promotion was mine."

Jocely—yeah, that's her real name—has this bizarre obsession with competing with me. In her twisted head, the only reason I got into college, landed this gig, and got promoted to lead is because of my ties to the Woods and Beckett families.

Look, Victoria helped pay my tuition, sure. But my actual grades and test scores got me through the university gates.

Eleanor, Victoria, Arthur, Silas—they’d move mountains for me if I begged. I get that. But I don't play that card. I busted my ass to get here. Nobody handed me a damn thing.

I actually tried explaining that to her once. Tried to show her the sweat equity I put in. But she didn't want the truth. She preferred her own bitter little fantasy.
