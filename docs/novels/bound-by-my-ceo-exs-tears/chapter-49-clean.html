Chapter 49

It’s been like two months since my place burned to ashes. Since then? Total radio silence. No psycho attacks, nothing. I actually started hoping the creep had finally backed off. But the chief shut that down fast. "Don't get comfortable," he warned. "Keep your eyes peeled. Freaks like this don't just quit. They play the long game. They wait, they plan, and they strike when you think you're safe." I got his point, sure. But when weeks pass without a peep, you can't help but breathe a little easier. You get sloppy. You let your guard down.

Honestly, these past few months have been heaven. Obviously, nothing beats my time with Asher, but still, life’s been pretty damn good. And that’s mostly thanks to Adrian. Every single second with him feels like a revelation. I love being near him. If I'm being totally real with myself? Yeah, I’m falling for the guy.

He’s literally everything I wanted Declan to be—sweet, attentive, actually present. And god, the bedroom stuff? Mind-blowing. When he looks at me with those soft eyes, I keep praying he’s feeling the exact same vibe. I can actually picture a future with him. Well, if he’s down for it. People probably think I’m rushing things. Maybe. But honestly? Screw that. I wasted nine miserable years loving a guy who treated me like dirt. Nine years of pure hate. So yeah, when real happiness finally stares me in the face, I’m grabbing it with both hands. No hesitation. No second thoughts.

My phone buzzes, snapping me out of my Adrian-induced daydream. I was floating on air, completely untouchable.

"Where the hell are you?" Piper’s voice blasts through the speaker, practically vibrating.

Right. Our weekly hangout. I was running late. Totally Adrian’s fault—his tongue does magic things.

"Chill, babe. I’m literally pulling up right now," I say, rolling my eyes even if she can't see me.

Piper hates when people are late. Hilarious, considering she’s tardy like eighty percent of the time.

"Fine!" she huffs, then *click*—she hangs up.

I park, kill the engine, and jog inside. The club is packed, but I spot her in the VIP lounge almost instantly. We usually do dinner, but Piper wanted to blow off some steam tonight, and I was totally down. I’m not a club girl. I’ve only been drunk twice in my life. First, the night Declan and I made a huge mistake. Second, the night I first hopped into bed with Adrian. Both times started at a bar. Funny, isn't it? Alcohol was the catalyst for both guys. But with Adrian, I was just tipsy and remember every single touch. With Declan? Just blurry, messy fragments.

"About time," Piper grumbles as I slide onto the couch opposite her.

The VIP section keeps the thumping bass at bay, so we don't have to scream.

"Sorry, got a bit... tied up," I say, flashing a dirty smirk.

She grins instantly. She knows exactly what "tied up" means. No explanation needed.

"Well, cheers to that, babe!" she squeals.

She hoists her glass and downs the liquid fire in one go. Yep, she’s definitely already buzzed.

"You have to try this. It’s the absolute shit," she says, waving over the bartender to get me one.

A minute later, a glass slides in front of me. I take a sip, and my eyes go wide.

"Told you," she beams.

"Holy crap, this is amazing," I gasp.

It’s perfect—sweet, smooth, zero bitter aftertaste. I hate the harsh burn of regular booze, which is why I barely drink.

"So, how’s life?" I ask after a moment.

"Ugh, brutal. This week was a total nightmare," she sighs, slumping back.

"Need a hand with anything?"

"Only if you can clone me, give me extra limbs, and a brand-new brain," she groans against the cushions.

I laugh. "Yeah, sorry. Can't help you there."

"And you? Any word from Declan?" she asks.

Ever since his fistfight with Adrian, Declan’s been a ghost. Even when it comes to Asher. I call, he ignores it. I text, and his lawyer replies instead. I should be thrilled. I’m the one who demanded space in the first place. But it feels wrong. Like he’s actively dodging me, and I have no clue why.

I sigh. "Nope. He’s ghosting. I’m not gonna beg. Besides, I told him to stay away. Maybe he’s finally respecting my wishes."

"Doubt it," Piper mutters, her eyes drifting.

"Do you know something?"

"Not really. Just a gut feeling something went down."

I look at her, skeptical. Maybe Aurora ordered him to stay away? Nah. Declan doesn't take orders from anyone, especially when it involves Asher.

I shake my head to clear the thoughts. "Whatever. Doesn't matter tonight. We're here to party."

"Hell yeah," she grins, checking her phone. "Shoot, gotta pee. Back in a flash."

Before I can even blink, she’s darting away.

I wait. Five minutes pass. Ten. Still no Piper. My stomach does a nervous flip. I stand up and head toward the restrooms.

Empty. No sign of her. Panic kicks in. I sprint back to the VIP area.

"Hey, the girl I was with—did you see where she went?" I ask the bartender, heart hammering.

He shrugs with a polite smile. "Yeah, she slipped out the back door a minute ago."

"Thanks," I mumble, heading out. Why the hell would she leave? Her purse and phone are literally still on the table.

I step into the alley, scanning the shadows. Nothing. I pull out my phone to call Corbin, but before I can dial, a brutal blow cracks against the back of my skull. Everything goes black.

When I blink open my eyes, my vision is a blurry mess. My head throbs like a bass drum. It takes a second to register the cold reality: I’ve been taken.

My wrists are bound, hanging from a chain on the ceiling. I thrash, metal rattling loudly.

"Save your breath... you're not walking out of here alive," a rough voice grunts from the dark.

I squint, my eyes locking onto him. It’s an empty, dusty warehouse. Completely abandoned.

The guy speaking looks late thirties, hair thinning and blonde. He’s got a scruffy beard framing thin, cruel lips. His amber eyes stare at me with pure malice.

"Who are you? What do you want?" My voice shakes. I'm terrified.

"Who I am doesn't matter. And you already know what I want," he says, shrugging.

My worst nightmare. My luck finally ran out. I’m trapped with a psychopath, and nobody is coming to save me.

"Please, just let me go," I sob, tears spilling over. The thought of Asher growing up without a mom tears me apart. "I have a son. Please."

"No can do, sweetheart. I’m getting paid a massive paycheck to put you down."

"I have money! I can pay you whatever you want. I’m loaded," I scramble, trying to find a way out. If it’s just cash, I can fix this.

"Man, I wish it was just about the cash," he chuckles, looking way too proud of himself. "But I have a reputation. I never leave a job unfinished."

Asshole. Acting like being a hitman is some noble career.

"Please. I’ll do anything. Just name it."

He steps closer, towering over me. He draws a cold blade, dragging the flat of it down my cheek.

"You’re a pretty thing. I’d love to play," he whispers, his hot breath making my skin crawl. "But you're not worth ruining my track record."

Before his disgusting hands can do more, the heavy warehouse door screeches open. We both snap our heads toward the entrance. For a split second, hope surges. I'm saved.

Then, the guy speaks, shattering my world.

"Boss, look what I got," he grins, beaming with pride. "Told you I’d deliver."

My blood runs ice-cold. The sheer weight of the betrayal crushes my chest. I can't breathe. I can't believe it.

The one person I trusted. My worst enemy.

How the hell did I not see this coming?
