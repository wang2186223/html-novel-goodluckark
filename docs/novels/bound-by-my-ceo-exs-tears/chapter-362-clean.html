NovelVibe

Chapter 369

"Seriously, why do I ever listen to you guys?" I groan, throwing a glare at Orion and Clara. "Look at the time. We are so late."

Zero remorse from either of them. Clara is practically beaming, and Orion has this smug grin plastered on his face. They look incredibly pleased with themselves.

I let out a massive sigh. I'm doomed. These two are a package deal, and they are always going to team up against me. I throw a fake-angry look at Clara. "Wow. No loyalty. None at all."

"Come on, you have to admit it was fun," she says, leaning forward and grabbing the backs of our seats.

She is glowing. Honestly, ever since we got back, she’s been on cloud nine. We had good times before, sure, but this is next-level happy.

Her bond with Owen was okay, but this thing with Orion? Totally different league. Maybe it’s the DNA, or maybe they’re just cut from the same cloth. I don't know. All I know is they just click. With Owen, it always felt like a struggle.

It’s tough to put into words, but watching her let loose around Orion is the best thing ever. She hasn't erased Owen, though. His photo is still right on her nightstand. The other day, I caught her talking to it. God, it wrecked me and healed me all at once. She told him she missed him, but that he shouldn't worry because we’re doing great. I literally hid in our room and sobbed for an hour after that.

But she wasn't lying. We really are happy. Especially her. She’s finally breaking out of her shell, and it’s gorgeous.

"Mom?"

I spin around to look at her. "Yeah, okay. You win. It was a blast."

They’d ganged up on me to stay for a celebratory lunch. The occasion? Me finally agreeing to turn this house into our actual home. Honestly, ordering takeout and eating on the dusty floor of a completely empty house was awesome. It’s going straight into my core memories. Orion didn't even blink about ruining his designer pants on the floor when Clara suggested the picnic.

Then we just ran wild around the property. We laid in the grass, soaked up the sun, and played hide-and-seek with Clara. I was having so much fun I totally blanked on the dinner plans.

"Ugh, this is a disaster," I mutter, snapping back to reality. "Your mom is going to hate me. I'm the host, and we're late."

"Breathe," he says, still wearing that stupidly handsome grin. "She’s just going to see how happy we are. That's it."

Easy for him to say. My stomach is still doing flips. We've only met his parents twice, and both times were super brief. Tonight is supposed to be this cozy, intimate thing, and I really don't want to screw up my first real impression.

Half an hour later, we pull up to the building. Orion parks, we pile out, and hit the elevator. I'm silently begging the universe that they aren't waiting for us.

"I look like a hot mess," I whisper. My chest is thumping so loud I'm worried the neighbors can hear it.

"Willow, seriously, chill," Orion says, grabbing the doorknob. "It's fine. We're fine."

The second the door swings open, I bolt. I sprint to the bedroom to find something—anything—better to wear. Since it's casual, I throw on some baggy jeans and a simple cami.

I'm just finishing up when the front door clicks. I take a massive breath, try to stop shaking, and head out. I walk into the living room right as Orion is wrapping his arms around his mom.

"Tell me we didn't beat you here," she says, smiling warmly.

"Nah, you're good. We got sucked into a family day and lost track of time. Willow was actually freaking out that we'd keep you waiting."

I roll my eyes so hard it hurts. Seriously? Why throw me under the bus? He could've just lied! I take one more breath and step into the room.

"Hey everyone," I say. Both of them look over.

"Hey, sweetie!" his mom beams, immediately pulling me into a tight squeeze and planting a kiss on both my cheeks.

As she steps back, her husband steps up. I reach out for a handshake, but he just laughs, pulls me in, and gives me a warm bear hug with a couple of pats on the back.

"Grandma! Grandpa!"

Clara screeches, bursting out of her room at Mach speed. I quickly step back just in time to avoid getting trampled as she slams right into Orion's dad. He scoops her up, laughing, while his mom hovers right next to them, practically vibrating.

"My turn! Give me my baby!" she demands, snatching Clara right out of his arms.

She squeezes her tight, then leads her over to the couch with her husband trailing right behind. They sit down and immediately start babbling with her.

"And... we're invisible," Orion mutters in my ear, grinning like an idiot.

"Well, yeah. She's the favorite," I whisper back.

"Excuse me? I'm their actual child," he whines.

I roll my eyes. This whole pouty, childish side of him is brand new to me. "More like a giant toddler," I tease.

"Hey, I'm the golden child. That definitely beats the grandkid card."

"Oh, please. You have a twin. You're not that special. You and Declan are on the exact same level."

He whips his head around, giving me this mock-offended glare. "Uh, absolutely not. I'm the baby."

"By like, three minutes."

He shrugs, totally unbothered. "Still counts. I'm the baby."

"Okay, I'm done with you. I'm going to go check on dinner," I laugh, shaking my head as I head to the kitchen.

Seriously, this goofy side of him is wild. I never saw it coming. Is it crazy that I'm totally obsessed with it? He used to be this untouchable, icy guy. Seeing him like this? It makes him real. And honestly? I'm so here for it.
