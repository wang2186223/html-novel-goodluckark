NovelVibe

Chapter 437

I idle-spin the ice in my glass, totally zoned out.

Walking back into my office after leaving Piper’s place was a trip. The whole room was spotless. No trace of the absolute wreckage I’d left behind. Some magician had even swapped my smashed-up desk for a shiny new one.

Chloe kept throwing me these side-eyes. I could practically feel her burning to ask what went down, but I shut it out. She was probably dying to know what kind of drama Piper and I had to trigger a total freak-out like that. Honestly, thank God Chloe keeps her mouth shut. No gossip. If she had a big mouth, my mom would’ve already busted through that door, arms crossed, giving me that classic disappointed-mom glare and demanding to know why I made Piper cry.

The whole day was a blur. Just a thick, heavy fog I couldn't shake. I actually sat through a whole-ass meeting, but don't ask me what it was about. Zero memory. Luckily, Dad and Arthur were no-shows. Good thing, too, because I was a total wreck. My brain was all over the place, and my temper was on a hair-trigger. The staff definitely picked up on the vibe. They gave me a wide berth. One stupid comment from anyone, and I would've lost my shit again.

Once the clock hit five, I bolted straight to the club. I needed a drink, bad. Anything to take the edge off and block out the mess I’d made.

I tip the glass back and swallow the whiskey. It burns like hell, but that’s it. No magic fix. The ache in my chest is still there, and my head is still spinning. For a second, I thought about getting something harder. But yeah, no. We know how that story ends. It was the pills that screwed me over in the first place.

"Why the long face, man?"

A voice snaps me out of it. I’d know that voice anywhere.

I look up. Yep, Jasper.

We’ve been tight since we were kids, right after his dad got divorced and they moved next door to my mom's place. Back then, he was this super quiet, closed-off kid. But my mom had a knack for cracking people open, especially kids. No wonder her charity foundation does so well. She’s just got that touch—plus, she used to teach, so she knew exactly how to handle troubled kids.

"Piper's pregnant," I say.

Dead silence.

He drops into the chair opposite me. His eyebrows shoot up, and then this slow, smug grin spreads across his face.

"Well, holy shit. Never thought I'd see the day she actually settled down and popped out a kid with some guy. Guess Clara Sterling’s matchmaking finally worked."

I frown, totally lost.

Wait, did he mean Clara Sterling helped Piper pull off some baby-trap scheme? The thought makes my stomach drop. Would my own cousin seriously backstab me like that?

My hands ball into fists. "What the hell do you mean by that?"

"Mean by what? The settling down part, or Clara playing cupid?"

"Both."

Jasper pours himself a glass and leans way back. "Look, everyone knew she was crazy about you. You marrying Seraphina probably broke her. Hell, it would've broken me. As far as I know, she hasn't had a real boyfriend since."

My mind flashes back to my wedding day. Honestly, I barely even looked at Piper. Why would I? I was marrying the absolute girl of my dreams. I think I threw her a quick glance, but that was it. Like I said, she was background noise. Only my gorgeous wife mattered.

"And what about Clara?"

"Clara’s been trying to hook her up with literally every single guy in her contact list. Guess one of them finally won her over. Sucks they kept me in the dark, though."

Jasper and Piper always got along. They’re actually pretty tight, grab drinks and stuff sometimes.

It’s kind of a joke, honestly. Everyone in my circle loves Piper. They think she's great. Everyone except me. Nobody could ever figure out why I couldn't stand her. I didn't even get why I hated her guts when we were kids, but the older we got, the more it made sense.

"She just found out," I mumble.

Jasper stops mid-sip, his eyes narrowing. "And she told *you* first? Like, out of everyone, she came to you?"

I can see the gears turning in his head. He’s putting the puzzle together, staring right through me like he’s reading my mind.

"So why the hell would she run to you first, especially with how messy things are between you two... unless, wait. Is the kid yours?"

I stay dead silent.

There’s a damn good reason Jasper is a top-tier construction CEO. The guy is sharp as a whip. He can tear you apart without even trying, just by watching you and finding your weak spot.

"Tell me I'm wrong, man."

"I can't."

God, I wish I could. I'd give anything for it to be some other guy's kid. But wishing doesn't change a damn thing.

He just stares, completely blown away. "How the hell did you manage that? You literally hate her guts."

"What the fuck am I supposed to do now?" I drag my hands down my face, messing up my hair. "I don't want this kid. And if my folks find out, especially Mom, they’re gonna force me to 'do the right thing.'"

Jasper doesn't say a word. Honestly, what is there to say? He knows I'm spot on. He knows exactly how they'll react if they find out Piper is carrying my kid.

"My dad did the 'right thing.' He married Mom because she got knocked up. And they spent nine miserable years hating each other. If I do this, it’s just history repeating itself."

"Yeah, but they figured it out eventually," Jasper says. "And now they're, like, totally obsessed with each other."
