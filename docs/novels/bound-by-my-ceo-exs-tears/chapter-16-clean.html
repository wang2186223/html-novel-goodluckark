Chapter 16

Lyra

"Mom? I missed you. Why didn't you call?" Asher’s voice cracked. It killed me.

God, I wanted to squeeze him. Just to feel his little ribs, to prove to myself I was still here. Still his mother. Not some ghost.

"I'm so sorry, sweetie. I lost my phone. Had to borrow your dad's."

"Can we FaceTime? I want to see your face," he demanded, sounding way too grown-up.

He knew. Kids always know when the world is shaking. But no way was he seeing me hooked up to hospital monitors. He’d freak, demand to come home, and with some psycho targeting me? I couldn't risk him. Not in a million years.

Honestly, it still hadn't sunk in. Someone actually wanted me dead. Me.

"Not right now, baby. No video calls allowed in this ward," I lied.

"That's stupid! Who even makes that rule?" he yelled into the receiver.

He was hurting. I let the attitude slide.

"Asher..."

"I just want to see you, Mom. Can't Dad fix it? He can make them do whatever he wants. I know he can." He was sobbing now, and my chest literally ached.

I glanced up. Declan was standing there, wearing this smug, stupid grin. Yeah, Asher knew how his father worked. One word from a Thorne, and the world bows down.

"Not this time, kiddo. Hey, how about I call you tomorrow on Skype?"

"Promise?"

"Double promise."

I was getting discharged today anyway. That gave me twenty-four hours to look human again.

"Okay, Mom. Talk tomorrow. Love you."

"Love you too, baby. Bye."

"Grandma wants to—"

I cut the call. Click. Rude? Sure. But I just couldn't deal.

Declan was staring, one eyebrow arched. I couldn't read his face. I'd told him to get lost a dozen times, but the guy was like superglue. I even begged the nurse to kick him out, and he literally threatened to buy the hospital and shut it down.

So yeah, my shadow was here every single day. It was suffocating. Especially after the disaster three days ago when I woke up to find Aurora and Corbin hovering over my bed. The second Corbin left to grab a bite, Aurora pounced. Like a damn vulture.

Three days ago

"Nice act, Lyra," Aurora spat the moment the door clicked shut behind Corbin.

"What the hell are you on about?"

I was exhausted. My body ached, and I had zero energy for her psycho drama.

"This little 'accident.' You blew up your own car just to get Declan to look at you. Pathetic. But guess what? It won't work."

I let out a laugh. Big mistake. The vibration sent a sharp pain straight through my cracked ribs.

"Let me get this straight. You think I bombed my own car, landed in the ICU, all for Declan's attention?"

She was out of her mind. And everyone called *me* the dumb one in the family. They should hear the absolute garbage coming out of Miss Perfect's mouth.

"Duh. Why else? Who'd want to kill you? You're nobody. Declan doesn't give a shit if you breathe or bleed. None of us do. You're a blip, Lyra. Your death wouldn't even make the news." She sneered, looking down her nose at me.

I have never wanted to smash someone's face in so badly.

"So you came here to kick me while I'm down? That your grand plan, Aurora?"

Why was she even allowed in here? I wanted her gone. The thought of sleeping with her nearby made my skin crawl.

"Just keeping it real. Honestly, I hope the mob doesn't get you first. I want you alive to watch me strip away everything you ever took from me. Including your kid. He's going to call me Mom soon."

Seriously, how did we share blood? Yeah, I screwed her over in the past, but damn. Haven't I paid enough?

"You're a psycho, Aurora. But get this through your thick skull: Asher will never call you Mom. Remember the airport? He walked right past you. You're nothing to him, and you'll still be nothing even if you drag Declan to the altar."

Her face twisted. The smug grin vanished, replaced by pure rage.

"Whatever. Declan screams my name when we're in bed. I'm going to make sure he gets me pregnant, and once I have his baby, he'll forget you and your little brat ever existed. He never loved you, Lyra. Every time he touched you, he was wishing it was me. How many times did he whisper my name in your ear?"

It stung. God, it stung. But I ignored the sex talk. It was the "brat" comment that did it. I saw red. I grabbed the glass vase from the nightstand and hurled it straight at her face.

She shrieked, ducking just in time. The vase shattered against the wall right as Corbin walked back in.

"Are you insane?!" Aurora screamed.

"Get the hell out! Both of you!" I shrieked back.

The nurse rushed in, eyes wide.

"Lyra, what's going on?" Corbin asked, trying to sound all caring. I hated it.

I remembered every single time he treated me like garbage. Every time he made me feel like dirt while putting Aurora on a pedestal.

"Ask your fucking sister," I spat, glaring at them. I turned to the nurse. "Get them out. Now."

My head was pounding. The heart monitor was beeping like crazy.

The nurse turned on them. "You need to leave. Now. You're spiking her vitals."

"She's my sister! You can't kick me out!" Corbin snapped.

I scoffed. Sister? Since when?

"I'm calling security," the nurse said, her polite voice totally gone.

"Lyra, please," Corbin begged.

I was done. They'd taken everything, chewed it up, and spat it in my face. I had nothing left for them. No love, no energy. Just empty space.

"Leave, Corbin. And stay the hell away from me."

I rolled over, closed my eyes, and shut them out. After some arguing, they finally cleared out. Adrian came by later and actually made me laugh. First time in days.

"What did you say?" Declan's voice snapped me back.

"I asked if you're ready to go," he repeated.

I stared at him. Was Aurora right? Would he really dump Asher? I wanted to believe he wouldn't, but Declan was a wild card. Aurora was his gold standard. Did he care about her more than his own son?

"Why are you even here, Declan?" I asked instead.

He frowned. "What?"

"We both know you hate my guts. Why aren't you at home celebrating your reunion with Aurora?" The bitterness tasted like copper in my mouth.

He sighed. "You're looking for a fight, and I'm not biting. Let's just get you checked out and go home."

"I don't need help from you. Just go, Declan. You'd rather be anywhere else anyway."

"Oh, really? No help? Who's signing the papers, Lyra? You literally have zero friends."

"Adrian. Adrian will take me."

Declan's face went stone cold. His jaw clenched, his fists tightening.

"Over my dead fucking body," he growled. "You have two choices. I take you home, or you rot in this bed for another week. Either way, that bastard isn't touching you."

"Seriously, what is your problem? You couldn't wait to get rid of me, and now you're clinging to me like a rash. I don't want drama with Emma because of you. I just want some peace."

He needed to back off. I didn't need anyone accusing me of using my injuries to seduce him.

"Speaking of... what actually happened between you and Aurora the day of the crash?"

"I'm sure she already gave you her version," I said, meaning her lies.

"I want yours."

I swung my legs out of bed, moving slowly, and headed for the bathroom. "Doesn't matter. You'll believe her anyway. You always do."

"Lyra—"

I shut the door in his face.

Getting dressed was a workout. By the time I pulled my clothes on, I was panting and dizzy.

I leaned against the wall for support as I walked out. Declan reached for my arm, but I brushed him off. I slumped into the wheelchair the nurse had left. The doctor had already signed off on my release.

I grabbed my duffel bag and plopped it on my lap.

"Let's go. Let's get this over with so we don't have to look at each other," I mumbled, my eyelids heavy.

He sighed but kept his mouth shut. He wheeled me out to the lobby.

After that, everything went blurry. I was too exhausted to keep my eyes open. I drifted off.

When I woke up, I was in Declan's arms. He was carrying me up a familiar flight of stairs.

"What are you doing?" I mumbled, half-asleep.

"Taking you to bed. You passed out in the lobby."

His chest was warm, his voice a low rumble. I couldn't help it—I snuggled closer. He let out a soft chuckle, but I was too tired to care. He smelled amazing. Warm and safe. My eyes slipped shut again.

It felt like floating.

He laid me down on the soft mattress, then climbed in beside me. I rolled over, resting my head right against his chest. His arms wrapped around my waist, pulling me tight.

As I drifted back to sleep, I thought about how perfect this felt. In this dream, he didn't push me away. In this dream, I was exactly where I wanted to be.
