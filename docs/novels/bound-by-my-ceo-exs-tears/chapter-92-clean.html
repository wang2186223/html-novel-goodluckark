NovelVibe

Chapter 93

"Hey, how's it going?" Mom's voice crackles through the speaker. "How are you, the kiddo, and Asher?"

Another business trip, another flight out of the country. Must be nice having a private jet. Declan has two—his personal ride and the family one. I've never even stepped foot on either. Guess that's what happens when your husband never takes you anywhere.

Back when my bank account exploded, I actually toyed with buying my own plane. Then I snapped out of it. Like, why? I barely travel. If I ever need to get out of town, I'll just charter a flight or grab a first-class ticket. Much easier.

"Lyra? You there?"

"Oh, sorry, Mom. Yeah, we're great. Just really miss you guys, honestly."

No lie there. They've been gone a week, with another week to go. Asher and I are totally lost without them. Crazy how fast they became our entire world.

"Miss you more, sweetie. Your dad sends his love. He'll call tonight once his meetings wrap up."

"Sounds good."

"So, any new baby gear? Four months left, Lyra! The little one will be here before we know it." She's practically squealing. Classic Mom.

"Are you kidding? I can't buy a thing because everyone else beat me to it," I chuckle. "This kid is going to be ridiculously spoiled."

Seriously, the nursery is overflowing. I thought Mom, Dad, Piper, and Audrey went overboard. Then Kaelen entered the chat.

Ever since I let him back in, the guy's been flooding us with adorable baby stuff. How does a dude on the run even shop? Beats me. Oh, and everything is pink. He's totally convinced it's a girl, just like Asher and Adrian are.

My grin fades. Thinking about him always brings a dark cloud. I made a promise, sure, but Dad raised me, even if we fought constantly.

"I seriously can't wait," Mom's voice snaps me out of the spiral. "I missed Asher's delivery, so I'm totally making up for it this time."

"Hey, Mom, can I buzz you back? I'm grabbing lunch with Piper and Audrey, and I think they just pulled up."

Total lie—it wasn't them—but my head was already in a bad place.

"Of course. Love you, Lyra."

"Love you too, Mom." I tap the screen to end the call.

I drop the phone on the table and let out a heavy sigh. Instantly, my brain starts eating itself. Kaelen isn't just a threat to my late father's memory. He's a threat to Declan. Yeah, Declan's a jerk, but he's still Asher's dad. Kaelen wants blood, and if Declan dies, it'll absolutely break my son.

Plus, Kaelen might target anyone Declan cares about. And Asher loves those same people—well, minus Aurora. I'm totally stuck. Half of me thinks I screwed up big time by trusting Kaelen. The other half says trust your gut, and my gut says I did the right thing.

"Whoa, deep thoughts?"

"Jesus, Audrey! You scared the crap out of me!" I slap a hand over my racing heart.

"My bad," she says, looking guilty. "I thought you saw me."

I was so zoned out I didn't even hear them walk into our private booth.

"All good. Just... got a lot going on."

"Wanna talk about it?" Piper asks, sliding into her chair.

"Nah, not really."

It's not that I want to keep secrets. I just don't know how to explain that the most wanted man in the country is actually my unborn baby's uncle. Or that we're texting, and—oh yeah—I'm letting him play uncle.

"Is this about Declan again?" Piper leans in, eyes gleaming with gossip. "Corbin said Declan called him the other night, totally wasted."

I frown. Declan? Wasted? He hasn't gotten hammered since Asher was a baby. Besides, he's been completely MIA since he crashed my place that night.

I still can't believe I actually clocked him in the face. Honestly? Best feeling ever. I've wanted to do that for years.

"Why was he drinking so much?" Audrey asks.

Piper shrugs. "No clue, and Corbin doesn't either. But Orion definitely knows, and I'll bet my life Lyra does too."

Two pairs of eyes lock onto me. I squirm in my seat.

"Spill, Lyra. Now," Piper orders.

"Seriously, I have no idea. He just showed up at my house late at night, acting super weird, so I kicked him out."

"Define weird," Audrey presses.

"Well, Asher brought up... suitors," I say, catching myself before I blurt out Kaelen's name. "And Declan lost his absolute mind. He got super jealous, claimed I belonged to him, and said he wouldn't let me sleep around like some cheap hooker."

Just thinking about it makes my blood boil. The nerve of that guy! Belong to him? Since when? I'm a human being, not his property.

"Ooh, possessive. Hot," Piper swoons. "Never thought I'd see Declan mark his territory like that."

I stare at her. Is she out of her mind?

"Are you for real, Piper? We're talking about Declan. The guy who literally told me a million times that I'm nothing to him. He hates my guts, remember?"

The old hurt is gone, replaced by pure rage. All that suffering, and for what? He's still ruining my life.

"I'm telling you, he finally realized he wants you," Piper insists. "Why else would he—"

"Even if he does—which, no way—it doesn't matter. Too little, too late. I gave him nine years of chances during our marriage, and he blew every single one. All I got was pain. I'm not playing this game anymore."
