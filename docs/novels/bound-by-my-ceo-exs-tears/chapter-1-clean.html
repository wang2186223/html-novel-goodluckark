NovelVibe

Chapter 1

My fingers grip the steering wheel, slick with sweat. I kill the engine. The massive estate looms ahead, and my legs feel like lead as I step out. It’s over. Actually, officially over. The signed papers are heavy in my bag, a physical reminder of my freedom. I’m just here to drop off his copy and grab Asher. That’s it.

I slip through the front door. Low murmurs drift from the back of the house. I freeze right outside the kitchen, the air suddenly freezing in my lungs.

"But why can't you just live with us?" Asher’s small voice cracks. "Me and Mom?"

I press a hand to my ribs, trying to hold my chest together. God, the hurt in his voice kills me. I’d die for my kid, but this split? It had to happen. We were a trainwreck from day one. A total, absolute mistake. I was just too blind to see it.

"Hey, buddy," Declan says. His tone is incredibly gentle. "You know why. Your mom and I aren't a team anymore."

It hits me like a slap. In nine years, he never used that voice with me. Not once. With me, he was always a brick wall. Cold. Dead.

"But why?" Asher presses.

"Sometimes people just drift apart, kiddo," Declan mutters.

I can picture his scowl. He’s trying to shut the kid down, shut the conversation down. But Asher has my DNA. He doesn't let things go.

"Don't you love her anymore?"

My lungs lock up. I stumble back a step, my spine hitting the drywall. My pulse hammers in my ears. I wait, holding my breath.

I already know the damn answer. The whole world knows it. He doesn't love me. Never did, never fucking will. It’s a brutal fact. Yet, some pathetic part of me still wants to hear what he says. Will he lie to protect our son, or just lay it out?

A throat clears. Declan is squirming. "Asher..."

"Dad. Yes or no. Do you love Mom?" Asher’s voice is dead serious.

A heavy sigh echoes in the kitchen. "I love her because she gave me you," Declan says.

A cop-out. A total dodge.

I squeeze my eyes shut, a wave of agony crashing over me. God, it still stings. Why did I expect anything else? He never said those words. Not at the altar. Not in the delivery room. Not even when we were tangled in the sheets, sweating and breathless. He kept his heart locked in a vault. I poured everything into him, and he paid me back in scars. Our marriage was a crowded bed. Me, him, and her ghost. The woman he’s been obsessed with for nearly a decade.

I swipe a tear away before it can fall. No more crying over a guy who treats me like trash.

"Eavesdropping is pretty trashy, you know."

His deep baritone snaps me out of it. I pull my shoulders back, plaster on a mask, and walk in. Declan is leaning against the marble island. My brand-new ex-husband. His grey eyes lock onto me, dripping with sarcasm.

I look at Asher. My whole world. The one perfect thing I got out of this mess. He’s got his dad’s killer bone structure and those piercing grey eyes, but my messy brown hair.

"Hey, guys," I force a smile.

"Mommy!" Asher drops his crust, slides off the stool, and bolts into my arms, wrapping his arms around my waist. "I missed you so much."

"Missed you more, baby." I press a kiss to his forehead, and he scampers back to his lunch.

I shift my weight, feeling like a trespasser. This place used to be mine, but I’m a ghost here now. Honestly? I always was. He built this mansion for *her*. Every paint swatch, every tile—it was her dream, not mine. I should’ve known right then. He was never going to let her go. He was never going to love me back.

"Why are you here?" Declan checks his Rolex, his jaw tight. "We had a deal. No crashing my visitation."

"I know, I know," I say, holding up my hands. "The court finalized the papers today. I figured I'd drop off your copy when I came for Asher."

His face turns to granite. His mouth hardens into a flat line. That look always chips away at my soul. I’ve loved this man my entire life, and to him, I’m just an annoyance. He broke me, piece by piece, for years. And I let him. I kept hoping he’d wake up and love me. Stupid.

I thought marriage would heal the lonely kid inside me. I thought I’d finally be wanted. What a joke. It was a living hell. I spent years competing with a ghost, a perfect memory I could never beat.

I rub my sternum, trying to massage the ache away. It’s useless. Months apart, and it still fucking hurts.

"Asher, go upstairs," Declan orders, his teeth grit so tight his jaw muscles twitch. He spits out the word "mother" like it's poison.

Asher looks at us, sensing the tension. "No yelling," he warns, pointing a finger before heading out.

The second the kid is gone, Declan slams his fist onto the marble. The bang echoes. His eyes are pure ice.

"You have an office address, Lyra. You don't crash my time with my kid!" he snarls, fists clenched, looking ready to tear the kitchen apart.

"Declan, please—"

"No! Shut the fuck up! You ruined my life nine years ago. Then you pull this divorce stunt. What, is this your sick way of punishing me? Taking my kid because I didn't love you? Well, guess what? I fucking hate you."

He’s panting, chest heaving. His words hit like hollow-point bullets, tearing right through whatever was left of my heart.

"I... I..."

What do you even say to that? How do you answer the man you love telling you he hates your guts?

"Get the fuck out of my house," he spits. "I'll drop Asher off later."

I slide the papers onto the counter, my hands shaking. I open my mouth to apologize, but my phone buzzes in my purse. I pull it out. Screen reads: MOM.

I want to decline it, but she only calls when the world is ending. I swipe answer and press it to my ear.

"Hey, Mo—"

"Hospital. Now. Your father's been shot!" she screams, hysterical, and the line goes dead.

The phone slips through my fingers, clattering onto the floor. I freeze, totally numb.

"What?" Declan's voice cuts through the fog.

My heart is hammering against my ribs. I scoop up the phone, staring at the blank screen. "My dad," I whisper. "He's been shot."
