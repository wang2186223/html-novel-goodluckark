NovelVibe

Chapter 391

I actually own one of their products. It used to be my absolute go-to. Well, past tense, because Orion and I are a thing now, so it’s just gathering dust. But seriously, you can’t tell the difference. It feels so damn lifelike. They make other gear, sure, but their personal massagers are my holy grail.

"Yeah, well, it took a ton of digging, and we all got our hands dirty. Honestly, the whole R&D phase was a blast," Lyra says, flashing a grin.

"Since you're such a fan of our stuff," Piper smirks, leaning in. "Why not buy in? Become a partner."

My brows knit together. "I don't know, guys. Orion just handed my family's firm back to me. If I link up with an adult toy brand, won't that tank our reputation? People are so incredibly judgy."

"Relax," Lyra cuts in. "We’re basically ghosts here. We’ve got a CEO and a VP running the public show. They’re the face. We pull the strings. Sure, they help, but literally nobody knows we started this thing except those two and our husbands."

God, it’s tempting. Seriously tempting.

"Sleep on it," Celeste suggests. "Let us know when you're ready."

"Deal. But I've gotta run it by Orion first."

"Totally fine!" Piper chirps, still buzzing with energy.

We fall quiet, sipping our drinks. I look at them, totally floored. My friends are absolute bosses. Having women like this in my corner is a massive wake-up call. Seeing what they’ve built makes me want to step up my own game. Big time.

Lyra lets out this heavy, exhausted sigh.

"What's up?" Piper asks. "Baby keeping you up?"

"No. It's Asher."

We all look over. Asher is straight-up freezing Piper out, acting like she’s invisible. Clara Sterling already filled me in on her massive crush on him, and honestly, my heart breaks for her.

"I swear, I don't get that kid!" Lyra fumes. "He’s sweet to literally everyone else. Piper is amazing, and I adore her. It kills me to see him treat her like garbage."

"Maybe try talking to him?" I say, feeling this sudden, weird ache tighten in my chest.

"I did! I'm out of ideas. She’s so obviously crazy about him. She literally told me once she’s going to marry him someday. But that’s no excuse for him to be a total jerk. Why does he hate her so much?"

I feel her pain. It hits way too close to home, and not just because she’s Clara’s bestie.

"Give them time. They’re just kids," Piper chimes in, giving Lyra’s hand a reassuring squeeze. "Maybe they’ll figure it out when they grow up a bit."

Lyra sighs again. "I hope so. Honestly, I’d love to have Piper as a daughter-in-law. I just hope Asher doesn't trash something that could be beautiful. They’re young, but there’s this weird spark between them. I can't explain it. I just hope my son isn't dumb enough to blow it."

She excuses herself and marches straight toward Asher. There’s just something about that girl that got under my skin. I still can’t put my finger on it.

I lean back in my seat, watching Piper, trying to figure out why she draws me in like a moth to a flame.

Nothing clicks, no matter how hard I think. Eventually, I just drop it and claim Piper as my own. I had no clue the answer would hit me years down the road, when she’s all grown up and staring down a massive, unthinkable tragedy.
