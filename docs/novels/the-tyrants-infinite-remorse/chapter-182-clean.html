Chapter 184

Graham had no clue. He thought Caspian was just naturally tough. He didn't know about the years of brutal pack training, or that Caspian wasn't even human. Not like him, anyway. Werewolves slipped into human spaces like ghosts. Nobody noticed them. But to a wolf, spotting a human was a joke. Humans stank. They had this loud, unmistakable scent that screamed *human* from a mile away.

Caspian let out a dry, raspy chuckle. It grated through his teeth. Every single step felt like a knife dragging through his ribs. He tried to walk straight, but he couldn't hide the hitch in his stride. He was pushing it, and it showed.

"You can't afford me, man," Caspian said, keeping his voice dead level despite the agony. "Unless you've got ten billion a month to throw around, I'll be at your desk tomorrow morning."

Graham snorted, but he nodded. A flash of disappointment crossed his face. He knew Caspian was worth way more than that, and Caspian was already doing him a massive favor by even showing up.

"Look, once I make director, I'm bringing you in the right way. I'll get you what you're actually worth," Graham said. "Guys like you don't just grow on trees." They both laughed, a quick, sharp sound that cut the tension. Then the heavy shit settled right back over them.

"So... you get the files?" Graham asked.

The second the question popped, Elara flashed in Caspian's mind. But whatever. Lysander was on that side of things, and he wouldn't screw it up. Caspian had done his part. Dominic was down, and everything Caspian cared about was safe. Nothing stood between him and Elara now. Well, except for the fact that she owed him big time.

He hadn't figured out how he'd cash in that favor yet, or what he'd even ask for. But deep down, he knew a day would come when he'd need her, and he'd need her bad.

"She got out. We gotta find her to grab the stuff," Caspian said.

They headed toward the triage area. The place was a total shitshow. Sirens wailed, paramedics scrambled between rigs, shouting for gear and checking pulses. Survivors from the fortress were scattered everywhere. Some shivered under shock blankets, some sobbed quietly, and others just stared into space, totally blank.

Caspian didn't give a damn about any of them. His mind was locked on one person.

Elara.

She was out cold when Lysander hauled her out. Now, every tick of the clock made his skin itch. His brain looped through worst-case scenarios. Was she already hooked up to an IV inside? Or was she still sitting in some ambulance out here, waiting for a doctor?

Graham cut into his head. "So, where is she?"

Caspian scanned the mess. If Elara was awake, she'd probably be patching people up. But this place was massive. She could be anywhere.

His phone was still sitting in Lysander's car. Great. He didn't even know Lysander's or Elara's numbers by heart. The realization made him want to kick something.

"We have to hunt her down. She's lost in this crowd," Caspian muttered, his voice tight with irritation. "Let's find Lysander. Wherever he is, she'll be right there."

He was sure of it. Though, thinking about how Lysander secretly pined for her made Caspian's brow twitch.

"Over there," Graham said, pointing.

Caspian squinted. "I don't see shit. Just people."

But Graham was already moving. He pointed again, but an agent cut him off. "Sir, they just brought Dominic in. Where do we put him?"

"Check the ER. He's busted up bad," Graham barked, his eyes still locked on the crowd. "Wait, isn't that Lysander?"

Caspian whipped his head around. His chest went tight. Elara was walking straight toward them. She was right next to Lysander, wearing his jacket.

A massive wave of relief hit him, knocking the wind right out of him. The knot in his chest unraveled. She was fine. Walking on her own. Unhurt.

A smirk started to twitch on his lips.

Then she reached him. Without a word, she lunged and threw a hard, clean right hook straight into his jaw. *Crack.*

"You don't know shit about teamwork," she spat. Her eyes burned with pure rage. She glared at him for a split second, then blew right past him. No stopping. No looking back.

Caspian stood there, frozen. His face stung, but the shock hurt way worse than the punch.

Beside him, Graham's jaw was on the floor. He looked at Caspian, then at Elara, totally lost.

It wasn't until Lysander walked up and filled them in that the guilt finally hit Caspian, heavy and cold in his gut. It hurt worse than his bruised ribs. Damn. How the hell was he going to make this up to her?
