Chapter 327

Elara saw right through Caspian. He tried to play it cool, but his eyes gave him away. The guy was spiraling. The mere thought of Thane having anything to do with Esperanza was eating him alive.

Honestly? It made her sick to her stomach too. But she wasn't going to hand him some cheap, fake reassurance. Not without hard proof.

"Look, we don't say anything until the lab results are back," she said. She grabbed his hand, giving it a firm squeeze. "No guessing games. We wait for the facts."

Caspian didn't relax. His jaw stayed locked. He was trying to hide the tension, but he was failing miserably.

She leaned in. "Hey. Look at me, V." He finally met her eyes. "Thane wanted that baby dead. Remember that."

His face went pitch black.

"Even if the DNA says she's mine," she said, dead serious, "you are her dad. Period. That piece of shit doesn't get to claim a kid he tried to murder."

Anger flared in her chest. The memories hit her like a truck—the lies, the agony, the years she’d never get back. All because of him.

"To me, Thane already killed his kid," she spat, her voice dropping to a freezing whisper. "And he's still going to pay for it."

A wave of fierce, mama-bear energy surged through her. Esperanza deserved the world. Elara let out a breath, a slow smile returning to her lips. "Esperanza is ours, Caspian. Yours and mine."

Boom. Just like that, the tension drained right out of his shoulders.

He let out a breath he didn't even know he was holding. His grip on her hand tightened, and a real, genuine grin broke across his face.

He grabbed his fork, scooped up some scrambled eggs, and shoved it right toward her mouth. "Open up."

She laughed, shaking her head. "Caspian, seriously—"

"Open."

She rolled her eyes but took the bite anyway, chewing as he smirked like he’d just won a medal. Feeding her was officially his new favorite hobby. "Fine," she mumbled.

His grin widened. "Good girl." He leaned back, his face turning serious again. "Alright. Let's run both. Maternity and paternity."

She nodded. Yeah. That was the only way. No more guessing, just cold, hard data.

"But there's something else," he said, his eyes narrowing in thought. "We need to head out to the pack. Tonight."

She blinked. "Wait, tonight?"

He nodded. "We'll dump the wedding planning on Lysander."

She snorted. Poor Lysander. The guy had no clue he was about to get saddled with a whole-ass wedding.

"I need the exact date of the Blood Moon Festival to deal with Thane," Caspian said, his eyes darkening again. "And we need to get your memories back. For real this time."

The room went quiet. Elara sat there, letting it sink in. She thought about how far they'd come since they crossed paths again.

Honestly, the idea of remembering used to scare the shit out of her. She’d been terrified of what she’d find, terrified of the mate bond messing up her life. But now? Everything had changed.

She stared at him. Really looked at him. This guy wasn't some stranger anymore. Somehow, she’d fallen for him all over again. Not because of some magic bond or old history, but because of the man standing right in front of her. The stubborn-ass alpha, the crazy protective dad, the guy who literally learned to cook just to make her breakfast.

She smiled. Yeah, she wanted those memories. She wanted their history, their first kiss, their first fight—every single piece they’d lost. Plus, there was no way the universe was cruel enough to bring her back to her kid just to pair them up with other people. Right? God, she hoped not.

"Okay," she said, nodding. "I'll swing by the office first to clear my schedule. Then I'm heading straight to the hospital."

"To see Julian?"

"Yeah. I need to talk to him." She tapped her fingers on the table, thinking fast. "And I'll drop off our DNA samples at the lab."

Before she could start listing her whole to-do list, Caspian reached out and grabbed both of her hands. He pushed the plates aside, locking his eyes onto hers. His gaze softened, turning intense and warm.

"Babe," he murmured, his thumbs tracing circles on the back of her hands. "You're carrying way too much right now."

Elara frowned. He wasn't wrong. Work, the wedding, Esperanza, her missing memories, the pack trip, the DNA tests... her life was a circus.

Caspian smiled, a little sad. "Look, I can't fix everything for you," he admitted. If he could carry her weight, he’d do it in a heartbeat. "But can you at least let me handle the small stuff?"

She blinked, confused. "Like what?"

He took a deep breath, staring right into her eyes. "Honestly?" A smirk tugged at his lips. "I want us to have a ton of kids in the future."

Her face flushed hot instantly. The sudden pivot caught her completely off guard.

But Caspian wasn't joking. He looked like he’d already planned out their entire family tree. "I don't want you dealing with all this stress alone," he said softly.

Something warm and gooey melted in her chest. He wasn't just talking about babies. He was talking about being her rock, her partner.

"I can't get pregnant," he teased, flashing a quick grin. "Trust me, if I could, I'd take the hit for you."

Elara burst out laughing.

Caspian chuckled, then sobered up. "But seriously. You know how it goes. The stronger the alpha, the rougher the pregnancy."

His words hit a nerve, dragging up old, buried memories. God, those months had been brutal. The sleepless nights, the constant body aches, the sheer exhaustion. By the third trimester, she’d had to drop out of her classes. Even with Eleanor helping her, it had been a living hell.

She looked down. Back then, she’d gone through all of that completely alone. Now, she had a man standing right next to her, begging to share the load.

Her chest tight with emotion, she looked back up at him, her eyes soft. "So," she whispered, her voice cracking slightly, "what exactly are you trying to say, Caspian?"
