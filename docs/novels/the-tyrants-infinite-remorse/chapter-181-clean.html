Chapter 183

The puzzle pieces clicked, and Dominic’s stomach dropped. Elara and Caspian. Partners from day one. The truth hit him like a freight train.

But hell no, he wasn't done. This was his turf. He built this damn fortress to keep himself safe. His guys were still out there, hidden and armed.

Kill Caspian, kill the bomb, salvage the business, and slip away before the feds showed up. Simple. If that went south, he’d just bolt to his backup safehouse and start from scratch.

Dominic forced his spine straight, ignoring the agony screaming through his ribs. "You sound just like her," he spat. "But you don't get to end me. Guards!"

His shout bounced off the walls. Nothing. Just dead silence.

Caspian let out a dry, mocking laugh. It stung worse than a slap.

"Seriously, dude?" Caspian smirked, eyes cold as ice. "Still clueless? Your crew is wiped out. The survivors are in zip-ties getting grilled, and every single hostage is already out of here."

Cold dread finally crept into Dominic’s chest.

One woman. Elara. She did this.

She walked right into his house, ripped his empire to shreds, trashed his life's work, and burned it all to the ground.

Caspian took a step forward. His voice was quiet, but each word cut like a blade. "The girl you wrote off? Yeah, she hacked your network. She took everything. Files, casino ledgers, wire transfers, tapes—you name it. She’s got your other spots mapped out too. They’re getting raided next. With what we have, you'll rot in a supermax forever if you're lucky."

While he talked, Caspian prayed Elara’s kid was safe with the rescued hostages. That was the whole point of this suicide mission.

She’d almost died tonight just to get her little girl back. The thought of her sacrifice made his blood boil.

Dominic snapped. Pain and pure humiliation boiled over.

"Screw you!" he screamed, lunging forward. "I'll rip you apart, find that bitch, and carve her up myself!"

He charged. Caspian didn't flinch. He met him halfway.

Their foreheads collided with a sickening crack. Dominic stumbled back, his brain rattling inside his skull.

Caspian didn't miss a beat. As Dominic tried to scramble up, Caspian planted a heavy boot right into his chest, sending him crashing back down.

"I've been waiting for this," Caspian growled, chest heaving.

Dominic grabbed Caspian’s ankle, yanked hard, and dragged him down. They hit the floor in a tangled mess.

Dominic tried to mount him, but Caspian flipped the script. He rolled, pinned Dominic down, and started raining fists onto his face.

No technique. Just pure, unadulterated violence.

Every blow carried weeks of bottled-up rage. The anger of watching Elara suffer in Dominic's twisted world.

Bones popped. Blood splattered.

Caspian stopped seeing a man. He just saw the monster who hurt Elara.

He hadn't beaten anyone this bad in years. By the time he stopped, Dominic was a broken, bloody heap.

Caspian wanted to kill him. He really did. But he stopped. Dominic needed to face the music for the lives he ruined and the bodies he harvested.

Suddenly, Caspian’s watch beeped. Loud. Sixty seconds before the place blew.

He glanced at the window. No time for the stairs.

His eyes scanned the floor and snagged on one of Elara’s high heels.

Bingo. He snatched the stiletto and hurled it. The sharp heel shattered the glass of the French doors.

Good enough.

Caspian grabbed Dominic’s limp body, hauled him up, and threw them both through the shattered glass just as the bomb detonated.

The shockwave hit them mid-air. The world went white.

They slammed hard onto a car hood below, bounced, and rolled onto the asphalt.

Behind them, the massive fortress folded like a house of cards.

Thick black smoke choked the sky as fire devoured the ruins.

Sirens wailed. Cop cars swarmed the lawn.

Fire trucks, medics, and FBI agents flooded the scene, sorting through the chaos.

Graham jogged over as Caspian was dragging himself up.

Dominic, a bloody mess, was already cuffed and loaded into an ambulance under heavy guard.

Graham hauled Caspian to his feet, looking at him with pure respect.

"Told you, man," Graham grinned. "You belong in the Bureau. I still got a spot for you."

Caspian’s reply knocked the wind right out of him.
