Chapter 155

Nobody saw the kid slip out of the backseat. Asher stumbled forward, legs shaking but determined, and slammed his tiny arms around Elara’s knees. He clung to her like a lifeline.

It wasn’t the tug on her jeans that got her. It was his voice—cracked, tiny, and hitting her right in the ribs.

She looked down. Fast.

Asher stared up, his big eyes shining with a look she hadn’t seen in forever. Hope. It wrecked her, digging up stuff she’d spent months trying to bury.

"Mommy," he whispered, his grip tightening as he started to shake. "I was bad. You can lock me in my room, okay? I’m sorry. But... can you make me pancakes? Please?"

The words felt like a punch. A kid shouldn't carry that kind of guilt.

Behind them, Lysander froze. His tough-guy act cracked, eyes watering up. He wasn't the only one. Elara blinked hard, wiping a tear before it could even track down her cheek. No way she was showing weakness now.

"Fine," she said, keeping her voice dead flat despite the knot in her throat. "I'll make the pancakes." She paused, setting the boundary. "But get one thing straight. I’m not your mommy."

She figured he’d drop his hands and back off.

Nope. The kid squeezed harder, burying his face in her legs.

"No," he mumbled. "You are. You're my mommy."

Elara let out a sharp breath, her body going rigid. She tried to pry herself loose, but the kid was like glue. Before things got weirder, Lysander stepped in. He dropped to one knee, getting eye-to-eye with the boy.

"Hey, bud," Lysander said, keeping it soft. "Let Uncle take you inside, yeah?"

Asher looked up and actually managed a weak, exhausted smile. "Uncle Lysander," he mumbled. "Punish me."

Lysander’s face softened—a rare sight. "Nah, kiddo. Knowing you messed up is enough. Besides, Elara’s not mad." He shot a quick look at her, then back to the boy. "Let go of her so she can get cooking."

That did the trick.

Asher’s fingers slid off her jeans. But the second he stood on his own, his knees buckled. He swayed, head spinning.

Before he hit the dirt, Elara lunged. She scooped him up, holding him tight against her chest.

The moment she had him, her brain went into overdrive.

She checked him over, taking in the dry lips, the pale skin. Her face went from worried to pure ice. She whipped her head toward Thane.

"Are you freaking kidding me?" she spat, her voice sharp enough to draw blood. "What kind of father are you? He’s starving and dehydrated. Do you have any clue what this does to his body?"

Thane didn't even try to argue. He looked totally defeated. "He wouldn't eat," he muttered, voice barely a whisper. "Kept saying he only wanted your pancakes. Nothing else."

Elara gave him one last, burning glare, then turned her back on him. She adjusted the kid in her arms and marched toward the house.

Lysander followed, hopping into his ride and rolling past the gates. Thane, though? Different story.

Thane took a step forward, but the heavy iron gates slammed shut right in his face. Clang. Message received.

"Just because she’s helping the kid doesn't mean you're off the hook," Lysander called out coldly from the other side.

Thane stopped dead. He stared at the locked gate, then slowly backed away. No point fighting it. He walked back to his car, climbed in, and prepped to spend the night in the driver's seat. The cramped space didn't matter. Only one thing did.

Asher was safe.

Staring out into the dark, Thane leaned his head back. Memories started hitting him. Back when Elara was his Managing Director, life was easy. She ran everything. No complaints, no drama, no asking for credit.

Even when Asher got sick back then, Thane never panicked. He never felt this crushing weight. Why? Because Elara always handled it. Quietly.

But now? Whole different ballgame.

Now he was paying the price for being blind. For taking her for granted.

A sharp ring cut through the quiet. Thane frowned. Not his ringtone. He rummaged around the console until his hand hit a phone. He checked the screen, his face darkening.

Briar’s phone.

Caller ID: Orion.

Thane pressed his lips together, anger flaring. He swiped accept but stayed dead silent, just listening.

Orion didn't waste time.

"Briar, what the hell is this?" Orion barked, sounding pissed. "You playing games with me? If I don't get my cash by tomorrow, I'm showing up at the pack. Seriously." He let out a nasty laugh. "I'd love to see how you explain that to your little Chief Commander boy toy."

Thane’s jaw clenched. Pure, cold fury flooded his chest.

*Chief Commander boy toy.*

"Briar? You there or what? Stop messing around," Orion snapped.

Thane kept his mouth shut, letting the anger simmer. He weighed his options. Should he rip into the guy now, or let him keep talking and dig his own grave?
