Chapter 5

Briar’s legs gave out. Thane caught her mid-drop, scooped her up, and let out a guttural roar. The copper stink of fresh blood hit his nose hard. It triggered his inner beast. He spun on his heel, aiming for the medical wing, but Briar’s shaking fingers yanked his shirt collar.

"Rav... wait," she wheezed. "She’s running."

Thane froze. He blinked, tracking Briar’s gaze.

Red pooled from Briar’s wrists, soaking his sleeves. But Briar didn't care about the cuts. She stared straight at Elara. Elara stood a few feet away, back straight, a tiny duffel bag sitting by her boots.

"Running?" Thane’s voice cracked. No way. That bag couldn't hold her life. "Where the hell is she going?"

It was past midnight. The mansion was dead quiet. Long, creepy shadows stretched across the marble floor. That stupid little bag made his stomach twist with pure rage.

Briar wasn't about to let Elara just walk. No freaking way. Elara had played queen for too long. Just once, Briar wanted to watch her hit rock bottom.

"It’s just a scratch," Briar lied quickly, keeping her voice steady but tight. "It'll heal. Eventually." She gulped, ramping up the drama. "But her? She tried to bolt. I tried to stop her, and she freaking stabbed me."

The lie hung in the air. Elara tilted her head. A smirk played on her lips.

"Still a liar, I see," Elara said. Her voice was cool. Way too cool. But those icy blue eyes flashed with a promise that made Briar shrink back.

"Aren't you scared of what I'll do next?" Elara whispered.

Briar flinched. Thane snapped.

"Lysander!" he bellowed, shaking the walls.

Heavy boots pounded the stairs. Lysander appeared, stiff and ready for a fight. "Chief Commander."

Thane glared at Elara like she was trash, then locked eyes with Lysander. "Throw her in the dungeon," he barked, pointing a finger. "Keep her there till I figure out how to ruin her."

Nobody breathed. But Lysander didn't budge. "Can't do that, sir."

"Excuse me?" Thane snarled, pivoting. His fuse was gone. "I said, lock her up."

The dungeon was old-school. They used to throw rogue wolves and traitors in there back when things were bloody. Now? Nobody used it. It had been gathering dust for ten years.

"Sorry, Chief," Lysander said, his voice flat but stubborn. "I'm not doing it."

A collective gasp sucked the air out of the room. Elara’s eyes went wide. Caspian’s did too.

"She’s still my boss," Lysander added.

Briar bit her lip, trying not to scream. What did Elara have on this guy? Why the hell did he respect her so much?

Thane let out a nasty laugh. "We're getting a divorce," he spat. "She signed the papers. She’s not your Managing Director anymore."

Lysander didn't blink. "Then do it yourself, Chief. The paperwork isn't processed yet. Technically, she’s still my boss. Only you have the legal right to lay a hand on her."

The logic was airtight. Thane hated it. Gritting his teeth, he set Briar down on a chair. He stood up, squared his shoulders, and stalked toward Elara like a predator.

Elara kept her chin up. Zero fear. "Touch me," she dared, "and I’m calling the Council. You know what they'll do to you."

Thane sneered. "The Council needs proof. Witnesses. You’ve got zero."

God, he hated her. He hated how she stood between him and Briar. He hated that she was a walking reminder of a bond he wanted dead and buried.

Well, at least she signed the papers. Soon, she’d be nobody.

"I'm her witness."

Lysander’s voice sliced through the room. Thane froze, jaw dropping.

"Do you have any idea what you're saying right now?" Thane growled, his eyes turning murderous.

Lysander stared right back. "Yeah, Chief. I do." He reached into his jacket, pulled out a white envelope, and slapped it into Thane’s hand. "Which is why I'm quitting. Consider this my resignation as Deputy."

The world spun. Even Caspian, who’d been playing spectator, couldn't stay quiet. "Are you seriously throwing your pack away," he gasped, "for a woman like her?"

He couldn't even find the words to insult Elara properly. Lysander turned to him, totally calm. "Caspian," he said, "watch your mouth."

Briar’s eyes darted back and forth. Panic set in. She saw Caspian’s gaze soften as he looked at Elara. She couldn't let that happen.

"She’s never going to leave Thane!" Briar blurted. "She used to brag about it constantly. Ask anyone in the pack, or the other Lunas! If she crawls back to him, Lysander, you’re going to look like a total clown."

Lysander gave a cold, faint smile. "I'm not quitting because of Elara."

"Then why?" Thane and Caspian demanded together.

Lysander swallowed. "It's personal."

Elara let out a dry laugh. "Honestly? I hope you two live happily ever after," she said, deadpan. "Now that I think about it, you two idiots deserve each other."

Lysander kept smiling, but Thane lost his mind. "Elara," he roared, "if I didn't respect your parents..." He stopped, his voice turning ice-cold. "You won't get a single dime of alimony. Not until you get on your knees and apologize to Briar."

Briar smirked, watching Elara like a hawk. She couldn't wait. How would Elara survive without his cash? Would she beg? Or would she starve in the gutter?

But what Elara did next shocked everyone in the room.
