Chapter 102

Caspian ditched Julian and spotted Thane almost instantly. The room was packed with investors, waitstaff, and nosey guests, but Caspian could always pick his most trusted ally out of any crowd.

Thane was basically his brother. They had gone through hell and back together, winning some and losing some. Nobody got Caspian's messy life quite like him.

Caspian locked onto that connection. Very few people in their circle could offer him that kind of understanding.

Thane had this way of fixing Caspian's messes quietly. No awkward questions, no picking at old scabs. He just cleaned things up and acted like nothing happened, giving Caspian some damn breathing room from his own baggage.

The last thing Caspian needed was someone constantly pointing at his scars. Thane got that, respecting the boundary without Caspian ever having to say a word.

That was the glue keeping them tight for years.

But Thane's crew? Caspian never trusted those guys. The other Chief Commanders had reputations that made his stomach turn.

That bad vibe had been eating at him ever since he eavesdropped on them earlier. Plus, he couldn't shake the image of Elara collapsing, poisoned by wolfbane. It kept looping in his head.

Caspian pushed through the crowd, dodging tables and rich snobs. A cold, hard plan clicked into place.

Thane was his best friend, sure. But Caspian made a silent vow: if Thane had even a finger in what happened to Elara tonight, they were done. Period. No amount of history or shared memories would save him. Some lines you just don't cross.

"Thane!" Caspian barked, spotting him.

Thane was holding court by a high-top table, laughing and shooting the breeze with some investors, looking totally relaxed.

Caspian's voice sliced right through the chatter. Thane's laugh died instantly. His shoulders went rigid before he even spun around.

Looking confused, Thane excused himself and walked over.

"Caspian, what's up, man?" Thane asked, checking out Caspian's stiff posture with genuine curiosity.

Caspian didn't say a word at first. He just closed the distance, leaning in close so the nearby humans wouldn't catch a single whisper.

"What did you do to Sera?" Caspian growled, barely holding back his rage.

The question carried a heavy, dark history. Caspian hadn't forgotten what Thane did to his own kid, all because Elara was the mother.

If Thane could discard his own blood over a grudge against Elara, he was capable of anything.

This wasn't about trust anymore. It was about knowing exactly how dark his best friend could get when his ego or revenge took the wheel.

Thane blinked, totally lost. "Wait, what?"

Caspian leaned in closer, jaw clenched. "Someone slipped her wolfbane tonight. Tell me you didn't have a hand in this."

Caspian locked eyes with him, watching for any twitch, any tell, any hint of guilt.

Thane looked completely blindsided. But Caspian had survived too long in the Commander world to trust a pretty face.

Thane's brow furrowed, genuine worry taking over.

"No way," Thane said, shaking his head fast. "Look, I hate her guts, but I don't want her dead. I want her to suffer for what she did to Briar."

Every Commander knew what wolfbane did. Without the antidote, it slowly eats a wolf from the inside out until they die.

Slipping her that stuff meant someone wanted her in a coffin. Caspian wasn't about to let that slide.

"Your boys did this, Thane," Caspian said, his voice tight with anger.

Thane stared at him, catching something weird in Caspian's tone. It almost sounded like Caspian actually cared about Elara.

Caspian quickly covered. "Everyone here is technically my responsibility tonight, you know."

Understanding hit Thane, and he frowned. "My boys? Dude, everyone here is my friend."

Caspian's jaw tightened. "Finn and Riven. I heard them talking."

Thane's eyes went wide. "What? Seriously?"

"Look, I texted them earlier to invest in Elara's company for me," Thane blurted out. "You know I can't talk to her directly after all that drama. And after she blocked your server, I thought she might actually pull this off. I desperately need cash since she pulled her funding."

Caspian stared, thrown off, as Thane kept talking.

"If Finn did something crazy, she must've rejected their offer," Thane said. "They tripled my original investment amount, so they were seriously trying to fund her little game. But I never told them to poison her!"

Caspian narrowed his eyes, pressing harder. "Not even to get even for those leaked nudes of yours?"

The question hung heavy in the air, but Thane shook his head immediately, looking pissed.

"I told you," Thane snapped. "I want her to suffer, and dying is too easy. I had nothing to do with the poison."

Caspian stayed quiet, reading Thane's face, weighing his words.

He hated Thane's petty grudge, but the panic and frustration in his eyes looked real. This wasn't the face of a guilty man.

Caspian let out a slow breath, the tension in his chest easing up.

"Fine," Caspian said. "I'll handle it."

Before Thane could reply, Caspian turned on his heel, scanning the crowd until he spotted his targets.

Finn and Riven were hanging near the exit, playing it cool, smiling and saying goodbye to guests, trying to slip out unnoticed.

Rage flared in Caspian's chest.

"Hey! Stop!" Caspian roared across the room.

Both men froze. They looked back for a split second, panic written all over their faces when they saw him.

Then, they bolted.

And that was the biggest mistake they could have made.
