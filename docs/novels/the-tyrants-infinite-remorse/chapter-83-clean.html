Chapter 83

The drama finally died down. For the first time in days, the noise stopped, and Caspian actually slept. Just a few hours, but his brain stopped buzzing. Then his eyes snapped open. Before he could even stretch, his phone buzzed. The screen flashed. Security alerts. Bohemian Grove. One after another.

The notifications piled up fast. He didn't waste time. He threw off the covers, hit the shower, and threw on a sleek black suit—perfect for the crowd he was about to face. He bolted out the door. He didn't even look at the video feed until he was behind the wheel.

Elara’s face popped up on the screen. Caspian let out a long breath. Damn. Trouble. Big trouble. The exact kind of mess where Thane always lost his mind.

Caspian had sworn to stay out of their toxic little game. The divorce was done. The war should've been over. But Thane was a hothead. Pride always beat logic with that guy. If Elara pushed his buttons in public, Thane would swing first and think later. It would ruin him.

So, Caspian had to move. He walked into the venue just as Thane’s hand went up. He caught Thane's wrist a split second before it hit Elara’s face. Everyone was staring at the drama, so nobody even saw Caspian slip inside.

Unlike Thane, Caspian knew exactly what Elara could do. He hadn't forgotten how she cleaned out his bank accounts. He still felt that sting. She looked like a saint, but she was pure poison.

But Thane’s reaction caught him off guard. Thane glared at him, looking totally betrayed. "Do you have any idea what the hell she just did?" Thane spat, his jaw clenched.

"Doesn't matter," Caspian said, cold as ice. He shoved Thane’s arm down. Right then, Julian grabbed Elara and pulled her back, shielding her.

Caspian gripped Thane’s shoulder and dragged him away from the crowd. He leaned in, keeping his voice low but lethal. "What the hell are you doing, man? She’s baiting you. Can't you see that? She wants to bleed you dry again."

Thane froze. The rage left his eyes, replaced by sudden panic. He’d figured if he left her alone, she’d leave him alone. That was his dumb logic. But now, replaying the last ten seconds, it clicked. A public screaming match. A room full of witnesses. His hand raised. If he’d touched her—even a scratch—she’d have a golden ticket to sue him for every dime.

"She cornered me," Thane muttered, his face falling as the truth hit him. "But... yeah. You're right. I almost fell for it. My bad."

"You almost ruined everything," Caspian snapped, not hiding his disgust. "You're the host here. Act like a leader, not some psycho who can't control his temper."

That stung. Thane actually looked guilty. Caspian’s sudden, brutal intervention had completely rattled him. For a split second, a crazy thought popped into Thane's head—was Caspian protecting Elara because he had feelings for her?

But he shook it off. No way. This was Caspian. The guy didn't do feelings. Human, wolf, whatever—women were just a distraction to him. Thane felt stupid for even thinking it.

"I screwed up," Thane said quietly.

"Yeah, you did," Caspian said, zero mercy.

Caspian turned back to the crowd. He squared his shoulders, his face instantly turning into a mask of cool control. "Sorry about the little drama, folks," he called out, his voice cutting right through the room. "And sorry I'm late. The party starts now. Talk shop, mingle, and have a good time."

The room instantly relaxed. The contrast between the two men was brutal. Thane demanded respect like a bully; Caspian just had it. Smooth jazz kicked back in, drowning out the tension. People started talking again. Someone laughed. Glasses clinked.

David Sterling walked right up and gave Caspian a firm handshake. "Glad you made it, man."

"Me too," Caspian said, smooth as silk.

Out of the corner of his eye, Caspian saw Julian grab a drink and hand it to Elara, looking at her like she was the only girl in the room.

"Excuse me," Caspian muttered to Mark, then headed straight for them.

He stopped right in front of Julian. "Caspian, right?"

Julian turned, sticking his hand out confidently. "Caspian. Good to see you again." Even in a room full of guys in custom-tailored suits, Caspian owned the space. It wasn't just his looks. He had this heavy, unshakeable presence.

"Your old man talks a big game about you," Caspian said. "Take that job he's offering, and you're officially in the club."

Julian took a slow breath. "Look, it's the dream. But medicine is my thing. I don't know how I'm supposed to run a massive corporation and still take care of my patients."

"It's totally doable," Elara chimed in, her voice smooth. "You just need the right team. Delegate the boring stuff."

Julian looked at her, totally whipped. It wasn't just because she was hot. It was the sheer confidence dripping from her. Caspian watched them, taking it all in.

Then, a lightbulb went off. "Sera," Caspian said, eyeing her up. "How did you even get past security?" She looked different. Glowing. Like she'd been living the high life. But she was the last person he expected here. Knowing her, she'd never play the role of some guy's trophy date.

Julian cleared his throat. "Oh, my bad. She's my girlfriend. Actually, how do you two even know each other?"

*Girlfriend.* The word bounced around Caspian's head. No wonder Thane lost his mind. Was Thane jealous? Hell no. Thane was obsessed with Briar. But Elara showing up with a human? That was wild. Was she trying to make a point, or had she actually moved on?

"Huh. Interesting," Caspian said slowly. "So, how long have you guys been a thing?"

He caught the quick, tense look they exchanged.
