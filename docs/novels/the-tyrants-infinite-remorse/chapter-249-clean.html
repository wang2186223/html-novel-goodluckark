Chapter 252

Elara felt the brutal weight crushing down on his back. He was taking it all, bracing hard, refusing to let the heavy trunk flatten them.

Then came the sound. It made her stomach do a sick flip.

A wet, heavy crack. Bone giving way. The vibration shuddered straight through his body into hers—she felt the snap more than she heard it. His inner wolf was clawing at the surface, desperate to take the wheel and shoulder the load.

Shouts exploded around them. Warriors swarmed the spot. The crushing pressure suddenly vanished as a bunch of guys shoved their shoulders under the trunk and rolled the damn thing off him.

Caspian didn't move right away. He hovered over her for a beat too long, then pushed up just enough to look down. Rain slid off his jawline, dripping right onto her cheek. His eyes darted over her face, checking for damage.

"You good?"

She opened her mouth to speak.

Then, a heavy vibration rattled the mud. Something massive was hauling ass toward them. A giant wolf burst through the treeline, shifting mid-stride. In a blink, the beast folded back into a man.

Thane stood there in the downpour, chest heaving, completely out of breath. His eyes were wild—that look when your brain is panicking but your body is still catching up.

"Caspian, Sera—"

Then he froze.

Whatever he was about to yell got stuck in his throat. His eyes dropped. He stared at Caspian hovering over her, both of them soaked to the bone, tangled up in the dirt. Thane just stood in the rain, totally lost for words, his face a mess of mixed emotions.

One of the guys stepped up and tossed Thane a dry emergency wrap. It was soaked before he even got it on. The rain was coming down like a waterfall, but honestly, nobody gave a shit about dry clothes right now. Thane couldn't take his eyes off them.

Elara was flat on her back in the muck. Caspian was pinned right over her, completely drenched, the rain hammering them like it had a personal vendetta.

Caspian tried to shift, attempting to back off and give her space, but his muscles locked up.

A low, ugly groan ripped from his throat—the kind of sound you make when pain hits you out of nowhere. He went totally rigid.

"Ah, shit—"

"Don't move." Elara's voice was dead steady. No panic. No screaming. Just pure, cold doctor mode. Her brain was already miles ahead of everyone else in the storm. "Stay completely still, Caspian."

The nearest warriors lunged forward, hands out, ready to haul him up.

"Stop!" Elara snapped, throwing a hand up without even looking at them. "Don't touch him. He's got a fracture. You grab him wrong, and you'll wreck his spine."

The guys backed off instantly.

Thane let out a long breath he'd been holding since he cleared the gate. The tension in his chest snapped. The crazy scenario his brain had cooked up in those three seconds completely evaporated.

He saw it now. The fallen tree. The crushed roof on Elara's side of the car. The mud. The way Caspian had thrown his body over hers like a human shield.

It wasn't what it looked like.

And it made sense why Caspian's wolf hadn't healed him yet. A break that bad, right near the spine, meant even a killer wolf had to wait for a manual reset before the magic kicked in.

"Caspian." Elara's voice snapped everyone back to reality. "Look at me."

His eyes locked onto hers. The usual walls were gone. Pain has a way of stripping a guy's armor off, and right now he looked almost raw. His jaw was clenched, his gaze glued to her like she was his only lifeline in the storm.

"Can you wiggle your hands?"

He gave a tight nod, rain dripping from his hair onto her face.

"Okay. Plant your palms flat on the ground. Hold yourself up. I'm going to slide out, and then I'll pop that bone back in. Can you do that?"

Another nod.

He went to plant his hands, but Gideon was being a total dick.

*I'm actually loving this,* the wolf snorted in his head, completely chill. *Why do you think I haven't fixed us yet?*

Caspian bit his lip so hard he almost drew blood. The pain was blinding, but Gideon was just basking in it like a dog by a fireplace.

Still, Caspian pressed his palms into the slush and pushed. His arms shook violently as he forced himself into a shaky plank, his spine screaming in agony.

The second she had clearance, Elara slid out from under him, fast and clean.

But the mud had other plans.

Caspian's grip gave out. He faceplanted straight into the muck.

He lay there, face-down in the dirt, rain pelting his head, refusing to make a sound.

*You better heal me the second she's done,* he growled at Gideon inside his head. *Or I swear to God, you'll never see her again.*

Gideon went dead silent. Total radio silence, acting like he hadn't heard a thing.

Caspian squeezed his eyes shut.

"It's okay," Elara murmured, dropping to her knees in the wet dirt. "Just stay still."

The rain was blinding. She blinked it out of her eyes, shoving her wet hair back with one hand. With the other, she grabbed the hem of his soaked shirt and yanked it up. She needed her hands to do the talking since she couldn't see worth a damn in this storm.

Her fingers traced his spine, pressing lightly, reading the bones through her fingertips with practiced ease.

*Damn, he is so hot,* Blaze purred in the back of her mind. Elara felt her cheeks instantly heat up, completely embarrassed by her wolf's shameless flirting.
