Chapter 54

Thane’s blowup hit Caspian right in the gut. He froze. Then he glanced at Elara. She was wearing this tiny, smug smirk. Boom—the pieces fell into place. This wasn't just about cash, blackmail, or watching him sweat. The chick was systematically wrecking his bond with Thane, slowly peeling it apart like old tape. Classic divide-and-conquer. It was a brutal, textbook lesson, and Caspian felt every bit of the sting. Note to self: never underestimate a woman again. Don't mistake a pretty face for a soft heart, and never assume a quiet girl won't slit your throat. That exact mistake was why he was stuck in this stupid, quiet cafe while his entire life got shredded. He wanted to kick himself, but regret doesn't fix a damn thing.

Sure, he was worth almost a trillion bucks. His name alone could crash Wall Street. But losing thirty billion dollars? That was a massive slap in the face. It felt like a sick joke, mocking every single year of blood, sweat, and cutthroat deals he’d poured into his empire.

"Look, Thane," Caspian said, keeping his voice dead level. He stared him down, hoping the guy would use his brain. "If I wanted you to pay her off, why wouldn't I just write the check myself?" Thane didn't budge. His jaw just clenched tighter.

"You could've wired the damn money without all this drama!" Thane snapped. He waved his hands around, totally losing his cool and tripping over his words. "I mean, look at you two! Just sitting here like you're on a freaking coffee date!"

Caspian shut his mouth. For once, he had zero comebacks. Meanwhile, Elara just sat there, cool as ice. She had this little, amused grin, like she’d scripted the whole damn show. That smile made Caspian’s stomach do a sick flip. For the first time ever, he felt like a fly stuck in a spider's web. He couldn't see the trap, but he knew he was already wrapped up tight.

"Thane, seriously, use your head," Caspian said. He dropped the boss-man act, sounding totally real. "She played you, and she played me the exact same way. That's why I'm sitting here."

Thane stopped. His brain scrambled to connect the dots. If Caspian and Elara were keeping secrets, Lysander wouldn't be standing right there, watching them like a hawk. Besides, Caspian only got dragged into this mess because he had Thane's back. He got burned because of loyalty.

"My bad," Thane mumbled. He rubbed his face, all the fight leaving him. "I'm... I'm not thinking straight, man."

Elara raised an eyebrow. She was clearly done playing nice. She looked back at Caspian. "Alright," she said, cold as hell. "Pay up."

Lysander moved fast. He stepped up and shoved the bank info in front of him. A second later, Caspian's phone buzzed. He stared at the screen, his face dropping. He looked up slowly. "Wait. This account... it's his?"

Elara leaned forward, making sure he got the message. Her smirk didn't budge. "Duh. What did you think would happen?" she said. "You screwed with his family's stock. You wrecked their business. Actions have consequences, babe. You break it, you buy it."

Caspian balled his fists, his face twitching with rage. He wanted to scream, but he bit his tongue. Since she still held his server hostage, he couldn't let her see how pissed he was. He forced his voice to stay flat. "And my server? When do I get it back?"

Lysander silently handed Elara a laptop and some weird little gadget. Both Caspian and Thane leaned in, watching like hawks. Elara’s fingers flew across the keys, blurring.

The laptop let out a soft beep. Caspian whipped out his phone. His eyes went wide. A notification popped up: server back online, 100% active.

He got it now. Hacking the system was the tough part. Fixing it was a joke because she’d already cloned and backed up every single file onto her own virtual server. A couple lines of code, and boom—everything was back to normal.

"Holy shit," Caspian whispered, staring at her like he'd seen a ghost. "You're Nova Ghost."

Elara gave him a cold, empty smile. "We're done here," she said, waving him off. "Get lost."

She brushed him off like a pesky fly. Caspian stood up, but his gut twisted. Whatever she had in store for Thane was going to be way worse than what she’d done to him. He paused. Elara’s eyes snapped to his, sharp as daggers. "Keep standing there," she said, deadpan, "and I'll ruin both of you."

Orion, the biggest player in the country, just got kicked to the curb like trash. No respect, no power, nothing. He'd never been bitched around like this in his life. And it was all because of one girl: Seraphine.

"Hey, Caspian, I'll pay you back, man. Every cent. Just let me get my feet under me," Thane said, jumping up to follow him.

Caspian stopped him with a cold look and shook his head. "Forget the money," he said quietly. "But as of right now, I'm out of your drama. Permanently."

That hit Thane like a freight train. Whenever his life went to hell, Caspian was always the one guy who had his back. Losing him felt like a physical punch to the gut. Caspian gave his shoulder a quick squeeze, turned, and walked out. Only then did Thane realize why Elara picked this specific joint. It was dead quiet, empty, and perfectly timed. Everyone was back at work, leaving her with the exact quiet, controlled space she wanted.

Thane slid into Caspian’s empty chair. He glared at Elara, his eyes cold. "Give me the account info," he said flatly.

Elara nodded at Lysander, who handed Thane the paper. Thane looked at the numbers, and his jaw dropped. "Wait, what?" he stammered, staring at her in total shock. "All this cash you're bleeding me for... you're just handing it over to him?"
