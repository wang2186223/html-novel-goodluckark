NovelVibe

Chapter 2

Nobody bought it. Not for a second. Six times they shoved the divorce papers in Elara’s face, and six times the Managing Director threw them back. She’d sat there and taken it while her old nanny’s kid strutted around her house like she paid the mortgage. The whole pack talked trash behind her back. They laughed. They felt sorry for her. In their eyes, she was just a pushover.

Then she spoke, and the room went dead silent. Like a knife hitting the floor.

"No way," someone in the back muttered. "She’s bluffing. She’ll come crawling back. She always does."

Another guy snorted. "Duh. Elara’s not dumping Chief Commander Thane. She loves her fancy title too much to walk."

Thane stood right in the middle of the room, looking like a million bucks in a custom cream suit. But for a split second, his smug face cracked. He figured she was playing games. I mean, the guy was the number-two big shot in the whole New York scene. Rich, powerful, dangerous. He could crush her with a phone call. Sure, the law protected Lunas, but laws have loopholes. Besides, this was embarrassing. He’d already talked so much trash about her before she even showed up.

He’d hyped this party up, sent out the invites weeks ago. Big-time Alphas blew off major meetings just to watch her beg. Thane was 100% sure she’d fold like a cheap tent. But this? This cold shoulder? It threw him off. Almost every heavy hitter in the city was in the room. Only a couple of guys missed it—like his best bud Orion from Grimroot, who couldn't make it.

"You're making a mistake," Thane finally said, keeping his voice cool and steady. He stared her down, sizing her up. "You know the rules. You divorce a Chief Commander, and you're blacklisted. No other Commander will touch you."

The crowd of Alphas mumbled, nodding along.

Thane puffed out his chest. "We have a pact, Elara. We don't do hand-me-downs. You won't even land a beta. You'll end up with some bottom-tier nobody."

He looked at her like she was trash. He meant it, too. But Elara didn't blink. She'd seen that look a thousand times. Even in a cheap, paper-thin hospital gown, she stood tall, shoulders back, chin up. Thane looked exactly like he did the night she fell for him—on that hill during the Moon Festival when she turned eighteen. Her heart had been pounding out of her chest when she yelled to everyone, "Thane is mine!"

Back then, the crowd had stared at him, waiting. He just shrugged it off. Later, he told everyone, "I'm with Briar. She's actually sweet, unlike Elara." Since Elara grew up as a beta's kid, everyone called her a spoiled princess. But Briar? She was a professional victim. Tears on cue, soft voice, the whole nine yards. She claimed Elara bullied her, and everyone bought it hook, line, and sinker—Thane, the pack, even Elara's own folks. Elara never argued. She figured the truth would come out eventually.

What a joke that was.

"Hey, Thane," Elara said, her voice cutting right through the chatter. "I'm the Managing Director. You seriously think I don't know how a divorce works?"

Her eyes were dead, totally blank. For once, Thane looked nervous. No crying, no begging. Just a creepy, quiet vibe that freaked him out.

"You've got some nerve," he spat. "You trapped me. You got my parents to force this marriage, and now you're playing the victim?"

"Just take the damn papers," Elara interrupted, shoving the signed documents right under his nose.

Thane froze, staring at the pages. Then Briar slid in next to him, wearing a sparkly gold dress that made her blonde hair look like a shampoo commercial. She had this tiny, smug smirk.

"Oh, Elara," Briar purred, sounding totally fake-nice. "Thane only asked for my help because you were out cold. Now that you're awake, we can just call off the party. No need to get a divorce, right?"

Elara forced a smile, but her eyes stayed ice-cold. She wanted to scream, wanted to call out the bullshit right then and there. But with all these eyes on her, she kept her mouth shut. She scanned the room. "Where's Asher?" Her chest tightened. He wasn't there.

"School," Thane muttered, sounding a bit jumpy.

"He's the only thing I'm taking with me," Elara said.

"No way!" Thane and Briar yelled together.

Elara raised an eyebrow, totally unfazed. She didn't need a fancy title to look like a boss. Even in a hospital gown, she owned the room. "You honestly thought I'd leave my kid with you?"

Thane shot a look at Briar, then grabbed Elara's arm and dragged her to the side. "Stop embarrassing me," he hissed. "I'll explain later. Just go."

She didn't say a word. She just turned on her heel and left.

The school was close, but that thirty-minute walk felt like a marathon. Her chest ached with every step. She was still weak, but she kept moving. When Asher saw her, his face fell. "Why are you here?" he snapped.

It hurt like hell, but she forced a smile and reached for his arm. "Hey, buddy. Sorry I was gone so long. Let's go home, okay?"

"No way," Asher yelled, ripping his arm back. "I hate you!"

The words hit her like a punch. She thought he'd be happy to see her. Not this. Not from her own kid.

"Asher," she said, trying to keep her voice from shaking. "I'm your mom. We're leaving this place. You're coming with me."

She reached out again, but he jumped back like she was radioactive. "No!" he screamed. "You're not my mom! Get away from me!"

People started staring. Elara forced a fake smile, keeping her voice down. "Hey, look, you're mad, but don't say that."

Before she could grab him, a teacher walked out. "Oh, Managing Director?" the guy gasped. "You're awake? Briar said you were a goner."

Elara ignored him, keeping her eyes on her kid. "I'm taking Asher."

The teacher stepped in her way. "Uh, sorry, ma'am. Thane said only Briar has custody of the kid now."

Elara froze.

"Actually," the teacher mumbled, looking uncomfortable, "he said Briar is his real mom."

Something in her brain just broke. "Are you freaking crazy?" she snapped.

The teacher shook his head. "I thought so too. Until I saw the DNA test." He handed her a piece of paper.

Her eyes went blurry. Her legs turned to jelly. "No way..." she whispered, her voice cracking. "This is fake. Asher is my..."

The ground rushed up to meet her.
