Chapter 220

Rhys was practically breathing down Elara's neck in the lab. Total watchdog mode. He wasn't taking any risks with her. None. He literally stood there and stared until she cleaned her plate, knowing damn well she usually just skips meals.

But Asher? That was the real shocker. The kid didn't throw a fit. He just sat there, totally chill, scarfing down food. This was the same picky brat who usually only eats pancakes. Now? He was stealing meatballs right off Elara's plate like they belonged to him.

Time flew. They worked, they talked, and before they knew it, the kid started flagging. Asher's eyelids drooped. He was losing the battle with sleep, his tiny body just running on empty.

Elara caught it. "Hey," she nudged Rhys. "It's late. Get him to bed."

Rhys paused. He didn't want to leave her side. Not even for five minutes. His inner guard dog was screaming. He stared at her, let out a heavy sigh, and muttered, "Look, I can get someone else to do it. I hate leaving you by yourself."

Elara just smirked. She had this "I can handle my own shit" look. She looked him dead in the eye. "Chill. I'm wrapping up for the night. Tomorrow I'll run the tests and whip up more of Asher's serum. Seriously, don't sweat it. Once you tuck him in, meet me in the east woods. I need a run."

Rhys scanned her face, trying to find an excuse to stay, but he gave up. He nodded, still looking worried as hell, scooped up Asher, and walked out.

Elara wasted no time. She slipped out into the cool night air. It felt good, fresh, a sharp contrast to the stuffy lab. She headed down the trail toward the east woods. It was dark, but she wasn't alone. Whispers started up in the shadows.

"Told you. Zero shame," someone sneered, not even trying to hide it. "Crawled right back, just like we knew she would."

Another voice chimed in, sounding way too pleased. "Yeah, word is Commander Thane won't even spare her a glance now."

Elara kept her pace. Didn't look back. Didn't give them the satisfaction. She acted like they were ghosts, though her muscles tensed up a bit. Inside her head, Blaze was losing it. *Let me rip their throats out,* the wolf growled, itching for a fight.

*Shut up,* Elara thought, shutting her wolf down instantly. She kept moving, eyes forward, until a girl stepped right in front of her, cutting her off.

"Oh, Managing Director Sera... you're back?" the girl said, trying to play it cool, but her eyes were practically dripping with judgment.

Elara stared her down, her eyes cold enough to freeze water. When she spoke, she didn't yell, but her voice had teeth. "Why the title? I don't run this place anymore."

The girl opened her mouth, but Elara cut her off. "I'm here as a doctor. You piss me off, and those sick families from the chemical leak? They don't get cured. Simple as that." She paused, letting the threat hang in the air as she scanned the group. "And trust me, I keep my promises."

That shut them up real quick. The girl's face went blank. Instantly, they all went quiet, probably pinging Briar on the mindlink to see if she was telling the truth. Elara didn't wait around. She brushed past them, leaving them in the dust. The pack's chatter faded, replaced by the deep, quiet hum of the forest.

Deep in the woods, it was dead silent. Perfect. She didn't waste a second. She peeled off her clothes, draped them over a low branch, and stepped back, naked in the cool air. Her skin was already tingling, ready for the shift.

Blaze was clawing to get out. The shift hit her fast. Bones popped and snapped, reshaping in a second. Her skin vanished under a coat of pure, snow-white fur that practically glowed under the moon.

Boom. Her senses dialed up to eleven. She could hear leaves rustling miles away, smell every little critter in the brush. She flexed her claws, digging them into the dirt, and let out a low, rumbling purr of pure satisfaction.

*God, this feels amazing,* Blaze purred in her head. The wolf took the wheel, bounding through the trees with pure, raw power. Elara just let go. For the first time in months, she stopped fighting it. She let the wolf run wild, and the sheer relief of it washed over her like a wave. No more holding back.

They ran until the adrenaline finally started to fade. Slowly, the bones shifted back, and Elara was standing there naked again, catching her breath. She reached for her shirt, but froze. A scent hit her nose. Heavy. Strange. It made her inner wolf sit up.

*Sera... let's go hunt,* Blaze whispered, her voice suddenly dark and hungry. *That's a Fenrir wolf.*

Panic flared. Elara felt her body shifting again—Blaze was forcing it. If the wolf lost her mind, it'd be a bloodbath. *No, Blaze, stop! Let me handle this!* Elara screamed internally.

Too late. Blaze was already in charge, stalking through the shadows like a ghost. She locked onto the target, leaped, and slammed the massive Fenrir wolf straight into the dirt. She pinned it down, baring her fangs, ready to rip its throat out.

*STOP!*

Elara yanked the reins back, forcing her body to shift back to human at the absolute last second. Her muscles screamed from the strain, but she stopped the bite. The wolf underneath her started shifting back, too. Elara scrambled off, her heart hammering as she realized who she was looking at.

"Holy shit... you?"

Her voice was barely a whisper, her mind spinning. She stared down at her, her eyes narrowing as the pieces clicked together. "A Fenrir," she muttered, the reality of it hitting her like a truck. "Does Thane even know about this?"
