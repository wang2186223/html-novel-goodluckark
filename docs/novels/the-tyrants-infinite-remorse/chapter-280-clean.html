Chapter 283

Caspian froze. He stared at her like she was some wild variable he hadn't planned for. If Elara weren't so damn exhausted, she probably would've laughed.

She was a pro at this. Put on the fake smile. Keep the voice steady. Give them the pretty, easy version of Elara. She did it so much it was practically muscle memory. But right now? The mask had cracked.

"It's nothing," she muttered. She clawed at his fingers, trying to pry his hand off her forearm. "Seriously, let go of me."

Her eyes darted left. Down the corridor. The exit door sat right there, glowing red. Like a way out. A promise.

Caspian tracked her eyes. He didn't squeeze harder, but he didn't loosen up either. He knew. One loose finger and she’d bolt.

"No way. Not until you tell me why the hell you're crying."

Elara looked up. Her lips twitched into something ugly and sharp—not a real smile, just the ghost of one. When she spoke, the words just fell out, completely drained of any fight.

"You have my whole life mapped out," she said, dropping each word like a stone. "Every single detail." She stared straight into his eyes, blinking back the wetness. "But you? You're a vault. Locked tight. Nothing exists unless you feel like sharing."

She knew his game. Give him an inch, and he’d dodge, change the subject, or flip the script. She wasn't giving him that chance.

"Who's Aurelia’s mom?" She threw it out fast, cutting through the air. "Where is she? Why is she wiped from existence?" She scanned his face, desperate. "And what happened between you two that made you—"

She cut herself off.

Not because she was done. She had a million more lined up. But his face changed, and the words died in her throat.

He went cold. Not his usual public poker face, either. This was dark. Heavy. Like the sky turning black right before a tornado hits, making you realize you're standing right in its path.

"I don't get why you're losing your mind over this," he said. His voice was low, vibrating with tension. Not pissed, but close.

Elara tugged her arm again. Still locked in. She glanced around. People were watching now. A nurse slowed her pace. Some guy by the vending machine was staring right at them.

Her skin crawled under the stares. Caspian didn't seem to give a shit. Or maybe he just didn't care.

"Back with the pack," she said, watching his jaw clench. "And in the outlands. You pried. You watched me. You asked about my life, my business—stuff that had zero to do with our deal."

She locked eyes with him, forcing him to look. "And you? Nothing. Not one damn detail. Not about Aurelia—" She bit her lip, correcting herself. "Not about anyone."

Elara swallowed hard, her throat tight.

She thought about the gossip. The stories that trailed him everywhere like a bad storm. Loud, ugly, and easy to buy into.

The ruthless Chief Commander. The guy who kept everyone away because he simply didn't give a damn about anyone.

She'd bought into it. It was easier that way—kept her from getting too close. But now? With his hand wrapped around her arm and his face cracking open? She wasn't so sure.

"Where's her mom?" she asked again. Quieter this time, but steady. "And don't say Linnea." She knew better. Linnea acted like a nanny, not a mother. You could see it in how she moved. "So who is she, Caspian?"

She yanked her arm. "Let go of me."

He held on. Not to hurt her. She could tell. It was a careful grip, like he was holding glass. Squeeze too hard and it breaks; let go and it's gone.

His eyes showed he was spiraling, trying to figure something out. It drove her crazy not knowing what he was thinking.

"Wait," Caspian muttered. His jaw twitched. He spoke slowly, spitting the words out. "You're jealous."

Elara let out a dry, pathetic laugh. The kind that slips out when you're too tired to pretend you're not hurt.

"Jealous?" She spat the word back at him. "Do I even have the right to be?"

Her eyes went dead. The fight just drained out of her. "Honestly, I don't even know what the hell we are."

She waved her free hand between them. The hospital. His hand on her arm. The past month. Aurelia talking about how much her daddy loved her. All of it.

A tear rolled down her cheek. She didn't even notice until Caspian reached up, his thumb aiming for her face.

She jerked her head back. Out of reach. She wasn't some mess he could just wipe away and fix.

"You have a kid," she said, forcing her voice to stay steady. "And you never told me. Not once. Why?" She stared at him, begging him with her eyes to just tell her the damn truth.

Caspian took a heavy breath. His calm facade was ripping at the seams.

"Can you really blame me for keeping that to myself?"

Elara blinked. "Are you kidding me?"
