NovelVibe

Chapter 320

Lysander was just glad Caspian trusted him enough to lay out the whole curse thing. The fact that the Chief Commander was offering him a real spot in their future proved one thing: this wasn't some cheap political play. It was pure devotion. Caspian loved Elara to death, and he knew Elara trusted Lysander just as much.

"Look, if I'm heading back to the pack, I need people I can actually trust around me," Caspian said, sinking back into the couch as he ran through every possible worst-case scenario. "Gideon? I’d take a bullet for him, no question. But Sera? She’s got zero allies there. Plus, my mom is going to make things hell because she’s totally obsessed with Linnea."

Lysander’s face fell. He glared at Caspian, his posture instantly stiffening. "Whoa, hold up. Who the hell is Linnea? You never brought her up."

Caspian rubbed the back of his neck, sighing as he laid out the whole history with Linnea. When he finally shut up, Lysander was just staring at him, his forehead creased in a deep scowl. He let the silence stretch, chewing on the information. Then, he shrugged. "Whatever. If Sera’s cool with it, I guess I am too."

His eyes softened, Elara’s face flashing in his mind. "I know Sera better than anyone. She’s smart, she gets people, and she knows how to dodge drama. She doesn't start shit. But if you push her? Yeah, she’ll bite. Hard." A faint smile tugged at his lips. "She’s always been like that."

Caspian just listened, taking it all in.

"Alright," Lysander said, pointing a finger. "I’m in. But only if this plan actually holds together."

They both knew how fast things could go sideways. No matter how much you prep, life loves to throw a wrench in the gears. But they had to try.

Caspian gave a firm nod. "I’ve got you. No matter what."

Lysander’s eyes welled up. He swallowed hard, trying to choke down the sudden wave of emotion. He looked away, letting out a dry, hollow laugh.

"Just... make her happy, man," he muttered, his voice cracking. "She’s been through absolute hell."

The words hung heavy in the quiet room. "I hated her for the longest time," Lysander confessed, staring at the floor. "I blamed her for shit that wasn't even her fault. Every single time things went south, she was the one taking the fall." He clenched his jaw. "She could’ve bailed. A million times. But she didn't. She just stood there and took the hits."

Caspian felt a sharp squeeze in his chest. He knew that pain all too well. Elara had spent years bleeding out, with nobody there to catch her or love her the way she deserved.

"I get it, Lysander," Caspian said, his voice dropping to a gravelly whisper. "That’s why we’re changing the game. It’s her turn to be happy. Finally."

A small, genuine smile broke through Caspian's heavy mood as he thought of her. "That’s why I’m leaving her in your hands during the Moon Festival. She wants to keep us under wraps. Even after we tie the knot, she wants to wait until the dust settles."

It was killing him. Keeping her a secret felt like trying to hold back a flood. Every bone in his body wanted to scream to the world that she was his. But he got it. He knew why she was scared. After everything she’d survived, she needed to set the pace. If hiding made her feel safe, he’d play along.

Lysander let out a soft chuckle. "Relax, man. You’ve got nothing to worry about. Besides, Rhys is gonna be there."

Caspian’s face went instantly cold. "No."

Lysander snorted at the lightning-fast shutdown. "You don't trust him?"

"Not a chance."

Caspian tossed his phone onto the coffee table. Lysander glanced down. The screen flashed with a new alert, revealing the lock screen. Lysander burst out laughing. "Oh, man. I seriously hope Thane never sees that wallpaper."

Caspian didn't even look. "Let him look. Who cares?"

Lysander pressed his lips together, shaking his head. The guy had balls, but it made him nervous. He just hoped Caspian wouldn't blow up Elara's life. But hey, first things first.

Lysander stood up, stretching his arms over his head. "Well, she’s clearly not sleeping here tonight. Might as well haul her stuff up to your place."

Caspian bolted up. That was the whole reason he’d driven over.

They headed upstairs. Elara’s sweet, familiar scent hit Caspian in the hallway, making his chest ache with a sudden, sharp longing.

He stepped into her room and looked around. Nothing had moved. Everything was exactly where she’d left it, like the room was just holding its breath, waiting for her to walk back in.

They started packing. "Hey," Caspian said, grabbing a box. "I owe your folks an apology. I was a total dick to them. I’m gonna go see them and make it right."

Lysander nodded. "Yeah. That’d mean a lot to them."

"And Chief Commander Arthur and Managing Director Eleanor," Caspian added. "I need to face them, too."

The names tasted like ash. He had a lot of burnt bridges to rebuild, a lot of people he’d screwed over.

Lysander dropped a few books into a box and looked up. "You better invite her nanny, too. Audrey and her husband, Arthur." He smiled. "Briar’s parents worship Elara. Honestly, they love her as much as we do."

Something clicked in Caspian’s brain. His hands froze on the sweater he was holding.
