NovelVibe

Chapter 174

Caspian froze. Hell no. He wasn't about to admit he’d custom-ordered that dress, let alone spent forty-eight hours in Paris micromanaging every single stitch. The whole "visiting the pack" story? Mostly BS. He’d actually spent the trip tracking down high-tech gear, hidden blades, and tech she’d need for the night. No way he was leaving her safety to some random third party. The second he got back, he barely gave himself time to throw on some clean clothes before bolting straight to her room.

"She’s got the same body as any other girl," he muttered, trying to sound bored. But his eyes stayed glued to her way too long.

Elara rolled her eyes. She walked over, tugging at the hem. This silk thing was way too revealing. She hated showing this much skin. It made her feel totally naked, and not in a good way.

"Time for the gear," Caspian said.

He stepped up close. His face was deadpan, totally focused as he started clipping gadgets onto the fabric. His hands were rock steady. The guy moved like a pro.

"Knife sheath goes here," he muttered, dropping to one knee. "Right in the thigh slit."

He worked the weapon into place, super precise. Elara stiffened. His fingers grazed her bare thigh. Damn it. It was just business, but her heart did a stupid little flip anyway.

"Putting micro-speakers in the lining now," he murmured, clearly sensing her freeze up. "You'll hear us perfectly. No one else will notice a thing."

Elara swallowed hard. She didn't say a word. He was just too close.

Then Caspian pulled out these glittering gold stilettos. "These aren't just for show. The heels are sharp enough to puncture a skull."

Elara raised an eyebrow. She slid her feet in. Wow. Perfect fit, and surprisingly easy to walk in.

Caspian stood up and tossed her a tiny clutch bag.

"Press this little button right here," he showed her the hidden latch. "Then aim and squeeze."

Lysander’s jaw dropped. "Wait, that purse is a gun?"

Caspian nodded. "Easiest way to sneak heat past the metal detectors."

Elara felt a sudden rush of thanks she kept to herself. Bringing Caspian in was the smartest move she’d made. Just like Lysander, he took the mission seriously. Actually, scratch that—Caspian took it to a whole different level. He thought of the tiny details everyone else missed.

Caspian reached out to fix a strap on her shoulder. His fingertips brushed her bare skin. A sudden shiver ran down her spine. He was just prepping her for a dangerous gig, making sure she’d survive, but there was this heavy, unspoken vibe between them that neither wanted to touch.

Finally, he grabbed the necklace. The diamonds flashed under the lights. He stepped behind her, wrapping it around her neck. His fingers moved slow, lingering against her skin as he clicked the clasp shut.

"Camera's right here," he whispered.

His knuckles grazed her throat as he adjusted the chain. Elara bit her lip to keep from shivering again. The necklace looked gorgeous, sparkling like crazy, but she knew it was basically a high-tech spy tool.

Once he backed off, Elara took a breath and whipped out her folding phone. She flipped it open into a tablet, the screen's blue glow lighting up her face.

A bunch of hacking programs popped up.

"I can kill some of the security sensors right before I hit the gate," she said. "Should make things a breeze."

"Nice," Caspian nodded. "Once we're close, I'll bail to grab my FBI contact. Lysander, you drive her through. Tell the guards he's just your driver and he'll be back to pick you up later."

Elara listened close as he laid out the rest.

"When Lysander rolls back in, they won't bother searching the car again. The plates will be in their system. My buddy and I will stay low in the trunk until it's go-time."

Elara liked the sound of that. "I'll tell Dominic I have to powder my nose," she said. "That gives me a window to kill his cameras before the party starts."

Lysander frowned. "Won't those systems just reboot?"

Elara smirked. "Already uploaded a virus to their main server. I just need to get close enough to trigger it, then I'll mirror everything to a secure server before they even smell a rat."

Caspian raised an eyebrow, looking impressed. "Damn. You're scarier than I am."

The plan went off without a hitch. By the time they hit Dominic’s mansion, the security was insane.

Guards everywhere. Cameras on every wall. Hidden sensors, beefy bodyguards at every corner. But the funny thing was, nobody noticed their tech going dark. Why? Because the second Elara stepped out of the car, every single guy froze.

She looked absolutely stunning. People literally forgot to breathe.

The guards ignored their screens. The security team forgot their protocols. The bodyguards practically tripped over themselves to open doors for her.

Lysander drove off smoothly. Elara walked inside.

She walked down these massive hallways filled with flowers and warm, dim lighting. Finally, she hit a giant room glowing under massive gold chandeliers.

Right in the middle sat a single table. It was covered in so many candles and flowers it looked like a cheesy scene from a trashy romance flick, not a private dinner.

Then Dominic stepped out.

Of course, he was wearing all white again.

"My Donner," he smiled. "You made it."
