Chapter 253

Elara cut the thought off fast. *Nope. Shut it down. Not happening, Blaze. Seriously, stop.* Her wolf shut up.

But her hands kept working, and damn, this was nothing like patching up regular humans. Humans didn't pack this kind of heat. Under the freezing rain, his muscles felt rock-hard, burning hot, and perfectly sculpted. Touching him like this was doing things to her brain she absolutely refused to process. There. She found the spot.

"Brace yourself," she muttered.

"Just do it," he grunted.

She shoved. Hard, dead-on, right by his spine. *Crack.* The snap drowned out the storm for a split second. Caspian locked up, ripping out a raw, ugly gasp he definitely wanted to keep inside. Then, boom. Finished.

The tension drained out of him instantly. He rolled onto his back, staring blankly at the sky while the downpour washed the mud off his face in dirty streaks. He just lay there, chest heaving.

"Thanks, Sera," he breathed.

"Don't," she said, back on her knees. She was a total mess—mud caked on her arms and legs, slowly melting in the rain. "Thank *you*. For grabbing me. I didn't even see that damn tree coming." She offered him a hand.

He stared at her fingers for a beat, then grabbed them. He pulled himself up, keeping his weight off her, and spun around. He finally saw the wreck. The passenger side roof was completely pancaked. No minor scratch. If she’d stayed in that seat, she’d be dead.

He stared at the metal heap. "I'll buzz my beta. Get us a ride."

Elara shook her head. "Just tow it. We need shelter, fast. Look around—the roads are a disaster."

He didn't push back.

"My place is open. For both of you."

They whipped around. Thane stood a few feet away, dripping wet in his emergency gear, water pooling on his nose. He looked totally composed, but he'd obviously seen the whole show. When he first saw them tangled on the ground, his face had gone rigid with pure jealousy. Now, he had it under wraps. He got it. Whatever pissed him off, he swallowed it.

"Seriously, come to the packhouse," he repeated, voice steady.

Elara turned away. "Hell no. I am not staying there." Cold. Final. Done deal.

Caspian squinted at her through the downpour. "Want my two cents?"

With Rhys gone, she had zero backup. Just her, shivering in the mud, with Caspian waiting.

She sighed. "Shoot."

"Go to the packhouse." He saw her mouth open to argue and cut her off. "Look, it's huge. You'll get your own space, promise. No drama. Once this storm blows over, dinner's on me, like we planned. Deal?"

She bit her lip. God, she hated this. But then she remembered her bag was still in the lab. Her dry clothes. Her phone. And, oh yeah, a steaming hot shower. Standing in the freezing mud with water dripping down her spine, that shower sounded like heaven.

She groaned. "Fine." The kind of 'fine' that meant *I hate everything about this.*

"Great. Packhouse. We'll grab a mechanic on the way," Caspian said, visibly relieved.

"I'll deal with the car," Thane chimed in. "I'll ping Rhys to bring a spare ride for you guys." He was clearly kissing ass, hoping she'd forgive him and keep working on the cure.

Caspian eyed the wreck. The passenger side was a crushed soda can. Maybe one person could squeeze into the driver's seat if they got creative.

"Fix the car, sure," Caspian said. "But skip the extra ride."

Thane blinked. "Then how are you—"

"I'll just carry her." The words slipped out before he could stop them.

The rain kept pouring. Elara and Thane stared at him, identical looks of utter disbelief on their faces. They were waiting for the punchline.

Caspian just shrugged and walked over to her. "Hey, you just popped my spine back in," he said, totally casual. "Gotta test the merchandise, right?"

"Caspian," Thane hissed, stepping in close and dropping his voice. "Dude, you literally can't stand women. What the hell?"

"She fixed my back." Caspian didn't even blink. "What, you wanted to leave me face down in the dirt? She stepped up." He glanced at the gate, then at Elara shivering. "And I'm not making her hike back in a storm. Rhys is busy with Asher. End of story."

He laid it out like facts. Thane clenched his jaw because, damn it, the guy was right. Any comeback would just sound like a jealous boyfriend.

Caspian stopped right in front of her. Elara took a step back, brushing wet hair out of her eyes. She was already shaking her head. "No way. You're out of your mind."

Caspian stared down at her. "Do I look like I'm playing?"
