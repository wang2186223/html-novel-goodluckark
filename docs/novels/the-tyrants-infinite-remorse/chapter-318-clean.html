NovelVibe

Chapter 322

Elara’s eyelids fluttered open. She blinked against the light, staring blankly at the ceiling. *Where the hell am I?* The thought looped in her head as she tried to map her surroundings.

The vibe was weirdly familiar, but off. She scanned the room—the sleek furniture, the color scheme, that faint, sweet scent, and her massive computer monitors. It was all brand new, yet it felt like a warm hug. Safe.

A grin tugged at her mouth. For a split second, she thought she was back at Stone’s place. A sharp pang of nostalgia hit her chest, tight and sudden. Then reality crashed the party. Nope. Not the stone mansion. Too many details didn't line up.

"Caspian?" she muttered. Her voice cut through the dead silence. She waited for the door to swing open, expecting him to stroll in with that stupidly hot smirk that always turned her brain to mush. Nothing. Just quiet. A tiny, annoying wave of disappointment sank into her chest.

Then she spotted it—a folded piece of paper on the mattress next to her. The disappointment vanished, replaced by a spark of curiosity. She grabbed it, flipped it open, and instantly beamed.

*I drew a bath for you. Esperanza’s room is the second door on the right.*

Her smile stretched wider. Sure, the bath was sweet, but it was the name that got her. *Esperanza.* He didn't use Aurelia, the name everyone else clung to. He used the name she chose. That little detail hit her right in the feels, way harder than he probably intended.

She tossed the note aside, slid out of bed, and padded over to the bathroom. She crossed the threshold and stopped dead.

"Oh, holy hell..." she breathed.

Low, crimson lighting washed over the space, bouncing off the marble walls in a way that felt incredibly high-end and sexy. Her jaw dropped at the massive jacuzzi. The water was dyed a deep, rich red. God knows what he poured in there, but rose petals drifted across the surface, and the air smelled absolutely divine.

The scent was pure magic—soothing and warm. Elara didn't hesitate. She shed her clothes and slid into the tub. The moment the hot water hit her skin, a long, shaky sigh escaped her. Her muscles melted. The heat seeped straight into her bones, dissolving tension she didn't even know she was holding.

She rested her head on the rim and shut her eyes. God, this was heaven. She’d spent her whole life busting her ass for everyone else, never once pausing to pamper herself. Caspian had set this all up in a single night. For the first time in forever, her brain went totally quiet. No anxiety, no stress, no fear. Just pure, unadulterated bliss.

She let out a low, satisfied groan, sinking up to her chin. Okay, now she got why rich people blew crazy money on this stuff. If she didn't have a mountain of work waiting, she’d stay pruned in here all damn day. Maybe all week.

But there was someone she wanted to see way more than this tub or her fancy screens—her baby girl. She smiled at the thought. Dragging herself out of the warm water took actual physical effort, but she promised herself she’d be back. Tonight. Absolutely tonight.

Honestly, she’d already made her decision. No use lying to herself anymore. In her head, she’d already unpacked her bags in Caspian’s mansion. Done deal. Her daughter was here, and that was all that mattered. The fact that Caspian came with the package? Just a very, *very* hot bonus. A dangerously hot bonus. Her cheeks flushed at the thought.

Somehow, Caspian had become her safe haven. Strong, protective, rock-solid—like a lion guarding his pride. For a girl who’d spent her life being ignored, dismissed, and underestimated, his focus on her felt surreal. He looked at her like she was the only thing that mattered. Like she was precious. Like he’d burn the whole damn world to the ground before letting anyone touch a hair on her head. It was intoxicating.

By the time she got dressed and walked toward Esperanza’s room, she felt lighter than she had in months. She hadn't even checked her emails or messaged her tech team. A total first. But when she opened the door, she blinked. Linnea was inside. Okay, not a total shocker—Linnea was the nanny, so of course she’d be hanging out with the kid.

But then, something slipped from Linnea’s fingers. Elara’s reflexes, honed by years of being on edge, kicked in. She lunged, snatching the paper out of midair before it hit the floor. Linnea let out a breath, looking embarrassed as she rubbed the back of her neck.

"Whoops, sorry," Linnea muttered with a nervous chuckle. "Mari usually goes ballistic if anyone touches her drawings. I know, I know, I shouldn't be snooping. But I saw this guy last night, and his face was so familiar. Then it clicked—I’d seen him in one of her sketches."

Linnea looked up, locking eyes with Elara for the first time, and froze.

A sudden tightness gripped Linnea's chest. It caught her totally off guard—this rush of warmth, comfort, and just... pure love. She couldn't explain it. But being near Elara felt exactly like being near Esperanza. Same vibe. Same magnetic pull that made you want to trust them instantly. *Oh,* Linnea thought. *Now I get why Caspian is losing his absolute mind over her.* The woman just had this unexplainable, special spark.

"Honestly," Linnea whispered, "I don't think Mari has ever even met this guy."

She watched Elara’s face, and yeah, her gut was right. There was definitely a connection between that drawing and the man she’d spotted at dawn.
