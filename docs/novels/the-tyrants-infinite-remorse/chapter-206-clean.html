NovelVibe

Chapter 208

Finn’s call hit Thane’s phone a few days back. It started like small talk, but both knew better. Finn didn't just call—he dropped a bunch of video clips from the MindNest launch. The screen flashed with blinding lights, screaming crowds, and pure, raw hype. This wasn't luck; it was a calculated takeover. And right in the center of the frame stood Elara. She owned the room, effortlessly drawing every eye. Thane stared, a bitter knot tightening in his gut.

"Even if we tried to buy in," Thane muttered, his throat dry, "she’d slam the door in our face. We made our bed, Finn. Now we sleep in it." He wasn't mad. Just numb.

But Finn wouldn't drop it. "Dude, she was your wife," he snapped. "Yeah, ex-wife, whatever. But she was crazy about you. You don't just switch that off." He paused, letting the words sink in. "Dump Briar. Seriously. You do that, and maybe Elara takes you back."

Thane slumped back, staring at nothing. Memories of the old Elara clashed with this new, fierce version. The difference stung.

"I can't," Thane said, his voice dropping to a flat, hard whisper. "She’s done with me. She doesn't love me anymore."

Finn snorted. "Yeah, right. I’ve seen crazier shit. Couples tear each other apart and still end up in the same bed." He shifted gears, his voice turning sharp. "Besides, look at the market. Every brand she touches is going straight to the moon. The Forbes list is getting totally wrecked."

That got under Thane's skin.

"We finally stabilized, and we're sitting at fifteen," Finn went on. "Your beta’s crew? Number five. And his dad? Number two."

The numbers felt like a punch to the ribs. Thane swallowed, his throat burning like sand. "If I hadn't... if I hadn't killed our baby," he choked out, the heavy guilt finally breaking through his armor. "Maybe I'd have a shot."

He cleared his throat, trying to convince himself. "But whatever. I’ve got Asher. And Briar... she’s my choice. I love her."

"Fine," Finn sighed. "Forget I said anything."

The line went dead, but Thane couldn't shake the words.

An hour later, his phone buzzed again. A photo from Finn. The caption read: *Never seen him look at anyone like this.*

Thane tapped it open. His chest went cold.

Caspian.

He was standing right next to Elara. No touching, nothing dirty, but the vibe was loud and clear. Caspian’s usual icy stare was completely gone, replaced by something warm. Something dangerous.

Thane stared at the screen, jealousy flaring hot before he forced it down. *It’s business,* he lied to himself. *Actually, it helps us.*

He typed a fast reply: *Good. Glad he put money in. If shit hits the fan, he can bail us out.*

He hit send, trying to believe his own bullshit.

The day Elara was supposed to land, Thane set up a house for her right by the pack territory, just like Rhys suggested. Rhys booked himself a hotel room.

But when night fell, she was a no-show. No pack house, no hotel. Nothing.

Rhys was MIA too.

Thane dialed both of them. Straight to voicemail. The robotic operator voice looped until he wanted to throw his phone at the wall.

*Don't panic,* he told himself. *There's a reason.*

But morning came, and still nothing. The silence felt heavy, suffocating.

He grabbed his keys, ready to take Asher and go look for them. Then his phone rang. Caspian.

Thane picked up, his shoulders tense. "Yeah?"

"Just talked to Lysander," Caspian barked, getting straight to the point. "He says there's trouble at the pack. What's the deal?"

"Rhys took off yesterday before sunrise," Thane said, his voice tight. "He should've been back last night. Neither him nor Elara showed up. Both phones are dead. I'm heading out to find them now."

He hung up.

Thane buckled Asher into his seat, his mind racing through worst-case scenarios. He started the engine and rolled toward the front gate.

Suddenly, the iron gates swung open.

A car rolled in.

Rhys’s car.

Thane slammed on the brakes, his brow furrowing. He threw his door open and stepped out, waving them to stop.

But when he saw them, his stomach dropped.

They were smiling. Totally relaxed. No scratches, no panic. You don't look like that after disappearing for twelve hours.

"I've been calling you guys all night," Thane said, stepping up to the driver's side, his eyes darting from Rhys to Elara. The second Elara caught his eye, her face hardened. She looked away, shutting him out.

"Why were your phones off?" Thane demanded.

Rhys gave him a cool, easy look. "It's a long story," he said, keeping his voice totally flat.

Thane’s jaw clenched. Something was definitely off.

"Let's go inside," Rhys said, opening his door. "We'll tell you everything."
