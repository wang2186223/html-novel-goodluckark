Chapter 120

"She’s still hooked on me, man. Once she hears Briar is out of the picture, she’ll come crawling right back." Thane spat the words out, totally convinced his own lies and trash behavior didn't matter as long as he said what she wanted to hear. Inside Caspian’s office, the air turned rancid. Caspian stared at him, suddenly wondering how the hell he’d run with this guy for years without realizing he was completely, utterly blind.

Caspian shifted his eyes over to Elara. She sat there, dead quiet, thumbing through her phone like she had all the time in the world. The shitstorm outside didn't even register on her face. She was pure ice.

But watching her now, Caspian got a chill. The sweet girl Thane used to walk all over? Gone. Whatever soft, warm heart she used to have had turned into a razor-sharp blade. She was dangerous now, way more dangerous than anyone in the pack ever guessed.

Sure, she was supposedly dating Julian, but Caspian wasn't buying it. Her heart wasn't in the game. Zero spark, zero warmth when she talked about the guy. Every time she looked up from that screen, her eyes burned with one thing: pure, cold payback.

Why Julian, though? The whole setup smelled off. Caspian’s gut screamed that she had an angle, some hidden play he couldn't see yet. But right now, he had to shelve that puzzle. Thane’s mess was blowing up in their faces.

"Yeah, well. Good luck with that, buddy," Caspian muttered, his voice flat and distant. He didn't wait for a reply. He swiped the screen and cut the call before Thane could spout any more stupid shit.

The office went dead quiet for about two seconds. Then another phone buzzed, vibrating against the desk. Lysander checked the screen, then shot a look at Elara. "It’s Thane again." Elara didn't even blink. She’d ditched her old number the second she hit the city, blocking him out. But a desperate guy will always try to slide through the back door when the front one is locked.

"Tell him I don't waste my breath on dead men," she said. Her voice was flat. No anger, just nothing.

Lysander smirked. He tapped the screen, hit speaker, and let the whole room in on it. "Hey, man. She says she’s too busy to talk to a corpse."

Thane’s voice exploded through the speaker, frantic and sweating. "Lysander, come on, man! For old times' sake, help me out here!" He sounded like a guy watching his house burn down. "Look, I’m sorry about the kid, okay? But I can't bring her back. Just tell Sera to come home. Everything goes back to normal. I’ll kick Briar out. I'll banish her from the pack, I swear!"

Lysander let out a sharp, nasty laugh. "Dude, you're the only one still living in yesterday," he said, throwing a quick glance at Elara. "Sera’s done. She’s with Julian now," Lysander added. But his eyes told a different story—something way more complicated than the lie he was selling. He paused, letting the silence stretch before twisting the knife. "Asher needs actual parents, Thane. You made your bed. Now lie in it."

*Click.* He killed the call.

The quiet that followed felt damn good. For once, Lysander was actually glad she was fake-dating Julian. It kept garbage like Thane far enough away so they couldn't mess with her head again.

"You sure know how to speak for her," Caspian said, narrowing his eyes as he sized Lysander up.

Lysander just grinned, totally relaxed. "Sera and I are tight like that. Half the time, I know what she’s thinking before she even opens her mouth."

That rubbed Caspian the wrong way, though he couldn't pinpoint why. He shot a look at Elara, waiting for her to shut Lysander down, but she didn't even look up. Then, her phone buzzed again.

This time, Elara swiped up and put it on speaker immediately. She wanted Caspian to hear this. She wanted him to know exactly who was pulling the strings behind the sudden ruin of Finn and Riven’s packs.

"Augustine," Elara said, her voice dripping with cold authority. "Two Alphas slipped something in my drink at a club. Handle them."

Caspian froze. He lunged forward, his face pale, panic bleeding into his voice. "Sera, Jesus! You already bankrupted them. Stop this before someone gets killed!"
