Chapter 205

Eleanor couldn't shake the thought. It just sat there, bugging her. How the hell did Elara not know? It was her own damn bloodline.

But then it clicked. The realization hit her like a punch to the gut.

Chuck and Susan.

Those two were so obsessed with Briar, so busy spoiling her rotten, that they completely shut Elara out. They ignored their own kid in ways that left deep, invisible scars. Crucial family history? Pride? Legacy? They didn't bother passing any of it down. They just kept their mouths shut. And Elara was the one who took the hit for their silence.

Whatever. None of that mattered now. She finally had a real lead.

Even though she had to head back to the pack for a couple of days, her brain was already spinning. She gripped this new detail like a lifeline. For once, finding her kid didn't feel like a total pipe dream.

"Hey, I was there the day you popped out," Eleanor said, her voice dropping as everyone at the table stopped eating and looked up. "I saw it myself. That mark. You got it from your mom."

Elara froze. Her eyes locked onto Eleanor.

"Your mom told me her own mother had it," Eleanor went on, leaning in. "And her grandmother before that. It goes way back."

Elara knit her brows, trying to process. "Wait... so my birthmark isn't just some random spot? It's a family thing?"

Eleanor nodded fast. "Exactly. It's a maternal line thing. Anyone with that mark is 100% connected to your mother's blood."

The words didn't crush Elara. Instead, a warm rush of relief flooded her chest, making her head spin a little. She’d thought about this before, sure, but she’d talked herself out of it, thinking it was just a stupid guess. Now? Hearing Eleanor say it so damn sure made everything click.

"Thank you," Elara whispered, her eyes watering. "Seriously. Thank you for telling me."

Eleanor didn't soften up. She kept her eyes hard. "Look, you would've known ages ago if you hadn't kept us in the dark. And look, this doesn't let Thane off the hook. The guy literally tried to murder her."

The vibe in the room instantly went cold.

Arthur let out a heavy sigh, leaning back in his chair, his face turning to stone. "I still don't get how a guy can be that heartless," he muttered to himself. Then he looked over at Lysander. His eyes held a whole new level of respect now.

"Son," Arthur said, his voice thick. "Thanks for ignoring your Chief Commander when he went off the deep end. I'm a Chief Commander myself. I know exactly what kind of hell you risked. If Thane had caught you..." He trailed off, the unspoken threat hanging in the air.

Lysander let out a dry, tight chuckle. He didn't look proud. Just uncomfortable.

"Chief Commander Arthur—"

"Call me Dad," Arthur cut him off, dead serious.

Lysander managed a real smile this time. "Dad," he said, the word sounding a bit weird on his tongue. He paused, then his face fell. "Look, I'm not gonna sit here and play the hero."

Everyone quieted down, staring at him.

"I hated Sera back then," he said, totally blunt. "I wanted that kid gone. Period."

Elara gripped the edge of the table, but she didn't say a word.

"I didn't stop because I had some sudden moral compass," Lysander muttered, staring down. "It was the baby. She started crying. That's it. That's what made me freeze." He clenched his jaw. "So, yeah. Don't put me on a pedestal. I'm just as dirty as Thane. I actually thought about doing it."

Before the silence got too awkward, Elara reached over and grabbed his hand, squeezing it tight.

"No," she said, looking right at him. "You're not."

He looked up, meeting her eyes.

"You didn't go through with it," she said, her voice steady. "That's what matters. That shows who you really are."

Eleanor nodded right away. "Sera's right," she said, her voice warming up again. Then, like she couldn't hold it in anymore, she blurted, "Honestly, you should just marry our daughter. Be our son-in-law."

Lyra beamed. "Yeah, we couldn't ask for anyone better."

But the second the words left Lyra's mouth, the mood shifted.

Elara flinched. It was quick, just a tiny flicker of hesitation, but it was enough to make the whole table go dead silent.

Arthur caught it instantly. "Sera?" he asked, brow furrowing. "What, you don't like Lysander? You guys are great together." He looked back and forth between them. "Lysander, you love her, right?" he asked, his voice softer, hopeful. "Just promise me you'll never treat her the way Thane did."

Lysander smiled, but his eyes stayed totally blank. "I..." he started, sounding uncharacteristically unsure.

"Dad, stop, it's not like that," Elara jumped in, talking fast to shut it down. "Lysander doesn't see me that way. He just thinks of me as a little sis—"

"What if I don't?" Lysander's voice cut her off, sharp, heavy, and leaving absolutely no room for doubt.
