Chapter 181

"Is that...?" Lysander choked out the words. He didn't want to get his hopes up, but then came the sound. Footsteps. Heavy, messy, and fast. They rattled through the dark tunnel. One set, then three, then a whole damn stampede. It got louder. Fast. No mistaking it now. A crowd was hauling ass toward the light.

The noise built to a roar. A massive group was rushing the exit.

Lysander felt a sting in his eyes. His chest squeezed hard, like a fist letting go. "Holy shit," he breathed, his voice cracking. "It actually worked. They're coming out. The hostages are free."

Graham didn't move a muscle. He just stood there, jaw dropped, staring blankly into the pitch-black opening. His brain couldn't process the sight. "How the hell is this happening?" he mumbled.

"You gonna drool all night, or are we doing this?" Caspian barked. The words cut right through Graham's daze. Graham blinked, shook his head, and snapped right back into cop mode. He grabbed his radio, barking orders, calling in the medics, and setting up the perimeter.

Then Julian burst in. He was panting, sweating, looking like absolute hell. The drive over had clearly wrecked him. "Lysander! Where's Sera?" he yelled, grabbing him. Lysander opened his mouth, pointing toward the main building, but the words died in his throat. The first wave of hostages burst out of the tunnel.

It was a flood. Dozens of them. They stumbled, tripped, and collapsed onto the dirt. Some were bleeding. Some were crying. They looked like ghosts—starved, terrified, completely broken. "Damn," Lysander muttered, turning to Caspian. "There’s gotta be a hundred more still stuck in there. We have to go back in."

Caspian didn't blink. He locked eyes with Graham, his face cold as stone. "Keep this shitshow contained out here," Caspian warned, his voice dead serious. "And pray we get her out in one piece. Because if she dies, you can kiss that evidence goodbye."

Graham didn't flinch this time. The hesitation was gone. "Just don't get killed," Graham grunted. "The second my backup rolls up, I'm coming in hot behind you." Caspian gave a tight nod and turned to leave. "Hey!" Graham called out. "It's a war zone in there. If Dominic is as smart as they say, he definitely left a nasty surprise waiting for you."

Caspian didn't even slow down. No way in hell was he leaving Elara behind. If the whole place blew, he was going in. "He built that fortress to keep people out," Caspian said, staring at the massive concrete block ahead. "But the bastard outsmarted himself."

"What do you mean?" Graham asked, jogging to keep up.

"The whole place is a vault," Caspian explained. "Soundproof walls. Separate guard units. Jammed comms. Even the biometric locks are fried."

Graham scowled. "Then how the hell do you plan on getting past the front door?"

Graham glanced toward the tunnel, but Caspian just gave a cold, humorless smirk. "I've got a way. Just keep these people alive."

Graham nodded. "On it."

Suddenly, boots crunched on gravel. A squad of guards rounded the corner, guns raised, ready to paint the walls. Lysander and Caspian reached for their holsters, but *bang, bang, bang*—Graham fired first. Three clean shots. The guards crumpled before they could even aim.

Caspian looked back. Graham tossed him a quick nod. "Go. I've got you covered."

They didn't waste another second. Caspian and Lysander bolted into the estate. Behind them, sirens wailed as Graham's backup finally arrived. Graham didn't wait around. He grabbed his gear and started hunting for an entry point. He wasn't about to sit on his hands.

Inside, the air was thick. Every step was a gamble. Luckily, Lysander had the blueprints burned into his brain. He and Elara had spent hours memorizing every corridor, vent, and staircase for this exact nightmare. But knowing the map didn't make the guards disappear. They were everywhere—hiding in the shadows, waiting around every corner.

"Stay on my ass," Caspian muttered. They ducked through a wrecked hallway, stepping over shattered wood and groaning bodies. Gunfire echoed from the upper floors, mixed with distant screams.

They stopped at a junction. Caspian turned to Lysander, and the next words out of his mouth hit like a physical blow.
