Chapter 336

Caspian bailed from the office way ahead of schedule. He’d spent thirty minutes cramming with Caleb—running through reports, signing off on paperwork, and dumping instructions for the urgent projects.

Usually, he’d hang around. Not today. He needed to tie up a million loose ends before he and Elara hit the road for the pack.

He wanted real answers, damn it. The dirt they’d dug up on Esperanza changed the whole game. For once, the truth actually felt within arm's reach.

He navigated the city streets, a thought popping into his head: Lysander. Caspian smirked. He still couldn't believe the Deputy Chief actually agreed to crash at the mansion instead of heading back to Stone Manor.

It made things easier, sure, but it gave Caspian something else. Family. Not the blood kind, and not some pack law BS. Just real family.

The more he hung out with Lysander, the more he got why Elara trusted the guy. He was loyal as hell, solid, protective, and surprisingly funny when he wasn't trying to play the scary tough guy.

Caspian snorted. Honestly, having Lysander around was like suddenly getting the brother he never knew he wanted. Guys like that don't grow on trees. Caspian was keeping him close.

Traffic was a breeze, so he pulled up to the hospital early. The place was a madhouse. Doctors sprinting, nurses darting between rooms, patients packing the waiting area.

This chaotic mess was exactly why Elara loved the place.

He walked into the main hall and spotted his target immediately.

Julian was huddled with two nurses, flipping through charts. The doc looked like hell. Dark bags under his eyes, a wrinkled white coat—classic signs of a brutal shift.

Julian looked up, eyes widening when they locked onto Caspian. He muttered something to the nurses and jogged over. "Caspian?" He raised an eyebrow. "You dying or what?"

Caspian almost rolled his eyes. Right, because he only showed up at a hospital if he was bleeding out. "Got a second?" He jerked his chin toward the nurses.

Julian followed his gaze, looked down at his charts, and let out a heavy sigh. "Yeah. Give me five." He shoved the files at a nurse. "Meet you in there."

Caspian gave a quick nod and headed to the office. True to his word, Julian walked in a few minutes later.

The doc shut the door and collapsed into his chair, looking completely wiped. He yanked his tie loose. "My shift ended ten minutes ago, man," he said, offering a tired grin. "So, what's the emergency?"

Caspian stood there for a second, then sat across from him. "It's about Elara."

Julian sat up straight. A smirk crept onto his face. "What, she finally realize you didn't actually save her ass at Bohemian Grove?"

Caspian didn't smile. "Not even close." Julian's smirk vanished when he saw Caspian's face. The guy was dead serious. Caspian leaned in. "Whatever we say in here, it doesn't leave this room."

Julian shifted, suddenly tense. Not scared, just realizing things were about to get messy. He glanced out at the quiet hallway. "Well, luckily, the coast is clear."

Caspian nodded. "Good. I'm marrying Elara next week."

Dead silence. Julian blinked. Once. Twice. "You're... marrying Elara?" The color drained from his face. Suddenly, all the weird puzzle pieces clicked. The constant bickering, the weird vibe, Caspian always hovering around her, his reaction to the proposal, the way Elara acted whenever Caspian walked in.

Julian sank back, totally stunned. "You two were literally trying to kill each other. What kind of messed-up love story is this?"

Caspian let out a dry, sarcastic laugh. "Trust me, it's a long-ass story." Julian didn't doubt it.

Caspian laced his fingers together, his eyes softening. "But here's the short version. Sera, Thane, and I grew up together."

Julian froze. The puzzle pieces slammed together even faster. Bohemian Grove flashed in his mind. The weird look Elara had given them, the hesitation, that unspoken history.

"I knew it," Julian said, pointing a finger. "Something was off that day. When you and Thane showed up, I knew she recognized you guys. She just played it off like it was nothing."

Caspian’s face darkened. "We had some... history. Misunderstandings." That was a massive understatement. Years of lies, mind games, and pain. But he wasn't going to dump all that baggage now. "I'm telling you this because I need a favor. If Thane finds out about the wedding before we're ready, it's going to hit the fan. If he comes sniffing around here looking for her, call me immediately."

Julian nodded slowly, absorbing the weight of it. But something was still bugging him. He leaned back, squinting at Caspian. "Can I ask you something?"

"Shoot."

"If you're so crazy about her, why keep the wedding a secret?"

Caspian let out a breath. "Because Thane is her ex-husband."

The room went ice-cold. Julian just stared. "Oh." His face went through a dozen emotions. "Okay, let me get this straight. Thane is your best friend—"

"Was," Caspian cut in.

Julian nodded. "Right, *was*. And now you're marrying his ex-wife." He winced. "Honestly, man? If I were him, I'd feel totally stabbed in the back."

Caspian didn't flinch. He got how it looked from the outside. "If you knew the whole story, you wouldn't say that." The sheer certainty in his voice made Julian shut up.

Whatever went down between those three, it was way deeper than he wanted to dig. Julian was smart enough to drop it.

"So," Julian said, changing gears. "What's the actual play here?"

Caspian grinned. Julian immediately got suspicious. "I'm going to spoil my wife rotten."

Julian blinked. "Say what?"

"Surprise vacations, trips around the world, lots of quality time," Caspian said, using it to cover up their pack business.

Julian groaned, burying his face in his hands. "You're trying to get her to quit, aren't you?"

Caspian laughed. "No way. Sera loves being a surgeon. I get that. She'll still be around for the big stuff—emergencies, crazy surgeries, anything that needs her touch."

Julian let out a breath. "Okay, thank God." He shook his head, still processing. "I still can't wrap my head around this." He narrowed his eyes, a sly grin spreading. "Though... I always knew there was some heat between you two. You used that whole 'saving her life' thing to guilt her into marrying you, didn't you?"

Caspian's smile vanished instantly. "No." The word shot out so fast Julian almost cracked up. Caspian leaned in, dead serious. "And she can never hear that. Got it?"

Julian was hooked now. "Okay, if that wasn't it, how the hell did you pull it off?"

Caspian stood up, smooth and effortless, ending the meeting. He flashed a polite, almost menacing smile. "Great catching up, Doctor Hawthorne."

Julian groaned. "Seriously, man?"

Caspian ignored him, heading for the door. "I'll send you the wedding details. Don't flake."

"Caspian," Julian called out as Caspian grabbed the doorknob. "Come on, how'd you do it?"
