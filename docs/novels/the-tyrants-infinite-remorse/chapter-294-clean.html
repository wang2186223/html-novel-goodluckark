NovelVibe

Chapter 297

Caspian tracked a crack on the ceiling with his eyes. "She modeled," he muttered. "That’s the whole reason Graham and I even met. I hired him. Paid him cash to shadow her, keep her out of trouble when I wasn't there. He was already in that scene, you know? It was just easier that way."

Elara swallowed the news in silence. Great. Graham. Just another puzzle piece she’d been blind to, another thread wrapping around her neck. No wonder Caspian played dumb earlier when she asked how they hooked up. He was dodging. The whole damn thing traced back to his ex. A human ex, no less.

That photo burned in her mind's eye. Blair Easton. Some girl wearing her face, her eyes, even dying her hair to match. A walking, breathing band-aid for Caspian’s grief.

"So, where is she?" Elara kept her eyes glued to the glass chandelier above. The truth hit her chest like a lead weight. Inside, something was breaking. A tiny, stupid hope she hadn't even realized she was nursing was turning to dust.

God, she’d wanted that little girl to be hers. She’d prayed for it. Just once, she wanted the Moon Goddess to actually give a damn and grant her one real wish.

It started the exact second those big blue eyes looked up and whispered *Mom*. It wrecked her. Sure, the timeline was messy, the age didn't totally fit. But she’d ignored the math. She figured whoever found the kid, or whoever Lysander dumped her with, just got the dates wrong.

But now? Caspian’s words killed it. That secret dream shriveled up, freezing to death in the open air. Still, she kept her face totally blank. Stone-cold. She’d always been a pro at hiding her shit.

"Delilah," she repeated, her voice flat. "Where’s she hiding?"

"Around," Caspian shrugged. "In the city somewhere. We broke up ages ago, though. I don't let her near Aurelia. Period."

Elara’s brows knit together. "She’s the kid’s mother, Caspian. She has rights. Whatever drama you two had, you don't weaponize the kid." Her voice didn't wobble, even though the words felt like rubbing salt in a brand-new wound.

She meant it, too. But mentally? She was already planning a DNA test. Screw the looks. Faces lie. Doppelgangers exist. If there was a one-in-a-million shot, she had to prove it. She’d check the kid for birthmarks, too—something unique, something you can't fake.

"Delilah’s human," Caspian said. His tone was tired, like a script he’d run through a thousand times in his head until the pain went numb. "And the breakup? That’s on me. Entirely. She wasn't the issue. I was."

He sighed, slow and heavy. "I wanted her to be you, Elara. Not just a carbon copy on the outside. I wanted your brain, your heart. I kept nagging her to go into med school, start a business, do *something* with her life."

"But she wasn't built that way. She just wanted to walk runways, party, and coast. She was chill, and I hated her for it because I couldn't get it."

He stopped, swallowing hard. "Plus, she wouldn't claim me in public. Behind closed doors? Sure, she was sweet, super clingy, all over me. But out there? With paparazzi or friends? I was a ghost. A nobody. She’d look straight through me."

Elara kept her mouth shut. Who in their right mind treats a guy like Caspian like a dirty secret? Then again, she’d seen those old videos on his phone. She’d done the exact same thing to him once. But hey, no memory, no guilt. She wasn't going to beat herself up for a past life.

"I dragged it out way too long," Caspian muttered. "Only because she looked like you. Pathetic, right? But that's the truth."

A dark smirk flashed on his lips, then died. "Graham finally staged an intervention. Told me to snap out of it, stop self-sabotaging. I almost threw him through a wall."

The smirk returned, colder this time. "Then he dropped the photos on me."

Elara glanced over at him.

"Delilah was living a double life. Clubbing every single weekend, even weekdays when she claimed she was booked. Getting completely wasted, blacking out. Hooking up with random guys when the bar lights came on."

Caspian’s voice was deadpan. The rage was gone, burned out long ago. "Graham had the receipts. Photos, timestamps, addresses. Everything."

He shook his head, staring back up. "That’s when it clicked. She had your face, but she wasn't you. Never would be. I was just torturing both of us. So, yeah. I dumped her."

Elara let the silence hang. "And what about Aurelia?"

"I'm getting there," Caspian said. "Hold on."
