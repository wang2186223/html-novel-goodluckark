Chapter 89

Elara went totally ghost-white. The whole room instantly locked up, like everyone suddenly realized things had just gone sideways.

Julian’s face fell. His easy smile vanished, replaced by a tense, angry scowl. He opened his mouth to speak but stopped, sensing a really bad vibe.

The billionaires around the table stopped laughing. Confusion hit them one by one, a quiet wave of "what the hell is happening" washing over the lounge.

Julian gulped down his nerves. He braced himself and looked straight at Caspian, the guy running the whole show.

"Hey, Caspian," Julian said, trying to keep his voice from shaking. "Is option three... like, actually what I think it is?"

The question dropped like a bomb. Total, suffocating silence crushed the room.

Every single rich guy turned to Caspian. They all knew he was the one who made the final call.

Nobody muttered a word. But their eyes said it all—they were just waiting for Caspian to say yes or no.

Caspian slowly looked over at Thane. His face went stone-cold. He was officially done with this crap.

"Alright, that’s enough," Caspian said, his voice low and dangerous.

Thane’s smug grin slipped. Panic flashed in his eyes for a split second. He leaned in close to Caspian, whispering so only they could hear.

"Come on, Caspian, help me out here," Thane muttered, sounding pathetic for a guy with his ego. "I’ve got her right where I want her. I just want to bleed her dry for what she stole from me."

Caspian raised an eyebrow. Suddenly, the lightbulb went off.

Thane knew Elara would never say yes to that dirty option. He didn't want her to agree; he just wanted to slap her with a massive cash penalty when she refused.

That was a cheap trick, and they didn't play like that here. Caspian absolutely hated shady, backhanded moves.

He hadn't built his empire by playing petty games. His whole brand was about power, money, and sticking to the code.

"Your beef with her is your own business," Caspian said, cool but firm. "But she didn't come here just for you. The other guys deserve a shot at her pitch. Some of them actually want to hear it."

Thane’s face fell. He’d hoped Caspian would just back him up, but the older guys in the room knew the drill. Especially Victor, who’d been watching the whole drama play out.

If Thane pushed it, Victor would definitely tell his son that Thane tried to pull a fast one and Caspian let it slide.

That would ruin Caspian’s reputation, and Thane knew that meant major trouble for himself down the road.

"Hey, chill, I was just kidding," Thane said, letting out a fake laugh that didn't hide his clenched jaw.

Elara finally let out a breath she didn't know she was holding. Her chest loosened, and she could actually breathe again.

Thane started to open his mouth again, but Caspian cut him off with a sharp hand gesture.

"Cut the crap," Caspian snapped, scanning the table to remind everyone who was boss. "We’re burning daylight here. Let’s keep it simple. You play chess against our best player. You win, you get to pitch your product to the room. You lose, you pack your bags and get out."

Elara blinked. Chess? Wait, did she hear that right?

She looked around the table. A few guys nodded. Okay, so he wasn't kidding.

Julian breathed a sigh of relief. This was way better than whatever sleazy trap Thane had been setting up.

Even if she lost, she could leave with her head held high. And Julian had already decided he was walking out with her the second it was over.

The others were busy thinking it over, but Elara wasn't about to let Thane off the hook for his little stunt.

"We both know Mr. Walker wasn't joking about that other option," Elara said, looking straight at Caspian. "And honestly, I don't know whose side you were on. Would you have actually played fair if Julian hadn't opened his mouth?"
