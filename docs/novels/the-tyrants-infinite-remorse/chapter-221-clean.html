Chapter 223

"She chills me out. Get closer, dude." Gideon’s voice buzzed in Caspian’s head. It sounded weirdly steady. They stood back, tracking the drama between Elara, Briar, and Thane.

Caspian bolted back. The second Elara turned their way, his boots hit the dirt. He needed distance. Fast. If she got too close, things would get messy, and his brain couldn't process that yet.

He didn't make it two steps. "Are you trying to kill me?" Gideon barked, his chill vibe vanishing. "Her wolf is the only thing keeping me glued together until we fix this mate bond. Show some damn gratitude."

Caspian froze. He clenched his jaw, hesitated, then gave in. He ducked behind a thick pine, hiding his frame but keeping his eyes locked on the clearing.

He watched Rhys walk up to Elara. Nothing shady was happening. No flirting, nothing. But a heavy, ugly knot still twisted in Caspian's gut as he listened.

"Hey, Sera, my bad I'm late," Rhys said, stopping right in front of her. He looked wrecked. "I should've blocked Briar from leaving the house when you went into the trees."

Elara shrugged. She acted like it was whatever, but her eyes flashed. "Don't sweat it. My wolf wanted a piece of her, and she took it. We're square."

Rhys stared, his face darkening. "So, I heard some rumors," he said slowly. "Did she always treat you like this?" He got it now. The stories about Elara bullying Briar were total BS. Briar was the real instigator.

Elara sighed, her shoulders dropping. "This? Please. This is light work," she said with a dry, humorless smirk. "The pack buys her crap every time anyway. I just wish she'd stay the hell out of my face."

Rhys rubbed his neck. "Look, I think Thane actually buys your side of the story this time—"

"Don't fall for his crap," Elara snapped, her eyes narrowing. Her voice was dead cold. "He used to swallow every lie she fed him. Last time he accused me of beating her up, I actually did it. Just to give him a real reason." No bragging. No guilt. Just cold, hard facts.

Rhys let out a long breath. "Damn. I'm sorry you went through that," he muttered, his voice softening. "Lysander should've had your back."

Elara looked away, her eyes scanning the dirt. "Lysander was a beta," she said quietly. "You know how that goes." Rhys nodded. No explanation needed. A beta's loyalty is a leash. They obey the Chief Commander, period. Right and wrong don't even enter the equation.

He cleared his throat, changing the subject. "Alright, let's get you to the hotel. I grabbed my bags. I'll crash in the room next to yours, same as last night. On the way, we can talk about the MindNest investments you set up for me. And seriously, take whatever you need from my stocks. I don't care."

Elara let out a tired chuckle. "Fine, I won't say no to free money," she said, her voice softening. "But hey, how's Asher doing?"

They strolled toward the parking lot. "He's great," Rhys said. "Seriously, I've never seen the kid smile like that." Elara's face softened, a genuine smile tugging at her lips. Then a voice shattered the peace from behind.

"She's coming with me."

They spun around. Caspian stood there, and the moment Elara locked eyes with him, her stomach did a violent flip.

"Oh, come on, can we please run with him just once?" Blaze chimed in, practically bouncing. "I know you don't care, but his wolf is hot. Like, seriously hot."

Elara balled her fists. *Shut up,* she thought, then looked at Caspian. "No thanks. I'm good with Rhys."

"I didn't ask," Caspian growled, taking a heavy step forward. His voice dropped an octave, dead serious. "I'm driving you to the hotel. I'm keeping watch tonight."

"Hell yes!" Blaze cheered. "Thane is gonna lose his freaking mind—"

But Elara wasn't stupid. Caspian's wolf was bad news, and she knew it. With Blaze already hyped up and smelling blood, putting two ticking time bombs in the same car was a disaster waiting to happen. "I said no," she snapped.

Caspian didn't budge. He kept coming, ignoring her completely. He crowded her space, towering over her until she couldn't look away. "Look," he muttered, his voice dropping low. "I came here for you. We need to talk."

He was right there. His heat radiated off him, pressing against her skin. Her pulse hammered against her ribs, a wild, physical reaction she couldn't shut down.

Then—

"What the hell is this?" Thane's voice sliced through the air. He stepped out of the shadows, eyes sharp, having seen way too much already.
