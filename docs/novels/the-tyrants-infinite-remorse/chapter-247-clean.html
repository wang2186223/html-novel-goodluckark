Chapter 250

"Damn, Caspian and Elara look hot together," a patient muttered from her cot. She sat up, her voice cutting right through the heavy downpour outside. "Too bad it’s a no-go. Alphas have that whole strict bro-code about exes."

The girl in the next bed chimed in fast, like she’d been dying to say it. "Yeah, but codes get broken. It just gets super messy. No way Caspian risks his neck for his best bud's ex-wife. That’s a whole different level of drama."

"They’re divorced, though!" The first girl crossed her arms, shutting down the argument. "Look, I hated her guts before. Seriously. But today? She basically saved my life. She deserves to be happy, period."

"Caspian hates women, dude." The second girl rolled her eyes. "Like, universally."

"Please. There’s always that one girl who wrecks the rules." She waved her hand, dismissing it. "Look at our own boss. Terrifying to everyone, right? Except his co-director. She’s his literal kryptonite."

A few feet away, Charles pushed himself off the floor. His knees popped. His lower back screamed. He’d been crouching, scraping up the ruined cure. But the pain vanished the second he spotted Thane through the window. Thane was just standing there outside the ward, getting absolutely drenched.

Felix’s stomach did a hard flip.

He stood up fast, pointing a finger at the gossiping patients. "If you two have the energy to sit there and talk shit like this is a social club, you’re discharged. Pack up and get out before you ruin my day."

Thane had heard enough. Honestly, it wasn't even the first time someone had pushed this crap on him. *Caspian looks at Elara differently. Caspian treats her like she’s special.*

He’d heard it a million times. Every single time, he brushed it off as pure garbage.

He knew Caspian. The guy was all about the green. Cash. Business. Period. Caspian was a cold, calculating bastard who didn't move a finger unless it lined his pockets first.

Whatever he was doing for Elara, it was a transaction. Nothing more.

Plus, after everything Caspian had survived, no woman was getting inside his head. No way. The only exception would be a fated mate. That’s why the guy was obsessed with fixing the mate bond. He needed magic to crack his own shell. A regular she-wolf couldn't do shit.

So, no. Elara wasn't breaking anything.

Thane let out a slow breath, staring into the gray rain.

*They look good together.*

Yeah, right. Sure.

Elara didn't belong in Caspian's league. She didn't deserve that. Honestly, she was right where she belonged—dating some basic human named Julian. That was her speed.

Which meant Briar actually won. She married up, way higher than Elara. Thane liked that thought. It felt right.

As for Caspian? Zero chance. Caspian wouldn't touch Thane's leftovers. Elara was a closed book, pages already rotting. She was beneath him emotionally, even if she had a fat bank account. It wasn't mean; it was just facts.

He stood in the rain, repeating the lie until he almost believed it.

Almost.

He scanned the driveway. His jaw clenched. Caspian was gone.

No car. Just wet gravel where it used to be.

Thane stood there, rain pouring down his face, thinking fast. He had to get Elara back. The cure was half-baked.

He’d pushed too hard. He could admit that now, quietly, inside his own head. He’d broken her, and now everything was screwed.

But the cure was the priority. He had to focus.

He opened the mind-link, hitting the gate guards hard.

*Did Caspian leave yet?*

The reply came back instantly.

*Negative. The storm knocked down some massive trees across the road. We’re trying to clear them, but it’s slow going with this weather. They’re stuck.*

Something tight in Thane’s chest loosened. He breathed.

*Where are they? Is Sera with him?*

He froze. The name slipped out before he could stop it. He bit his lip. He meant to say *Elara*. Keep it professional.

But the old nickname slipped out anyway. At least she wasn't there to roll her eyes and correct him with that bored, sassy look she always gave him.

*Caspian is out of the car trying to clear the brush. Director Sera is still inside.*

A pause. Short, but heavy.

Then the guard’s voice changed. The panic was instant.

*Oh, shit.*

Thane frowned, rain dripping off his chin. *What? Talk to me.*

*It’s... it’s Director Sera. The...*

That was it.

Thane didn't think. His body just moved. One second he was standing there soaking wet, the next his bones were cracking and shifting. He dropped to all fours, his wolf taking over in a violent burst.

He was sprinting before the transformation even finished, paws tearing up the mud, hauling ass toward the gate.

The rain was a solid wall. Everything was a cold, loud blur.

He hit the gate and skidded to a stop.

And what he saw out there shattered the lock on a box he’d kept buried deep inside his chest for years.
