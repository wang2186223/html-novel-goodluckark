NovelVibe

Chapter 334

Lysander kept his eyes glued to the asphalt, shaking his head. "Well, at least your psycho-protective boyfriend isn't breathing down my neck anymore."

Honestly, he loved how crazy Caspian was about Elara, but damn, it was a lot of pressure for a guy like him. Sure, he came from money, but he’d been trained to serve his whole life. That kind of stress could break anyone.

But his joke hit the spot. Elara let out a real, unfiltered laugh—the kind she couldn't choke back if she tried. "Boyfriend." God, that word felt so weird. She’d never actually had a real relationship before.

Now, she felt like some giddy teenager. Lysander’s grin stretched wide. For years, he’d watched her carry shit that would’ve absolutely crushed anyone else.

Hearing her laugh like that? Best freaking reward ever.

"Never seen this side of Caspian," she said, sinking back into the passenger seat. "And honestly? I kind of love it."

Lysander snorted, shooting an eyebrow up. "Kind of? Uh, Sera, the guy shadows you like you’re gonna vanish the second he blinks."

Her face flushed instant fire. "Shut up, no he doesn't."

"Oh, he absolutely does," he shot back, grin widening. "If you told him tomorrow you wanted a piece of the moon, he’d probably start welding a rocket in the backyard tonight."

She burst out laughing again. It was a ridiculous image, but with Caspian? Yeah, totally believable.

A nice, easy silence settled over the car. Then Lysander spoke up, his voice dropping into something softer, way more real. "Seriously, though. I’m really happy for you."

Elara turned to him. The pure warmth in his eyes hit her right in the chest. "You deserve this, Elara."

Lysander’s knuckles went white on the wheel. "After all the garbage Thane put you through..." A dark shadow crossed his face. "I still remember the nasty shit he said to you."

She did too. Some wounds don't just fade. They scab over, they stop bleeding, but the scars stay put.

Lysander clenched his jaw, pushing through. "When he threw that challenge in your face and said no Chief Commander would ever put a ring on your finger... God, I hated myself."

Elara’s brow furrowed. "Why?"

"Because I felt so fucking useless," he muttered, his voice cracking a little. "I wanted you to end up with someone a million times better than him. Someone stronger. Someone who actually deserved you."

A massive wave of emotion hit Elara’s chest. Lysander had been her rock through every single heartbreak, every bloody battle, every impossible corner she’d been backed into.

She reached over, giving his arm a firm, grateful squeeze. "You were never useless. You had my back the best way you could—even when you risked everything and ignored his orders to throw me in the dungeon."

Lysander didn't say anything, but the look in his eyes said it all.

They chatted easily for the rest of the ride. By the time the car pulled up to the office building, they’d both flipped the switch back to serious work mode.

The second they walked into her office, Lysander went full professional. He dropped a stack of folders onto her desk with a neat slap. "We’re still bringing on more human staff."

Elara nodded. That was top priority.

He kept going. "The training program is solid. Goal’s the same: we need enough capable humans running the daily grind so we can head back to the pack for good."

"Perfect. Once we’re back from the moon festival, we’ll huddle with the board. For now, keep the weekly reports coming. After the meeting, we can scale them back to monthly or quarterly."

Lysander nodded, flipping open another file. "Orion and his crew wrapped up the heavy lifting on the second game."

That got her attention fast. "Oh?"

A proud smirk hit his face. "Yeah. I tested it myself."

Elara chuckled. "And?"

"It’s addictive as hell," he said.

"That bad?"

"Worse," he laughed, shaking his head. "I lost three freaking hours and didn't even notice." He pulled up the demo on his phone, and Elara’s jaw dropped.

"Serious gamers are gonna eat this up." They’d built this one specifically because of customer feedback. Some people were straight-up game addicts, and MindNest wasn't hitting that itch for them.

Lysander wasn't done. He cracked open another report. "You also wanted us to look into small businesses."

Elara nodded. "Right."

"We’ve started buying up a few promising ones. We’ll restructure, dump some cash in. Iris is currently training in the investment department under Iris."

Elara was seriously impressed; this expansion was moving at warp speed.

He added proudly, "Oh, and the kids' game is almost wrapped up too."

"So, basically, everyone’s drowning in work," she said, smiling wide.

"Totally swamped," Lysander agreed, leaning back. "The team is working their asses off."

The pride in his voice was classic Deputy Chief. Reliable, fiercely loyal. No matter what job he had, those core traits never changed.

"Thanks, Lysander."

The raw sincerity in her voice made him wave his hand, brushing it off. "Hold on, I’m not done yet."

Elara gestured for him to keep going. He grabbed another paper. "I hired a Deputy CEO."

Her eyebrows shot up. Lysander nodded, totally confident. "Human. Tons of experience. She starts next week."

Then he looked her dead in the eye. "I assume Caspian told you about the pack position he offered me."

The reminder brought last night’s conversation rushing back in a heavy wave. Lysander spent the next few minutes laying it all out—the talks, the blueprints, the massive responsibilities Caspian wanted him to shoulder.

By the time he finished, Elara was totally speechless, her heart hammering against her ribs. "I can't believe he did all that for me." It felt completely unreal.

Lysander let out a soft chuckle. "He loves you, Elara. From what I’ve seen, you’re the only woman he’s ever looked at." Simple, direct, absolute truth. "And because of that, he wants to make your life easier."

A warm, glowing heat bloomed in her chest. Lysander pointed a finger at her. "So stop stressing. Just focus on getting your memories back." Then he flashed a massive grin. "I’ll handle the wedding with the redhead."

Elara froze. But her next reaction only left Lysander looking completely confused.
