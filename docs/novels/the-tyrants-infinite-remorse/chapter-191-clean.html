Chapter 193

Elara chewed on Orion’s update. Thane had Briar locked down on house arrest. Finally. The guy was finally sniffing out her bullshit. Sure, he didn't have the whole puzzle yet, but the seed of doubt was planted. Perfect. Elara needed him blind a little longer. He had way too much left to lose.

Let him play house with Asher. Let him play the doting dad, let that bond wrap around his heart until it choked him. She wanted him so deep in that father-son fantasy that ripping it away would tear his chest open. That was the play. Make the fall fatal. Let him think the kid was his forever, then—bam. The truth that Asher wasn't his blood would leave him a total wreck.

"Yeah, got it," Elara said, keeping her voice dead level while her chest burned. "Dump the files on me. And seriously, steer clear of Briar." Thane's radar was up. If Orion kept sniffing around, he’d blow the whole thing. The guy had zero chill, always digging, always rushing. One wrong move and the trap snaps shut too early. Thane picked his poison years ago when he chose that viper. Now? Time to drink up. "You got it, boss," Orion muttered and hung up.

Two minutes later, her phone went nuts. Ping. Ping. Ping. Orion’s data dump hit her inbox like a landslide. Elara scrolled, her eyes scanning the screen until she finally leaned back, totally stunned. Holy hell. It was a trainwreck. Briar’s sweet-girl act was a masterclass in fraud. Underneath the pretty face lay a mountain of trash. Shady wire transfers, encrypted chats, secret hookups. Enough nuclear-grade dirt to blow her entire life to pieces.

The next few days flew by in a blur of work at the new setup. Lysander had seriously outdone himself. He’d cloned her entire home tech rig here so she could swap desks without losing a single beat. She stood there, just taking it in. The layout was killer. He’d split the floor plan perfectly—a quiet, cozy lounge to decompress when the stress hit, and a sleek, high-end conference zone to talk big business without anyone poking their nose into her personal space.

A warm buzz bloomed in her chest. She looked around, catching his eye. "Hey. Thanks, Lysander," she murmured, meaning every word. "Seriously. It’s perfect." A massive grin broke across his face. He’d been sweating her reaction, clearly. "Well, hold on. I’ve got one more thing," he said, tapping a wall panel. The door clicked open. Orion strolled in.

"Sup, boss," Orion beamed, chest puffed out. "Reporting for duty." Elara couldn't help it—a huge smile split her face. "They treating you right?" she asked. The guy looked like he’d won the lottery. "Dude, Mr. Stone hooked me up with a sick three-bedroom penthouse and a sweet ride," he said, practically vibrating. "And the paycheck? Insane. Thank you so much."

Elara chuckled, waving him to a chair. "Alright, let's get you up to speed on the tech so you can run this show. Shout if you get lost." Orion locked in. For the next three hours, she walked him through MindNest's guts. She broke down the OS, the firewalls, the encrypted comms, and the backend admin panels. By the end of the crash course, Orion’s jaw was on the floor.

"Uh, are we scaling up?" Orion asked, rubbing his neck. "If we're going global, the backend load is gonna be crazy." Lysander kicked back in his seat. "Already on it. I'm scouting talent now. If we need more hands, just hire them." Elara smirked, nudging Orion. "Oh, by the way—Lysander’s the CEO of MindNest. So, yeah, he’s the big boss." Orion sat up straight, giving Lysander a quick nod. "Got it. Sir." Lysander stood up with a chuckle. "Come on. Let's go meet the team."

The afternoon flew by. By clock-out time, everyone was dead tired but buzzing. Elara was packing her bag when a thought hit her. "Hey, we need to send Caspian a thank-you gift tomorrow." Lysander paused, then nodded. "Way ahead of you. I'll handle it." Next morning, he sent the package over in her name. Easy peasy. Except when the reply came back, the room went dead silent. It was like someone sucked all the oxygen right out of the air.
