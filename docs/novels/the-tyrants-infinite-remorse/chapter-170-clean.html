Chapter 172

Caspian went stiff. Her words sliced right through him. Damn. Was he really that easy to read? Was the stress literally written all over his face?

Usually, Thane was his guy. Whenever life turned into a dumpster fire, Thane got it. Caspian would walk in ready to blow his brains out and walk out feeling like he could actually breathe again.

Not anymore, though.

Thane had his own mountain of crap to deal with. Plus, Caspian was playing double agent for Elara now. One slip, one dumb look, one loose lip, and the whole house of cards would blow up.

Avoiding Thane was just basic survival.

And spilling his guts to Elara? Yeah, right. Caspian didn't do the whole "feelings" thing with women. Especially not his buddy's ex-wife. No way. Keep it strictly business. No messy vibes, no drama. Safe.

"I got this," he grunted, shutting her down. "Just don't skip your sessions with Lysander."

Elara flinched. She tried to play it cool, but he caught that flash of hurt in her eyes before she masked it.

Whatever. Why did she even care? What did she expect, a hug?

They weren't buddies.

She had to keep reminding herself of that every time she caught herself staring at him like an idiot.

The next few days were a blur of ghosting.

Elara pulled the early-bird routine at the clinic—in fast, out faster, dodging everyone. She couriered Thane’s meds instead of hand-delivering them. The rest of her time? Sweating it out in the gym with Lysander.

Twenty-four hours before D-day, her phone buzzed. Dominic. She stared at the screen, took a breath, and swiped green.

"Hey," Dominic purred, sounding way too pleased with himself. "Quick question. Red or white?"

Elara pinched her nose. She hated booze. Water was her jam.

"Red," she lied.

He chuckled. "Yeah, figured. You look like a red wine girl."

Ugh. A wave of pure annoyance hit her. She gripped the phone until her knuckles turned white, but kept her voice sweet. "Is that all?"

"Nah," Dominic said. "Just planning tomorrow. Gotta make sure everything's perfect."

Elara rolled her eyes so hard she saw brain.

Then he dropped it. "So, what size lingerie do you wear?"

Elara choked on her own spit. Wait, what? Did he seriously just ask that? "Excuse me?"

"My bad," Dominic said, not sounding sorry at all. "You're probably the old-school type. We can skip the fancy underwear for the first date."

His voice dropped an octave, dripping with that greasy charm. "Maybe next time."

Elara ground her teeth. Her jaw literally ached.

God, she wanted to reach through the screen and rip his throat out.

But he kept going, totally oblivious. "What kind of ride do you like?"

"Don't care about cars," she snapped. "I'm a gamer."

Silence. Then he laughed. "Huh. You're definitely different."

Elara smirked. "You have no idea."

"See you tomorrow night, then," Dominic said. "And pack a bag. You're staying over."

This time, Elara actually smiled. Oh, yeah. Tomorrow, this whole nightmare would be over.

She hung up and whipped around to Lysander. "Did you get Orion's place? And the wheels?"

Lysander nodded. "Yep. Done. The car's waiting for him the second he lands."

Elara let out a breath she didn't know she was holding. "Thanks."

The next day dragged like a funeral. Every hour felt like a year.

She kept checking her phone, itching to text Caspian. Was he back from the pack?

What if he flaked? But honestly, she just wanted him there. Things always felt less chaotic when he was around.

A few hours before the date, she was putting on the finishing touches when someone knocked.

Lysander poked his head in. He looked super awkward, like he'd just walked in on his parents.

"Hey, Sera," he mumbled, scratching his neck. "Caspian's here. He wants in."

Elara's heart did a flip.

He actually showed up. A wave of panic and weird relief hit her at the same time, making her chest tight.

Awesome, he kept his word. But why the hell was he coming to her bedroom?

"Tell him I'll be out in two," she said, scrambling.

Lysander sighed, looking like he wanted to be literally anywhere else.

"Yeah, well," Lysander said. "He said—and I'm quoting here—he needs to make sure your outfit isn't too trashy for tonight."

Elara stared. "Wait. What?"
