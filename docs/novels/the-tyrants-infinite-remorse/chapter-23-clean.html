NovelVibe

Chapter 23

Thane’s call to Caspian earlier had gone straight to voicemail. Never a good sign. Caspian was always buried in some corporate bloodbath unless he was dodging calls. But when the guy actually showed up, the timing was perfect. Thane felt his chest loosen up a bit.

Caspian stopped at the door, raising an eyebrow at the setup. Elara was sitting right there by the desk, heels dug into the floor, spine stiff as a board. Thane was stuck behind his desk, jaw locked tight.

"Am I interrupting?" Caspian asked, already turning on his heel like he wanted out.

Thane waved him in. "Nope. Right on time."

Caspian stepped inside, letting the door click shut. He’d seen the missed calls but had been stuck in back-to-back meetings all morning. He’d planned to grab Thane for a quick lunch to talk, not walk into a room with Elara in it.

Just looking at her made his blood boil. It was instant, stupid, and totally uncalled for. Old grudges have a way of popping back up. He glared. "You two back together or what?" He tried to sound casual, but it came out sharp.

Elara rolled her eyes, crossing her arms. "In his dreams," she snapped. "I’m just taking what’s mine. Too bad he’s terrified to let go."

Caspian glanced at Thane, then locked eyes with her again. God, this woman. He still remembered their last fight. She’d called him an idiot to his face, and he wasn’t about to let that slide.

"Say you get all that cash," Caspian said, stepping closer, his voice dropping to a chill. "What then? Nobody in this town will touch you. I’ll personally blackball you."

Lysander went stiff. Elara just smiled—not a nervous twitch, but a cold, slow smirk. "Then I’ll start my own damn company."

Caspian blinked, caught off guard for a second, before his mouth twisted into a nasty grin. "Right. Good luck finding partners for that."

Thane let out a small, smug smile, knowing Caspian had the power to back it up. But Lysander leaned over and whispered in Elara’s ear, "Focus on Thane. Don't poke the bear."

Elara let out a slow breath, stood up, and let her heels click against the floor. She marched right up to Caspian, standing toe-to-toe, looking him straight in the eye. "I already did," she said, cool as ice. "And don't forget what I warned you about, Caspian."

Caspian snorted. The sheer nerve of this girl. Before he could snap back, Thane grabbed his arm and pulled him to the side, whispering, "If I hand over the shares, the board will lose their minds. The company will tank. Can you buy her out?"

Caspian scowled. He already owned a chunk of Walker Global, but buying more wasn't on his radar. "That takes time," he muttered. "I don't keep hundreds of millions in cash just sitting in a bank account."

Thane nodded. He got it. No real billionaire kept liquid cash like that. "She won't wait," Thane said, grim. "She wants it now."

Caspian sighed, frustrated, and turned back to Elara. "Thane signs today. I’ll wire you the cash next week. Deal?" He hoped she’d back down. Just this once.

"No deal," Elara said instantly.

Caspian’s face went dark. "Look, I’m trying to be nice here. Take the deal, and I won't mess with your business." He was ready to lie for his buddy, but that just pissed Elara off more. She stared him down. She could wait, sure, but she wanted to hurt them now. Taking the cash immediately would wreck Walker Global, and that was payback for what they did to her kid.

"I want the money now," she said, hard as nails. "It's way overdue."

Caspian was seething. Nobody rejected him twice. Lysander looked between them, sweating. "Sera, come on. A week isn't that long."

She didn't budge. "Now."

Thane let out a harsh, dry laugh. "Oh, I get it," he sneered. "You're just bitter and jealous because I never loved you. Because I dumped you." He squared his shoulders. "Fine. Let's just remarry."

Elara stared at him like he was dirt. "You are seriously delusional," she said flatly. "I despise you, Thane. Get out of my face."

The words stung. Thane froze. Right then, his phone buzzed loudly on the desk, breaking the silence.

Elara glanced at the screen. The caller ID read "Love" with two red hearts. She smirked. "Your little chick is calling. Go play house."

Lysander snorted, trying not to laugh. Even Caspian had to bite his lip. He hated Elara, but Briar was a nightmare. He just couldn't say it out loud.

Thane snatched up the phone, his face changing instantly. Briar's voice came through, shaking. "Rav... where are you? They're wheeling me into surgery. I'm scared."

Thane's grip tightened on the phone. Without a word, he grabbed a pen and scribbled his name on the papers. "I'm taking the jet," he said softly. "I'll be there soon."

That soft voice cut Elara deeper than any insult. He knew how to care, how to protect. He just never wanted to do it for her.

With the signed papers in hand, she turned and walked out without looking back.

Thane turned to Caspian, rushing to follow her. "I gotta get back to the pack. Need to name a beta. Call Roric if anything comes up." He ran for the door, but stopped at the threshold, looking back. "About ruining Elara... we still on for that?"
