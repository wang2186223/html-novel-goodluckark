NovelVibe

Chapter 35

Valerie, Elara, and Lysander squeezed into some fancy joint a block from Ashkael Towers. The place reeked of expensive yeast, vintage red, and old-money hush. No scenes allowed here.

They ordered. Water hit the glasses. Elara leaned back, locking eyes with Valerie. Cool. Direct.

"Alright," Elara said. "Spill. What went down?"

Valerie forced a pathetic little laugh. She grabbed her water, chugging half of it like it was straight tequila.

"Briar wanted the Asher cure formula," Valerie said, throat tight. "We fought. Suddenly, my actual job was the prize for whoever cracked it first. So, yeah. I walked. Done with the pack drama."

She stared at the wall, eyes blank.

"Figured I'd crash at my guy's place till I landed something else." A nasty smirk crossed her face. "But, oh, guess what?"

Elara didn't even blink. She saw the trainwreck coming. "He was screwing someone else."

Valerie nodded. Elara let out a sharp, dry laugh.

"And you're losing your mind over *that*?" Elara scoffed. "Seriously? A cheating prick isn't worth a headache, let alone a breakdown."

Valerie just stared. She was totally floored. Elara’s divorce was barely a month old, but look at her—glowing, sharp, totally put-together. Meanwhile, Valerie felt like her chest was being ripped open.

"I'm not tough like you," Valerie muttered. She wanted a double shot of whiskey, bad, but this wasn't a dive bar. She had to play nice.

"Nobody's born tough," Elara said, her voice dropping a notch. "It's a choice." She softened, but barely. "You think I didn't spend nights crying, begging Thane to love me?"

Valerie blinked. No way. That didn't fit the ice-queen Elara who walked the hospital halls ignoring the gossip.

"I don't buy it," Valerie said.

"Believe it." Elara shrugged as the waiter dropped their plates. Once he cleared out, she leaned in. "That's how I learned. Tears don't fix men. If a guy loves you, he protects you. He doesn't hurt you on purpose. And if he doesn't love you..." She paused, deadpan. "Nothing you do matters. You could die for him, and he still wouldn't care."

Valerie swallowed the hard truth. "Yeah. You're right. When the other girl showed up, he completely lost his mind. Like his whole world ended."

Her jaw clenched. "But me? The one he actually betrayed? He didn't even look at me. I get it now. Screw him. I'm putting everything into my career."

"Now you're talking," Elara said. No one starts strong. You just survive the hits. Elara had lost her own kid, and she was still clawing her way up. "Got a lead on a job?"

"Haven't even looked," Valerie muttered.

Elara smiled. "Get on it. You're too good. The world always needs real doctors."

They started eating. Silverware scraped the plates. Lysander, quiet until now, looked up at Valerie. "Saw you with Caspian's boy, Caleb. What's the deal there?"

Valerie stuffed her face. First time she'd had an appetite since the breakup.

"Caleb?" she said, chewing. "He's my ex's best friend. But, like, actually decent. They had a huge blowout over me, and Caleb offered me his couch." She smiled a little. "He's sweet. For real."

Elara raised an eyebrow. Caleb worked for Caspian. That was a red flag.

"I gotta run," Elara said, wiping her mouth. Short and sweet. "Got stuff to handle."

Lysander slapped a stack of twenties on the table.

Valerie’s face fell. "Wait, where are you working? Let me come with."

Elara gave a small smile. "Nowhere yet. I'm taking a break from medicine. Too draining. I want to set up some businesses I can just hand off to managers first."

Valerie pressed her lips. "Well, when you're ready, call me. I'm in."

"Deal," Elara said. She liked Valerie's drive. When she finally built her own clinic, Valerie was first on the list.

Outside, Elara's phone buzzed. Valerie waved and hopped into a cab. Elara pulled her phone from her expensive bag, expecting some work emergency.

She opened the text. A video loaded. Her face went totally blank.

Lysander leaned over, squinting at the screen.

"Ugh, gross," he spat. "She's so desperate. Just block her."

Elara wasn't even mad. She was just frozen by the caption under the clip.

*Eight years of keeping his bed warm, and he still wants me like his favorite cheat meal. I'm the only one who makes him feel like a real man. You never could, sweetie. Never will.*

"Eight years..." Elara whispered, fingers shaking.

Lysander frowned. He thought she was still crying over Thane.

"That night..." Elara's voice cracked. "He thought I was Briar. They were already hooking up before the Moon Festival. Doing everything. I wasn't the one who got cheated on, Lysander. I was just the speed bump."

Silence.

Then Lysander muttered, "If she's so secure... why the hell is she sending you this?"
