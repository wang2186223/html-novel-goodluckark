Chapter 105

The two guys stared at each other, totally brain-fried. They couldn't wrap their heads around it. A Chief Commander throwing down like this? Over a single, hated she-wolf? No way. It made zero sense.

"Caspian, you're seriously kicking us out of the circle? Like, for real?" Riven’s heart hammered against his ribs. Reality was sinking in, hard. Caspian ran the damn council. The guy was supposed to be Mr. Public Relations, not some babysitter dealing with petty high school drama.

Caspian just stared. Dead eyes. Ice-cold voice. "You're already out," he said, the quiet threat hanging heavy. "Tonight, this ends two ways. Option one: you both resign as Alphas. Then, you pay with flesh."

Silence dropped like a brick. The midnight dark hid their pale faces, but the air was thick enough to choke on. They were terrified.

"Say what?" Riven thrashed against his binds, screaming. "That's total bullshit, Caspian!" *Crack.* Caspian’s fist shut him up, slamming right into his jaw.

"Watch your mouth," Caspian muttered, his voice a flat warning.

Riven’s vision blurred. Tears stung his eyes, his head spinning from the impact. He tried to blink the pain away. "You seriously expect us to step down over that bi—" He cut himself off, feeling the phantom sting of the last punch. "I mean... Elara."

Caspian pursed his lips, playing out the scenario. "Actually, scratch that. I don't run your packs anyway." He shrugged, a casual gesture that sent shivers down their spines. "New plan. You two whip each other. Last man standing wins. The other dies."

Finn started shaking. He couldn't stop the tremors. "Caspian, are you out of your goddamn mind?" His voice cracked, giving away his panic.

Caspian let out a slow, humorless smirk. "You wanted Elara dead tonight. If help hadn't shown up, she'd be a corpse. You think you just get a pass?" He locked eyes with them. "Attempted murder is still murder, boys."

Riven’s shoulders slumped. Fighting was useless. It only got him punched. "Fine," he muttered, exhausted and pissed off. "We'll whip each other if that's what you want. But cut the dying part, okay?"

Caspian paused, another idea clicking into place. He gave a slow nod. "Sure. But you don't get the final option until you're done."

They were cornered. They knew it. Neither liked it, but saying no would only make things worse. "Fine," they grunted.

Finn’s brain was already spinning. *The second he unties us, we bolt. We just refuse to play his game.*

But Caspian wasn't stupid. He saw right through it. He walked over and untied Riven. Only Riven.

"You're up," Caspian said, tossing the leather whip at Riven's feet. "Give him twenty." He kept the number low. He needed them both alive and on their feet for what came next.

Riven picked up the whip. His hands shook under the weight. "What if I say no?"

Caspian's eyes went pitch black. "I'll kill you and bury you right here. No one will ever find you." He glanced at Finn. "Same goes for him."

Riven swallowed hard, looking at Finn. There was no way out of this hell.

He raised his arm and swung. *Crack!* The leather bit into Finn's back.

Again and again. Twenty times. Riven kept his eyes down, refusing to look at his friend. Finn groaned, the leather tearing his flesh, but he swallowed his screams. Pride was all he had left.

By the end, Riven was panting, drenched in sweat, his chest heaving.

Caspian stepped in, snatched the whip, and tied Riven back to the tree. Then he freed Finn and handed him the leather.

Finn gripped the handle, his knuckles turning white. Rage burned hot in his chest. *Next time I see Elara, she's dead. I'll make sure of it.*

But his body was wrecked. The broken glass from earlier and the fresh lashes had him burning. His first few swings were weak, barely grazing Riven.

Caspian stepped closer, eyes narrowing. "If you can't swing it right, I'll let him add his lashes to yours."

That did it. Finn dug deep, channeling his remaining strength, and lashed out. Riven took the hits. He didn't make a sound, but his eyes were bloodshot and his back was a bloody mess.

The twentieth strike landed. Silence fell over the woods again.

"Good," Caspian said, looking at the two bloody, broken Alphas. "Now, to walk out of here tonight, I need a part of your body."

Both men froze.
