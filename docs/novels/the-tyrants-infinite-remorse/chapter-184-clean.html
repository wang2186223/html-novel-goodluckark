Chapter 186

Caspian stood there. Alive. Bruised. Breathing.

Elara’s knees turned to jelly. Holy shit. Just a second ago, she’d watched that building burn, totally convinced he was dead. The terror in her chest flipped. It flared into pure, hot rage.

She marched right up and clocked him in the jaw. Hard.

"You suck at teamwork," she spat.

Then she bolted. If she stayed, she’d sob, and she wasn't doing that in front of him.

Lysander stayed behind, staring at Caspian, who just stood there like a dummy.

"You could’ve given us a heads-up, man," Lysander sighed, shaking his head. "She literally tried to dive into the fire when the place blew."

Caspian froze. His face dropped. The shock faded, replaced by something real. *She did that? For him?* He’d had a whole defense speech ready, but now? His chest tightened.

"So, that’s her?" Graham watched her walk down the packed hospital hallway. "Maybe I should recruit her. FBI could use someone like that."

Lysander let out a dry, tired chuckle. "Yeah, no. Don't bother. She’s a doctor. She lives for this stuff."

Graham blinked. "A doctor? Seriously? How’d she get mixed up with Dominic then?"

The question hung there. Graham looked at Caspian, but Caspian wasn't listening. His eyes were glued to Elara.

"I'm gonna go check on her," Lysander said. "She hasn't slept a wink." He walked off, leaving them.

He found her buried in work.

She was running around the ER like the night hadn't almost killed her. Checking charts. Bandaging wounds. Barking orders at nurses.

"Sera, seriously, take a break," Lysander said, stepping in.

Julian was right there, arms crossed. "Yeah, go home. I’ve got this covered."

Elara didn't even look up. "No."

She couldn't stop. What if a test got messed up? What if they missed a kid? What if her daughter was here and she walked right past her?

She’d spent hours checking the kids. Looking for anyone her daughter's age. Nothing. No resemblance. No mark. She knew the mark wasn't real science, but damn, she’d hoped.

She glared at Julian. "Isn't this your day off? Why are you even here?"

Julian shut up. He couldn't argue with that.

Around them, FBI suits were everywhere. Grabbing statements, taking names, trying to piece together the fortress raid.

A couple of agents tried to corner Elara. "No comment," she snapped, brushing past. She wasn't talking to them.

Not that it mattered. The news was already blowing up. Every TV screen showed the raid, the blast, Dominic in cuffs. His empire was dead.

"Sera, come here," Julian called out a bit later.

She followed him to a curtained bed and stopped cold.

Dominic.

He was beat up, barely moving, but they still had him cuffed to the bed frame. Wrist and ankle.

Elara stared. A cold smirk touched her lips. He looked so small. Powerless. Caspian had absolutely wrecked him. The big bad monster, locked up and bleeding.

"Are you going to patch him up?" Julian asked.

Elara shook her head. No way. For the first time in her career, she was walking away from a patient. She couldn't do it.

A massive yawn caught her. God, she was running on empty. "I'm done," she muttered, turning her back on him.

She was charting another patient when a voice popped up.

"Hey. Coffee?"

Elara stiffened. She turned. Caspian stood there, holding a cup, looking like death.

"Is this poisoned?" she asked, though the anger was gone.

Caspian just pointed at the cup. Written on the cardboard: *Sorry.*

Elara’s mouth twitched into a smile. "Thanks for not dying," she murmured, taking the cup.

Caspian nodded and walked away.

Hours later, Julian walked up, holding a thick folder. He looked dead serious.

"DNA results are in," he said. "We can start matching kids with their families."

Elara’s heart slammed against her ribs. "Let me see," she whispered.
