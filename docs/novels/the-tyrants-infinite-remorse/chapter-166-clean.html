Chapter 168

Elara slid into the shotgun seat. The heavy weight in her chest? Gone, finally. She felt lighter. The second she dropped the news about Orion joining the team, Lysander lost his cool. No filter.

"Okay, seriously, do you have some crazy voodoo magic that makes guys just drop to their knees?" Lysander shot her a smirk, but his eyes said he wasn't totally kidding. "Or am I missing something here?"

It made zero sense to him. Orion was a hardass. He didn't bend. Yet Elara got him to dump his whole life and hit reset, all because she saved his old man.

Elara sank into the leather, wearing a sly grin. "Hey, isn't that why you're riding shotgun with me? Or did my 'voodoo' work on you too?"

Lysander snorted, shaking his head. Just like that, the vibe in the car went back to normal. Easy. Comfortable.

Once the chatter died down, Elara grabbed her laptop. She pulled up Caspian's intel on the bombs inside Dominic's fortress. Locked in. Whenever Lysander drove, she either crashed or worked. Right now, her brain was running on high gear.

She lost track of time. The screen blurred until the car suddenly rolled to a stop, snapping her back to reality.

"Heads up. We got company," Lysander muttered. Elara's head snapped up.

Arthur and Audrey. Briar's folks.

Elara slammed the laptop shut. She shoved it at Lysander, popped the door, and stepped out. Cool, calm, but totally on guard.

"How'd you get my address?" she asked. Her voice was flat, but she didn't bother hiding the sharp edge in it.

She used to love them. Audrey had been like a real mother to her. But after the hell Briar and Thane put her through, everything was messed up. Her feelings were a tangled, ugly knot.

"It was a nightmare," Audrey sighed, looking exhausted but relieved. "We had to go to the Chief Commander and literally beg him for this address."

She took a step closer, eyes swimming with regret. "Sera, oh my god, I had no clue what Briar was up to. Not until Commander Thane called Arthur and spilled everything."

Elara watched them. Slowly, her shoulders dropped. They hadn't changed. Same warm faces, same honest eyes. They were the only people who had ever made her feel like she actually had a home.

"It's not on you guys," Elara said, her voice losing some of its bite. "Briar really messed me up, and that's why I..."

"You cut off the meds and the vitamins," Audrey cut in softly, tears welling up. "We get it, sweetie. Honestly, anyone would've done the same damn thing."

She exhaled shakily. "But you still sent us cash. Even after all that. We didn't even know until Eleanor told us. It... it just broke our hearts."

Audrey choked up, tears spilling over. "Sera, I can't believe I raised a monster like that. I am so, so sorry for what she did to you. Please... can you ever forgive me?"

Arthur stood tall, his voice rock solid. "I already told Thane we're done with Briar. We cut her off. We can't back someone who does that kind of crap."

Elara frowned, her mind spinning. Her own mother was trash, and Audrey had basically raised her. But Briar was their flesh and blood, and Briar was the one who ruined her life.

"Don't do that for my sake," Elara said, looking from one to the other. "She's your kid, no matter how bad she screwed up."

Arthur didn't blink. "So are you, kiddo. We don't forget what you did for her. Ever."

His words hit hard, dragging up all the old memories she'd tried so damn hard to bury.

"She had that rare blood type and was sick as hell, and you were the one giving her blood to keep her breathing. You stuck by her. You gave her everything you had. And then she turns around and steals your house? No way. We can't let that slide."

For a second, Elara flashed back. All those sacrifices she'd locked away came rushing back. If her parents hadn't brought it up, she probably would've kept pretending none of it ever happened.

She'd actually wondered if they were in on it. If they were cheering Briar on from the sidelines. But looking at them now? No way. They had no idea.

"Sorry I went ghost on you guys," Elara said, her voice tired but softer. "Things have been crazy. Look, let's not freeze out here. Come inside. Let's talk."

But Audrey shook her head, stopping her in her tracks. What she said next hit Elara right in the gut, catching her completely off guard.
