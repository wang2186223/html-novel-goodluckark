Chapter 291

Caspian yanked the phone from his ear.

"A spell?" He spat the word out, testing the flavor of it. Nope. Still sounded like pure crazy. The silence in the room just made it sound stupider. "Are you freaking kidding me? How does that even work?"

He froze. Behind those dark eyes, his brain was working overtime, trying to force this weird-ass puzzle piece into a picture that already made no sense. Then, boom. Something clicked. He dropped his arm, letting the phone dangle, and marched right up to Elara.

He got right in her space. She had to crane her neck just to look him in the eye. He stared down at her, but the usual cold, calculating mask was totally gone. Instead, he looked raw. Guilt and straight-up confusion were plastered all over his face, totally bare for once.

"Sera," he muttered, his voice gravelly. "Do you trust me, like, at all?"

The "Yes" popped out of her mouth before she could even think about it. It shocked her how steady she sounded.

A tiny twitch crossed his face. He nodded once, absorbing it. "You want these memories back?"

"Yeah," she blurted. Way too fast. Way too desperate. But damn, that blank space in her brain was suddenly driving her absolutely nuts.

His mouth twitched into a half-smirk. It was actually... soft. Weird. "Then close your eyes, okay?"

A million questions hit her brain at once. She choked them down, shut her eyelids, and waited. The air in the room suddenly felt super thick.

She heard the rustle of his clothes first. That heavy, boss-man energy of his shifted, wrapping around her. Then, boom—his hands gripped her waist and yanked her flush against him. No hesitation. Her body, the absolute traitor, melted right into him before her brain could protest.

"Sera," he breathed, his chest rumbling against hers. "Focus. Try to remember. It’d be easier back with the pack, but let's see what we can do here."

She parted her lips to ask what the pack had to do with anything, but the words died in her throat. He slammed his mouth onto hers. That tiny gap was all the invitation he needed. He took his sweet time, deepening the kiss like he had all day. For three glorious seconds, her brain completely short-circuited.

Then, the floodgates opened.

Weird, glitchy pictures flashed behind her eyelids. They were blurry, washed-out, like polaroids left out in the sun. She heard her own laugh. Saw giant pine trees waving. Heard his voice, but younger, happier. Felt her tiny hand swallowed up by a massive palm.

The clips sped up, hitting her like rapid-fire punches. A massive pressure built behind her eyes, swelling until it flat-out hurt. It felt like someone was using a crowbar on her brain, forcing open a rusty lock.

She shoved her hands against his chest, pushing him off.

"Stop, stop!"

Caspian recoiled instantly. No questions asked. He just backed off. When she blinked her eyes open, the look on his face made her stomach drop. He looked totally gutted. Like she’d physically slapped him.

"Ow, it hurts," she groaned, digging her knuckles into her temples.

"Wait, my kiss hurts you?" He looked totally lost.

She shook her head fast. "No, not you. The memories. It feels like someone is chucking bricks at the inside of my skull."

His expression hardened. The hurt was still there, but now he looked dead serious. He reached out, surprisingly gentle, and tucked a stray strand of hair behind her ear.

"Okay, then stop. Don't force it," he muttered, jaw tight. "We're not doing this here. Not like this." He dropped his hand. "Clear your schedule. I'm taking you to the pack for a few days. Our shaman can fix this."

"Can't the shaman just come to the city?"

Caspian grimaced. "No. The human world messes with the vibe. Whatever magic the shaman uses, it gets totally blocked here. Too much static."

She chewed on that for a second. Surprisingly, she didn't feel like arguing. No attitude, no stubbornness. She just wanted the truth. She wanted to know who the hell she was before her brain got wiped.

"Honestly?" She sighed, letting her shoulders drop. "Yeah. Let's do it. Thanks."

Caspian nodded, his eyes already glazing over as he started planning the logistics. Typical. The guy was always ten steps ahead.

Then, before she could stop her own stupid mouth, she blurted, "Hey. Can you kiss me again?"

Caspian froze solid. A massive blush crawled up her neck. Mortified, she stared hard at his collarbone. "I mean, I won't try to remember anything this time. I just..." She shut up. She didn't finish because, honestly, she had no clue what she was even trying to say.

Two agonizing seconds ticked by.

Then, his finger hooked under her chin. Super gentle. He nudged her face up until she had to look at him. She expected him to smirk or look smug, but nope. His eyes were incredibly warm, totally open, like he’d been holding back for ages.

He leaned down and kissed her again. This time, it wasn't gentle. It was hungry. Sure of itself. His hand slid up her ribs, burning hot even through her shirt. She finally stopped overthinking and just let herself melt.

His palm slid higher, cupping her breast.

She grabbed his wrist, stopping him right there. She pulled her mouth away, a sudden thought hitting her like cold water. Panting a little, she stared up at him.

"Hey," she croaked, clearing her throat to get her voice back. "You never told me about Aurelia's mom."
