Chapter 330

It clicked. Dumb, but it made sense. Esperanza grabbed his neck, giving his shoulder a heavy, dramatic pat. "Bout time, Dad. You're finally getting it."

Lysander snorted. Elara bit back a grin. Caspian just stood there, totally lost.

The kid just roasted him and praised him in one breath. And she looked dead serious, which made it way funnier.

He rubbed her back, shifting gears. "Hey, so, that test..."

"It's fine, Daddy," she cut in, her voice so steady it made everyone look. "Whatever you and Mommy want. I trust you guys."

Caspian blinked. Total whiplash. Yesterday she hated the idea of human school, and now she was just handing them the reins?

But she was glowing. Actually happy. And Elara was the reason. Caspian caught Elara's eye, and yeah, she saw it too.

Caspian cleared his throat, getting back to the heavy stuff. "Okay, tell me. After Lysander handed you off to that lady... how'd you end up with Delilah?"

He'd believe anything now. The kid knew stuff she shouldn't. Esperanza scrunched her nose. "I didn't go to Delilah first."

Heads snapped up. "Wait, really?"

She shrugged. "Nope. She took me to her place first. Her realm." The room went dead silent. "I lived there for a whole year."

Elara's chest tightened. A year? No way.

"What was it like?" Lysander whispered.

Esperanza tilted her head. "Nice. Quiet. She looked after me." She smiled. "I drank her milk."

That froze them. Then she dropped another one. "She had a picture of Mommy. Every time she showed it to me, she'd say the same thing."

Elara leaned forward, hanging on every word. "What'd she say?"

Esperanza's blue eyes locked onto hers. "She said, 'Your mommy doesn't know you're gone. If she knew, she'd come get you.'"

Boom. Elara sat there, numb. Her brain was spinning. A realm? Not here, not the pack lands. Where the hell was she?

The Eternal Spirit. Had to be. The question burned in her throat. She opened her mouth to ask.

But Esperanza kept going, and what came out next blew their minds. The kid started rattling off details—insane, hyper-specific stuff no toddler should remember. The room grew colder with every word.

She talked like she was describing a trip to the playground.

The adults sat frozen, hanging on. Esperanza swung her legs under her chair. "Then, like, a war started over there."

Just like that. Casual. Lysander coughed, nearly spraying his coffee. "A war?"

"Yeah." She pouted, remembering. "The lady got scared. She said if I stayed, I'd never see Mommy again."

Silence again. Caspian threw Elara a look. Neither dared to speak. Don't interrupt the flow.

Esperanza's lip quivered. "She said I was weird. Like, they couldn't wipe my brain like the others."

Goosebumps. Elara's pulse spiked. "Wipe your brain?"

Esperanza shrugged. "Yeah. People who go there have to forget everything before they leave. That's the rule."

She paused, thinking. "She said if she knew I'd remember, she never would've sent me."

Nobody breathed. It sounded insane, but every crazy piece fit perfectly.

Caspian felt a heavy knot of guilt twist in his gut.

Why hadn't he listened? Why did he ignore her?

He'd missed so many signs. He could've solved this months ago. He could've had Elara back sooner.

It sucked to think about, but dwelling on it was useless. Focus on the now. "Then what?" he asked softly.

Esperanza looked up. "She dropped me in the human world. Said as long as Mommy kept hoping, we'd find each other. Then she left me at the cafe with Delilah and vanished."

Elara's jaw dropped. Esperanza nodded. "She said Delilah would help me find my new dad. And he'd bring me to Mommy."

The words hit like a truck. For Elara, it was destiny. For Caspian, a second chance. For Lysander, proof that some cosmic force was pulling the strings.

A thick, heavy silence settled over them as they tried to piece the puzzle together.

Minutes ticked by. Nobody spoke. Then, a voice cracked the quiet. "She told me something weird once."
