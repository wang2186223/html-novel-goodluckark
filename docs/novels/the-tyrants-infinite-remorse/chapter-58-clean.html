Chapter 58

The question hit Elara like a slap. Total dead air on the line. Her brain went into overdrive, crunching numbers at warp speed.

She dragged air into her lungs, slow and steady. Rhys had a point. Caspian wasn't an idiot. He watched every penny, and weird cash flows toward Rhys would raise red flags immediately.

With Rhys gone, Caspian would wonder. Like, is the temp boss actually better than the actual CEO? He’d start digging into Locke Group’s stock. And since Thane was still in his pocket, they’d talk. The guy would put two and two together in a heartbeat.

Elara shut her eyes, sinking into her chair. Her mind was a chessboard, pieces sliding into place. One wrong move and she was done. She couldn't screw this up, not when she was this close to tearing down Briar’s house of cards.

"He won't find a damn thing if I don't leave breadcrumbs," she said, her voice dropping to a cool, sharp whisper. "We just have to play it smart."

Rhys kept his mouth shut. He could hear it in her voice—she was done panicking. Now, she was hunting.

"I'm not going to just pump your stock overnight," she said, her fingers already slamming keys on her laptop. "It's gotta be a slow burn. I'll fake some market hype. Competitor drama, industry shifts—stuff that has zero to do with you."

She stopped, her eyes narrowing. "Besides, Caspian is a creature of habit. He lives for spreadsheets and logic. If your stock climbs alongside the market recovery, he'll just think it's luck and decent management. He won't sniff around unless it looks fake."

Rhys let out a breath. He believed her, but his chest still felt tight.

"And if he actually starts poking around?" he asked, keeping his voice low. He needed to know her backup plan.

"Then he'll find exactly what I feed him," she said, a cold edge to her words. "I'll scrub the digital trail, reroute the money, or just leak some massive scandal to throw him off. If the math looks legit, he won't doubt it. Or, hell, I'll just point him toward the King brothers."

"They're my brokers," Rhys said.

Elara sighed. "Yeah, I stopped using them, but I'll make it work. I need my people at the top. Once I get the majority share, I'm taking Caspian down. No hesitation."

Lysander sat in the corner, eyebrows raised. Damn. Elara was ready for war.

Silence stretched between them. Rhys stared at the wall, realizing Elara wasn't some victim anymore. She was a force of nature. This wasn't just payback. It was pure, unadulterated control.

"You've got this all mapped out, huh?" he muttered. He was almost in awe. The broken woman the pack used to gossip about was gone.

He froze. Someone was walking his way. Briar. She was sashaying over, putting on her best innocent-girl face.

"Gotta go," Rhys whispered fast. "Briar's coming."

He killed the call and shoved his phone away just as she stepped up.

"Who was that?" she asked. Her voice was sweet, but her eyes were lasers, scanning his face for a tell.

It pissed him off, but Elara’s advice flashed in his head: *Keep your cool. Play the game.*

"Just my acting COO," Rhys said, smooth as silk. "I mean, I did leave my whole company behind to move here, remember?"

Briar nodded, looking impressed. She’d tagged along to enough of Thane’s business meetings to know what real power looked like, and Rhys practically oozed it.

"Honestly, it's crazy you walked away from all that for the pack," she said, sounding almost wistful.

"I didn't walk away," he said, letting a small smirk slip. "I just hire people who actually know what they're doing." He tilted his head. "So, what's up? Why were you looking for me?"

"It's Asher," she said, squeezing her brows together like she actually cared. "Where is he? Weren't you guys together earlier?"

"Up in his room," Rhys said. He took a step closer, dropping his voice to a cozy whisper. "I'll walk with you. You look kind of down."

She stiffened. He caught the tiny flinch.

"Hey, don't sweat it," he murmured. "Whoever leaked that video is just jealous of you."

Boom. Briar's shoulders dropped. Her guard crumbled instantly under the praise.

"You... you really think so?" she whispered, looking up at him.

"I know so," Rhys said, flashing her a slow, killer smile. "Trust me, nobody touches you. Not even Elara. Whoever did that is green with envy."

Briar's eyes flared with a hungry, desperate look. Not for love, but for power. She practically swelled up, breathing heavy as she drank in the compliment.

Right on cue, his phone buzzed. He pulled it out and answered right in front of her.

"Yeah? No, hold the trade," he barked into the receiver. "I'm sending a proxy. Just park about fifty billion in it for now."

He hung up and slid the phone back into his pocket like he hadn't just thrown around GDP-level cash.

"Fifty billion?" Briar's jaw hit the floor. "Holy crap. You're *that* rich?"
