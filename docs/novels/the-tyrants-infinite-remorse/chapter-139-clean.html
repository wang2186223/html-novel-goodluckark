Chapter 140

Julian blew out a heavy breath, dumping a weight he’d clearly carried too long. "Look, there are whispers," he muttered, dropping his voice to a tense crawl. "And I buy them. Why? Because of the patients I patch up. They roll in shattered, shaking, mouths locked shut. They won't rat him out. Dominic's got eyes everywhere. He tracks their moms, their buddies, anyone they love."

He stopped, shivering like the words tasted like poison. "Sera... word is, he’s got a dungeon under his place. A legit one. Locks up kids, women, guys. But it gets worse. They’re harvesting organs. And other sick crap. Stuff that makes my stomach turn just thinking about it."

The news hit Elara like a physical punch, knocking the wind right out of her. Her ribs squeezed tight, ready to snap under the sudden pressure. Instantly, her mind locked onto her deepest, darkest nightmare.

Her little girl.

What if the kidnapper hadn't just tucked her away somewhere safe? What if they sold her? What if her baby was trapped with a monster like Dominic right now?

The thought choked her, wrapping around her throat and squeezing hard. But then, something clicked. The panic burned away, replaced by a cold, hard wall of pure focus. She hadn't felt this sharp in ages.

"Thanks for the heads-up," she said. Her voice sounded dead calm, totally hiding the hurricane in her chest. "Catch you tomorrow."

Both guys caught the shift instantly. It was subtle, but it was there. Her eyes went icy, locking onto something invisible. She’d made up her mind, and there was no turning back.

Julian figured she was just wiped out, totally done for the day. Lysander knew better. He kept his mouth shut until they climbed into the car and slammed the doors. "Alright, what's the play?"

Elara sank into the headrest, organizing her thoughts into a solid plan. "We're killing two birds with one stone."

Lysander frowned, glancing over with a puzzled look. "Spell it out for me."

She looked at him, her face eerily blank. "Remember that software I built for Orion?" She watched his eyes light up as he connected the dots.

He nodded. "Yeah, what about it?"

"I'm using it on Dominic," she said, her voice dropping an octave. "I've got his personal cell number. That's our backdoor."

Lysander gripped the wheel so hard his knuckles turned white. "Sera, hold on. Are you sure about this?" His voice was low, super cautious. "There are no wolves mixed up in his sick games. And what if we bust this place open and your kid isn't even there?"

The temperature in the car plummeted instantly. It felt like her sheer anger was freezing the air.

Lysander winced, wishing he could eat his words. He squeezed the wheel tighter, scrambling to backtrack. "Look, sorry. I'm just looking at the angles. Dominic is a psycho. The smart move is staying the hell away from him."

Elara glared at him, her eyes drilling holes into his skull. It made his stomach twist. "So we just let him slide?" she whispered, her words sharp as glass. "He gets to torture people until he grabs someone you actually care about?"

That hit him right in the gut. He couldn't shake the words.

"And even if my girl isn't in that basement," she murmured, her voice softer but still rock-solid, "at least I'll know I tried. For her. Wherever she is."

Silence stretched between them again, but the confusion was gone. He got it now.

Lysander let out a long sigh and nodded. "Fine," he said. "I'm in, whatever it takes. But Sera, if we're doing this, we need Caspian." He took a quick peek at her, then focused back on the road. "The guy's got the cash, the muscle, and the reach. He's the only one who can go toe-to-toe with a monster like Dominic."

Elara slumped back, staring out the window. Caspian.

A tiny, wicked smirk touched her lips. "Then I'll drag him into it."

Lysander didn't doubt her for a second. If anyone could force Caspian's hand, it was Elara. He chuckled under his breath. Caspian was a beast. Sure, Thane was tough, but according to the pack rumors, Caspian was on a whole different level of terrifying.

Back at the house, Elara bolted upstairs. She stripped and jumped into a hot shower, letting the steam melt the knots in her shoulders. Then she sat at her desk and dived straight into the code.

Hours flew by. She didn't even look at the clock. She built, tweaked, and polished the code, her fingers flying across the keys. Her brain was ten steps ahead, patching bugs and finding backdoors before they even popped up.

When she finally hit save, her body gave out. Her eyes went blurry, heavy as lead. She didn't even try to fight the crash. She just face-planted onto the bed and blacked out.

Three hours later, she blinked her eyes open, feeling like she'd been dragged out of a swamp. She stumbled downstairs, expecting the usual quiet morning.

Instead, she stopped dead.

Lysander was standing there, looking like hell. He was sweaty, his shirt clinging to his chest, and the whole place smelled like a damn greenhouse. It wasn't just a faint whiff. The floral scent was thick enough to choke on, filling every single corner of the room.

She looked around, her brow furrowing as she took in the scene. "Uh, what's with the jungle?" she asked, her voice laced with suspicion.

Lysander squeezed out a fake smile. "Dominic," he said, rubbing the back of his neck. "He sent all of this. And there's more on the way. We literally don't have room for them."

Elara raised an eyebrow, but she wasn't laughing. Her chest tightened again. Dominic didn't do nice things. This wasn't a gift; it was a play. She stared at the massive piles of flowers, her eyes narrowing.

"So," she muttered. "What's the bait?"
