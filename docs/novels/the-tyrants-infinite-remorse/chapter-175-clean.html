Chapter 177

"Hey, sorry. Just looking for the bathroom. Got totally lost," Elara said. She kept her voice dead level. Inside, her heart hammered against her ribs. Those heavy footsteps behind her were way too close.

The guy was a wall. He stepped down, blocking the narrow hallway with massive shoulders. The air turned to lead. He stared her down, scanning her from her boots to her hair, weighing her story.

"Restroom's upstairs," he grunted. He jerked a thumb behind her, bored.

Then he saw it. The cracked door. His body went rigid. His hand flew to his radio. One call and she was dead. Elara didn't wait.

She threw her weight forward. Her heavy clutch swung in a brutal arc, slamming right into his temple. A perfect, hard strike.

Bullseye. The guard folded like a cheap tent, hitting the concrete floor cold. He never knew what hit him.

Elara stood over him, chest heaving. She squeezed her purse, took a ragged breath, and let it out.

Perks of a medical degree—she knew the sweet spots. One hit. Done.

Behind her, the woman stared, jaw dropped. Fear turned to pure shock. Then reality snapped her back to life.

"Hey, she's on our side," the woman hissed, turning to the others. She started shaking them, fast. "Wake up. Get up."

That did it. The room woke up. Eyes fluttered open. You could feel the sudden spark of hope in the dark.

Elara kept her eyes on the hall while they shuffled toward the open door. They were weak, stumbling, but moving on pure adrenaline.

The woman grabbed Elara’s arm on her way out. Her grip was weak but tight. "There's more," she whispered, shaking. "Kids. Downstairs."

Elara looked at the basement stairs. Pitch black. Her stomach did a flip.

Panic flared—what if she was too late? She killed the thought. No time for doubts. She bolted down the steps.

The air got freezing and thick. Shadows crept up the walls. By the time she hit the bottom, her lungs burned.

Kids. Dozens of them. Tiny bodies curled up, half-dead, staring at nothing. They looked way worse than the crowd upstairs.

A lump hit her throat. She swallowed it down. No time to cry. She found the back exit, pulled out her hairpin, and jammed it into the lock. Her hands didn't shake. *Click.* The door swung open.

Cool air rushed in. "Go," she whispered loudly. "Run. There are people outside who will save you."

Nobody moved. They just stared, blank and confused. Freedom didn't make sense to them.

Elara didn't wait. She grabbed the nearest kid. She started shoving them toward the door, gentle but firm. "Move! Go, go, go!" she ordered.

That broke the spell. They started moving, scrambling, pushing past each other in a desperate rush for the exit.

"Hey! What the hell?!" a voice roared. Elara spun. A guard raised his pistol. *BANG.*

A little girl collapsed. Blood bloomed across her shirt. It happened in a heartbeat.

Elara lost it. "You bastard!" she screamed. She reached down her thigh, ripping the hidden knife from her dress slit.

She lunged, closing the gap instantly, and buried the blade deep into his throat. Clean and brutal.

His eyes popped. He made a wet, choking sound and hit the floor, bleeding out.

Elara ignored him. She dropped next to the girl, hands pressing hard against the wound, checking her vitals.

"Hey, look at me. Stay with me, okay?" she muttered, praying the kid could hear her.

"How do we help?" a tiny voice asked.

Elara looked up. A pale boy stood there, terrified but holding his ground. Tears threatened to blind her, but she blinked them back. "Get out of here," she said, her voice strained. "I've got her."

"She's my sister," he said, shaking his head. "No way."

Elara swallowed the lump in her throat and nodded, keeping pressure on the wound. "Can you lift her?"

He blinked once, then squared his shoulders. "Yeah."

"Good. Take her. Run. There are medics outside. They'll save her."

He nodded, scooped his sister up, and bolted for the door.

Elara watched them vanish, took a second, then stood up. Next stop: the labs.

She sprinted down the hall, kicked open a door, and stepped into a sterile lab smelling of bleach and chemicals. Monitors beeped everywhere.

A guy in a white coat spun around, shocked. Before he could even blink, Elara closed in and punched him right in the jaw. His head snapped back.

"You're a disgusting piece of trash," she spat.

"I—I had no choice! I wanted to live!" he whined, holding his bleeding face.

Elara stood tall. "The feds are outside. This place is surrounded. Get everyone out right now, or you'll rot in a federal prison forever."

That broke him. He scrambled, screaming orders at his staff, pushing the remaining prisoners toward the exits.

Elara trailed behind them, alert. Suddenly, a hand grabbed her collar. She was slammed hard onto the concrete. Pain flared in her ribs, knocking the wind right out of her.

She rolled over and scrambled up, ignoring her aching muscles. Two massive guards blocked her path. "Fucking spy," one growled.

They lunged. Elara didn't flinch. She met them head-on, fast and lethal. She used their own weight against them, dodging, striking pressure points, and breaking their rhythm. One went down. Then the second.

Silence. Just her heavy breathing. Caspian’s training had paid off. It was the only reason she was still breathing.

"Everyone's out!" the lab doctor yelled, already running out the door.

Elara limped after him. Then, a cold click echoed behind her. A gun cocking.

"I wanted you as my donor," Dominic's voice cut through the dark. No charm. Just ice. Elara froze. "But you chose to stab me in the back."

The silence stretched, heavy and suffocating. "Now... you die."
