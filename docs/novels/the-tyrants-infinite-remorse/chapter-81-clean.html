Chapter 81

Thane stared. The woman in the midnight-blue dress was a total stranger. It hugged every single curve, turning heads without her even trying. The fabric caught the firelight, shimmering like crazy every time she moved. She looked unreal. Seven years of marriage, and she’d never worn anything like that for him. Not once. Not for anniversaries, not for pack stuff, not in bed. Back then, she was just plain. Invisible. Now? She was glowing. Like she’d saved all this hotness for someone else.

It pissed him off. Way more than it should have.

Years ago, after their last run-in, he’d sworn he was done with her. File closed. Deleted. He figured she’d stay out of his lane. But nope. She always crawled back into his world.

Elara actually wanted a quiet night. Just nod, play dumb, and walk away. She’d already spotted some familiar faces—big-shot Alphas who got the politics and didn't need things spelled out. But then there were the humans. Guys like Julian. They didn't get pack ranks, Alpha dominance, or the kind of baggage you can't explain over a glass of Merlot. She couldn't exactly tell Julian, "Hey, by the way, my ex is a psycho Chief Commander with major control issues." That would go down like a lead balloon.

And of course, Thane had to start drama.

Honestly, if she didn't need his bank account stable so Rhys could force that DNA test between him and Asher, she would’ve shorted his stocks out of pure spite. She had the power. She’d thought about it, too. But timing was everything. He needed to stay rich for now. She'd have to hit him from another angle.

Victor stood there stiff as a board, sweating bullets. He was terrified Elara would trash Julian’s business deals. He almost stepped in to drag her away, but then he remembered how she’d helped Ethan. He swallowed his words.

"Mr. Walker," Julian said, pulling Elara tight against his hip. "You got a problem with my girl?"

It wasn't just show. Victor could tell. Julian actually fell for her. Bold move for a guy with so much on the line. Stupid, maybe. But real.

"Your girl?" Thane let out a harsh, loud laugh.

The chatter around them died.

David Sterling, always down for drama that wasn't his, smirked. "Well, if we didn't all know about your wife, Briar, I'd think you were hitting on her yourself, Walker."

A wave of chuckles rippled through the crowd. Elara’s smile turned bitter. Seven years, and Thane never thought she was good enough to stand beside him. Nobody even knew who she was, yet his mistress was famous as his "wife." It was pathetic, but Elara felt a surge of pride for dumping that trash marriage.

"Hell no," Thane snapped, scowling. "She—"

"I’m his evil sister," Elara cut in, flashing a sweet smile.

The word *evil* hung heavy. She wasn't joking.

Julian blinked. "Sera... wait. You're related to Walker? Why didn't you say anything? I thought the last name was just a coincidence. You guys look nothing alike."

Good point, but Elara ignored it.

"She's not my sister," Thane growled.

Elara laughed, a sharp sound that cut through the room. The plus-ones and escorts stared. To them, any chick without a ring was just a date, and watching one talk back to the big boss was wild.

"Should I call Mom and Dad?" Elara teased, tilting her head. "Not my fault they disowned you and kept me."

Boom. People started whispering. You could see the realization hitting their faces. Thane felt his face burn. She was shredding his reputation, and he hated losing control in public.

He took a step toward her.

Julian instantly blocked him, shielding Elara. Deals were great, but if Thane got physical, Julian was ready to throw down. "Sorry about her," Julian forced a nervous chuckle. "I guess I spoil my girlfriend too much. You probably should've done the same. You know, being her brother."

"I am NOT her brother!" Thane roared.

Julian shut his mouth real quick.

Elara didn't even flinch. "I'm adopted," she said, totally chill. "So what? Mom and Dad love me. Even if you're a hater."

The crowd relaxed a bit. Adoption made sense. No resemblance, easy story.

Thane wasn't buying it. He flashed a nasty smile and stepped right into her personal space. Julian tried to get between them, but Thane shoved past him. He was bigger, stronger, radiating that scary Alpha energy. He leaned down, his voice a rough whisper.

"Didn't I tell you? No guy in my league would ever touch you. Best you'd get is some trash pack wolf. But this? A weak-ass human? You're gonna be stuck in this pathetic life forever, b—"

He didn't finish. The word *bitch* was barely out when Elara slammed her heel onto his foot. He gasped, and before he could move, her knee went straight into his groin. Hard. He doubled over, and her fist connected with his jaw. *Crack.* A solid punch. She shoved him back with everything she had.

The room went dead silent, then exploded into gasps.

David Sterling’s jaw dropped. "Did she just deck the chairman?"

Victor slapped a hand over his face, panicking. "We are so screwed," he muttered. Thane was stumbling, and the entire room was staring at the girl who had just leveled the most powerful man in the building.
