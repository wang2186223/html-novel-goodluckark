Chapter 196

Centenary Syndicate. An hour back.

"Chief, they’re getting worse," Rhys said, sounding panicked.

Thane didn't blink. He just glared, more annoyed than pissed. "Dude," Thane muttered, "I told you. Just call me Thane."

No attitude, just facts. Rhys had pumped cash into Walkers Global, basically saving the company, so they were tight now. But Thane had bigger shit to worry about. The air in the room felt heavy, suffocating. This nightmare felt way too familiar. His chest tightened.

"We’ve seen this movie before," Thane said, piecing the puzzle together. "But last time... Sera took the worst of it." His voice went quiet, protective. "Not doing that again."

He locked eyes with Rhys. "Where’s it coming from?"

Rhys shook his head, looking totally defeated. "No clue. It’s too fast. You breathe it, you drop. By the time we try to bag a sample..." He sighed. "Poof. Gone. It just vanishes into thin air. Nothing to test."

This was a total wildcard. Dangerous as hell.

Thane clenched his jaw. He looked over at Asher. The kid was totally clueless about the chaos, just chewing on some dry pancakes. Thane had made them, and yeah, he sucked at it. Ever since Elara dumped the kid on him, Thane was solely on the hook. No backup. He’d actually spent hours reading cookbooks, trying to learn how to flip a damn pancake for his kid. Asher clearly hated them, but he kept chewing anyway. Kid was loyal. That tiny gesture melted a piece of Thane’s frozen heart.

Thane snapped back to reality, his face hardening. "How many down?"

"Eight," Rhys said, his voice dropping. "Only one’s pulling through, mostly because he was far from the blast zone." He paused. "The others got blindsided. We don't even know ground zero."

Thane ran a hand through his hair, racking his brain. "Where’s Briar?"

"In her room," Rhys said. "Still throwing a fit because you took her phone and tech away."

Thane froze. A lightbulb went off. "Wait. Briar has a medical degree," he muttered.

He closed his eyes and hit the mindlink. *Briar. Get to the hospital. Chemical leak. They need hands.*

Nothing but dead air for a second. Then her voice cracked through, sounding weak and shaky. *Rav... I can't. I'm really sick. I can't work like this.* She hesitated, then whispered, *Can you please lift the lockdown? Or... just come see me?*

Thane’s face went dark. He was already pissed, and this just set him off. He was still seething about her texting other guys. He hadn't even told her about that Orion guy calling her phone—he was just waiting for the bastard to show up so he could break him. He snapped back through the link, harsh and cold. *I'm already babysitting Asher. Now I have to baby you too? Seriously, how many kids do I have?*

The words hung in the air, brutal, but he didn't care.

In her room, Briar froze. The words stung like a slap. Something inside her snapped, the warmth between them instantly turning to ice. *Rav... you think I'm a child?* she whispered back, her voice trembling.

No reply. He cut the link, leaving her in dead silence.

Thane shrugged it off, his jaw tight. Easier to just block her out. "Forget her. She's useless," he told Rhys. "I'll call Sera."

Rhys raised an eyebrow. "Sera? The Managing Director? You think she'll actually help?" He paused, choosing his words. "I heard... things. About how you guys treated her."

The elephant in the room was huge, but Thane didn't care. "She saved Asher," Thane said. "She'll do it."

Rhys bit his lip and nodded, though he wasn't buying it. "Sure. Worth a shot. She's a good soul, helping the kid." Rhys kept his voice level. He actually knew a lot more about Elara's secrets than he let on, but Thane didn't need to know that.

Thane pulled out his phone and dialed.

The call lasted maybe ten seconds. When he hung up, his face was pure thunder.

Rhys smirked a little, keeping his eyes sharp. "Let me guess. She hung up on you?"

Thane didn't answer. He stared at the screen, his mind spinning. "She can blow me off," he muttered, "but she won't blow off my parents."

He immediately dialed another number.

It rang twice, then went straight to voicemail. Thane cursed, his face darkening. He hit redial instantly. This time, it picked up.

"Thane," his dad's voice was cold as ice. "Why do you think I'm ignoring you?"

Thane winced, but he swallowed his pride. "Dad," he said, his voice tight. "Hate me all you want. But this isn't about me." He took a sharp breath. "The pack is in trouble."
