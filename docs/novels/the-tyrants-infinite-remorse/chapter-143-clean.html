Chapter 145

The room went dead silent. You could hear a pin drop. Outside, the wind rattled the glass, making the quiet inside feel even heavier.

Briar stood there, chest heaving. Her head spun. Then she caught Thane’s eye. It wasn't just fury burning in them. It was pure letdown. That hurt way worse.

"Briar," Thane growled, his voice dropping to a gravelly whisper. "What the hell is this? I trusted you with my kid. With Asher."

For once, she had zero comebacks. Her mouth popped open, but nothing came out. His stare nailed her to the floor, stripping away her usual smug attitude.

"Tell me Elara didn't do a better job raising him than you ever did," he snapped.

That did it. She snapped. "Rav, are you serious right now?" she cried, her voice cracking. "I was his damn nanny! I did everything for him!"

Thane’s face turned to stone. "Sure," he spat. "But who snuck into his room every single night? I saw Elara. She’d check his fever, adjust the AC, tuck him in, dose his meds. While everyone else was fast asleep."

Briar flinched. She had no clue. Even after they shoved Elara out and told her to back off, she still did the dirty work. No credit, no complaints. Just did it.

Thane couldn't shake the memory. Standing in the dark hallway, watching Elara whisper to the kid, smoothing his blankets.

All those prepped meals Briar grabbed from the fridge and claimed as her own? Elara cooked every single one.

That’s why he couldn't sign those damn papers. He’d given her a brutal choice: play second fiddle to Briar or get out. Elara just ripped the prenup to shreds and stayed to take the hits.

The truth hit Briar like a physical blow. "But... I tried," she whimpered, her voice cracking under the weight of it. "I really did."

She gulped, forcing her chin up. "Look, this isn't about that. It's the meds. Dr. Ray said Elara messed with my base formula. She hid the final steps."

The lie slid out of her mouth like butter. Rhys’s eyebrows shot up, but he kept his mouth shut. If lying paid the bills, the girl would be a billionaire.

She spun on the doctor, trapping him. "Right, Dr. Ray? That’s exactly what you told me."

Charles froze. He’d just snagged Valerie’s old job as chief of medicine. One slip-up and he was toast. He looked from Briar to Thane, running the numbers in his head.

He gave a tight nod. "Yeah. Director Sera is a genius. She knew the kid's chart inside out. She wiped her files and trashed the samples before she left. We're flying blind trying to copy her work."

Rhys stood in the corner, stomach churning. These weren't little white lies anymore. They were building a bomb.

Thane didn't say a word. He just turned on his heel and marched into the ICU. The rest of them trailed behind like lost puppies.

Asher’s pale face lit up the second Thane walked in. He struggled to sit up, a weak grin on his face. "Dad! You're finally here."

Thane’s tough exterior melted instantly. He rushed over, wrapping his arms around the boy like he was made of glass. "Missed you more, buddy," he mumbled.

Rhys watched them, a knot forming in his throat. Then he remembered Elara’s text about the DNA test. The puzzle pieces were starting to click.

"I'm gonna fix this, okay?" Thane promised, running a hand through the kid's messy hair. "I swear."

"Sir," the doctor interrupted, keeping his voice low but urgent. "That shot was just a band-aid. If we want him to survive this..." He took a breath. "We need Director Sera back."

Thane went rigid. Before he could speak, Asher piped up, his voice tiny but clear. "Yeah, Dad. I really miss Mom."

Briar’s face went paper-white. She lunged forward, grabbing the kid’s arm with a fake, plastered-on smile. "Asher, sweetie, what are you talking about? *I'm* your mommy."

Asher yanked his arm back, looking right through her. "Yeah, but you're always on your phone."

"Mom actually talked to me," the kid mumbled, his voice dipping. "She made those chocolate chip pancakes. She made me feel... like I mattered."

He looked up at Thane, his big eyes begging. "Dad... I want Mom. Can you please bring her home?"
