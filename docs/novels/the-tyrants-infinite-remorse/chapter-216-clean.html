Chapter 218

"Look," Elara said, her hands moving with fast, steady precision as she kept her eyes on her work. "I'll run some quick tests first to make sure it actually works. Then I'll dose the sick patients in the beds and leave some extra in the pharmacy."

She sounded like she was ordering takeout, not saving lives. "When that batch runs dry, you pay me to make more. For now? Fifty grand flat. As for where you put me up tonight, pay whatever you want. I don't care."

Thane let out a breath he didn't know he was holding. Damn. Fifty grand? After the crazy numbers she’d tossed around earlier, this was a total steal. Relief washed over him.

"That's it?" Thane stared at her, trying to figure out the catch. "You're charging that little?"

Elara kept her eyes on her tools. "I'm a doctor and a chemist," she said, cool and confident. "Back in med school in the city, I did some time with big pharma too."

"I hate sitting around doing nothing. So, I learned cyber hacking just to keep my brain busy and stay ahead of the game."

Her voice softened, just a bit. "Some stuff I figured out on my own. Some I learned the hard way. Point is, my side hustles pay enough. I don't need to squeeze you for cash."

She paused, still staring at her work. "I don't rip people off for medicine. That's a rule of mine. But my brain? My actual skills? Yeah, those cost real money."

*Damn, I love the badass she-wolf you've turned into,* Blaze purred in her head, practically beaming with pride. It made Elara smile inside.

*They tried to break me by dumping me in this hellhole,* Elara thought back, her mind sharp. *Joke's on them. I learned stuff they couldn't figure out in a lifetime. I became their worst nightmare.*

*Well, that explains why you act like a forty-year-old boss when you're only twenty-eight,* Blaze teased.

Elara ignored the jab, but she couldn't help but smirk.

Across the room, Rhys just stared. He was totally blown away. The girl had backbone, and he couldn't help but respect that. For a second, he actually felt a pang of jealousy toward Lysander. What was it like to hang out with a girl this smart every day? To watch her work up close?

"Thanks," Thane said, clearing his throat. He still sounded a bit unsure. "But yeah, we definitely can't afford your training fees for our medics."

"Figured as much," Elara said instantly. She didn't even blink, just kept working. Her total lack of care rubbed him the wrong way.

Thane scowled, getting annoyed. "Hey. Would it kill you to actually look at me when we're talking?"

She stopped.

Slowly, she spun her head around, locked eyes with him for a split second, and let out a massive, fake yawn like he was boring her to death.

"There, I looked," she said, her voice deadpan. "But honestly? You're not interesting enough to keep my attention."

The room went dead quiet. Then Rhys snorted. He tried to choke it back, but a loud laugh escaped anyway. "Whoops, sorry," Rhys muttered, though he was grinning ear to ear. "Man, I didn't know she had claws."

Thane swallowed his comeback. He turned on his heel and stormed out before he made a bigger fool of himself. His face was tense as he walked over to Felix, who was practically vibrating with anticipation.

One look at Thane's face told Felix everything.

"No go on the training," Thane said flatly. "She's way out of our budget."

Felix didn't budge. "Chief, take it out of my pay," he pleaded, his eyes wide with desperation. "Seriously, I don't care about the money. I need to learn this."

Thane stared at him. He felt a pang of respect for the guy's drive. Felix really wanted this. But facts were facts.

"She wants ten billion," Thane said.

That shut him up. Felix's jaw dropped. Any argument he had died right there. "Oh... okay, Chief," he stammered, deflating. "I guess... I'll just try to copy whatever she makes."

Thane's eyes narrowed as a memory clicked. "Wait. You were there when Briar tried to copy Asher's serum, right?"

Felix nodded fast. Thank god Briar had taken the fall back then. Even if she was a snake, she'd kept him out of the firing line.

"And you saw what a disaster that was," Thane said, his voice dropping an octave.

Felix squirmed, his shoulders tensing up as he looked at the floor. "We... we missed a few ingredients," he mumbled. "We couldn't source everything."

"Exactly," Thane snapped, cutting him off. "So don't even think about doing it again." He glared at Felix, letting the silence stretch. "You almost killed my kid."

That was that. Felix had nothing left to say. He slunk away, heading back to his office with a heavy pit in his stomach, all his excitement completely gone.

But the dread quickly turned into pure panic. The second he locked his office door, he tapped into the mindlink, his mental voice shaking.

*Co-Director... I think Director Sera is back. And she's out for blood.*
