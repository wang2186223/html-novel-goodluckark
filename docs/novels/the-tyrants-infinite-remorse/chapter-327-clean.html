Chapter 331

All eyes snapped to the door. Linnea stood there, dead quiet. She’d been lurking, listening to the whole damn thing.

She took a slow step in, locking eyes with Esperanza. "She told me once. Said her old man was bad news. Said she had to run like hell from him."

Caspian’s brow furrowed. Linnea gave a tight nod. "Thane was visiting the pack right around then. Honestly? Back then, I had no freaking clue what she was talking about."

Suddenly, the puzzle pieces clicked. For everyone.

It used to sting. Bad. But now? The air cleared. She finally got it.

The girl next to Caspian wasn’t just some random chick. She was *the* girl. The legend his folks always brought up. The one who wrecked him, then somehow put him back together.

For months, Linnea had been running from the truth. Now, she just swallowed it. Time to pack up and move on. It hurt, sure, but it wasn't a killer blow. Probably because she could see how crazy Caspian was about Elara, and how Elara looked right back at him.

She shot Elara a quick, soft smile. She’d braced herself for some stuck-up attitude, but Elara had been nothing but sweet.

Any other girl would’ve kicked her to the curb by now. Elara didn't. That spoke volumes.

Over at the table, Elara was still staring at Esperanza. That one line was stuck in her head. The "dangerous old man" part. She couldn't let it go. "Hey, Espe."

The kid looked up. "Tell me about this old daddy of yours."

Boom. Esperanza’s goofy grin vanished. Her face went totally blank, way too serious for a little kid.

The adults exchanged a look. This wasn't just kid talk. Esperanza clasped her tiny hands. "The lady told me stuff."

"Like what?" Caspian kept his voice low, careful.

Esperanza glanced at him, then Elara. "She said old daddy has, like, magic powers."

The room went ice-cold. Caspian’s eyes went razor-thin. "Powers?"

Esperanza nodded. "But he doesn't know it. If he’d been nice to Mommy, he’d be the biggest, baddest boss ever."

That hit like a freight train. Elara went stiff. Lysander sat up, rigid. Even Linnea’s jaw dropped.

Esperanza kept going. "The lady said he got weak because of the girl he picked."

Nobody needed a map. She meant Briar.

The kid pointed a tiny finger at the ceiling. "But if he dumps her, his mojo comes back. Like, way stronger."

Silence, heavy and thick. Caspian’s brain was racing. Years ago, Thane was a beast. Way stronger than Caspian.

Everyone thought he’d be the ultimate Alpha. Then he just... stopped growing. Hit a wall. Nobody knew why. Now, thanks to Esperanza, it made sense. Briar was his kryptonite.

Elara sat there, mind blown. It lined up perfectly with what Caspian had told her. Thane used to be a monster. If Briar was draining him, that was his blind spot. And blind spots could be exploited.

She wasn't just plotting revenge, but knowledge was leverage. Every piece of the puzzle brought them closer to burying Thane and Briar for good.

Then Esperanza flashed a bright, goofy grin. "But don't sweat it."

Everyone stared. The kid pointed at Caspian, then Elara. "You and Mommy are way stronger than him."

The sheer confidence in her voice made them blink. She didn't have a single doubt.

For a second, nobody breathed. Elara sat there, head spinning with a million questions. But one question drowned out the noise. Something she’d been dying to ask.

She seized the moment. "Espe."

The kid looked over. Elara’s chest hammered. "Do you have a birthmark?"

Esperanza shook her head. "Nope."

She scrunched her face, about to say more, but Linnea cut in. "Actually, she does," Linnea said softly.

Every head snapped toward her. Linnea walked over, dropping to a crouch next to the kid's chair. "I’ve seen it. When I was washing and drying her hair."

Esperanza blinked. Linnea reached out, gently pulling the girl’s thick hair away from her neck.

The second the skin showed, the room went dead silent.

Right there, below the hairline, was a perfect crescent moon.

Everyone froze. Elara stopped breathing. Caspian stared, eyes wide.

Lysander slowly sat up. It was identical to Elara’s. The exact same shape as the pendant and bracelet Caspian had given her back in the day.

For a long, heavy beat, nobody said a word. It was too much.

Elara’s hand flew to her own neck, her eyes glued to the kid's mark. Her heart was pounding like a bass drum in her ears.

Caspian suddenly bolted upright, the truth hitting him like a slap in the face. His eyes stretched wide. "I saw it," he muttered, like he was talking to a ghost. "The day Delilah brought her to me. I gave her a bath and dried her hair myself." He looked at Esperanza, a tiny, stunned smile tugging at his lips. "I remember the mark."

Then his face twisted in frustration. "But then I hired a nanny. Stopped doing the baths. And I just... forgot."

He felt like a total idiot. How did he miss that? The clues were staring him right in the face.

Across the table, Elara couldn't look away from Esperanza. Every single doubt she had just crumbled to dust.

The stories, the vibes, the face, and now this mark. It all pointed to one thing.

Suddenly, she didn't even care about the DNA test. This was proof enough. Something deeper than science or paperwork.

Lysander got it too. A massive grin broke across his face. "Well, guess we can skip the DNA test," he said, reading her mind.

His eyes practically glowed with relief. After days of sweating it out, they finally had their answer.

But Esperanza shook her head. "No. You still gotta do it."

Lysander blinked. "Wait, why?"
