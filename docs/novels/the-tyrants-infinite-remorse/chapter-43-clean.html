Chapter 43

The drinks arrived. Lysander was already glued to his phone, totally checked out behind his usual "don't touch me" vibe. Elara took the chance to actually size up the family sitting across from them.

The woman sat up straight, smoothed out her shirt, and flashed a polite, practiced smile. "Hey, I'm Aurelia," she said.

Elara instantly noticed the change. She looked way brighter today, lighter, like some massive weight had just slid off her shoulders. It wasn't just because she wasn't wearing sunglasses; her kid was actually doing better. Funny how a little hope can completely reboot a person's face and vibe.

"This is my boy, Milo," Clara said, pulling the kid close. She ran her fingers through his hair—pure mama bear instinct, even now that the danger was over. "My husband's on his way, by the way."

Elara leaned in with a warm smile, trying not to seem cold or unapproachable. "I'm Seraphine," she said easily, then pointed to the guy next to her. "And this is Logan Sterling."

She missed Clara's reaction at first. The woman froze. Her eyes did this weird, slow blink, and her smile cracked for a split second before she forced it back together.

It only clicked for Elara when Clara repeated the name. "Wait. Elara... Walker?" The tone shift was subtle, but it was definitely there.

Elara tilted her head, a hint of suspicion creeping in. "Uh, you know me from somewhere?" she asked, keeping it casual.

Clara squeezed out another smile, way tighter this time. "The Sovereign Circle," Clara said after a beat. "My husband's in it. He mentioned your name after he got a call from Orion."

A heavy sigh slipped out before Elara could stop it. Just like that, her appetite vanished. She slumped back, feeling that familiar, annoying knot tighten in her chest. God, she hated this—the politics, the elite circles, the power-tripping names. She was already plotting an excuse to bail when footsteps headed their way.

A tall, sharp-dressed, middle-aged guy walked up, oozing that "I'm the boss" energy. "Hey," he said, sticking his hand out. "I'm Augustus. My wife told me what you did for our kid. Just wanted to say thanks face-to-face."

Elara stood up halfway to shake his hand, keeping it genuinely polite. "No problem," she said softly. "I'm just glad he's doing better."

Victor nodded, pure relief on his face, before his eyes drifted over to Lysander. He sized him up, scanning him up and down. "Are you, uh... her boyfriend?"

Lysander let out a dry chuckle, scratching his neck awkwardly, but Elara beat him to the punch. "He's family. Close family."

The second she said it, something flickered and died in Lysander's eyes. It wasn't dramatic, but she would've caught it if she'd been looking. *Close family.* Ouch. So that's what they were to strangers. Basically, "he's like a brother." Nothing more.

"Got it," Victor said, totally missing the awkward vibe shift. "Look, my oldest son runs Saint Peter's Hospital. He wants to pitch you an offer."

Elara blinked, totally caught off guard. Saint Peter's was the biggest private clinic in the city. That was literally her dream gig for when she was ready to get back into medicine.

Victor leaned in, getting hyped. "When Ethan's pediatrician told him how you diagnosed him, he was losing his mind. My son doesn't impress easy, trust me. But he said your call was insanely sharp, thorough, and lightyears ahead of what anyone else saw."

Elara squirmed a bit in her seat. Victor looked at her, full of hope. "So, what do you say? You interested?"

Elara hesitated. Slow down, dude. He was throwing way too much at her, and her brain was still lagging. "Look," she said, picking her words carefully. "I love medicine, seriously. But right now, I'm trying to get some other businesses off the ground. It's just... not the right time."

Victor's face fell. Elara felt a little bad, so she softened the blow. "But hey, if things change down the road, I'll hit you up."

Victor just stared at her for a solid five seconds, trying to figure her out like some weird puzzle. "Okay, but if you won't take the job, how am I supposed to pay you back for saving my kid?"

Elara pursed her lips and shook her head. "You don't need to pay me for being a decent human."

Victor frowned. "Come on. There's gotta be something."

Lysander cleared his throat, jumping in before she could speak—a move he'd seriously regret later. "Why not get her into the Sovereign Circle?" he suggested. "She's trying to launch a business. She needs connections, maybe some billionaire backing."

Victor raised an eyebrow, looking amused. "A doctor wanting to swim with the sharks in business?"

Elara didn't flinch. "Yep. That's the plan."

Victor pursed his lips, leaning back and tapping his fingers on the table, chewing it over. "Well, I—"

He was about to speak when Clara cut in, her voice suddenly sharp as a knife. "She's Seraphine."

The words hit like shattering glass. Victor froze instantly. Whatever he was about to say died right in his throat. His face went from curious to completely shocked in a split second. His eyes went wide as he stared at Elara. "Wait... you're the one who got blacklisted?"

The air at the table instantly turned to ice.
