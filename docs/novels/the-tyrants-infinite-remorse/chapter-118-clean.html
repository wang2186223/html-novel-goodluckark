Chapter 119

Caspian’s phone buzzed, vibrating against his palm. A knot tightened in his gut. Had he messed up big time letting Elara set up camp in his office? Probably. But he brought this on himself. He invited her in. No running from it now. When Thane's name lit up the screen, Caspian knew he had to pick up.

He took a slow, deep breath, swiped the screen, and hit speaker. The line went live, filling the quiet room.

"Thane. What's the deal?"

Thane sounded wrecked. The usual cool-guy act? Gone. Just straight-up, shaky panic.

"Caspian, man, Finn and Riven's backers are walking," Thane blurted, words tripping over each other like he was watching his house burn down. "You gotta help me. Talk to them. Or, hell, throw some cash in yourself? Please, man. For old times' sake."

That "old times" guilt trip hung in the air. Caspian ignored it. His eyes drifted across the desk to Elara. She was staring right at him. Her blue eyes practically glowed, but they were ice-cold. The room felt like a freezer suddenly. Caspian’s throat went dry.

He cleared his throat. "I warned you, Thane," Caspian said, keeping his voice dead flat. Zero pity. "It's over. Actually, I'm calling my guys next to yank my own money out."

Lysander smirked from the corner. Classic. You can take the girl out of the boardroom, but Elara was still the boss. Leaving the pack hadn't softened her. It just made her sharper. Deadlier.

"Wait, what?" Thane gasped through the speaker, choking on his own breath. "Is Sera doing this? Is this her?"

Caspian leaned back, totally unfazed. "Who else, Thane? You poked the bear. She's smart, and I told you what would happen if you kept pushing. You promised I wouldn't lose a dime. Well, I'm not risking it. If I leave my cash in there, it's gone by tonight."

Finn and Riven were tanking fast. It was brutal. Thane's last bankruptcy was slow compared to this. This was three times faster—like a high-rise imploding in seconds, dust flying before the bricks even hit the dirt.

Caspian let the silence stretch. "Or..." he murmured.

Thane jumped on it like a lifeline. "Or what? Tell me!"

Caspian propped his elbow on the armrest, cutting a glance at Elara. "Or you shut down your own shop and pay them back yourself. After all, your ex got targeted because of your mess."

The room went dead silent. Then Thane snapped. "I didn't tell them to use wolfsbane on her! Okay? I don't want her dead. I just wanted to make her life hell."

Boom. The vibe in the room shifted instantly. Elara's eyes went pitch black. Her calm face cracked, and a shiver ran straight down Caspian's spine. He knew right then they'd crossed a line.

Caspian stared at the screen, wanting to hang up right then. He didn't say a word. He just set the phone down as a heavy, suffocating silence filled the room. A nasty thought hit him: What if Elara put Thane on her hit list next?

Luckily, Elara wasn't gunning for Thane. Not yet. Actually, she needed him relaxed. She needed him to crawl back to the pack so Rhys could use him for that DNA test. That was the only way to settle this crap once and for all.

While Caspian sat there stressing, Thane was on the other end, cooking up a new plan.

"Don't sweat it," Thane muttered, trying to sound like he had it under control. He slumped back in his chair, exhaling. He figured he had the perfect fix. "I know what she's after," he whispered to himself. "I'll just give her what she wants."

Caspian frowned, staring at the phone. "What does she want, Thane?" he demanded, hoping the idiot wasn't digging his own grave. But Thane's reply made one thing crystal clear: the guy was too stupid to save, and he'd already crossed the point of no return.
