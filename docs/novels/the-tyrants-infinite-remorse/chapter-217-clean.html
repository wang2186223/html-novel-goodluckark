Chapter 219

Briar bolted from the pack house. The air inside was pure suffocation. She hit the garden, desperate for a breeze to stop her skin from crawling. She took one clean breath. Then, *bam*. A mindlink slammed into her skull. She winced, hard.

Her face scrunched up. "What the hell are you talking about?" she shot back.

The answer came instantly, dripping with pure rage. "I asked Director Sera to show me how to make the chemical explosion cure. And guess what? The bitch wants ten billion. Ten. Billion. Can you freaking believe that?"

Briar froze solid. The number looped in her brain. Ten billion. No way. Her eyes went wide, her throat locking up. That wasn't just cash; it was a fantasy number she couldn't even dream of touching. But Elara had just tossed it out like pocket change.

Briar swallowed hard, her fists clenching. A cold knot twisted in her gut. "Did you tell Thane?" she demanded.

"He's the one who asked her!" Elara snapped, her voice buzzing with annoyance. "But he won't pay. Says we're broke. Like, seriously? Suddenly she's some untouchable genius overnight. Nobody can get near her. Hell, even the beta is acting like her damn personal bodyguard."

Briar's jaw clenched. Thane hovering around Elara? Fine. She could handle that. She could manipulate that. But Rhys? No way. Rhys was a whole different beast. The guy had more money than Thane, and that thought made her stomach turn. She thinned her lips, her voice turning icy. "Don't sweat it. I'll put her back in her place. Just watch me."

A beat of silence. Then Elara's voice came back, quieter. "Word is, she's not staying long anyway."

Briar stared blankly into the dark garden, her eyes narrowing into slits. "I know."

Thane marched toward the office with Caspian. He looked calm, but his head was a mess. Thank god Asher was watching Elara. That was one less headache for now, giving him a quick second to drown in paperwork before the next disaster hit. Around them, the pack was buzzing. People hurried by, doing their usual stuff. But here and there, groups were huddled up, whispering. They were totally locked into their gossip, completely missing the Chief Commander walking right up to them.

"So, I heard Director Sera is back," a girl whispered, practically dripping with jealousy. The words cut straight through the noise, hitting Thane's ears instantly.

"Ugh, of course she crawled back," another snorted. "But honestly? Nobody gives a crap about her anymore."

"I hope the Chief makes the Co-Director the official boss just to slap her in the face. Sera never belonged here. She's a nobody."

"Wait, but did you see her?" another chimed in, sounding both jealous and obsessed. "She looks totally different. Like, human supermodel hot."

"Yeah, right," a guy scoffed. "She used to look like a total hag, just begging the Chief to look her way. The only time she wasn't pathetic was in training. Back then, she was a beast. Nobody could touch her."

"And at the hospital, she—"

Caspian cleared his throat, loud and sharp. The gossip died instantly. They turned around, saw Thane, and went pale as ghosts. Panic hit them like a truck. They scrambled, vanishing down the hallways in seconds. Total silence.

Thane stood frozen. His face darkened, a heavy weight sinking into his chest. "Is that really what they thought of her?" he muttered, mostly to himself. "Is that how they treated her this whole time?" He'd heard the whispers before during training. He'd seen the looks. But he'd always brushed it off as harmless drama. Now, hearing it out loud? It felt toxic.

Caspian stared at him, his eyes wide with disbelief. "Are you seriously telling me you had no idea?" he asked. The tone made Thane flinch. He couldn't even answer. Shame washed over him, hot and heavy. He looked away, trying to shove the guilt down, but it stuck. *I'll deal with this at the next training session,* he promised himself. But right now, duty called. And it was brutal.

Hours flew by. They drowned in the workload. Thane buried his face in reports, while Caspian practically glued his eyes to his phone, firing off emails and putting out fires. They grabbed a quick bite at some point, barely talking, both lost in their own heads. Then, right back to the grind. The outside world vanished. Just the endless click of keys and rustle of paper.

Until the peace shattered. Briar's mindlink ripped into Thane's brain, screaming, frantic, and loud.

*"Rav... Sera is trying to kill me!"*
