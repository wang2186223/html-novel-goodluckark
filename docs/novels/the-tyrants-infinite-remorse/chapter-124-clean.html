Chapter 125

Thane ground his teeth until his jaw ached, muscles bunching hard under his skin. He was losing his mind. He kept running the tape back, over and over, but it made zero sense. How the hell did a tiny spark turn into this massive, raging wildfire?

The whole situation felt like some sick joke. And Elara? God, her taking it this far didn't just piss him off—it seriously creeped him out.

"How was I supposed to know she’d blow up over something so damn stupid?" Thane snapped. He slammed his back against his chair, fingers tapping a frantic, angry beat on the mahogany desk. He took a slow breath, trying to keep his lid on.

"Seriously, I didn't think she'd go totally psycho. I mean, she’s still breathing, right? So why the hell did you guys pull that stunt without looping me in? If you’d just opened your mouths, I would’ve shut it down right then and there."

He was scrambling to pass the buck, desperate to shift the target off his own back. Finn ducked his head, looking like a kicked puppy. He tried to play it cool, but the guilt was written all over his face.

"Yeah, I get it," Finn mumbled. He rubbed the back of his neck, his face tight with regret. "I just jumped the gun, man. Didn't think. Now? Everything we built is completely trashed. She ratted us out to the council. We're done. In a couple of days, they'll strip our titles, and we'll have absolutely nothing left."

Finn's words hung in the air like toxic smog. The sheer despair in his voice snuffed out the last tiny bit of hope Thane was holding onto.

Thane just stared, his brain short-circuiting. He shook his head. "Sera... Jesus. How can she be this cold-blooded?" The name just slipped out, a reflex.

Even after all their history, he couldn't wrap his head around it. Since when did she become this ruthless monster who ruins lives for fun? He squared his shoulders, eyes locking in. "Whatever. Stop stressing. I'll go handle her. I'll fix this."

Riven threw up a hand, cutting him off instantly.

"Save your breath," Riven said. His voice was flat, totally defeated. "We already went down that road. We rang her up, literally begged her to back off. But she laid down the law. No negotiations."

Thane's brow furrowed. "What kind of terms?"

Riven let out a dry, humorless laugh. "She said we get our businesses and titles back on one condition: we give her our lives."

The room went dead silent. You could've heard a pin drop.

Riven slumped back, exhaling a long, shaky breath. He looked totally lost, a mix of shock and grief in his eyes. "I mean, she always played dirty when you crossed her, but this? I don't get how she got this dark," he muttered. "Sera used to be so damn sweet."

Finn glanced over at Riven, the two of them locking eyes for a long beat. Then, Finn finally asked the one question he'd been dying to ask.

"Okay, if she was such an angel," Finn said, picking his words carefully, "then why the hell did you cheat on her?"

Riven’s face darkened instantly, his eyebrows snapping together. "For the last time, I didn't cheat," he snapped. "Briar was always my girl. Period. But my folks kept shoving Elara down my throat, no matter how many times I told them to back off."

He stopped, the old memories clearly eating at him, making his face twist.

"Seven years back, at the Moon Festival, I was finally gonna lay it all out for my parents," he said, his voice dropping to a bitter whisper. "Briar was knocked up. I wanted to put a ring on her finger, do it right, and raise our kid together."

Finn leaned in, totally hooked. "So what went wrong?"

"What went wrong," Thane cut in with a nasty laugh, "is that Elara slipped me a roofie and crawled into my bed."

Finn and Riven just stared, jaws dropping. Thane kept going, the words pouring out.

"She got pregnant on purpose that night. Once my parents got wind of it, they cornered me into a shotgun wedding. I was trapped," he spat, his voice dripping with pure venom. "That bitch ruined my future with Briar. She literally stole Briar's life."

His eyes went dead cold. "No way was my son growing up outside the pack house. And since Elara popped early—right around the same time Briar went into labor—I took matters into my own hands," he said, flat-voiced. "I got rid of Elara's baby girl and swapped her with Briar's kid."

Finn and Riven went stiff as statues. "Sera went into labor way too early, so the timing was perfect," Thane went on, talking about it like he was explaining a simple trade-in, not a horror story. "I just handed Briar's boy to Elara and let her raise him."

He paused, looking slightly annoyed. "My only screw-up was actually telling her. We were screaming at each other, I lost my temper, and the truth just slipped out."

Riven stared at him like he was a freaking alien.

"You murdered your own baby?" Riven whispered, his voice shaking with pure disgust. "Man, I don't care how much I hate a girl, I would never put down my own flesh and blood."

For once, Thane felt a sharp, nasty pang in his gut. Acid burned the back of his throat, and he had to swallow hard to keep from throwing up right there.

"Whatever, that's old news," he muttered, waving his hand to brush it off. "What do you guys actually want from me?"

Finn hesitated, shifting in his seat.

"Well... look, we lost everything because of your mess. We figured it's only fair you help us get back on our feet," he said, testing the waters. "We were hoping you'd hook us up with some jobs at your firm."

The second those words left Finn's mouth, Thane went totally rigid.

These past few months had taught him one brutal lesson about Elara, and he wasn't about to forget it. When she goes on a warpath, she doesn't just destroy her target.

Anyone dumb enough to throw them a lifeline gets dragged straight into the meat grinder with them.

Look at what happened to Caspian when he tried to back Thane up. Thane wasn't about to be her next casualty.

"Yeah... that's a terrible idea," Thane said slowly, forcing a fake, polite smile onto his face. "If I hire you guys, Sera's going to put a target on my back too. I can't risk losing the last few things I actually own."

Riven cocked an eyebrow, reading him like an open book. "Listen to yourself, man," Riven said, a smug little smirk creeping onto his face. "You sound just like Caspian."

He leaned in close, a mocking glint in his eyes as he dropped the ultimate question.

"Are you seriously terrified of your ex-wife?"
