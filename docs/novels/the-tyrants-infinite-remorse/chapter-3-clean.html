NovelVibe

Chapter 3

"Mom! Thank god you're here," a kid whimpered, sounding totally freaked. "That psycho tried to kidnap me."

"Hey, it's okay, buddy," a woman's voice cut in, smooth but dead serious. "Nobody's touching you. Your dad's got this."

Elara heard them before she could even blink. The words buzzed in her skull, fuzzy and annoying, like a bad hangover.

She forced her eyes open. The ceiling of the pack house stared back—rough wooden beams, blurry as hell, like she was looking up from the bottom of a pool.

She had zero clue how she ended up on this bed. The last thing she remembered? Her legs turning to jelly and that damn DNA report slipping from her shaking hands, fluttering down to the floor.

Wait, this was crazy. Her chest throbbed. She knew she was pregnant. It happened right after that wild, rough night Thane claimed her—the night that locked her into the marriage she’d secretly craved.

She’d dragged herself through nine brutal months of puke, bone-deep fatigue, and pure agony. She went into labor. She screamed, she bled, she heard a baby cry. Then she blacked out. She had the kid. So what the hell was this?

Thane’s face came into focus. His eyes were pure ice, glaring down at her with raw hate. "You got a death wish?" he spat. "Who the hell do you think you are, trying to run off with my kid?"

Elara kept her face blank. Lysander grabbed her arm, pulling her up. Her body was one giant bruise, but she tuned it out, her brain firing on all cylinders. Over by the wall, Briar was just standing there, wearing this smug, nasty little grin. She wasn't even trying to hide how thrilled she was.

"Thane," Elara said, keeping her voice dead level while her insides screamed. "I gave birth to a boy. If Asher isn't mine, then where is my actual son?"

Thane let out a nasty laugh, his eyes turning pitch black. "Are you stupid?" he sneered. "You drugged me to get into my bed. You seriously thought I'd let you keep my kid after that?"

He dropped the next words like a bomb, slow and brutal. "Briar popped that same night. I made sure your kid disappeared."

Something snapped in Elara's head. She lost it. Screaming like a wild animal, she threw herself at him, fingers clawing for his throat, her fangs sliding out, ready to rip him open.

Thane didn't even sweat. He caught her, flipped her, and slammed her hard onto the floorboards. The impact knocked the wind right out of her, pain shooting straight to her bones.

"You're tough," Thane said, standing over her like a giant. "But I'm the Chief Commander. Nobody beats me."

Elara dragged herself up, her arms shaking, her brain totally numb. "No," she croaked. "Where's my boy?"

"You mean your girl," Thane shot back. "She never even got a breath of air. I burned her and mixed her ashes into the concrete of this very house."

The walls spun. Her knees gave out again, her eyes going blurry. It was all a lie. She'd spent years raising a kid that wasn't hers. She'd given up everything for Asher—even the rare cure for his genetic disease. She'd handed it over to save the kid of the monsters who killed her own baby.

It hit her like a truck. If she'd just signed those divorce papers sooner, she would've gone to her grave never knowing. Thane would've kept this sick secret forever.

"I didn't trap you," she whispered, her voice finally cracking. She never begged or made excuses, but she had to say it. "You took what you wanted from me that night. You forced it."

"Cut the crap," Thane barked. "We have the receipts. You paid off the bartender to drug my glass. If you hadn't, Briar would be running this place, not you. You messed with the wrong guy."

Elara went white. Why did she even bother? Thane had already made up his mind, and nothing she said mattered. Losing the guy you loved was bad enough. Finding out he murdered your baby? That was a whole different level of hell.

"Fine, believe whatever you want," she choked out, tears finally spilling over. "But she was your kid too. How could you be such a monster?"

Thane didn't even blink. "Exactly," he said, cold as ice. "She had my blood, which is why she had to go. Why do you think I never laid a finger on you again? You're pathetic, Elara."

She’d put up with this fake, loveless marriage for years, trying her best every single day to be the perfect wife and leader. She knocked back his divorce papers six times. Why? Because of the kid. She didn't want him growing up in a broken home.

But now? It was all a sick joke. Her heart was in a million pieces, and she didn't even know where to start picking them up.

Briar piped up, her voice dripping with fake sweetness. "Look, Elara, it's fine. I'm not mad. You can keep playing mommy to Asher. You've done a great job keeping him alive with that nasty disease of his."

The bitch was gloating. The subtext was loud and clear: *I killed your kid, and you're still keeping mine alive. Plus, I've got your cure.*

But Briar didn't know the catch. Nobody did. That medicine wasn't a permanent fix. It was a leash. Sure, Asher could run around and play as long as he took it. But miss just one dose? The sickness would hit him like a freight train.

That's why she'd tried to grab him. But now? Screw it. She didn't care anymore. A weird, icy calm settled over her, pumped up by pure adrenaline. She looked Thane dead in the eye.

"You killed my baby," she said, her voice barely a whisper but sharp as a knife. "And I saved yours. Let the spirits sort this out."

She turned on her heel and walked right past him, her boots heavy on the floor, just wanting to get out of this suffocating hellhole.

She almost made it to the door. Then she slammed right into a wall of solid muscle. The guy didn't budge an inch, radiating pure, terrifying power. She looked up. A pair of killer, pitch-black eyes stared down at her. Elara froze.
