Chapter 101

Caspian's prayer barely faded before Elara sneezed. Loud. Hard. The noise shattered the quiet, and Caspian froze.

His eyes snapped open. He locked his gaze on her, muscles tight.

She gasped. Her chest heaved, sucking in air like she’d just broken the surface after drowning.

Relief hit him like a truck. His shoulders dropped. The knot in his chest finally unraveled, and he let out his own breath.

"She's alive," he breathed, the words slipping out with a shaky sigh.

He stared for a few beats. He had to make sure. He couldn't let his guard down until he knew the spark wouldn't just flicker out.

Once he knew she was out of the woods, the panic vanished. His cold, calculating brain took over.

He stood up, marched to the door, and yanked it open. He almost plowed right into Julian, who was standing in the hall.

Julian blinked, totally caught off guard.

"Caspian? Wait, you got Sera?" Julian stammered, his eyes darting over the chairman's rigid posture.

Caspian brushed right past him. "Keep your mouth shut about this," he ordered, his voice pure steel. "Elara cannot find out."

Julian's brow furrowed. "What the hell happened?" Then he spotted it—smudged lipstick on Caspian's lips. He swallowed hard, a sick feeling rising in his gut. Did Caspian cross the line with her? The thought made his stomach turn.

"Poison," Caspian snapped, keeping it brief. "I found the antidote. She took it."

Julian gasped. "Poisoned? Who did it?"

"Some loser she rejected tonight," Caspian sneered, disgust dripping from his voice. "The guy couldn't handle a 'no' and threw a tantrum."

His jaw clenched. "I'll handle him."

Julian stood there, shaking. Before he could open his mouth, Caspian leaned in close.

"If Elara finds out I saved her," Caspian whispered, "I will ruin your father. Got it?"

He wasn't bluffing. The coldness in his eyes proved it.

Caspian turned and walked away, disappearing down the hall without looking back.

Julian froze. He'd never seen the chairman lose his cool like that. But he couldn't dwell on it—he had to check on Elara.

He rushed into the room. Elara was sitting up in bed, still struggling to breathe.

"Sera!" Julian ran over and grabbed her shoulders, holding her steady. "Hey, hey, breathe. You're okay. I've got you."

Her eyes popped open. When she saw Julian, she finally relaxed.

"Julian... thank god," she rasped, her throat burning. "How... how did I get here?"

Her head started to clear. She remembered a dream—her wolf saying goodbye. But she could still feel the beast inside her, alive, even if the city's weird energy blocked their connection.

This place didn't belong to the pack. She sniffed the air. Wolfsbane. And something sharp—the antidote.

Then, another scent hit her. Her heart squeezed. Caspian. His scent was all over the room.

Oh, no. Did he save her?

God, she hoped not. How could she even look at him if he was the one who kept her alive?

She loathed Caspian almost as much as Thane. Sure, she'd used Caspian to mess with Thane's head, trying to tear their friendship apart, but that plan hadn't worked yet.

"You got poisoned," Julian said, choosing his words carefully. He remembered Caspian's threat. "I brought you here to crash."

He sounded totally sincere, but she wasn't buying it.

Julian didn't know jack about wolfsbane, let alone how to cure it. He couldn't have pulled this off.

"Then why does this place smell exactly like Caspian?" she asked, her eyes narrowing.

Julian hesitated. "Uh, it's his room. He saw you were messed up and let me bring you here. He's the chairman, you know? He has to look out for everyone. Even you."

He quickly added, "Don't sweat it. He's running the party tonight, so he went to hunt down the guy who did this."

Elara's face went cold. "No way," she spat, anger boiling up. "I want a name."

Her voice turned deadly. "When I find out who did this, I'm going to break him myself."

Julian shut his mouth. One look at her eyes told him she wasn't kidding.

"Look, let's just get you home first," he said, trying to change the subject. "You shouldn't be here."

Elara thought about it, then nodded. Yeah, she needed to get out of here to clear her head and figure this mess out.

But the second they stepped out into the hallway, all hell broke loose.

The whole damn place was in total chaos.
