NovelVibe

Chapter 38

Caspian wanted to dump Thane. Like, completely. If it was just about Elara, boom, done. No doubts, no looking back. Just a clean, hard cut. That was his usual move. But Thane wasn’t some random business guy. He was his ride-or-die. And looking at him right now? Man, it hit hard.

Thane looked like absolute shit. Red-rimmed eyes, hollow cheeks, shoulders totally slumped. Caspian had never seen him like this. The guy clearly hadn’t slept in days. His suit looked three sizes too big, and his jaw was clenched so tight it looked ready to snap.

Caspian realized something. Whatever pain he was feeling, Thane had it ten times worse. The anger in Caspian’s throat just dissolved. He stood up fast, his chair scraping the floor. "I’m hitting the Stone Group first."

Thane’s head snapped up. "I’m coming."

Caspian shook his head. "Nah. You got your own fires to put out." The office was a mess, and they both knew it. So, they split. Again.

Footsteps and engines echoed in the concrete garage. Thane went left. Caspian went right. Same destination, different roads. Less than an hour later, Caspian walked into the Stone Group lobby. The vibe shifted instantly. The receptionist froze.

She literally shrank back. Everyone knew Caspian was waging war on them. The market was crazy, and here he was, walking right in. Bad news, obviously. But Caspian didn’t yell. "I need to see Stone," he said, voice like ice. The girl swallowed hard, her hand shaking as she grabbed the phone. "I’ll—I’ll let him know." Her voice cracked. "Sir... Caspian is here."

On the other end, Felix blinked. What the hell? Caspian never just showed up unless things were really good or really bad. And with the market bleeding? Felix couldn’t guess. "Tell him to wait," Felix muttered, hung up, and immediately called Lysander.

The line picked up. Felix didn’t even say hello. "Lysander. Caspian is downstairs. Tell me you didn’t cause this."

Silence on the line. Lysander looked over at Elara. She was typing like crazy, lines of code flying down her screen. She had this look on her face—smug, dangerous, totally in control. Lysander kept his eyes on her as he told his dad, "Tell him you don’t know anything."

Felix closed his eyes. He knew that tone. She sounded like a high-powered exec, even out here. He hung up, totally lost, and dialed another number. Elara wasn’t going to talk, but he knew who would. Arthur.

It rang once. Arthur picked up. "Felix! Man, it’s been forever." Felix cut him off. "Arthur, sorry to skip the small talk, but I’m in a rush. What’s the deal with Elara?"

A beat of silence. "What’s going on?" Arthur asked. Felix spilled it all. The stock spikes, the shifting shares, and Caspian waiting in his lobby.

He expected Arthur to freak out. Instead, the guy just laughed. "Well," Arthur chuckled, "she did warn him." Felix scowled. Arthur kept going: "I was worried about her at first, but damn, she’s got skills I didn’t even know about. The medical stuff is just the start."

Hearing Arthur laugh actually made Felix relax a bit. "Look, if she’s on your side, you’re golden," Arthur said. "She’s the one who saved our pack’s finances, too." Felix nodded, feeling a wave of relief. "Thanks. I’ll let you know how it goes."

Arthur laughed again. "Can’t wait. Honestly? I love seeing Caspian get knocked down a peg, even if it doesn’t last. Who would’ve thought his best friend’s ex-wife would be the one to do it?"

Felix almost brought up Lysander, almost asked Arthur to help out, but the intercom buzzed. "Gotta go. Talk soon."

He hit the intercom, but before he could speak, Caspian’s voice blasted through. Cold. Sharp. "You too busy for me, Felix? Or are you hiding?" He’d snatched the phone right out of the secretary’s hand.

Felix took a deep breath, forcing a fake smile. "Caspian, I was on a call. Come on up. Didn’t realize it was urgent." The line went dead with a clatter. A second later, the office door swung open.

Caspian walked in. Tall, sharp suit, totally composed. You’d never guess his company was bleeding cash from a thousand cuts. He looked untouchable. "Mr. Stone," Caspian said, cool as ever. "We need to talk."

Felix waved a hand. "You’re already in. What’s on your mind?" He didn’t stand. Old guy privilege. Caspian marched over, sat down, and leaned in. No foreplay. "Who’s your broker?" Felix blinked, totally thrown. Caspian kept his voice low, steady. "I’ve got a job for her."
