Chapter 82

Thane’s face went pitch black. The room went dead silent. *Oh, crap,* Elara thought. She’d definitely pushed him too far this time.

Pissing him off behind closed doors? Easy. But doing it here, in front of a bunch of ego-tripping billionaire Alphas? That was a whole different ball game. These guys practically ate dominance for breakfast.

If Thane snapped right now, nobody in this room could actually stop him.

Sure, a couple of Alphas here had the muscle, but they were all his yes-men. They survived by kissing his ass. No way they’d risk their necks for her. They’d take his side even if he murdered someone.

And Thane? When he was backed into a corner, he was a total wildcard.

She couldn’t beat him in a fistfight. Muscle wasn’t her thing. She played the long game—brains, timing, and ruining him without ever leaving a mark.

But looking at his locked jaw and those pitch-black eyes, she knew better than to test his limits right now. Not in public.

Time to move. She whipped out her phone. *Great.* The battery icon was flashing red. Classic. She’d forgotten to charge the damn thing. She hit dial anyway, holding her breath.

Ringing... then a beep. "Dad!" she wailed, turning on the waterworks instantly. "Thane’s picking on me again!"

*Click.* The phone died. Talk about perfect timing.

She slid the phone down, instantly switching to her victim face. She widened her eyes, looking perfectly, tragically hurt. A few onlookers snickered, hiding their faces.

She looked freaking adorable. Way too cute and fragile to actually hurt anyone.

No one looking at her now would believe she’d just kneed a massive Alpha in the balls five minutes ago.

If they only knew what this bastard had put her through. Thane made her crazy, and she wasn’t stopping until she completely destroyed him.

Right on cue, Thane’s phone buzzed.

He shot her a look that could freeze hell over, then swiped accept. "Dad, seriously? You believe literally everything she says."

"Duh, of course I do," Arthur’s voice boomed through the receiver.

Thane’s jaw twitched. He looked pissed. He tapped the speaker button and held the phone up. "Fine. Let’s ask the room. Hey, did I bully her?"

Silence stretched. People traded glances. "Nope," a few pack Alphas chimed in.

Elara scanned the crowd, locking their faces into her mental blacklist. *I’ll deal with you guys later.*

Thane scoffed. "See? And didn't she literally just hit me?"

"Because you insulted me!" Elara shot back, not missing a beat. "You called my guy weak. What was I supposed to do, say thank you?"

Julian blinked, a smug, proud grin spreading across his face. Wow. She was actually standing up for him against Thane. The realization hit him hard, making his chest swell. He pulled her a little closer, wrapping his arm tighter around her waist.

Arthur sighed heavily on the line. "Thane, grow up. You’re always causing drama. One more complaint from her, and I’m stripping your title as—"

Thane hung up before his dad could even finish saying "Chief Commander."

His jaw clenched so hard a muscle jumped in his cheek. His eyes flashed red. Elara knew that look. He was absolutely livid, and the public embarrassment was pushing him over the edge.

Boom. He lunged. His hand clamped onto her arm like a vice, yanking her forward. She flew toward him like a ragdoll. It was honestly insulting how easy it was for him to lift her.

She crashed right into his chest. Julian’s grip slipped. He didn't mean to let go, but it happened too fast.

Julian felt a wave of shame. Letting her get pulled away like that? It felt like a total fail. Like he wasn't man enough to protect her.

Julian stepped up, but Thane glared him down. "Don't even think about it, pal," Thane growled.

The threat was loud and clear. Elara looked at Julian, giving him a chill smile despite the crazy tension. "It's cool," she murmured. "He won't do anything."

Her chill vibe calmed him down a bit, but he stayed right there, ready to jump in if things went south.

She ripped her arm out of Thane’s grip and stood her ground, staring him right in the eye.

"Seriously, what is your deal?" she teased, a smirk playing on her lips. "Are you, like, falling for your adopted sister now or something?"

Heads snapped toward them. The gossip mill was practically spinning.

Thane looked around, feeling everyone's eyes on him. The whispers were starting. Dying of embarrassment, he tried to drag her toward a quiet corner.

She yanked back. "Say it here, or stop wasting my time. I’ve got a boyfriend to get back to."

Hearing her call Julian "my man" felt like a slap in the face.

"Cut the crap," he snapped. "I don't want you. Never did, never will."

Elara burst out laughing, loud enough for the whole room to hear.

But under the chandelier light, Thane froze. Something was off. She was glowing—and it wasn't makeup. Her skin was clear, her eyes were bright, and she just looked... lighter.

She looked younger. Like a teenager who hadn't been crushed by life yet.

Did dumping him really do that? Was their marriage such a nightmare that leaving him literally brought her back to life?

"Honestly? I'd rather date a total psycho," she spat, "than ever get back with you."

That did it. He snapped. Blinded by rage and bruised ego, his hand flew up.

The crowd gasped. But before his hand could connect with her face, two hands grabbed his wrist mid-air.

He expected Julian. But the other guy? His stomach dropped. It was Caspian.

Thane turned slowly, staring in total shock. His own best friend was holding him back in front of the entire room.

Something inside him just broke.

"What the hell, man?" Thane hissed, his voice cracking with pure betrayal. "Why are you stopping me? Do you have any idea what she just did?"
