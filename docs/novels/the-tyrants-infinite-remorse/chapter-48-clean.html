Elara just shrugged. The guy was throwing a toddler tantrum, and she'd dealt with way worse.

She tracked his eyes as they darted across the screen. For a split second, his face went blank with shock.

Just a second. Then the wall went right back up, his expression hardening into pure denial. "She hit send by mistake," he muttered. "You're framing her. This is a setup."

And there it was.

Elara had promised herself she wouldn't care. But a sharp, cold ache still bloomed in her chest anyway, heavy and sour.

Caspian's fists clenched so hard his knuckles turned white. He'd already burned billions on top-tier tech firms to fix this disaster, and now she was asking for the exact same insane figure Nova Ghost had quoted him.
