NovelVibe

Chapter 27

Thane locked up. The thought wasn't new. Honestly, it had crossed his mind a million times. He and Elara had shared a roof for years without a single touch. Just two polite strangers holding a piece of paper that said they were married.

It was a cold, business-first deal. Keep up appearances, live totally separate lives.

But when he finally brought up making it official—staying married on paper only—Elara shut it down.

Thane had backed off, too embarrassed to push. He was the one who wanted out anyway. Pushing harder felt desperate, fake. So he swallowed his words, burying the whole mess deep down with his pride and regrets.

Now, staring at Briar, he couldn't say it out loud. "Elara's not stupid. She'll see right through it. She'll know I'm just chasing the cash."

Briar blinked. Not the answer she wanted. A flash of annoyance crossed her face, but she wiped it clean, keeping her voice smooth and sharp. "Then we just make more money. Simple."

Thane nodded, slow. "Yeah. That's why I have to hit the city."

His head was pounding. The stress was eating him alive. Then, a sudden thought hit him like a slap.

"Hey," Thane said, his voice dropping into boss-mode. "Where are we on the medical supplies? And I haven't seen any cash moving in the account."

Briar bit her lip. She'd set a hard two-week deadline. That was all she needed. She was sure her threats had lit a fire under those doctors.

"I'm on it," she said, smooth as silk. "Trust me. Two weeks, max." Then, like it was nothing: "Oh, and I canned Valerie. She was getting on my damn nerves."

Thane nudged her back, eyes wide. "You fired Valerie? Since when? Why didn't you tell me?"

She tilted her head, reading him. "You look pissed. She talked down to me, Thane. Acted like I was stupid. I don't take that crap."

"Oh." Thane let out a breath, his face softening. Well, if she disrespected Briar, she had to go. "Still, you should've looped me in. I would've made it hurt worse."

Briar smirked, but a knock cut her off. Thane went to the door.

Rhys stood there, holding a sleek new phone. "Chief. Got your new phone set up. SIM's in. It's already blowing up."

"Thanks." Thane shut the door, looked at the screen, and the color drained right out of his face.

"Who the hell approved half a million to logistics?" he barked. "That kind of cash needs my signature."

Briar felt a cold sweat break out. With Lysander and Elara gone, she was the only one signing off on stuff. If Thane didn't do it, she was dead in the water.

"I—I'm sorry," she stammered. "They said it was urgent. I didn't think—"

Thane pinched the bridge of his nose. His head was absolutely killing him. "So what, anything labeled 'urgent' just gets a free pass?"

Briar shut up. A single tear rolled down her cheek. "I'm sorry, I just..."

Something in Thane snapped. He pulled her close, his voice dropping. "Hey. Look, I didn't mean to yell. There's just too much shit going on right now."

He sighed. "Once I sort out this city mess, I promise we'll take that vacation."

Her lips curved. Briar spun around, straddling his lap, pressing her bare skin right against him. "Do I get to come?" she whispered. "We have a Deputy Chief now, don't we?"

Thane forced a fake smile. "And who watches Asher?"

The room went dead quiet.

It was always Elara. Whenever Briar went anywhere with him, Elara watched the kid. Briar thought about suggesting her parents, but she knew Thane would shoot it down. Asher was the heir. Only the Chief or the Managing Director could watch him.

Her parents were nobody. And Thane's parents? No way. Not after what they did to Elara.

Briar swallowed the lump in her throat, fear creeping in. What if Elara wormed her way back into his life?

No. She had to lock this down.

"Fine," she said, keeping it light. "If you have to go, let me help you unwind first."

She slid off his lap, secretly propping her phone up on the table before climbing back onto him. What followed was pure heat—heavy breathing, skin on skin, every single moan caught on camera.

The next morning, Rhys stood by the door, saying goodbye to Thane over breakfast. Asher was clinging to his dad's leg, eyes watery.

"Dad," the kid mumbled. "When are you coming home?"

"I don't know, buddy," Thane said, messing up his hair and kissing his cheek. "Soon. I've got some stuff to handle."

Briar pouted. "I could still come. Rhys can handle Asher."

Thane looked at Rhys. The guy shrugged. "Yeah, I don't mind. I love kids."

But Thane didn't trust him. Not fully. He shook his head. "No. Too much pack business. Stay here, let Rhys help you train."

Rhys stiffened at the mention of training her, but Thane didn't have time to ask. He turned and walked out.

Later, Briar smirked as she hit send on the video, routing it straight to Elara.

*See that?* she texted, her words dripping with venom. *Eight years in his bed, and he still craves me like his favorite meal. I'm the only woman who makes him feel like a real man. You never could, and you never will.*

She smiled, a nasty, slow grin, typing one last line before hitting send.

*You were never hot to him, Elara. Not once. Don't flatter yourself. No Chief would ever want you.*

She waited. Nothing.

At first, she was just annoyed. Then, as the silence dragged on, she got nervous. It took a few days, but when Elara finally texted back, the reply hit like a freight train. It was so brutal, Briar couldn't even show her face to the pack.
