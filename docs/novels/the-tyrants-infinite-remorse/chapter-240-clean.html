Chapter 243

Caspian’s face went dark. Fast. Like someone flipped a switch behind his eyes.

*Don't let this happen,* Gideon barked in his head. *Not until we fix the mate bond.*

"Look, it's... it's a mess—" Thane muttered, then cleared his throat. "Rav."

Briar jumped in, her voice smooth as silk. She grabbed his arm, a move she’d clearly practiced in the mirror a thousand times to make it look natural.

"Let them go," she said. "They apologized, and hey, they're helping pay for the cure anyway."

Thane cut his eyes at her. It was a look that asked a very quiet, very uncomfortable question.

But the whole pack was staring. Asher was standing right there, three feet away. He had a room to run, feelings be damned.

He spoke, and the temperature in the gym plummeted.

Briar stared at his face, searching for any sign of softening. Nothing. He wouldn't do this to her. Not in public. Not in front of everyone. No way.

But he did. His voice sounded like boots on frozen concrete. "Briar, you need the workout too. Start praying Elara is nice enough to tell you to stop."

The humiliation hit her faster than the words. Her vision went white, her knees turned to jelly, and she hit the floor. Out cold. The entire gym watched her drop in dead silence.

Elara was already on her feet. "I've got her." Sure, she was a doctor, but she had other plans for this little check-up.

She strolled over, knelt down by Briar, and leaned in close. Close enough that only Briar would hear her.

"You sent hitmen after me," Elara whispered, her voice sweet as pie, like they were just chatting about the weather. "No pressure, but I've got a scorpion in my bag. Open your eyes in three seconds, or it goes straight down your pants."

"Sera!" Briar’s eyes snapped open. She bolted upright like she’d been hit with a taser.

Elara stood up, dusted off her hands, and looked totally chill. "Thought so. Faker."

She walked back to her bench, not even looking back, and sat down like she hadn't just threatened someone.

The gym stayed dead silent. Nobody breathed.

Inside Thane, something clicked. A heavy door shutting on a room he’d been locked out of for a long time.

He watched Briar scramble off the floor. He stared at her face, genuinely wondering what the hell he had ever seen in her. Why did he choose her over Elara?

What went wrong during those years? Why did he hate Elara when she’d done nothing but scream her love for him from the rooftops?

It came down to one thing. Briar gave him a son. He could overlook her bullshit for that, even if he felt sick to his stomach right now.

He turned to the crowd. "Pair up. We're burning daylight."

The gym slowly came back to life. People moved quietly, grabbing partners without the usual trash-talk, still reeling from the drama.

Elara stood up. "Asher, want to spar?"

Asher’s face lit up like Christmas. He was already walking over before she even finished the sentence.

Thane stepped right between them. "Asher, sit down and watch. You train at the academy with your peers." He looked up. "Caspian, you're with Rhys. I'll take Sera."

"Hell no," Elara said. No hesitation, no room to argue. He never trained with her when she was his director. Why start now?

Thane’s jaw tightened. He figured throwing Briar under the bus would buy him some points with Elara. Guess he was wrong.

*Regret it yet?* Sage, his wolf, asked. The voice was smug, like he’d been waiting years to say it.

Thane snapped back in his mind. *I have Asher. So no, I don't regret shit.* He paused, then added, *I'll win her back. Slowly. She's soft. She always has been.*

*I tried talking to her wolf,* Sage said, his voice dropping, sounding heavy. *She shut me out. Completely. Face it, Thane. They want nothing to do with us.*

Thane blocked it out. He refused to believe it. There had to be something left of the girl who used to love this pack more than life itself.

You don't just stop caring overnight. You don't turn to ice.

*If she hates us so much,* Thane argued, *why does she let Asher call her mom?*

Sage sighed, done arguing, but he had to get the last word in. *I told you letting Briar in would ruin you. If you think Elara loving Asher means she's going to crawl back to you, you're delusional.*

He paused. *And honestly? Watching her right now... she's not just pissed. She's planning something. And we're in the crosshairs.*

*Sage,* Thane snapped, his mental voice sharp. *Shut up. You don't know what you're talking about.*

The wolf went quiet. Maybe he was obeying, or maybe he was just tired of talking to a brick wall.

Thane looked back at Elara. She stood there, cool and collected, already making her next move.

"Fine," she said, her voice smooth, just stepping right around his roadblock. "If I can't have Asher, I'll take Rhys. You and Caspian can go at it. Two Alphas sparring? Now that's a show."

She knew the gap between Caspian and Thane. She was setting a trap, and she knew it.

Getting his ass kicked in his own gym, in front of his own men? That would cost Thane way more than any fine or apology.

Thane saw the trap instantly. "As Commander, I'm ordering you to train with me. Rhys is my beta. And Caspian doesn't spar with women."

"Actually, I'm down," Caspian said, sounding completely relaxed, like he had all day and nothing to lose.

Thane’s face went white.
