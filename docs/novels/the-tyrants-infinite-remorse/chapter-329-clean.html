Chapter 333

Caspian’s face gave him away before he even opened his mouth. That guilty little smirk said it all. Elara crossed her arms, locking eyes with him. "So you did do it."

He threw his hands up, playing innocent. "Hey, I *could* have just bought brand-new stuff," he teased, dragging out the "could." It worked; she couldn't help but smile.

"But?" she prompted.

He closed the gap between them, his tone dropping to a soft murmur. "I know how people get. We cling to our things. Especially when they actually mean something."

That did it. Elara’s defenses crumbled instantly. Caspian shrugged. "Replacing everything just felt cheap. Wrong, you know?"

Looking at him now, her chest ached with how fast she was turning to mush. It was always this. The tiny, stupid details nobody else gave a crap about—he caught them every single time.

A massive grin took over her face. Seriously, every second with this guy just dragged her deeper. It was becoming a real hazard. Not that she wanted a way out, though.

"You’re way too good to me," she muttered, the truth slipping out before she could stop it. Caspian beamed, looking smug as hell. And honestly? Fair enough. But then a lightbulb went off. "Wait a second." She pointed a finger toward the hall. "What was that whole thing with Linnea earlier?"

He snorted immediately, like the memory was still the funniest thing ever. Elara narrowed her eyes, giving him her best warning look. "Caspian. Spill."

"Okay, okay, hands up," he laughed, throwing his palms up again. "My mom basically gifted Linnea a bunch of... well, barely-there clothes."

Elara blinked. Once. Twice. "She bought her what now?"

Caspian’s grin stretched wider at her sheer confusion. "Super skimpy stuff. Like, straight-up lingerie."

Elara stood frozen, her brain short-circuiting as she tried to process the words. Finally, she managed to speak. "Your mother bought Linnea sexy underwear?" Saying it out loud made her sound completely insane.

Caspian just nodded. "Yeah. To get me into bed."

Silence stretched. One beat. Two beats. Three. Then, Elara just lost it. She burst out laughing, the absolute absurdity of the whole thing bubbling right out of her. Caspian rubbed his temple, looking exhausted.

"Trust me, it wasn't funny when it happened."

"Oh, but it’s hilarious right now," she wheezed, still giggling.

He let out a dramatic, long-suffering sigh. "I told her to throw it all out or pack her bags. Guess she liked having a job."

Elara shook her head, still reeling. The whole plot felt like some trashy reality TV show. But the lingerie wasn't even the wildest part—it was his mom's sheer, shameless hustle.

And then, out of nowhere, a tiny green monster reared its head. Just a little spark of jealousy. Okay, maybe a medium-sized one. Linnea was gorgeous. Period. Anyone with eyes could see that.

That bright red hair of hers literally stopped traffic. Plus, she was quiet, sweet, and totally gentle—basically the dream girl for your average Alpha. The type who never talked back.

Elara had tons of Managing Director friends who fit that exact mold: sweet, agreeable, easy to manage.

And then there was Elara. Stubborn. Loud. Hot-headed when pushed, ambitious, and always, *always* opinionated. She couldn't sit quietly and play nice if her life depended on it. She was the literal opposite of what Alphas usually went for.

A slow smile tugged at her lips. But then again, only Caspian would be weird enough to prefer a handful like her. The thought warmed her right up.

"Your mom is going to absolutely hate me, isn't she?" she admitted.

Caspian offered a soft, knowing smile. He didn't brush it off, mostly because he'd already stressed about this exact thing a million times. He reached out, his fingers gently tucking a stray lock of hair behind her ear. "You're wrong, babe."

Elara raised a highly skeptical eyebrow. Caspian just smiled back. He knew that once his mom actually met the real Elara, the game would change. Sure, Elara was a powerhouse—successful, sharp, and respected—but she didn't have a mean bone in her body. She was actually incredibly sweet, way more than she let on. His mom just hadn't seen that side yet.

"Don't sweat it," he murmured. "When the time comes, she'll see it."

Elara wasn't totally sold, but she let it slide. Then, another detail clicked, and her brow furrowed. "Wait. You said we're heading to the pack territory."

"Yep."

"Then why are you talking like your mom won't even see me there?"

It was a fair question, but Caspian had already mapped this out. His smile was pure comfort. "I also remember you saying you wanted to keep us on the down-low for now."

Elara nodded instantly. That was still the plan, no question.

Caspian pulled her a little closer, wrapping his arms around her. "And I'm down with that." His voice dropped to a low rumble. "But I can't guarantee my pack will keep their mouths shut if they spot you."

The mere thought made him frown. Gossip in a werewolf pack traveled faster than a viral tweet. The second someone caught a glimpse of Elara, half the pack would know. An hour later, the whole territory would be talking. By nightfall, the neighbors would have the scoop.

"So, we're bypassing the main territory. I'm taking you somewhere private."

Her curiosity flared. "Where?"

He gave her a mysterious, teasing grin that immediately made her suspicious. "You'll see."

"Caspian." Her warning tone only made him chuckle.

"I'm having our shaman meet us there," he went on, his eyes softening. "We're going to figure out what's going on with your memories."

That did it. The mention of her past locked her in. The drama with his mom and the pack instantly evaporated. Only one thing mattered: getting her missing life back.

Caspian gave her hand a reassuring squeeze. "Trust me." His warm smile made it impossible not to. "You're gonna love this place."

An hour later, they were finally ready to roll. Elara showered Esperanza's face with a bunch of loud, dramatic kisses, promising to call her the second she could, before heading out the door.

Surprisingly, the kid didn't throw a fit or beg to tag along. Instead, Esperanza just stood by the door next to Linnea, waving happily.

"Don't work too hard, Mommy!" The sweet little voice followed Elara all the way to the car, keeping her warm inside for the whole drive.

The second the car cleared the driveway, Lysander let out a massive, dramatic sigh of relief. "Finally."

Elara shot him a raised eyebrow. "What?"

Lysander's reply completely threw her off.
