Chapter 324

Esperanza’s eyes darted from the paper to her mom, then back down to the sketch. She let out this massive, theatrical sigh. It was so over-the-top Elara almost snorted, despite how wrecked her nerves were.

"Yeah, I can," the kid said, totally bored. "But, like, you have to actually believe me. Promise?"

Elara felt a sharp tug in her chest. No way was she brushing her daughter off. Not after everything. Especially not with these sketches showing stuff that made zero sense—secrets that should’ve stayed buried.

"I do," Elara whispered, squeezing her hand. "I believe every single word, baby."

Esperanza let out a breath, looking like a huge weight just slid off her tiny shoulders. "Okay, cool." She wiggled back into her pillows, getting comfy.

"So, two ladies had babies on the exact same night," she started. Hearing that sweet, tiny voice lay out such a creepy story was seriously jarring. "This one went into labor on her own." She tapped a finger on one of the drawings. "But the other one? The doctors had to, like, force the baby out. Induced."

Elara’s stomach did a sick flip. Suddenly, the puzzle pieces clicked. That was why she and Briar had delivered on the very same night.

Esperanza didn't even pause. "The lady who went into labor naturally had a baby girl." Her finger slid across the paper. "The other lady had a boy."

The temperature in the bedroom seemed to plummet.

"The husband of the girl's mom wanted it kept a total secret," the kid went on, her voice completely flat, like she was just talking about her Barbie dolls. "So he handed the baby girl over to his Deputy Chief. Then he took the baby boy and gave him to his actual wife."

A cold shiver raced straight down Elara’s spine. What came next nearly stopped her heart cold.

"And he told the Deputy Chief to kill the little girl."

The paper shook in Elara’s hands. The color drained right out of her face, her breath hitching. Every gut instinct screamed that these weren't just weird dreams. They were straight-up visions of the past. Was her kid actually gifted? Did she have some kind of crazy powers?

Her eyes locked onto another sketch—the one with the guy who looked identical to Lysander. She snatched it up so fast she almost dropped the whole pile.

The drawing showed him holding a newborn, standing right in front of some lady in a heavy cloak. You couldn't see her face at all, which matched exactly what Lysander had told her before. Terror and a tiny spark of hope tangled in her chest.

"What... what about this one?" she managed, her voice cracking.

Esperanza glanced down. "Oh, that." She grinned a little. "The Deputy Chief didn't actually kill her. He gave her to that lady instead." She pointed at the hooded figure. "She keeps popping up in my dreams."

The room literally spun. Elara gasped for air, her pulse thumping like a bass drum in her ears. The dreams, the sketches, the baby swap, the mystery woman, Lysander, Thane... it was all bleeding together. But there was still no sign of Delilah or Caspian in any of this.

She leaned in, desperate. "Hey, tell me. How did you end up with Daddy?"

Esperanza just threw her arms up, letting out a massive yawn. "Mommy," she whined, instantly going back to her drama-queen self. "I'm, like, starving." Another huge yawn. "Super hungry. Pancakes first, okay?"

Despite the absolute chaos in her head, Elara almost snorted. Unbelievable. Only her kid could hold life-altering secrets hostage for a stack of pancakes.

She took a slow, deep breath, then another, forcing her racing heart to chill out. The truth wasn't going anywhere. She'd get her answers eventually. Right now, her little girl came first. Period.

"Fine," she said, a real smile finally breaking through. "Pancakes first."

Esperanza looked smug as hell, totally victorious. Elara just rolled her eyes affectionately and scooped the kid up.

"I have legs, Mommy," Esperanza giggled, squirming.

"I know, I know. Just let me hold you," Elara said. The girl wrapped her arms around Elara's neck, and Elara smiled. "Let's go get some food."

"I can show you where the kitchen is!" Esperanza offered, puffing out her chest.

"Oh, really?" Elara teased, just keeping the chatter going.

"Yep! I know where everything is, even though I never actually go in there."

Elara laughed softly at the kid's confidence. The second they stepped into the hall, there was Linnea, waiting just like she'd promised.

Linnea stood up straight. "I'll guide you guys," she said, then hesitated. "Uh, hope you don't mind if I tag along?"

Elara waved a hand dismissively. "Not at all." And she meant it. She didn't see Linnea as some rival or threat. She just saw a girl drowning in feelings she never asked for, trying her best to hide emotions that were slipping out anyway. That was it.

They headed downstairs together. The massive house was slowly waking up, morning light pouring through the giant windows, the rich scent of fresh coffee drifting through the halls. Esperanza kept pointing at random stuff along the way, acting like a tiny tour guide.

By the time they hit the kitchen, Elara was actually smiling again. But that smile died the second she crossed the threshold. She froze. Two guys were already inside. One was Caspian. The other—

Her eyes went wide. "Lysander?"

The blonde guy whipped around, his face instantly lighting up. "Sera!" Then he groaned, throwing his hands up. "Ugh, you totally ruined the surprise!"

Elara couldn't help but laugh. Classic Lysander, whining about getting caught.

Then his gaze drifted down. The second he saw Esperanza, the rest of the world just vanished. His jaw dropped, pure awe washing over his face. "Goddess," he breathed. "She's... she's gorgeous."

Before anyone could say a word, he gently took Esperanza from Elara’s arms. The little girl stared at him, quiet, not scared at all—just super curious.

Then, a spark of recognition hit her. A massive grin spread across her face, and she threw her arms right around Lysander's neck. The sudden hug left everyone in the room completely stunned.

"I remember you."

Lysander went rigid. Every muscle locked up. His smile vanished, his breath catching in his throat.

Esperanza just snuggled closer. "You saved me."

Dead silence slammed into the kitchen. Total and absolute. Even the sizzling on the stove seemed to mute. Nobody moved. Nobody breathed. For a few long, heavy seconds, you could've heard a pin drop.
