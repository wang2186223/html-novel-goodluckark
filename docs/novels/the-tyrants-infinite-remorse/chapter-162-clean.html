Chapter 164

Elara froze. The color drained straight out of her face, her mind spinning into overdrive. She didn't just need Caspian's muscle or his network. She needed him to actually trust her. But the truth was a total mess. Spitting it out meant dragging his best buddy into the mud, and that was a massive gamble.

"Let’s get one thing straight, Caspian," she said, keeping her voice flat and cold. "We aren't buddies. I’m glad you’re helping, and yeah, I appreciate the backup. But that’s the line. Don't cross it." She spun on her heel, ready to bolt before things got messy.

But Caspian wasn't buying the act. He saw right through her—the missed details, the half-truths. "Sera," he barked, stopping her dead in her tracks. "Look, if I’m putting my neck on the line to keep you breathing, the least you can do is cut the crap and tell me the truth."

Nearby, the gunshots kept blasting, sharp and deafening. Lysander was totally in his element, tearing up the shooting range like he’d finally found his happy place. The constant bang-bang-bang rattled her skull, making it impossible to think straight.

Caspian saw her eyes darting around, visibly twitching at the noise. His voice softened up a bit. "Hey. Too loud? Let’s talk in my ride."

Elara sucked in a breath, gave a tight nod, and looked over at Lysander. "I’ll be out in the lot," she yelled over the noise. "Make it quick." Lysander flashed her a goofy grin. She actually let out a tiny laugh, then turned and walked away.

They climbed into the SUV, slamming the doors shut. Instantly, the vibe shifted. The roar of the guns vanished, replaced by a heavy, suffocating quiet. Just the two of them, trapped in a tight space. It felt weird. Different. Neither of them knew what to make of it.

Elara broke the ice to stop her chest from tightening up. "Thane is your ride-or-die," she said, dead serious. "Whatever comes out of my mouth next stays in this car. You don't breathe a word to him."

Caspian hesitated. Sure, Thane was his closest bro, and they didn't keep secrets. But now he had to sit on a massive bomb—and of all things, it was about Thane’s ex-wife. Still, he nodded. "My lips are sealed."

Elara took a beat to steady her shaking nerves, then let it drop. "Lysander didn't kill my baby. When Thane gave the order to do it, Lysander backed out."

Caspian went stiff. Total shock hit him first, then a wave of pure relief, though he still looked like he’d just seen a ghost. "Damn... that’s actually amazing news," he muttered.

"Yeah, it is," Elara said, her face still hard as stone. "But there’s a catch." She glanced out the window, scanning the empty parking lot to make sure nobody was lurking nearby.

Caspian leaned in, his voice dropping to a whisper. "What kind of catch?"

"He has no idea who he gave her to," she said. That one sentence changed the whole damn game.

Caspian slumped back against his seat, putting the puzzle pieces together. "So... you think she’s locked up with Dominic’s people?"

Elara looked down at her lap. "I’m out of options," she whispered. For a split second, the tough-girl act slipped, showing the raw, broken mother underneath. "It’s a long shot, but I have to try."

Caspian watched her, tracking the sudden crack in her armor. This wasn't some tactical play or a revenge plot anymore. This was her kid. "I’m in," he said, keeping his voice steady but cautious. "But you gotta prepare yourself for the worst, okay?"

She nodded slowly. "I know. She could be dead. She could be in that hellhole. Or maybe she’s safe somewhere. I just don’t know."

The silence stretched, thick and heavy. Then Caspian threw a wrench in it. "If you do find her... doesn't Thane deserve to know?"

Elara’s eyes went pitch black. The vulnerability vanished, replaced by pure ice. Caspian got the message instantly. He threw his hands up. "Or, uh, maybe not. I mean, he’s already got Asher."

"If you open your mouth to him..." Elara trailed off, her voice dropping to a deadly whisper.

"Got it," Caspian cut her off, letting out a breath. "You’d probably murder me. Fair enough." That got a tiny, humorless smirk out of her. She couldn't handle being this close to him anymore. "I'm going to my car," she said, grabbing the door handle.

Just as she popped the door, her phone buzzed. She checked the screen and picked up. "Julian?"

"Sera, we got a situation. Get over here right now," Julian barked.

"On my way," she said instantly.

She turned back to Caspian. "Tell Lysander I took the car. Emergency at the hospital."

She marched toward the driver's side, her mind already racing ahead. But before she could grab the handle, Caspian called out.

"Let me drive."

The words slipped out before he could stop them. Even he looked a little shocked that he’d just offered to play chauffeur.
