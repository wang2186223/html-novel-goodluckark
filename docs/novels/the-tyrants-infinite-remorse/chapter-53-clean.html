NovelVibe

Chapter 53

Elara was halfway home when her phone buzzed. Caspian. She spun the wheel, pulling into a high-end, tucked-away coffee shop in the financial district. It was the kind of place where you paid extra just so people wouldn't overhear your business.

The place was dead. Just a few shadows sitting under dim lights. The smell of espresso filled the room—cozy, sure, but totally fake given what was about to go down.

Caspian usually walked around like he owned the damn world. He didn't bend, and he definitely didn't compromise unless it lined his pockets. But tonight? He was walking into a trap, forced to play nice. Or at least look like it. The guy didn't do humble.

He walked in with a clenched jaw. A muscle ticked in his cheek. He looked pissed but kept it tight, like he’d practiced his "chill face" in the mirror before leaving the house.

*Stay cool,* he kept telling himself. Caleb said that’s how you win. Just get her to unlock the server, and then? Boom. He’d block her number and never look at her face again. This whole mess would just be a stupid blip on his radar.

Elara walked in a minute later. She looked sharp, the kind of gorgeous that made guys forget she could ruin their lives. Her heels clicked on the tile. She held her head high, her eyes totally dead. Ever since her life blew up, she had this coldness in her.

No fake smiles. No "hey, how are you." They weren't friends. They were here for blood.

She slid into the seat across from him, locked her fingers, and stared him down. "Wire the cash. Dump Thane. Do that, and you get your server back."

Caspian’s face twisted. "You doubled the price, and now you’re giving me demands? Seriously?" He snapped, his temper slipping for a split second before he locked it down. Showing weakness to her was a bad move.

She let out a tiny, sharp smirk. "Oh, I can make it worse."

Caspian leaned back, crossing his arms like a shield. His gut told him to watch his step. "Alright. Let’s hear it."

"Ten billion. Cut Thane loose," she said, her voice flat, unreadable. "And pull every single dollar you have out of Walker Global."

The air went cold. Caspian froze. He stared at her, trying to find a smirk, a twitch, anything to show she was bluffing.

"You’re shitting me, right?" he muttered.

Elara raised an eyebrow. "Do I look like I’m playing? Thane and his little side chick killed my baby. I’m getting even."

It was quiet, but the pain in her voice was like a live wire. No tears, no screaming, just pure, dangerous grief.

Caspian ground his teeth. He had to play the logic card. "Look, Sera, you’re spiraling," he muttered, leaning in. "You don't get how business works. Here’s the deal. I’ll give you twenty billion. I stay out of your war with your ex. You give me my server. Deal?"

Twenty billion. That was huge. Elara took a slow breath, her brain spinning the math. Not just the cash, but the chess board.

Could a guy like Caspian really stay neutral? Rich guys like them always backed each other up when things got messy.

"You’re his boy," she said, narrowing her eyes. "You’ll help him the second he asks."

Caspian didn't blink. "Then you don't know how this works. Our shares make us both rich. It's scratch-my-back, scratch-yours. That’s why you’re stuck. You can't win this game alone. You need partners. You need a network."

He was right, and it pissed her off. That was why the Stone Group was stuck at number four on Forbes. They didn't have the network.

If she burned Walker Global to the ground, she’d burn a dozen innocent companies tied to it. She wasn't a monster. She didn't want collateral damage.

"Well?" Caspian pressed. "What's it gonna be?"

She snapped back to reality. "You swear you’re done helping him?" He nodded. Fast.

"Prove it," she said.

Caspian glanced over at Lysander, who was sitting a few tables away, keeping watch. Caspian pointed. "Ask your boyfriend. Thane probably called him already."

"Boyfriend?" Elara blinked, thrown off. "He’s family. I look out for him. He’s not my boyfriend."

She thought of Julian—their fake dating plan was coming up, and she didn't need rumors messing that up. But then she saw Caspian let out a tiny breath of relief. Why the hell did he care?

She turned to Lysander. "Hey. Any calls?"

"Thane," Lysander said. "Wants to meet. I told him I'd get back to him."

Elara turned back to Caspian, staring him down. "Send him the address," she said, a cold smile creeping onto her face. "Tell him to get his ass over here. Now."

Caspian frowned. "Wait, what are you doing?"

She leaned back, her eyes gleaming. "You said you're out, right? Let's watch you prove it."

Caspian’s face fell. He realized she wasn't taking his word for it. She wanted to watch the show live.
