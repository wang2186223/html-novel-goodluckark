Chapter 325

Caspian broke the silence first. He didn't have the whole story, but the kid's words still hit him like a freight train.

"Espe, what's that supposed to mean?" His voice stayed cool, his usual calm. But his eyes? Totally freaked.

Esperanza stared back with those huge, innocent blue eyes. "Daddy, do you believe me?" She locked onto him, hoping, but clearly scared he'd say no.

That question punched him right in the gut.

Last night, she wouldn't say a damn thing. Now? She was ready to spill everything if she could just trust them. No way in hell he was shutting her down.

He softened his face and leaned in close. "Okay, let's play pretend. Say this isn't some wild dream," he said, darting a quick look at Lysander before focusing back on her. "Say it's real. How do you know him?"

Esperanza immediately wrapped her tiny arms around Lysander's neck. Pure instinct.

She gripped his shirt, her eyes darting between her parents. "He was supposed to kill me," she said. Just like that.

The room went dead. Elara stopped breathing.

But the kid didn't blink. "But he gave me to that lady instead." She scrunched her nose, digging through her brain for the details. "And she gave him a goat to chop up."

The silence got heavier, suffocating. Lysander's chest squeezed tight, his heart thumping. "Damn. She's not making this up," he muttered.

Every eye in the room snapped to him. He ran a hand through his hair, trying to process. "I remember that," he said, his voice sounding hollow. "A farm animal in a place like that? It was weird. I used it to fool Thane."

He looked back at Esperanza. She looked way too sure. Way too familiar with stuff no kid should ever know.

"If she knows about the goat..." He stopped. The words died in his throat. There was only one explanation, and it was flat-out impossible.

Across the room, Elara took a slow, deep breath. Her mind was spinning. How did Esperanza end up with Caspian? What did she actually remember? Why was she tied to stuff from way before she was even born? Then she remembered: the kid wanted food.

Elara forced herself to focus on something simple. "I'm making pancakes. Right now," she said, grabbing a bowl.

Lysander scrowled. "Hey, we were cooking for you," he said, watching her. "You seriously need to do this right now?"

It was her time of the month. She should be resting, not sweating over a hot stove.

Elara ignored him, grabbing the flour. His scowl deepened.

Before he could argue, two tiny hands slapped onto his cheeks.

Lysander blinked, staring straight into Esperanza's deadpan face.

Since she was hitched on his hip, she had a clear shot. It was so sudden he almost snorted.

"Look at me," she ordered.

He looked. Her face was dead serious. "Esperanza talks when her belly is full."

Nobody moved. Lysander just stared at her like she'd just declared herself boss of the house. The kid didn't flinch. Just pure, unadulterated confidence.

His heart melted. A grin tugged at his mouth. Seriously, how could anyone say no to this kid? She was getting cuter by the second.

Linnea snorted a laugh. "Honestly, I'm just glad she wants to eat," she said, leaning against the counter. "Usually, I have to beg her to take a bite."

Before anyone could answer, the doorbell buzzed. Caspian started for the door, but Linnea cut him off.

"I got it," she said, already jogging out of the kitchen. They watched her disappear.

Caspian turned back to Elara, a smirk playing on his lips. "Well, once the pancakes are done, we eat, then head to the office."

He sounded amused. "First time cooking, actually."

He jerked a thumb at Lysander. "He's been showing me the ropes."

Elara froze, whisk in hand. She stared. These two powerful, terrifying men had spent their morning learning how to cook for her?

"Wait," she blinked. "You guys are cooking? For me? You didn't just tell the maids to do it?"

Caspian and Lysander traded a look, then nodded. Yeah. They did. They wanted to.

A warm wave hit Elara's chest, making her eyes sting.

After everything, she still wasn't used to being babied. She smiled softly. "Thank you," she whispered, her grin widening. "I'm definitely eating all of it."

Both men visibly relaxed. Elara almost laughed at how worried they'd been.

Then, footsteps. Outside the kitchen. Lots of them.

Everyone tensed.

Caspian stood up straight. Lysander's eyes went sharp. Clinging to Esperanza, he moved up beside Caspian, heading toward the noise.

The voices got louder. They rounded the corner. Caspian saw the visitors and froze, his jaw dropping.
