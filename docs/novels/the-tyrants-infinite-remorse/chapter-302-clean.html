NovelVibe

Chapter 306

Elara tried to wrench her face away. If she could just break eye contact, maybe her brain would start working again. Maybe she’d stop spinning. But he was right there, all sharp jawline and those damn eyes that saw right through her. No way he was letting her slide back into her shell.

"Two days," she croaked, grabbing the first argument she could find. "Two days ago you wanted to be buddies, Caspian. Now you’re trying to claim me?"

He didn't even blink. A tiny smirk tugged at his mouth—not a real laugh, just him dropping the act.

"The friend thing was just to get my foot in the door," he murmured, his voice dropping an octave. "You don't go around kissing your friends, do you, Sera?"

Heat flushed up her neck, hot and fast, straight to her ears. He definitely noticed. His eyes tracked the blush, locking onto her face.

"That first kiss," he said, like he’d replayed it a million times in his head. "That wasn't our wolves acting up."

"Gideon wanted stuff I wasn't going to take. Not unless you wanted it too. But that kiss? I let it happen because I wanted to. No wolf-mind-control. Just me."

He stopped, staring her down. "And you know damn well it was the same for you. You don't let some guy put his mouth on yours if you don't feel it."

His words slammed right through her defenses. She couldn't even fight back. That was the worst part—it was true. She’d never let a guy get that close without a spark.

Elara wasn't that kind of girl. She didn't just let things happen to her. Caspian knew it, too. He’d always gotten her, even when she thought she was completely invisible to him.

Her lips parted. She scrambled for a comeback. "I..."

"If you’d pulled back the second we touched," Caspian cut in, his voice soft, "I would’ve walked. I would’ve figured I read it wrong and let you go. But you didn't, Sera. You kissed me back. And right then, I knew."

Something in his eyes went totally still. "Your brain just hadn't caught up yet."

"I tried to back off right after," she snapped.

A dry, knowing look crossed his face. "So did I. Every single time."

He pushed up slightly, giving her some breathing room, but kept his eyes locked on hers. "We're the same, you and me. Experts at burying what we feel and calling it something else."

He let the words hang. Then his jaw clenched.

"When Thane sent those first divorce papers, I thought, *Okay. Finally. She can breathe.*" His voice got rough. "Then he showed up at my place with Briar. Spouted all kinds of trash about you. Made me want to break his jaw. I figured after that mess, you were done."

"But you rejected the papers. You let him keep the keys to the pack accounts. You let him chip away at you, and you still stayed."

Elara’s throat went tight. She kept her mouth shut.

"Every time he came back with some new demand, I thought, *This is it. This is the dealbreaker.*" His voice went flat, totally drained. "But you just bent. You didn't break. And I started thinking maybe I was the idiot here, thinking I actually had a shot."

He exhaled hard through his nose. "I told myself I’d rather be alone than be some backup plan. I’m nobody’s second choice."

Elara stared at the ceiling, swallowing hard before she could look back at him.

"And then," he said, his face tightening with some old, raw pain, "he told me he was making Briar co-Managing Director. Done deal. Like you didn't even matter. I wanted to go to the ceremony just to see if you'd actually let him do it, but my meetings ran late. By the time I got there, it was over. And then you literally ran into me."

His lips thinned. "But you were like ice, Sera. You looked right through me. I thought I’d waited too long. That the door was shut."

Elara lay frozen under him, processing. All those years she’d spent chasing Thane’s shadow, hoping he’d look at her, Caspian had been right there in the background. Waiting. Stubbornly fighting his own quiet war.

Did it hurt to realize that? Yeah. Hell yeah, it did. More than she wanted to admit.

She breathed out. "I stayed for Asher. You know that," she whispered.

"But that last demand... I couldn't do it. I was stuck in bed for months after that chemical attack. Months, Caspian. Briar set the whole thing up, I saved the pack anyway, and Thane never even walked through my door to check on me. Not once."

Her eyes went cold. "And now it's happening again. Same girl, same crap, and here I am. Again." She paused, swallowing the lump in her throat. "But you... after the divorce, you didn't do anything either. You just stood there and asked me to give Briar my blood. My blood, Caspian."

Anger flared, hot and sharp. She shoved at his chest with everything she had. He didn't budge, but he rolled off her anyway, looking completely wrecked with guilt.
