Chapter 212

"Look who's back," Blaze grumbled in her head the second they hit the hospital parking lot. She sounded groggy, like she’d just rolled out of bed after a decade-long nap. "Ugh. You seriously couldn't stay away, could you?"

Elara grinned. A heavy knot in her chest finally loosened. God, she needed this. Hearing that voice made the world stop spinning. *Missed you, babe,* she thought, her mental voice a little shaky. *Like, seriously.*

"Yeah, yeah, you got me now," Blaze snapped, her usual attitude roaring back. "Can't say the feeling's mutual, though. I was having the best sleep of my life without your constant drama." She paused. Then, her mental tone went ice-cold. "Hold up. Is that... him?"

Elara didn't need a name.

"Is that the prick?" Blaze hissed, sounding ready to murder. "Please tell me you didn't crawl back to him. Because if you did, I swear to God, I'm taking the wheel and crashing this body."

Elara snorted, a genuine laugh slipping out. Warmth flooded her. Man, she had missed this crazy, overprotective wolf.

*Not a chance in hell,* Elara shot back. She glanced at Thane, then immediately looked away like he was a piece of trash on the sidewalk. *I'm only fixing this mess because his folks asked. They're my family now, not his.* Her thoughts softened on the word *family*. *Catch you in a bit. I've got some business to handle.*

Rhys opened his mouth to say something, but Elara shut him down before he could even start.

"I told you I need your beta's help while I'm here," she said, her voice cutting through the air like a knife. She stared straight at Thane, her eyes hard and cool. "What I need from him is none of your business. Unless you want to make it your business?"

*Hell yeah,* Blaze purred. *Glad to see you finally grew a spine.*

Elara swallowed a sigh. The voice was a distraction, sure, but she wasn't about to block Blaze out. Not after losing her for so long. This bond was everything.

Across from her, Thane's knuckles turned white. He clamped his jaw shut, swallowing his anger. He knew he had to play nice. One wrong move and she'd walk, and then the cure was history.

"Fine," he choked out, the word tasting like poison. "We freaked out when we couldn't reach you. Your phones were dead."

"Oh, I turned them off on purpose," Elara said, crossing her arms and giving him a dry, mocking look. "I really didn't want any annoying distractions while I was getting my rest."

Thane's face darkened, a nasty scowl forming. "So you two spent the whole night together?"

Elara let out a massive, fake yawn, making sure he saw just how bored she was. She rolled her eyes. "And why do you care, exactly?" He shut up. She let the silence hang for a second before dropping the hammer. "We're both single adults, Thane." She flashed a wicked little smirk. "But don't worry. Your beta actually knows how to act like a gentleman."

She tilted her head, raising an eyebrow. "Now, are we going to keep playing twenty questions, or do you want me to actually save your people?"

"The patients," Thane muttered, instantly folding the second her eyes went cold. He looked at the beta. "Rhys, let's go."

"Wait, where?" Thane blurted out, taking a step forward like a desperate puppy.

Elara didn't even pause. "Uh, who else is going to keep your rabid pack from biting my head off?"

Thane swallowed hard, completely thrown off. "Hey, I'm the Chief Commander. I can—"

"Mom!"

A kid's voice cut him off. Asher slammed the car door and sprinted toward her, throwing his entire weight into her. Elara caught him on impact, her icy demeanor melting instantly as she wrapped her arms around him.

"Hey, buddy," she murmured, rubbing his back to soothe him. "You doing okay?"

*Whoa, hold on,* Blaze growled in her head, sounding pissed. *Why are you hugging that kid? He's his spawn.*

Elara squeezed Asher a little tighter. *Chill,* she thought back. *He's just a little kid. Plus, he finally figured out who the good guy is.*

Blaze grunted, still annoyed but dropping it. *Fine. But we are getting his mother's blood before we pack our bags. Do not forget that.*

*Deal,* Elara promised.

Just then, a familiar heat bloomed in her lower belly. Her period was starting. But there was no cramping, no agonizing pain this time. She'd prepped for it. Plus, the pack's raw energy was humming through her wolf, keeping her grounded and totally pain-free.

"I'm good now," Asher mumbled, burying his face in her shirt. "I just really missed you."

A few feet away, Thane just stood there, jaw slack. He stared at them like he'd just seen a ghost, completely speechless.

Elara gave Asher a soft smile and pulled back just enough to look at him. "Hey, want to come watch me do my thing?"

His eyes went wide. "For real?"

"For real."

"Awesome!"

She shot a freezing look over her shoulder at Thane. "Pack his stuff. Clothes, snacks, whatever he needs."

She didn't wait for him to answer. She spun on her heel and headed for the clinic doors, Asher glued to her side and Rhys trailing right behind them like a silent bodyguard.

Thane stood frozen in the parking lot, watching their retreating backs. He let out a long, heavy sigh and rubbed his face. For a guy who was supposed to be the Chief Commander, he felt like a damn errand boy.

Still, he dragged himself to the kitchen. He started whipping up a batch of pancakes for Asher, his hands moving on autopilot while a weird, heavy dread settled deep in his gut.

Then, his mindlink buzzed.

*Boss,* the gate guard's voice popped into his head, sounding tense. *Chief Commander Orion is here. He wants to see you. Now.*

Thane froze. His grip tightened on the spatula until his knuckles cracked. A cold shiver ran straight down his spine.

Something was seriously wrong.
